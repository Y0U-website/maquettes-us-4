Pediatric Restorative Dentistry In Parrish, FL

When it comes to ensuring your little one’s smile feels and functions at its best, we’re here to help. Treatment for dental decay varies based on the size, depth, and severity of the cavity, and we always aim for the most conservative, tooth-preserving approach that is appropriate. When possible, Sweet Street Pediatric Dentistry provides families with treatment options ranging from simple dental fillings to more advanced care such as pulp therapy, pediatric crowns, or extractions, all with the goal of restoring and protecting your child’s long-term oral health. Pediatric Stainless Steel Crowns

Pediatric stainless steel crowns are durable caps used to protect and restore back primary teeth that have severe cavities or significant enamel breakdown. They may also serve as temporary crowns for permanent molars in growing children with severe enamel defects or teeth needing root canal treatment, and they are made from non-toxic metal with no mercury.

Pediatric Premium Zirconia Cosmetic Crowns

Premium zirconia crowns are strong, tooth-colored (white) crowns used to restore primary front and back teeth that have significant decay or damage. These durable, natural-looking crowns blend beautifully with your child’s smile while protecting and strengthening the treated tooth.

Pediatric Pulp Caps

Pulp caps are a conservative treatment used in select cases when a cavity or minor injury gets close to the inner nerve of the tooth. A protective, biocompatible material such as MTA (Mineral Trioxide Aggregate) is placed over the affected area to help seal the tooth, support natural healing, and preserve the vitality of the nerve so your child’s tooth can continue developing normally.

Pediatric Extractions

When it comes to restoring your child&#x27;s smile, our pediatric preventative dentist will always explore alternative treatments before considering tooth extractions. Our primary focus is to preserve and restore damaged teeth whenever possible, but in some cases, an extraction is the best option to get your child’s smile back on track. We prioritize your child’s comfort by using proper techniques, and sedation is available as an option for families who prefer additional support. Say goodbye to discomfort and hello to a bright, healthy smile!

Space Maintenance

When baby teeth are lost too early, nearby teeth can shift and reduce the space needed for permanent teeth to come in properly. Space maintainers help preserve that space and support healthy dental development as your child grows. Depending on your child’s needs, we may use appliances such as a Band and Loop, Nance appliance, or Lower Lingual Holding Arch to help guide proper tooth eruption and reduce the risk of future orthodontic problems.
