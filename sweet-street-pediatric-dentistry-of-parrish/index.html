The practice

Our practice offers a wide range of services to ensure your child’s dental health is in the best hands. From preventive care to restorative treatments, we are here for you every step of the way.

Good oral health starts with prevention. We offer regular cleanings, fluoride treatments, and dental sealants to protect your child’s teeth and set the foundation for a cavity-free smile. Preventive care is one of the easiest ways to ensure a lifetime of healthy teeth!

Preserve your child’s natural teeth with pulpotomy treatments designed to treat cavities that have reached the inner nerve of the tooth. With our comfort-focused and conservative approach, we help keep tooth-saving treatments stress-free for you and your child.

For children with damaged or decayed teeth, dental crowns provide durable protection to restore both function and comfort. We offer stainless steel crowns for strength and reliability, ideal for active little ones, as well as zirconia (tooth-colored) crowns, a more aesthetic option that blends naturally with your child’s smile while maintaining excellent durability.

For children who feel anxious or require more extensive treatment, our pediatric sedation dentist provides safe and effective sedation options to ensure a calm, comfortable experience. All IV sedation is provided and closely monitored by a board-certified medical anesthesiologist for the highest level of safety and care.

At Sweet Street Pediatric Dentistry, we go the extra mile to deliver a dental experience that’s truly one of a kind. From our patient-centered pediatric approach to our emphasis on education and prevention, you can trust our team to care for your child like their own.

Pediatric-focused care We specialize in working with children, including those who may be anxious or have special needs, and provide personalized, compassionate treatment that makes every child feel at ease.

Prevention and education We empower families with the knowledge and tools needed for a lifetime of oral health, helping parents and kids make confident decisions about their care.
