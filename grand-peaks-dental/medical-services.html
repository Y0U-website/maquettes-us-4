Yes! Grand Peaks Medical welcomes patients with either Medicare or Medicaid. *Note: If you have a Medicaid Healthy Connections provider other than Grand Peaks, you may need a referral. To change your Healthy Connections provider you can contact the Healthy Connections office at (888) 528-5861.

We offer all types of wellness exams and physicals under one roof, including DOT, missionary, boy scout, and sports physicals. Pricing varies on most physicals, however boy scout and sports physical are free for those under the age of 19.

Yes! Dr. Jacob Curtis and Dr. Trevor Wilde provide prenatal and OB care at Grand Peaks Medical. Our Physician Assistants and Nurse Practitioners are able to care for our OB patients when the doctors are unavailable.

In all cases, if you feel your life is in danger, please seek care at an Emergency Room or call 911. If a patient presents themselves at our clinic, we evaluate them to see if the injury or illness is significant enough to receive care at an Emergency Room. We are able to treat sprains, strains, bruises, lacerations (even if they require stitches), and more. Many times, the cost savings over an Emergency Room are significant.

Yes! Our medical facilities have x-ray and laboratory equipment which allow many labs to be performed and results to be given to you before you leave the facility. Some labs are sent to an outside lab and additional charges may apply.

Yes! We offer a full service pharmacy, available to everyone. It is located in our Saint Anthony office but can be utilized at our Rexburg medical center! For our patients, we offer a discounted 340B program. For additional information, see our Pharmacy Services .

We ask that you pay for your visit or copay at the time of service. A copay is an amount set by your insurance company and your policy. Many visits do receive discounts based on the sliding fee scale for those who qualify based on family size andincome. In certain situations payment arrangements can be made.
