Why Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a titanium post placed in the jawbone that acts as a root for a custom crown. Once it fuses with the bone, it looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth — no slipping, no adhesive.

Two or more implants can also support a bridge or a denture for patients missing several teeth. At your free dental implant consultation in Pembroke Pines, Dr. Kiani explains which option fits your smile.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

  • One or more missing teeth
  • Healthy gums, or gum concerns treated first
  • Enough jawbone — or grafting to rebuild it
  • Medical conditions such as diabetes under control
  • Non-smokers, or willing to quit before treatment
  • Committed to brushing, flossing, and cleanings
