Our Team

Contact Us Family Photos Friendly Staff That Wants to Know You Better! At River Bend Dental, you’ll have a small, familiar team to greet you at every visit. We care about forming real relationships with our patients; our hygienists are particularly good about remembering the details of everyone who visits us, even to the point of knowing their birthdays. The brief biographies below can give you your first introduction to our team. We’re looking forward to meeting you for real at your first appointment, so give us a call today!

Ivette Ivette, Dental Assistant From Garland, TX, Ivette has been working in the dental field for almost a decade. She’s performed dental administrative work for five years and studied at the University of Texas Health Science Center in Houston to become a Registered Dental Assistant. She’s also certified in coronal polishing, pit & fissure sealant, and nitrous oxide. Her favorite part of the job is building connections with our patients.

In her free time, Ivette enjoys hanging out with her 3 dogs—Josephine, Via, and Texas. Her favorite hobbies include listening to music, going to concerts, and working on her 1994 Ford Lightning. One interesting thing about her: she loves learning about and working on cars and trucks!

Rae, Dental Hygienist

Rae has more than 25 years of experience in the dental field, and she joined our team at the end of 2023. A Texas native, she chose to complete the Dental Hygiene Program at Texas Woman’s University, and she now has her bachelor’s degree in dental hygiene. Throughout her career, her favorite part of her job has been getting to know patients a bit better at each and every appointment!

Rae and her husband of more than 20 years have three children together, who range from age 5 to 17. When she isn’t busy with her kids’ extracurricular activities, she enjoys running, baking, jet-skiing, and traveling to East Texas (which is where their family property is). Here’s something you might not know about her: she has an Aussiedoodle named Bear!
