Dr. Shiva Seyyadri is a UCLA post graduate with over 7 years of clinical experience , including 6 years serving patients at a community health center in Spokane, WA. He brings a comprehensive, patient-centered approach to dentistry with a focus on understanding the underlying risk factors that contribute to dental conditions.

Dr. Seyyadri is an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA) , the American Association of Endodontists (AAE) , the Washington State Dental Association (WSDA) , and the Clark County Dental Society (CCDS) .

He is committed to providing compassionate, high-quality care for the entire family — from young children to seniors.

In addition to his expertise in general dentistry, he specializes in advanced restorative and endodontic care. He enjoys educating patients about the risk factors that impact current oral health, prior dental work and any new work that may be needed while helping to create healthy and beautiful smiles for the community.

Dr. Ishita Bhalla began her journey in the United States by completing the Advanced Clinical Training (ACT) Program in Endodontics at UCLA . She then completed her General Practice Residency (GPR) at Providence Sacred Heart Medical Center in Spokane, followed by an AEGD residency through Northwest Dental Residency at Unify Community Health in Spokane.
