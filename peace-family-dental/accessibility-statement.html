Accessibility Statement

  • Our website can be navigated using a keyboard alone.
  • Color contrast ratios meet or exceed the WCAG 2.1 AA minimum of 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text.
  • Form fields include clearly associated labels.
  • Links include descriptive text so users know where each link leads.
  • Pages use proper heading structure (H1, H2, H3) to aid navigation.
  • Videos and multimedia include captions or transcripts where applicable.
  • Our site is compatible with common screen readers including NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver.

Technical Specifications

This website relies on the following technologies for conformance with WCAG 2.1:

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • WAI-ARIA (Accessible Rich Internet Applications)
  • JavaScript (used only to enhance, not replace, core functionality)

Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some areas may not yet fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards. We are actively working to address the following:

  • Some older PDF documents may not be fully accessible. We are working to provide accessible alternatives.
  • Third-party embedded content (such as maps or booking widgets) may have accessibility limitations beyond our direct control. We encourage providers to meet accessibility standards and we will seek accessible alternatives where possible.
