It s a privilege to treat you and a must for you and/or your child to feel happy and comfortable when you are with us.

When visiting our Dentist, the hard and soft tissues of the oral cavity will be examined. These areas include, but are not limited to : recedings or swellings in the gums, clicking sounds in the TMJ and tooth decay. Our Dentist may recommend routine radiographs to help with diagnosis.

For children, we offer treatment under IV sedation, for more information please call or make an appointment.

Preventive dentistry!!! Our top priority is to PREVENT dental problems. Prevention begins with dental education, good dental habits and hygiene, & regular visits to Samuel Dental.

Teeth whitening is an easy way to brighten teeth and enhance your smile.

Using state-of-the-art LED light technology, our Dentist may lighten your teeth 5-7 shades in just one hour with our Zoom whitening system.

We are proud to offer digital radiographs with the lowest possible x-ray exposure. Radiographs help us view the teeth & gums aiding in diagnosing cavities as well as impacted teeth.
