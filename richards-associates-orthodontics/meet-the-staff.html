Meet the Team

Our experienced team is ready to help you transform your smile.

Our Doctors

John M. Richards, D.D.S., M.S., P.A.

Dr. Richards graduated Magna Cum Laude from the University of Maryland. He then attended the Baltimore College of Dental Surgery for four years graduating at the top 5 % of his class. He then completed a hospital residency at University Hospital with special emphasis on oral surgery and cleft lip and palate treatment. This training was all in preparation for a three-year orthodontic residency which he completed at the University of Maryland in 1991.

Dr. Richards lives in Fort Myers with his wife Kimberley and together they have raised five children: Whitney, Kelly, Tyler, Krystal and Olivia. He loves sunny Southwest Florida and all it has to offer. He also loves practicing orthodontics and plans to continue helping people smile in southwest Florida for a long time!

Whitney Richards, D.D.S., M.S.

We are delighted to announce the addition of Dr. Whitney A Richards as our new Associate Orthodontist. We are honored to welcome her to our team, allowing us to continue providing the exceptional orthodontic care our community has come to trust.

Dr Richards is a Southwest Florida native who grew up right here in our practice, assisting her father, Dr. John M Richards. She is a proud graduate of Bishop Verot High School (2007), she went on to earn her Bachelor of Science in Biology from Florida State University (2012) and a Master’s degree in Biomedical Science from Barry University (2018). She then completed her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree at the University of Maryland (2022), followed by a three-year Orthodontic residency at the Georgia School of Orthodontics.

While in residency, Dr. Richards and her husband, Benjamin Briar, welcomed two beautiful daughters, Lily and Josie. In her free time, she enjoys spending time with family and friends and is thrilled to be back home in Fort Myers, where she looks forward to serving her community and enjoying all that Southwest Florida has to offer.

Dr Richards is an active member of the Florida Dental Association, American Dental Association, Southern Association of Orthodontists, and the American Association of Orthodontists. She is exceptionally well-trained and eager to bring her skill, compassion, and expertise to our patients.

Please join us in giving Dr Richards a warm welcome to our orthodontic family. We are truly grateful for the trust you place in us and remain committed to creating beautiful, confident smiles for years to come.

Our Staff

As team members, we all share Dr. Richard’s vision for making beautiful smiles and are especially interested in making all our patients feel right at home! We aim to provide an energetic and fun-loving atmosphere for every patient while putting their needs first to achieve efficient, comprehensive treatment and exceptional results.

Every staff member is well trained and proficient in their respective field, and many have been with us for several years, which allows us to create that family feeling for our patients. Members of our team have Orthodontic Expanded Functions and X-ray certifications. We aim to stay abreast of the latest technological advances by regularly attending continuing education courses and seminars.
