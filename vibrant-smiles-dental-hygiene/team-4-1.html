Meet The Hygienist

0 Vanessa Salinas RDHAP, BSDH Vanessa brings extensive experience and a deep commitment to dental care to her practice. She began her career as a dental assistant in 2008. She advanced her expertise by pursuing a degree in dental hygiene, becoming a Registered Dental Hygienist in 2018. Vanessa earned her Bachelor’s degree in Dental Hygiene in 2021 at West Los Angeles College, and further specialized in alternative dental care, achieving her RDHAP certification in 2023 from the University Of Pacific.

In addition to her practice, Vanessa is an instructor at a local community college, where she imparts her knowledge to future dental hygienists. She is an active member of the California Dental Association and other professional organizations, continually pursuing further education to stay at the forefront of the field.

Currently, Vanessa is working towards a Master’s degree in Public Health and aspires to establish an educational facility dedicated to providing continuing education courses for future dental providers and professionals. Her dedication to her patients and her profession is evident in her efforts to enhance oral health education and improve treatment outcomes.

Vanessa strives to create a nurturing environment where patients receive not only exceptional care but also a clear understanding of their oral health. She is passionate about empowering patients with the knowledge and tools necessary for achieving and maintaining optimal dental health while building a brighter future for the field through education and innovation.
