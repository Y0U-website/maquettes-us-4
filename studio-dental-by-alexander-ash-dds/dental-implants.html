Dental Implants

  • Healing Integration The implant bonds with bone over several months for stability.
  • Custom Crown Placement A natural-looking tooth is placed on top of the implant.
  • Full Function Restored Eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

All-on-4 Implants

All-on-4 implants provide a full-arch restoration using just four strategically placed implants. This efficient approach offers a stable, long-lasting solution for patients missing most or all of their teeth.

  • Full-Arch Restoration Replace an entire row of teeth with four implants. Efficient solution
  • Fewer Implants Required Minimizes surgical complexity.
  • Immediate Function Often allows for faster results.
  • Stable Secure Eliminates the need for removable dentures.

All-on-6 Implants

All-on-6 implants use six implants to support a full arch of teeth, offering additional stability and support for patients who need a more reinforced solution.

  • Enhanced Stability Additional implants provide extra support. Stronger foundation
  • Full Smile Restoration Replaces all missing teeth in one arch.
  • Improved Bite Strength Ideal for patients needing more durability.
  • Long-Term Solution Designed for longevity with proper care.

Implant Retained Crowns

For patients missing a single tooth, an implant-retained crown offers a natural-looking and fully functional replacement without affecting surrounding teeth.

  • Single Tooth Replacement Restores one missing tooth. Precise solution
  • Preserves Adjacent Teeth No need to alter nearby teeth.
  • Natural Appearance Custom-designed to blend seamlessly.
  • Strong Durable Functions like a natural tooth.

Implant Supported Bridge

An implant-supported bridge replaces multiple missing teeth by anchoring a bridge to dental implants rather than natural teeth.

  • Multiple Tooth Replacement Ideal for several missing teeth. Stable support
  • No Impact on Natural Teeth Unlike traditional bridges.
  • Improved Function Restores chewing and speaking ability.
  • Secure Fit Fixed in place for long-term reliability.

Implant Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures provide a more secure alternative to traditional dentures by anchoring them to implants for improved comfort and stability.

  • Secure Fit Eliminates slipping and movement. Improved comfort
  • Better Chewing Ability Allows for a more varied diet.
  • Enhanced Confidence Speak and smile without worry.
  • Removable or Fixed Options Customized to your preference.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer significant advantages over traditional tooth replacement methods like bridges or dentures.

  • Natural look and feel that blends with your smile
  • Long-term durability with proper care
  • Preserves jawbone and facial structure
  • Improves function, speech, and confidence

What to Expect from Treatment

The dental implant process typically takes several months, allowing time for proper healing and integration with the jawbone. While this timeline is longer than other options, the result is a stronger, more natural, and more permanent solution.
