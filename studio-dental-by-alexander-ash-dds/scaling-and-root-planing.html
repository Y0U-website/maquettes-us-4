Signs You May Need Deep Cleaning

Gum disease can develop without obvious symptoms at first, but certain signs may indicate the need for treatment.

  • Bleeding Gums Especially during brushing or flossing. Early warning sign
  • Persistent Bad Breath Caused by bacteria below the gums.
  • Gum Recession Teeth appearing longer than usual.
  • Loose Teeth Advanced gum disease affecting support structures.

Benefits of Scaling and Root Planing

This treatment is highly effective in managing gum disease and protecting your long-term oral health.

  • Reduces gum inflammation and bleeding
  • Helps prevent progression of periodontal disease
  • Supports reattachment of gum tissue
  • Protects against tooth and bone loss

Protect Your Gum Health

Early treatment is key to preventing more advanced periodontal issues. Scaling and root planing can help restore your gum health and create a stable foundation for your smile.

If you’re experiencing symptoms of gum disease, schedule a consultation to determine if deep cleaning is right for you.
