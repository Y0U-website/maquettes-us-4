The practice

In 2005, Dr. Hamilton and his wife, Becky, moved their family a short distance to Mapleton. There they raised their four children (Mickelle 30, Colton 28, Hailey 24, and Carson 22) and now the family has grown to include six (soon to be 8) grandchildren.

In April 2024, due to a myriad of circumstances, Ryan sold East Mountain Dental and joined Dr. Hulse and the outstanding team at Floss Family Dentistry. He brings with him over two decades of experience and plans to continue providing the same exceptional, compassionate care, surrounded by the incredible staff at Floss Family Dental.

When Ryan is not working, he loves spending time with his six grandchildren. He enjoys woodworking (aka tool collecting), and has always loved fishing, hunting, 4 Wheeling, and just being outdoors.

With many years of experience in all aspects of general dentistry, Dr. Westmoreland has worked both as a practice owner and as an associate alongside other respected dentists. She brings a well-rounded skill set, a compassionate chairside manner, and a deep commitment to helping patients achieve confident, healthy smiles.

Outside of the office, Dr. Westmoreland is the proud mother of three amazing children. She loves spending time outdoors and can often be found hiking, fishing, snowboarding, or camping with her family.
