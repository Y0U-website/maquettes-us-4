Our in-house membership plan covers preventive care plus a meaningful discount on everything else — predictable, no claims to file, no annual maximums. What’s included For a flat annual fee, our membership plan covers: Two regular cleanings per year (every 6 months)
  • Two comprehensive exams per year
  • All routine x-rays
  • Oral cancer screenings
  • Discount on additional treatment — fillings, crowns, root canals, dentures, and implants

How it works

  • Pay an annual fee (single, family, or pediatric pricing tiers).
  • Get all preventive care included — no copays, no deductibles, no insurance company in the middle.
  • Save on additional treatment if needed — your discount applies automatically at checkout.
  • No annual maximums — unlike PPO plans, there’s no $1,500 cap on what we can do for you in a year.

Is the membership plan right for me?

Note: The membership plan is not insurance — it’s a direct agreement between you and our practice. It can’t be combined with insurance benefits.

  • Don’t have dental insurance (or have a plan with very low coverage)
  • Want predictable annual dental costs
  • Plan to keep up with regular cleanings and preventive care
  • Want to avoid the paperwork, denials, and surprise bills that come with insurance
