0 We Are Shideler Dentistry Welcome to Shideler Dentistry, where tradition meets modern excellence in dental care. Dr. William Shideler proudly continues the legacy of his family’s dental practice, which has been a cornerstone of Downtown Valparaiso for over 40 years. As a third-generation dentist, Dr. Shideler is honored to follow in the footsteps of his father and grandfather in serving the oral health needs of our community.

For decades, our practice has been a trusted destination for comprehensive and compassionate dental care. Rooted in a rich history of family tradition, we combine time-honored values with cutting-edge techniques to deliver exceptional results for our patients.

At Shideler Dentistry, we believe in building lasting relationships with our patients based on trust, integrity, and personalized care. From routine cleanings to advanced restorative treatments, our experienced team is dedicated to helping you achieve and maintain a healthy, beautiful smile for life.

Experience the difference that generations of expertise can make in your dental care journey. Schedule your appointment with Dr. William Shideler at our historic Downtown Valparaiso location today.
