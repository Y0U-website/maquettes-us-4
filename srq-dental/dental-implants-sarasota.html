Dental Implants

Why are dental implants a good option for replacing missing teeth? What makes dental implants stand out is that they essentially act like natural teeth. The implant fuses with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration, which creates a strong and durable foundation for your replacement teeth. This makes them far more stable and long-lasting than dentures or bridges.

Different Applications for Dental Implants:

  • 0 Dental Implants Schedule Online Now! (941) 231-0225 A dental implant is made up of three separate parts : A sturdy metal post, usually made of titanium, is placed into your jawbone to replace a missing tooth (or teeth)
  • Once it heals, an abutment serves as a connector between the implant and the crown on top.
  • Lastly, a custom crown (a realistic-looking fake tooth) is attached to the implant, restoring both the look and function of your natural tooth. Implants are a long-lasting, permanent solution for tooth loss.
  • Durability : They can last for decades or even a lifetime with proper care, unlike bridges or dentures that may need to be replaced more frequently.
  • Natural Look & Feel : Once in place, implants look and function just like real teeth. They’re custom-made to match the color and shape of your existing teeth, so they blend in seamlessly.
  • Preservation of Bone Health : When you lose a tooth, the bone in that area can start to shrink (because it’s no longer being stimulated by the tooth root). Implants help preserve the surrounding bone.
  • Improved Function : Unlike dentures, which can slip and affect chewing, implants allow you to bite and chew as you would with natural teeth.
  • No Impact on Surrounding Teeth : With bridges, neighboring teeth often need to be filed down to support the prosthesis. Implants, on the other hand, stand alone without affecting the adjacent teeth.
  • Confidence Boost : Implants restore a full, natural smile and help with speaking and eating, so people often feel more confident after getting them!
  • Single Tooth Implants Single tooth implants can help restore both your smile and confidence. They are designed to feel and function like your natural teeth while preserving the health of your smile. They are strong, easy to maintain, and, best of all, permanent!
  • Implant Bridges Implant bridges are an excellent solution for replacing two or more adjacent teeth. They offer a long-lasting, natural-feeling, permanent restoration that functions just like your real teeth. In fact, implant bridges are 70 % stronger than removable partial dentures, so you can confidently enjoy your favorite foods like steak and apples without worry.
  • Full Arch Fixed Implants and Overdentures The next best thing to having your original teeth! Using two to six titanium implants, this option securely supports high-quality, custom-made teeth that not only restore your smile but also help preserve your facial structure. With regular brushing and flossing, maintaining your implants is simple. You can choose between permanent (All-on-4) or removable (overdenture) options, depending on your needs.
