Cosmetic Dentistry

At SRQ Dental, we offer a variety of different solutions to help you achieve a whiter, brighter smile. Whether you have always wanted a more attractive smile or are simply trying to restore your teeth’s natural beauty, we can help!

Veneers can correct chips, stains, discoloration, and tooth size or length issues, as well as create the illusion of straight teeth. Despite their transformative effects, they are a conservative solution for aesthetic smile makeovers.

Veneers are the perfect fusion of dental artistry and science, meticulously handcrafted and tailored to enhance your smile. These thin porcelain shells bond to the surface of your teeth, offering remarkable versatility while also having the ability to replicate the nuances of a natural tooth.

If you have missing or damaged teeth, a dental crown (for a single tooth) or bridge (for multiple teeth) can help restore both the function and beauty of your smile. Our experienced team specializes in restorative dentistry and can craft a stunning dental crown that looks and feels completely natural. Contact us or visit our office today to find out if a dental crown is the right solution for you!

For many people, straight teeth equals a beautiful smile. But did you know that a properly aligned bite is also essential for a healthier mouth? Crooked, overlapping, or rotated teeth can make daily cleaning more difficult and even accelerate the wear of your teeth. Aligning even a few out of place teeth can significantly improve your oral health.

And remember…straight teeth aren’t just for kids! In fact, adults often become some of the most committed orthodontic patients. It’s never too late to explore your options for achieving a bright, straight smile.

Professional teeth whitening is a simple, safe, and effective way to remove years of coffee, tea, wine, tobacco, and chocolate stains from your teeth.

There are countless teeth whitening products available at the drug store, and while we don’t discourage their use, you may want to explore the benefits of professional whitening for more effective and longer lasting results.

Dental bonding is a minimally invasive solution for restoring chipped, crooked, or discolored teeth. This procedure uses a tooth-colored filling material that is "bonded" to the tooth, enhancing its appearance.

Bonding is a more affordable cosmetic treatment option and can be completed in just one office visit. However, it’s important to note that dental bonding may be more prone to staining and chipping when compared to stronger porcelain alternatives, such as dental veneers or crowns.

Choosing the Right Cosmetic Dentist

It is important to take the time to find the right cosmetic dentist for you. You should feel comfortable with your doctor, while also taking their education and experience in the field into consideration. Cosmetic dental treatment has the ability to change your overall appearance in a dramatic and meaningful way. It is vital to ensure a comprehensive approach is taken to your care to help ensure a healthy, beautiful smile for years to come.

Will my Insurance Cover my Cosmetic Dental Procedure?

Most insurance plans do not offer coverage for elective procedures, including cosmetic dentistry. However, there are often portions of treatment that are still covered. After your consultation, our insurance coordinator will sit down with you and help navigate the different options available to help maximize your insurance benefits and minimize your out-of-pocket expenses.

Cost of Cosmetic Dental Treatment

The cost of cosmetic dental treatment varies greatly. If your dental insurance plan does not cover the cost of your treatment, we will work with you to explore various solutions.
