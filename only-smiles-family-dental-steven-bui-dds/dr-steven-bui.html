Dr. Bui’s Extensive Educational and Professional Background

Born and raised in Pasadena, Texas, Dr. Steven Bui embarked on his dental journey with a summa cum laude Bachelor of Science degree in Biology from the University of Houston. He then earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree from The University of Texas School of Dentistry-Houston in 2007. Following his doctorate, Dr. Bui completed a one-year residency program in Advanced Education in General Dentistry at the same institution, gaining formal training in comprehensive and cosmetic dentistry.

Advanced Training and Commitment to Comprehensive Dental Care

Dr. Bui&#x27;s dedication to offering the finest level of dental care is evident in his continual pursuit of education in new techniques and technologies. A member of prestigious associations like the American Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry, Dr. Bui is well-versed in the latest advancements in the field. His practice is marked by a commitment to innovative and comprehensive dental solutions.

Community Involvement and Personal Interests

Beyond the clinic, Dr. Bui is actively involved in community service. He works with the San Jose Clinic and has volunteered on mission trips to Guatemala, providing dental care to those with limited healthcare access. In his leisure time, Dr. Bui enjoys traveling, reading, outdoor activities, and spending time with family and friends. His dedication to the community is also recognized with the accolade of being voted Living Magazine&#x27;s Best Family Dentist in 2022 for the Bay Area.
