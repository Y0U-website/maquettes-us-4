What Are Dental Implants and How Do They Work?

Dental implants are small titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone where teeth are missing. They work by:

  • Serving as a sturdy base for replacement teeth (crowns, bridges, or dentures)
  • Integrating with the jawbone to form a secure and stable foundation
  • Preventing bone loss and maintaining facial structure integrity

What Are the Benefits of Choosing Dental Implants?

The benefits of dental implants include:

  • Improved appearance and confidence with a natural-looking tooth replacement
  • Enhanced chewing ability and speech clarity
  • Long-lasting solution with potential to last a lifetime with proper care
  • Preservation of jawbone health and prevention of adjacent teeth shifting

What Does the Dental Implant Process Involve?

The dental implant process typically involves:

  • An initial consultation and dental examination to determine suitability
  • The surgical placement of the implant into the jawbone
  • A healing period for osseointegration (integration of the implant with the bone)
  • Attaching a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture to the implant
