Expert Dental Implant Restoration Services

What Is Dental Implant Restoration and Why Is It Needed?

Dental implant restoration is the process of maintaining or replacing the prosthetic part of a dental implant. It&#x27;s needed when:

  • The dental crown, bridge, or denture attached to the implant becomes worn or damaged
  • Adjustments are required due to changes in the mouth or jawbone
  • To ensure the implant continues to function effectively and looks aesthetically pleasing

What Does Dental Implant Restoration Involve?

  • Replacing a damaged or worn crown, bridge, or denture
  • Adjusting the prosthetic for a better fit and comfort
  • Regular check-ups to monitor the condition of the implant and restoration

How to Care for Your Dental Implant Restoration?

Caring for your dental implant restoration involves:

  • Regular brushing and flossing to maintain oral hygiene
  • Routine dental check-ups and cleanings
  • Avoiding habits that could damage the restoration, such as chewing hard items
