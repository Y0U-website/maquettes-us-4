Dr. Paul Yi

Dr. Paul Yi, a graduate of the University of Maryland, Baltimore School of Dental Surgery (1985), is dedicated to the practice of general dentistry with an emphasis on maintaining excellent long-term oral health.

Dr. Yi firmly believes preventive dentistry offers patients the best path to lasting dental wellness. He dedicates significant time to patient education, helping individuals develop awareness of proper oral care techniques. For patients experiencing anxiety, Dr. Yi provides comfortable options, including laughing gas and anti-anxiety medications, to ensure positive long-term dental experiences.

Beyond his practice, Dr. Yi s true passion lies in dental mission work. Over the past twenty years, he has volunteered his expertise in Honduras, Costa Rica, Chile, Kenya, and Albania, bringing essential dental care to underserved communities. His compassion for those with dental needs continues to drive his commitment to mission work, which he plans to pursue as long as he feels called to serve.
