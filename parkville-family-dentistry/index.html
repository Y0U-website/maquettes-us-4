Regular dental exams are an integral part to maintaining a healthy oral cavity. During a routine exam, we will:

Examine teeth and gums for gingivitis as well as for signs of periodontal disease.

Dental bonding is used to repair chipped, discolored, or crooked teeth. The advantage of using bonding is that it comes in a variety of shades; thus, we can create fillings that match seamlessly with the rest of your teeth.

We always provide same-day care in cases of dental emergencies. Dental emergencies can include: chipping a tooth, suffering from a tooth infection, or damaging dental work. It is our utmost priority to relieve your pain, allay your fears, and prevent further dental problems as quickly and painlessly as possible.

Despite regular brushing, flossing, and checkups, sometimes your smile may need some help to last. Whether caused by injury or disease, an absent tooth or imperfect smile can easily be rectified with dental implants.
