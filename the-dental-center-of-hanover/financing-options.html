Payment & Financing Options

Many PPO dental insurance plans cover out-of-network specialists, often reimbursing a percentage of the procedure cost. The exact coverage depends on your individual plan, but our administrative team can help you understand what to expect before you begin treatment.

We understand that navigating healthcare expenses can be challenging, and we want to ensure transparency in our financial policies. We’re here to help!

Being an out-of-network provider gives you access to specialist-level care while still utilizing your dental insurance benefits. Importantly, they are not lost if when you receive treatment. Most PPO plans include out-of-network coverage, which typically reimburses you directly for a portion of your treatment costs after we submit your claim.

The advantages of out-of-network specialist care include access to advanced training and expertise that general dentists may not provide. Dr. Schiltz and Dr. Brooks bring specialized education and experience to complex cases involving dental implants, full mouth reconstruction, and cosmetic rehabilitation. This level of specialty care can lead to better long-term outcomes and fewer complications.

Our team will provide you with all the documentation you need to submit to your insurance company or file claims on your behalf. We’ll include detailed procedure codes, diagnostic information, and treatment notes that help your insurance company understand the necessity and complexity of your prosthodontic care. Many patients find that their out-of-network benefits cover a significant portion of their treatment, making specialist care more accessible than they initially expected.

At The Dental Center Collective, we believe that everyone deserves access to quality dental care. We offer flexible payment plan options to make dental implants more accessible for our patients. We work with multiple financing partners to provide interest-free payment plans for qualified patients, and our team will help you explore all available options and create a payment solution that fits comfortably within your budget.

Note that pricing varies based on individual case complexity, treatment needs, and your unique oral health situation. A comprehensive in-person consultation and examination are required to provide an accurate, personalized treatment plan and quote. During your consultation, we’ll discuss your specific needs and provide transparent pricing information tailored to your case.
