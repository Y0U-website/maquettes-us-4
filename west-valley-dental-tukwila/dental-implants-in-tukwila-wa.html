Explore a Variety of Implant Options

Dental implants provide several restoration possibilities, including:

  • Implant-Supported Crowns : Beautiful, lab-crafted crowns anchored to individual implants to restore your biting and chewing power. These replacement teeth will look and function like your natural teeth.
  • Implant-Supported Bridges : To replace several missing teeth in a row, we can attach a bridge to two or more implants, effectively filling the gap in your smile and enhancing your dining experience.
  • Implant-Retained Dentures : These dentures attach to dental implants using a snap-and-ball system, offering superior comfort and stability compared to traditional dentures.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures : Experience our most stable and high-performing dentures, which transfer chewing forces to your jawbone, mimicking the function of natural teeth.
  • Fixed Full-Arch Replacement Teeth : With just four implants per arch, we can secure a full row of non-removable, aesthetically pleasing new teeth, allowing you to eat whatever you like.
  • Mini Dental Implants : Ideal for patients with less bone density, these implants require no bone graft and have a quicker healing time due to their less invasive placement.

Implants Can Make a Huge Impact on Your Life

Dr. Hsue is passionate about using dental implants to enhance his patients’ lives. These small titanium posts integrate with your jawbone in a process known as osseointegration, forming a strong and lasting bond.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Dental implants are a long-term solution, often lasting for decades with proper care. While the average lifespan of a dental implant is about 10 to 15 years, many last much longer with regular dental checkups and good oral hygiene. Like any dental restoration, they can be compromised by gum disease and other oral health issues, so regular care is essential.
