What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a natural-looking, long-term solution for tooth replacement that surgically restores the form and function of your smile while helping prevent jawbone loss. These implants are composed of:

Together, these pieces make up a dental implant, which is often considered the gold standard of tooth replacement for patients with enough bone support.

  • A titanium post shaped like a natural tooth root.
  • A connecting abutment.
  • A custom restoration — like a crown, bridge, or denture — designed to blend seamlessly with the dimensions of our smile.

How Can Dental Implants Improve My Smile?

While dental crowns can provide protection for damaged teeth, implants are used to replace one tooth, multiple teeth, or an entire row of teeth. Dr. Nadine can discuss restorations designed to rehabilitate multiple missing teeth, such as implant-supported bridges and implant dentures.

  • Replace missing teeth and fill gaps within your smile
  • Strengthen your bite, giving you the ability to eat all your favorite foods again
  • Stimulate the regeneration of new bone and minimize jawbone loss
  • Prevent the shifting of surrounding teeth caused by gaps in your smile
  • Stop your facial shape from changing, which may occur when a missing tooth leads to a loss of volume in the jawbone structure
