About

Dr. Ralph B Montgomery founded Salt Lake Donated Dental Services (SLDDS) in 1990 and is a member of the Board of Directors and Chair Emeritus. He founded the clinic on a firm belief that a healthy smile should not be limited to only those with financial means. Dr. Montgomery graduated with his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Washington University School of Dentistry in 1954. He practiced dentistry for 36 years in Murray, Utah and served with the Clinical Research Associates of Provo, Utah where he participated for 21 years.

Board of Directors

Dr. Melvin Malmstrom (2002)

Dr. Malmstrom has served on the SLDDS Board since 2002 and acted as Board Chair since 2004. He graduated with his Doctor of Dental Surgery from University of the Pacific. He practiced dentistry for 15 years in West Jordan, Utah and acted as President of the Salt Lake District Dental Society in 2002. Dr. Malmstrom now pursues his interests in traveling, mathematics, and languages.

Nalyn R. Esplin (2006)

Nalyn Ricks Esplin joined the SLDDS Board in 2006. Nalyn is a retired Dental Business Manager. Currently, she is spending her time doing dental consulting, travelling, and studying to be a life/relationship coach. Nalyn is married to Steven Esplin; they have 3 daughters and live in Plano, TX.

Carol Coulter, Secretary (2007)

Retired Marketing Consultant, Maxcomm, Inc.

Carol Coulter has been serving on the SLDDS board since 2007. Carol worked for over 25 years in the training and development industry—designing, presenting and marketing workshops for business executives. In addition to working with SLDDS, she is actively engaged as a volunteer, board member and grant writer for Assistance League of Salt Lake City. Carol and her husband Dave are retired living in Salt Lake City. They have two children and five grandchildren and travel frequently.

Craig Wilson (2008)

Business Professor, LDS Business College

Craig Wilson, joined the SLDDC Board in 2008. Craig is a retired businessman and teacher. He currently is an adjunct professor at BYU in Provo and is involved with a number of different non-profit boards in the Salt Lake area. Craig is married to the former Cari Nydegger; they have five children and live in Holladay, UT.

R. Ted Stagg, Treasurer (2012)

Retired Certified Public Accountant, Stagg & Associates

Mitch Viele (2018)

Mitch Velie is corporate counsel at DigiCert, Inc., a premier provider of high-assurance digital certificates, including trusted SSL, private, managed PKI deployments, and device certificates for the emerging IoT market. Mitch’s work focuses on customer-facing contracts in EMEA and emerging markets. Dr. Burke Soffe (2018)

Dentist & Director of Clinical Education, Roseman University

Dr. Burke Soffe joined the SLDDS Board in 2018. Dr. Soffe is a general dentist and educator. He is a full-time faculty member at Roseman University of Health Sciences College of Dental Medicine and works at the SLDDS clinic one day a week. He is married to Lauren Soffe; they have three children and live in Cottonwood Heights, UT.

Stephanie Jensen (2019), Freelancer

Stephanie Jensen joined the board of directors for Salt Lake Donated Dental Services (SLDDS) in 2019. She graduated from Westminster College with degrees in English and Art, and was the executive director for SLDDS from 2003-2018. Stephanie has experience managing private dental practices (1992-1997), working internationally with grassroots community projects for the Peace Corps in Turkmenistan (1999-2001), and has developed and implemented HIPAA compliance plans for a local private insurance company (2002-2003). Stephanie and her family live in Chicago, Illinois where she does freelance work.

Lisa Harris (2019)

Lisa Harris is a Salt Lake native, she joined the SLDDC board in 2019. Lisa is a Dentrix Dental software trainer which takes her all over the country teaching dental offices to be more efficient and productive. She is interested in genealogy and is pursuing a degree in family history. Lisa and her husband Dave have 5 children and 8 grandchildren, a brown spotted dog and a pet black widow spider.

Carol Jent (2019)

Clinical Affairs, Ultradent Products, Inc.

Carol Jent is a dental hygiene graduate of the University of Alaska and later obtained an additional degree in Communications with an emphasis in healthcare from the University of Utah. She works for Ultradent Products, Inc. and is currently part of the Clinical Affairs Department which is involved in research, product development, and education. In her spare time, Carol’s favorite things include bicycling, reading, cooking, and exploring the world. Her favorite kinds of travel are humanitarian trips where she can use her dental hygiene education to give back.
