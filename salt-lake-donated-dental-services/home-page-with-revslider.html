Since 1990 SLDDS has been committed to creating a dental home for tens of thousands of individuals suffering from poor oral health. Open five days a week, our dental professionals provide care in a comprehensive and compassionate manner to hundreds of patients each month.

WHAT THEY ARE SAYING TESTIMONIALS

“What impresses me the most is the quality of service and clinical environment that they are able to provide with limited funding. The staff are welcoming, the equipment is excellently maintained, and the services are of the highest quality.”

“Salt Lake Donated Dental has greatly impacted the health and well-being of the Head Start community as we have regularly refer families to their clinic. We have had children in pain that are unable to be seen at another dental clinic due to finances, and Salt Lake Donated Dental has made them a priority. They provide quality care and do so with professionalism.”

Now with the financial and emotional help I’ve received here at Salt Lake Donated Dental Services my experience of life has improved so much. I now am joyful and confident, it is hard to express how truly grateful I am for everyone here at Donated Dental. Thank you for giving me a better quality of life!”

“I am a pleased and happy client of Donated Dental. I have been coming here for about 1 year now. I am very grateful for their services and their friendly atmosphere!
