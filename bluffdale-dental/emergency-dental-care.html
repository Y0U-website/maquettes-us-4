Emergency Dentist Near Bluffdale, UT

No one wants a dental emergency, but unfortunately, they do happen. In the case of a dental emergency, you deserve prompt, compassionate care. Whether you have knocked out a tooth, are in extreme pain or are missing a crown, our team is ready to address urgent pain, injuries or infections with the skill and kindness you need.

We work quickly to stabilize your situation, ease your discomfort and help you heal. Along with same-day emergency appointments during our office hours, we also see emergency patients after hours. For after-hours emergencies, please call our office.

What to Do in a Dental Emergency

If you have an emergency dental issue, call us as soon as possible. Depending on your emergency, we will provide instructions for immediately addressing your emergency until you can reach our office, such as controlling bleeding and preserving a knocked-out tooth.

When you arrive, we will quickly review your emergency, provide any anesthesia or sedation, and start the necessary emergency treatment.

After Your Dental Emergency

If your emergency requires follow-up care or a checkup, we will schedule accordingly. Our team will also give you the necessary post-care instructions, depending on the type of dental emergency you have.

FAQ

  • What qualifies as a dental emergency? A dental emergency usually includes severe tooth pain, knocked-out or broken teeth, uncontrolled bleeding or signs of infection like swelling. Prompt care is essential to relieve discomfort, prevent further damage and protect overall health.
  • What’s the best way to contact you for a dental emergency? It’s best to call our office for dental emergencies.
  • What should I do for a knocked-out tooth? To improve the chance of saving it, handle it carefully by the crown, place it in milk or saliva if possible, and seek dental care immediately.

Dental Implants

Often considered the gold standard in tooth replacement, dental implants are permanent posts placed into the jawbone and topped with custom crowns to replicate the look and function of natural teeth. Whether you’re missing one tooth or many, implants restore not only your bite but your quality of life.
