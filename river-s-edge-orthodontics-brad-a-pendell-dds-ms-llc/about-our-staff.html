Search this site Embedded Files Skip to main content Skip to navigation About Our Staff Dr. Brad Pendell Dr. Brad A. Pendell was born in Chillicothe, Ohio, in 1967 and grew up in Waverly, Ohio. Dr. Pendell graduated from Waverly High School in 1985 and attended The Ohio State University in Columbus earning a B.S. in Biology and a D.D.S. from the College of Dentistry in 1992.

From 1992 to 2000, Dr. Pendell served as dental officer for the United State Army. During the active duty service Brad was selected and completed an Orthodontic Residency at the University of Louisville in Kentucky. Dr. Pendell graduated the program with an M.S. in Oral Biology in 1998. After serving eight years in the US Army, Dr. Pendell entered orthodontic private practice in Chillicothe, Ohio, in 2000, where he currently practices.

While in the US Army, Dr. Pendell met and married his beautiful wife, Anne, at Camp Zama, Japan. God has since blessed the couple with three wonderful children, Adin, Abigail and Asher, and two yellow labs. Dr. Pendell and his family are heavily involved in their local church and committed to the Chillicothe and surrounding communities.

Dr. Pendell is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists, the American Dental Association, the Ohio Dental Association and the Central Ohio Better Business Bureau.
