What to Expect During Your Dental Exam

Regular dental cleanings and exams are the foundation of a healthy, beautiful smile and at Elite Smiles in West Covina, we’ve turned this essential routine care into a genuinely comfortable experience. Dental organizations generally recommend that most patients visit the dentist twice a year for routine exams and cleanings, and many PPO insurance plans cover these visits in full. Our comprehensive approach combines advanced diagnostic technology with a welcoming, patient-first environment, so your preventive visits are something you don’t have to dread.

While many dental practices in the San Gabriel Valley treat exams and cleanings as a quick, routine appointment, Dr. Manali Shah and our team use every visit as an opportunity to assess your complete oral health, catch potential concerns early, and build a personalized prevention plan around your unique needs and smile goals. Our practice is conveniently located in West Covina and serves patients throughout Covina, Baldwin Park, La Puente, Walnut, and the surrounding area.

Your comprehensive dental exam at Elite Smiles begins with a thorough assessment of your teeth, gums, jaw, and oral tissues using advanced digital imaging. We use digital X-rays which significantly reduce radiation exposure compared to traditional film along with 3D imaging technology to detect concerns that aren’t visible to the naked eye, often identifying potential issues months or even years before they become more serious or costly to treat.

During your exam, Dr. Manali Shah examines each tooth individually, checking for signs of decay, wear patterns, and structural integrity. We assess your gum health, screen for early signs of gum disease, and evaluate your bite alignment for any functional concerns. We also perform oral cancer screenings , examining your tongue, throat, cheeks, and other oral tissues for any abnormalities. Your comprehensive exam takes into account your full medical history, lifestyle factors, and aesthetic goals so every part of your oral health gets the attention it deserves.

Early Detection Saves Smiles and Money

Routine dental exams and cleanings are one of the most cost-effective investments you can make in your oral health. Small cavities caught early can often be treated with a conservative filling , helping you avoid the need for a crown or root canal down the road. Early-stage gum disease responds well to non-surgical treatment, while advanced periodontitis can require significantly more extensive and costly procedures.

Regular cleanings also remove bacterial buildup that contributes to both tooth decay and broader health concerns research continues to link untreated gum disease and oral bacteria to an increased risk of heart disease, diabetes complications, stroke, and other inflammatory conditions.

At Elite Smiles , we accept many PPO insurance plans, which often cover routine exams and cleanings twice a year at no cost to you. We also offer flexible financing options, including an in-house membership plan, to help make preventive care accessible for every budget including patients without insurance.

Building Long Term Oral Health

Regular exams and cleanings typically happen every six months, though some patients benefit from more frequent visits depending on their individual risk factors. We work with you to determine the right schedule, taking into account your dental history, current oral health, and commitment to home care.

Between visits, we support your oral health journey with educational resources and personalized recommendations. Whether you’re simply maintaining a healthy smile or working toward specific goals like Invisalign or veneers, preventive care provides the foundation for lasting results.

Our team tracks your progress over time, celebrating improvements and adjusting our approach as needed. We believe in giving patients a real understanding of their oral health explaining findings clearly and involving you in every treatment decision.

How Can We Help For You?

A professional dental cleaning removes plaque and tartar buildup that brushing and flossing alone cannot eliminate. Even with excellent home care, hard mineral deposits form below the gumline over time and can lead to gum disease, tooth decay, and eventually tooth loss. Regular cleanings by Dr. Manali Shah and our hygiene team at Elite Smiles in West Covina help prevent serious oral health problems and keep your smile bright and healthy.

Most patients benefit from professional dental cleanings every six months, which is the standard recommended by dental organizations nationwide. However, patients with gum disease, diabetes, or other risk factors may need more frequent cleanings-every three or four months. Dr. Manali Shah will assess your individual needs and create a personalized cleaning schedule to maintain optimal oral health.

A regular cleaning removes plaque and tartar from above the gumline and is a preventive procedure for patients with healthy gums. A deep cleaning, also called scaling and root planing, treats gum disease by removing buildup below the gumline and smoothing the tooth root. If Dr. Manali Shah detects early signs of gum disease during your exam at Elite Smiles, a deep cleaning may be recommended to prevent the condition from worsening.

Professional cleanings remove surface stains and tartar, which can make your teeth appear brighter and cleaner. However, they are not a whitening treatment. If you want a more dramatic whitening result, Dr. Manali Shah can discuss professional teeth whitening options available at Elite Smiles in West Covina. Our team can help you achieve a brighter, more confident smile.
