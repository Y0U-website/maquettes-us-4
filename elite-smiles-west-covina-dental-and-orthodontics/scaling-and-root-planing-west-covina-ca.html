What Is Scaling and Root Planing?

Scaling and root planing is a non-surgical periodontal procedure designed to treat gum disease by thoroughly cleaning below the gumline. Unlike a standard cleaning, it targets the deeper pockets of bacteria, plaque, and hardened tartar that form around the roots of the teeth when gum disease is present.

Scaling

Removes bacterial buildup from tooth surfaces and the pockets that form between teeth and gums.

Root Planing

Smooths root surfaces to eliminate rough areas where bacteria tend to accumulate, making it harder for new buildup to form. Together, these two steps give your gum tissue the best chance to heal and reattach to your teeth.

Who Needs Scaling and Root Planing in West Covina?

Not every patient with gum sensitivity will need a deep cleaning. This treatment is typically recommended when periodontitis has progressed beyond gingivitis. During your visit, Dr. Manali Shah will conduct a thorough evaluation to determine whether scaling and root planing or a standard cleaning is the right fit for you.

Common signs you may need this treatment include:

Recognizing these signs early gives you the best chance of treating gum disease before it progresses to bone loss or tooth loss.

  • Gum inflammation
  • Visible recession
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Shifting teeth
  • Deep periodontal pockets
