What Is Bone Grafting and Why Is It Needed?

Bone grafting is a procedure that restores or rebuilds areas of the jaw where bone density or volume has been lost. When a tooth is extracted or lost, the surrounding bone gradually begins to resorb without the stimulation a tooth root normally provides. This bone loss can leave the jaw too narrow or shallow to securely support an implant . Research published through the National Institutes of Health has shown that simultaneous bone augmentation with implant placement achieves a high clinical success rate, reinforcing that bone grafting is a well-established, predictable part of implant care.

We may recommend bone grafting if you’ve had a tooth extracted, experienced bone loss from gum disease, or gone an extended period with a missing tooth. In some cases, a sinus lift, a specialized form of bone augmentation for the upper jaw, may also be appropriate. Dr. Manali Shah always takes the most conservative approach possible, preserving as much of your natural bone structure as she can while still building the strong foundation your implant will need.

What to Expect During the Bone Grafting Process

The bone grafting process is typically straightforward, though the exact timeline depends on how much grafting is needed and how your jaw heals. During your initial consultation, Dr. Manali Shah or our periodontist will perform a comprehensive examination, review your digital X-rays and 3D imaging, discuss your oral health goals, and determine whether bone grafting is the best solution to prepare your jaw for long-term success. We take the time to answer your questions and ensure you fully understand each stage of treatment before moving forward.

Once your treatment plan is finalized, the grafting procedure is performed with your comfort as our priority. Depending on your needs, we may use advanced bone grafting materials to rebuild areas where the jawbone has deteriorated due to tooth loss, gum disease, or trauma. The graft acts as a foundation that encourages your body’s natural bone regeneration process, helping create the strength and volume needed to support future dental implants.

Recovery and Healing

After grafting, the treated area needs time to heal and fully integrate before an implant can be placed a process that typically takes a few months, depending on the amount of bone grafted and your individual healing response.

Throughout recovery, we provide clear post-operative instructions and remain available to answer any questions or concerns along the way. Many patients find the procedure far more comfortable than expected, especially with our sedation options available. Once healing is confirmed, we’ll coordinate the next phase of your treatment whether that’s a single-tooth implant or part of a larger full-mouth restoration plan.
