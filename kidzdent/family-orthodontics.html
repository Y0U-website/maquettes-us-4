Family Orthodontic Care for All Ages

When Should Patients Start Braces?

There are no age limits on exceptional orthodontic care. In fact according to the most recent surveys, over 25 % of today’s orthodontic patients are over the age of 21 . Patients should receive their first consultation by the age of seven. However, adults can benefit from orthodontics as well. Adults are finally getting the straight, self-confident smiles and health benefits they wished they’d received as children, without having to sacrifice the appearance of their smile.

Who Needs Braces?

Orthodontics are recommended for patients experiencing mild to severe dental alignment problems, including gaps between teeth, crowded teeth, or bite issues that include cross-bite, over and under-bite, or open bite.

How Much Do Braces Cost?

The price of braces varies, depending on the severity of dental issues and if multiple appliances are necessary to achieve healthy alignment. Old Bridge Orthodontics provides free orthodontic consultations for patients of all ages, as well as complimentary insurance benefit checks to determine coverage. We work with many insurance providers and offer affordable in-house payment plans to manage the cost of braces.

Early Orthodontics (Phase I Care) for Children

When children experience dental alignment issues with their smile, it’s important to address them proactively. Family Orthodontics at KidZdent offers dedicated care to children ages seven and up, correcting skeletal concerns and providing their best chance for a beautiful smile as adults. If necessary, we can extract teeth that impede proper alignment, and then pair children with the appropriate appliances. We may use palate expanders, headgear, and metal braces in their treatment and will coordinate with one of our pediatric dentists to ensure cohesive treatment.

Full Family Orthodontic Treatment

Teenagers and adults can both receive full braces to align their teeth and jaw for optimal function and beauty. We utilize materials that support efficient movement while minimizing aesthetic impact to improve comfort throughout the length of treatment.

We offer cosmetic components for traditional braces, such as tooth-colored brackets and wires , which allow the orthodontic appliance to blend in with your teeth.

Invisalign Clear Braces for Teens and Adults

Invisalign aligners can be an alternative to full metal braces , depending on your orthodontic needs. These removable braces allow you to maintain a normal lifestyle and are nearly invisible, so you too can achieve a straight smile with ease and comfort. Although not right for all patients, those who have minor to moderate alignment issues are best suited to receive this type of treatment.

Once patients choose invisible trays as the solution for their needs, our dentists take impressions of your smile and send them to the Invisalign lab. Invisalign fabricates and ships your aligners. You will return to Family Orthodontics at KidZdent to receive your aligners along with instructions on when to change them as you progress through your treatment.

Ceramic or Clear Bracket Braces

For patients in need of complex care, but who wish to avoid silver metal orthodontics , our Old Bridge Family Orthodontists here at KidZdent offer ceramic, clear, or tooth-colored bracket braces. These appliances are made from high-quality materials and are color-matched to the shade of your teeth to leave you with a seamless appearance. They are also stain-resistant and will not discolor over time.

Ceramic or clear bracket braces are highly recommended by our Old Bridge dentists for patients who have a difficult time managing removable aligners. These discreet braces still use the same framework as traditional metal wire orthodontics to provide you with effective treatment, but offer an improved appearance for our adult patients.
