Dr. Cavan Brunsden DMD, Pediatric Dentist Dr. Cavan Brunsden DMD, Pediatric Dentist was formerly an elementary school teacher and assistant principal. He completed his dental degree at Rutgers School of Dental Medicine where he graduated as a member of OKU, the national dental honor society. After a two-year pediatric dental residency at Albert Einstein/Montefiore Medical Center in New York, Dr. Cavan Brunsden joined our community as a pediatric dentist in 1981. He founded KidZdent with his wife and partner, Dr. Nancy Villa, in 1985.

As the state chairman of the New Jersey Dental Association, Give Kids A Smile Program , He is a strong advocate for children’s healthcare education throughout the state as well as right here in our own community. Dr. Brunsden DMD, Pediatric Dentist is dedicated to providing an environment of positive dental health experiences for children – and a lifetime of smiles.

Dr. Brunsden DMD, Pediatric Dentist is a Certified Pediatric Dentist who performs all phases of comprehensive, preventive, restorative and cosmetic dentistry for children. In addition, he has extensive experience with labial frenectomy , lingual frenectomy , sedation dentistry , early interceptive orthodontics , and special needs patient care .

Get to Know Dr. Cavan Brunsden DMD, Pediatric Dentist

When they opened the Brunsden-Villa Children’s Dental Care Center at Rutgers University, we saw a dream realized. A dream to influence the dental education of future children’s dentists so that they could impact children’s lives like we do. We are so proud of the role that we play in guiding the lives of our young patients and we are very grateful to you for the trust and confidence that you place in us to do the right thing for your child.

Dr. Brunsden DMD, Pediatric Dentist at KidZdent... creating smiles... changing lives.

Dr. Nancy Villa DMD, MSD Orthodontist Dr. Nancy Villa DMD, MSD Orthodontist grew up in Jersey City and became interested in the art of orthodontics while working as a dental assistant. She attended Rutgers University and received a bachelor’s degree in science. Next, she received her doctorate of medical dentistry at Rutgers School of Dental Medicine.

Following dental school, Dr. Villa DMD, MSD Orthodontist completed a two-year postgraduate program to become a specialist in orthodontics at Montefiore Medical Center and Albert Einstein College of Medicine in New York.

Dr. Nancy Villa DMD, MSD Orthodontist is a trustee for the New Jersey Dental Association and past president of the Middlesex County Dental Society. She provides exceptional orthodontic care for adults and children .

Get to Know Dr. Nancy Villa

As a child, I was embarrassed to smile because of my severely crowded, overlapped teeth. Both of my older brothers also had terribly crooked teeth. In those days, dental insurance was rare and my dad needed to get a second job to help pay for our braces. He always said it was the best investment he ever made!

During high school, I was very lucky to have been offered a part time job with an orthodontist, most likely because of my great smile. Since I had good hand eye coordination skills, working as an assistant was an absolute pleasure. In addition, I loved helping patients achieve a smile they were proud of.

Dr. Brunsden DMD, Pediatric Dentist and I were both lucky to have been introduced to one another at a dental society, at the same time he was ready to add an orthodontic department in his busy pediatric dental office. We were married the following year and have successfully managed to live and work together peacefully, most of the time…haha!

Our orthodontic office team is dedicated to providing excellent quality treatment for both children and adults in a fun, caring environment. We participate with a wonderful program called Donated Orthodontics Services where we provide free braces for children in need. All of us strive to help our patients learn to love going to the dentist. Our favorite compliment to receive from patients is that they have chosen dentistry as a career because of the positive experience they enjoyed in our office!

Dr. Villa DMD, MSD Orthodontist at KidZdent... creating beautiful & healthy smiles for happy patients!

Dr. Hillary Khodari DMD, Pediatric Dentist Dr. Hillary DMD, Pediatric Dentist graduated from Tufts University and attended University of Pennsylvania for Dental School. She completed a residency at Northwell Health: Cohen Children’s Medical Center at Long Island Jewish Medical Center. In residency, she was a Chief Resident and won the Resident of the Year Award. She is active in the Alpha Omega International Dental Fraternity, American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry and the American Dental Association.

Dr. Hillary DMD, Pediatric Dentist grew up as a KidZdent patient, who also worked in our office as a sterilization lab assistant during her undergraduate education years. Both of her parents are dentists, so she has had a love for the profession from a very early age. She loves to travel, and spent a semester studying in Argentina. Dr. Hillary DMD, Pediatric Dentist also played on the NCAA Division III Women’s Tennis Team at Tufts University. She is fluent in Spanish, loves reading, playing all kinds of board games, and spending quality time with family and friends.

Dr. Hillary DMD, Pediatric Dentist is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist who performs all phases of comprehensive, preventive, restorative and cosmetic dentistry for children. In addition, she also has extensive experience with sedation dentistry , early interceptive orthodontics , and special needs patient care .

In addition, she has extensive experience with providing pediatric aesthetic dentistry, oral health care, oral surgery procedures, frenectomy/frenuloplasty via laser surgery and special needs patient care. Her goal is to create a dental home which can address pediatric needs ranging from dental and craniofacial anomalies with a focus on oral airway health and whole-body well-being. During her free time she likes to dedicate herself to home renovating projects, traveling and being that she is a big foodie, you can catch her at the newest NYC restaurants.

Dr. Ilona Aleksandrovich DDS, Pediatric Dentist Dr. Ilona Aleksandrovich is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist who completed her undergraduate education at NYIT. She continued on to pursue a career in dentistry and obtained her Doctorate in Dental Surgery from New York University College of Dentistry in 2019. After graduating dental school, she completed a General Practice Residency at Staten Island University Hospital, where she then stayed at to complete a 2 year pediatric speciality program, serving as chief resident in her final year.

Dr. Ilona is an active member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Dental Association and the New York State Dental Association. Her goal is to provide the utmost quality of care to her patients. She believes that building a trustworthy relationship between herself, the patient and the parent is the first step of achieving a healthy and life long smile.

In her spare time Dr. Ilona enjoys spending quality time with her family and with her golden-doodle Milo. She enjoys traveling, and exploring different see types of cuisines.

Dr. Mega Manchanda DDS, Pediatric Dentist Dr. Mega DDS is a Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist who graduated college from SUNY Stony Brook, where she double majored in Biology and Anthropology. Dr. Mega obtained her doctorate from New York University College of Dentistry, where she served as President of the American Dental Association and learned to love organized dentistry and the importance of giving back to the community through countless outreach missions, both locally in surrounding boroughs and internationally in Nepal.

Continuing her education in the heart of Brooklyn, Dr. Mega then completed a one-year General Practice Residency, where she continued her passion for organized dentistry with the Second District Dental Society and volunteering for programs such as Give Kids a Smile. Knowing that she had to follow her passion for pediatrics, she took the plunge moving to Philadelphia, to train in Pediatric Residency at Temple University Hospital, where she served as class social chair.

Dr. Mega’s philosophy is if ‘Children have good dental experiences and learn the right habits early only they will never have bad experiences going to the dentist when they are older.’ Dr. Mega strives to give every child a wonderful experience, loves restorative dentistry, oral conscious sedation, and is all about putting the patient first! In her free time, Dr. Mega enjoys traveling with her husband, volunteering for local organizations, and learning authentic Indian recipes and some Tik Tok Dances too!

Additionally, although born in Germany, Dr. Mega is fluent in Hindi and Punjabi.

Dr. Vrinda Tarneja DDS, Pediatric Dentist Dr. Vrinda Tarneja is a Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist. She completed her Pediatric Dentistry training at Boston Children’s Hospital at Harvard University. Dr. Tarneja graduated summa cum laude with a Bachelor of Science from the University at Buffalo, where she also earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree and received the Pediatric and Community Dentistry Award upon graduation.

Throughout her career, Dr. Tarneja has demonstrated a passion for working with children, engaging in activities such as tutoring middle-school students and volunteering with organizations like Give Kids a Smile Day and the Special Olympics. She is an active member of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. Dr. Tarneja believes in educating both parents and children about oral health care to promote effective practices at home and tailors her approach to meet the unique needs of each child.

In her personal time, Dr. Tarneja enjoys traveling, dancing, spending time with her family, and exploring new restaurants. She is enthusiastic about serving and connecting with the local community.
