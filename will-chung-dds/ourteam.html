Our Team

We are very pleased to introduce Will Eunku Chung DDS, MSD!!

Dr. Will Eunku Chung earned his doctor of dental surgery degree from prestigious Seoul National University College of Dentistry. After a few years of working as a general practitioner, he decided to focus on the subject of his interest; Restorative, Implant and Prosthetic dentistry. He came to Seattle, and completed his specialty certificate in prosthodontics and master of science in dentistry degree at the University of Washington Graduate Prosthodontics Program. During more than 3 years of his specialty training, he was trained for dental implants both surgically and prosthetically.

He was a full time assistant professor at the University of Texas Houston Dental Branch until early 2009. He led the team of specialists/professors including an orthodontist, periodontist, and oral surgeon to treat patients with congenital deformities such as ectodermal dysplasia or incontinentia pigmenti. Currently, he serves as an affiliate assistant professor to the University of Washington Graduate Prosthodontic program.

He has spoken on multiple subjects in implant and prosthetic dentistry nationally and internationally to dental professionals and students. He is also serving as an outside expert reviewer for the implant journal, International Journal of Oral and Maxillofacial Implants.

Dr. Chung is also a compassionate dentist. He believes that what he enjoys today in his life are gifts from the community and loves to give back. He has been volunteering with Medical Teams International treating referred patients for surgical extractions over the weekend time. He was directly involved in denture projects for tent city people as well.

In his free time, he enjoys spending time with his family; wife, Sooyoung, and two sons, James Wooje and Keith Woojin.

Growing up in Taiwan from a large family of five siblings, Ann learned the essence of caring and nurturing from her late mother who was always kind and loving. Dr. Liou’s sense of gentle touch and the sensitivity to other’s feelings became an instinct early in her life.

After finishing dental school in Taiwan in 1981, she continued her dental training at the University of Southern California where she received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 1984. She then practiced four years as a general dentist in Los Angeles and San Diego.

During this clinical practice, Dr. Liou realized that there were patients with very complex dental issues that she was not comfortable treating. She realized in order to help patients with complicated conditions she had to go back to school again. Becoming a prosthodontist seemed to be the way to achieve her goal. She went back to school for a third time, this time to the University of Washington where she received a Master of Science in Dentistry degree in prosthodontics in 1991.

After finishing her specialty training, Dr. Liou was asked to teach at the school and became a full time assistant professor at the University of Washington.

As an avid learner, Dr. Liou enjoys taking lessons on cooking, golfing, opera singing and learning different languages. Her passion in life is to continue learning. She also enjoys spending time with friends and family.
