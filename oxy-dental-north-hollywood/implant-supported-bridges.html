Why Implant Supported Bridges Excel Over Traditional Options

These bridges deliver superior strength and longevity by integrating directly with your jawbone through titanium implants. They prevent bone loss in the areas of missing teeth, maintain proper facial structure, and distribute chewing forces evenly to protect surrounding teeth and gums. Patients enjoy full biting power, no slipping or discomfort, and easier hygiene since the bridge is fixed—no removable parts. With proper care, implant supported bridges can last 15+ years or more, making them a reliable long-term investment.

Who Is a Candidate for This Treatment?

If you have multiple consecutive missing teeth due to decay, injury, extraction, or other issues, you’re likely an excellent candidate. Good overall health, sufficient jawbone density, and healthy gums are ideal, though bone grafting can often make treatment possible even in challenging cases. During a complimentary consultation at OxyDental Noho, we’ll use detailed 3D imaging to evaluate your bone volume, bite alignment, and goals—ensuring this solution fits your needs and lifestyle perfectly.

The Implant Supported Bridge Procedure Step by Step

The process is precise and patient-focused. We begin with comprehensive planning and scans to determine optimal implant placement and bridge design. In a surgical appointment (often with sedation for comfort), the implants are placed into the jawbone. After a healing period of 3–6 months for osseointegration, we attach custom abutments and a porcelain bridge crafted to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and fit. Many patients receive a temporary bridge during healing for immediate function and confidence.

What Recovery Looks Like

Recovery is typically smooth and manageable. Most patients experience mild swelling or discomfort for a few days, controlled with rest, ice, and over-the-counter or prescribed medication. A soft-food diet supports initial healing, with most returning to normal activities quickly. Regular follow-ups monitor integration and bridge fit. Once fully healed, the final bridge is placed for a durable, natural-feeling restoration.

Daily Benefits That Transform Your Life

An implant supported bridge lets you chew comfortably, speak clearly, and smile with confidence—no gaps, no shifting teeth, no dietary restrictions. It preserves jawbone health, prevents facial collapse, and simplifies oral hygiene (brush and floss as you would natural teeth). Patients often report renewed self-esteem, easier social interactions, and the freedom to enjoy life without worrying about their smile.
