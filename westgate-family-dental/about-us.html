Jesslyn

Jesslyn is our full-time office manager. She started her dental career as a dental assistant in 1990. She went on to graduate from Owens College in 1994 as a Dental Hygienist. She joined Westgate Family Dental in 2008 as a hygienist but transitioned to become the office manager after obtaining a Bachelor’s degree in Health Care Administration from Lourdes University.

Dana

Dana

Dana is a full- time hygienist. She graduated from Owens Community College in 1983. She is also an EFDA which means she has a license to place fillings. In 1998, She went to U of M to learn how to administer local anesthetic. She was one of the first hygienists in the state of Ohio to receive this licensure. She has been with Dr. Beitelschees since January 2023, but they have known each other for over 30 years.
