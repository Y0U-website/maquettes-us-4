Who We Are

Dr. Irene Hermo, a DDS based in Northern California, has dedicated over 20 years of her professional life to providing compassionate and gentle oral healthcare to patients in Vacaville, Auburn, Roseville, and Carmichael.

After graduating from the University of the East - College of Dentistry in 1985, Dr. Hermo successfully passed the U.S. Western Regional Dental Board. She then embarked on her career as an Associate Dentist, serving communities in Auburn, Roseville, Carmichael, Vacaville, American Canyon, and Daly City. As a proud member of the ADA, CDA, and SDDS, Dr. Irene Hermo remains committed to delivering exceptional dental care to her patients.

“My goal is to provide my dental services to patients by understanding their needs, making it affordable, and bringing a comfortable environment at the same time. I believe that we all deserve a confident smile because it is key to everything in our lives.”

Tooth Spa Dentistry, offering dental care services in Lincoln, California, presents a warm, soothing, and inviting atmosphere where patients can enjoy a one-of-a-kind, spa-like experience alongside their dental treatments. Our team of professionals is fully trained and committed to delivering exceptional dental care.
