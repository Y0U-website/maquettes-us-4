Why Consider Orthodontic Treatment?

Orthodontics does more than improve appearance. Straight, properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, which lowers your risk of cavities and gum disease. Correcting a bad bite can also reduce uneven wear, jaw strain, and difficulty chewing. For many Rogers Park families, treatment is an investment in long-term dental health, not just aesthetics.

Who Is a Good Candidate?

Children, teens, and adults can all benefit from orthodontic care. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have their first orthodontic evaluation by around age 7, when early issues can be spotted. That said, it’s never too late a growing number of adults choose treatment to fix long-standing crowding or spacing.

What Are My Braces and Aligner Options?

Modern orthodontics offers more choices than ever:

During your visit, we’ll examine your teeth and bite and discuss which option best fits your goals, lifestyle, and budget. You can learn more about our dental services before you come in.

  • Traditional metal braces: Reliable and effective for a wide range of alignment issues, including complex cases.
  • Clear or ceramic braces: Work like metal braces but use tooth-colored brackets that are less noticeable.
  • Invisalign clear aligners: A series of removable, nearly invisible trays that gradually move teeth — popular with adults and older teens.

Braces vs. Invisalign: Which Is Better?

Neither is universally better the right choice depends on your case. Braces are fixed in place and handle complex movements well. Invisalign is removable, making eating and cleaning easier, and is hard to notice. Mild to moderate crowding or spacing often responds well to clear aligners, while more involved bite problems may be better suited to braces.

How Long Does Orthodontic Treatment Take?

Most treatment takes somewhere between 12 and 24 months, though simple cases can finish sooner and complex ones may take longer. Wearing aligners or following care instructions consistently has a big impact on staying on schedule. We’ll give you a personalized timeline at your consultation.

Will Treatment Be Uncomfortable?

It’s normal to feel some pressure or soreness for a few days after braces are placed or when you switch to a new aligner tray. This usually eases quickly and means your teeth are moving as planned. Over-the-counter pain relief and soft foods help during these short adjustment periods.

Caring for Your Teeth During Treatment

  • Brush after meals: Food can get trapped around brackets and wires, so thorough cleaning matters.
  • Floss daily: Use floss threaders or a water flosser around braces; remove aligners before cleaning.
  • Protect your aligners: Store them in their case and avoid hot drinks while wearing them.
  • Keep regular checkups: Routine cleanings and exams keep your teeth healthy throughout treatment.

Do you offer orthodontics for adults near Rogers Park?

Yes. Many of our orthodontic patients are adults who want to straighten their teeth discreetly with clear aligners or ceramic braces. There’s no upper age limit for treatment as long as your teeth and gums are healthy.

How much does orthodontic treatment cost?

Cost depends on the type of treatment and the complexity of your case. Many plans offer monthly payment options, and some dental insurance includes orthodontic benefits. We’ll review pricing and any coverage clearly before you begin.

Is Invisalign as effective as braces?

For many mild to moderate cases, Invisalign produces excellent results comparable to braces. More complex bite or alignment issues are sometimes better treated with braces. A consultation is the best way to know which is right for you.
