Invisalign & Braces in Chicago & Skokie, IL

Straighten Your Teeth with Invisalign or Braces

Vista Dental in Chicago and Skokie offers orthodontic treatment for adults, teens, and adolescents seeking a straighter, healthier smile. Whether you prefer the discreet convenience of Invisalign clear aligners or the proven effectiveness of traditional braces, our orthodontic team will evaluate your individual needs and recommend the most appropriate treatment for your desired results.

Traditional Metal Braces

Our traditional metal braces use modern lightweight alloys and heat-activated wires for a more comfortable orthodontic experience than braces of the past. Metal braces are highly effective for correcting complex alignment issues including severe crowding, significant bite problems, and rotated teeth. We offer braces for adults, teens, and adolescents.

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Invisalign is the clear, convenient, and removable way to straighten your teeth without metal brackets or wires. Each set of custom-designed clear aligners is worn for approximately two weeks before being replaced with the next set in the series, gradually moving your teeth into their ideal position. Invisalign aligners are virtually invisible, removable for eating and brushing, and comfortable to wear throughout your daily activities.

Invisalign treatment at Vista Dental starts at $3,900 and includes all custom aligners, progress check-ups, and retainers. Treatment duration varies by case, typically ranging from 6 to 18 months.

Orthodontics FAQ

Invisalign treatment at Vista Dental starts at $3,900. The final cost depends on the complexity of your case and treatment duration. Many PPO dental insurance plans provide orthodontic coverage that can significantly reduce your out-of-pocket cost. We also offer CareCredit and in-house financing with monthly payment options.

Invisalign is highly effective for most orthodontic cases including crowding, gaps, overbites, underbites, and crossbites. For severe or complex cases, traditional braces may be recommended. During your free orthodontic consultation at Vista Dental, our dentist will evaluate your specific needs and recommend the best option.

Most Invisalign treatments at Vista Dental take between 6 and 18 months, depending on the complexity of your case. Mild cases may be completed in as few as 6 months, while more complex alignment issues may take 12-18 months. You will see gradual improvement throughout your treatment.
