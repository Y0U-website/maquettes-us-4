The practice

I provide comprehensive dental services for children, adults, and senior dental patients in the Tupelo, MS area as well as the greater Northern Mississippi region:

I am the only Bioesthetic Dentist in North Mississippi. I offer all new patients an initial complimentary consultation so you can better understand how Bioesthetics may help you on your journey to improved dental health and wellness. Bioesthetic Principles are a key part of my practice and as a patient-focused, health-centered Dentist, I continually strive to operate from a perspective of whole…

I have been trained extensively in minimally invasive, holistic methods such as the VIVOS® System and MAGO (Maxillary Anterior Guided Orthotic) Splint Therapy. I am highly experienced in treating TMJ Disorder and Facial Pain, Sleep Breathing Disorders, Airway Health Issues, Myofunctional Therapy including Tongue-Tie & Lip-Tie Release, and other complex dental and oral health conditions.

I specialize in the diagnosis and treatment of complex TMJ patient cases involving degenerating chewing systems using Bioesthetic Principles, which focuses on complete dental restorative treatment for patients who have (TMJ) temporomandibular joint issues. I am a Level 4 Graduate having completed all 4 levels of training at The OBI Foundation for Bioesthetic Dentistry.

If you believe you have TMJ disorder, and you’re searching for relief from your pain and symptoms, I urge you to consider my advanced treatment option called “MAGO Therapy.” MAGO stands for Maxillary Anterior Guided Orthotic and is a non-surgical, non-invasive therapeutic approach, and is one of the most effective methods for providing long-term TMD relief.
