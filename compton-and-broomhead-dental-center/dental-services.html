Dental Services

We’re well-versed and skilled in both routine and specialty services so that we can keep you informed about procedures and treat all of your dentistry needs in our Munster office.

Life is better when you feel confident about your hygiene and your smile. From proactive routine cleanings to aesthetic treatments to problem-solving procedures, Compton and Broomhead Dental Care professionals are available to assess, address, and protect your smile while always prioritizing your comfort.

Our Full List of Dental Services:

See below for a list of services we offer and read more about the most common treatments and procedures. If you’re looking for a service that isn’t listed, feel free to drop us a line and we’ll see how we can help you.

Initial Oral Examinations

Phenomenal hygiene starts here. Come in for an initial oral examination and we’ll address your unique situation, offering diagnosis and treatment recommendations. After a visual examination, charting, and periodontal probing, we’ll take a panoramic x-ray of your anterior (front) and posterior (back) teeth so we can give you a 360-degree assessment and create a customized action plan together.

General Dentistry

Keep your pearly whites in perfect condition. From routine cleanings to occasional fillings, our general dentistry services are all about proactive care. Each time you recline in one of our chairs, we ensure you’re comfortable and at ease. We even have a massage therapist on-site to offer free hand massages during your cleanings. It’s our way of thanking you for your commitment to personal care, and a special reminder that visits to the dentist can be relaxing and rewarding.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Your smile is as unique to you as the sound of your laughter or the way you walk, but when you feel insecure about your teeth’s imperfections, it can negatively affect the quality of your life. From teeth whitening to veneers to Invisalign, we can troubleshoot any and all appearance-related issues you may have, from discoloration to crookedness. We’re more than happy to help you make peace with your smile and share more of your signature grins with the world.

Dentures and Implants

Restore your smile to its glory days so you can flash it with pure confidence. We believe all of our patients deserve to love their smiles. However, missing teeth can make it hard to enjoy routine behaviors, like eating, and create self-consciousness in social situations. Our implants and specialty denture services are a stellar solution for anyone suffering from mental or physical pain caused by missing teeth. Give us a call or come on into the office for a consultation and find out how we can get to the…root of the problem.

Emergency Dental Services

Accidents happen. Teeth break and teeth ache. Our dental professionals are understanding, knowledgeable, and avid problem solvers, ready to resolve issues through emergency dental solutions. Whether you’re suffering from a toothache or are in need of root canal therapy, we’ll provide the highest quality care in the most convenient and comfortable fashion, soothing your pain so that you can get back to your life and reclaim your well-being.
