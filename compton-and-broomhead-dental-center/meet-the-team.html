Meet the Team

Our practice has been caring for patients since 1957, and our team shares a passion for lifelong learning.

Voted #1 Best Dentist in Best of Region contest nine-years in a row

We’re here to improve the quality of your life. We know that all people have a need to feel supported when making decisions about their health, a need to feel heard and seen, and overall, a need to simply feel good about themselves. The Compton and Broomhead team is dedicated to serving patients through the highest quality care and motivated by a genuine desire to optimize your well-being. We advocate for expert service in a comfortable and convenient setting, always aiming to put you at ease. It’s our mission to make caring for your oral health a seamless and positive experience.

Our dentistry team has over 51-years of combined dental experience between them and a shared passion for lifelong learning. In fact, our staff continues to take higher education courses, studying the latest in technological advances and techniques to stay abreast of new developments in the industry and provide the best possible care to our patients.

Meet Dr. Compton

Dr. Eric is a dentist who offers a unique combination of artistic skill coupled with advanced scientific techniques. He has a special talent and vision for cosmetic dentistry. Dr. Eric has spent many hours studying the relationship between a healthy mouth and a healthy body. He has been bringing the best and the latest in dentistry to Northwest Indiana for over 30 years.

Dr. Eric is a member of the American Dental Association, the Indiana Dental Association, the Northwest Indiana Dental Association, the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, the American Laser Academy, the American Academy of General Dentistry, and the Chicago Dental Society. He is certified in sedation dentistry and belongs to the Dental Organization for Conscious Sedation.

He has fellowships in the International Congress of Oral Implantology and Midwest Implant Institute. Dr. Eric is also the owner of a Mini Dental Implant Center of America. He is on staff at Community Hospital and Franciscan Hospitals in Dyer and Hammond.

Dr. Eric started Dental Home Health, a service that brings dentistry to the houses and facilities for those unable to go to the dentist. He is also the director of Dental Services at Hartsfield Village.

Dr. Eric is an entrepreneur with multiple business ventures. In 2017, he was voted innovator of the year by The Society of Innovators.

When not in the office, Dr. Eric enjoys spending time with his family. He is married with four children. He and his wife are very supportive in the community and schools, and they serve on many boards. Dr. Eric also likes classic and exotic sports cars, hockey, golfing, tennis, and skiing.

Meet Dr. Broomhead

Dr. Mark J. Broomhead graduated dental school from the University of Illinois at Chicago in May 2005. There he received the Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

In 2017, Dr. Mark was an honoree for the Incisal Edge Dental Magazine. He was recognized as one of the top 40 dentists under 40 in the country.

Dr. Mark grew up in nearby Lansing, IL. In his free time, he loves spending time with his wife Amanda, who also works in the field of dentistry, as a registered dental hygienist, and spending time with his daughter. Dr. Mark also enjoys competing in International Triathlons, especially the Escape from Alcatraz Triathlon, where he was a member of “Team Fox” and raised funds for Parkinson’s research.

Meet Dr. Politakis

Outside of Dentistry, Dr. Politakis loves traveling, sports, and spending time with friends and family. His two favorite sports, by far, are football and golf. Meet Dr. Anderson

Dr. Natalie Anderson is a dedicated and passionate dental professional based in Valparaiso, IN. With a Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine and a Bachelor of Science in Neuroscience from Indiana University’s Hutton Honors College, Natalie brings a wealth of knowledge and expertise to our practice.

Dr. Natalie’s dedication to excellence has been recognized through honors such as the Hutton Honors College Degree, Magna Cum Laude distinction from Indiana University, Phi Beta Kappa society member and the James W. and Betty Dye Scholarship.

Committed to providing exceptional care, Dr. Natalie has developed comprehensive treatment plans, utilizing advanced dental technologies and collaborating with specialists. She is devoted to continuing education which ensures she stays at the forefront of advancements in dentistry. Passion for oral health extends to her volunteer work, where she provided dental care to underserved communities in Nicaragua and Panama. Dr. Natalie’s commitment to excellence is evident through her academic achievements and certifications in CPR and laser technology.

In her free time, Dr. Natalie enjoys cooking, reading, and exploring the great outdoors, having visited over 20 national parks and aiming to visit all 63 of them! Outside of work Dr. Natalie is a passionate animal lover who adores her black lab Benji and orange tabby Nala.

Dr. Natalie’s unwavering commitment to patient care, coupled with her diverse skills and experiences, make her a valuable asset to any dental practice. We are thrilled for you to meet her!
