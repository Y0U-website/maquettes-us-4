General Dentist With Extensive Dental Implant Experience

Dr. Ian Roberts, DDS

Dr. Ian Roberts received his Bachelor of Science degree from the University of Northern Colorado, and his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Colorado. He is a member of the American Dental Association (ADA) and the New Hampshire Dental Society. Dr. Roberts participates in many hours of continuing education ; focusing heavily on dental implant surgery and restorations . He is a member of Spear continuing education and Great Bay Dental Studies group.

Dr. Roberts was born in Nashua, NH and later moved to Ashby, MA where he spent most of his childhood. After high school, he served our country in the United States Marine Corps . Dr. Roberts met his wife Carissa while on leave from training and have been inseparable ever since.

After marrying, he relocated to Colorado to attend college and receive his dental degree. During his time in Colorado, he and his wife welcomed a son, Ben. With how important family is to Dr. Roberts, he and his family moved back to the area, a return to his NH roots. In 2018, the Roberts family grew with the arrival of a baby girl, Zoe. Dr. Roberts enjoys spending time with his family, skiing, hiking, road/mountain biking, motorcycling, sailing, and flying in his spare time.
