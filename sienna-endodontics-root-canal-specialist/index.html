The practice

Yes. Root canal therapy is a primary service at Sienna Endodontics. Treatment removes infected or inflamed pulp from inside the tooth to address the source of the problem and help preserve the natural tooth.

Yes. Sienna Endodontics provides endodontic retreatment for teeth that have not healed properly after a previous root canal or have developed another infection. Dr. Puri evaluates the tooth and determines whether retreatment may provide an opportunity to preserve it.

Yes. The practice treats cracked teeth and traumatic dental injuries, including knocked-out or displaced teeth. Prompt specialist evaluation can be important after dental trauma because timely treatment may improve the chance of preserving the natural tooth.

Yes. Endodontic surgery and endodontic microsurgery are available for cases where surgical treatment may be appropriate. Dr. Puri uses specialized techniques and modern technology to address complex endodontic conditions while focusing on natural tooth preservation.

Sienna Endodontics incorporates advanced technology into diagnosis and treatment to support precise endodontic care. Modern tools are combined with Dr. Puri’s specialist training and a patient-first approach to create a calm and focused treatment experience.

Yes. Sedation is one of the services available at Sienna Endodontics. Patients who feel anxious about endodontic treatment can discuss their comfort concerns and available options with the practice before their procedure.
