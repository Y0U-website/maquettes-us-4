First Dental Visit

Your comfort and satisfaction are important from the get-go—and that starts here, with an explanation of what you can expect at your first dental visit to your SmileAlive practice.

We’re always happy to answer any questions you may have regarding your first dentist visit—just call , write , or ask your dentist. We have two locations: Eugene, OR and Springfield, OR .

Hellos and paperwork

When you arrive at our office, you will be greeted by one of our treatment coordinators who will get you started on your new patient paperwork. Please come in 15 minutes before your appointment so you have time to complete your paperwork or you can save time by Access your new patient paperwork now .
