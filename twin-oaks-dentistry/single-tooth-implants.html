Single Implants Process

The process of receiving a single-tooth implant typically involves several steps:

  • Consultation: Your dentist will evaluate your oral health and discuss your treatment options.
  • Implant Placement: The implant fixture is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia.
  • Osseointegration: Over the next few months, the implant fuses with the surrounding bone tissue.
  • Abutment and Crown Placement: Once osseointegration is complete, an abutment is attached to the implant, and a digital scan is performed to design the final crown. Then the custom crown is placed on the abutment, completing the restoration.
  • Recovery from single-tooth implant surgery is relatively quick. Most patients experience minimal discomfort and return to normal activities within a few days. With proper care and maintenance, single-tooth implants can last a lifetime.
