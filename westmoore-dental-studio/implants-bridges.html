Preventative, Restorative and…

Here at Westmoore Dental, we offer two reliable solutions to help you with missing or loose teeth. No matter your dental concern, we can restore your teeth and make your smile healthier and more radiant than ever.

Dental Implants

I mplants are the closest you can come to natural teeth, and only your dentist will be able to tell the difference! They are the only dental restoration option that preserves natural bone, and they are actually proven to stimulate healthy bone growth. Implants provide an incredibly stable, attractive option for replacing a missing tooth.

Dental Bridges A bridge consists of connecting three or more teeth together. This can be done by using existing or implanted teeth as anchors. Bridges can either be permanent or removable, and they generally require a little more maintenance than implants.

We can complete any phase of treatment from a single implant, to a full mouth.

Research shows that if you have an overdenture, and we implant a couple of permanent teeth for the over denture to snap onto, it can give 60-80 % more retention to your loose denture.

Short of major surgery, we do 60-90 % of all procedures in house. In most cases, there is no need to refer you to a surgeon or another specialist! We plan your end result thoroughly ahead of time with the use of 3D scanners and an intraoral scanner (so you can skip intrusive, old school impressions) to create digital models and guides. We will find the perfect spot for your teeth before we even begin treatment so your result will last a lifetime. Our friendly staff is here to support you every step of the way, keep you informed at every stage, and make you as comfortable as possible.
