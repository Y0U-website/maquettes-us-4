​ Solutions for Replacing Multiple or All Teeth ​ If you’re missing several teeth or even all of them we offer advanced full-mouth options that go beyond traditional dentures. Implant-Retained Dentures Implant-retained dentures are a more secure, comfortable alternative to traditional dentures. A few implants are placed in the jawbone to hold your denture firmly in place. This means:

  • No more slipping or clicking
  • Improved stability when speaking and eating
  • Smaller, more natural-feeling denture design

They can be removable or fixed, depending on your needs and preferences. All-on-X Implants (e.g., All-on-4, All-on-6) All-on-X is a modern approach to full-mouth restoration using just 4 to 6 implants to support a full set of fixed teeth. It’s ideal for patients who want a permanent, non-removable option that looks and functions just like a natural set of teeth. Benefits of All-on-X:

  • ✅ Fewer implants needed
  • ✅ Often allows for same-day teeth
  • ✅ No need for messy adhesives
  • ✅ Easy to care for, just like real teeth
  • ✅ Great for people who have lost bone in the jaw

Wondering if implants are right for you? If you re in good overall health and have healthy gums, you may be a great candidate.
