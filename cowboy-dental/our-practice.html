Dr. Taylor Peterson

Dr. Peterson was born in California and raised in Centerville, Utah. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Business Management from Utah State University with a double minor in Chemistry and Biology before pursuing his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry. He completed his first two years at the main campus in OKC before finishing his clinical training at the OU Tulsa Clinic, where he gained extensive hands-on experience, achieving well above the minimum requirements for graduation.

Dr. Peterson has always enjoyed restoring, improving, and fixing things. Dentistry became the perfect way to combine his love of problem-solving with helping people. He is known for his gentle, compassionate approach and always tries to take time to listen to his patients, understand their concerns, and help them feel comfortable and cared for.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Peterson enjoys spending time with his wife, Amanda, whom he married in 2020, and their little boys. In his free time, he enjoys fixing things other than teeth, building computers, fish keeping, and amateur radio. He is also an active member of his church and values serving and connecting with his community.
