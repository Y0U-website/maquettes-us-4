Interceptive Orthodontics FAQs

What is interceptive orthodontics, and how is it different from traditional braces?

Interceptive orthodontics is an early, proactive approach to guiding jaw and tooth development in children while they are still growing. Unlike traditional braces, which correct alignment issues after they’ve developed, interceptive treatments help prevent severe misalignment, reduce the need for extractions, and improve breathing and facial development.

How do I know if my child needs early orthodontic treatment?

Signs your child may benefit from interceptive orthodontics include:

If you’re unsure, Dr. Audrey offers comprehensive evaluations to determine if early treatment is the right choice for your child.

  • Crowded or misaligned baby teeth
  • Mouth breathing or snoring
  • Speech difficulties or tongue thrusting
  • Difficulty chewing or biting properly
  • A receding or protruding jaw

Is interceptive orthodontics painful for my child?

Not at all! Our treatments are gentle, non-invasive, and designed to work with your child’s natural growth. Most children adapt quickly and experience little to no discomfort throughout treatment.
