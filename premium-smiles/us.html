Our Doctors

Meet

Dr. Marcela Echeverry D.M.D.

Dr. Marcela Echeverry has been practicing dentistry for almost two decades, she obtained her dental degree in her hometown Cali, Colombia. She then moved to Puerto Rico to pursue her US dental degree from the University of Puerto Rico where she graduated as Magna Cum Laude. Her passion and love for creating beautiful smiles motivates her to give her patients optimum treatment. Dr. Marcela also has an MBA in Health Administration and is fluent in Spanish. In her free time, she enjoys outdoor activities with her family and exercising.

Meet

Dr. Bruce Jacobs D.D.S.

Dr. Jacobs was born in New York and graduated from Brooklyn College with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology. From there he attended New York University College of Dentistry, and received his D.D.S degree. He also attended the prestigious L.D. Pankey Institute. Dr. Jacobs opened his office in Hallandale Beach in 1976 and has remained in the same location ever since providing treatment in all phases of Dentistry.
