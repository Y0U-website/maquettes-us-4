Dr. Kelvin Kawano

Dr. Kelvin Kawano has been helping his patients achieve optimal oral health and beautiful smiles since 1982.

Dr. Kawano grew up in Kingsburg California and attended Fresno State University where he received his Bachelors of Science in Zoology. Dr. Kawano attended the University of California, Los Angeles School of Dentistry and subsequently attended the Valley Medical General Practice Residency.

He has since been practicing in the Clovis community for over 30 years! He currently lives in Clovis area with his wife and has two children (Dr. Derek Kawano and Dr. Crystal Tom). In his free time, he enjoys pickleball, fishing and more pickleball!

Affiliations: American Dental Association (ADA) Lifetime Member, California Dental Association (CDA), Fresno-Madera Dental Society (FMDS), Seattle Study Club.

Dr. Derek Kawano

Dr. Derek Kawano grew up in Clovis, California and graduated from Clovis High School then attended the University of California, San Diego where he received his Bachelors in Science in Human Biology.

Like Dr. Kelvin, Dr. Derek attended the University of California, Los Angeles School of Dentistry.

While in dental school, Dr. Derek was an active member in the school, participating in various teaching and community outreach opportunities.

Dr. Derek met his wife Fiona while in school at UC San Diego. They have two son’s named Deklan and Brycen! Dr. Derek enjoys playing basketball and pickleball in his free time.

Affiliations: American Dental Association (ADA), California Dental Association (CDA), Fresno-Madera Dental Society (FMDS), Academy of General Dentistry, Seattle Study Club, Spear Education
