Services we offer

An alternative to metal filling, white composite fillings restores decayed or broken teeth and is a subtle and aesthetically pleasing.

Cosmetic bonding or tooth bonding is a cosmetic procedure that involves applying a custom-tinted composite resin to your teeth. It can repair a range of common complaints like gaps, discoloration, cracked teeth etc.

Veneers and crowns are both dental restoration methods that can improve the appearance and function of your teeth.

Root canal is a dental procedure that is used to treat and repair a badly decayed tooth. In this treatment the nerve and pulp are removed, and the inside of the tooth is cleaned and sealed to avoid further damage.

If you have a missing tooth, then a dental bridge treatment can help in bridging the gap between the teeth and help restore your smile, bite and oral health.

Implant is a modern dentistry solution when you have a single or multiple teeth missing. Implants also help preserve tooth-supporting bone that naturally deteriorates when a tooth is lost.

This procedure involves gum evaluation by a hygienist on a periodic basis to assess the health of your gums and bones around it. Early detection of gum diseases can prevent any irreversible damages in future like tooth loss.

Also known as conventional periodontal therapy or deep cleaning is a procedure involving removal of dental plaque and calculus and then smoothing, or planing, of the exposed surfaces of the roots.

Want sparkling white teeth? The teeth whitening process can lighten your teeth up to three shades, giving you clear, shining white teeth with our special teeth kit.

Using the best technology in 3D scanning, we replace conventional impressions for restorative and removable dentistry with quick, efficient and comfortable imaging for more accurate results.

A popular alternative to braces, Invisalign straightens teeth by guiding them to their natural position. Clear aligners that are virtually invisible help move teeth without causing much discomfort to patients.

Dental cleaning involves removing plaque and tartar from the teeth especially from the areas that are difficult to reach in routine tooth brushing and flossing.
