Providing compassionate, gentle dentistry is the heart of our practice. By offering accessible insurance and financing options, we are able to lower barriers to superior dental care, which allows us to make real differences in our patients’ lives. Everything we do at Worcester Cosmetic Restorative Dentistry is with you in mind. We love to have fun with our patients and hope that we will be able to form lasting friendships as we help maintain your smile. We always provide honest, straightforward advice and strive to keep you informed every step of the way.

Dr. Brien Hedstrom, DMD

Dr. Brien Hedstrom is originally from New Hampshire and spent many years studying and working in Boston. He completed his undergraduate degree from Boston College in 2005 before he pursued his dental education at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine where he earned his DMD. Prior to relocating back to Massachusetts to take over his uncle Tom’s practice, Brien practiced in Palm Beach Gardens, FL, where he was very well respected by both his patients and referring specialists.

Dr. Hedstrom comes from a family dedicated to health care, specifically oral health, as his father and three uncles became dentists before him. His many mentors taught him the intricacies of patient care, as well as the satisfaction that comes with helping restore a patient’s confidence in their smile. He cares deeply about providing the best quality care for his patients, and he is passionate about forming long lasting relationships with his patients both at the office and in the community.

In his free time Dr. Hedstrom enjoys golfing, skiing, spending time with friends and family, and the outdoors. He is an avid sports fan and loves to discuss college and professional sports with his patients.

Dr. Brian Leland, DMD MSD DABOI

Since graduating from the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Brian Leland has pursued an accelerated path in advanced surgical dentistry. Following a General Practice Residency in North Jersey’s Morristown Medical Center, he entered a rigorous Comprehensive Oral Implantology residency, culminating in a Master of Science in Dentistry (MSD) from Jacksonville University in 2026. He then went on to become one of the youngest dentists to achieve Diplomate status in the American Board of Oral Implantology (DABOI/ID), the highest recognition in the field of implant dentistry.

Dr. Leland’s clinical expertise was honed at the while living in Arizona and working full time at the Implant Pathway headquarters in Gilbert, Arizona. In a testament to his surgical skill and leadership, he rose from his resident position to a surgical mentor and national lecturer. He currently serves as the Course Director for Implant Pathway’s Nashville Fast Track, where he trains hundreds of dentists in the fundamentals and complexities of implant dentistry.

Dr. Leland focuses his clinical practice on implant surgery & complex restorations, third molar extractions, and hard tissue grafting

Dr. Leland is also conversational in Mandarin Chinese, allowing him to provide high-level care to a diverse patient base.

When he isn’t in private practice or teaching, you’ll likely find him skiing in Little Cottonwood Canyon, Utah, or exploring the outdoors with his wife and their dog.
