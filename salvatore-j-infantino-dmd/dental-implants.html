  • What is the procedure for dental implants? Here are the high-level steps you can expect when placing and restoring dental implants in Pittston, PA with Dr. Sal. Each patient is unique, so steps may vary depending on your needs. Initial consultation: Dr. Sal will listen closely to your concerns and desires so he can understand exactly what you’re looking for. From there, he’ll work with you to determine a treatment plan and get ready for your implant placement and/or restoration . For the initial implant placement, Dr. Sal will work closely with a top specialist in the area he knows and trusts.
  • Dental implant placement: A small metal implant post is surgically installed into your jawbone to replace your tooth root. Anesthesia and/or sedation may be used to ease any pain or discomfort.
  • Osseointegration: After the implant is placed, it will need a few months to heal, creating a sturdy and permanent foundation for your new tooth. You may receive a custom-fabricated temporary tooth while waiting for your permanent one.
  • Abutment placement: Once your healing is completed, a small connector piece, known as an abutment, is attached to your implant just above the gumline.
  • Crown attachment: Finally, Dr. Sal will complete your implant restoration with a beautiful new artificial tooth, attached to the implant via the abutment connector.
  • Am I a candidate for dental implants? Implants are a great solution for most people missing one or more teeth. However, there are a few factors to consider. These include: Age
  • Gum tissue health
  • Surrounding bone density
  • Overall health
  • Does getting dental implants hurt? You’d be surprised – most patients claim their dental implant surgery was much easier and more comfortable than they expected! During the procedure, you’ll be numb and won’t feel anything. After the procedure, over-the-counter pain medications, such as Tylenol or Advil , are sufficient to ease any discomfort. If you experience discomfort, it should go away after a few days. If fear of pain is stopping you from getting dental implants, let Dr. Sal know ! He can work with you to find a solution, such as sedation dentistry.
