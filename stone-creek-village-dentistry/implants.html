Why They’re Different:

Unlike dentures that slip or bridges that rely on other teeth, implants stand on their own. They’re built to last, and they don’t mess with your healthy teeth. That’s why we love offering them at Stone Creek Village Dentistry.

Implants may be right for you if:

  • You’re missing one or more teeth
  • You have adequate bone density (or are willing to consider bone grafting)
  • You’re in good overall health
  • You want a permanent, natural-looking solution
  • You’re tired of removable dentures

Dental Implants vs. Bridges vs. Dentures: Compare Your Options

When you’re weighing tooth replacement options, here’s how they stack up:

Choose Implants If:

  • ✅ You want the longest-lasting solution
  • ✅ You want to preserve jawbone health and facial structure
  • ✅ Adjacent teeth are healthy and you don’t want to file them down
  • ✅ You want something that functions like a natural tooth
  • ✅ You’re okay with a longer process for better long-term results

Choose Bridges If:

  • ✅ You want a faster solution (weeks vs. months)
  • ✅ You prefer to avoid surgery
  • ✅ Budget is a primary concern
  • ✅ Adjacent teeth already need crowns

Choose Dentures If:

Not sure which is right for you? our dentist will evaluate your oral health, bone density, and budget to recommend the best option.

  • ✅ You’re missing most or all teeth in an arch
  • ✅ You want the most affordable option upfront
  • ✅ You’re not a candidate for implants due to health or bone loss
  • ✅ You prefer a removable solution

The Benefits of Choosing Dental Implants

We’ve seen firsthand how dental implants transform lives. Here’s why they’re worth it:

Look Great:

They match your natural teeth, boosting your confidence. No one will know you had work done—unless you tell them.

Talk Easy:

No slipping means clear, worry-free speech. Say goodbye to mumbling or adjusting your teeth mid-conversation.

Feel Good:

Say goodbye to denture discomfort. Implants feel like your own teeth because they’re anchored in your jawbone.

Eat Anything:

Crunch apples or chew steak—no problem! Restore 90 %+ of your natural chewing function.

Stay Healthy:

They keep your jawbone strong and don’t harm nearby teeth. Implants stimulate bone growth, preventing the facial collapse that often comes with tooth loss.

Last Forever:

With care, they’re a lifetime investment. Most implants last 25+ years, and many last a lifetime.

The Implant Process: What to Expect

Your journey to a complete smile happens in clear, manageable steps:

Step 1: Comprehensive Consultation (60 minutes)

Your journey begins with a personalized consultation. We review your dental history, conduct a full oral examination, and use digital tools to assess bone quality. This step helps us determine the best implant solution—whether you need a single-tooth replacement or a complete restoration.

Step 2: Customized Treatment Planning

Every smile is unique. We create a detailed treatment plan that considers:

This plan is discussed in detail with you so you understand every step of the process, including the estimated cost.

  • The number of teeth to replace
  • The need for additional procedures (e.g., bone grafting, sinus lifts)
  • The most appropriate implant type to achieve a natural look

Step 3: Implant Surgery (60-90 minutes per implant)

Our skilled team performs the implant surgery using advanced, minimally invasive techniques. During the procedure:

Most patients report the procedure feels no worse than getting a tooth extracted.

  • Local anesthesia or sedation is provided to ensure your comfort
  • Implants are carefully positioned in your jawbone, promoting natural osseointegration
  • If necessary, bone grafting is done to reinforce the implant site

Step 4: Healing & Osseointegration (3-6 months)

After surgery, a healing period allows the implants to fuse with your bone. This is when the magic happens—your jawbone grows around the implant, creating a rock-solid foundation. Temporary teeth can be provided during this period.

Step 5: Final Restoration (45-60 minutes)

You’ll leave with a smile that looks and feels completely natural.

  • Custom crowns, bridges, or implant-supported dentures are attached
  • This final restoration is designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth
  • We adjust your bite for perfect comfort and function

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed in your jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. They support crowns, bridges, or dentures for a permanent, natural-looking solution.

A single implant typically ranges from $3,000-5,000 including the crown. We offer flexible financing and work with insurance. Call 831-920-6900 for a personalized estimate.

The procedure is done under local anesthesia, so you won’t feel pain. Most patients report minimal discomfort afterward, manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers.

With proper care, implants can last 25+ years or a lifetime. They have a 95-98 % success rate. Regular dental visits and good oral hygiene ensure longevity.

Most adults with missing teeth qualify. You need adequate bone density and good overall health. We’ll evaluate your specific case during a consultation at our Del Rey Oaks office.

From placement to final restoration typically takes 3-6 months. This allows time for osseointegration—the bone fusing with the implant. Temporary teeth can be provided during healing.

Implants replace the tooth root and don’t require altering adjacent teeth. Bridges use neighboring teeth for support. Implants preserve bone structure and last longer, but bridges are faster and less expensive.
