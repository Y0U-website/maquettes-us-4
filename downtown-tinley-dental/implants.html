How CBCT Imaging Enhances Implant Accuracy

Three-dimensional cone beam imaging reveals exact bone density, nerve locations, and sinus positions, allowing for precise creation of surgical guides. This digital precision enables less invasive procedures with minimal tissue trauma and optimal implant positioning for both function and aesthetics.

Bone Grafting and Sinus Lifts Make Implants Possible for More Patients

Even patients with significant bone loss can often receive implants with preparatory procedures like bone grafting and sinus lifts. Dr. Markus offers comprehensive bone regeneration services, including ridge preservation, socket grafting, and sinus elevation, to restore implant candidacy for patients who thought implants weren’t possible.

These foundation-building procedures ensure long-term implant success by creating sufficient bone volume for stable and lasting integration.

Bone Grafts Create a Solid Foundation for Long-Term Success

Both synthetic and natural bone graft materials can restore lost jawbone volume, with healing typically occurring over 3-4 months before implant placement. This investment in your jaw’s foundation ensures decades of stable implant function and preserves your facial structure.

Sinus Lifts for Upper Molar Implants

When upper jaw bone height is insufficient due to sinus proximity, a sinus lift procedure gently elevates the sinus membrane and adds bone graft material, creating adequate space for safe upper implant placement. This common procedure opens implant possibilities for many patients with upper tooth loss.

Dental Implants vs. Dentures and Bridges — What’s Right for You?

The best tooth replacement choice depends on your lifestyle, budget, oral health status, and personal preferences. Implants offer superior stability and bone preservation but require adequate bone density and healing time. Dentures provide immediate tooth replacement at a lower initial cost but may require ongoing adjustments. Bridges offer fixed replacement but require alteration of healthy adjacent teeth.

During consultation, we’ll discuss all options honestly to help you choose the solution that best fits your unique situation and long-term goals.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Ideal implant candidates have healthy gums, adequate jawbone density, and are non-smokers with good overall health. However, many patients who initially do not qualify can become candidates through bone grafting, sinus lifts, or other preparatory procedures that restore implant eligibility.

Age isn’t a limiting factor – we’ve successfully placed implants for patients from their twenties to their nineties, with success determined more by bone health and healing capacity than chronological age.

Dental Implant FAQs

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and maintenance, most dental implants last 20+ years and often a lifetime, making them the most cost-effective long-term solution for replacing missing teeth.

Are dental implants painful?

Thanks to guided surgery techniques and effective local anesthesia, most patients experience minimal discomfort during implant placement, often less than they anticipated. Post-operative pain is typically manageable with over-the-counter medications.

What do implants cost in Tinley Park?

With costs varying based on complexity and any preparatory procedures needed. We provide transparent estimates and flexible financing options during consultation.

Can I replace all my teeth with implants?

Yes, full-arch implant solutions, including snap-on dentures and fixed implant bridges, can replace all upper or lower teeth, providing a permanent, secure alternative to traditional dentures.

Can I get implants if I’ve had missing teeth for years?

Often, yes; however, long-term tooth loss may require bone grafting or sinus lift procedures to restore adequate bone volume for successful implant placement and integration.
