Dental Implant FAQs – What You Need to Know

The process begins with a detailed consultation and digital imaging. From there, we plan your treatment, perform the implant placement (with or without bone grafting), allow for healing (osseointegration), and finish with a custom-made crown or bridge.

Ceramic dental implants are metal-free, highly biocompatible, and offer excellent aesthetics. They’re ideal for patients with sensitivities or those who prefer a holistic approach to care.

The full process typically takes 5 to 8 months, depending on your healing and whether bone grafting is required.

Expect minor swelling and discomfort for a few days, which can be managed with prescribed medication. Most patients return to daily activities within 1–2 days.

With proper care and hygiene, dental implants can last 20 years or more—often a lifetime.
