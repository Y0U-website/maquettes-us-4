How Much Do Dental Implants Cost?

Let’s be real: implants aren’t the cheapest option upfront. On average, costs range from $3,000 to $5,000 per implant , depending on your location, number of implants, and any additional treatments (like bone grafting). But here’s the key—implants are designed to last decades. Compared to dentures or bridges that often need replacements, implants typically save patients money and hassle in the long run.

How Do Dental Implants Feel?

Most patients are surprised by how natural implants feel. Once the implant fuses with your jawbone (a process called osseointegration), it becomes part of your body. Eating, speaking, and smiling feels effortless—so much so that you might forget you even have them.

Frequently Asked Questions What are dental implants? Dental implants are artificial tooth roots, usually made of titanium, that are placed into the jawbone to support a crown, bridge, or denture. They function like natural teeth, giving you a permanent and stable solution for missing teeth.

How long do dental implants last? With proper care—brushing, flossing, and regular dental checkups—implants can last 20 years or more . In many cases, they last a lifetime.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants? You may be a candidate if you have healthy gums, enough jawbone density, and overall good health. Even if you’ve been told you don’t have enough bone, procedures like bone grafting can make implants possible.

How much do dental implants cost? The cost varies depending on the number of implants and additional treatments needed. On average, expect $3,000–$5,000 per implant , but remember—they’re designed to be a long-term solution that can save you money over time.

How long does it take to get dental implants? From start to finish, the process can take 3–6 months . This includes time for the implant to fuse with the jawbone (osseointegration). In some cases, same-day implants are possible.

Do dental implants look natural? Yes. The crown or bridge placed on top of an implant is custom-made to match your natural teeth in color, size, and shape. Most people won’t be able to tell the difference.

Can dental implants replace dentures? Absolutely. Many patients choose All-on-4 or All-on-6 implants to replace a full arch of dentures. Unlike traditional dentures, implant-supported options don’t slip, click, or require adhesives.

Are dental implants safe? Yes, dental implants have a success rate of over 95 % . When performed by an experienced dentist, they are one of the safest and most predictable dental procedures available.

How do I care for my dental implants? Caring for implants is just like caring for natural teeth: brush twice daily, floss once a day, and visit your dentist regularly. With proper maintenance, they’ll stay strong and healthy for decades.
