Frequently Asked Questions Are dental sealants safe? Yes, sealants are made of safe, biocompatible materials and are widely recommended by dentists and the American Dental Association.

At what age should kids get sealants? Sealants are usually applied when the first and second permanent molars erupt, around ages 6 and 12.

Do adults need dental sealants? Yes, adults with cavity-prone teeth or deep grooves can also benefit from sealants for added protection.

Can sealants be placed over cavities? Sealants are meant to prevent cavities, not treat them. However, in some cases, very early signs of decay may be sealed to stop progression.

How much do dental sealants cost? Sealants typically cost between $30 and $60 per tooth , and many dental insurance plans cover them for children under 18.

How do sealants differ from fluoride treatments? Fluoride strengthens enamel across the entire tooth surface, while sealants create a physical barrier in the grooves of back teeth.

Do sealants change the look or feel of teeth? Sealants are clear or tooth-colored and cannot be seen when talking or smiling. They feel smooth and natural after application.

How do I take care of teeth with sealants? Continue regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups. Sealants don’t replace good oral hygiene but add extra protection.

How often should sealants be reapplied? Sealants can last many years, but your dentist will check them at each visit and recommend reapplication if they wear down.
