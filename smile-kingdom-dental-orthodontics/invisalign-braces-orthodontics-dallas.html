Choose Your Orthodontic Adventure: Invisalign or Braces?

Invisalign: The Stealth Leopard of Orthodontics

  • Digital Mapping : We create a 3D map of your mouth, planning your tooth movement like charting a course through uncharted jungle territory.
  • Custom Aligners : You’ll receive a series of clear, custom-made aligners, each moving your teeth bit by bit, like a gentle stream shaping river rocks.
  • Biweekly Changes : Every two weeks, you’ll switch to a new set of aligners, progressing through your treatment like climbing to new levels in the jungle canopy.
  • Check-ins : We’ll see you every 6-8 weeks to ensure your smile safari is on track.
  • Nearly invisible - blend in like a leaf-tailed gecko!
  • Removable for eating and cleaning - no dietary restrictions!
  • Often shorter treatment time than traditional braces
  • Fewer office visits required - spend less time at the watering hole

Traditional Braces: The Mighty Gorilla of Teeth Straightening

  • Bracket Bonding : We’ll attach small brackets to your teeth, like setting up base camps for a mountain climb.
  • Wiring : The brackets are connected with a wire, creating a network that guides your teeth, like a series of jungle vines.
  • Regular Adjustments : You’ll visit us every 4-6 weeks for adjustments, tightening the wires to keep your teeth moving in the right direction.
  • Elastic Bands : In some cases, we’ll use elastic bands to apply extra force, like calling in reinforcements to move stubborn teeth.
  • Effective for complex orthodontic issues - no smile is too wild for these to tame!
  • Often the most economical option
  • Fun-colored bands to express your personality - be as colorful as a toucan’s beak!
  • Non-removable, so they’re always working (even when you’re sleeping like a sloth)
