What to Do in a Dental Emergency

  • Call Us Immediately : Contact us at 214-643-6222 for guidance and to schedule an emergency appointment.
  • Manage Pain : Use over-the-counter pain relievers if needed, but avoid aspirin for bleeding injuries.
  • Handle Knocked-Out Teeth Carefully : If a tooth is knocked out, hold it by the crown (not the root), rinse it gently, and try to place it back in the socket. If that’s not possible, keep it in milk or saliva until you reach our office.
  • Control Bleeding : Apply gentle pressure with a clean cloth or gauze to any bleeding areas.
  • Protect Broken Teeth : Cover any sharp edges with dental wax or temporary filling material from a pharmacy.
  • Avoid Home Remedies : Some home remedies can do more harm than good. Always consult with us before attempting any treatments.

Preventing Future Dental Emergencies

  • Maintain regular dental check-ups and cleanings
  • Wear a mouthguard during sports activities
  • Avoid using your teeth as tools to open packages or bottles
  • Don’t chew on ice, popcorn kernels, or other hard objects
  • Address dental issues promptly before they become emergencies
