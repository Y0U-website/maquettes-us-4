Meet the Doctors

Dr. Pejman Khosravian – Endodontist

Dr. Pejman Khosravian received his Doctor of Dental Medicine from Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine. Upon graduating from dental school, he completed a year of General Practice Residency at Jamaica Hospital in Queens, NY. His interest in endodontics then led me to New York University where He received his specialty degree in endodontics.

He opened up his first office in San Marcos, TX in 2010 and practiced there for 2 year. After reuniting with his brother, Dr. Pedram Khosravian, They opened their Austin office in 2012 and have been practicing in Austin since.
