C O N N E C T W I T H U S . W E D L O V E T O H E L P Y O U .

Dr. Grace graduated from the University of Buffalo School of Dental Medicine in 2005. After graduating from dental school, Dr. Grace attended a General Practice Residency Program at Stony Brook University which she completed in 2006. Prior to dental school, Dr. Grace practiced as a Physical Therapist in Hartford, Connecticut from 1996 - 2001.

Dr. Grace is a member of several dental associations including: the American Dental Association (ADA), New York State Dental Association (NYSDA), and the Suffolk County Dental Society (SCDS).

It’s a pleasure to welcome you to our family dental practice! We are a general dentistry office that treats all ages from children to the young at heart.
