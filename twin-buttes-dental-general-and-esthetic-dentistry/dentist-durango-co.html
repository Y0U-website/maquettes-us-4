MEET OUR TEAM

Trish Thomas, RDH, BA

Trish is a registered dental hygienist. She is originally from southern Florida but has lived in Colorado since 1994. She received her undergraduate degree in Kinesiology at the University of Colorado-Boulder in 2000 and her degree in dental hygiene at the Community College of Denver in 2004. She is passionate about helping people improve and maintain good oral health.

Trish enjoys mountain biking, nordic skiing, snowboarding, camping and playing outdoors in beautiful Durango with her two boys.

Toni Kendall

Toni is a San Diego native. She relocated in 2022 from the sunny shores of California to this beautiful mountain town. She is happy to be living closer to her family that has resided in Durango for 30 years. Toni has been helping people smile since 1998 when she started her dental career. She strives to give our patients a friendly, positive and professional dental experience.
