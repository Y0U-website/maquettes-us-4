What is the Full Mouth Restoration procedure like?

Once we’ve agreed on the full mouth reconstruction treatment plan, we begin with any necessary preparatory treatments. This might involve addressing underlying issues such as gum disease or tooth decay to create a healthy foundation for your restoration work. We also look at things like sleep-disordered breathing concerns or other habits that may have contributed to the breakdown of your smile. These initial treatments are crucial for the long-term success of your full mouth restoration.

The core of your restoration journey involves a series of restorative procedures. Depending on your needs, this may include the placement of dental implants, preparation for crowns or bridges, orthodontic adjustments, or other specialized treatments. Each of these procedures is performed with the utmost care and precision, utilizing the latest dental technologies to ensure optimal results.

As we near the end of your treatment plan, we’ll place your final restorations. Whether these are crowns, veneers, dental bridges, or implant-supported prosthetics, we meticulously ensure that each restoration fits perfectly and functions harmoniously with your bite. This attention to detail is what sets our full mouth restorations apart, providing you with a smile that’s not only beautiful but also comfortable and functional.

Your journey doesn’t end with the placement of your final restorations. We schedule regular follow-up appointments to monitor your progress and ensure the longevity of your new smile. These check-ups allow us to address any concerns promptly and provide guidance on maintaining your restored oral health.

At Dr. Dayna Cassandra and Associates, we’re committed to guiding you through every step of your full mouth restoration journey, from your initial consultation to your final follow-up, ensuring you achieve the healthy, beautiful smile you deserve.

What conditions does full mouth restoration address?

Full mouth restoration can effectively treat a wide range of dental issues, including:

By addressing these issues comprehensively, we can improve your oral health and enhance your overall quality of life.

  • Severely worn or broken teeth
  • Multiple missing teeth
  • Chronic jaw pain or TMJ disorders
  • Malocclusion (bite problems)
  • Extensive decay or gum disease
  • Congenital dental defects

What are the benefits of full mouth restoration?

Choosing full mouth restoration with Dr. Dayna Cassandra and Associates offers numerous benefits:

  • Improved Oral Health : We address all dental issues, creating a healthy foundation for your new smile.
  • Enhanced Functionality : Enjoy improved ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.
  • Improved Overall Health and Wellness : Chewing food properly allows for better digestion and overall health benefits. A well-functioning, good-looking smile can have a positive impact on your mental health.
  • Pain Relief : Resolve chronic jaw pain and headaches associated with dental problems.
  • Aesthetic Transformation : Achieve a beautiful, natural-looking smile that complements your facial features.
  • Boosted Self-Confidence : Feel proud to show off your new smile personally and professionally.
  • Long-Term Solution : Your restored smile can last for many years with proper care.
