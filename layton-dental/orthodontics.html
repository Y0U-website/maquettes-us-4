Our Orthodontic Services

  • Traditional Braces: Traditional metal braces remain a reliable solution for addressing complex alignment and bite issues. Durable and effective, they are a popular choice for both teens and adults.
  • Clear Aligners: For a more discreet option, our clear aligners offer the perfect balance of aesthetics and effectiveness. Designed to fit seamlessly into your lifestyle, these removable trays straighten teeth without the hassle of wires and brackets.
  • ClearCorrect: At Layton Dental, we also offer ClearCorrect , an advanced clear aligner system tailored for maximum comfort and precision.
  • Orthodontic Evaluations for Children: Early detection of alignment issues can simplify treatment. Our orthodontic evaluations ensure your child’s teeth and jaw develop properly, potentially reducing the need for extensive treatments later.

What Are the Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment?

  • Improved Appearance: Straight teeth contribute to a confident, radiant smile.
  • Enhanced Oral Health: Proper alignment reduces the risk of cavities, gum disease, and uneven wear on teeth.
  • Better Bite Functionality: Correcting bite issues enhances your ability to chew, speak, and maintain overall jaw health.
  • Long-Term Confidence: Orthodontics delivers lasting results, improving both aesthetics and functionality for years to come.

Orthodontics FAQs

  • What is the best age to start orthodontic treatment?: Orthodontic evaluations are recommended by age 7. However, it’s never too late to achieve a straight smile—our orthodontic solutions are ideal for teens and adults alike.
  • How long does orthodontic treatment take?: Treatment duration varies depending on the complexity of the case. On average, traditional braces take 18–24 months, while clear aligner treatments may take 6–18 months.
  • Are clear aligners as effective as braces?: Clear aligners are highly effective for mild to moderate alignment issues. For more complex cases, traditional braces may be recommended.
  • How much does orthodontic treatment cost?: Costs depend on the type of treatment and its duration. We offer flexible payment options to make orthodontics accessible for every patient.
