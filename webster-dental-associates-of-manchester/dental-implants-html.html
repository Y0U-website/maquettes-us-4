What Are Dental Implants?

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

When you are undergoing a significant treatment like dental implants, it’s important to have the convenience of visiting just one location for the entire process. Thanks to the advanced training of Dr. Golparvar, he can ensure that’s a reality during your smile upgrade. Though the path to dental implants will require multiple appointments over several months or more, their incredible benefits will make it worth your time invested. Every patient’s journey will be unique in some way, but there are four basic steps in the process for dental implants in Manchester that you should be aware of.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

Dental Implant Surgery

We prioritize patient comfort, so your mouth will be numbed before the procedure. Your implant dentist in Manchester will access your jawbone via small incisions in your gum tissue. Based on your particular needs, the implants will be placed at precise locations and angles in your jaw. Your gums will be sutured shut before a protective cap is placed over each implant post to shield them over the course of the healing process.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment Placement

At this stage, you will wait 3-6 months while the implants fuse with your jawbone via a process called osseointegration. This is an essential period for the long-term success of your treatment. After osseointegration has occurred, Dr. Golparvar will perform a second surgery, albeit smaller, to place metal connectors known as abutments on top of your implant posts. The abutment will act as the middleman between your posts and your customized dental crown, bridge, or denture.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restorations

Based on impressions taken of your mouth, a trusted dental lab will craft your restoration(s) to fit perfectly within your smile. They utilize materials that look virtually similar to natural teeth so that no one will be aware of your dental work unless you tell them. When they’re ready, Dr. Golparvar will attach your crown, bridge, or denture to your abutments so you can pick up a mirror and survey your new smile!

Benefits of Dental Implants

This can cause them to avoid certain foods that are not only an important aspect of their diet, but also enjoyable to eat! With the strength and stability of dental implants, you can restore most of your natural biting power, allowing you to cover all of your food groups in your diet.

  • Speak with Ease. Due to the added stability that dental implants provide your prosthetic, you won’t have to worry about tripping over your words due to slipping and shifting dentures.

Health Benefits

  • Maintain Better Oral Hygiene. Without any gaps in your smile, brushing and flossing your teeth effectively will be an easier task to take on, helping make your oral hygiene routine more efficient and minimizing your risk of developing oral health issues.
  • Preserve Your Jawbone & Facial Structure. Dental implants are able to naturally fuse with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration, keeping your jawbone from naturally deteriorating over time due to the fact that it’s not being stimulated.
  • Improve Your Overall Health. With a decreased risk of oral health problems due to a better oral hygiene routine, you’ll also be able to enjoy improved overall health. This is because common oral issues like gum disease have been linked to an increased risk of strokes, heart attacks, and even Alzheimer’s.

That leads to other life-changing advantages, like spending less time in the dental office for denture relining and replacements, and more time getting the most out of your life and enjoying your smile. These long-term benefits truly make this procedure priceless.

Who Dental Implants Can Help?

More patients are eligible for dental implants than ever before, but there are certain qualifications to keep in mind. For example, there needs to be a certain amount of bone tissue available so the implants can integrate properly. You’ll also need to have sufficient oral health, especially good gum health, so the healing process can occur as planned. If you are missing one or more teeth (or missing an entire arch), chances are dental implants are perfect for your needs!

Missing 1 Tooth

A single titanium implant post is used to replace an individual tooth, which is strong enough to hold a dedicated implant crown made from high-quality ceramic materials. Unlike bridges, you will not have to remove existing enamel from neighboring teeth just to get it placed. In order to keep the crown stable, an abutment is placed after the implant post has had time to integrate and heal inside of your bone and gum tissue.

Missing Multiple Teeth

If you have two or more consecutive missing teeth, a series of dental implants can be used to hold a dedicated dental bridge. Because dental implants are so strong, you won’t necessarily need to have an implant placed for every missing tooth. For example, a pair of dental implants can typically hold a two- or three-crown bridge. In the event more teeth need to be replaced, a partial denture combined with dental implants is also a consideration.

Missing All Teeth

Whether you are missing all your teeth in either your top or bottom arch, a series of implants can be placed throughout the jaw to provide a stable foundation for an implant denture. If you already have a denture, we’ll determine if it can be customized to fit with dental implants. If not, we’ll design a fully customized denture to ensure that it fits your mouth exactly and is made with gum- and tooth-colored materials.

The total price of getting dental implants in Manchester is influenced by several factors. Some of those include:

Dental Implants Post-Op Instructions

Since we place dental implants in-office, we make a point of ensuring that all of our patients know how to take care of their mouths properly following the procedure. You will need to take careful note of any post-op instructions we give you in order to avoid unnecessary complications during the recovery process. Below, you will find some general guidelines to keep in mind after you have received dental implants. Please let us know if you want to clarify any of the instructions you have been given.

What to Do Directly After Dental Implant Surgery

Once your dental implant surgery is complete, you should plan on spending the remainder of the day resting at home. A blood clot will form at the surgical site; in order to keep the healing process on schedule, you will need to protect this blood clot by:

  • Using a tissue to get rid of excess saliva instead of spitting.
  • Drinking all beverages straight out of the glass instead of through a straw.
  • Staying away from cigarettes and any other type of tobacco product.
  • Keeping your tongue and fingers away from the surgical site as much as possible.

Common Side Effects When Recovering from Dental Implant Placement

It’s normal to experience some minor discomfort, swelling, and bleeding in the days following your dental implant surgery. These symptoms are generally nothing to worry about as long as they don’t grow worse over time or last longer than expected. There are plenty of ways you can keep your post-surgical side effects under control, such as taking pain medication, holding a cold compress over any swollen areas, and using gauze to apply pressure to the surgical site if it starts to bleed.

Your Diet After Dental Implant Surgery

After dental implant surgery, it will likely be a few days before you can return to a normal diet. Until then, your meals should consist of soft and liquid foods. Applesauce, yogurt, scrambled eggs, and mashed potatoes are all great options. You can have soup as well, but only if it’s cold; you will need to avoid consuming anything hot for at least 24 to 48 hours following your surgery.

Post-Op Health & Oral Hygiene

To make sure that no issues occur while your dental implants are bonding with your jaw, you need to make oral hygiene a priority. You should continue to brush your teeth at least twice a day; just make sure that you’re especially gentle when cleaning around the site where the surgery was performed. To minimize your chances of infection, you should get in the habit of rinsing with salt water. It’s okay to use mouthwash as long as it doesn’t contain any alcohol.

What to Do After Your New Teeth Are Attached

Once your replacement teeth have been attached to your dental implant posts, you may notice some sensitivity in your gums. You should be able to keep the discomfort under control with over-the-counter pain medication. There shouldn’t be any swelling or bleeding at this point, so if you notice these or any other unusual symptoms, let us know right away.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

Dental implants can last for the rest of your life, yet this is only possible with the right maintenance. The lifespan of your dental implants will directly depend on how well you care for them. That’s why we’ve included some easy yet effective steps for dental implant care in Manchester. Read on to get the information you need to keep your newly restored smile safe.

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

While your implants themselves can’t be affected by cavities or decay, it’s more important than ever to practice great dental hygiene. Common dental issues like gum disease can lead to implant failure. If you’re not careful, infected gums won’t be able to properly support your implant. Instead, be sure to brush your teeth twice a day, paying close attention to your gumline. The best way to clean your gums is to angle your brush head at 45-degrees. Additionally, don’t forget to floss once a day to lift plaque and food debris off your gums.

Eat a Healthy Diet

Dental implants allow you to eat virtually anything you want, which is why we encourage you to consume a full, healthy, and nutritious diet. However, overindulging in foods that are extra hard, sticky, acidic, or sugary can damage your implants and cause them to fail if you’re not careful. Do your best to avoid these types of foods and try to eat items rich in calcium and vitamin D instead. These keep your jawbone and gums healthy and strong, increasing the longevity of your dental implants.

Break Bad Habits

While dental implants have a success rate of 95 % even after 10 years, there is a chance that they can fail. The use of tobacco products and smoking will slow down healing and encourage infection. Additionally, bad habits like chewing on non-food items or ice cubes can cause injury and implant failure. You should also avoid using your teeth for anything other than eating, speaking, and smiling do not use your smile to open packages or bottles because it could seriously damage your implants.

Protect Your Dental Implants

Your new smile is incredibly strong but not completely indestructible. If you play sports, or participate in any physical activity, wear a mouthguard to protect your dental implants from damage. Are you affected by bruxism (teeth grinding)? If so, make sure that you’re wearing a nightguard during sleep to prevent the extra wear and tear that grinding can cause. This will also relieve pressure on the jaw, making your smile more comfortable in general.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Visit our office twice a year for routine checkups. These appointments allow Dr. Golparvar to catch any potential issues with your implants as early as possible, saving you time and money in the future from avoided dental care. Your checkups are also a great time to ask us any questions you have about your oral health and dental implants. Plus, we will ensure the restorations you have still look lifelike, stable, and clean!

3D Cone-Beam Imaging/3D CT Scanning

While digital X-rays help with a lot of dentistry, they play a limited role in dental implant work. Our practice relies more on its CBCT machine instead.

Otherwise called a cone beam CT scanner, a CBCT machine creates a 3D scan of your mouth with little to no radiation. This scan shows many intricate details nerve pathways, blood vessels, etc. that a digital X-ray can’t capture well. As such, we use the CBCT machine to plan dental implant placement precisely and enhance the success of treatment. This method minimizes the chance of unexpected issues during surgery, and it helps your final implants work even better.

You shouldn’t need special preparation for 3D cone beam imaging. Here at our office, we typically have patients remove anything that disrupts it jewelry, eyeglasses, hairpins, and so on. Our team will let you retrieve such items after the scans are done.

Digital Impression System

Dental implants are capped with dental crowns after they’re placed. To ensure that step goes well, our team uses a digital impression system.

A digital impression system uses non-contact methods to get impressions of your mouth. More specifically, it takes scans of your teeth, bite, and oral tissues that don’t involve any goopy putty. That means it’s more accurate and comfortable than impression systems from decades ago. Using its results, lab workers can design and make lifelike restorations that blend well with your smile.

We typically use our digital impression system during the dental implant consultation. At that session, we take the required scans as part of a patient’s (potential) preparation for surgery. Doing so saves time (for you and our team) and helps your final implants look lifelike.

Dental Implant FAQs

Are Dental Implants Safe?

Dental implant treatment is considered to be completely safe when performed by a professional. On top of that, they currently have an average success rate that falls between 95 % and 98 %, giving you even more confidence to replace teeth via this method. Thanks to decades of research and development, dentists have nearly perfected the placement of dental implants and made the treatment extremely predictable, reducing the risk of complications. Our in-house specialists are happy to answer any questions you have about the safety and effectiveness of implants during your consultation.

What If I’m Not Eligible for Dental Implants?

Just because you are not eligible for treatment now does not mean you can’t receive it later. It really comes down to determining what you need in order to receive dental implants safely. For example, if you do not have enough healthy bone tissue to receive an implant, a bone graft may need to be performed. If you have gum disease that puts your implant at risk of failure, we’ll recommend gum therapy to ensure the gum tissue heals before you receive any kind of oral surgery.

Can Dental Implants Be Removed After They’re Placed?

Dental implants are not like dentures and should never be removed after they are placed. However, if they are failing and did not properly integrate with the jaw, they need to be removed. Keep in mind that even if you choose to restore your implants with a removable implant denture, the implants remain in placed despite the restoration being removable.
