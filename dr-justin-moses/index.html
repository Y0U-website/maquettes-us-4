Dr. Morgan, a second generation dentist, was born and raised in Dillon, CO and received a finance degree from the University of Colorado. After graduating, his passion for travel and adventure took him on a trip throughout Asia and the Middle East. It was on this trip that he realized his desire to return to school and become a dentist.

Dr. Morgan attended Creighton University’s Dental School and graduated Summa Cum Laude. He was inducted into the prestigious Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society upon graduation based on his excellent grades and clinical performance in all disciplines of dentistry. This honor is awarded only to the top 10 % of a graduating class.

Wanting to further his training, Dr. Morgan completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) through Idaho State University (ISU) in Boise, Idaho. In this program, he received further training in moderate sedation, implantology, endodontics, and oral surgery.

Dr. Morgan is excited to be a part of the Vail Valley community along with his wife and 3 daughters. Dr. Morgan enjoys ice hockey, snowboarding, skiing, mountain biking, backpacking, and anything that involves the outdoors. Dr. Morgan is here to help serve the Vail Valley as both a dentist and a member of the community.
