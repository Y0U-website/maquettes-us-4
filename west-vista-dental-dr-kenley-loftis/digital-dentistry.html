Digital Dentistry

We live in an age where everything is moving toward digital and computer-based solutions. Dentistry is no exception. At West Vista Dental, we combine the human element of dental care with digital dental technology to achieve optimal care for our patients.

Commitment To Technology, Accuracy, Immediacy

Digital dentistry allows for reduced exposure to radiation and earlier detection of dental issues, diseases, and disorders, including cavities, cancer, and gum disease. At West Vista Dental, we are committed to using this technology to increase our accuracy in diagnosis, immediacy of care and the highest levels of care possible for our patients.

Digital X-Rays

In the old days, x-rays done on film required processing that often took days. Even traditional x-rays with quick processing times are not nearly as fast or accurate as digital. So, when we take your digital x-rays, they link directly to our computers for immediate results we can see instantly.

3D Imaging

We utilize 3D imaging at West Vista Dental. This technology gives us three-dimensional images of your mouth. We can see your bones and soft tissue in addition to your teeth to get a clear picture of your oral structure and health. It allows us to be more efficient and more precise in our evaluation, diagnosis, care, treatment, and procedures.

CEREC Single Appointment Crowns

CEREC, or Chairside Economical Restoration of Esthetic Ceramics, is a technology that allows for crowns and tooth restoration in one visit. Instead of multiple appointments for impressions and temporary crown placement before an outside lab creates your crown, CEREC can be done in one appointment. We use a digital impression and specialized software to design and create the crown in minutes, so you can have it placed and be on your way without the need for another appointment.
