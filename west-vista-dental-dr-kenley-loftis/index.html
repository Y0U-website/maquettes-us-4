If you have a broken, damaged, or missing tooth, we can repair it and have your smile looking like new. A fractured, damaged, or heavily repaired tooth often requires a crown. A crown is a protective “covering” or cap that covers the old tooth. It looks like your old tooth and will protect it from further damage.

A bridge is a prosthetic put in the place of a missing tooth. We use your existing teeth on either side of the gap as anchors for the prosthetic, making it look natural and giving you back your original smile.
