FAQs

How are aligners different from traditional braces?

Aligners are clear teeth moulds that can be taken out; when placed on the teeth, they are much less visible than typical metal braces. They provide the benefit of being able to remove them for three meals and cleaning, as compared to traditional braces, which are non-detachable devices using brackets and wires to shift teeth.

Are orthodontic aligners suitable for all ages?

Orthodontic aligners are perfect for use by teenagers and adults. Generally, teenagers have a few advantages since their appearance is not affected, and adults are comfortable with their convenience and looks. However, it is important to remember that the suitability always relies on one’s oral condition, so a doctor’s visit is a must to find out the right treatment.

Do orthodontic aligners hurt?

Most patients may find that they feel minor pain or pressure during the first aligner insertion when their teeth are being moved. This happens only for a few days and most often is not as painful as it is with metal braces.

Are the aligners customized to fit your teeth?

Each Invisalign aligner is tailor-made to fit your teeth, resulting in an effective and comfortable solution. Imaging technology is of a high level and is used to prepare a plan unique to your situation.

How long do orthodontic aligners take to work?

The duration of treatment varies depending on individual cases but typically ranges from 6 to 18 months. Factors such as the complexity of tooth movement and patient compliance can affect the timeline.

Should I visit my orthodontist regularly during treatment?

Yes, you must keep coming for regular checkups every 4 to 6 weeks to see how much progress you have made and to get new aligners. Such visits not only confirm that your treatment is moving as expected but also make it possible to make any required changes.
