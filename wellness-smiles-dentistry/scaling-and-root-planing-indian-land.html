FAQs

Is scaling and root planing the same as deep cleaning?

Yes, scaling and root planing are often known as a deep cleaning. During this procedure, plaque and tartar are removed from beneath the gumline (scaling), and the tooth roots are smoothed out (root planing) to cause the gums to reattach to the teeth.

Can scaling and root planing help with gum disease?

Yes. Root scaling and planning is a good treatment for periodontal disease, especially if it is done early. Through proper cleaning below the gumline and smoothing the root surface of teeth, this treatment destroys gum-disease-causing bacteria that are responsible for inflammation and establishes the blockage of the illness spread.

Can scaling and root planing be completed in one visit?

The number of visits necessary for scaling and root planing will vary depending on the degree of gum disease and the quantity of plaque and tartar. Most often, the treatment is done in several visits, each treating a quadrant of the mouth to allow adequate cleaning and patient comfort.

What should you avoid eating after scaling and root planing?

Following the procedure, hard, crunchy, spicy, acidic, and hot foods should be avoided for a minimum of 48 hours. Nuts, chips, raw vegetables, citrus fruits, and hot drinks are some of them. Yogurt, mashed potatoes, and smoothies are some examples of soft foods that can help with easy recovery.

How does scaling and root planing improve oral health?

Scaling and root planing improve oral health by eliminating plaque and tartar deposits that cause gum disease. The treatment minimizes bleeding and inflammation of the gums, halts the advancement of the disease, and provides a healthier environment for healing and reattachment of the gums to the teeth.

Can scaling and root planing improve bad breath?

Yes, bad breath (halitosis) can be quite improved by scaling and root planing. Since this procedure removes the bacterial plaque that is responsible for gum disease, it removes one of the primary sources of continuous bad breath and results in healthier-smelling breath along with healthier teeth and gums.
