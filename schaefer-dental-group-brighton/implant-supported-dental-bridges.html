Before Your Appointment

  • 01 Bring a list of every medication you take, including supplements, since some can affect how implant sites heal.
  • 02 Eat a solid meal about two hours before your appointment because the numbing and procedure length may delay your next one.
  • 03 Wear a short-sleeved or loose-fitting top so we can easily place a blood pressure cuff during your implant bridge visit.

After Your Appointment

  • 01 Rinse gently around the implant bridge with warm salt water two to three times a day for the first week to keep the surgical sites clean.
  • 02 Stick to soft foods like mashed potatoes, smoothies, and scrambled eggs for five to seven days so the implants can begin fusing with the bone.
  • 03 Hold off on brushing directly over the bridge abutments for the first 48 hours, then switch to a soft-bristle brush and clean around each post carefully.
