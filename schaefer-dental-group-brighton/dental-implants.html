Before Your Appointment

  • 01 Arrange a ride home before your appointment, since implant placement involves local anesthesia and sometimes sedation.
  • 02 Eat a solid meal two hours beforehand so you&#x27;re comfortable during a procedure where your mouth will be numb for several hours.
  • 03 Bring a list of every medication and supplement you take, especially blood thinners, since these can affect healing around the implant site.

After Your Appointment

  • 01 Stick to soft foods like smoothies, mashed potatoes, and scrambled eggs for the first three to five days while the implant site heals.
  • 02 Rinse gently with warm salt water starting the day after surgery, but avoid swishing hard near the implant area for the first week.
  • 03 Hold off on exercise and heavy lifting for at least 48 hours so blood pressure stays low and the implant site can clot properly.
