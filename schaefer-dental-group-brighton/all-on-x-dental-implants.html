Book Appointment (810) 229-2126 BEYOND THE SURFACE Advanced All-on-X Dental Implants Technology Patients missing most of an arch often have uneven bone loss, and placing posts accurately in thinning bone requires more than a steady hand. A cone beam CT scan builds a full 3D model of your jaw, showing us exactly how much bone sits above each nerve canal and below each sinus floor. We use that model to design a printed surgical guide that locks each post into its planned position and angle during surgery , removing guesswork from the equation.

Because the guide speeds placement, we have time to mill and fit your temporary teeth in a day, right here in Brighton, MI. Your final arch is made from monolithic zirconia, a single solid piece roughly five times harder than acrylic. It won’t warp, absorb odors, or yellow the way older removable options did. The surface mimics natural enamel so well that the teeth look wet and translucent under light, just like the real thing.

Before Your Appointment

  • 01 Arrange a ride home before your appointment, since sedation is part of the All-on-X implant procedure and you won&#x27;t be able to drive.
  • 02 Eat a soft, filling meal about two hours beforehand so you&#x27;re comfortable during what can be a longer surgical visit.
  • 03 Bring a list of every medication you take, including blood thinners or supplements, so we can plan your implant sedation safely.

After Your Appointment

  • 01 Stick to cool, soft foods like smoothies, mashed potatoes, and broth for the first week while your All-on-4® implants integrate.
  • 02 Rinse gently with the prescribed antimicrobial mouthwash twice daily, but avoid swishing hard around the surgical sites for 72 hours.
  • 03 Hold off on brushing near the implant posts for the first few days. Use the soft surgical brush we provide to clean your temporary arch.
