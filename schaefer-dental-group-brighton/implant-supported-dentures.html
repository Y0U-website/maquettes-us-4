Before Your Appointment

  • 01 Bring your current dentures to the consultation so we can evaluate fit, wear patterns, and how implant placement should differ.
  • 02 List every medication you take, including blood thinners or supplements, since some may need to be paused before implant surgery.
  • 03 Arrange a ride home before your appointment day, because sedation used during implant placement makes driving unsafe afterward.

After Your Appointment

  • 01 Stick to soft foods like mashed potatoes, smoothies, and soup for the first week while your gums heal around the implant posts.
  • 02 Rinse gently with warm salt water after meals for the first two weeks, but avoid brushing directly on the surgical sites until we clear you.
  • 03 Hold off on removing or lifting your new denture to check the implants. Leave it in place so the tissue heals snugly against the attachments.
