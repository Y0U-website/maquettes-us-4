Invisalign

Clear, discreet, and removable are just a few of the characteristics associated with Invisalign. Using customized, transparent aligners that shift teeth into their proper place, they allow self-conscious adults and teens to work toward a straighter smile without enduring treatment that is visibly noticeable to others. These trays require 20-22 hours of wear time and must be switched out every few weeks to ensure everything moves forward correctly. By following the guidelines for treatment, you may see results in as little as 12-18 months.

Fastbraces

When the desire for a straighter smile cannot wait, an alternative solution may be available Fastbraces. Similar to traditional metal brackets and wires, these appliances produce faster results by moving the roots of teeth and their crowns at the same time. This is vastly different than traditional braces, which move these components separately. In the end, your treatment time will be significantly reduced, allowing you to start enjoying the benefits of a straighter smile much sooner.
