Benefits of Dental Implants

It is impossible to overlook the benefits that dental implants can provide. In addition to being considered the gold standard in tooth replacement, they offer patients the chance to recapture a more youthful appearance one that can be enjoyed for decades. But a more aesthetically pleasing smile isn’t the only advantage. Others include:

Day-to-Day Benefits

Let’s start with the benefits of dental implants that you’ll likely notice first:

  • Improved Bite Strength Apart from the standard caution dentists provide when it comes to eating hard and crunchy foods, there are virtually no food restrictions with dental implants. They act just like natural teeth, so you can enjoy your favorite meals without worrying about your teeth shifting out of place.
  • Clearer Communication Like many other patients, you may have run into trouble enunciating clearly in the weeks, months, and years following tooth loss . Fortunately, dental implants can replace one, a few, or an entire arch of teeth, restoring your normal speaking patterns in the process.
  • Easy Maintenance While other tooth-replacement options require special cleaning products, dental implants do not! Just commit to healthy habits at home, like brushing twice a day. Then, come to our office every six months so we can periodically examine the condition of your restoration.

Health Benefits

Dental implants in Riverdale Park can also have a positive impact on your oral and overall health by:

  • Preventing Dental Drift Even just one missing tooth can throw off the alignment of your entire bite. Fortunately, dental implants can fill any open spaces in your smile and, in the process, prevent dental drift.
  • Preserving More of Your Natural Smile Although dental bridges are effective, there is a downside: they require the teeth surrounding the gap to be altered. Luckily, that’s not the case for dental implants since they are self-supporting.
  • Allowing You to Eat More Nutrient-Dense Foods We mentioned above that dental implants can restore the strength of your bite. As a result, you can enjoy a wider variety of foods, which makes getting the vitamins, minerals, and nutrients that your body needs easier.

Long-Term Benefits

There are also several long-term benefits of dental implants in Riverdale Park, such as:

  • They Have a High Success Rate Upon initial placement, dental implants have a 95 % success rate a percentage that can remain just as high 10 years later if you commit to taking proper care of your new smile.
  • They Are Long-Lasting Dentures and dental bridges require replacement after a decade or so; however, dental implants have been known to last 30 years or more. Some cases indicate that these prosthetics can even last a lifetime!
  • They Help Prevent Tooth and Bone Loss Once placed within the jawbone, dental implants deliver stimulation to the area to prevent resorption. As a result, the chances of additional tooth and bone loss are lowered, allowing you to maintain a more supple and vibrant appearance.

The 4 Step Dental Implant Process

Dental implants are a complicated treatment with many moving parts, but the process boils down to four main phases consultation, surgery, healing, and restoration. And while the minor oral surgery makes the procedure difficult for many dentists to perform in-house, our team has it down to a science. At My Riverdale Dentist , we complete the entire process in-house, from the first consultation to the final restoration. That means no outside specialist, no extra travel, and no referral fees. If you’re curious about how dental implants work in Riverdale, keep reading below.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

Your treatment begins with a detailed consultation at our Riverdale Park office. During this visit, we’ll examine your mouth, take digital images, and review your medical history. Every piece of information we gather at this stage is necessary to confirm you’re a candidate, avoid complications, and ensure success.

Sometimes, we may recommend preliminary treatments, such as bone grafting or gum disease treatment to prepare your smile. If so, we’ll detail it in the comprehensive treatment plan you’ll receive before you leave our office.

Dental Implant Surgery

Next comes the placement of your dental implants. Because we perform this minor oral surgery right here at My Riverdale Dentist, you won’t need to go to an unfamiliar outside specialist. Instead, you’ll see friendly faces you can trust throughout your treatment.

After numbing the area and, if needed, providing sedation , we insert the implant posts into your jawbone at precise angles and depths. Thanks to the anesthetic, you won’t feel anything other than pressure from the instruments.

Once your implants are placed, you’ll be given detailed aftercare instructions to manage any soreness and protect the implant sites while they begin to heal.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment Placement

Over the next several months, a process called osseointegration occurs within the implant sites. During this time, your jawbone fuses with the titanium fixtures, creating a stable foundation similar to natural tooth roots.

We’ll schedule periodic checkups to monitor your progress and answer any questions you may have about your timeline or treatment in general. Once healing is complete, we’ll attach small connector pieces called abutments to the tops of the implants. These abutments will eventually hold your crown, bridge, or denture in place.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restorations

The final step is attaching your custom restoration. Using precise impressions and digital scans, we have a local partnered dental lab design customized prosthetics that match your natural teeth in color, shape, and size. When your restoration is ready, we secure it to the abutments and make any final adjustments to your bite.

You’ll leave our Riverdale Park office with teeth that look, feel, and perform just like the real thing. With proper care and regular checkups, your dental implants can last for decades if not a lifetime!

Missing One Tooth

A fixed bridge can replace a single missing tooth; however, it requires altering healthy abutment teeth on either side of the gap for it to remain in place. Instead of relying on these anchors to secure your new teeth, we can install a single titanium post into your jawbone and top it with a metal abutment and customized dental crown for a fully restored smile.

Missing Multiple Teeth

Replacing multiple missing teeth may have you thinking about whether to choose a fixed bridge or partial denture, but if you want something with more stability, opt for a restoration that’s held in place by dental implants. We can position two titanium posts into the exterior spaces before attaching a custom implant bridge or partial, effectively filling in the gap and making it easier to eat and speak.

Missing All Teeth

Dentures might be the first thing you think of when learning you need to replace an entire row of teeth; however, relying on natural suction and messy adhesive doesn’t have to be the answer. Instead, we can place between four and six dental implant posts along the arch to deliver maximum support to the newly crafted denture that we will attach. Not only does this create a complete and more confident appearance, but it also allows you to experience daily life with a set of teeth that look natural, feel comfortable, and work just like your regular ones.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

When you invest your valuable time and money on rebuilding your smile with dental implants, it’s only natural to want them to last. The good news is that they can last for 30 years or longer with the right habits in place. More specifically, we recommend the following:

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

All too often, patients assume that they don’t need to consistently brush, floss, and rinse with mouthwash since dental implants cannot decay. However, peri-implantitis (a form of gum disease) is one of the leading causes of failure. So, it’s crucial that you commit to a solid oral hygiene regimen at home. If you’re unsure which products to use, like which toothpaste is best, let us know! We’d be happy to answer any questions you have pertaining to dental care.

Eat a Healthy Diet

Another important aspect of dental implant care in Riverdale Park is being selective about what you eat. After all, even though hard pretzels, raw almonds, and tortilla chips are delicious, their crunchy consistency can cause your restoration to break. So, it’s best to incorporate lots of softer, more nutrient-dense foods into your diet, including steamed carrots, fresh bananas, cottage cheese, and plain yogurt. You should also drink plenty of water throughout the day (this will help prevent dry mouth).

Break Bad Habits

Of course, keeping your dental implants in pristine condition also comes down to what NOT to do. That starts with using tobacco products, since smoking has been linked to an increased risk of failure. You also should avoid chewing on ice cubes, biting your nails, or using your teeth as tools to open things.

Protect Your Dental Implants

Avoiding the previously mentioned habits will certainly help protect your dental implants from harm. However, if you like to stay active by playing pickleball, lifting weights, riding your bike, or running, then you also need to wear a mouthguard. If you don’t have one already, don’t buy one online. Instead, schedule an appointment at our dental office in Riverdale Park so we can have a custom one made.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Lastly, you need to schedule routine visits with your implant dentist in Riverdale Park. This is important for several reasons, starting with the fact that it allows us to periodically examine your teeth, your gums, and your dental implants. This also ensures that we can complete an oral cancer screening, remove built-up tartar, and answer any questions you have pertaining to your oral health.
