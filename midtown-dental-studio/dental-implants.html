The Dental Implant Treatment Process

First, you will visit Dr. David for a complimentary implant consultation, and he will determine if this treatment is right for you. If you’re approved for treatment, he will begin developing your treatment plan right away.

Your implant will be surgically implanted into your mouth a few weeks after your initial consultation. Dr. David will use a comprehensive treatment plan and guide to ensure the implant is placed perfectly.

Dental implants may be an option if you have missing teeth, healthy gums, and enough jawbone to support an implant. Your dentist will evaluate your oral health and goals to determine the right approach.

Mild swelling or tenderness is common after implant placement. Follow your dentist’s instructions, maintain good oral hygiene, and attend recommended follow-up visits to support proper healing and long-term implant health.

With proper care and regular dental visits, dental implants can provide long-lasting tooth replacement. The restoration attached to the implant may eventually need replacement due to normal wear.

What Affects the Cost of Dental Implants?

The cost of implants depends on the number of teeth being replaced, the type of restoration, and whether additional procedures such as extractions or bone grafting are needed. Dental insurance may provide limited coverage, so your out-of-pocket cost can vary.

Financing Options

Financing or payment plans may be available to help make dental implant treatment more manageable. Our team can review your options and explain expected costs before treatment begins.

Which Dental Implant Option Is Right for You?

✔ Single-Tooth Implants: Replace one missing tooth with an implant and custom crown while preserving neighboring teeth. ✔ Implant-Supported Bridges: Replace multiple missing teeth with a bridge secured by dental implants. ✔ Implant-Supported Dentures: Use implants to stabilize removable or fixed dentures for improved comfort, confidence, and function.
