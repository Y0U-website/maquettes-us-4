Accessibility Statement - Midtown Dental Studio

At Midtown Dental Studio, we are committed to ensuring our website and office environment are accessible and welcoming to all patients, including individuals with disabilities.

We believe everyone should be able to access quality dental information and care with ease, dignity, and independence.

Website Accessibility

We strive to make our website user-friendly and accessible for visitors of all abilities by working toward recognized accessibility standards and best practices.

Our ongoing accessibility efforts may include:

Compatibility with common screen readers and assistive technologies

We continuously review and improve our website experience to enhance accessibility for all users.

Office Accessibility

Midtown Dental Studio is committed to providing accessible dental care for every patient who visits our office.

We aim to accommodate individuals with mobility, hearing, vision, or other accessibility needs whenever possible. If you require assistance during your visit or would like to discuss accommodations ahead of your appointment, please contact our team.

Third-Party Content

While we strive to ensure accessibility across our website, some third-party tools or external links may not be fully accessible in every situation. We encourage users to contact us if they experience any difficulty accessing content or services.

Need Assistance?

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of our website, need assistance scheduling an appointment, or would like to request accommodations, we are happy to help.
