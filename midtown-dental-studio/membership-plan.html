Membership Plan FAQ

A dental membership plan is an in-house savings program offered by Midtown Dental Studio. It allows patients to pay a monthly or yearly fee in exchange for preventive care and discounts on treatments, without using traditional insurance.

Our membership plan covers essential preventive care, including professional cleanings, regular exams, fluoride treatment (or irrigation for adults), routine x-rays, and one emergency exam per year. It’s designed to help you maintain a healthy smile without relying on traditional insurance.

Unlike dental insurance, our plan has no waiting periods, no annual maximums, and no missing tooth clause. You can begin treatment immediately and receive care without worrying about coverage limits or denied claims.
