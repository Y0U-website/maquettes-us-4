Not too long ago, all fillings were metal. Although metal fillings were – and still are – effective to this day, they’re unsightly and can even be noticeable enough to potentially have a negative effect on one’s self-esteem.

But those were the old days. Dental technology has advanced to the point where tooth colored bonding and fillings are now possible, thereby eliminating a lot of the visual concerns about having a cavity filled or a broken tooth patched up.

For more information on the process, ease and benefits of the dental bonding and tooth colored fillings, contact our office today .

We will be happy to consult with you and get your smile back on the path to lighting up the room.

Bonding is a quick fix where we apply a tooth-colored composite resin to repair minor chips, cracks, or gaps.

They are metal-free composite fillings used to fix cavities. They blend right in, so no one can tell you had a cavity.
