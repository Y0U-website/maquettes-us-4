Dental Implants in Pleasanton, Dublin & Livermore, CA

What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts that serve as artificial tooth roots, which are surgically placed into your jawbone. These biocompatible implants fuse with the bone over time, creating a strong and stable foundation to support various dental restorations, such as crowns, bridges, or dentures. Dental implants are the gold standard for tooth replacement, mimicking the look, feel, and function of natural teeth.

Why Choose Implant Dentistry?

The Dental Implant Process Our skilled dental team ensures a comfortable and seamless dental implant journey:

  • Natural Aesthetics : Dental implants are custom-designed to blend seamlessly with your existing teeth, providing a natural and attractive smile.
  • Long-term Solution : With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, making them a durable and cost-effective option for tooth replacement.
  • Improved Functionality : Dental implant placement can replace missing teeth to restore full chewing ability and speech.
  • Preservation of Bone Density : Mouth dental implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss that often occurs with missing teeth and maintaining facial structure.
  • No Impact on Adjacent Teeth : Unlike traditional bridges, dental implants do not require the alteration of adjacent healthy teeth for support.
  • Initial Consultation : During your first visit, we assess your oral health and discuss your prosthetic teeth replacement goals to determine if dental implants are the right option for you.
  • Implant Placement : The dental implant is precisely placed into the jawbone through a minor surgical procedure. Local anesthesia ensures a painless and comfortable experience.
  • Healing and Osseointegration : Over the next few months, the implant fuses with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration, providing a sturdy anchor for the final restoration.
  • Abutment Placement : Once osseointegration is complete, an abutment is attached to the implant, connecting it to the dental restoration.
  • Custom Restoration : Our expert dental lab creates a custom crown , bridge , or denture to fit your unique smile, ensuring a natural and seamless appearance.

Treatment Areas for Dental Implants

  • For missing teeth : Missing teeth can result from tooth decay, gum disease, injury, or genetics. This can impact chewing, speech, and the alignment of remaining teeth.
  • For cracked teeth : Cracked teeth occur when teeth develop a split or crack due to trauma, grinding, or biting into hard objects. Cracks can cause pain and sensitivity, especially when chewing.
  • For tooth loss : Tooth loss is the complete detachment of a tooth from its socket, often due to decay, gum disease, or injury. It impacts chewing, speech, and smile aesthetics.
  • For ridge augmentation : Ridge augmentation is a surgical procedure to restore the natural contour of the jawbone after tooth loss or gum disease, often in preparation for dental implants.

Frequently Asked Questions How long do dental implants last? Dental implants can last anywhere from 10-30 years, with many lasting a lifetime if properly maintained. The longevity of your implant depends on your overall health, dental habits, bone density, and commitment to continued maintenance.

How painful is getting a dental implant?

Dental implant surgery itself is not painful due to the use of local anesthesia, but patients typically experience mild to moderate discomfort for 3-5 days after the procedure. Discomfort or pain can be easily managed with over-the-counter pain medications.

Should I get a dental implant or a bridge?

The choice depends on your specific situation, but implants generally offer better long-term results and provide superior durability.

Are you put to sleep for dental implants?

Most patients are not put fully to sleep for dental implants, but instead receive local anesthesia that numbs the area while keeping them awake. Depending on patient needs and anxiety levels, we offer a variety of options ranging from local anesthesia to general anesthesia.

Are dental implants safe?

Yes, dental implants are extremely safe when placed by an experienced dental professional. Implant technology is well-advanced, and the procedure has a high success rate, making dental implants a widely trusted tooth replacement treatment.

Who is a candidate for dental implants?

Ideal candidates for dental implants have healthy gums, sufficient jawbone density, and good overall oral health. If bone loss has occurred, bone grafting procedures can often make implants possible for more patients.

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Most dental insurance plans do not cover the full cost of dental implants. Some policies may partially cover components such as crowns or extractions, but it’s important to review your insurance policy - give us a call today!

What are the benefits of dental implants compared to dentures or bridges?

Dental implants prevent bone loss and preserve facial structure, unlike dentures or fixed bridges. They offer stable support, don’t slip or cause discomfort, and allow you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.
