Achieving a beautiful, straight smile

What is Orthodontic Treatment?

Orthodontic treatment is a specialized branch of dentistry focused on diagnosing, preventing, and correcting dental irregularities like crooked teeth. It involves the use of various orthodontic appliances, including braces, clear aligners (such as Invisalign® ), and/or other specialized appliances for straightening teeth, correcting bite issues, and achieving an optimal, healthy smile and functional bite.

When is Orthodontic Treatment Needed?

Orthodontic treatment may be recommended for the following conditions:

Facial Imbalance : In some cases, clinical orthodontics can help address facial imbalances caused by jaw discrepancies.

Malocclusions : Malocclusions, also known as “bad bites,” refer to misalignments of the teeth and jaw, such as overbites, underbites, crossbites, and open bites.

Crowded or Spaced Teeth : Teeth that are too crowded or widely spaced can be straightened and aligned for improved aesthetics and easier oral hygiene.

Improper Tooth Eruption : Orthodontic intervention can guide erupting permanent teeth into their proper positions, preventing future dental issues.

The Orthodontic Treatment Process: Our orthodontic team ensures a comprehensive and personalized treatment process:

  • Initial Consultation : During your first visit, we conduct a thorough examination, including X-rays and digital scans, to assess your orthodontic needs.
  • Custom Treatment Plan : Based on your unique requirements and smile goals, we create a customized treatment plan tailored to achieve optimal results.
  • Orthodontic Appliances : We offer a range of orthodontic appliances, including traditional metal braces, clear aligners (such as Invisalign® ), and other specialized appliances, depending on your specific case.
  • Treatment Progress : Regular check-up appointments allow us to monitor your progress and make any necessary adjustments to your orthodontic appliances for better oral health.
  • Retainers : After completing the active phase of orthodontic treatment, we provide you with retainers to maintain the newly achieved alignment and prevent teeth from shifting.
  • Enhanced Aesthetics : Modern dentistry and orthodontic treatment improves the alignment of your teeth, enhancing the overall aesthetics of your smile.
  • Improved Oral Health : Straight teeth from traditional braces are easier to clean and maintain, reducing the risk of dental issues such as cavities and gum disease.
  • Optimal Function : Correcting bite issues and aligning the jaw enhances chewing efficiency and speech clarity with advanced dental care treatment options.
  • Boosted Confidence : A beautiful and aligned smile cared for by a renowned dental professional can boost your self-confidence and leave a lasting positive impression.

Treatment Areas for Orthodontics

  • For misaligned teeth: Misaligned teeth occur when the upper and lower teeth don’t align properly. This condition can affect chewing, cause jaw pain, and lead to an uneven bite or aesthetic concerns.
  • For overbite: An overbite is when the upper front teeth overlap significantly with the lower front teeth. This can cause wear on teeth, jaw pain, and sometimes difficulties with speech or eating.
  • For underbite: An underbite is a condition where the lower front teeth protrude beyond the upper front teeth. It can cause chewing and speaking issues and might lead to jaw strain.
  • For crowded teeth: Crowded teeth occur when there isn’t enough space in the jaw for teeth to align properly. This can complicate oral hygiene and increase the risk of decay and gum disease.
  • For gapped teeth: Gapped teeth, or diastema, refer to spaces between teeth. While often harmless, gaps can sometimes affect bite function or cause food to get stuck between teeth.
  • For dental drift: Dental drift refers to the gradual movement of teeth from their original position, often caused by tooth loss or periodontal issues.
  • For headaches: Dental issues like bruxism, TMJ disorder, or misaligned teeth can contribute to chronic headaches and discomfort in the jaw area.

Frequently Asked Questions What is the cheapest option for braces? Traditional metal braces are typically the most affordable option for orthodontic treatment. They are durable, effective, and less expensive compared to other types of braces.

How long do braces last?

Most people wear braces for 1-3 years, but the exact duration depends on the severity of misalignment, patient age, and how well orthodontic instructions are followed.

Whats the difference between braces and Invisalign?

Braces use metal brackets and wires that are attached to teeth to shift their position, while Invisalign uses clear, removable plastic aligners that gradually shift teeth overtime. Braces tend to be more effective for severe misalignment cases, while Invisalign is preferred for mild to moderate corrections.

Do invisible braces actually work?

For those with mild to moderate misalignment, invisible braces (Invisalign) are highly effective with a success rate of approximately 96 %.

When is orthodontic treatment recommended?

Treatment may be recommended for people with bite issues, including overbite, underbite, crossbite, or open bite, crowded or widely spaced teeth, improper tooth eruption, or jaw misalignment that affects function or aesthetics.

What orthodontic treatment options are available?

Dublin Corners Dental offers several appliance options depending on your needs, including traditional metal braces, clear aligners such as Invisalign, and other specialized orthodontic appliances.

How does the treatment process work?

The process begins with an initial consultation that includes a full exam and X-rays or digital scans to assess your teeth and bite. A custom treatment plan is developed, and regular check-ups are scheduled to monitor progress and make adjustments. Once active treatment is complete, retainers are provided to maintain the new alignment.

Is Invisalign a good alternative to braces?

Yes. Clear aligners such as Invisalign are removable and less noticeable than traditional braces. They are a good option if you want a more discreet treatment, and your orthodontic issues can be corrected with aligners.

Will orthodontic treatment improve both appearance and oral health?

Yes. Straightening teeth and correcting bite problems can enhance the appearance of your smile, improve chewing and biting function, and make teeth easier to clean, which supports long-term oral health.

Do all orthodontic cases require the same appliance?

No. The best appliance depends on individual needs, the severity of misalignment, bite issues, jaw structure, and personal preferences for appearance and comfort. The orthodontic team will recommend the most appropriate option during your evaluation.

What happens after braces or aligners are removed?

After completing active orthodontic treatment, retainers are provided to help maintain teeth alignment and prevent shifting over time.

Can orthodontic treatment be done at any age?

Yes. Orthodontic treatment, including braces or clear aligners, is appropriate for both younger patients with developing teeth and adults who want to improve alignment, function, or aesthetics.

How do I start the process if I am interested?

You can start by scheduling an orthodontic consultation at Dublin Corners Dental. During this visit, the team will examine your teeth and bite, take any needed X-rays or scans, and discuss treatment options to develop a personalized plan.
