Why our care feels different

All under one roof

With advanced implant training, cosmetic expertise, and everything in between, Dr. Ingy keeps even complex cases in-house.

Only what you need

You won’t be rushed or sold into something you don’t need. We explain your options clearly so you can make decisions that feel right.

No judgement, ever

If it’s been years, you’re not the only one. We meet you where you are with no lectures and no pressure.

Modern tools make it easier

To put it simple, technology makes your care smoother and more comfortable. We use 3D imaging and digital scanning to plan precisely and skip the messy impressions.

Dr. Ingy Alhelawe

Dr. Ingy was born in Alexandria, Egypt and moved to New Jersey at the age of two. Raised by parents who valued education deeply, she always knew she wanted a career that combined science, precision, and helping people feel more confident.

She earned her undergraduate degree from Rutgers University, studying Biological Sciences, Psychology, and Middle Eastern Studies before completing her Master’s in Biomedical Sciences at UMDNJ. She went on to receive her DMD from Boston University’s Goldman School of Dental Medicine.

Her postgraduate General Practice Residency at Einstein Medical Center provided advanced training in implant dentistry and complex restorative care under a team of experienced clinicians. That experience shaped the kind of practice she would eventually build — one that balances technical precision with genuine compassion.

Now, with over 10 years of experience, Dr. Ingy is especially passionate about helping patients who may feel anxious or who haven’t been to the dentist in years. She’s all about treating patients the way she would treat her own family.

Outside the office, she’s an avid traveler and foodie who loves exploring new cultures and new restaurants.

Dr. Mary Ann Halim

Dr. Mary is a general dentist at Bayville Dental Arts, dedicated to providing gentle, attentive care to patients of all ages. She earned her dental degree from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in 2023.

Dr. Mary is known for her compassion and patience with patients, paired with a meticulous attention to detail in every procedure. Outside the practice, she’s a lifelong painter and drawer, loves to travel, and spends her free time with her husband and daughter.

Dr. Mary looks forward to welcoming you to Bayville Dental Arts and helping you achieve a healthy, confident smile.
