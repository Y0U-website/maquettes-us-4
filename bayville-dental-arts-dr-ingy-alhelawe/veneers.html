The benefits of veneers

We plan before we prep Your bite, facial balance, and long-term health are evaluated before anything touches your teeth because cosmetic work should never be rushed.

Not everyone needs ten veneers Sometimes it’s two. Sometimes it’s six. Sometimes it’s not veneers at all. Treatment is customized to what actually makes sense for you.

No one will ask what you had done When veneers are planned properly, they don’t look obvious. They just look like your smile, slightly brighter, more even, more polished.

Our treatments

Heading Text

Composite fillings

Start with what’s bothering you

We begin with a conversation about what you’d like to change. Dr. Ingy will evaluate your teeth, your bite, and your overall oral health before recommending veneers.

Plan before anything changes

Using digital smile design, we map out your proposed results so you can see the direction before treatment begins. We discuss how many veneers make sense, what shade works for you, and what the timeline will look like. Nothing moves forward without your full understanding.

Conservative preparation

If veneers are the right fit, we prepare your teeth carefully and conservatively. The goal is to preserve healthy structure while creating space for a natural-looking result. Temporary veneers may be placed so you can preview the feel of your new smile.

Final placement and refinements

Once your custom veneers are ready, they’re bonded precisely and checked for fit, balance, and comfort. We make small refinements if needed so your bite feels natural, and you’ll leave with a smile that looks intentional.
