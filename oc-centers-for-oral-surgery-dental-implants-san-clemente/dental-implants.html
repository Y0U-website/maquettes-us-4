Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants act as artificial tooth roots that are securely placed in the jawbone, providing an exceptional combination of strength, stability, and natural-looking results. With proper care, implants can last for decades and may even last a lifetime.

Because they are designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth, dental implants can help improve your ability to speak, eat, and smile with confidence. They also eliminate many of the concerns associated with removable tooth replacement options, such as gum irritation or movement while chewing.

How is a Dental Implant Placed?

The dental implant process begins with a consultation at one of our Orange County locations. Our experienced oral surgeons will evaluate your oral health, discuss your goals, and develop a personalized treatment plan.

During the procedure, the implant is placed into the jawbone to serve as a replacement tooth root. A healing period follows, allowing the implant to fuse with the surrounding bone through a process called osseointegration. Depending on your bone structure and individual needs, bone grafting may be recommended to create a stable foundation for the implant.

Once healing is complete, a custom restoration can be placed to restore the appearance and function of your smile.

How Long Does Dental Implant Surgery Take?

The length of dental implant surgery varies depending on the number of implants being placed, the condition of the jawbone, and whether additional procedures are needed.

Our team at Orange County Centers for Oral Surgery & Implants prioritizes your comfort and safety throughout every stage of treatment. After the implant has healed, a custom crown or other restoration can be attached. A temporary restoration may also be used during the healing period when appropriate.

What Can I Eat After Dental Implant Surgery?

After surgery, patients are typically advised to eat soft foods to avoid disturbing the treatment area. As healing progresses, you can gradually return to your normal diet based on the recommendations provided by your oral surgeon.

Once fully restored, dental implants can provide strong, stable chewing power, allowing you to comfortably enjoy many of your favorite foods again.

What Maintenance Is Required?

Dental implants are designed to be relatively easy to maintain. Regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental checkups are essential for protecting the implant and the surrounding gum tissue.

Whether your treatment includes a single implant, an implant-supported bridge, or implant-supported dentures, our team will provide personalized instructions to help you maintain your results.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Dental implants are known for their longevity. Many implants can last 25 years or longer, and with excellent oral hygiene and regular professional care, they may last a lifetime.

The restoration attached to the implant may eventually require replacement due to normal wear, but the implant itself is designed to provide lasting support.
