Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a long-lasting solution for replacing missing teeth. Unlike dentures or bridges, implants integrate directly with your jawbone, providing unmatched stability and strength. Benefits include:

  • A natural look and feel
  • No slipping or discomfort
  • Preservation of bone and facial structure
  • Long-term durability with proper care
  • Improved chewing, speech, and overall confidence

Types of Implant Services We Offer

Single Tooth Replacement Perfect for replacing a missing tooth without affecting neighboring teeth.

Implant-Supported Bridges Restore multiple missing teeth with a stable, implant-anchored solution.

Full-Arch Implants (All-on-4/6) A complete set of teeth secured with just four to six implants—ideal for full-mouth reconstruction.

Bone Grafting and Sinus Lifts For patients who’ve lost bone density, Dr. Etemad offers advanced grafting procedures to ensure a strong foundation for implants.

Are Dental Implants Right for You?

During your consultation, Dr. Etemad will conduct a comprehensive exam, take 3D scans, and discuss your goals to determine if implants are the right choice for you.

  • One or more missing teeth
  • Healthy gums and adequate bone support
  • A commitment to good oral hygiene
  • No untreated chronic conditions affecting healing
