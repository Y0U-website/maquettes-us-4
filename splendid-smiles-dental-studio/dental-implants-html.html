What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small prosthetics made of titanium and placed within the jawbone to serve as the foundation for a fully restored smile. Delivering the same bone stimulation as natural tooth roots, dental implants fuse with the bone and support custom restorations that fill in the spaces left behind by missing teeth. They’re known to last decades or a lifetime with regular maintenance and are the closest alternative to natural teeth.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

Unlike traditional restorations like dentures and dental bridges, getting dental implants requires a unique, multi-step process which spans several months. Although this may seem like a long time, the benefits you’ll receive from this treatment are well worth the investment.

At Splendid Smiles Dental Studio , we’re proud to complete the entire dental implant treatment process right here in our Warrenton office instead of having to refer you to an outside specialist. This means you can expect the same skilled, compassionate care from our friendly, familiar team!

Although every patient’s dental implant journey will be slightly different, there are four main steps you can expect to take along the way: the initial consultation, surgery, osseointegration, and of course, the delivery of your final restoration. Here’s a closer look at how dental implants work in Warrenton.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

At your initial dental implant consultation, your dentist will review your oral health and medical history to determine if you are a good candidate for treatment. If necessary, we can work with you to complete any preliminary procedures like gum disease therapy, tooth extractions, or bone grafting. Next, we can move on to planning the rest of your dental implant treatment, including walking you through what to expect step-by-step, as well as the timeline and total cost.

Dental Implant Surgery

At Splendid Smiles Dental Studio, we proudly place our patients’ dental implants right here in our Warrenton office. This means you won’t need to drive around from practice to practice, file multiple claims, or place your trust in an unfamiliar dental team.

On the day of your dental implant surgery, your dentist will first numb your mouth to ensure you are comfortable during the procedure. We also offer sedation options upon request to help keep you calm and relaxed. Once numb, your dentist will make an incision in the gums, insert your dental implant post(s)into your jawbone, close the gums, and place protective caps over the dental implant posts.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment Placement

Osseointegration is the healing period where your dental implant fuses with your jawbone. This process can take up to six months and will vary depending on the patient. Osseointegration is essential for dental implant success, as the implant must be strong and secure in the jaw before it can be used as intended.

In some cases, your dentist may be able to place the dental implant abutments on the posts during the first surgery so you will not require a second procedure; however, this will depend on your individual circumstances and will be discussed by your dentist at your initial consultation. If your abutments cannot be placed at the first procedure, a second procedure will be scheduled once your mouth has healed. This will require additional incisions to be made in the gums, but the healing time will be much shorter and less involved than your initial procedure.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restorations

Finally, you’ll return one last time to Splendid Smiles Dental Studio to have your final restoration(s) placed. These may be in the form of a crown, bridge, or denture, depending on how many teeth you’re replacing. Before you go, we’ll check to make sure your bite is comfortable, and if everything looks and feels great, you’ll be ready to go show off and enjoy all the incredible benefits of your newly rebuilt smile!

Benefits of Dental Implants

The benefits of dental implants cannot be overlooked, especially when compared to dentures or dental bridges. When choosing this type of tooth replacement , you can expect the following noteworthy benefits:

Day-to-Day Benefits

Once you’ve healed from the procedure and your restoration is securely in place, you’ll enjoy these benefits of dental implants in Warrenton:

  • Restored Bite Force Since dental implants are secured to your jawbone and topped with a custom-made, high-quality restoration, you won’t be stuck eating the same soft, bland foods on repeat. Instead, you’ll be able to enjoy all of your favorites!
  • Greater Confidence Oftentimes, patients notice a sunken-in, hollow appearance following tooth loss. Fortunately, dental implants can replace any number of missing teeth and are extremely lifelike. As a result, you’ll enjoy a boost to your self-esteem!
  • Clearer Communication Without a full row of teeth, you may develop a lisp or have a hard time enunciating clearly. The good news is that dental implants can fill in the open spaces, restoring your normal speaking patterns in the process.

Health Benefits

Dental implants can also benefit your oral and overall health by:

  • Preserving More of Your Natural Smile While dental bridges require the teeth surrounding the gap to be altered, that’s not the case for dental implants. Instead, we can insert the implant post into the open space and secure a custom restoration on top!
  • Preventing Dental Drift Dental drift is when the teeth surrounding the gap begin to move, negatively impacting the alignment of your bite in the process. Dental implants can help prevent that from happening by filling any open spaces in your smile.
  • Being Easy to Maintain Unlike other tooth-replacement options, dental implants don’t require any additional instruments or products for maintenance! Just use the same best practices you use to maintain your natural teeth, like brushing twice a day.

Long-Term Benefits

Dental implants also offer several long-term benefits, including:

  • They Are Cost-Effective Oftentimes, patients assume that dental implants will break the bank. However, they come with several unique benefits, including a long lifespan. As a result, they often end up being a great choice for a patient’s smile and their wallet!
  • They Have a Long Lifespan Speaking of their long lifespan, dental implants can last for 30 years or more as long as you commit to taking proper care of them (i.e., brushing your teeth twice a day, wearing a mouthguard during sports, eating mostly healthy, well-balanced meals).
  • They Help Prevent Bone Loss Other tooth-replacement options, like dentures, rest against your gums. Dental implants, on the other hand, are anchored to your jawbone. The stimulation this provides helps support this part of your facial structure.

Dental Implant Post-Op Instructions

Now that you’ve had your dental implants surgically placed, it’s time for the recovery process! Thankfully, it’s nothing to worry about. Below, we’ll share some dental implant post-op instructions in Warrenton as well as some additional information about what you can expect during this time, including which symptoms are normal and what you can do to help your smile heal. Should you ever experience any intense, abnormal pain or any other strange side effects that aren’t mentioned here, don’t hesitate to give us a call for assistance.

What to Do Directly After Dental Implant Surgery

Following your dental implant procedure , your biggest priority is to leave the forming blood clot(s) on your incision site(s) untouched so that the healing process can happen smoothly. To that end, please be sure to follow these important rules:

  • Don’t spit, instead use tissues or swallow saliva.
  • Never drink from a straw until your dentist has given you the green light.
  • Don’t smoke for at least the first 24 hours, or longer if possible.
  • Avoid touching your incision site(s) with your fingers and tongue.

Common Side Effects When Recovering from Dental Implant Placement

It is completely normal to experience some mild side effects, especially during the first few days after your dental implant surgery, including:

Each of these side effects should be temporary and gradually subside with time. If this is ever not the case, please let us know.

  • Intermittent bleeding, which can go on for several days and be lessened by gently biting on gauze.
  • Swelling is common over the first 72 hours and could last for up to a week. You can apply warm or cold compresses to help.
  • General discomfort can be alleviated with prescription or over-the-counter pain medication.

Your Diet After Dental Implant Surgery

In the first few days after your dental implant surgery, we recommend sticking to a softer diet, including the following:

You can return to your normal diet as soon as you feel up to it; however, we recommend that you limit any overly crunchy or tough foods at first and avoid directly chewing on your dental implant site(s) for as long as possible.

  • Mashed potatoes
  • Yogurt
  • Ice cream
  • Pudding
  • Soup (not too hot)
  • Scrambled eggs
  • Pasta
  • Hummus

Post-Op Health & Oral Hygiene

On the day after your dental implant surgery, it is okay to brush your teeth as usual but be very careful when brushing around the surgical site(s).

Rinse your mouth two to three times a day (ideally after eating) with saltwater. You can also use prescription mouthwash if you have it but be sure to avoid any products with high levels of alcohol like Listerine or Scope.

What to Do After Your New Teeth Are Attached

Congratulations! The hardest part of dental implant treatment is now officially behind you! Next, you’ll return to our office to receive your new crown, bridge, or denture. When these prosthetics are attached to the implant(s), you may experience some minor, temporary sensitivity. Thankfully, this should be very easy to control with an OTC pain reliever. There should be no swelling, bleeding, or extensive recovery at this stage. In fact, you should be ready to show off your gorgeous new smile that very same day!

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

After investing your valuable time and money to rebuild your smile following tooth loss , it’s only natural to want your dental implants to last. Fortunately, many patients enjoy theirs for 30+ years! The key is prioritizing healthy habits and breaking bad ones. More specifically, we recommend:

Commit to a Solid Oral Hygiene Routine At Home

Sometimes, patients assume that they can let their at-home oral hygiene regimen slip a bit. After all, dental implants can’t decay, right? While that’s true, your natural teeth still can. Plus, one of the leading causes of dental implant failure is peri-implantitis (a form of gum disease). So, it’s extremely important that you incorporate good habits into your daily routine, like brushing your teeth each morning and evening with a soft-bristle toothbrush and fluoridated toothpaste.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Another important aspect of dental implant care in Warrenton is scheduling (and attending!) a checkup and cleaning every six months. These routine visits are crucial for several reasons, starting with the fact that they help us catch oral cancer in the early stages. They also allow us to periodically examine the condition of your dental implant, take a look at your gums, and remove built-up plaque and tartar.

Eat Mostly Healthy, Well-Balanced Meals

Since dental implants are crafted from only the best materials, and they get their strength from your jawbone, you’ll be able to enjoy all of your favorite foods. However, it’s still important to eat mostly healthy, well-balanced meals that are filled with cooked vegetables, seasonal fruits, healthy fats, and lean protein. Not only will this ensure that your body gets the nutrients, vitamins, and minerals it needs, but it will also reduce your risk of developing tooth decay or gum disease.

Break Harmful Habits

Whether you have one dental implant or an entire arch, it’s crucial that you break harmful habits, like smoking, biting your fingernails, chewing on ice cubes, or using your teeth as tools to open things. This is important because, like your natural teeth, your dental implants can break with enough pressure. So, you need to avoid doing anything that increases the risk of them chipping or cracking.

Protect Your Dental Implants

Do you like to stay active by playing tennis, riding your bike, or lifting weights? If so, we recommend wearing a mouthguard. This will ensure that your teeth, your gums, and your dental implants are protected from impact from a rogue ball, an accidental fall, or constant clenching. If you don’t have one already, then give our team at Splendid Smiles Dental Studio a call so we can have a custom one made for you.

Important reminder: You should also wear a nightguard if you grind or clench your teeth at night!

Dental Implant FAQs

Are Dental Implants Removable?

Some patients in Warrenton wonder whether dental implants function like dentures, which are intended to be removed and reinserted regularly for cleanings. However, this treatment is unique because implants are the only restorations placed in your jaw and fuse with the bone to become a more permanent part of your mouth.

Unless you’ve opted for a specific type of implant dentures, which can sometimes be removable, usually these prosthetics are not meant to come out once they’re cemented in place.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Dental implants’ unique placement in your jawbone offers several advantages over alternative treatments, such as dentures and dental bridges, including enhanced longevity. They’re so firmly rooted in your mouth that they’re unlikely to become destabilized or dislodged.

As a result, these replacement teeth often last 20+ years, but that’s only as long as they’re cared for correctly. They can’t develop cavities on their own, but if the surrounding bone or gum tissue develops disease or decay, they could fail. You can help maintain them by brushing and flossing twice daily, avoiding overly hard, sticky, and sugary foods, and visiting our team every 6 months for a routine checkup and cleaning.

Does Getting Dental Implants Hurt?

It’s natural to feel a little bit nervous about the dental implant procedure because it involves oral surgery, which has the potential to hurt. But our compassionate providers understand that you might be anxious, and we will do everything we can to help you feel calm and comfortable throughout your appointment.

For instance, we use state-of-the-art equipment to place and restore dental implants in the office for a more convenient, streamlined experience. Then, we’ll numb your mouth with a local anesthetic before we begin. We also offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) and oral conscious sedation as a pill that can help you feel at ease while we place your new teeth.

How Successful Are Dental Implants?

You might hesitate to schedule your implant consultation if you’re worried you’ll undergo the procedure only for your restorations to fail right away. Thankfully, this procedure has an incredibly high success rate of over 90 %, meaning that most patients won’t experience complications, as long as they follow the provided post-operative instructions.

With that said, these restorations are not invincible, and their success can depend significantly on different factors unique to your circumstances. Certain habits, like ignoring your twice-daily oral hygiene routine, biting fingernails, using teeth as tools to open packages, or smoking cigarettes or vaping, can all contribute to potential failure.
