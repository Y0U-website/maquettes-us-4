Meet Our Warrenton, VA Dentist, Dr. Sachi Brahmbhatt

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Originally from Ahmedabad, India just like her husband, Dr. Nayak and Dr. Brahmbhatt have a young baby boy. She grew up as an only child with her mother, who is a retired schoolteacher, and her father, who is a retired bank clerk. She has always been known for her bubbly personality and good sense of humor. In her free time, Dr. Brahmbhatt enjoys traveling to different states and hiking. In fact, she goes on a hike almost every weekend, with some of her favorite places being Shenandoah National Park and Billy Goat Trail. She also loves playing badminton and tennis.
