Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are an excellent way to protect your teeth from cavities and long-term enamel wear. While sealants are commonly recommended for children, they’re also a smart preventive option for teens and adults who want to reduce their risk of tooth decay — especially in the deep grooves of the back teeth where brushing alone may not reach as effectively.

The process is quick, comfortable, and non-invasive. We apply a thin layer of tooth-colored resin to your molars, sealing off the tiny pits and grooves where bacteria and plaque tend to build up. Then, a gentle curing light hardens the material to form a smooth, protective barrier that can last for years, helping keep your smile healthier between dental visits.

Night Guards

Unconscious teeth grinding and clenching — known as bruxism — often happens during sleep, and many patients don’t realize it’s occurring until symptoms appear. Over time, grinding can wear down enamel, cause jaw pain and headaches, and even lead to cracked or damaged teeth. A custom night guard offers a simple, effective solution by creating a comfortable barrier between your teeth to prevent grinding-related damage.

Our custom-fit night guards are designed to match your bite and can be made from different materials depending on the severity of your grinding. With consistent use, you can protect your teeth, reduce tension and jaw discomfort, and wake up feeling refreshed.

We proudly provide custom night guards for patients in Dilworth, Sedgefield, South End, and throughout Charlotte. If you&#x27;re ready to relieve discomfort and protect your smile, schedule your visit today.

TMJ/TMD Treatment

Does your jaw click or pop when you open your mouth? Do you regularly experience soreness, facial tension, headaches, or difficulty enjoying your favorite foods? These symptoms may indicate TMJ/TMD (temporomandibular joint disorder), which affects the joint that connects your jaw to your skull.

The good news — TMJ/TMD is treatable. We offer conservative, minimally invasive treatment options designed to relieve jaw pain, reduce inflammation, and restore natural movement. Treatment may include custom night guards, bite adjustments, muscle-relaxation strategies, and personalized home-care recommendations.

You don’t have to live with jaw discomfort. If you&#x27;re noticing jaw pain, popping, or ongoing tension, reach out to schedule a consultation. We&#x27;re here to help you find relief and get back to living — and smiling — with comfort and confidence.
