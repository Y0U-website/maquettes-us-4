FAQs

What does a dental hygienist do? Dental hygienists are skilled in identifying early signs of oral conditions such as gingivitis and addressing them with preventive treatments. They also provide valuable guidance on effective dental care practices to help you maintain optimal oral health.

How often should I go to a dental hygienist? Generally, it’s recommended that individuals visit their dentist twice a year. During these routine checkups, both the dentist and the hygienist conduct thorough examinations. Regular visits not only ensure professional cleaning but also enable early detection of potential dental issues, helping to maintain optimal oral health.

What is a comprehensive dental examination? A thorough dental checkup involves examining each tooth for signs of disease and decay, as well as assessing the health of your gums and the bone structure supporting your teeth. This comprehensive evaluation is crucial for maintaining your overall oral, head, and neck health. While first-time patients typically undergo this assessment, it’s also important for long-term patients to have regular checkups to ensure ongoing oral health.
