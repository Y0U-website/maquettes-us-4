  • Intrinsic Stains: These form within the tooth’s inner structure, called the dentin. Causes can include certain medications, trauma to a tooth that causes it to darken, excessive fluoride exposure during childhood, or simply the natural aging process, which causes enamel to thin and the yellowish dentin to show through.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is purple teeth whitening? This trend uses color theory, the purple tint is meant to cancel out yellow tones on the surface for an immediate, but very temporary, brightening effect. It does not bleach or whiten the tooth enamel.

What is teeth whitening in office bleaching? This is the professional, in-chair whitening service we offer at our New Port Richey office. It involves the controlled application of a high-concentration bleaching gel for fast, dramatic results.

Should I brush my teeth before using whitening strips? Yes. Brushing and flossing before applying any strips removes plaque and debris, allowing for more even contact with the tooth surface.

Are whitening strips safe for my enamel and gum health? Yes, whitening strips are safe when used as directed. They contain mild bleaching agents that won’t harm enamel if not overused. However, leaving them on too long or using them too often can cause tooth sensitivity or gum irritation. Avoid letting the strips touch your gums, and wait about 30 minutes before brushing after use. If you have sensitive teeth or gum issues, consult your dentist before starting treatment.
