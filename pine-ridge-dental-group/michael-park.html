Dr. Michael Park

Meet Dr. Michael Park, DDS

Patient Care Philosophy

Dr. Park believes in treating every patient like family, offering personalized care in a warm and comfortable environment. He is dedicated to delivering exceptional dental care tailored to each individual’s needs, ensuring that patients feel relaxed and informed throughout their dental journey. Whether it’s routine care or advanced procedures, Dr. Park’s goal is to create a positive and lasting experience for every patient.

Building Patient Relationships

Dr. Park values long-term relationships with his patients, taking the time to listen to their concerns and work together on treatment plans that prioritize their dental health and overall well-being. His friendly and approachable demeanor, combined with his patient-centered approach, has helped him build trust and loyalty among his patients over the years.

Life Outside Dentistry

Outside of his professional life, Dr. Park enjoys spending quality time with his wife, Julie, and their three grown children. Now that they are empty nesters, Dr. Park and Julie are enjoying life’s adventures together. He is passionate about sports, fitness, and the outdoors, and loves activities like hiking, fishing, and golfing. Dr. Park’s love for an active lifestyle is mirrored in the care and energy he brings to his dental practice.
