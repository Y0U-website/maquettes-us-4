Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept my insurance?

We are in-network with most major PPO dental insurance providers. Please contact our office to verify your specific plan, and we’ll help you maximize your benefits.

Do you provide emergency dental care?

Yes, we offer same-day emergency dental appointments for urgent issues like tooth pain, broken teeth, or dental injuries. Call us right away if you need immediate care.

What should I do if I have dental anxiety?

We understand that many patients feel anxious about dental visits. Our team offers a range of sedation options, including nitrous oxide (laughing gas) and oral sedation, to help you feel comfortable and relaxed.

How can I schedule an appointment?

You can schedule an appointment by calling our office or using our online scheduling tool. We’re here to help find a time that works for you.
