What To Expect From A Routine Cleaning & Exam

During your routine cleaning and exam with Chester Dental, you will be brought back into our exam room, where our hygienist or dental assistant will check to see if you need any x-rays or photos of your mouth taken.

Once this is complete, they will look over the x-rays and your mouth for any potential problems or concerns. Some examples include cavities, gingivitis, damage to your teeth, toothaches, and more. For children and teens, they will check for any teeth growing in molars and wisdom teeth.

Then they will clean and floss your teeth, scrape and remove any tartar and plaque buildup, and get your teeth fresh and clean. The hygienist will provide your information to the dentist, who will come in and speak with you about any concerns and recommendations for your oral health.

After that, you will be all good to go; but don’t forget to schedule your six-month checkup before you leave!
