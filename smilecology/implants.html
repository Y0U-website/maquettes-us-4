The Smilecology Dental Implant Process

We take the time to offer a comprehensive, detailed method to ensure that each of our patients receives optimal care and results

Your treatment begins with a comprehensive consultation. Dr. Phan will examine your mouth condition, digitally X-ray it, and even 3D scan the architecture and density of your jawbone. We will discuss your expectations, review your health record, and determine whether you’re a good implant candidate.

Every smile is unique. We will customize our treatment to you—one implant, several implants, or a full arch of implant-supported dentures.

After creating the plan, the titanium implant posts are inserted in your jawbone under local anesthetic for your comfort. Precision-guided methods are used for the most accurate and proper placement and for the fastest healing rate.

Healing time varies but is usually 3–6 months. The implant stabilizes with the bone to provide a hard, stable base. During this period, temporary restoration or crowns can be delivered for function and aesthetics.

When the healing process is finished, a crown, bridge, or denture that appears to be your own is cemented over the implant post. All restorations are carefully designed to match your natural teeth in shape, size, and color, both functionally and cosmetically.

Advanced Technology for Greater Success

Smilecology doctors desire to provide you with the highest quality of care with the latest technology available to create the highest accuracy, safety, and success of our implant procedures.

Our in-office CEREC facility allows us to design, mill, and cement ceramic restorations all in one session. This decreases treatment time significantly and creates a perfect fit for your implant crown.

We utilize the most advanced imaging to create high-definition, 3D pictures of your jaw and mouth. This allows us to plan implant placement precisely and minimize complications.
