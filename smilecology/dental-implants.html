Dental implants

  • Implants both look and feel more natural than other restorative choices.
  • Implants provide a biting force that is equal to or greater than the bite force of natural teeth.
  • Dental implants allow you to eat anything and say anything with ease.
  • Dental implants don’t compromise your healthy neighboring teeth, because they don’t rely on them for support.
  • While traditional bridges are only meant to last for ten years or less, dental implants can last a lifetime!

Post-Treatment Care

Think of your new dental implants as the same as your natural teeth. They require diligent brushing and flossing, along with twice yearly dental checkups. The better you care for your new smile, the longer it will last!
