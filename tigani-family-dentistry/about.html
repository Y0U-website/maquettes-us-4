About Us

Rooted in Family. Driven by Compassion. Focused on You.

At Tigani Family Dentistry, we’ve been proudly serving the Pike Creek and Wilmington communities since 1975. As a second-generation, family-owned dental practice, we believe in building relationships just as much as we believe in delivering excellent dental care. We take the time to get to know you—your goals, your concerns, your smile—and tailor your care to fit your life.

From the moment you walk in, you’ll feel the difference: a welcoming space, a team that remembers your name, and a level of attention that puts your comfort first. Whether you’re here for a routine cleaning, a full restoration, or advanced treatments for snoring or sleep apnea, we’re committed to helping you feel confident in your care.

At the heart of everything we do is a deep dedication to doing what’s best for our patients. You’re not just a chart here—you’re part of our dental family.

We’d Love to Welcome You to the Family Whether you’re new to the area, looking for a dental team that truly listens, or simply overdue for a checkup—we’re here for you.

Let’s get you smiling with comfort and confidence.

Why Choose Tigani Family Dentistry?

We know you have options when it comes to dental care. Here’s why generations of families continue to trust us with their smiles:

Family-Owned & Operated : We treat every patient the way we’d treat our own family—because many of you are.

Full-Service Dentistry : From general checkups to cosmetic, orthodontic, and sleep dentistry, we provide complete care under one roof.

In-House Lab: Our on-site lab technician allows us to create custom appliances like night guards and flippers quickly and precisely.

Advanced Sleep Solutions : As certified Vivos providers, we offer CPAP alternatives, laser treatments, and take-home sleep studies.

Experienced, Compassionate Team : Every team member brings a unique combination of skill, personality, and genuine care for your well-being.

Most PPO Insurances Accepted : We make it easy to navigate your benefits, and we’re always happy to verify your coverage for you.

When you choose Tigani Family Dentistry, you’re choosing a team that values trust, integrity, and your comfort—every step of the way.

Meet our team

of dental care specialists

At Tigani Family Dentistry, we’re more than a dental team—we’re a family. With decades of experience, deep community roots, and a shared passion for patient care, each member of our staff brings unique talents and a warm touch to your experience with us.

Dr. Kristin M. Tigani-Taylor, D.D.S. Co-Owner and Dentist

A Delaware native and Georgetown graduate, Dr. Kristin earned her dental degree from the University of Maryland and completed her residency at Christiana Care Health System. She has been a dentist with the practice since 2010 and brings a forward-thinking approach to patient care, with advanced training in airway-focused dentistry, Vivos therapies, and Botox. Passionate about continuous improvement, Dr. Kristin is known for her attention to detail, love of puzzles, and for keeping the team on their toes—in the best way.

Dr. James V. Tigani IV, D.D.S. Co-Owner and Dentist

Dr. Jimmy began as a dental assistant in the practice in 2006 before earning his dental degree from the University of Maryland and completing a residency at the University of Iowa. He officially joined as a dentist in 2015. A certified Vivos provider since 2019, he brings a modern approach to general and airway dentistry. Outside the office, he enjoys running, cooking, ballroom dancing, and playfully bothering his sister, Dr. Kristin.
