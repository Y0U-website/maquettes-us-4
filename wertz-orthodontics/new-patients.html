Cost Info

At Wertz Orthodontics, we believe high-quality orthodontic care should be accessible for every smile and every budget. Our team offers flexible payment plans, accepts major credit cards and many insurance plans, and helps patients understand the cost of braces, clear aligners, and other orthodontic treatment options before care begins.

We are also pleased to offer Sunbit as an additional financing option, giving patients a simple way to pay over time for orthodontic treatment. Sunbit offers flexible payment plans for dental care, a fast application process, no hard credit check, and no late fees, origination fees, penalty fees, or penalty APRs.

Our dedicated staff can assist with insurance claims, payment questions, online account access, payment history, and receipts, helping make your orthodontic experience as smooth and stress-free as possible.

Treatment Options for a Signature Smile

When it comes to orthodontic treatment, Wertz Orthodontics offers a range of specialized options to address your unique needs. Our experienced orthodontists have undergone extensive training in dentofacial orthopedics , making them experts in correcting jaw irregularities and optimizing facial aesthetics.

Invisalign , one of our popular treatment choices, is a discreet and lifestyle-friendly alternative to traditional braces. These virtually invisible aligners are removable, allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods and maintain oral hygiene with ease. Say goodbye to the self-consciousness associated with metal braces, as Invisalign aligners blend seamlessly with your natural smile while effectively straightening your teeth.

For individuals with severe jaw irregularities, surgical orthodontics provides a comprehensive solution. By combining orthodontic techniques with jaw surgery, this approach improves chewing, breathing, and speaking functions, while also achieving optimal teeth alignment. Our skilled orthodontists work closely with you to develop a personalized treatment plan that aligns with your goals and maximizes results.

We also specialize in TMJ/TMD treatment . Temporomandibular disorder affects millions of Americans, causing restricted jaw movement, headaches, and chronic facial and neck pain. Our orthodontic interventions are tailored to alleviate the symptoms and restore proper jaw function, providing you with relief and enhanced quality of life.
