BRACES

At Wertz Ortho , we believe that everyone deserves a smile they can be proud of, which is why we offer a range of orthodontic solutions, including braces. Our team of skilled professionals is dedicated to providing exceptional care and personalized treatment plans to meet your specific goals and needs. Whether you opt for traditional metal braces or ceramic braces , you can trust our expertise to deliver effective and lasting results.

METAL BRACES

Metal braces are a popular and reliable option for straightening teeth. Utilizing high-quality stainless steel brackets and archwires, metal braces effectively apply gentle pressure to gradually shift teeth into proper alignment. With our expertise and personalized treatment plans, we ensure that metal braces are comfortable and efficient, giving you the confidence to showcase your smile throughout the treatment process.

CERAMIC BRACES

For those seeking a more discreet option, Wertz Ortho offers ceramic braces that provide the same effective results with a subtle appearance. These braces feature tooth-colored or clear brackets that blend harmoniously with your natural teeth, making them less noticeable. With ceramic braces, you can confidently go through your orthodontic journey without feeling self-conscious about your smile.
