Meet Dr. An

Dr. An is a dedicated orthodontist committed to creating confident, healthy smiles. She earned her undergraduate degree from Boston College and her Doctor of Dental Surgery from Columbia University. She then completed her orthodontic residency and Master of Dental Science at the University of Connecticut, where she also served as Chief Resident.

As a Board-Certified Orthodontist through the American Board of Orthodontics, Dr. An is passionate about delivering personalized, high-quality care. She stays at the forefront of the latest advancements in the field including research on artificial intelligence in orthodontics recognized at the North Eastern Society of Orthodontics and the Dr. William R. Proffit Resident Scholar Award competition to ensure her patients receive the best possible results.

When she’s not in the office, Dr. An lives in Enola with her husband, Jason, and their rescue tuxedo cat, Tucker. She loves traveling, exploring local restaurants, and winding down with her favorite games on the Nintendo Switch.
