Treatment Options for Teens

Every teen’s orthodontic path is different, with treatment lengths typically lasting from 14 to 24 months.

At Uptown Orthodontics, we use the latest orthodontic treatments to reduce treatment times and create your teen’s dream smile faster with the same great results.

Dr. Skabelund or Dr. Jacobs will discuss all available options with you and your teen. Our mission? To deliver efficient, comfortable orthodontic solutions that meet your teen’s needs, whether they’re in the classroom, on the field, or in the spotlight.

  • Metal braces : Modern metal braces are made with medical-grade stainless steel, which remains an affordable option for achieving straighter teeth. Durable and stain-resistant, this orthodontic treatment is perfect for people looking for a reliable yet low-maintenance option.
  • Clear braces : Similar in function to traditional metal braces, clear braces closely mimic the color of your teeth for a more discreet look. Smaller and more rounded, ceramic brackets are less irritating than their stainless-steel counterparts, making them more comfortable for everyday wear.
  • Clear Aligners : We offer clear aligners for teens for those who prefer comfort and discreteness. A series of near-invisible aligner trays will gradually guide your teen’s teeth into place. These aligners are removable as well, allowing your teen to enjoy food without worrying about damage or maintenance.

The Best Time to Treat Smiles

The teenage years mark the next stage in your kid’s development, with their adult teeth mostly in place. An orthodontic evaluation at this stage ensures they enter adulthood with their teeth and jaws in excellent, healthy condition.

If something needs to be fixed, Dr. Skabelund or Dr. Jacobs will recommend the right treatment option for your teen’s unique needs and lifestyle requirements, letting them get a better smile without sacrificing their teenage years.
