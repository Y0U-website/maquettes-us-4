FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Dental and medical science and techniques are constantly changing as new research and developments take place. In order to stay up to date, Dr. Ginsburg regularly attends continuing education classes and conferences that focus on the latest techniques in cosmetic, restorative and preventive dentistry.

Some recent highlights of Dr. Ginsburg’s continuing education include a one year long Synergy Training Program for Advanced Implant and Restorative techniques, as well as a two day retreat, to the Zimmer Dental Implant Institute in Parsippany, NJ for the latest implant restorative technology and techniques. Both of these educational experiences were in collaboration with the periodontal surgical specialists that we work with for your treatment.

Dr. Ginsburg also belongs to a variety of professional organizations including The American Dental Association, The Massachusetts Dental Society and The Tufts Dental Alumni Association.

When you first visit our practice, Dr. Ginsburg and his hygienist will recommend how often it will be necessary for you to come for regular cleanings and check-ups after a comprehensive oral examination.

Cosmetic dentistry, veneers, whitening treatments etc. are usually not covered by insurance.

We feel that your insurance should never dictate the quality or timing of your treatment. If you have extensive or long term dental care, we will determine the estimated time it will take to complete your treatment and make payment arrangements to coincide with the estimated time period. If you have any questions, please call us at 617.536.9057 .

We know your schedule can change abruptly. If you need to cancel an appointment, please call us as soon as possible so we can reschedule your appointment. If you cancel less than 48 hours before your scheduled time, we reserve the right to charge you a cancellation fee.

If you have an emergency, please call our office number at 617.536.9057 and follow the instructions to contact Dr. Ginsburg.

Our office accepts cash, check and credit cards such as Visa, Master Card, Discover or American Express. We also offer payment plans sponsored by CareCredit. Please contact our office for details. Any amount not covered by insurance is the patient’s responsibility.

In addition, we offer digital radiography which reduces radiation exposure by 90 %.

We keep up-to-date on the latest sterilization procedures and research. Our instruments are thoroughly sterilized in accordance with American Dental Association accepted guidelines. Our sterilization center maximizes instrument sterilization and eliminates the chance of cross-contamination.

Additionally, our entire office staff is CPR certified according to the guidelines of the American Heart Association. We also have an Automated External Defibrillator (AED) in our office for your safety.

If you have other questions about safety or sterilization, Dr. Ginsburg would be happy to talk to you.
