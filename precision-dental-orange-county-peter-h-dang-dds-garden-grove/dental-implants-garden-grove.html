What to Expect in a Dental Implant Procedure

The biggest drawback of dental implants is that the procedure takes six to eight months to complete. But you only need a few dental appointments, so the treatment won’t disrupt your daily life.

After the implant has been surgically implanted in your jawbone, it needs 3-6 months to heal and integrate with the jawbone, in a process known as osseointegration. This process is necessary to create a strong and sturdy support for your tooth replacement. In the meantime, you will be fitted with a temporary crown to help you eat, speak, and smile normally.

When the implant has bonded with the jawbone, the dentist will uncover it and attach a small collar, the abutment. This requires about two weeks to heal, after which an impression can be taken to create your custom replacement tooth.

When the restoration is complete, your mouth will function like it used to. You will be able to enjoy all your favorite foods and smile confidently, without requiring any extra dental care work beyond regular brushing and flossing.

Am I a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults can replace one or more teeth using dental implants, either as a singular treatment or in combination with full dentures. However, you need healthy bone tissue to bond with the titanium implant. Depending on your case, the dentist may recommend bone grafting before implant placement.
