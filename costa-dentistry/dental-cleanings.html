What is fluoride?

Fluoride is a naturally occurring mineral. It is found in water and in many foods, and it is often added to toothpaste. It is used as a preventive treatment in dentistry to help strengthen your teeth against tooth decay and other problems.

Is fluoride treatment safe for adults and kids?

Yes, absolutely. Professional fluoride treatments are safe and beneficial for patients of all ages. The concentration used in a dental office is higher than what’s found in toothpaste or tap water, but the amount applied in a single treatment is very small and poses no health risk. Your dentist will let you know whether a fluoride treatment is recommended at each visit based on your cavity risk and oral health.

How long do sealants last?

Sealants can last up to 10 years if you continue to maintain good oral hygiene and visit your dentist for regular checkups.

How long does a dental cleaning take?

A typical cleaning and exam appointment takes about 60 minutes for most patients.

Are dental cleanings covered by insurance?

Most dental insurance plans cover two preventive cleanings and exams per year. We recommend checking with your insurance provider for the specifics of your plan.
