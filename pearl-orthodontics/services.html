Services

At Pearl Orthodontics, We Treat Children,Teens & Adults

Traditional Metal Braces Our most time-tested option, traditional metal braces are highly effective for correcting a wide range of orthodontic concerns. Today’s braces are smaller, more comfortable, and more efficient than ever ideal for kids, teens, and adults alike. We are proud of the metal braces that we use, they are manufactured right here in the United States.

Ceramic Braces Ceramic braces function just like metal braces but use clear or tooth-colored brackets, making them a more discreet option. They’re a popular choice for teens and adults who want a less noticeable treatment without compromising results. We are proud of the ceramic braces that we use, they are manufactured right here in the United States.

Clear Aligners & Invisalign Our clear aligner systems (such as Invisalign ) offer a virtually invisible way to straighten teeth without brackets or wires. Removable, comfortable, and customized to fit your lifestyle, perfect for busy professionals and image conscious teens.

Expanders & Early Treatment We specialize in early interceptive treatment for children. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have their first orthodontic evaluation at the age of 7. This allows the orthodontist to identify and correct any potential issues while your child is still growing.

Adult Orthodontics It’s never too late for a great smile. Whether it’s your first time considering treatment or you’re revisiting orthodontics, we offer aesthetic options like ceramic braces and clear aligners tailored for adult needs, schedules, and goals.
