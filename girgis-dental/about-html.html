Dr. Philip Girgis

Dr. Philip Girgis has been practicing in La Grange since 1993. He received his undergraduate degree from the University of Illinois at Chicago and his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine. Following dental school, he completed a General Practice Residency at Lakeside Medical Center (Northwestern University Dental School).

Dr. Philip is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine, American Dental Association, Illinois State Dental Society and Chicago Dental Society, where he is currently serving on the peer review committee. Dr. Philip is also a member of the medical staff at La Grange Hospital, Hinsdale Hospital and RML Hospital.

He recently celebrated 30 years of marriage with his wife, Maggie. Together, they have 5 children and 8 grandchildren (more on the way...). When he is away from the office he enjoys traveling, boating and following the Chicago Cubs.

Dr. Loretta Lam

Dr. Loretta Lam completed her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree at Northwestern University Dental School in Chicago. Dr. Lam has over twenty years of dental experience. She attended Loyola University of Chicago for her undergraduate degree, where she was 60th on the Dean’s List and elected to the National Honor Society in Psychology.

Her philosophy in dentistry centers around listening to the needs of the patients and working with them to achieve a happy and healthy smile. Patients find her gentle approach to dentistry very calming. Dr. Lam has over a hundred hours of continuing education in dentistry and always enjoys enhancing her dental knowledge.

In her spare time, she performs improv, takes lessons for guitar and piano, enjoys water activities, likes to learn different dancing styles, relaxes her mind with tai chi, and volunteers at a dog shelter where she cares for unwanted dogs and help them learn what it means to be loved again.

Dr. Peter Girgis

Dr. Peter Girgis is a graduate of Lyons Township High School. Following high school, he attended Wheaton College where he graduated with honors in political science. He then attended the Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine for his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree, where he was elected graduation class speaker. He also received an internship certificate in Oral and Maxillofacial surgery from the Medical College of Wisconsin.

Dr. Peter holds or has held memberships in the following organizations: American Dental Association, Illinois State Dental Society, Chicago Dental Society, the American Academy of Implant Dentistry and the Christian Medical and Dental Association.

Outside the office, Dr. Girgis enjoys spending time with his wife, Sally, and two girls, Audrey and Emma. He enjoys competing in half marathons and reading in his free time.

Dr. Jacqueline Wisinski

Dr. Jackie Wisinski received her undergraduate degree from Loyola University Chicago and her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Illinois at Chicago. During dental school she received and award from Illinois Academy of General Dentistry and was elected to be a member of the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honor Society.

Dr. Jackie is a member of the American Dental Association, Chicago Dental Society, Illinois State Dental Society, the Academy of General Dentistry, and the American Association of Endodontists. She also holds a membership with the American Dental Education Association where she was very active during her time at dental school. She currently is continuing her passion for teaching as a faculty member at the University of Illinois at Chicago College of Dentistry.

When not immersed in patient care, she can usually be found outdoors training for a race, playing at the park with her dog, Bailey, or exploring the city of Chicago with her husband of 10 years, Brandon. Together they enjoy traveling and spending time with family and friends.

Dr. Rob Girgis

Dr. Rob Girgis has been practicing dentistry since 1989 and orthodontics since 1993. He received his Bachelor of Science at Purdue University followed by his DDS from Northwestern University and his Certificate of Orthodontics at Eastman Dental Center, University of Rochester. While at Northwestern Dental School, he received the American Association of Periodontists’ Annual Senior Award and was elected to the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honor Society.

Dr. Rob is passionate about continuing education to keep up to date with the latest skills and technologies. He is a member of the Illinois Society of Orthodontists, American Association of Orthodontists, Midwestern Society of Orthodontists, American Dental Association, Illinois State Dental Society, Chicago Dental Society, and C-12 Group (a Christian CEO forum).

Dr. Rob grew up in Hinsdale, IL and has called Naperville home since 1993. He has been married to his wife Laura since 1987 and they have three adult children and a Golden Retriever. When not working, Dr. Rob likes to keep active: golfing, traveling, snow skiing, running, biking, and learning about cars. Time with his family and friends is a priority.

Long-Term Relationships

Our patients truly matter to us. We take the time to get to know each patient on a personal level and deliver customized care focused on your needs, goals and comfort. We seek to build long-term relationships based on trust and integrity, and we look forward to serving you through the years.

Exceptional Customer Service

Providing great customer service is one of the things we do best! We have a welcoming staff, comfortable environment, gentle team and patient-focused mindset. From the front desk to the treatment chair, we work hard to make your experience great at every visit.

Quality Dentistry

You will be in expert hands here. Dr. Girgis and his team are highly skilled in their fields and work with up-to-date dental technology in order to provide only the highest standard of care. Excellence is their constant goal.

On-Time Appointments

We recognize your time is valuable, which is why we are committed to providing on-time appointments and prompt care. We keep the wait time minimal so everything can run smoothly. While efficient, we also never compromise on quality.

Friendly Team

One thing patients love about our practice is our kind and caring team! We are attentive and patient, demonstrating constant compassion and respect. But we also love to have fun! Our optimistic and enthusiastic personalities are contagious. We sure love seeing our patients smile!

Open Communication

We realize that open communication is key to a successful dental visit and, ultimately, your lasting oral health. Not only do we listen carefully to our patients’ needs, we take the time to answer questions and explain all the details of care prior to any treatment. You will be confident and well-informed here.

If you value these qualities in a dentist, call us today! We’re passionate about your smile and passionate about helping you achieve optimal health!

Our team is skilled, knowledgeable and detailed.
