Implant placement requires special training, as it is a surgical procedure. Our team is highly-trained in implant placement to meet your dental needs.

Implants require healthy gums and adequate bone. A patient must also be committed to excellent oral hygiene and regular dental visits, as these are critical to the long-term success of dental implants.

How do dental implants work? Dental implants fuse with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration. Once fully integrated, they provide a stable and long-lasting anchor for prosthetic teeth, restoring function and aesthetics.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants? Ideal candidates for dental implants should have good oral and overall health, sufficient jawbone density, and healthy gums. However, a consultation with a dentist or oral surgeon is essential to determine eligibility.
