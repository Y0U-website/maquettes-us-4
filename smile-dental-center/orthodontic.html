Orthodontic

Invisalign treatment is an orthodontic treatment of straightening your teeth without the embarrassment and pain of traditional metal braces. Invisalign is the invisible braces made from series of clear plastic molds (aligners). It is an orthodontic treatment system that eliminates the use of metallic braces. Invisalign clear braces straighten the crooked teeth and reduce the gap between teeth.

  • Smooth and comfortable
  • Eat whenever you want
  • Removable for cleaning

Inman Aligner

Inman Aligner is the perfect solution to crowding or protrusion of the front teeth. It is really fast, very safe, and great value compared to other systems.

The Inman Aligner has two spring aligner bars in front and behind your front teeth creating a gentle pressure that guides these teeth into an ideal position. Once treatment is completed, a retainer is used to prevent relapse.
