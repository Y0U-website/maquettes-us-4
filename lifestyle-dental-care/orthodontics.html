Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment

Orthodontic treatment is about more than just aesthetics; it’s about enhancing your oral health and overall wellbeing. Here are some key benefits:

Improved Oral Health

Properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of cavities, gum disease, and other oral health issues.

Enhanced Confidence

A straighter smile boosts your self-esteem and confidence, making you more willing to engage socially and professionally. You can show off your teeth without feeling self-conscious.

Better Functionality

Correcting misaligned bites improves chewing and speaking functions, which can alleviate discomfort and prevent jaw problems. This also helps prevent premature tooth wear.

Long-Term Dental Stability

Orthodontic treatment helps ensure your teeth remain in their correct positions, reducing the likelihood of future dental issues.

Can I Get Invisalign at Lifestyle Dental Care?

Yes! We offer both traditional metal braces and Invisalign. Patients of all ages are choosing Invisalign because it offers a more discreet treatment without using metal wires and brackets. Invisalign uses a series of clear plastic aligners to move your teeth into the proper position and restore your bite. These aligners are virtually invisible, and are removable. For these reasons, Invisalign is a very popular orthodontic treatment.

What Are the Benefits of Traditional Metal Braces?

Metal braces are the most versatile and powerful method of orthodontic correction. They can be used to treat even the most serious and difficult orthodontic problems, including jaw discrepancies, overbites and underbites, and they are able to work alongside other orthodontic devices such as palate expanders and headgear. Also, metal braces are not removable, unlike Invisalign, so they will straighten your teeth 24/7.

What Are the Benefits of Invisalign?

Invisalign aligners are virtually invisible when worn and since they are removable, you can eat and drink without dietary restrictions, which means that you can clean your teeth more effectively. They are perfect for patients who play sports, instruments, and for patients with sensory concerns. Invisalign offers a very comfortable treatment. The plastic aligners shift your teeth gradually with gentle pressure and they won’t irritate your cheeks or gums. Since aligners don’t require regular adjustments, you’ll spend less time in the dentist’s chair during your treatment.
