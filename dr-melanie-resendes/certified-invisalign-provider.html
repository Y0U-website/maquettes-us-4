Invisalign treatment is the clear alternative to metal braces for kids, teens, and adults. Invisalign straightens teeth and corrects the bite using a series of clear aligners. These comfortable aligners are nearly invisible to the naked eye and are removed during eating or drinking so there are no food restrictions. There are fewer appointments required during Invisalign treatment as wires and brackets do not need to be adjusted. Invisalign allows you to go about your life normally!
