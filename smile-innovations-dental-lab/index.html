We are the only dental lab in North Florida offering Micron Mapper Photogrammetry technology for full-arch implant workflows.

This advanced system digitally captures implant locations with a level of precision that helps simplify one of the most technique-sensitive parts of full-arch treatment . By eliminating the need for physical dental models, MicronMapper makes photogrammetry more efficient and cost-effective. This reduction in chairtime and overall treatment duration enhances the patient experience.
