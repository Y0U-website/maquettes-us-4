Digital Systems That Support Better Outcomes

Our restorative workflow is built on digital systems that help improve design accuracy, record capture, and communication between the doctor and lab.

This combination allows us to work across analog and digital workflows while keeping the doctor more involved throughout the case.

  • Micron Mapper for highly accurate measurement of full-arch implant records, replacing physical jigs
  • TRIOS 5 Intraoral Scanner for patient records during surgery and final record acquisition
  • Medit T710 Bench Top Scanner for fast, accurate scanning of analog models within 4 microns using ISO 12836 scanning
  • Exocad for prosthetic design, digital record alignment, and doctor design review
  • coDiagnostiX for implant surgical guide design
  • RealGuide for 3i and Zimmer implant restorations, surgical guide design, and prosthetic bar design

A More Collaborative Design Process

We use Exocad as our primary CAD platform for designing dental prosthetics because it gives us the flexibility to work from multiple forms of patient data in one workflow.

That visibility matters. It allows doctors to review the case before manufacturing begins rather than simply opening a finished case at delivery.

  • Align and use STL files, CBCT data, and patient photos
  • Design restorations with greater flexibility
  • Share design previews with doctors before production
  • Improve collaboration on patient-specific prosthetics

Full-Arch Solutions With Better Structure and Planning

Full-arch treatment requires more than just fabrication. It requires accurate records, a stable digital workflow, and materials that perform well over time. This workflow helps support passive fit, improve coordination between the restorative and surgical phases, and give doctors a more dependable path through complex implant treatment.

As the only dental lab in North Florida offering MicronMapper technology , Smile Innovations Dental Lab gives doctors access to a more advanced full-arch workflow built around exceptional accuracy, reduced reliance on physical jigs, and stronger digital case coordination. Learn how!

  • Micron Mapper for highly accurate implant record capture
  • RealGuide for prosthetic bar design and implant-based planning
  • Titanium bars with zirconia or PMMA
  • Aidite zirconia , selected for consistent firing, fit, and esthetic performance in full-arch implant restorations

Materials Selected for Strength and Esthetics

Material choice affects fit, longevity, esthetics, and how well a restoration performs in real clinical use. We work with materials that support both restorative function and refined final outcomes.

Materials we use include:

Aidite zirconia is especially valuable in full-arch implant restorations because it offers consistent firing and fit along with strong esthetic results. MIYO allows us to enhance the beauty of monolithic zirconia by adding the character and visual refinement more often associated with a layered prosthesis.

This gives doctors a practical combination of strength and esthetics without overcomplicating the case.

  • Aidite Zirconia

  • Printed tooth resin
