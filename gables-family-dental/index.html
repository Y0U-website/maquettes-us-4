The practice

The team believes that a trip to the dentist should be pleasant and stress-free. That’s why each of the offices was designed with comfort in mind. The staff is highly trained and provides personalized, gentle care in a welcoming environment.

The office staff is friendly and helpful, and the dentists are attentive. Anyone can receive a free initial consultation, and the team offers competitive pricing.

People of all ages can access a variety of oral health services, including dental implants , dentures , endodontics, general dentistry , and pediatric dentistry . The team also offers cosmetic dentistry , including veneers , crowns , bridges, and teeth whitening , as well as orthodontics with Invisalign clear aligners.

If necessary, the team can also perform root canals and other types of oral surgery, like tooth extractions.

Dental implants are almost as strong as your permanent teeth. If you brush and floss regularly and visit the team at Gables Family Dental twice a year, they can last a lifetime.

Dental implants can also improve your oral health. By replacing missing permanent teeth with implants, you can prevent your remaining healthy teeth from moving out of the proper position.

At Gables Family Dental, getting implants requires a series of three appointments.
