Dental Implants

If you lose one (or several) permanent teeth due to trauma, extractions, or decay, consider dental implants. Dental implants can restore your smile and make daily tasks like biting and chewing much easier. At Gables Family Dental, the team of dentists boasts years of experience placing and restoring dental implants. To make an appointment at the practice in Miami or Coral Gables, Florida, call the office today, or book online.

Dental Implants Q & A

What are dental implants?

A dental implant is a screw-like post made of titanium or another durable material. They replace your natural tooth roots and provide a supportive foundation for dental restorations like crowns, a bridge, or fixed dentures.

Dental implants are almost as strong as your permanent teeth. If you brush and floss regularly and visit the team at Gables Family Dental twice a year, they can last a lifetime.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

Dental implants provide a variety of benefits, including:

Dental implants can also improve your oral health. By replacing missing permanent teeth with implants, you can prevent your remaining healthy teeth from moving out of the proper position.

  • Easier biting and chewing
  • Improved speech
  • Confidence
  • Durability
  • More defined facial features

What does getting dental implants involve?

At Gables Family Dental, getting implants requires a series of three appointments.

First dental implants visit

Before placing dental implants, your Gables Family Dental provider conducts an oral exam and assesses the health of your gums and jaw. If you have enough bone to support the implants and healthy gum tissue, your provider administers a local anesthetic and inserts a number of titanium posts into your jaw.

Following placement, your provider writes a prescription for pain medication and provides at-home care instructions.

Second dental implants visit

When the implants finish integrating with your jaw, you return to Gables Family Dental. Your provider takes digital scans of your mouth and sends them to a dental facility. Technicians at the facility use your scans to design and create a custom dental restoration that fits your implants perfectly.

Third dental implants visit

When your new restorations are finished, you visit Gables Family Dental for the last time. Your provider attaches the restorations to your dental implants, polishes your teeth, and provides you with at-home care instructions.
