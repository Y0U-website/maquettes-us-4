Dental Implant Process

Dental implants are small titanium posts carefully placed into the jawbone to replace missing teeth. These posts serve as secure anchors for artificial teeth, ensuring a natural look and feel. Through osseointegration, the titanium bonds seamlessly with the bone, creating a durable and stable foundation for the implant.

After the initial placement, the gums are given several weeks to heal. Once healed, the next step involves creating and attaching custom-made crowns or prosthetics to the implant post. This process may require minor oral surgery to fit the anchor securely into the jawbone. Typically, the bone takes up to six months to fully integrate with the implant.

In most cases, modern dental implants can be placed in a single surgery, as the anchor and post are attached simultaneously. However, some situations may require a second procedure, depending on individual needs and the case’s complexity.
