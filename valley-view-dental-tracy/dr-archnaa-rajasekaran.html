Dr. Archnaa Rajasekaran Dentist in Tracy, CA

Dr. Archnaa Rajasekaran, DDS

Dr. Archnaa Rajasekeran, DDS, a proud alumna of the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) School of Dentistry Dr. Rajasekeran is a compassionate and highly skilled general dentist with a strong commitment to providing gentle, personalized dental care. Her clinical expertise spans preventive care, restorative dentistry, cosmetic enhancements, and clear aligner therapy, including Invisalign® and ClearCorrect™.

She is especially passionate about patient education, working closely with individuals to promote lifelong oral health and confident smiles. Known for her calm, friendly, and attentive approach, Dr. Rajasekeran ensures every patient feels comfortable, heard, and cared for during their visit.
