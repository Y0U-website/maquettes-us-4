The practice

Regular dental exams are an important part of maintaining good oral health. At Five Oaks New, we recommend that you come in for a routine exam every six months. During your exam, we will check for signs of tooth decay or gum disease and provide a thorough cleaning to help keep your teeth and gums healthy.

Good oral hygiene is essential for maintaining healthy teeth and gums. At Five Oaks Dental, we offer a range of services, including professional cleanings, fluoride treatments, and dental sealants. Our team will work with you to create a personalized oral health plan tailored to your unique needs, helping you maintain a healthy smile for years to come.

If you have missing or damaged teeth, dentures or veneers may be an effective solution. At Five Oaks Dental, we offer a variety of denture and veneer options designed to restore your smile and boost your confidence. Our team will help you choose the treatment that s right for you.

We offer an in House Dental Plan for patients who do not have insurance.

We also support Care Credit which offers no interest payment options.

Services

If you have a cavity, we can provide you with a dental filling to restore your tooth to its natural shape and function. We offer a range of filling materials, including composite resin and porcelain, to match the color of your existing teeth.

We believe there is no true substitute for your natural teeth and strive to preserve them as best we can. Crowns are an effective solution for restoring and protecting teeth with significant damage, helping to maintain their strength and function.

Dental implants are the next best thing after your natural teeth. Socker preservation facilitates healthy bone growth after extraction and maximizes the success rate of dental implants.

If you re looking to brighten your smile, we offer professional teeth whitening services. Our teeth whitening treatments are safe, effective, and can help you achieve a brighter, more confident smile.
