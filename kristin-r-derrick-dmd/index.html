Clinton Dental Care has a long history of serving patients from Laurens County and surrounding areas. We offer a wide array of services including from basic hygiene and preventive care, to emergency care, cosmetic dentistry-all porcelain crowns and porcelain veneers, root canals, dental implant restorations, teeth whitening, and the latest materials in restorative dentistry.

With this full array of services, we are able to care for the needs of patients from 2 to 102 years of age. When more complex procedures require the need of a specialist, we are blessed to have a core group of specialists we work with daily.
