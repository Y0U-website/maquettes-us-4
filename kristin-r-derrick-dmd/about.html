DENTIST

Dr. Kristin Derrick, DMD, FAGD

Dr. Derrick is a member of the SC Dental Association, American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), and the LD Pankey Alumni Association. Over the past fourteen years she has amassed over 600 hours of continuing education and in July 2017, she was awarded fellowship status in general dentistry from the AGD. There are only 105 dentists in South Carolina who have achieved this distinction and 6,500 in the country.

“These awards mean that your general dentist cares about long-term dental health for you and your family and demonstrates that concern by pursuing additional designations. The Fellowship (FAGD) and Mastership (MAGD) Awards are two of the most rigorous continuing dental education programs today. They help your dentist stay up to date on the most current dental procedures in order to offer you the highest quality of care.” AGD website

The Pankey Institute has been the key to changing the way Dr. Derrick practices dentistry. They teach you to start with the patient as a whole, to look at the complete picture, before preparing a dental plan. She finished the core curriculum in 2017, attends annual meetings and looks forward to returning to Key Biscayne, FL.

Dr. Derrick loves spending time with her daughter, Addy, and being outside, especially in the water. In her free time, she loves the beach, cooking and baking, and going to USC sporting events!.
