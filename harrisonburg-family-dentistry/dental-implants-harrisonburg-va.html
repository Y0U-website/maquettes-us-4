What is a dental implant?

A dental implant consists of a titanium post that is surgically placed in your jawbone, beneath the gum line. The post serves as the root of your natural tooth. A crown is then placed on top to serve as the natural tooth. There are two main types of dental implants. Endosteal implants are placed directly into the jawbone. Subperiosteal implants are placed on top of the jaw.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

Dental implants have many benefits. They not only replace missing teeth, but they can also improve your jaw structure to reduce sagging. An implant is sturdy and stable, unlike a denture, which can come loose. The titanium implant allows you to eat your favorite foods without the risk of tooth decay.

Is a dental implant right for you?

If you’re missing one or more teeth, or even all of your teeth, you might be a good candidate for dental implants. Even those who suffer from bone loss can get implants. There are procedures that we can perform to help prepare you for implants if you choose.

If dental implants aren’t a good option for you, we can offer dentures as alternatives. There are two main types”

  • Full dentures. Full dentures replace all your teeth on the top, bottom or both. Upper dentures cover the roof of your mouth, while lower dentures are shaped like a horseshoe.
  • Partial dentures. A partial denture allows you to keep some of your teeth. The denture rests on a metal framework that attaches to the teeth. An acrylic base is often used so that it matches the color of your gums and creates a natural look. Crowns are sometimes used on your natural teeth to anchor your partial denture in place.

How should you care for your dental implants?

Your dental implants can last a lifetime if you practice good oral health habits at home. To increase success with your new dental implants, we recommend the following:

  • Brush and floss daily.
  • Get your teeth cleaned and examined at least every six months.
  • If you smoke, consider quitting.
  • Avoid eating hard foods such as hard candy and ice.
