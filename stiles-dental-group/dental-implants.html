  • Tooth extraction : If required, we will extract your tooth/teeth to prepare for the implant(s). We will also perform a dental bone graft if bone loss occurs around the implant site.
  • Implant placement : The implant is placed in the bone, and a healing cap is placed over it to start the healing process.
  • Implant healing : During the healing phase, a process called osseointegration occurs, where the jawbone heals around the implant and fuses with it. This provides a stable foundation for the new artificial tooth. Typically, this process takes 4 to 6 months to complete.
  • Abutment/crown placement : The abutment is a small, titanium piece used to connect your artificial tooth (crown) to the implant. The abutment is attached to the implant, and the crown is placed on it.
