I have a strong responsibility to safeguard my patients, my staff and the environment against potentially harmful materials and to improve the quality of life.

When I first began explaining how mercury and fluoride were actually poisons, I was considered an outsider. This was in 1981, and dentistry was lacking solid scientific evidence about how the materials we used affected the rest of the body. After all, a filling is really an implant, and it’s just inches from your brain.

In 1984, I joined 13 other dentists and research PhD’s from the US and Canada to form the International Academy of Oral Medicine and Toxicology. In the following five years, we helped fund the first solid scientific studies that are now the gold standard for the effects of mercury on brain cells and the physiological impact on other organs of the human body. Aditi Patel was born in Michigan and grew up in Clarkston. She is happy to be back working in her hometown! She went to Michigan State University for undergrad and University of Pennsylvania for dental school. She became interested in dentistry when she realized she can help people while also building long-term relationships with patients. Placing the patient s comfort first is a high priority for her also. Her goal is to help ease any fears, while treating each patient individually.

Slyvia Zannis, DDS, NMD, AIAOMT, FAGD, FICOI

Dr. Zannis’s journey in dentistry began with a simple idea: I want to help people feel better. Inspired by her grandfather’s example of service, she pursued a B.S. in Human Biology from Michigan State University, followed by her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry. A Northville native she is overjoyed to be able to spend more time in Michigan with her family.

To further her education, she completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) certificate at Boston University. While practicing in the Boston area, Dr. Zannis was introduced to biological dentistry, an approach that integrates dentistry and medicine recognizing that the mouth is a gateway to overall health. She later served as Dental Director of an integrative functional medicine clinic in New England and earned her NMD degree through ACIMD.

Dr. Zannis is SMART-certified and an active member of the IAOMT. She holds fellowships in ceramic implantology and through the Academy of General Dentistry in recognition of her dedication to continuing education.

Outside the office, Dr. Zannis enjoys golf, tennis, skiing, and finding creative adventures for her son.

In Memory Sam Gafencu, DDS, AIAOMT

SMART certified and Healthy Start certified

In memory of Dr. Samuel Gafencu, a cherished member of our dental family. His unwavering commitment to his family, his patients, and his faith continues to inspire the work we do every day.

Samuel Gafencu, DDS, AIAOMT better known as Dr. Sam, is a native of Michigan. He attended Wayne State University for his undergraduate degree, after which he attended and graduated from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry. Dr. Sam was drawn to dentistry upon realizing his passion for helping people and his love for science could be applied in this profession.

Upon graduation, Dr. Sam practiced in a few traditional dental offices, not practicing mercury-safe dentistry. With the failing health of one of his family members, he started to question the impact modern dental practices had on one s health. He began researching and learning about the effects of mercury and fluoride on the human body. The research he found led him to the IAOMT, and the rest is history. He began applying the true science to his practice and personal life. He has committed his efforts to developing his skills and techniques to use the safest methods available.
