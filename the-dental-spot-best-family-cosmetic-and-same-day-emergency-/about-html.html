Meet Your Mooresville Dentist

A neighborhood practice built on genuine care.

We’re a Mooresville-rooted team committed to making thoughtful, modern dentistry feel calm, clear, and personal.

Compassionate care, artistic craft.

Dr. Jenna Debbs is a compassionate general dentist with extensive training in cosmetic, restorative, and surgical care. A graduate of the New York University College of Dentistry with Invisalign certification, she completed a general practice residency at Northwell-North Shore University Hospital.

Drawing on her psychology degree from Boston University, Dr. Debbs uses her calm, gentle chairside manner to alleviate dental anxiety, blending a meticulous artistic touch with collaborative patient care to design dream smiles.

Outside the office, she brings that same creative energy to her personal life — whether she’s unwinding and dancing to house music, experimenting with new recipes in the kitchen, or exploring the local culinary scene.

Texas-born, Charlotte-raised — back home where it matters.

Dr. Peyman Gharavizadeh — Dr. G to his patients — was born in Texas, spent his early childhood in Europe, and grew up in Charlotte, where his family put down roots and built a life. After earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery at NYU College of Dentistry in New York City, he came home to be near family and serve the communities he calls his own.

Soccer was his first love — and still is — but somewhere along the way he came to love basketball just as much. That same drive shows up in his work. Dr. G is deeply passionate about cosmetic dentistry and the surgical side of the craft, including dental implants, and has spent years refining both — because beautiful, lasting results take real skill, not shortcuts.

Outside the operatory, he’s a photographer. He’s quietly built a practice of blending dentistry and photography together — using imaging to educate patients, plan cases visually, and help bring the smile in their mind’s eye to life. If you’re considering a cosmetic case, expect to be heard, walked through your options carefully, and shown exactly what’s possible before you ever sit in the chair.
