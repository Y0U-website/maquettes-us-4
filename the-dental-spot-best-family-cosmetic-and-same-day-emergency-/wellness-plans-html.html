How our plans work.

Is this insurance?

No. Our Wellness Plans and the Healthy Smiles Annual Program are in-house discount memberships paid directly to The Dental Spot. They cannot be combined with dental insurance.

Can I use it the day I sign up?

Yes — there are no waiting periods, deductibles, or annual maximums. Once you’re enrolled, your benefits start immediately at your first visit.
