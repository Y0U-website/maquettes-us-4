Breathing Patterns & Airway Health

A child’s breathing pattern is one of the most overlooked influences on orthodontic development. Mouth breathing causes the tongue to drop, the arch to narrow, and the lower jaw to shift back over time — catching these patterns early gives us options that aren’t available once growth is complete.

  • Signs of sleep-disordered breathing
  • Narrow arches from restricted nasal airflow

Everything You Need to Know About Pediatric Orthodontic Evaluations

No referral is needed! You are welcome to contact our office directly to schedule your child’s evaluation at any time.

Whether X-rays are taken at the first visit depends on your child’s age and what we observe during the clinical examination. For younger children, a visual assessment may be enough to start, but when we need a closer look at what is developing beneath the gumline, we use our 3D X-ray technology to get the detail that informs an accurate treatment plan.

Anxiety about orthodontic visits is completely normal, and we structure every appointment with that in mind, introducing each step before we do it, keeping our pace unhurried, and giving your child time to feel comfortable before moving forward. For children who need a little more preparation, we are happy to schedule a brief introductory visit to tour the office and meet the team before any clinical assessment takes place.
