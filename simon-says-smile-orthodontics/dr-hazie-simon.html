Treatment Philosophy & Patient Approach

At Simon Says Smile Orthodontics, Dr. Hazie Simon believes great orthodontic care is about more than just straightening teeth, it is about making every patient feel informed, comfortable, and genuinely cared for throughout their entire treatment journey. Dr. Simon takes the time to understand each child’s unique needs and designs personalized treatment plans that deliver lasting results in an environment that kids and families actually look forward to visiting.
