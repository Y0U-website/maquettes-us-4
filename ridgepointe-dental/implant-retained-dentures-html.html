Am I A Candidate for Implant-Retained Dentures?

When you visit our office in The Colony, Dr. Amos will perform a tedious and thorough examination that starts with X-ray images being captured to determine the condition of your jawbone. This is a critical step because it allows us to determine how to proceed to best meet your needs. Because such a careful approach is taken to the dental implant procedure, it has a 95 % success rate.

An equally important part of your visit is the consultation you’ll have with Dr. Amos, at which time he’ll explain his findings, make recommendations, listen to your concerns and answer any questions you may have. Once you feel comfortable to move forward, you’ll be guided down the road to being fully restored!

The Implant-Retained Denture Procedure

Even if you’ve suffered bone loss due to the prolonged absence of your teeth, the All-on-4 method is a viable option. One reason is that the posterior implants are angled at 45 degrees to reduce the need for bone grafting. This is made possible because Dr. Amos will carefully locate the denser parts of the bone to anchor your implants.

After the four fixtures are placed, the normal healing time is around three to six months, which also allows time for new bone cells to grow and fuse with the implants through a process called osseointegration.

The Benefits of Combining Dentures and Dental Implants

When you receive the combined therapy of dentures and dental implants, you no longer have to settle for the slipping and gum irritation that many wearers of traditional dentures experience. Instead, you’ll enjoy a secure restoration without having to compromise your smile aesthetics.

Another perk is that you won’t have to worry about removing your dentures at night to clean and soak them. You’ll simply care for your new fixtures like you would a normal set of teeth, which includes brushing and flossing at least two times a day.

A final advantage of being fitted with implant-retained dentures is that you can expect to regain up to 75 % of your original chewing capacity.
