Dr. Sahara Pradhan

Dr. Sahara Pradhan is originally from Maryland and has always been passionate about helping others through healthcare. She attended Howard University in Washington, DC, on a full-ride scholarship and graduated with a Bachelor of Science degree with Honors. She then continued her education at Howard University College of Dentistry, earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree in 2018.

Following dental school, Dr. Pradhan moved to Alaska while her husband was serving in the U.S. Army. Living in different regions of the country has given her the opportunity to connect with diverse communities and care for patients from a wide range of backgrounds.

Dr. Pradhan began her dental career at a community health center in Waterloo, Iowa, where she gained valuable experience providing comprehensive dental care to patients with varying needs while helping improve access to quality oral healthcare. She later relocated to the Carolinas, where she has proudly served patients throughout Fort Mill, Indian Land, and the greater Charlotte area.

Known for her compassionate and patient-centered approach, Dr. Pradhan believes that exceptional dentistry begins with trust, open communication, and a genuine understanding of each patient’s goals. She takes the time to listen, educate, and develop personalized treatment plans that help patients feel comfortable, informed, and confident in their care.

Committed to clinical excellence and lifelong learning, Dr. Pradhan regularly pursues continuing education and advanced training to stay current with the latest advancements in dentistry, facial esthetics, and patient care. She is an active member of the Academy of General Dentistry, the South Charlotte Study Club, and the American Academy of Facial Esthetics.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Pradhan enjoys spending time with her husband and son. She enjoys cooking and swimming. She is passionate about helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles and strives to create a positive dental experience that supports both oral health and overall well-being.
