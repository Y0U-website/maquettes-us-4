Whether your child is ready for their first evaluation or you’re an adult looking to finally straighten your smile, Abari Orthodontics and Oral Surgery offers personalized orthodontic treatment for patients of all ages in San Dimas and the surrounding communities. Our orthodontists take the time to understand your goals and design a treatment plan that fits your life.

We offer a full range of orthodontic options, including traditional metal braces, ceramic braces, Invisalign, ClearCorrect, and our own same-day Abari Aligners. From interceptive treatment for growing children to discreet clear aligners for working adults, we have the experience and technology to deliver results you’ll be proud of.
