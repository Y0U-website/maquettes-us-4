What Is Orthodontics?

Orthodontics is a specialized branch of dentistry focused on diagnosing, preventing, and correcting misaligned teeth and jaws. When teeth are crowded, spaced too far apart, or the bite is off, an orthodontist uses appliances like braces or clear aligners to gradually guide everything into proper alignment.

The benefits of orthodontic treatment go well beyond aesthetics. Properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, which reduces the risk of cavities and gum disease. A corrected bite can also relieve jaw discomfort, improve chewing function, and even support clearer speech. Orthodontics is about health just as much as it is about a beautiful smile.

Who Needs Orthodontic Treatment?

One of the most common questions patients have when learning about orthodontics is whether they actually need it. Some signs that you or your child may benefit from seeing an orthodontist include:

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. Early assessment allows the orthodontist to monitor development and identify issues while the jaw is still growing. That said, orthodontic treatment is effective at any age, and adults make up a significant and growing portion of orthodontic patients.

  • Crowded or overlapping teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Difficulty chewing or biting
  • Teeth that do not meet properly when the mouth closes
  • Jaw shifting, clicking, or discomfort
  • Mouth breathing or speech issues related to tooth position

Treatment Options

When most people think about orthodontics, they picture metal braces. But today’s patients have more choices than ever.

Your orthodontist will evaluate your teeth, bite, jaw, and overall oral health before recommending the right treatment plan for your specific situation.

  • Traditional metal braces remain one of the most effective options for correcting complex cases. They use metal brackets and wires to steadily move teeth into alignment. Modern metal braces are smaller, more comfortable, and more precise than older versions.
  • Ceramic braces work the same way as metal braces but use tooth-colored or clear brackets that blend more naturally with your teeth. They offer the effectiveness of traditional braces with a subtler appearance.
  • Clear aligners are a series of removable, custom-fit trays that gradually shift teeth without brackets or wires. Options like Invisalign and ClearCorrect have become popular with both teens and adults who want a discreet, flexible treatment experience. What to Expect During Treatment

Most orthodontic treatment takes between 12 and 24 months, depending on the complexity of your case and how well you follow your orthodontist’s instructions . You will have regular check-in appointments, typically every four to eight weeks, so your progress can be monitored and adjustments can be made.

Once treatment is complete, you will move into the retention phase. Retainers are essential at this stage because teeth naturally want to drift back toward their original positions. Wearing your retainer as directed protects your results for the long term.

Frequently Asked Questions About Orthodontics

Does orthodontic treatment hurt?

Most patients experience some pressure or mild soreness when braces are first placed or after adjustments. This typically fades within a day or two. Over-the-counter pain relievers and orthodontic wax can help with any irritation.

How do I know if I am a good candidate for clear aligners?

Clear aligners work well for mild to moderate alignment issues. For more complex cases involving significant bite correction, traditional braces may be more effective. Your orthodontist will help you determine the best fit during your consultation .

Does my child need a referral to see an orthodontist?

No. You can contact our office directly to schedule an evaluation. Many families come to us on their own, while others are referred by their general dentist.

Does insurance cover orthodontic treatment?

Many dental insurance plans include orthodontic benefits. Our team is happy to help you understand your coverage before treatment begins.
