Frequently Asked Questions about Implant-Supported Dentures

  • Do I need to replace missing teeth? Yes. You have an increased risk of gum disease and bone loss if you’re missing teeth. You also may have difficulty chewing, speaking without slurring, and smiling. If you’re ready to restore your smile, please schedule your consultation for implant-supported dentures in North Las Vegas, NV.
  • What is the difference between dentures and implant-supported dentures? Implant-supported dentures are permanently fixed in your mouth, making them more stable than dentures that are removable. Implant-supported dentures allow you to eat and speak as you did before without slurring. They’re also easier to maintain than dentures. Our restorative dentists will help you choose the best tooth restoration for your needs, goals, and budget.
  • What’s the difference between implant-supported dentures and implants? Implant-supported dentures can replace multiple teeth with as few as four dental implants for stability. Dental implants replace each individual tooth.
  • How many teeth can I replace with overdentures? We can replace up to an entire top or bottom arch of teeth with overdentures.
  • How do I care for my snap-on dentures? Care for your snap-on dentures as you would your natural teeth: brush twice a day, floss, and keep your scheduled dental exams and cleanings.
