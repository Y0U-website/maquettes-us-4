Frequently Asked Questions about Dental Implants

  • Do I need to replace missing teeth? Yes. You may have difficulty eating, smiling, and speaking clearly if you do not replace missing teeth. Missing teeth also increase your risk of gum disease and bone loss and may cause damage to your remaining healthy teeth as they shift. If you’re missing teeth or need new dentures, please contact our North Las Vegas dentist for an implants consultation. We’ll discuss your needs and goals to match you with the best tooth replacement option.
  • What are dental implants made of? Most dental implants are made of titanium. This material is durable, lightweight, and super strong.
  • What is the difference between a denture and a dental implant? Both dentures and dental implants can replace your missing teeth, but dental implants are permanently placed in your mouth vs. dentures, which are removable. You may slur your words with removable dentures, and they take a period of adjustment that some people find uncomfortable. They also require adjustment over time and may become lost or damaged.
  • How long do dental implants last? Dental implants can last decades and even your entire lifetime if you brush and floss regularly, maintain regular cleanings and exams, and avoid smoking.
