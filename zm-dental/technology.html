The Fotona Laser System

The Fotona laser is an advanced laser system that uses high-intensity light beams to target specific areas in the mouth with great precision. It delivers concentrated light energy by emitting specific wavelengths that are absorbed by water and hydroxyapatite in teeth and tissues, generating gentle heat to remove decay and bacteria.

The result is dentistry that’s often more comfortable and conservative than traditional methods — preserving more of your healthy tooth structure.
