Dr. Zineb Mediouni

Dr. Mediouni is a cosmetic dentist and trained prosthodontist serving the Greater Carrollwood and Tampa area. She believes in providing comprehensive dental treatment tailored to each patient’s individual needs.

With advanced training in both the function and aesthetics of the smile, Dr. Mediouni performs a full range of dental procedures — including esthetic and implant restorations, reconstructive dentistry, veneers, crowns, dentures and full-mouth rehabilitation. Because she handles complex restorative cases in-house, patients receive cohesive, expertly coordinated care from start to finish.

What Is a Prosthodontist?

A prosthodontist is a dental specialist with years of additional training beyond dental school, focused on the restoration and replacement of teeth. In short — they are the recognized experts in restoring smiles to look, feel and function naturally.

Advanced Training Extra years of specialized education in restorative and reconstructive dentistry beyond a general dental degree.

Aesthetics & Function Smiles that don’t just look beautiful, but bite, chew and feel the way natural teeth should.

Complex Cases Implants, full-mouth rehabilitation and major restorative work coordinated under one expert.
