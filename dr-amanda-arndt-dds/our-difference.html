Our Difference

We strive to create healthier smiles through personalized care.

At Arndt Dental , we are dedicated to providing exceptional oral health care and promoting overall well-being.

We create a welcoming and respectful environment where patients are treated like family. Our team values honesty, kindness, and empathy, and we are committed to achieving top-quality results that last.

We are passionate about proving to each patient that dental care can be a comfortable experience.

With extensive training in esthetic dentistry, Dr. Arndt leads our team in a collaborative approach to smile design, working closely with specialists to redesign your smile with beautiful, long-lasting results. This is our difference."

The Arndt Dental team knows that people experience barriers to receiving dental care. Whether it’s due to their finances, dental anxiety or fear, we work to break down those barriers.

By offering in house financial solutions to patients without dental insurance, we make your payments realistic to remove those financial barriers.

Dr. Arndt is qualified to offer sedation dentistry to those who experience anxiety or fear. There are several options available. Please speak to a team member about sedation dentistry. We are here to make your dental experience comfortable for you by removing all barriers to care.
