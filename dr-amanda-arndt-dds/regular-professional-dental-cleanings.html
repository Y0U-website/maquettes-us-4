Regular Professional Dental Cleanings

Regular professional dental cleanings are a crucial aspect of maintaining optimal oral health. Even with diligent at-home care, it is impossible to remove all the debris that accumulates on teeth over time. This debris, in the form of plaque and tartar, can lead to tooth decay and gum disease if left untreated.

Why are regular cleanings so important?

Regular dental exams and cleanings keep your teeth clean and prevent dental issues, such as cavities and gum disease. Our trained dental hygienists use sophisticated instruments to remove tartar and plaque that cannot be removed by brushing and flossing alone. Additionally, regular cleanings allow for early detection and treatment of any developing dental issues, ensuring better outcomes.

How often should I get a cleaning?

Your dentist will recommend a minimum of one cleaning every six months for most people. However, depending on your specific health conditions, they may suggest more frequent cleanings.
