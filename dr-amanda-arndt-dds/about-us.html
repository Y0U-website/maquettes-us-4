Dr. Amanda L. Arndt

Dr. Amanda L. Arndt is a highly skilled and experienced cosmetic and esthetic dentist based in Tustin, California. Born in Northridge, she graduated with honors from UCLA with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology. She then went on to receive her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University of the Pacific School of Dentistry in San Francisco.
