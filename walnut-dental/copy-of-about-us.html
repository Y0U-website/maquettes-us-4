Our Team

Dr. Janelle Logronio, DMD

Dr. Janelle Logronio earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston. She practices all aspects of general dentistry and is committed to providing thoughtful, high-quality care tailored to each patient’s individual needs.

Dr. Logronio believes in taking the time to listen to her patients, understand their concerns, and help them feel informed and comfortable throughout their treatment. She values building trusting, long-term relationships with her patients and strives to create a welcoming, comfortable, and stress-free dental experience.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Logronio enjoys spending time with her husband and young son, discovering new sushi restaurants, and watching a good thriller.

Dr. Joshua P. Lee, DDS

Dr. Joshua Lee received his Bachelor of Science degree in Biophysics from Johns Hopkins University in 2007 and acquired his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Maryland School of Dentistry in 2012.

Dr. Lee practices General Dentistry, including, but not limited to periodontal therapy, restorative dentistry, endodontic dentistry, fixed and removable prosthodontics, and oral surgery. He constantly strives to improve his dentistry knowledge and skills by participating in various continuous dental education seminars and trainings. He takes time to get to know his patients on a personal level, listening closely to their needs and concerns, so he can give them a smile they can be proud of. He welcomes people of all ages in need of dental care.

His philosophy is to treat his patients in the best possible way in a comfortable, stress free environment and to develop long-term relationships with patients. In his spare time, he enjoys spending time with his wife, Jihee, and two children, Sophia and Hudson.

Dr. Su Jin Bae, DDS

Dr. Su Jin Bae is originally from Seoul and she received her BA degree in Psychology from Emory University in 2011. She then earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery from New York University College of Dentistry in 2016. During her dental training, Dr. Bae worked with the pediatric mobile dental unit for NYU community outreach, providing dental treatment to children in New York’s local public schools. This is where she developed a devotion in serving the local community.

After working at a private office in Connecticut, she then decided to commit four years at a health center in Rhode Island. She was awarded Exceptional Health Care Provider at the company in 2018. When her family decided to settle in Massachusetts, she transferred to Neighborhealth to serve the local Boston community providing all phases of general dentistry. This was where Dr. Bae worked as leader by not only managing staff as a lead dentist but also teaching senior dental students of Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine as a preceptor.

Dr. Bae loves what she does and is dedicated to mastering her craft every day. She strives to provide the highest quality of care. She treats her patients with compassion and aims to build trusting long term relationships. She is known to have gentle and delicate hands and will do everything she can to make each patient feel comfortable and special.

Outside of work, Dr. Bae spends time with her husband and young daughter. Her family loves to travel, explore nature and visit local cafes & restaurants.

Lily Ngo, Office Manager

Lily brings over 20 years of experience, ranging from roles in dental assistance to office management. Her extensive expertise in insurance, coupled with her communication skills, promotes transparency and efficiency, ensuring exceptional patient care.

A work-life balance is an important ingredient in her happiness and the key to her warm smile. Simple things in life, such as spending time with loved ones, caring for her plants, and playing with her dogs, bring her so much joy and laughter. Simplicity is the key.

Renée Doan, Hygienist

Renée earned her dental hygiene degree at Middlesex Community College in 2008. She obtained her Bachelor s Degree of Technology, Dental Hygiene in 2011.

Renée has worked in this office for 17 years. She has a gift of making sure that all patients are comfortable and relaxed during the dental visit. Patients love to see her not only because of her great respectful attitude, but also because of her exceptional disposition and attentiveness to details. Patients and staff all appreciate her talent and dedication that make the care outstanding.

She has a true passion to help patients achieve a beautiful and healthy smile. "It s the relationship that I build with my patients that make it a great profession!"

When Renée is not with her dental team, she is a busy mom of three! She loves photography (mostly of her children). She enjoys skiing and traveling with her husband and kids.

Xuan Huynh , Hygienist

Xuan graduated in 2008 with a Dental Hygiene degree from Middlesex Community College and brings over 17 years of experience to her role. Before studying dental hygiene, Xuan earned a Bachelor of Fine Arts degree at Boston University. Her artistic background plays a key part in her exceptional skills as a dental hygienist.

Xuan is known for her warm, welcoming demeanor, always greeting patients with a smile and ensuring they feel at ease. She is dedicated to addressing each patient’s individual needs and providing the highest level of care through professionalism, thoroughness, and attention to detail.

Outside of work, Xuan enjoys traveling the world with her husband and three children, as well as painting, drawing, flower arranging, and gardening.

Diane Ngo, Hygienist

Diane is a licensed Registered Dental Hygienist dedicated to promoting optimal oral health and patient education. Known for her warm and approachable demeanor, Diane thrives on building meaningful, personable relationships with her patients, helping them feel comfortable and cared for during every visit. One of her favorite parts of her work is developing strong rapport with her patients, which she believes plays a crucial role in achieving optimal oral health outcomes.

She earned her degree in Dental Hygiene from the Community College of Rhode Island, where she built a strong foundation in oral health assessment, periodontal therapy, and patient education. Committed to lifelong learning, Diane regularly pursues continuing education in advanced areas such as local anesthesia, radiology, and oral cancer screening, ensuring she remains at the forefront of dental hygiene practices. ​

Beyond the dental office, Diane is a long-time salsa dancer, performer, and instructor. She has been involved in the vibrant world of salsa for many years, traveling across the country to showcase her talents with her dance company. Her passion for dance is more than a hobby it’s a way for her to connect with others and share joy, much like her work as a dental hygienist. Whether she’s teaching a class or performing on stage, Diane brings energy, enthusiasm, and a sense of community to everything she does.
