Young Children

Where do you start? The American Association of Orthodontists and the American Dental Association recommend that kids be evaluated for orthodontic care by age 7. Not every child needs treatment that young. In fact, the vast majority do not. But making sure the teeth come in properly will help treatment go much smoother in the future. If your child does not need an early phase of treatment, they will join our Growth and Guidance program and be monitored every 6-12 months at no cost to you.

Adolescents and Teens

“You need treatment!” Most kids don’t like to hear these words. The good news is not every child between the ages of 11-15 needs orthodontic treatment. But if they do, our doctors and team will help your family navigate whether traditional braces, Invisalign, or other treatment options are best for your child. We make treatment as fun and convenient as possible so that every child looks forward to their visits.

Adults, Too!

Most adults are a great candidate for Invisalign treatment. Invisalign is a customized series of removable, clear plastic trays or aligners that will gently and efficiently align your teeth. This is a great option for adults who want treatment that is more discreet and convenient for their busy schedules.

But don’t worry! We also offer traditional metal braces or LightForce clear braces for those adults who may not be great candidates for Invisalign or who prefer braces!
