We Use a Mother’s Approach to Pediatric Dentistry

Rocklin Pediatric Dentistry cares for children from Rocklin and the surrounding communities of Roseville, Lincoln, Loomis, and Granite Bay.

The practice

Our skilled, compassionate team manages everything from regular cleanings and exams to tooth-colored fillings, sealants, and SPRIG crowns. We also provide complimentary initial well baby visits for children three years of age and younger.

Your child’s comfort is important to us. We offer many modern amenities, including ceiling-mounted TVs and noise-canceling headphones. We also provide happy visits and opportunities for them to play with dental tools to show them that a trip to the dentist can be fun.

For children with existing dental anxiety, we offer nitrous oxide and in-office IV sedation to help them relax.
