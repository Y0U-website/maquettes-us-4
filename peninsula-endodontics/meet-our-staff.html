Sean McGrath

Office Manager

Sean is the office manager at Peninsula Endo and husband to owner-doctor Bianca Aboubakare. He is also a teacher at Chadwick School, where he has worked the past 10 years.

Carolina Valles

Front Office Manager

Carolina (“Caro”) is our welcoming Front Office Manager and a proud Long Beach native. With over five years of experience in dentistry, she’s often the first friendly face you’ll see and is dedicated to making every patient feel comfortable, informed, and genuinely cared for. She brings a strong blend of patient care, organization, and leadership to keep the practice running smoothly. Carolina is also pursuing her Dental Hygiene degree at West Coast University and is expected to graduate in 2028.

Christian Villarreal Camarena

Dental Assistant

Christian (“Chris”) is a warm and dedicated Endodontic Dental Assistant. Originally from Nayarit, Mexico, she has called California home for the past six years. Christian earned her Dental degree from the Autonomous University of Nayarit in 2014 and completed her specialty training in Endodontics at the National Autonomous University of Mexico in 2017. She is currently working toward obtaining her U.S. dental license, driven by her passion for patient care and professional growth.

Joana Cervantes Gallardo

Receptionist

Joana is our receptionist, one of the friendly faces you will see when you visit our office. She has worked in Endodontic Specialty offices for over four years. In our spa-like office environment, Joana enjoys helping patients feel relaxed and cared for from the moment they walk through the door. She is committed to making every visit as smooth and welcoming as possible.
