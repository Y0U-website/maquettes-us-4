FAQS

It is generally recommended to have a dental cleaning every six months to maintain good oral hygiene. Regular cleanings help remove plaque buildup, prevent cavities and gum disease, and support long-term oral health.

A routine dental cleaning focuses on removing plaque and tartar from above the gumline to maintain overall oral hygiene. A deep cleaning, on the other hand, targets buildup below the gumline to treat and manage gum disease more effectively.

Regular dental cleanings help remove harmful bacteria and plaque buildup that can otherwise lead to cavities and gum disease. These preventive visits play an important role in maintaining healthy teeth and gums and supporting long-term oral health.

Dental X-rays help detect issues that may not be visible during a physical examination, such as cavities between teeth, bone loss, or impacted teeth. This allows for more accurate diagnosis and comprehensive dental care.
