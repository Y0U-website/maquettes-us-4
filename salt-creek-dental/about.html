Our dedicated staff combines experience, compassion, and advanced care to make every visit comfortable, personalized, and stress-free.

With nearly a decade of experience in the dental field, I’ve been a dedicated Dental Assistant since 2016 and currently serve as the Office Lead at our practice. I pride myself on creating a welcoming environment for every patient who walks through our doors. Known for being outgoing, approachable, and highly organized, I help keep our office running smoothly while making sure everyone—patients and team members alike—feels at ease and cared for. Whether I’m assisting chairside or managing daily operations, I bring positive energy and a genuine passion for helping others.

My wife (Pam) and I have 5 grown children and 14 grandchildren. We enjoy traveling, golf, pickleball, music and chasing grandkids around. I enjoy employing all the new dental technologies as we acquire them.

Tiffany has been a Registered Dental Assistant (RDA) since 2018 and is a valued member of our dental team. I have worked in the dental field in various capacities for 38 years. I have a deep affection for our patients and enjoy the fast pace.

My small-town roots inspire a warm, welcoming atmosphere, with patient care always as the top priority. Outside of the office, I enjoy life on the ranch with my husband of nearly 10 years and our two young daughters.

I love building relationships with patients and take pride in making them feel comfortable and cared for during every visit.
