Meet Dr. Puja Agarwal

Dr. Puja Agarwal is a compassionate and dedicated general dentist with a strong commitment to delivering high-quality, personalized dental care. With extensive experience in restorative and preventive dentistry, she is known for her gentle approach, clear communication, and ability to put even the most anxious patients at ease.

Her philosophy centers around proactive, patient-focused care — helping individuals and families maintain healthy, confident smiles through education, early intervention, and lasting relationships. Dr. Agarwal takes pride in making every patient feel heard, supported, and empowered in their dental health journey.

Outside the office, Dr. Agarwal enjoys spending quality time with her husband and daughters. Whether visiting local farms or enjoying the outdoors, she values staying connected to nature and creating lasting memories with her family.

Meet Dr. Samna Minhas

Dr. Samna Minhas is a skilled and compassionate general dentist with a deep commitment to comprehensive, patient-centered care. She began her dental journey at the Altamash Institute of Dental Medicine in Pakistan — affiliated with the prestigious Karolinska Institute in Sweden — where she graduated at the top of her class with a Bachelor of Dental Surgery degree in 2014, earning the honor of Gold Medalist .

After moving to the United States, Dr. Minhas furthered her education and received her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine . She went on to complete a rigorous General Practice Residency with a special focus on clinical oncology at the renowned Roswell Park Comprehensive Cancer Center , deepening her expertise in treating medically complex patients.

Dr. Minhas is passionate about improving lives through dental care and offers a wide range of treatment options for both children and adults. Her approach combines technical excellence with a gentle, understanding manner — always prioritizing each patient’s needs, expectations, and long-term confidence in their smile.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Minhas cherishes spending time with her family and friends, enjoying meaningful moments and staying grounded in her values of care and connection.
