Dental Implants FAQs

With appropriate care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. This includes regular check-ups with Dr. Vallo, maintaining good oral hygiene practices, and following post-treatment care instructions.

We prioritize your comfort during the implant procedure. Most patients report minimal discomfort, comparable to that of a routine tooth extraction. Dr. Vallo uses advanced techniques and appropriate anesthesia to ensure a comfortable experience.

Dental implants are versatile and can be used to replace multiple teeth, whether they are adjacent to each other or scattered across your mouth. Dr. Vallo can use implants to support bridges or dentures, offering a comprehensive solution for various tooth replacement needs

Caring for dental implants involves maintaining good oral hygiene practices. Brush twice a day, floss daily, and use an antibacterial mouthwash as recommended by Dr. Vallo.
