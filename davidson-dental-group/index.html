At Davidson Dental Group, Dr. Davidson specializes in dental implant restoration. If you need to receive dental implants first, Dr. Davidson can coordinate treatment with an oral surgeon in the area.

Dental implants also improve your oral hygiene. By replacing one (or multiple) teeth with implants, you can reduce the risk of other issues, including gum recession and your healthy remaining teeth from shifting out of position.

If you have healthy gums and enough jawbone to support one (or several) implants, Dr. Davidson refers you to an oral surgeon for dental implant placement. Once you receive the implants, your mouth heals for several months while the implants integrate with your jawbone.
