Implants Q&A

If you’ve lost teeth due to extractions, trauma, or poor oral hygiene, dental implants can rehabilitate your smile. At Davidson Dental Group, serving San Leandro, Castro Valley, Oakland, California, and the surrounding areas, general dentist Elena Davidson, DDS, boasts years of experience restoring dental implants. She regularly works with oral surgeons in the area to coordinate implant placement, ensuring you achieve a healthy, vibrant smile. To make an appointment, call the office today or book online.

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are small, screw-like posts that an oral surgeon inserts into your jaw during an outpatient procedure. Following placement, the posts integrate with your jawbone, providing a durable base for restorations like crowns, fixed dentures, or a bridge.

At Davidson Dental Group, Dr. Davidson specializes in dental implant restoration. If you need to receive dental implants first, Dr. Davidson can coordinate treatment with an oral surgeon in the area.

Dental implants also improve your oral hygiene. By replacing one (or multiple) teeth with implants, you can reduce the risk of other issues, including gum recession and your healthy remaining teeth from shifting out of position.

What does getting dental implants involve?

If you have healthy gums and enough jawbone to support one (or several) implants, Dr. Davidson refers you to an oral surgeon for dental implant placement. Once you receive the implants, your mouth heals for several months while the implants integrate with your jawbone.

When the healing process is complete, you return to Davidson Dental Group. Dr. Davidson takes digital scans of your mouth and sends them to a dental facility that manufactures your custom restorations.

A few weeks later, when the facility completes your bridge, fixed dentures, or dental crowns, you visit Davidson Dental Group for the last time. Dr. Davidson attaches the restoration to your dental implants and provides care instructions.
