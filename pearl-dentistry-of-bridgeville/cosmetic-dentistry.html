FAQs

Q: What is the most popular cosmetic dental procedure?

A: Teeth whitening is often considered the most popular cosmetic dental procedure as it helps enhance the appearance of teeth by lightning their color.

Q: What is the difference between aesthetic and cosmetic dentistry?

A: Aesthetic dentistry and cosmetic dentistry are often used interchangeably, but some dentists make a distinction. Aesthetic dentistry focuses on improving the overall appearance of teeth, including their shape, size, and alignment. Cosmetic dentistry primarily deals with procedures that improve the appearance of teeth but may not necessarily address their function or health.

A: The permanence of cosmetic dentistry depends on the specific procedure. Some treatments, like dental implants, can be considered permanent, while others, like teeth whitening, may require touch-ups over time.

Q: Can cosmetic dentistry fix crooked teeth?

A: Yes, cosmetic dentistry can fix crooked teeth. Orthodontic treatments such as braces or clear aligners can straighten misaligned teeth, a common cosmetic concern.

Q: Does dental insurance cover cosmetic dentistry?

A: In most cases, dental insurance does not cover cosmetic procedures. However, some dental insurance plans may offer partial coverage for cosmetic treatments that provide functional benefits, such as dental crowns for damaged teeth. You must check with your insurance provider to understand what cosmetic treatments may be covered.
