Orthodontic Acceleration: How Does It Work?

Orthodontic acceleration technology is an innovative and exciting approach that expedites the teeth straightening process with Invisalign clear aligners. Our experienced orthodontist near you utilizes a special treatment technique to reshape the bone surrounding your teeth, making the entire process faster and more comfortable.

Micro-Osteoperforations (MOPs)

One aspect of this treatment involves the use of micro-osteoperforations (MOPs). Using a specialized tool, our orthodontist creates small incisions in the cortical bone around your teeth. These tiny incisions stimulate your body to release protein, which accelerates the process of bone remodeling. As a result, your teeth align more rapidly as new bone forms where needed.

Easy Aftercare Tips for Accelerated Orthodontics

After an acceleration treatment, you can jump right back into your normal activities! However, to ensure the best care for your teeth, the orthodontist near you will suggest the following simple aftercare tips:

Avoid Anti-Inflammatory Painkillers

Refrain from taking anti-inflammatory medications such as aspirin or ibuprofen during the initial 48 hours of treatment or as suggested by your orthodontist. It will allow your body’s natural healing process to manage the inflammation on its own. Acetaminophen, otherwise known as Tylenol or Paracetamol is a pain medication that does not interfere with bone remodeling and will provide you with better tooth movement.

Dietary Tips

Avoid spicy foods and those high in citric acid or capsaicin so that your treatment can be as comfortable and effective as possible. Foods that are hard and sticky can also affect your recovery, so the orthodontist near you might tell you to avoid them.

Wear Your Retainer

After completing your accelerated orthodontic treatment, it’s essential to wear a retainer at night to prevent your teeth from shifting back. This step is crucial for keeping your results, especially for the upper jaw and roof of the mouth.

By following these aftercare tips, you can optimize your orthodontic treatment and maintain beautiful results for a lifetime.
