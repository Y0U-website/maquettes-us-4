How Long Will My All on 4 Procedure Take?

The All on 4 procedure is designed to be efficient and patient-friendly. In most cases, the implant placement for one arch takes about two to three hours. This means patients can complete the surgical part of the All on 4 implants process in a single visit for each arch.

The final permanent teeth are typically placed within three to six months. This staged process helps protect your investment in your full mouth dental implants cost and ensures long-lasting results.
