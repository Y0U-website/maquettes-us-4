Understanding All-on-4, All-on-6, and All-on-8

All-on-4, All-on-6, and All-on-8 systems are advanced dental solutions that replace an entire arch of teeth with a fixed bridge supported by implants.

Full arch restoration planning helps decide the right number of implants based on individual needs for support, function, and long-term success.

All-on-4 uses four implants, often angled to maximize bone use and avoid nerves.

All-on-6 provides additional support with six implants for greater stability and durability. Patients searching for full arch dental implants near me often choose this when full arch restoration is needed for a stronger long-term solution.

All-on-8 utilizes eight implants when even more support is required, such as in cases of lower bone quality or higher bite forces.

The right approach depends on factors like bone levels, bite, and functional requirements. Occasionally, customized implants may be used to ensure the safest and strongest outcome for full arch dental implants near you.
