The Process

  • 1 Free Consultation & 3D CT Imaging Your first visit includes a comprehensive oral exam and cone-beam CT scan. We map exact bone density, nerve positions, and ideal implant placement before a single instrument touches your mouth. You leave with a clear treatment plan and firm price.
  • 2 Implant Placement (60–90 Minutes) Under local anesthesia, we create a small pilot hole and thread the Nobel Biocare titanium post into the jawbone. A healing cap is placed. Most patients return to normal activities the next day.
  • 3 Osseointegration (8–12 Weeks) The titanium fuses with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration — creating a foundation stronger than a natural tooth root. A temporary crown keeps your smile intact throughout healing.
  • 4 Final Ceramic Crown Placement Once integration is confirmed, your permanent ceramic crown is bonded. Shade-matched to your surrounding teeth, polished, and adjusted until the bite is perfect. Your new tooth is ready for a lifetime of use.
