The Process

  • 1 Digital Smile Design & 3D CBCT Planning Your All-on-4 journey starts with a comprehensive 3D cone-beam CT (CBCT) scan and Digital Smile Design session. Our surgeon maps your bone density, gum health, and facial structure to create a precise surgical plan. You preview your new smile digitally before anything begins.
  • 2 Extractions (If Needed) & Implant Placement Any remaining failing teeth are removed at the same appointment. Four Nobel Biocare implants are precisely placed — two straight at the front, two angled posteriorly. The angled placement is the key innovation: it maximizes bone engagement without grafting.
  • 3 Same-Day Provisional Arch — Teeth in a Day A fixed provisional arch is attached directly to your new implants the same day. You leave Bird Road with a full, functional set of teeth the same afternoon. Eat soft foods, speak clearly, and smile confidently from day one — no removable dentures.
  • 4 Osseointegration Period (3–6 Months) The implants integrate with your jawbone during this period. You wear the provisional arch throughout — it looks natural and functions well. Post-op check-ups monitor integration progress at our Bird Road clinic.
  • 5 Final E.max Ceramic / Zirconia Arch Once osseointegration is confirmed, your definitive full-arch prosthesis is fabricated in high-strength E.max ceramic or zirconia — stain-resistant, natural-looking, and built to last decades. The final arch is permanently screw-retained.
