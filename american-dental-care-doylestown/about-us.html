About Us

Ever since joining American Dental Care in 2003, Dr. Fouad has been committed to pursuing excellence in the art and science of cosmetic dentistry. “I’m enthusiastic and passionate about providing exceptional dentistry that makes a difference in my patients’ lives,” she says. Dr. Fouad firmly believes in offering her patients the most up-to-date treatment possible and helping them reclaim strong, seamlessly attractive smiles with the latest technology. And patient comfort is also a priority. Dr. Fouad is fluent in three languages: Arabic, French, and English.

Dr. Fouad is a proud mother of three beautiful children. She enjoys spending time with her family and also practices meditation in different yoga classes.

Dr. Eldrad Drori

Dr. Eldad Drori grew up in Saint Paul, Minnesota with an unconventional educational background. In 2002 he received his Rabbinical Ordination in Kfar Chabad, Israel, and then he worked with a community in San Diego, California as Assistant Rabbi and Cantor. Subsequently, he received his Doctorate of Dental Medicine at Maurice H. Kornberg School of Dentistry to be able to care for the medically related oral needs of people. He completed a fellowship in oral implantology and has extensive training in the surgical needs of individuals. He is fluent in Hebrew and English.

Dr. Eldad sees patients as individuals. He recognizes the balance between the patient knowing what they feel and want, and the care he wants to provide for them. The dental needs of a person is a harmonious balance between function and beauty. Each person is unique in their own way and each treatment is specific to the needs of that individual. In essence, he looks at life and likewise dentistry as the long but short road (ask him to explain when you sit in the chair).

When Dr. Eldad is not found doing dentistry, you will probably find him powerlifting at the gym while chewing gum. He is also an everlasting student of knowledge, always with a book in hand…ok, he just might be on the phone speaking to one of his 10 siblings or parents.
