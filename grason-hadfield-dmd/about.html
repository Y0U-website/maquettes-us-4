Serving the Community We Call Home

Rooted in the Treasure Valley

Eagle and the surrounding Treasure Valley have grown quickly, and that growth includes a wider mix of families than ever before. Dr. Hadfield built Level Up Family Dental with that changing community in mind from day one.

Being fluent in Spanish lets him connect directly with more families across the area without relying on a translator or a delayed conversation. It is one small piece of a larger effort to make sure every patient who walks through the door feels like they are talking to someone who actually understands them.

That same community focus extends to how the practice thinks about accessibility more broadly, from flexible scheduling to a range of financing options for families without dental insurance. Eagle is growing, and we want to grow alongside it.

Meet Dr Grason Hadfield

Dr. Grason Hadfield founded Level Up Family Dental to create a dental home where patients feel genuinely cared for, not rushed through appointments. A true Idaho native, Dr. Hadfield grew up in Star and attended Eagle High School before going on to pursue his dental career — and he’s proud to bring that journey full circle by serving the community he calls home. Based in Eagle, Idaho, he offers a wide range of services under one roof, from routine cleanings and fillings to root canals, wisdom teeth extractions, and dental implants, so families rarely need to be referred elsewhere.

Trained through the United States Navy’s Advanced Education in General Dentistry program, Dr. Hadfield has also pursued additional coursework in cosmetic dentistry, implant placement, and Clear Aligners. He is fluent in Spanish, which helps him connect with even more families across the Treasure Valley.
