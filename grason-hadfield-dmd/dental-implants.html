A Careful Process, Worth Every Step

Implant treatment unfolds in stages, and we guide you through each one. It begins with a consultation and digital imaging to evaluate your bone and plan the ideal implant position. Placement itself is a precise, comfortable procedure, followed by a healing period of a few months while the bone integrates with the post. Once healed, we attach your custom crown and fine-tune your bite.

Throughout the process, you always know what comes next, how long it takes, and what it costs. No surprises, just steady progress toward a complete smile.

Most Healthy Adults Qualify

Good candidates for implants have healthy gums and adequate jawbone to support the post, which we evaluate with digital imaging at your consultation. Even patients who have been missing teeth for years are often still candidates, and routine exams and cleanings before and after treatment help keep the foundation strong.

If an implant is not the right fit for your situation, we say so and walk you through the alternatives honestly. The right treatment is the one serving you best, not the one on a sales sheet.
