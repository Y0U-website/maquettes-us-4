Frequently Asked Questions about Braces

  • Why do I need to straighten my teeth? If you have crooked, gapped, or misaligned teeth, you’re at a higher risk of tooth decay and periodontal disease. You may also experience tension in the jaw, neck, and head, leading to clenching or grinding. To learn about the best way to straighten your teeth, please schedule a consultation for braces in Summerlin Las Vegas, NV.
  • Can I straighten my teeth at home? The supervision of a dentist or orthodontist is crucial to straightening teeth. At-home aligner kits are risky, and mistakes with homemade dental impressions are common. Given the risks associated with misaligned teeth (grinding, headaches, jaw pain, tooth decay, gum disease), we strongly recommend straightening your teeth under the supervision of our trained and certified clinicians.
  • How long will my treatment take? The average treatment time for braces is two years. Some patients achieve their desired results faster. You are the most important factor in your treatment; the more compliant you are, the faster you’ll see results. Our Las Vegas orthodontist will walk you through the process and let you know what to expect during your braces treatment.
  • What should I avoid while in braces? Avoid hard and sticky foods, i.e., hard candies, nuts, popcorn, gum, taffy, etc.
  • How do I care for my teeth after braces? You’ll wear a clear aligner at night following treatment to keep your teeth in position.
