Frequently Asked Questions about Invisalign

  • Why do I need to straighten my teeth? If you have crooked, gapped, or misaligned teeth, you’re at a higher risk of tooth decay and periodontal disease. You may also experience tension in the jaw, neck, and head, leading to clenching or grinding. If you’re dreaming of a straighter smile, please contact us for an Invisalign consultation in Las Vegas, NV.
  • Can I straighten my teeth at home? The supervision of a dentist or orthodontist is crucial to straightening teeth. At-home aligner kits are risky, and mistakes with homemade dental impressions are common. Given the risks associated with misaligned teeth (grinding, headaches, jaw pain, tooth decay, gum disease), we strongly recommend straightening your teeth under the supervision of our trained and certified clinicians.
  • How long does Invisalign take? The average treatment time for Invisalign is one year. Some patients achieve their desired results in as little as six months. You’re the most important factor in your treatment the more compliant you are, the faster you’ll see results.
  • Is Invisalign as effective as metal braces? Yes! In most cases, Invisalign clear aligners are as effective as traditional metal braces. Find out if you’re a candidate by scheduling an appointment with our Summerlin Las Vegasorthodontist.
  • How do I care for my teeth after Invisalign? You’ll wear a clear aligner at night following treatment to keep your teeth in position.
