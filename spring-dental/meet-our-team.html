SERVICES BLOGS PATIENT INFO Call Us Call Us Meet Our Team Dr. Gursimran Sidhu DMD – Trusted General Dentist in Quincy, MA

Dr. Gursimran Sidhu, DMD, is a highly skilled and compassionate general dentist in Quincy, Massachusetts, proudly serving patients from Quincy and surrounding communities. With over a decade of experience in comprehensive dentistry, Dr. Sidhu is committed to delivering personalized, high-quality dental care in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

Dr. Sidhu earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the prestigious Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine at Boston University. She also holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA) in Health Care Management, allowing her to combine clinical excellence with efficient, patient-focused dental practice management. Her early dental education includes a Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) from Government Dental College & Hospital in Patiala, India.

As the owner and lead dentist at Spring Dental, PC, in North Quincy , Dr. Sidhu offers a full range of general and family dentistry services, including preventive care, dental cleanings, fillings, crowns and bridges, root canal therapy, periodontal treatment, extractions, and pediatric dental care. She is known for creating customized treatment plans that focus on long-term oral health, function, and aesthetics.

Dr. Sidhu has also served as a general dentist with the Massachusetts Department of Correction, gaining valuable experience managing complex dental cases and dental emergencies. Earlier in her career, she worked as a Research Associate at Harvard University’s School of Engineering and Applied Sciences, contributing to advanced research in dental and tissue engineering.

A proud member of the American Dental Association (ADA) and the Massachusetts Dental Society, Dr. Sidhu stays current with the latest advancements in modern dentistry through continuing education. She is passionate about patient education and believes in empowering patients to make informed decisions about their oral health.

Outside the office, Dr. Sidhu has participated in community outreach and volunteer dental programs, providing free dental screenings and promoting oral health awareness. If you are looking for a trusted dentist in Quincy, MA, Dr. Gursimran Sidhu and her team at Spring Dental are dedicated to helping you and your family achieve healthy, confident smiles.

Dr. Ansh Desai

Dr. Ansh Desai is an experienced general dentist in Quincy, Massachusetts, providing comprehensive, patient-focused dental care to individuals and families in Quincy and surrounding communities. He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine and brings more than a decade of clinical experience to his practice.

Dr. Desai offers a wide range of dental services, including preventive care, restorative and cosmetic dentistry, surgical dental implants, root canal therapy, extractions, bone grafting, crowns and bridges, and dentures. His approach emphasizes thorough diagnosis, personalized treatment planning, and long-term oral health.

He has been practicing dentistry in Massachusetts since 2013 and has been serving patients at Spring Dental in Quincy since 2017. Dr. Desai is licensed in the state of Massachusetts and holds both Massachusetts Controlled Substance and Federal DEA registrations.

Dr. Desai is an active member of the American Dental Association, Massachusetts Dental Society, and the American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID), reflecting his commitment to continuing education and advanced dental techniques.

In addition to his clinical background, Dr. Desai proudly served as a Captain in the United States Army Reserve, an experience that strengthened his leadership, attention to detail, and dedication to service.

Dr. Desai is passionate about helping patients from Quincy and nearby communities achieve healthy, confident smiles through honest communication, gentle care, and modern dentistry.
