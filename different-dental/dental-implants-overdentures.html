  • Do not touch, pull, or adjust the implant site, stitches, healing cap, or temporary restoration. If your temporary tooth or restoration feels loose, contact Different Dental for guidance.

Which is better: dental implants or traditional dentures?

Most dental experts agree that dental implants are preferred because they are more stable and help to preserve jawbone density. But patients with tighter budgets can use traditional dentures as an effective alternative to dental implants.

How long will my dental implant last?

When you are consistent about proper oral hygiene and regular dental visits, then dental implants can last for decades… or even for the rest of your life.

When is the best time to get dental implants?

Ideally, the implant post should be placed as soon as possible after dental extraction. If you have been missing teeth for many years, then implants might not be an option due to bone loss in the area. Sometimes, bone grafting can be an option to strengthen the jawbone so that implants can be placed.

What is the difference between implants and dentures?

Implants involve posts that are secured to the jawbone, with the dental restorations fixed on top of these posts. On the other hand, dentures are removable prosthetics that are designed to fit snugly over the gumline.

How much do dental implants cost in Woodridge?

The cost of teeth implants depends on how many teeth you’re replacing and whether you need preparatory treatment such as a bone graft. At your consultation we’ll provide a clear, itemized estimate and review payment options, including financing through Cherry.

What are overdentures, and how are they different from regular dentures?

Overdentures are dentures that snap onto dental implants instead of resting on the gums. A maxillary overdenture replaces upper teeth and a mandibular overdenture replaces lower teeth. Because they’re anchored to implants, permanent dental implants and overdentures are far more stable than traditional dentures.

How do I care for my dental implants long term?

Care for full dental implants just like natural teeth: brush twice a day, clean between them daily, and keep up regular checkups at our Woodridge office. With good care, teeth implants can last for decades.
