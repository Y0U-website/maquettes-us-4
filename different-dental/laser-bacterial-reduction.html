What does LANAP mean?

LANAP stands for Laser-Assisted New Attachment Procedure, which is a specific type of laser treatment for gum disease (the PerioLase MVP-7). There are also other types of laser bacterial reduction treatments. Ask your local dental office about the type of technology they are using.

LBR dental hygiene is effective for various stages of gum disease, including advanced periodontal disease. It can help gum tissue to regenerate and reattach to the bone.

Is gum laser treatment different from deep cleaning?

Gum laser treatment is a supportive therapy that complements deep cleaning. But the laser cannot replace the mechanical removal of plaque and tartar under the gumline.

Laser bacterial reduction therapy uses a dental laser to reduce harmful bacteria in the gum pockets, often alongside a cleaning or gum treatment. It’s a gentle, minimally invasive way to support gum health and healing.

Is this the same as LANAP laser treatment?

They’re related laser gum therapies. LANAP is a specific laser protocol for treating gum disease, while laser bacterial reduction focuses on lowering bacteria. During your visit we’ll explain which laser approach fits your gum health.

Does laser treatment for gums hurt?

Most patients find perio laser treatment very comfortable, with little to no downtime. It’s often gentler than traditional gum procedures and can be used to help with gum recession and infection.

Coverage varies by plan and by the specific treatment. Our team will help you check your benefits and can arrange financing through Cherry if needed.
