Dental Implants in Arlington…

  • How long do dental implants last? With proper care, including good oral hygiene and regular dental checkups, the implant itself can often last 20 years or more—many even last a lifetime. The crown attached to the implant may need to be replaced after 10 to 15 years due to normal wear. Your dentist will help you properly maintain your implant for lasting comfort and function.
  • Are dental implants safe? Dental implants are considered a safe, long-lasting solution for replacing missing teeth when placed by a qualified dentist or oral surgeon. Made from biocompatible materials, they fuse with the jawbone to create a stable foundation for crowns or dentures. Most patients heal without complications, and success rates are very high. A thorough evaluation and good oral hygiene help ensure the best results and lasting comfort for many people today, everywhere.
