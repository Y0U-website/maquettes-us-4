Caring for your dental implants

Implants don’t get cavities, but the gums around them still need care. Brush and floss daily, and keep up with regular checkups and professional cleanings — our hygienist will show you exactly how to keep your implant healthy for the long run. If you grind your teeth at night, we’ll talk about a custom night guard to protect your investment.

Is a dental implant right for you?

The best candidates for dental implants are adults in good general health with healthy gums and enough jawbone to support the post. But even if you’ve been told in the past that you don’t have enough bone, don’t assume implants are off the table — modern 3D imaging often reveals options that older x-rays missed, and Dr. Adel will give you a straight answer after evaluating your specific situation.

Implants may not be the right first step if you have untreated gum disease or certain uncontrolled health conditions — and if that’s the case, we’ll tell you so and discuss alternatives like a bridge or partial denture. Our conservative philosophy means we recommend implants only when they’re genuinely the best solution for you, not simply the most comprehensive one.

Many of our implant patients come to us after years of hiding their smile or struggling with an uncomfortable denture. If that sounds familiar, a consultation is the easiest way to find out what’s possible — there’s no pressure and no obligation, just clear information about your options.

Dental implant FAQs

Straight answers about implants, comfort, candidacy, and cost.

With good oral hygiene and regular dental checkups, dental implants can last for decades — often a lifetime. The crown on top may eventually need refreshing, but the implant post itself is designed to be a permanent solution.

Most patients are surprised by how comfortable the procedure is. The area is fully numbed, the guided placement is precise and efficient, and any post-visit soreness is typically mild and manageable with over-the-counter pain relief.

Most healthy adults with missing teeth are candidates. You’ll need healthy gums and enough jawbone to support the post. At your consultation, Dr. Adel will evaluate your bone, gums, and overall health with digital imaging and give you an honest answer.

Cost depends on how many implants you need, the type of restoration, and your specific clinical situation. The best way to get an accurate number is a consultation with 3D imaging — we’ll give you clear, upfront pricing before anything is scheduled. We accept most PPO dental insurance plans and offer an in-house membership plan for patients without insurance.

A bridge fills a gap by crowning the two neighboring teeth, which must be filed down to support it. An implant replaces the tooth independently — no healthy tooth structure is removed, and it also preserves the jawbone beneath the gap.

A typical single-implant case takes a few months from consultation to final crown, because the post needs time to fuse with the bone. Guided digital planning keeps each appointment efficient, and we’ll give you a clear timeline at your first visit.
