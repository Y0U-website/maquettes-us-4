Our general dentistry services

Routine care is where healthy smiles are made — and where most dental problems are caught while they’re still small and simple to fix.

Comprehensive exams & digital x-rays

Every checkup includes a thorough examination of your teeth, gums, bite, and soft tissues, supported by low-radiation digital x-rays that reveal what the eye can’t see: decay between teeth, bone changes, and early warning signs. Catching issues early is what keeps treatment conservative — a small filling today instead of a root canal next year.

Professional dental cleanings

Even the best brushing and flossing miss spots. Our professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup, polish your teeth, and leave your gums healthier — while giving us a chance to spot concerns early. Most patients do best with a cleaning every six months.

Tooth-colored fillings

When decay is caught early, a tooth-colored composite filling repairs it conservatively — we remove only the damaged portion and rebuild the tooth with resin matched to your natural shade. No metal, no noticeable repair: just your tooth, whole again.

Dental crowns — including same-day crowns

When a tooth is too damaged for a filling — after a large cavity, a fracture, or a root canal — a crown rebuilds its strength and shape. Our same-day crowns are designed and milled right in the office, so many crowns are completed in a single visit: no temporary crown, no second appointment, no messy impressions.
