What Our Clients Say

What type of dental implants do you use?

We use premium Swiss Straumann implants , which are globally recognized for their superior quality, durability, and high success rates. These medical-grade titanium or ceramic implants are designed to integrate seamlessly with your bone for a long-lasting, natural-looking result.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with one or more missing teeth are candidates for implants. During your consultation in our Encino office, Dr. Irena Starchenko will use advanced CBCT 3D scans to evaluate your jawbone density. Implants are ideal because they help preserve your natural jawbone structure and prevent the bone loss typically associated with missing teeth.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The timeline varies depending on your individual needs. After the initial placement of the Straumann implant, a healing period of a few months is usually required for the implant to fuse with the bone (osseointegration). Once healed, we place a custom-made crown to complete your smile. We offer modern technology to make this process as efficient and comfortable as possible.

Are dental implants better than bridges or dentures?

While bridges and dentures are effective, dental implants are considered the gold standard for tooth replacement. Unlike bridges, implants do not require altering adjacent healthy teeth. Unlike dentures, they are fixed permanently in your mouth, meaning no slipping, no clicking, and no dietary restrictions.

Does the dental implant procedure hurt?

Patient comfort is our priority. We use localized anesthesia to ensure the area is completely numb during the procedure. For patients with dental anxiety, we also offer sedation options to provide a calm and relaxing experience. Most patients report that the discomfort after the procedure is minimal and manageable with over-the-counter medication.
