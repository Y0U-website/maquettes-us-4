About Dr. Parker & R Dentist Draper

Dr. Richard G. Parker was born in Richfield Utah. He grew up in the small town of Joseph Utah. He obtained his bachelor of science degree in zoology and a minor in chemistry at Southern Utah University.

He attended Indiana University School of Dentistry in Indianapolis Indiana where he received his doctorate degree in dental sciences, graduating in 2003. He has completed cerec training and now offers patients the convenience of having one appointment for crowns and onlays.

Dr. Parker continues to increase his knowledge in the field of dentistry. Dr. Parker enjoys getting to know his patients on a personal level and cares about their well being. He tries to create a friendly environment where patients can come in and feel that their oral needs are being met.

After dental school he practiced as an associate dentist in three other offices in the Salt Lake Valley. He has been in his current state of the art office since June 2004. Dr. Parker enjoys dentistry, learning new and advanced technology, meeting new patients and getting to know them. While not in the office he enjoys spending time with his wife Hanna, and his four children Skyler, Tyler, Sierra, and Kyle. Together they enjoy hunting, fishing, camping, boating, and riding ATVs in the beautiful landscape that Utah has to offer.
