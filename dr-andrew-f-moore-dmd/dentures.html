Restore Your Smile With Comfortable, Natural-Looking Dentures

Missing teeth can make everyday activities like eating and speaking more difficult. Dentures provide a practical way to replace missing teeth and restore the appearance and function of your smile.

Moore Family Dental Care offers full and partial denture options to help patients find a solution that fits their individual needs. We can also provide denture repairs and adjustments to help maintain a comfortable fit.

Full Dentures

Full dentures are designed to replace all of the teeth in the upper or lower arch. Custom-made to fit your mouth, dentures can restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with greater confidence.

Our team can evaluate your oral health and discuss whether full dentures are right for you.

Partial Dentures

Partial dentures can replace multiple missing teeth while working alongside your remaining natural teeth. They are designed to restore your smile and help improve everyday chewing and speaking.

We can discuss your options and help determine which type of partial denture best meets your needs.

Digital Dentures

Digital denture technology uses digital tools to help plan and create dentures with greater consistency. This approach can make the denture process more streamlined while supporting a customized fit and appearance.

During your consultation, we can explain the available denture options and determine what may work best for your smile.

Denture Repairs & Adjustments

Dentures can change in fit over time or become damaged through everyday use. Repairs and adjustments can help address issues such as discomfort, looseness, or minor damage.

If your dentures no longer fit comfortably or need attention, contact Moore Family Dental Care for an evaluation.
