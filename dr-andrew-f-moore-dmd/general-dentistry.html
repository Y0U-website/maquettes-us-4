Comprehensive Dental Care for Your Whole Family

Maintaining good oral health starts with consistent, preventive dental care. At Moore Family Dental Care, we provide comprehensive general dentistry services to help patients of all ages maintain healthy teeth and gums. From routine exams and cleanings to restorative treatments and periodontal care, our team is here to support your dental health at every stage of life.

Our goal is to provide dependable dental care in a comfortable, welcoming environment while helping you understand your treatment options and maintain a healthy smile.

Exams & Cleanings

Regular dental exams and professional cleanings are an important part of maintaining your oral health. During a routine visit, your teeth and gums can be evaluated for signs of cavities, gum disease, and other dental concerns. Professional cleanings help remove plaque and tartar that regular brushing and flossing may not completely eliminate.

Routine dental visits also give our team an opportunity to monitor changes in your oral health and recommend treatment when needed. Keeping up with regular exams and cleanings can help address potential problems before they become more serious.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

When a tooth has been damaged by decay, a filling can restore its function and help protect it from further damage. Tooth-colored fillings are designed to blend naturally with your surrounding teeth, providing a more discreet alternative to traditional metal-colored fillings.

The affected portion of the tooth is removed, the area is cleaned, and the tooth-colored material is carefully placed to restore the tooth. These fillings can be used to treat cavities while maintaining a natural-looking appearance.

Root Canal Therapy

When infection or inflammation reaches the inner portion of a tooth, root canal therapy may be recommended to save the natural tooth. This treatment removes the affected tissue from inside the tooth, cleans and disinfects the root canals, and seals the tooth to help prevent further infection.

Root canal therapy can help preserve a damaged tooth and restore its ability to function normally. If you experience persistent tooth pain, sensitivity, swelling, or other concerning symptoms, a dental evaluation can help determine whether root canal treatment is appropriate.

Periodontal Care

Healthy gums are an essential part of maintaining a healthy smile. Periodontal care focuses on preventing, identifying, and treating gum disease and other conditions affecting the tissues that support your teeth.

Professional periodontal cleanings may be recommended when plaque and tartar have accumulated below the gumline or when gum disease requires more comprehensive cleaning. Regular dental visits can help monitor the condition of your gums and support long-term oral health.

Family Dentistry

Dental care is important at every age. Moore Family Dental Care provides general dental services for families, making it easier to maintain routine care for children, teens, adults, and older family members.

Whether you need a routine cleaning, restorative treatment, periodontal care, or an evaluation for a specific dental concern, our team can help you understand your options and take the next step toward maintaining a healthy smile.
