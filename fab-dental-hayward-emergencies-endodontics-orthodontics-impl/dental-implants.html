Why Implants?

Implants are a permanent, long term solution to missing teeth. Learn more about the benefits of Implants .

Implants procedure is usually followed by a crown on top of the implant to make it look like a real tooth. The crown is made in the color of your existing teeth.

510-342-3908 Long Term Solution Dental Implants are made from Titanium - one of the hardest substance known to man. They can last a lifetime. Hence, they are a great long-term solution for replacing missing teeth. The success rate of implants is over 95 %.

No Daily Removal

Dental Implants are placed in your jawbone and are permanently attached there. Hence, you don’t have to remove them every night, or clean them like dentures. You can go about your day without worrying about them.

Prevent Jawbone Loss

Dental implants are like artificial roots for your teeth. To human body, they feel like real teeth roots and keep the jawbone from deteriorating.

Comfort, Look & Feel

Dental implants look and feel like real teeth. You’ll be able to eat your favorite foods, talk with comfort, and smile with confidence again.

Alternatives to Implants

There are multiple alternatives to implants for teeth replacements. It is best to know about all of them and then decide the one that suits you best.

When you come in for a consultation, we will go over all these options with you so that you can make an informed decision.

Dentures Regular Dentures are the first choice of many because they are non-invasive appliance. Natural, existing teeth may need to be prepared for dentures to fit properly. In some cases, teeth may need to be extracted and your mouth before new dentures can be placed. [Learn More... ]

Dental Bridge Sometimes there are healthy teeth around a missing teeth and a Dental Bridge can be used to bridge the gap between the healthy teeth. This removes the need for a surgery. [Learn More ...]

Implant Dentures A cheaper alternative to Full Mouth Implant could be Implant Supported Dentures. In Implant Supported Dentures, a few implants are placed in the jaw, and then a denture snaps-on to those implants. The benefit of this over dentures is that the denture doesn’t slip. [Learn More ...]

Full Mouth Implants If you want to get rid of the need to remove and re-attach dentures every night, full mouth implants are a great option. They give the feeling of natural teeth. Full Mouth Implants can be a costly procedure. [Learn More ...]
