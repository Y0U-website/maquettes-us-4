What Counts As Dental Emergency?

Chipped Tooth If you have a chipped or broken tooth, visit a local emergency dentist as fast as you can. If you have the broken piece of your tooth, bring it with you to the appointment. If you can’t find the broken piece, don’t worry, the dentist can still fix it.

Knocked Out Tooth If you have met an accident and a tooth has fallen out, it is a dental emergency! Get a same-day appointment from an urgent dental care facility. It’s best to preserve the broken tooth in warm milk or water until you get to the dentist’s office.

Urgent Root Canal If you have a tooth that hurts a lot or is very sensitive, it could mean that the nerves in the roots of your tooth are exposed. This can be a painful problem, and it’s important to see a dentist as soon as you can. The dentist will be able to do a root canal.

Painful Dental Abscess Dental Abscess or abscessed tooth means that there is infection in or around a tooth. It is easily treatable by a dentist. You don’t have to go to an emergency dentist for this. A general dentist can treat tooth abscess.

Urgent Tooth Extraction A cracked, broken or decayed tooth sometimes needs tooth removal. A painful wisdom tooth also needs extraction. A dentist can do emergency tooth extraction on the same day as your appointment.

Same Day Crowns & Fillings If a crown or filling has popped out, then it is best to see the urgent dental care on the same day or next day. Otherwise, there is a chance that food will get stuck in the area where the filling or crown came off.
