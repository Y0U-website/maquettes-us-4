What Is a Dental Implant?

Missing teeth can affect your ability to eat, speak, and smile confidently, not to mention impact your oral health. According to the American Academy of Implant Dentistry , dental implants are considered one of the most reliable tooth replacement options. Fortunately, dental implants offer a permanent solution to replace missing teeth, restoring both function and appearance. We offer affordable dental implants at Diamond District Dental Cosmetic and Implant Dentistry to help you regain a natural-looking, healthy smile.

A Dental Implants in Midtown NYC, consists of three parts: the implant post, which acts as the tooth’s root; the abutment, which connects the post to the final restoration; and the crown, bridge, or complete prosthesis that mimics the appearance of a natural tooth. With dental implants, you can enjoy a long-lasting, stable replacement for your missing teeth, and our experienced team is here to guide you through every step of the process. Whether you need a single implant or full mouth dental implants, we’re committed to restoring your smile with precision and care.

Implant Options to Fit Every Smile Need

Single Implants

Single dental implants are a highly effective solution for replacing a missing tooth, providing a stable and permanent option that closely resembles the look and function of a natural tooth. The implant consists of a titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as a sturdy foundation for a custom-made crown that restores aesthetics and functionality.

With proper care and maintenance, single implants can last many years, improving oral health by preventing bone loss and preserving adjacent teeth.

Dental Bridges

Dental bridges are a popular restorative solution for replacing one or more missing teeth by bridging the gap between adjacent teeth. They consist of two or more crowns placed on the natural teeth surrounding the gap, with a false tooth (or teeth) in the middle, effectively restoring both function and aesthetics.

Bridges can improve chewing ability, maintain facial structure, and prevent surrounding teeth from shifting, providing a durable and natural-looking solution for tooth loss.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures offer a stable and secure alternative to traditional dentures by anchoring them to dental implants placed in the jawbone. This type of denture provides enhanced comfort and function, allowing patients to eat and speak confidently without the worry of slippage common with conventional dentures.

Implant-supported dentures help preserve jawbone health by preventing bone loss. They offer a more natural look and feel while significantly improving overall oral health.

Full Mouth Dental Implants

Full arch dental implants are a revolutionary solution for individuals experiencing extensive tooth loss or seeking to replace an entire arch of teeth. At Diamond District Dental Cosmetic and Implant Dentistry, we have developed our implant protocol, the Diamond District Dental Full Arch, which utilizes a custom number of implant posts tailored to your unique dental needs.

These implants are strategically inserted into your jawbone to provide optimal support and unmatched function, ensuring a complete set of fixed prosthetic teeth that offers stability and reliability. This treatment restores a natural-looking smile and improves oral health by preventing bone loss and enhancing chewing efficiency, allowing patients to enjoy a confident and vibrant lifestyle.

Zygomatic Implants

Zygomatic implants are specialized dental implants designed for patients with significant bone loss in the upper jaw. They are an ideal solution when traditional implants are not feasible. These implants are anchored in the zygomatic bone (cheekbone) rather than the maxilla, providing a stable foundation for an entire arch of prosthetic teeth without the need for extensive bone grafting.

By utilizing zygomatic implants, patients can achieve a functional and aesthetically pleasing smile more quickly, often in a single surgical procedure, greatly enhancing their quality of life.

The Dental Implant Procedure

Consult & Planning Your dental implant treatment begins with an in-depth consultation. During this appointment, we will evaluate your oral health, take detailed digital scans, and discuss your dental concerns, goals, and expectations.

Based on this information, we create a custom treatment plan tailored to your needs, whether you’re getting single or full mouth dental implants. We’ll also review the timeline, the dental implant cost, and any other questions you may have. Our goal is to ensure you feel fully informed and comfortable before moving forward.

The Implant Procedure On the day of your surgery, we will utilize our customized implant protocol, Diamond Dental Full Arch, to ensure your specific dental needs are met. We will administer your choice of sedation and make small incisions in your gums to access the underlying bone. Following our protocol, we will place 4-6 strategically positioned implants in your jawbone, tailored to provide optimal support for your new smile and attach an abutment connector.

For many patients, we offer same-day temporary teeth, allowing for immediate attachment of a functional restoration right after the procedure! This means you can leave our office with a beautiful smile while your implants heal, giving you confidence and comfort during your recovery.

Follow-Up Appointment After your dental implant surgery, a follow-up appointment is scheduled to monitor your healing progress and ensure the implant integrates well with the jawbone. During this visit, we’ll check for any signs of infection, adjust temporary restorations if necessary, and provide further guidance on caring for your implants during the healing phase.

This follow-up is essential for ensuring that everything is on track and your implant is stable. We’ll answer any questions about the healing process and prepare you for the next step.

Healing & Final Restorations The healing period typically takes a few months as the implant fuses with the jawbone during osseointegration. Once the healing is complete, you’ll return to our dental office to receive your permanent restoration, whether it’s a crown, bridge, or full arch of teeth.

This final step involves placing the custom-designed restoration onto the implant, giving you a fully functional and natural-looking smile. With proper care, your dental implants will last for many years, providing a beautiful and durable solution for missing teeth.
