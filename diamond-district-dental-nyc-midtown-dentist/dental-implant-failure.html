What is LAPIP and How Can it Help?

LAPIP, or Laser-Assisted Peri-Implantitis Procedure, is a highly effective treatment for peri-implantitis that uses a laser to gently remove infected tissue and bacteria around the implant. Unlike traditional surgical methods, LAPIP is minimally invasive and promotes natural healing without the need for cutting or sutures.

The laser can disinfect the implant area while preserving healthy tissue and bone, which helps stabilize failing dental implants. This technique benefits patients who want to save their implants without undergoing extensive surgical procedures. By addressing the root cause of infection, LAPIP can help restore the health of your implants and extend their lifespan.

How You Can Benefit from the LAPIP Process

  • Minimally invasive, no cutting or sutures required
  • Targets and removes harmful bacteria while preserving healthy tissue
  • Promotes natural bone regeneration and healing
  • Reduces the risk of implant failures due to peri-implantitis
  • Faster recovery time
  • Saves dental implants without the need for replacement

What to Expect from the LAPIP Procedure

Unlike surgical procedures that require cutting and stitching, LAPIP is gentle and minimally invasive, allowing for a faster recovery. After the treatment, we’ll schedule follow-up visits to monitor the healing process and ensure the implant remains stable. The goal is to restore the health of your dental implants and prevent further complications from peri-implantitis.
