Who is a Candidate for Zygomatic Implants?

Zygomatic implants offer a stable, durable option for patients who want to restore their smile without additional surgical procedures. You might consider zygomatic dental implants if you’ve previously been told you are not eligible for regular implants or if you’re looking for a faster, more reliable way to regain a complete set of teeth. With zygomatic implants, patients who thought they were out of options can now enjoy the benefits of a strong, healthy smile.

What to Expect from the Zygomatic Implant Process

Consult & Planning Your treatment begins with a thorough consultation, during which we assess your oral health and determine whether zygomatic implants are the best solution. Using advanced imaging technology , we evaluate your bone structure and create a custom treatment plan. We will discuss the details of your treatment plan, including the cost of treatment and scheduling, and take the time to answer all your questions.

The Procedure During the procedure, we anchor long implant screws into the zygoma, also known as the cheekbone. Typically, this approach involves placing two zygomatic implants alongside two to four traditional implants in the front part of the jaw where the upper front teeth used to be.

If teeth need to be extracted before implant placement, we can perform the extractions during the same surgery. Before you leave, we’ll provide a natural-looking temporary restoration so you can immediately enjoy the benefits of your brand-new smile!

Recovery & Final Restoration Placement Over the next 3-6 months, your implants will fuse to your zygomatic bone through osseointegration. Once healed, you’ll receive a permanent restoration and successful integration of implants with your underlying bone. Like many other patients who have chosen zygomatic implants in New York, NY, you’ll relish remarkable functional and aesthetic advantages for years, potentially even a lifetime!
