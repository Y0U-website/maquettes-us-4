Our services

At Vertex Endodontics, we provide full-service solutions to ensure quality results for every patient. You have access to a comprehensive treatment plan to save your tooth and protect your smile’s overall quality and appearance.

Modern dental services to save the structure of a tooth by removing infection within the inner area, then sealing the tooth to protect it in the future.

In the rare situation that problems continue after a root canal, a retreatment can be an effective solution.

The endodontist can reach the tip of the root with a small incision, then treat the affected area as needed.

When an accident or injury occurs, it’s important to talk to an endodontist to minimize the risk of complications in the future.

Detailed images are helpful in the diagnostic process and also improve the overall quality of treatments.

Developing techniques offer an effective way to regenerate both soft and hard tissue.

Relax during your appointment with light or deep sedation, available in our office for your convenience.
