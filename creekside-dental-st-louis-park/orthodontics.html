Our Orthodontic Services

At Creekside Dental, we provide both traditional and modern orthodontic solutions. After a complete consultation and digital evaluation, we’ll recommend the approach that best meets your needs.

1. Braces

We offer metal braces for children, teens, and adults who want a reliable and time-tested solution. Braces are ideal for more complex alignment issues and allow for precise adjustments throughout treatment.

2. Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are a discreet, removable option for mild to moderate alignment concerns. Popular with adults and teens, they offer flexibility, comfort, and the freedom to eat without restrictions.

3. Invisalign

As a leading brand of clear aligners, Invisalign provides predictable, customized movement through a series of nearly invisible trays. Ideal for patients looking for an aesthetic, low-profile alternative to braces.

No matter which method you choose, we’ll walk you through every phase of treatment — from planning to retention — ensuring consistent, high-quality care.

Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment

Straightening your teeth isn’t just about appearances. Orthodontics can improve your oral health, jaw comfort, and overall confidence.

Orthodontics is often part of a larger treatment plan that includes restorative or cosmetic dentistry , especially when spacing or alignment needs to be corrected before placing veneers, crowns, or implants.

  • Easier brushing and flossing
  • Reduced risk of cavities and gum disease
  • Better bite alignment and jaw function
  • Decreased risk of tooth wear and chipping
  • Prevention of TMJ-related discomfort
  • Improved speech and chewing efficiency

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment timelines vary depending on the complexity of the case. Most patients wear braces or aligners for 12–24 months.

Is orthodontic treatment painful?

You may feel some soreness or pressure after adjustments or when switching aligner trays, but this is typically mild and temporary.

Do I need to change my diet with braces?

Yes — patients with braces should avoid sticky, hard, or crunchy foods that could damage brackets or wires. Clear aligner users can eat normally but must remove trays during meals.

How often do I need to visit during treatment?

Typically every 4–6 weeks for braces and every 6–10 weeks for clear aligner patients. These visits ensure your treatment is progressing as planned.

Are orthodontic results permanent?

Yes — as long as you wear your retainer consistently after treatment. Retainers help maintain the new position of your teeth long term.
