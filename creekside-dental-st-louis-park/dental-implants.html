Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer both functional and aesthetic advantages over traditional tooth replacement options. They are the most advanced method of tooth restoration available today.

  • Permanent, non-removable solution
  • Prevents bone loss in the jaw
  • Preserves facial shape and structure
  • No need to modify adjacent teeth (unlike bridges)
  • Natural look and feel
  • Reliable chewing function and bite stability
  • Easy maintenance with daily brushing and flossing

Are Dental Implants Right for You?

You may be a candidate for dental implants if you:

If you’ve been missing teeth for some time, we may recommend bone grafting or other preparatory procedures before implant placement. We&#x27;ll conduct a thorough evaluation to determine the best approach.

  • Are missing one or more teeth
  • Have adequate jawbone to support an implant
  • Are in good overall oral and medical health
  • Prefer a fixed alternative to removable dentures
  • Do not smoke or are willing to quit during the healing process

Dental Implant Process at Creekside Dental

Our dental implant process is designed to be efficient, comfortable, and tailored to your specific needs.

1. Consultation and Treatment Planning

We start with a detailed exam, including imaging, to assess your bone structure and determine whether implants are right for you. If you&#x27;re not a candidate for a single implant, we may recommend other options such as a dental bridge or partial denture.

2. Implant Placement

The implant post is placed surgically into your jawbone. Sedation options are available through our sedation dentistry services for maximum comfort.

3. Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next few months, the implant integrates with your bone. We’ll monitor your healing and schedule follow-ups to ensure everything progresses smoothly.

4. Restoration

Once the implant has fully healed, we’ll place a custom restoration — typically a crown — onto the implant post. The result is a complete, functional tooth that blends seamlessly with your natural smile.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Dental implants require the same level of care as natural teeth:

With consistent care, most implants can last a lifetime.

  • Brush twice a day with a soft-bristled toothbrush
  • Floss daily, including around the implant crown
  • Use a water flosser or interdental brushes for hard-to-reach areas
  • Schedule regular dental cleanings and exams
  • Avoid chewing on very hard foods or objects (e.g., ice, pens)

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

Are dental implants painful?

Implant surgery is typically performed under local anesthesia, and sedation is available if needed. Most patients experience only mild discomfort afterward.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, the implant post can last a lifetime. The crown or restoration may need replacement after 10–15 years due to wear.

Are implants better than dentures or bridges?

Implants offer superior stability, function, and bone preservation compared to dentures or bridges. However, each case is unique, and we’ll help you choose the right option.

Can I get implants if I already wear dentures?

Yes. Many denture wearers choose to upgrade to implant-retained dentures for improved comfort and security.

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Coverage varies by provider and plan. Our team will review your benefits and explain any out-of-pocket costs before starting treatment.
