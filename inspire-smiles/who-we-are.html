About Our Dental Practice

"The Fire Of Inspire" ‍ Elona Gaball, DDS, CHPC, Cosmetic and Restorative Dentist

I am an Accreditation Candidate Member of the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, which brings a tremendous amount of experience to my comprehensive oral care. I pride myself on my mastery of the art of dentistry, high character, and gentle approach. Because I treat my patients like family, they find me approachable and accessible.

My practice philosophy can be summarized in three words: "love," "honesty," and "wellness." I only recommend treatments that are indicated and will contribute to your specific wellness. I take an open, truthful approach to comprehensive conservative care, ensuring my patients understand their options in a soothing environment. I believe that the most beautiful smile is a healthy, natural smile—and that all well-done dentistry is cosmetic.

In my free time, I enjoy spending time with my husband and children, as well as practicing yoga, running, hiking, and eating healthy. I also love serving in my church. I&#x27;m passionate about personal development, attending Tony Robbins events, and participating in Landmark training.

As a proud and patriotic naturalized American citizen, I immigrated to the United States from Russia at the age of 15. I am also fluent in Russian!

Stephen Hutton, DMD, MS, MPH Periodontal Plastic Surgeon/Implantologist

I am passionate about providing the highest quality of care to my patients at every encounter. In addition to my clinical practice, I have had the privilege of serving as adjunct faculty for postgraduate hospital General Practice Residency Dental Residents for over 10 years. I am board-certified by the American Board of Periodontology and the International Congress of Oral Implantologists in Periodontics and Implant Dentistry. In my practice, I focus on integrating the relationship between oral health and systemic health to provide my patients with comprehensive care.

As a result of his experience in an array of public and private clinics serving diverse patient populations, Dr. Steve’s judgment in surgery is keen. He has not only dedicated himself to thousands of hours of continuing education, but he also lectures around the country to other dental professionals about treatment of periodontal disease and dental implant surgery using the latest digital planning and design workflows to precisely place implants and prevent peri-implant disease and complications.

Dr. Steve has published several articles related to oral plastic surgery, oral pathology and the relationship between tobacco use and developing oral lesions.
