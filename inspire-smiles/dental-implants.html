Your Personalized Implant Consultation

During your first personalized consultation at Inspire Smiles, we conduct a comprehensive, tailored implant evaluation to ensure that you’re a candidate for treatment. If necessary, we may recommend advanced procedures like a bone graft or sinus lift to prepare your jaw for the implants, creating a strong, seamless foundation for your smile transformation.

Precision Implant Placement

Once your treatment plan is finalized, we will guide you through the process of implant placement with unmatched precision. Your mouth will be carefully numbed, and the implant post will be surgically placed into your jawbone. For multiple implants, this process is repeated, ensuring the perfect alignment for a flawless smile.

Your Path to Healing

Post-surgery, we’ll make sure you&#x27;re comfortable as the implants bond with your bone, a process that typically takes 3-6 months. During this time, we’ll schedule follow-up visits to monitor your progress so everything heals beautifully. As your jaw heals, we collaborate with Beverly Hills Dental Laboratory to craft a custom restoration that complements your natural smile. Each restoration is meticulously designed, creating a final result that feels as unique as you are.

The Final Touch

When your implants have fully integrated, we’ll place your final dental restoration with precision, checking the fit for impeccable alignment. Once everything is perfect, we’ll bond the restoration in place, and you’ll leave with a radiant smile—one that feels authentically you. Enjoy renewed confidence and a complimentary gift card to celebrate your transformation.
