Get to Know Dr. Tarangini Suggala

Dr. Suggala believes that dental anxiety and the perception of pain associated with dentistry keep many patients from getting dental care and achieving the beautiful healthy smiles they so deserve. She exhibits patience in listening and understanding the needs of each dental situation. Ultimately, her goal is to develop professional and personal trust between the patient and herself. She took over Rosemeade Dental practice with the same vision of serving communities in and around Carrollton and the northern DFW area.

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Suggala earned her dental degree in India and practiced dentistry for more than three years before moving to the USA. The next step was to complete dental school a second time. Due to her hard work and extensive knowledge, she graduated with honors from The Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine. Today, she has more than a decade of experience providing patients in her treatment chair with the personalized, compassionate, and top-tier dentistry they deserve.

Dr. Suggala loves her profession and brings that passion and excitement for dental care to the office every day. As part of her dedication to excellent dental care, she stays on the leading edge of the dental field by participating in continuing education courses. As a result, she has experience in cosmetic, restorative, and surgical dentistry. Plus, she is an Invisalign certified provider!

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Outside work Dr. Suggala likes to spend time with her family, travel, and participate in community and cultural activities.
