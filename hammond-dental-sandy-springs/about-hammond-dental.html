About Hammond Dental

A Beautiful Practice for all of Your Dental Needs

Hammond Dental prides itself on providing excellent care for patients. With routine checkups and cleanings done every day, the team at Hammond will treat your teeth with the utmost care and will help walk you through every step of the process. Because dental visits can be a bit anxiety-inducing, the staff of Hammond Dental works hard to ensure that every patient feels comfortable and informed throughout every step of the process.

Appointments: Easy Online Booking!

If you would like to make an appointment, please contact our office by phone or email. Patients may also contact us after hours via Facebook Messenger , or by texting us. Emergency calls and appointments are available and welcome for both new and existing patients. We also offer easy online booking; click here to book your appointment now.

Before coming to your appointment please check our COVID-19 Safety guidelines

Cancellations

If you are unable to keep an appointment, we ask that you kindly provide us with at least 48 hours’ notice. We ask for this advance notice so we can offer this appointment to another patient. A $35 fee will be charged if a patient does not show up for an appointment without sufficient notice.
