Lorraine Celis D.D.S.

In 2002 Dr. Celis established her own practice in South Bend, Indiana providing comprehensive dental care to the community. Her practice also sees patients with a variety of special needs and language barriers. Dr. Celis is bi-lingual speaking English and Spanish languages fluently. By building strong relationships with her patients she hopes to communicate the importance of dental health.

She has been an active member of the American Dental Association , Indiana Dental Association and North Central Dental Association since 1998. A Past President of North Central Dental Association from 2010-2011, she has served as the Chair of Communications Council for the Indiana Dental Association and continues advocacy for her dental association.

An accomplished artist and photographer, Dr. Celis enjoys painting and photographing everything from beautiful flowers to motorcycle trials . She has lived in South Bend, Indiana for over fifteen years with her husband Mont and their two dogs.
