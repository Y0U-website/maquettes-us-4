Payment Plans

Jefferson Dental Center offers payment plans for established patients. A valid credit card or checking account is required. We will automatically deduct from your checking or credit card your payment and apply it to your account balance.

To set up a payment plan please have your Name, Address, billing information (ie. Credit Card or Checking Account) when you meet with our office manager to discuss your treatment plan. All payments will be recurring based on your choice of daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly.
