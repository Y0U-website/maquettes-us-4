The practice

Dr Almeida and Dr. Celis practice general dentistry in a modern dental practice located on Jefferson Boulevard in South Bend, Indiana. Their team of experienced dental professionals offers the highest level of conservative and ethical dentistry to their patients and their families

Jefferson Dental Center can provide you and your family all phases of general dentistry including hygiene, restorative, crown and bridge as well as oral surgery and removable dentures and partials. We accept most insurance and can offer payment plans in-house or through Care Credit, a nationally known lender for health care needs.

We offer a variety of appointment times to fit our patients busy schedules. Late afternoon appointments can be scheduled for afterwork and school.

We use modern dental technology to offer our patients the best dental health. Our technology also helps us to be more productive thereby keeping our dental fees lower.

In 2002 Dr. Celis established her own practice in South Bend, Indiana providing comprehensive dental care to the community. Her practice also sees patients with a variety of special needs and language barriers. Dr. Celis is bi-lingual speaking English and Spanish languages fluently. By building strong relationships with her patients she hopes to communicate the importance of dental health.

She has been an active member of the American Dental Association , Indiana Dental Association and North Central Dental Association since 1998. A Past President of North Central Dental Association from 2010-2011, she has served as the Chair of Communications Council for the Indiana Dental Association and continues advocacy for her dental association.
