Children Dental Care and…

  • Don’t let them swallow toothpaste – Children only need a pea-sized amount on their brush, and they should be taught how to rinse and spit.
  • Schedule dental appointments – every 6 months. These visits will include a thorough examination and a professional cleaning by your dentist at Smile 32 Dental Center or a dental hygienist.
  • Begin Flossing – before bedtime, once they get their permanent teeth.
  • Braces or extraction – to correct crooked or crowded teeth in their teen years.
  • Fluoride supplements – may be recommended by your dentist.

Prevention with regular dental visits

Regular dental visits serve two important purposes – to give teeth a very thorough cleaning, and to examine teeth and gums for signs of serious problems. Children can resist cavities with good diet and proper dental hygiene. Your dentist at Smile 32 Dental Center can discuss specific methods to fight tooth decay, including fluoride treatments and special sealants for children’s permanent molars.

Child Dental Care and prevention is important

Regular visits give your dentist at Smile 32 Dental Center a chance to detect small problems before they become major ones. Good hygiene and dental visits are cost-effective in the long run, preventing teeth repair and replacement.

Children’s Fear of Dentists

Smile 32 Dental Center recommends bringing your child to one of your routine dental visits. This will help them get acquainted with us and realize there is no fear in seeing us. We are experienced with children, will put your child at ease in a stress-free environment.
