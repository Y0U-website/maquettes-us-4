Adult Care and Prevention

  • Floss Your Teeth – Every day so that you can reach those places where your toothbrush will not reach.
  • Schedule Regular Dental visits – Your dentist at Smile 32 Dental Center recommend every 6 months. However, if you are prone to plaque build-up, we recommend visits that are more frequent. These visits include a thorough examination and a professional cleaning.
  • Discuss painful or problem areas – Your dentist at Smile 32 Dental Center as soon as possible, so that more serious problems can be prevented.

Regular Visits are a Must

There are two reasons why regular visits are essential. Your dentist can thoroughly clean your teeth and examine your teeth and gums for serious problems. Prevention of serious problems is essential care.

We never judge or criticize you for not keeping up on regular visits. You won’t get scolded here. We provide helpful and gentle care to develop the smile you’ve always wanted.
