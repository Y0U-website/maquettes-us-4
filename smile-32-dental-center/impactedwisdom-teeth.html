Impacted/Wisdom Teeth

Teeth that don’t come out of the gums properly are called impacted teeth. Sometimes they can appear partially through the gums, which can lead to problems such as tooth decay. Other times they can come in at an angle, which can crowd other teeth.

Problems with Wisdom Teeth

We recommend extraction of impacted wisdom teeth because they can cause problems in your mouth and with other teeth. Ask Smile 32 Dental Center to show you the position of your teeth on the x-ray, and explain the significance of their position.

The Location of Wisdom Teeth

Wisdom teeth are the third set of molars, the chewing teeth that appear in the back of your mouth. They usually appear in your late teens or early twenties.

Wisdom Teeth Extraction

Ask Smile 32 Dental Center can explain the details and options available for extraction of teeth. The removal of wisdom teeth is a common procedure and is only necessary if there is lack of room in the jaw or they are at an angle, crowding other teeth. Smile 32 Dental Center can accomplish the procedure or they can refer you to a specialist if needed. Extraction requires a local anesthetic to the numb the area.

Keeping Wisdom Teeth

Smart 32 Dental Centers only recommends extraction if there is no room for more teeth or when they are impacted. Many people can keep their wisdom teeth. It just depends on if they are going to cause problems for other teeth.
