Introduction to Orthodontic Treatment

Orthodontic treatment is a specialized branch of dentistry that focuses on correcting misaligned teeth and jaws. At Walnut Creek Dental, we offer a wide range of orthodontic options to address your treatment needs and help you achieve the smile of your dreams.

Our orthodontic treatment services are designed to straighten teeth, correct bite issues, and enhance the overall aesthetics of your smile.

Orthodontic Treatment Options We Offer in Walnut Creek

At Walnut Creek Dental, we offer a variety of orthodontic treatment options to cater to the diverse needs of our patients. These options include:

Our traditional metal braces are a tried and tested solution to straighten your teeth. They feature metal brackets that are bonded to your teeth and connected with a wire. They apply gentle pressure to shift your teeth into their ideal positions gradually.

We use modern advancements to make traditional braces more comfortable and discreet than ever before.

Clear aligners are also a popular alternative to traditional braces. Our Invisalign Treatment is top-notch and can help you achieve your teeth alignment goals discreetly.

These virtually invisible aligners are custom-made to fit snugly over your teeth and gently move them into alignment. Our clear aligners offer the advantage of being removable, allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods and maintain optimal oral hygiene during treatment.

If you are looking to accelerate your orthodontic treatment journey, we offer accelerated orthodontics. Using advanced techniques and technologies, we can significantly reduce treatment times while maintaining excellent results.

Our Comprehensive Approach to Orthodontic Treatment at Walnut Creek Dental

When you choose Walnut Creek Dental, you can expect comprehensive orthodontic treatment. We offer customized treatment plans created specifically for your dental problems.

We believe in taking an all-inclusive approach to Braces and Orthodontics. We do not only consider the alignment of your teeth but also the overall health and functionality of your mouth.

During your initial consultation, our skilled orthodontists will thoroughly examine your teeth, jaws, and facial structure.

This evaluation allows us to identify any orthodontic issues and determine the most suitable treatment options for you. We will take the time to listen to your goals and issues, and then we will ensure that your treatment plan aligns with your expectations.
