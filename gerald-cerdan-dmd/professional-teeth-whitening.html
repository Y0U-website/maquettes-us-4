Why Should I Get Professional Teeth Whitening?

Professional teeth whitening is a cosmetic dental procedure that can significantly improve the appearance of your smile. It&#x27;s an excellent option for individuals who have stained or discolored teeth due to factors like aging, smoking, and consuming certain foods and beverages. Unlike over-the-counter whitening products, professional teeth whitening treatments are tailored to your specific needs and can provide more noticeable and long-lasting results. Plus, the procedure is supervised by a dentist, ensuring safety and effectiveness.

The Professional Teeth Whitening Treatment Process

Initial Examination

The teeth whitening process begins with an initial examination. Dr. DeSimone will assess your oral health and discuss your whitening goals. This step is essential to ensure that you are a suitable candidate for professional teeth whitening and to determine the best whitening method for you.

Cleaning and Preparation

Once your suitability for the treatment has been confirmed, the next step is cleaning and preparation. This involves removing any plaque or tartar from your teeth and protecting your gums with a special barrier. This step ensures that the whitening agent can effectively penetrate your teeth for optimal results.

Whitening Procedure

The final step is the whitening procedure itself. Dr. DeSimone will apply a high-concentration whitening gel to your teeth. This gel is activated with a special light, which speeds up the whitening process. The gel is typically left on the teeth for about 15-20 minutes. After the gel is removed, you&#x27;ll be left with a noticeably whiter and brighter smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does professional whitening damage enamel?

When performed under a dentist&#x27;s supervision, professional teeth whitening in Winter Haven is considered safe and doesn&#x27;t cause lasting damage to your enamel. Some patients notice temporary tooth sensitivity afterward, but this typically fades on its own within a short time.

How much does it cost?

In-office teeth whitening is one of the more affordable cosmetic treatments we offer, though your exact cost depends on your specific treatment plan. We&#x27;re a fee-for-service practice, but we&#x27;ll gladly submit claims for PPO insurance plans and offer financing through CareCredit and Proceed if you&#x27;d like to spread out the cost.

How long do results last?

Whitening results aren&#x27;t permanent, since the same things that caused staining in the first place, like coffee, tea, red wine, and tobacco, will gradually cause new discoloration to build back up. Because of this, many patients schedule occasional touch-up treatments to keep their smile bright.

Will I have tooth sensitivity?

Some patients do experience temporary tooth sensitivity after whitening, which happens when the whitening agent reaches the inner layer of the tooth, though it typically fades quickly on its own. If you already have sensitive teeth, mention it beforehand so your dentist can plan accordingly.
