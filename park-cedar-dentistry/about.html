Dr. Summer Suttles

Dr. Summer Suttles is a proud Charlotte area native, bringing a deep understanding of our community&#x27;s needs to her dental practice. Her journey in dentistry began right here in North Carolina, where she completed both her undergraduate studies and dental education at the prestigious University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.

Dr. Suttles&#x27; love for her hometown and passion for oral health drives her commitment to providing top-notch dental care to her neighbors. Her approach combines the latest in dental techniques with a warm, approachable demeanor that puts patients at ease. When she&#x27;s not crafting beautiful smiles, Dr. Suttles enjoys working out and enjoying quality time with her family. Her love for fashion and home aesthetics shines through in her cosmetic dental work with her acute attention to detail and symmetry.

Dr. Suttles believes that a healthy smile is key to overall wellbeing and confidence. She&#x27;s dedicated to not just treating dental issues, but also educating her patients about preventive care , ensuring that the smiles she creates last a lifetime. She particularly is passionate about making same day CEREC crowns to deliver a seamless, more accurate, and easier experience for patients.

Dr. Suttles also acknowledges an epidemic of jaw-related stress among her patients and spends time educating them on the causes of teeth clenching and grinding, and various ways to prevent and treat related symptoms and damage to ensure a healthy smile.

Meet

When she&#x27;s not in the office, Dr. Snapp is an avid hiker, often found exploring the beautiful trails of North Carolina and beyond. Your new patient exam is all about getting to know you and your smile. We&#x27;ll start with a friendly chat about your dental history and goals. Then, we&#x27;ll perform a comprehensive exam, including digital X-rays and intraoral photos.
