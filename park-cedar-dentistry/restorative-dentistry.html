Dental Fillings

At Park Cedar Dentistry in Charlotte, NC, we offer dental fillings designed to restore your smile in the gentlest and most comfortable way possible. When tooth decay or damage occurs, a filling is used to repair the area, prevent further harm, and maintain the natural structure of the tooth. Our patients appreciate that this common procedure is performed with great precision and a warm touch.

Dental Crowns and Bridges

When you&#x27;re missing more than a few teeth in a row, your oral wellness takes a hit — not to mention your confidence and overall health as well. With same-day crowns and bridges , our team can restore your bite and complete your smile quickly and comfortably. Since crowns and bridges are affixed to your smile, they won’t move or shift, making them a great alternative to partial dentures. There&#x27;s also no invasive surgery involved, so you can skip the healing time and get back to smiling again. With our same-day technology, you can get a crown and bridge in just a single visit!

Dentures

If missing teeth are affecting your smile or confidence, dentures can provide a life-changing solution. Dentures are custom-made dental appliances designed to replace missing teeth, enhancing both function and appearance. Whether you’ve lost several teeth or all of them, dentures restore your ability to chew, speak, and smile comfortably and confidently.
