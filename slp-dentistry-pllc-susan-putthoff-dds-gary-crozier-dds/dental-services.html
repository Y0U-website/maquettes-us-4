Dental Services

Dental Services

P reventive Dentistry

Procedures that prevent disease, or reduce the likelihood of its advancement.

"An Ounce of Prevention is worth a Pound of Cure."

  • New Patient Exams (comprehensive)
  • Personalized Consultations
  • Periodic Exams (check-ups)
  • Dental Cleanings
  • Fluoride Treatments
  • Dental X Rays
  • Sealants
  • Oral Hygiene Instructions
  • Oral Cancer Screening

Cosmetic Dentistry

Procedures to enhance the beauty of the teeth and the smile.

  • Smile Makeovers
  • Porcelain Crowns
  • Porcelain Veneers
  • Composite Bonding
  • Tooth Whitening
  • Clear Orthodontic Liners for mild tooth straightening

Restorative Services

Procedures to repair your existing teeth.

  • Fillings Composite Tooth Color Fillings
  • Glass Ionomer Fillings
  • Silver Fillings
  • Crowns
  • Onlays
  • Build Ups for core strength
  • Root Canals

Dental Prosthetics

Procedures to replace missing teeth.

  • Using Natural Teeth Crowns
  • Bridges
  • Removable Partials
  • Removable Partial Repairs
  • Removable Partial Relines
  • Dentures with Locator Snaps on Natural Roots
  • Using Implants Crowns
  • Bridges
  • Removable Partials with Locator Snaps
  • Implant Supported Dentures
  • Edentulous (no teeth remaining) Traditional Dentures
  • Denture Repairs
  • Denture Relines

Periodontal Treatment

Procedures to correct problems with teeth, bone, and gums. Healthy gums and bones are important in keeping your entire body healthy.

  • Gum Disease Treatment-non surgical
  • Periodontal Maintenance
  • Splinting
  • Bite Adjustments

Oral Sports Appliances

Sports guards protect the teeth and jaw from damage during impact sports.

  • Custom Mouthguards

Occlusal Therapy

Procedures to prevent or treat functional problems related to the biting forces of the teeth, and function of the jaw.

  • Temporomandibular disorders (TMD / TMJ)
  • Night Guards
  • Occlusal Adjustments
