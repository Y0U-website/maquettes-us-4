Accessibility Statement

  • Navigate screens using your keyboard
  • Increase text size
  • Magnify your screen
  • Adjust background and text colors
  • Make your mouse pointer more visible (Windows only)

Closed Captioning

Closed captioning provides a transcript for video audio tracks, synchronized with the visuals. This benefits people who are deaf, hard of hearing, or in noisy environments. Most of ADA’s video content includes captions. Learn how to turn captions on and off in YouTube.

Volume Controls

Your computer, tablet, or mobile device includes volume control features. Each video or audio service may also have its own volume settings. We recommend adjusting both your device and media player controls to optimize your experience.
