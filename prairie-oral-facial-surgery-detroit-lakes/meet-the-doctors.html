  • OUR SURGEON

RYAN SMART, D.M.D., M.D., F.A.C.S.

Currently, Dr. Smart serves as the Owner and Partner at Prairie Oral & Facial Surgery in Fargo, ND. Prior to this, he was a Staff Oral & Maxillofacial/Head & Neck Surgeon at Essentia Health and High Desert Oral & Facial Surgery. His experience also includes roles in public health at the Montana Department of Public Health and Human Services.

Dr. Smart holds an Assistant Clinical Professorship at the University of North Dakota School of Medicine. He has been a guest lecturer at Harvard Medical School and Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, sharing his insights on a range of topics from intimate partner violence to anesthesiology.

He has an extensive list of publications and presentations, contributing to journals and speaking at conferences on topics ranging from Head and Neck Reconstructive Surgery to Oral & Maxillofacial Pathology.

Among his many accolades, Dr. Smart has been a recipient and nominee for the “Golden Stethoscope” recognition for clinical care. He has also received numerous academic honors, including the Dr. Frank Susi Endowed Prize Fund for Excellence in Research and induction into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society. A highlight in his list of honors is serving as an Honorary Torch Bearer in the 2002 Olympic Winter Games.

Dr. Smart is certified in Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS), Pediatric Advanced Cardiac Life Support (PALS), and Advanced Trauma Life Support (ATLS). He holds unrestricted medical and dental licensures in Texas and North Dakota.
