Dental Implant Surgery is Less Painful Than It Seems

Teeth go missing for many reasons—from impact injuries to gum disease. However, the pain of losing your teeth is far greater than dental implant surgery, done with the proper anesthesia. The healing process is carefully monitored for three to eight months thereafter.

Once your bone and implants integrate osseointegration and gums have healed, your surgical dental implants will feel completely normal! After that, you won’t feel any pain at all. As long as you take care of your gums, you’ll never have to have another implant surgery.

Understanding How Dental Implants Work

You may think the implant crown is placed into the bone or gum socket to replace a lost tooth. That’s not the case!

Our dentist first evaluates the patient’s existing bone structure. If the patient has the bone structure to fit the implant fixture, then the gum will heal around the surgically placed metal implant for a few months. Once this has healed, the implant crown or crowns can be fitted.

If there is significant bone loss where your teeth are missing, that’s not good. Our dentist will request a visit to a specialist for a bone graft surgery. This will prepare your jawbone structure for successful implant placement. In fact, our doctors believe you should place a bone graft immediately after a tooth extraction. That’s what he does after removing a tooth in order to prevent bone loss. Implants are the best prosthetic procedure to replace missing teeth.

It’s important to understand that implants need regular cleanings, like your regular teeth. You’ll need to schedule them to ensure that your implants last and that your gums remain healthy!
