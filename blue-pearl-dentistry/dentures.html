Dentures in Los Angeles

Dentures at Blue Pearl Dentistry

Patients from Carthay, Beverly Grove, Mid-City, and surrounding Los Angeles neighborhoods choose dentures to restore confidence, improve chewing function, and support facial structure. Each denture is designed for comfort, stability, and a natural appearance.

Why Patients Choose Dentures

Modern dentures are lighter, stronger, and more natural-looking than before. They remain a reliable and accessible solution for full or partial tooth replacement.

  • Restores Function Improves chewing, speech, and overall comfort. Ideal for extensive tooth loss.
  • Natural Esthetics Customized to complement your facial structure and desired shade.
  • Custom Fit Designed to match your bite and gum contours for comfortable daily wear.
  • Affordable Options Available with or without implants based on your goals.

How Dentures Are Made

We take detailed scans and impressions to create a denture that fits securely and naturally. Wax try-ins allow us to refine shape and fit before finalizing your prosthesis.

  • Digital scans and impressions for precision
  • Wax try-ins to adjust shape, fit, and esthetics
  • High-strength acrylic and ceramic tooth materials
  • Implant-supported stability options

Immediate Dentures

Immediate dentures are placed the same day teeth are extracted, ensuring you never leave without teeth during the healing process.

  • Same-Day Results Provides a complete smile immediately after extractions.
  • Protective Support Helps shield healing gum tissue and manage swelling.
  • Transitional Appliance Adjustments or replacement are required once healing is complete.

Partial Dentures

Partial dentures replace multiple missing teeth while keeping the remaining natural teeth in place. Flexible or metal clasps provide secure, comfortable retention.

  • Replaces Several Teeth Ideal for patients maintaining some healthy natural teeth.
  • Comfortable Secure Designed to match your gum line, bite, and esthetics.
  • Easy Care Removable for cleaning and maintenance.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures attach to dental implants for exceptional stability, preventing the slipping or shifting common with traditional dentures.

  • Exceptional Stability Attaches securely to implant abutments for confidence.
  • Improved Function Allows stronger chewing and a more varied diet.
  • Bone Support Implants stimulate jawbone to reduce bone loss.

Full Dentures

Full dentures replace an entire upper or lower arch of missing teeth, restoring facial support and everyday function with a natural appearance.

  • Complete Arch Replacement Restores a full upper or lower smile.
  • Precise Fit Crafted to improve comfort, function, and retention.
  • Natural Materials High-quality acrylics and ceramics for a realistic look.

Which Denture Option Is Right for You?

We evaluate your oral health, bone structure, esthetic goals, and lifestyle to determine the most suitable denture option. You’ll receive guidance on long-term care, relining needs, and maintenance.

Next Steps

If you are missing several or all of your teeth, dentures may be a comfortable and effective way to restore your smile. We work with many PPO plans and offer flexible financing options to make treatment accessible.
