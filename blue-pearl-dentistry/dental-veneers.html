Dental Veneers in Los Angeles

Veneers at Blue Pearl Dentistry

Patients throughout Carthay, Beverly Grove, Mid-City, and nearby Los Angeles communities trust our veneers for a natural, confident smile. Veneers are thin, durable shells bonded to the front of teeth to correct imperfections with stunning, long-lasting results.

What Veneers Can Improve

Veneers offer one of the most versatile cosmetic solutions, addressing both aesthetic concerns and minor structural issues.

  • Stains Discoloration Covers deep or resistant stains. Great when whitening isn’t enough.
  • Chips Worn Edges Restores smooth, uniform tooth shape.
  • Crooked or Uneven Teeth Creates straighter-looking alignment without orthodontics.
  • Gaps Between Teeth Closes small spaces for a more balanced smile.

How Veneers Work

The veneer process begins with a personalized cosmetic consultation. We evaluate your teeth, discuss your goals, and design a plan that fits your face shape, tooth proportions, and desired outcome.

  • Digital imaging and previews
  • Color-matched ceramic and composite materials
  • CAD/CAM and same-day CEREC options for certain restorations
  • Precision bonding techniques for lasting results

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers offer the most durable and natural-looking cosmetic enhancement. They are custom-crafted to your ideal shade, shape, and translucency, providing exceptional aesthetics and longevity.

  • Highly Durable Strong ceramic material that resists chips and stains.
  • Customized Aesthetics Tailored to your unique smile design and facial features.
  • Long-Lasting Results Often lasting a decade or longer with proper care.

Lumineers

Lumineers are ultra-thin veneers that require little to no removal of natural enamel. They are ideal for patients seeking a minimally invasive cosmetic upgrade.

  • No-Prep or Minimal-Prep Often bonded without modifying the underlying tooth.
  • Lightweight Natural Thin design offers a comfortable, seamless appearance.
  • Reversible in Many Cases Conserves natural tooth structure for flexibility.

Which Veneer Option Is Right for You?

During your cosmetic consultation, we’ll assess your enamel thickness, tooth position, shade goals, and aesthetic preferences to recommend the ideal veneer type—porcelain or Lumineers.
