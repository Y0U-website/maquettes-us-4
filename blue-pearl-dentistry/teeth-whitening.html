Teeth Whitening in Los Angeles

Teeth Whitening at Blue Pearl Dentistry

Patients throughout Carthay, Beverly Grove, Mid-City, and nearby Los Angeles communities come to us for whitening options that deliver noticeable, lasting improvements. Our professional treatments safely lift deep stains caused by coffee, tea, red wine, aging, and everyday habits.

Why Choose Professional Whitening?

Compared to over-the-counter products, professional whitening offers stronger, safer formulas with more predictable results. Each option is customized to your teeth and supervised by experienced professionals.

  • Fast, Noticeable Results Dramatically brighter smiles with minimal sensitivity. Great for events.
  • Customized Treatment Whitening plans tailored to your shade goals and stain type.
  • Safe Dentist-Guided Monitored by professionals for comfort and optimal results.
  • Multiple Options Choose in-office, take-home kits, or KöR trays based on your lifestyle.

In-Office Teeth Whitening

Our in-office whitening treatments offer the fastest and most dramatic results. Using professional-strength gel and protective measures for your gums and enamel, we brighten your smile comfortably in just one visit.

  • Immediate Results Many patients leave 2–3 shades brighter after their appointment.
  • Professional Protection Teeth and gums are carefully prepared to minimize sensitivity.
  • Ideal for Deep Stains Effective on discoloration caused by coffee, tea, wine, and aging.

Teeth Whitening Take-Home Kits

Our take-home kits offer gradual brightening with the flexibility to whiten on your schedule. These kits use custom-fitted trays and professional-grade gel for consistent results.

  • Custom Trays Designed to fit your teeth comfortably and distribute gel evenly.
  • Gentle, Controlled Whitening Ideal for patients who prefer slower, steady improvement.
  • Convenient Flexible Whiten at home, at your own pace.

KöR Whitening Trays

KöR Whitening is known for its ability to address deep, resistant staining with enhanced brightness and longer-lasting results. It combines in-office and at-home steps depending on your customized plan.

  • Deep Stain Removal Especially effective for older or stubborn discoloration.
  • Advanced Tray Design Seals out saliva to keep whitening gel potent longer.
  • Long-Lasting Results Many patients enjoy brighter smiles with less maintenance.

Which Whitening Option Is Right for You?

Your dentist will help evaluate your tooth shade, stain type, and sensitivity levels to determine the best whitening approach. Each system has unique strengths, and we’ll guide you toward the option that fits your goals.

Next Steps

If you’re ready to brighten your smile, schedule a consultation to explore your whitening options. We work with many PPO plans and offer flexible financing to make cosmetic care accessible.
