Dental Implants in Los Angeles

Dental Implants at Blue Pearl Dentistry

Patients from Carthay, Beverly Grove, Mid-City, and surrounding Los Angeles communities choose dental implants for their stability, longevity, and natural appearance. Implants replace missing roots with a secure foundation for crowns, bridges, or full-arch prosthetics.

Why Dental Implants?

Dental implants help preserve bone, improve chewing function, and restore confidence with results that look and feel like natural teeth. They also offer more durability than traditional dentures or bridges.

  • Strong Stable Implants fuse with bone for long-term reliability. Excellent for single or multiple teeth.
  • Natural Appearance Custom restorations match your smile for seamless results.
  • Protects Jawbone Helps prevent bone loss after tooth loss.
  • Improved Chewing Speech Offers comfort and function similar to natural teeth.

How Dental Implants Work

Dental implants involve placing a small titanium post in the jawbone, followed by a healing period where the implant integrates with the bone. After healing, we attach an abutment and custom restoration to complete your new tooth or teeth.

  • 3D digital imaging and cone beam CT for precise planning
  • Guided implant placement for accuracy and comfort
  • Custom abutments and ceramic restorations for natural esthetics
  • Digital impressions for a comfortable, efficient experience

Single Tooth Dental Implants

A single implant is an excellent replacement for one missing tooth. It restores full chewing ability and prevents surrounding teeth from shifting out of place.

  • Independent Support Replaces one tooth without affecting neighboring teeth.
  • Custom Ceramic Crowns Designed to match your natural shade and shape.
  • Long-Term Durability A stable solution that can last decades with proper care.

Full Mouth Dental Implants (All-On Options)

Full mouth implant solutions provide a stable, comfortable alternative to removable dentures. Using as few as four to six implants per arch, we support a full set of fixed teeth for improved function and esthetics.

  • All-On Restorations Secure, fixed teeth supported by strategically placed implants.
  • No Slipping or Irritation More stable and comfortable than removable dentures.
  • Restores Full Chewing Ability Enjoy a wider range of foods with confidence.

Implant-Supported Bridges

When several teeth in a row are missing, an implant-supported bridge fills the gap using two or more implants for support. This option is more stable and longer-lasting than a traditional bridge.

  • Replaces Multiple Teeth Ideal when several adjacent teeth are missing.
  • Preserves Healthy Teeth No need to file down neighboring natural teeth.
  • Strong, Esthetic Restorations Ceramic bridges designed for durability and beauty.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most patients with good oral health, healthy gums, and sufficient jawbone density qualify for implants. Even if bone loss has occurred, additional procedures may help prepare the area for successful treatment. We’ll evaluate your needs and provide personalized recommendations.

Next Steps

If you’re missing one or more teeth, dental implants may be the ideal long-term solution. We work with many PPO plans and offer flexible financing options to support your care.
