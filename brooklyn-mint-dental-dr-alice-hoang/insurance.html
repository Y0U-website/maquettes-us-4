Insurance Brooklyn

0 Insurance Brooklyn Mint Dental accepts most PPO dental insurance plans as an out-of-network provider. Many of our patients find there is a nominal difference, if any, in what their insurance will cover at our office, especially for preventative care, like check-ups and cleanings and they decide to see us regardless. Often these procedures are covered with $0 out of pocket for our patients.

Our team can give you a complimentary pricing estimate of what your insurance coverage could look like in our office. Please note that while our practice and insurance companies try to be as accurate as possible, insurance coverage varies by patient and by plan. Whether you’re in-network or out-of-network at Brooklyn Mint Dental, we provide quality dental care to all patients who walk through our doors. Your insurance company ultimately decides what they are willing to reimburse.

Our team does its best to ensure you make the most of your insurance plan benefits. We always strive to provide you estimates based on the information insurance is willing to provide us prior and send insurance claims out after services are completed. That said, because your insurance company, an unaffiliated 3rd party, ultimately decides your reimbursement, we still are unable to guarantee your coverage. We recommend patients to reach out to their HR or insurance company so they can fully understand the terms and conditions about their specific insurance policy.

Please contact us with the name of your insurance, subscriber ID, and your date of birth to provide you with this information and we can let you know how you can best use your benefits at Brooklyn Mint Dental.
