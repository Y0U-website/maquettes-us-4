Dr. Hansen’s Approach to Care

Dr. Hansen understands that anxiety often prevents patients from visiting the dentist in Cottonwood Heights. That’s why he does everything he can to make the experience smooth and easy for his patients, removing one of the biggest barriers between people and their oral health. He is dedicated to learning about cutting-edge technology and advances in dentistry and leads by example when it comes to helping his patients feel like valued family members whenever they are here.

Dental Education & Training

After attending BYU, Dr. Hansen graduated from the University of Louisville with his dental degree in 2012. He then completed a year-long residency at the University of Utah, receiving advanced training in cosmetic dentistry, sedation dentistry, and dental implants. He has taken courses in functional aesthetics with the renowned Dr. Hornbrook and dedicates hours of continuing education focused on oral surgery, occlusion, and more each year.

Outside of the Dental Office

Dr. Hansen and his wife met in a dance class at BYU, and they now have five energetic children, Lydia, Emree, Zander, Deakon, and LaRue. When he isn’t workshopping his dad jokes with them, he likes to mountain bike, volunteer with his church’s youth program, play piano, and coach his kids’ teams.
