What Are Implant Dentures?

You’re likely already familiar with the concept of a traditional denture, which sits directly on your gums. Implant dentures are different in that they rely on sturdy, long-lasting dental implant posts to hold them in place. The posts are surgically inserted into the jaw, and they fuse with the surrounding bone via a process called osseointegration; this allows them to serve the same functions as your natural tooth roots.

When you get implant dentures, they can either be fixed or removable. Both options have specific advantages that make them worth considering. We can help you figure out which type of implant denture is right for you when you visit us for your initial consultation.

Fixed/Permanent Implant Dentures

Fixed implant dentures remain anchored to the implant posts at all times. This can make them an attractive choice for patients who don’t want to have to remove their prosthesis to clean it. However, fixed implant dentures often need more implant posts to support them, so they usually require higher levels of jawbone density.

Removable Implant Dentures

When you get a removable implant denture, the implant posts will stay in place 24/7, but the prosthesis will be made so that you can take it out of your mouth at any time. Removable implant dentures typically call for fewer implant posts, so they can be a better option for those with less jawbone density.

The Implant Denture Process

There are four main steps for getting implant dentures:

  • During an initial consultation, our team will confirm that implant dentures are a viable option for rebuilding your smile. We will also check to see if any preliminary treatments might need to be completed to prepare your mouth for dental implants in Cottonwood Heights.
  • A specific number of implant posts will be surgically placed in predetermined spots in your mouth.
  • Once osseointegration is complete (which normally takes a matter of months), abutments will be attached to the implant posts.
  • A customized denture will be created and attached to the implant posts via the abutments.

Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

We can only determine your candidacy for implant dentures after examining your mouth and evaluating your overall oral health situation. The treatment may be a good fit for you if:

  • You have lost most or all of your teeth.
  • You aren’t suffering from gum disease or another type of oral health issue that can increase the risk of dental implant failure.
  • There’s plenty of bone present in your jaw.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Unlike traditional dentures, implant dentures will not slip out of place while you’re eating a meal or speaking to someone. Furthermore, since the implant posts can stimulate the jaw the same way natural tooth roots do, they can help fight bone loss. And if you’re diligent about taking care of your implant dentures, you can expect them to last for a very long time.
