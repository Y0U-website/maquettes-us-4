The practice

After finishing four years of dental school in Philadelphia, Dr. CJ completed a two-year pediatric dental residency at Northwestern/Children’s Memorial Hospital in Chicago, IL. For 2 years, Dr. CJ received extensive training in understanding child development, behavior management, trauma and treatment of infants, children, adolescents and special needs patients. Dr. CJ has been a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist since 2010.

While at Temple, Dr. CJ organized two mission trips to Haiti through the Haitian Health Foundation. There, he and fellow students provided much needed dental care to children and adults. He also served as Class President and Student Body President.

He continued his studies in Pediatric Dentistry at Northwestern University and The Children’s Memorial Hospital in Chicago, where he served as Chief Resident in 2008. After residency, Dr. CJ worked in private practice in the Chicagoland suburbs providing high quality pediatric dental care. Dr. Wagner prides himself on providing the highest quality dental care with his trademark sense of humor and easy-going style that helps children and parents feel at ease during their visits.

In his free time, you can find Dr. CJ at Bucks games, making and drinking coffee, listening to the smoothest of smooth music, playing drums and tie dying anything he can find.

Dr. CJ lives in Mequon, Wisconsin with his wife Danielle, their four children, and two Australian Labradoodles Remy and Duncan!
