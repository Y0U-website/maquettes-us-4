Our Doctors

As a dental specialist, Dr. Dersley is one of Maryland’s top experts in helping patients with major dental problems. He has helped his patients overcome such problems as: embarrassment with their teeth, severe dental anxiety, social problems due to not being able to eat a meal comfortably, and chronic dental pain due to unhealthy teeth.

Dr. Dersley believes that when you can smile with confidence, chew comfortably and have a healthy mouth, life is better. His focus is to provide you with a plan that will ensure comfortable care, minimal pain, and a confident and healthy smile for a lifetime.

Dr. Murphy is a University of Maryland School of Dentistry graduate who recently completed a General Practice Residency at Wellspan York Hospital in York, Pennsylvania. In residency, she received advanced training in the hospital and emergency settings and provided routine dental care to the underserved population. As a Southern Maryland native, she is excited to return to her home state and provide for her community.

Before becoming a dentist, she earned her undergraduate degree in Biology at Salisbury University and received a Masters of Public Health from West Virginia University.

In her free time, she enjoys longboarding, sailing on the Chesapeake, exploring National Parks, and spending time with her family, friends, and her lovable retriever, Goose.
