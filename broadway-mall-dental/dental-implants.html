How Do Implants Work?

An implant is a way to replace a missing tooth. A person with several missing teeth can get just as many implants. The foundation of the implant is a titanium screw. We place these screws into the jawbone and wait for them to fixate firmly. This can take several months. During this period, we’ll attach a temporary artificial tooth so patients can eat and live freely.

After we’re satisfied with how firm the screw is attached, we’ll remove the temporary tooth and attach a permanent one. The whole setup is fixed and can’t be removed at home or anywhere other than the dentist’s office.

In patients with weak jawbones, we might need to perform a bone graft. This is the usage of synthetic or cadaver bone to strengthen the jawbone to allow the attachment of a titanium screw. Having to use a graft will lengthen the entire process.

How Long Do They Last?

There’s no expiration date on implants. They should last for the person’s lifetime. Sometimes the covering tooth gets cracked or damaged. When that happens, we can easily replace it without any impact on the screw. The titanium screw is the most important part.

Artificial teeth aren’t indestructible, and patients need to continue taking care of their dental health. It’s also important to know that artificial teeth can stain. Unlike natural teeth, whitening products don’t work on artificial teeth. If an artificial tooth becomes deeply stained, we can replace it if you’re unhappy with how it looks.
