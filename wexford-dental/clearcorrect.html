ClearCorrect

In Wexford

ClearCorrect is a modern orthodontic treatment that uses clear, custom-made aligners to straighten teeth without the use of traditional metal braces. These aligners are removable and comfortable, offering a discreet option for individuals who want to improve their smile without the visibility of traditional braces. ClearCorrect aligners are made from smooth, durable plastic that gently shifts teeth into their desired positions over time.

Benefits of ClearCorrect

Wexford Dentist

Invisible Treatment

ClearCorrect aligners are made from transparent plastic, making them nearly invisible when worn. This allows you to straighten your teeth discreetly without the visible appearance of traditional metal braces, boosting your confidence during treatment.

Removable for Convenience

The aligners are removable, offering the convenience of taking them out for eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing. This flexibility helps maintain your oral hygiene and allows you to enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions.

Custom-Tailored Treatment

Each set of ClearCorrect aligners is custom-made to fit your teeth precisely. Dr. Mele uses 3D technology to design a treatment plan that gradually shifts your teeth into the desired position for optimal results.

Did You Know?

Did you know that ClearCorrect aligners are made from a comfortable, smooth plastic that reduces the irritation often caused by traditional braces? The custom-made, clear design provides an almost invisible treatment option for those seeking orthodontic care.

The ClearCorrect Treatment Process

Wexford Dentist

Consultation and Planning

Dr. Mele will begin by assessing your teeth, discussing your goals, and creating a custom treatment plan. Digital scans and impressions of your teeth will be taken to design your aligners.

Wearing the Aligners

You will wear each set of ClearCorrect aligners for about 1-2 weeks before moving on to the next set. Dr. Mele will ensure that your aligners fit properly and provide the necessary pressure to move your teeth.

Regular Check-Ups

Dr. Mele will schedule regular visits to monitor your progress. During these visits, she will provide you with new aligners and make adjustments to your treatment plan as needed to ensure success.
