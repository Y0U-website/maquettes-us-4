Aligners

In Wexford

Clear aligners are an orthodontic treatment that gradually moves teeth into their correct position using custom-designed, transparent trays. These trays are discreet, comfortable, and removable, making them a popular option for patients looking to straighten their teeth without traditional metal braces.

Benefits of Aligners

Wexford Dentist

Discreet Appearance

Aligners are clear and custom-made to fit your teeth, making them nearly invisible. You can straighten your teeth without the noticeable appearance of traditional metal braces.

Comfortable and Removable

Aligners are made from smooth plastic, offering a comfortable fit. Since they are removable, you can take them out when eating, drinking, or brushing, making them convenient and easy to maintain.

Customizable Treatment Plan

Aligners are designed based on a personalized treatment plan created from 3D scans of your teeth. Dr. Mele will guide you through the process, ensuring that each set of aligners moves your teeth into the desired position.

Did You Know?

Did you know that clear aligners are a great option for both teens and adults who want to straighten their teeth without the visibility of traditional metal braces? Aligners can be worn for many dental issues, including spacing, crowding, and bite correction

The Aligners Treatment Process

Wexford Dentist

Consultation and Impressions

Dr. Mele will begin by assessing your teeth and discussing your cosmetic goals. Detailed impressions and digital scans of your teeth will be used to create your custom aligners.

Wearing Your Aligners

Each set of aligners is worn for approximately 1-2 weeks. Dr. Mele will ensure that your aligners fit properly and make adjustments as necessary throughout the treatment.

Regular Check-Ups

Dr. Mele will schedule regular visits to monitor your progress. At each visit, new aligners are provided, and any necessary changes are made to keep your treatment moving forward.
