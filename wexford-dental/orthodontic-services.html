Orthodontic Services

Invisalign®

Wexford Dentist

Invisalign® is a discreet and comfortable orthodontic treatment that uses clear, removable aligners to gradually straighten your teeth. Custom-made for your unique smile, Invisalign® aligners are nearly invisible and allow for easier oral hygiene. This treatment option offers flexibility and is ideal for patients seeking a more subtle approach to orthodontics.

Orthodontic Services

Aligners

Wexford Dentist

Orthodontic aligners are clear, plastic trays that gently shift your teeth into proper alignment over time. Like Invisalign®, aligners are designed to fit your unique smile and can be removed for eating, drinking, and cleaning. Aligners offer a comfortable, convenient alternative to traditional metal braces, with fewer office visits required.

Orthodontic Services

ClearCorrect

Wexford Dentist

ClearCorrect is a clear aligner system similar to Invisalign® that works to straighten teeth using custom-made, comfortable trays. ClearCorrect offers a discreet treatment option for patients who want to correct misalignment without the visibility of traditional braces. The system is effective in treating a wide range of orthodontic issues, including spacing and crowding.

SureSmile

Wexford Dentist

SureSmile uses advanced digital imaging technology to create personalized aligners that move your teeth with precision and efficiency. The treatment is designed for comfort and fast results, and SureSmile aligners are nearly invisible, offering a convenient, subtle option for patients who want to achieve a straighter smile without traditional braces.
