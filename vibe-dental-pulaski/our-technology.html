Our Technology

Our Technology Dental Services Contact Us Advanced Technology Enhances Your Patient Experience At Vibe Dental Pulaski, we invest in the latest dental technology to ensure your care is accurate, efficient, and as comfortable as possible. Our advanced tools allow us to diagnose with precision, treat with confidence, and make your visits smoother from start to finish. From imaging to treatment, our technology supports a higher standard of dental care for every patient, every time.

Say goodbye to messy, uncomfortable molds. Digital impressions use advanced scanning technology to create a highly accurate 3D model of your teeth and gums. This makes procedures like crowns, bridges, and clear aligners faster, more precise, and more comfortable. It also reduces the need for retakes, saving you time in the chair.

Digital X-rays offer clearer images with significantly less radiation than traditional X-rays. They allow us to detect cavities, bone loss, and other dental issues early, often before you feel symptoms. The process is quick, safe, and images appear instantly on our screens. This helps us diagnose more accurately and discuss your treatment options right away.

Our intraoral camera captures high-resolution images from inside your mouth in real time. This small, handheld tool helps us spot issues that may be missed with the naked eye. It also allows you to see exactly what we see, making it easier to understand your diagnosis and recommended treatments. It’s a powerful tool for transparency and patient education.

Laser dentistry provides a gentle, modern alternative to traditional tools, especially for soft tissue treatments. It can be used to treat gum disease, reshape gums, or remove lesions with minimal discomfort. Many laser procedures are faster, quieter, and require less healing time. It also often reduces the need for anesthesia or sutures.

Our soft tissue laser is specifically designed to treat the gums and other soft areas of the mouth with precision. It can be used to contour gum lines, treat infections, or assist in healing cold sores. The laser minimizes bleeding and swelling, making recovery faster and more comfortable. It’s an ideal tool for patients looking for minimally invasive treatment options.
