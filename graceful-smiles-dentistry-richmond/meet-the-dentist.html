Dr. Yican Cao

Meet Dr. Yican Cao, a dedicated dentist who graduated from NYU College of Dentistry in 2021. With a passion for providing exceptional patient care, Dr. Cao combines advanced dental techniques with a warm, welcoming approach. Committed to staying at the forefront of dental innovations, Dr. Cao aims to create healthy, beautiful smiles while ensuring that each patient feels comfortable and informed. Whether you’re seeking routine care or specialized treatment, Dr. Cao is here to help you achieve your dental goals.

Dr. Timothy Wang

Dr. Timothy Wang attended The University of Texas at Austin where he majored in Biology. He then went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery from NYU College of Dentistry. After dental school, Dr. Wang completed a rigorous general practice residency at St. Barnabas Hospital in New York, where he gained advanced training in various dental procedures.

Since graduating, Dr. Wang has worked in multiple dental offices, both corporate and private, where he has accumulated valuable experience across a range of procedures and patient needs. Dr. Wang is committed to provide high-quality care tailored to each patient’s unique circumstances.

Outside of his practice, he enjoys traveling, trying new restaurants, and spending time with his family and friends.
