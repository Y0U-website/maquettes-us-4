What are Orthodontics?

Orthodontic treatment is essential in correcting the look and feel of your smile. If your teeth are crowded, crooked or if you have an underbite or overbite, you can benefit from treatment. Patients of all ages can benefit from orthodontic work if they are unhappy with their smiles. In fact, it’s not uncommon for adults to need re-treatment later in life because they never maintained their results from previous treatment.

Why are Orthodontics needed?

You may need orthodontic treatment if your teeth are crooked, crowded or misaligned. Likewise, treatment may be necessary if you have an overbite or underbite. Treatment is specific to each individual patient. Some patients will need to wear braces for only a few months while others benefit from a few years worth of orthodontics.

Who’s a good candidate for Orthodontics?

Most patients who have crooked or misaligned teeth can benefit from orthodontic treatment. You are never too old to come in for orthodontic care. Treatment doesn’t just help the look of your smile, but it can help in creating a healthier mouth and teeth that are easier to clean. We will examine your teeth prior to beginning any type of treatment to create a personalized orthodontic plan.
