Dr. Daniel T. Richardson grew up in Edmonton, Canada. He received his undergraduate degree from Oakwood College and his Doctorate of Dental Medicine from the University of Pennsylvania. He then obtained his medical degree at Harvard Medical School.

Prior to entering private practice, Dr. Richardson completed a rigorous oral and maxillofacial surgical internship and residency program at Massachusetts General Hospital and Harvard-affiliated hospitals, including Children’s Hospital of Boston and Brigham and Women’s Hospital. In 2009, Dr. Richardson opened Richardson Center Oral & Facial Surgery. He and his staff are dedicated to prioritizing excellence of care in a friendly and relaxed environment, where each patient is cared for as an individual.
