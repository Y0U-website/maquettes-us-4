MEET OUR DOCTORS

Dr. Kristin Vernon was born and raised in San Diego, CA. She attended San Diego State University and graduated with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology. In 1999, Dr. Vernon was awarded her Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) from Northwestern University Dental School in Chicago, IL. Upon graduation, she decided to further advance her knowledge in dentistry by completing a 1-year Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency program through the U.S. Army. Dr. Vernon served 2 more years in the Army, stationed at Ft. Lewis, WA (JBLM)

Dr. Shin grew up in Silverdale, WA and graduated from the University of Washington School of Dentistry. What drew her to dentistry was her heart for the underserved. When she’s not in the office, she enjoys volunteering at the Olympia Union Gospel Mission Dental Clinic and serving in the children’s ministry at her church. She and her husband love to travel, run, try new recipes, and explore the outdoors. Please share any must see places at your next visit!

Fun fact: Dr. Shin did a summer sunrise summit and also a winter summit of Mount St Helens in the same year!
