  • Membership - Nampa Smiles Smart Dental Care. Simple Savings. No Insurance? No Problem. The Membership gives you affordable, predictable dental care with built-in savings and zero insurance hassle. Membership Call Today Why We Created the Membership Plan We recognize that not everyone has dental insurance,and too often patients get priced out of preventive care. We’d rather see you twice a year for cleanings than several times for emergencies. Our membership program makes dental care affordable and accessible for every family. Program Guidelines Must not have dental insurance
  • Participant fee must be paid before the first appointment.
  • Can not be used as a Secondary Insurance
  • All participant fees are non-refundable.
  • Can Only be used at Todays Dentistry
  • Participation is valid for one (1) year.
  • Prepay $299 for the year
  • Restrictions apply with Care Credit.
  • Does not apply with out of office referrals, e.g., specialists.
  • Non-transferable between offices.
  • Todays Dentistry reserves the right to amend the program at any time.
  • Limited Exams are not included in the MSP and will be charged separately but at a discounted rate.
  • Participants must pay an annual renewal fee on or before their anniversary date to receive ongoing benefits.
