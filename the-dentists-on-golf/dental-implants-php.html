Dental Implants are an option for individuals who have lost teeth due to some accident, injury, or disease. Dental implants are different from dentures and other tooth replacement procedures. Because they can involve the surgical placement of titanium posts in the jawbone. Over a period of months, the surgically placed titanium posts combine with the bone, which allows them to act as stable replacements for natural tooth roots. Titanium posts are secured with replacement teeth to provide natural-looking, sturdy restorations.

Most dental implant procedures begin in a similar process. To receive dental implant services you must have healthy gums and surrounding teeth. While periodontal disease candidates are not good for dental implant treatment services.
