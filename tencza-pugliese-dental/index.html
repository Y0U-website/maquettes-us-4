The practice

Our dental services include preventive and restorative treatment to ensure that your whole family has great oral health. We’ll help you get on a regular cleaning schedule where our dental team can help you maintain good oral hygiene with exams every six months. You’ll get a deep cleaning by one of our professional staff members, x-rays, or fluoride treatment, depending on your dental needs.

Dental emergencies are never fun, but we’re here to help. If you have a toothache, chipped tooth, or other dental emergency, we’ll do our best to get you in for a dentist appointment as soon as possible. We understand that dental emergencies can be painful and disruptive, so we’ll do everything we can to get you out of pain and on your way.

Dr. Tencza (AKA Dr.Lou) grew up in Northeastern, PA. He completed dental school at the University of Maryland College of Dental Surgery, and did his residency at the Washington DC Veterans’ Administration Medical Center.

Dr. Lou has been practicing dentistry for over 26 years. He is a family and cosmetic dentist providing excellent dental care in Easton, PA and the surrounding communities. With his genuine kindness and a gentle hand, he has established long-lasting relationships with his patients. He believes in taking care of the person first, then taking care of the patient .

Dr. Lou continues his education by attending conferences and courses conducted by some of the country’s leading dental experts, and belongs to various dental organizations, including the ADA, AACD, and the AGD to name a few.
