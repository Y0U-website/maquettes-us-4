What is early orthodontic treatment?

Identification of any potential teeth or jaw problems in young ones early is straightforward! As your child’s baby teeth start to fall out and their adult teeth come in, we can keep everything aligned. The process can save them from needing more complex treatments later in their teen years.

We suggest bringing your child in for an orthodontic checkup as soon as their baby teeth begin to loosen!

Why might early orthodontic treatment be needed?

Early orthodontic treatment has many advantages. It can help avoid the need for more complex procedures later. By guiding permanent teeth into proper alignment as they emerge, we can ensure your child’s smile is full, natural, and healthy. Since misaligned teeth can lead to oral hygiene problems and affect self-confidence, early orthodontic treatment is a great option to discuss for your child.

FAQs

Who is a candidate for early orthodontic treatment?

Early orthodontic treatment is ideal for children who:

An exam with x-rays will help determine the best course of action.

  • Have crowded or misaligned teeth
  • Show early signs of bite issues
  • Have difficulty chewing or speaking due to tooth alignment
  • Have permanent teeth that are coming in improperly

What happens during an early orthodontic consultation?

The dentist will gently take x-rays, make impressions, and do a check-up to see how your child’s teeth and mouth are growing. Depending on what they find, they might suggest some helpful tools like retainers, braces, or other little things to help.

At what age should my child be evaluated for orthodontic treatment?

An orthodontic checkup around age 7 is generally normal. It helps the specialists find any potential small issues before they become bigger ones.

Will my child need braces right away?

Not necessarily! Some children may need simple appliances first, while others may require braces. The treatment plan depends on their individual needs.

How long does early orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment duration varies, but early intervention is often shorter than waiting until the teenage years. Regular follow-ups ensure progress stays on track.

Does early orthodontic treatment hurt?

The process is gentle, and any discomfort is minimal. Appliances and braces are designed to be comfortable while guiding proper tooth alignment.

Can early treatment prevent the need for braces later?

In many cases, early orthodontic intervention can reduce the severity of future dental issues. However, some children may still need braces later for fine-tuning.

What happens if we don’t get early treatment?

Without early intervention, misalignments may worsen, making orthodontic treatment more complex and time-consuming in the future.
