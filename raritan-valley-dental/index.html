The practice

Modern dentistry means better dentistry. When you seek care at our office, rest assured we use the latest technologies to enhance your outcomes.

We offer sedation dentistry to make your visit calm and relaxing. Often called "laughing gas," Nitrous oxide is a very safe, mild sedative that will help you leave feeling comfortable and confident.

We are insurance friendly and accept our region’s most popular PPO plans. We also offer payment plans to help fit any budget.

Dr. Chen’s goal with every patient is to create a healthy and functional smile. His approach is to educate his patients about their dental health, and use state-of-the-art technology to provide the best care possible.

The quality of our work can be understood by the transformations we have made with our patient’s lives.
