All-On-4 Permanent Dentures vs Traditional Dentures

When we think about dentures we might think about removable dentures you might see sitting on the nightstand next to your grandparent’s bed when you were a kid. For generations these types of dentures have been a common solution for lost adult teeth. But they come with some serious drawbacks. These implants are removable which means they can slip around and make it difficult to speak or chew on food. Additionally, All-On-4 Dental Implants has some key benefits.

Keep Your Face Structure

The structure and shape of our faces is impacted by the loss of teeth. Dental implants maintain the natural structure of our face by not just replacing the crowns of the teeth we lost but also replacing some of the roots with the implant posts. This helps maintain our natural facial structure.

Form & Function

All-On-4 dental implants are the closet replacement for natural teeth. Because they replace both the crowns and the roots of the teeth they feel and function just like normal teeth. And because they are permanent you do not have to worry about them slipping or falling out during the day.
