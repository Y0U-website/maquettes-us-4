About Whiting Dental Arts

When it comes to dental health, no one will treat you better than the team at Whiting Dental Arts. We’ll get to know you, your health history, and your dental needs to help you achieve the healthy, beautiful smile you deserve.

The Whiting Dental Arts team has cared for families in the greater Boston area for more than 50 years. It’s not unusual that four generations of a family will come see us in a week for an appointment.

We’re grateful to watch our patients and families grow up with healthy, happy smiles. Meet the friendly experts who will care for your family’s teeth.

Meet Our Staff!

Michael Faynzilberg D.M.D

Michael Faynzilberg D.M.D. has owned Whiting Dental Arts since 2017 and his roots in dentistry run deep. Starting at age 11, Dr. Faynzilberg joined two of his uncles (both dentists) in their dental lab where he helped create dental restorations for patients. He worked in the lab throughout high school and developed a passion for dentistry and exemplary pride in his work.

Dr. Faynzilberg graduated from the Boston University Goldman School of Dental Medicine in 2013 and received multiple awards for outstanding patient management and care. He began teaching dental courses prior to graduation and continues to teach as a clinical instructor in the department of general dentistry.

After meeting Dr. Sidney Whiting, the original owner of Whiting Dental Arts, Dr. Faynzilberg fell in love with the practice. He knew right away that he wanted to carry on Dr. Whiting’s legacy of honest, family-centered dental care in the heart of the North Shore. And he’s doing just that, with the latest in dental technology and an expert, friendly staff that truly care about their patients’ well-being and dental health.
