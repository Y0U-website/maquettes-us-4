FAQs

Where is Vachon Dental located?

Vachon Dental is conveniently located in the heart of the Machester community, making it easily accessible for patients seeking quality dental care. Our address is prominently displayed on our website for your reference.

Does Vachon Dental accept dental insurance?

Yes, Vachon Dental accepts most major dental insurance plans. Our team is happy to assist you with insurance queries to ensure you receive the maximum benefits available under your plan.

How can I book an appointment at Vachon Dental?

Booking an appointment is quick and easy. You can call our office directly, use the online booking form on our website, or contact us via email. We strive to provide flexible scheduling to suit your needs.

How can I contact Vachon Dental for more information?

For additional information, you can reach us by phone, email, or through our website’s contact page. Our friendly staff is ready to answer any questions and assist you with your dental care needs.

What should I expect during my first visit to Vachon Dental?

During your first visit, you will receive a comprehensive examination, including a review of your dental history and any necessary X-rays. Our team will discuss treatment options and develop a personalized care plan to keep your smile healthy.

What services does Vachon Dental offer?

We offer a comprehensive range of dental services including preventive care, restorative treatments, cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, and emergency dental care tailored to meet the needs of every patient.

What are the office hours at Vachon Dental?

Our office hours are designed to accommodate busy schedules. We are open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, with extended hours on certain days. Please check our website or contact us for specific times.

What payment options are available at Vachon Dental?

We provide several payment options including cash, major credit cards, and flexible payment plans. Our goal is to make dental care accessible and affordable for all patients.

Are emergency dental services available at Vachon Dental?

Yes, we understand that dental emergencies can happen unexpectedly. Vachon Dental offers prompt emergency services to provide relief and immediate care when you need it most.

What makes Vachon Dental different from other dental clinics?

Vachon Dental stands out through its commitment to patient comfort, cutting-edge technology, and personalized care. Our experienced team focuses on building lasting relationships, ensuring each visit is as pleasant and effective as possible.
