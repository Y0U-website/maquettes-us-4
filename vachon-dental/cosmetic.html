ClearCorrect

If you’ve been searching for clear aligners in Manchester, NH, Vachon Dental offers ClearCorrect — a proven alternative to traditional braces that lets you straighten your teeth discreetly and comfortably. ClearCorrect works similarly to Invisalign: a series of custom, removable clear trays gradually shifts your teeth into alignment, without metal brackets or wires.

These aligners enable you to correct the pattern and orientation of your teeth without the inconvenience of traditional metal braces, making oral hygiene routines much easier to maintain. Your ClearCorrect journey begins with an evaluation by your dentist, who will take X-rays, photos, and impressions of your teeth to create a personalized series of aligners through ClearCorrect’s lab.

Dentures

At Vachon Dental, we provide both conventional and immediate complete dentures to suit your needs and timeline. Immediate dentures are placed right after teeth removal, requiring measurements and jaw models taken beforehand for a seamless fit. Additionally, our practice offers overdentures, which can be placed over natural teeth or implants for enhanced stability and support. Crowns & Bridges

Dental crowns are custom-made caps placed over damaged or decayed teeth to protect them and enhance their appearance. They are ideal for restoring strength to weakened teeth or improving the look of misshapen ones. Dental bridges are fixed appliances used to replace one or more missing teeth, effectively filling gaps and restoring proper bite function. Whether using a traditional or implant-supported bridge, we ensure a natural look and secure fit.

FAQs

Is ClearCorrect Right for Me?

ClearCorrect is ideal for adults and teens looking to correct mild to moderate crowding, spacing, or bite issues. During your consultation at our Manchester, NH office, Dr. Vachon or Dr. Rust will evaluate your teeth and let you know if you’re a candidate.

What s the Difference between ClearCorrect vs. Invisalign?

Both ClearCorrect and Invisalign are FDA-cleared, effective clear aligner systems. ClearCorrect is often more affordable and is provided right here at your Manchester dental office — no referral to a separate orthodontist needed.

How Much Does ClearCorrect Cost in Manchester, NH?

ClearCorrect treatment costs vary depending on case complexity. Many dental insurance plans provide partial coverage for orthodontic treatment. Our team will review your coverage and provide a clear estimate before you begin treatment.
