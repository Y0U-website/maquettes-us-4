Dr. Marye

Robert Marye, D.D.S

“It is the art of dentistry that I love, it is the science of dentistry that I understand and it is the passion for dental education either for myself, a patient or for a student that I most enjoy about my profession.”

“A native Texan, Dr. Marye grew up in Dallas and graduated from Jesuit College Preparatory. After earning his Bachelorâs degree from the University of Texas at Austin, he dedicated his career to healthcare and the practice of dentistry.”

“Dr. Marye has served as the Dallas Mavericks Team Dentist since 2002. He has served as adjunct faculty for Baylor College of Dentistry in the School for Public Health as a Preceptor and for Texas Women’s University in the School of Dental Hygiene where he has taught Embryology, Histology and Head and Neck Anatomy for the past seven years.”

“Dr. Marye volunteers his time to his children’s pursuits at their school, Liberty Christian and to the dental clinic at Christian Community Action. He has also served as treasurer on the board for the North Texas Book Festival, a foundation supporting literacy programs in libraries and schools in North Texas.”

“Dr. Marye is a member of the American Dental Association, the Texas Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.”

“Dr. Marye and his wife, Rachel, have a small farm in Bartonville. Their family includes three children: Gabrielle, George and Gianna, three dogs: Gus, Sugar Bear and Pee Wee, three barn kitties: Roscoe, Maybelle and Elsie, three horses: Bueno, Stormy and Booger, and four mini donkeys: Brushy, Flossy, Coco and Sharann. They also have a few chickens so patients are welcome to farm fresh eggs.”
