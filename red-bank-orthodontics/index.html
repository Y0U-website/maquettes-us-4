Experts recommend children have an initial consultation with an orthodontist by the age of 7 because there already might be a problem occuring or treatment may be needed in the future. Additionally, no patient is "too old" for orthodontic treatment!

Traditional metal braces are the most common type of braces that straighten teeth by using archwires tied in with elastic ties to brackets adhered to your teeth.

With nothing on the front of your teeth, your progress stays discreet. Behind the teeth brackets work 24/7, so you can eat, talk, and smile without changing your routine.
