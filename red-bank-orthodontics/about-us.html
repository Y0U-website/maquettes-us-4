What Sets Us Apart

Digital Impressions & Scanning

On-Site 3D Printing

On-site 3D printing for quick turnaround of retainers and in-house aligners.

Custom Fit Appliances

All expanders and orthodontic appliances have metal 3D-printed bands. This means a custom fit and no uncomfortable separators are needed.

"Training Aligner" Program

Training aligners are available to help all patients determine if aligners are the right choice for them prior to committing to that treatment option.

Patient-Centered Approach

In order to create individualized treatment that best suit each patient&#x27;s needs, Dr. Quigley considers every part of the patient when recommending treatment. She fully believes that working in collaboration with her patients results in better health outcomes that support over all well-being.

Holistic Treatment Philosophy

Dr. Quigley’s holistic approach to treatment considers the whole person, not only their orthodontic needs, when recommending treatment. When underlying causes of orthodontic issues are suspected, Dr. Quigley collaborates with other dental and healthcare providers so that these issues can be identified and treated accordingly.

Neurodivergent-Friendly Care

Dr. Quigley and the Red Bank Orthodontics team have extensive experience treating neurodivergent patients and modifying procedures for an easier sensory experience.

InSmile Provider

Dr. Quigley is the longest active provider of InSmile (behind the teeth braces) in Monmouth County.
