Dr. Suzanne Quigley

Dr. Quigley, a lifelong “local,” was born in Red Bank and raised in Lincroft. She is a graduate of Saint Leo the Great School in Lincroft, Red Bank Catholic High School, and Villanova University. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in 1999 and was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honors Society that same year.
