Why are Only 4 Dental Implants Necessary?

With All-on-4 implants, there’s no need to replace each missing tooth with its own dental implant. Instead, a hybrid appliance is anchored on top of four strategically placed implants in your arch, similar to a dental bridge. Because implants are stronger than natural teeth, they’re able to support the weight of a multi-tooth restoration without the need for additional dental implants. However, in some cases, up to six dental implants for additional support.

Potential Drawbacks

While All-on-4 implants offer many benefits, they may not be suitable for everyone. It’s crucial to consider factors such as your overall health, the condition of your remaining teeth, and the health of your bone support. The all-on-4 procedure has a high success rate, but there is always a minor risk of complications, such as infection or implant failure.

What Does an All-on-4 Appliance Feel and Look Like?

All-on-4 implants are significantly more streamlined, comfortable, and attractive than traditional dentures. Rather than a full “plate” that covers the entire roof of your mouth, All-on-4 implants are a hybrid appliance that’s similar to a dental bridge. The fixture looks and feels more like natural teeth than any other type of tooth replacement.

Speaking and eating with All-on-4 implants is extremely comfortable, as its slim design mirrors the function of anatomical teeth. Plus, each one is handcrafted to your aesthetic preferences, allowing optimal cosmetic results.

Getting All-on-4 Implants at Sila Dental

The process of getting All-on-4 dental implants at Sila Dental begins with scheduling your /dentist-appointment/. After that, the process is fairly straightforward and includes:

At Sila Dental, we make sure you’re comfortable and informed at every stage of the All-on-4 implant process. Our dedicated team is always available to answer any questions and to provide support and care as you journey towards your new smile.

  • Initial consultation: During this meeting, our doctor will thoroughly examine your oral health status and discuss your treatment expectations. This may also include taking X-rays or CT scans to help plan the precise placement of the implants.
  • Implant placement: Our dental surgeon will place the four implants into your jawbone on the day of surgery. The procedure is minimally invasive thanks to advanced technology, and patients usually experience minimal discomfort.
  • Temporary teeth: Once the implants are placed, you will be fitted with a temporary set of teeth that allow you to eat and function normally while your mouth heals and the implants integrate with your jawbone.
  • Final placement: After a healing period of about three to six months, during which your implants will fuse with your jawbone in a process known as osseointegration, the temporary teeth are replaced with your final prosthetic teeth. These teeth are custom-made to match your natural teeth and fit perfectly in your mouth.
