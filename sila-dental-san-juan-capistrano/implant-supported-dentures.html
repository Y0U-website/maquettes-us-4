Implant Supported Dentures vs Traditional Dentures

Implant dentures offer several advantages over traditional dentures. Let’s look at some of the biggest reasons to consider them.

  • Stability and function: Implant dentures offer superior stability and reduce the risk of slippage, allowing you to eat a wider range of foods compared to traditional dentures.
  • Enhanced comfort and looks: Because implant dentures are custom-made to fit your mouth and anchored securely, they are typically more comfortable and look more natural than traditional dentures.
  • Preservation of jawbone structure: Dental implants help preserve the jawbone and help prevent the bone loss that often occurs with tooth loss.

Who Can Get Implant Supported Dentures?

Ideal candidates for implant dentures are typically patients who have lost most or all of their teeth due to decay, gum disease, or accidents. These patients should also have sufficient bone density in their jaw to support the implant placement. Additionally, being in good general health is important to ensure a successful implant procedure.

It is worth noting that certain conditions and habits, such as uncontrolled diabetes or smoking, may impact your eligibility for implant dentures. Our dentist will carefully assess your overall health and discuss any potential concerns before proceeding with the treatment. By considering these factors and working closely with our dentist, you can decide whether implant dentures are the right solution for you.

Risks of Implant Dentures

Like any surgical procedure, there are potential risks associated with implant dentures. However, it is important to understand that these risks are relatively rare and can be minimized with proper care and regular follow-ups. Our periodontist will provide you with detailed instructions on how to care for your implant dentures so that you can reduce the chances of infection or implant failure.

Caring for Implant Dentures

Proper care is imperative for maintaining the longevity and oral health of your implant dentures. Like natural teeth, regular brushing, flossing, and nightly removal for cleaning are essential.

Regular dental visits are also vital for professional cleaning, assessing denture conditions, and addressing concerns. By following these practices, you can extend the lifespan of your dentures and enjoy a reliable, long-term solution for restoring your smile.
