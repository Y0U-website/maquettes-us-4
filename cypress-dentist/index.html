The practice

Finding a dentist you truly trust changes everything. At Cypress Dentist, Dr. James Chan and our dedicated team believe you deserve to feel informed, comfortable, and confident every time you sit in our chair.

For over three decades, this Cypress office has been a cornerstone of the community. Dr. Yung Ming Chiu built a legacy of honest, family-focused dentistry, and when he retired in November 2025, he personally chose Dr. Chan to carry that tradition forward.

Today, Cypress Dentist combines that same patient-first philosophy with modern technology and a fresh, detail-oriented approach. Whether you need a routine cleaning or a complex restoration, we are here to guide you every step of the way.

Everything we do is designed to go beyond what you expect from a dental visit.

State-of-the-art technology Our office is equipped with fully digital X-rays, CBCT imaging, and intra-oral photography to deliver precise diagnostics and treatment planning.

Compassionate Care Dr. Chan uses a gentle, multi-step anesthesia technique and checks in with you at every stage to keep anxiety and discomfort to an absolute minimum.

Transparent Communication Every finding is presented on-screen and explained in plain language so you always understand your diagnosis, your options, and your costs before treatment begins.

Preventive Dentistry Protect your smile before problems start with routine preventative care. We focus on keeping your teeth and gums healthy so you can spend less time in the dental chair.

Services

From preventive care to cosmetic transformations, we’re here for all your dental needs.

Emergency Dentist Act fast when tooth pain or a dental injury catches you off guard. Dr. Chan delivers same-day care to get you out of pain and back to your day.

Cosmetic Dentistry Enhance your smile with personalized cosmetic treatments tailored to your goals. Dr. Chan helps you achieve natural-looking results you’ll love to show off.
