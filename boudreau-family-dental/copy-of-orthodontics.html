Night Guards

Do you wake up with a sore jaw, or notice that your teeth are wearing, and your bite is uneven? Have you cracked molars without realizing it? You are probably clenching or grinding also known as bruxism and could benefit from an occlusal guard. Frequently referred to as a night guard, an occlusal guard is a removable appliance that fits over your upper or lower teeth that is usually worn at night. We offer both soft and hard night guards, and recommend them to all patients who grind, as well as those who have expensive implants and veneers to protect them from failing.
