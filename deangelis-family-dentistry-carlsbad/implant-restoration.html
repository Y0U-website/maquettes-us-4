Frequently Asked Questions

What are dental implants and how do they work?

At DeAngelis Family Dentistry, dental implants are small titanium posts placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. After healing, a custom crown is attached to the implant. The implant fuses with the bone, creating a stable foundation that looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth.

What is the recovery time after dental implant surgery?

Initial healing after dental implant placement typically takes a few days to a week for most patients. Full integration with the jawbone can take several months. We monitor healing closely and guide you through each stage before placing the final restoration.

Does dental insurance cover dental implants?

Some dental insurance plans provide partial coverage for dental implants, while others may cover only certain portions of treatment, such as the crown. Coverage varies by plan. Our team reviews your benefits and explains estimated costs before beginning treatment.

How do I care for my dental implants after placement?

Dental implants should be cared for just like natural teeth. Brush twice daily, floss regularly, and attend routine dental exams and cleanings. Good oral hygiene helps prevent gum inflammation around the implant and supports long-term success.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

The implant procedure is typically comfortable. We numb the area thoroughly before treatment, so you should not feel pain during placement. Mild soreness afterward is normal and usually manageable with recommended medications. Most patients return to normal activities within a day or two.
