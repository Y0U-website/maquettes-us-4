Frequently Asked Questions

What can clear aligners treat, like crowding, gaps, or bite issues?

At DeAngelis Family Dentistry, we use clear aligners to treat mild to moderate crowding, spacing, and certain bite concerns. Aligners can help correct overlapping teeth, small gaps, overbites, under bites, and cross bites. Treatment is customized to gradually shift teeth into healthier, more balanced alignment.

How many hours a day do I need to wear clear aligners for them to work?

For clear aligners to work properly, we recommend wearing them 20 to 22 hours per day. They should only be removed for eating, drinking anything other than water, and brushing or flossing. Consistent wear is essential to keep your treatment on schedule.

How often will I need checkups during clear aligner treatment?

During clear aligner treatment, we typically schedule checkups every six to eight weeks. These visits allow us to monitor your progress, ensure teeth are moving as planned, and provide your next sets of aligners. Appointments are usually brief and focused on tracking results.

Can I eat and drink normally with clear aligners, and when do I take them out?

Yes. You should remove your aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water. This allows you to enjoy your normal diet without restrictions. After meals, brush your teeth before placing the aligners back in to help prevent staining and plaque buildup.

How do I clean my aligners and keep them from staining or smelling?

We recommend rinsing your aligners each time you remove them and gently brushing them with a soft toothbrush and clear, mild soap. Avoid hot water, which can warp the plastic. Keeping your teeth clean and storing aligners properly helps prevent staining and odor.
