Frequently Asked Questions

What Are All-On-4 Dental Implants?

At DeAngelis Family Dentistry, All-On-4 dental implants are a full-arch tooth replacement solution that uses four strategically placed implants to support a fixed set of teeth. This approach restores an entire upper or lower arch with fewer implants, providing stability, function, and a natural-looking smile.

How Long Do All-On-4 Dental Implants Last?

With proper care, the dental implants themselves can last many years, often decades. The attached prosthetic teeth may need maintenance or replacement over time due to normal wear. Regular dental visits and good oral hygiene are essential to protect your long-term results.

What Are the Benefits of All-On-4 Compared to Traditional Implants?

All-On-4 uses fewer implants to support a full arch, which may reduce treatment time and surgical procedures compared to placing individual implants for each missing tooth. This method provides strong support, improved stability over removable dentures, and a fixed solution designed for comfort and function.

Does Dental Insurance Cover All-On-4 Implants?

Insurance coverage for All-On-4 implants varies by plan. Some policies may cover portions of the procedure, such as extractions or the final restoration, while others provide limited implant benefits. Our team reviews your insurance details and discusses financing options before starting treatment.
