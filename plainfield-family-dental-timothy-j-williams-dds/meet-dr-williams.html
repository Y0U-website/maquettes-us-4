Meet the Dentist

Timothy J. Williams, DDS

Dr. Timothy J. Williams

Dr. Williams graduated from Purdue University in 1988 with a Bachelors Degree in Biology. He graduated from Indiana University School of Dentistry in 1992 with a Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree. He is a member of the American Dental Association, Indiana Dental Association, and Indianapolis District Dental Society.

He has stayed in Indiana, practicing dentistry and raising a family. He currently resides in Avon with his wife and family. They have a son, and two daughters.

When not in the office, he enjoys reading, traveling, and various outdoor activities.
