Implant Dentistry Services

Implants are surgically-positioned titanium posts that act as a missing tooth’s root structure. Once properly positioned in the bone beneath the gum line, the post allows for a replacement tooth, bridge or even full denture to be anchored solidly to the jaw.

Dr. Stroud has extensive knowledge of implant dentistry and is one of the co-instructors of an internationally-recognized post-doctoral course in implant dentistry. He would be happy to discuss the benefits of supplementing your existing dentition with implants.

Implants are an alternative to crowns and bridges used to replace teeth that have been lost to fracture, a failed root canal or accident. They can be used for single teeth, 2-4 teeth, or a full arch.

Implant dentistry begins with the end in mind. Planning is key. Based upon your individual needs for function and aesthetics, bone grafting, orthodontics, or occlusal therapy may be needed prior to receiving your implant to ensure that proper placement occurs.

With the right conditions and proper care, an implant can give excellent function and aesthetics to last a lifetime.
