Frank M. Stroud, DDS

Dr. Frank Stroud graduated from Sheldon High School in Eugene, and continued his education at the University of Oregon, graduating with a bachelor of science in biology and double major in chemistry. He furthered his education at the Marquette University School of Dentistry and University of North Carolina Chapel Hill to complete his doctor of dental science degree.

After 15 years of teaching and practicing in Portland, Dr. Stroud returned to his hometown to raise his family and serve the community where his passion for dentistry began. His journey into dentistry was first sparked when, as many of us do, he got braces. In middle school, as his teeth began to shift, he realized what a healthy, functional set of teeth does for one’s well being. From then on, was focused on educating himself to reach the goal of helping people achieve that “Full Mouth Smile”.

When he’s not in his office or continuing to learn new ways to help his patients, you might find Dr. Stroud out and about riding his bike, working with his son’s scout troop, or just enjoying a concert or play.

We Welcome You to Join Us in Our Office

Our entire office is committed to helping patients feel relaxed and comfortable with any procedure that we offer. Patients often comment that they really enjoy FMS Dental because of our warm, friendly staff who genuinely cares about every patient’s health and well being. We aim to give all of our patients a professional and positive experience with plenty of smiles and laughter along the way.

We are in a big and bright office, fully equipped with new technology to better serve our patients. But not all things require new technology. We take time to listen to our patients, give them options for treatment, and talk them through any procedure with as much, or as little, detail as they may like. Dr. Stroud delights in talking with people, hearing their symptoms and resolving issues that they may not even know they have, or ones that others dentists have had a difficult time solving.
