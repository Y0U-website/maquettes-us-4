Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Dental implants look like natural teeth, and quite unnoticeable to the general eye. This makes them perfect for anyone who has lost teeth and needs a replacement.
  • They also prevent bone loss meaning that you get to keep your teeth for longer without having to worry about losing your teeth.
  • Dental implants will support your facial structure. Once you lose your teeth, especially due to old age, your face may have a sunken look which distorts your face. Dental implants by Smiley Dental Boston can fix this.

How long do Dental Implants Last?

Oral Hygiene Routines : Routine oral hygiene is very crucial for the stability of an implant. If you have an implant, you should do regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental check-ups will help prevent gum disease and other oral health issue impacting the integrity of a dental implant. Poor oral hygiene can result in an infection affecting the gums and bone around the implant, potentially leading to implant failure.

Bite Force and Teeth Grinding : Excessive bite force or teeth grinding (bruxism) can put undue stress on dental implants, potentially leading to damage or failure over time. If you grind your teeth, your dentist may recommend wearing a nightguard to protect the implant from excessive force during sleep.

The Underlying Bone Quality : The success of dental implants largely depends on the quality and quantity of the jawbone where the implant is placed. Sufficient bone density is needed to support the implant and ensure its stability. In cases where bone density is low, bone grafting may be required before implant placement. The better the bone health, the longer the implant is likely to last.

Lifestyle : Certain lifestyle habits can affect the lifespan of dental implants. Smoking, for example, can significantly increase the risk of implant failure due to its negative impact on oral health and healing. Similarly, excessive alcohol consumption and poor diet can weaken the immune system, making it harder for the body to maintain the implant site.

Periodic Dental Check-Ups : Regular dental check-ups are essential for monitoring the condition of your dental implants. During these visits, your dentist in Boston Smiley Dental can assess the implant, clean hard-to-reach areas , and address any issues before they become more serious. Proactive maintenance and early intervention can extend the lifespan of your implants.
