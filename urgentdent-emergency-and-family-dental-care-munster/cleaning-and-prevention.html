Why Are Regular Dental Cleanings Important?

Plaque and tartar, also known as dental calculus, are the buildup of bacteria, food particles, and minerals on your teeth. Over time, plaque hardens into tartar, which can only be removed by a professional dental cleaning. If left untreated, plaque and tartar can contribute to various oral health problems, including:

According to the American Dental Association (ADA), regular dental checkups, including professional cleanings, are crucial for preventing these dental issues and maintaining optimal oral health.

  • Gingivitis (inflammation of the gums)
  • Periodontitis (gum disease)
  • Tooth decay
  • Cavities
  • Bad breath (halitosis)

What to Expect During a Dental Cleaning at Urgentdent?

Our dental cleaning appointments are designed to be comfortable and efficient. Here’s a general overview of what to expect:

  • Medical History Review: We will begin by reviewing your medical and dental history to ensure your safety and tailor the cleaning to your specific needs.
  • Oral Examination: The dentist will perform a thorough oral examination to assess your overall oral health, check for any signs of gum disease, and identify potential problems.
  • Professional Cleaning: A dental hygienist will use specialized tools to gently remove plaque and tartar buildup from your teeth, both above and below the gum line.
  • Flossing: The hygienist will floss your teeth to remove any remaining plaque and food particles between your teeth.
  • Teeth Polishing: A polishing paste will be used to remove surface stains and leave your teeth smooth and shiny.
  • Fluoride Treatment (Optional): In some cases, a fluoride treatment may be recommended to strengthen your tooth enamel and prevent cavities. This is especially important for children and those at high risk of tooth decay.
  • Oral Hygiene Education: Our dental hygienist will provide personalized oral hygiene instructions on proper brushing and flossing techniques to maintain good oral health at home.

Maintaining Good Oral Hygiene Between Cleanings

Brushing your teeth twice daily with a fluoride toothpaste and flossing once a day are essential practices for maintaining good oral health between dental cleanings. Here are some additional tips:

  • Use a soft-bried toothbrush: A soft-bristled toothbrush effectively removes plaque and bacteria without damaging your gums.
  • Replace your toothbrush every 3-4 months: Toothbrushes become less effective over time as the bristles wear down.
  • Consider using an electric toothbrush: Electric toothbrushes can be an effective tool for removing plaque and bacteria, especially for those who struggle with manual brushing techniques.
  • Choose sugar-free gum: Chewing sugar-free gum after meals can help stimulate saliva production, which washes away food particles and neutralizes harmful acids in your mouth.
  • Maintain a healthy diet: Limit sugary and acidic foods that contribute to tooth decay.
  • Schedule regular dental checkups: In addition to regular cleanings, schedule dental checkups as recommended by your dentist, typically every six months.
