What is Orthodontics?

Orthodontics is a dental specialty focused on correcting misaligned teeth and jaws. Orthodontic treatment can improve your smile’s aesthetics, but it also offers numerous functional benefits, including:

  • Improved chewing ability
  • Enhanced speech clarity
  • Reduced risk of tooth decay and gum disease
  • Increased self-confidence

Who Can Benefit from Orthodontic Treatment?

Orthodontic treatment can address a variety of orthodontic issues, including:

If you have any concerns about your smile’s alignment or your child’s developing teeth, schedule a consultation with our orthodontists at Urgentdent . Early intervention can often prevent more complex orthodontic problems later in life.

  • Crowding: When teeth are too close together, they can become difficult to clean and increase the risk of cavities and gum disease.
  • Spacing: Gaps between teeth can affect your speech and overall smile aesthetics.
  • Malocclusions (bad bites): Misaligned bites can cause difficulty chewing, jaw pain, and wear and tear on teeth.
  • Open bite: When the upper and lower front teeth don’t touch when you close your mouth.
  • Deep bite: When the upper front teeth significantly overlap the lower front teeth.

The Orthodontic Treatment Process at Urgentdent

Our orthodontic treatment process is personalized and designed to achieve optimal results. Here’s a general overview:

  • Consultation: During your initial consultation , our orthodontist will discuss your goals, examine your mouth and teeth, and take X-rays to assess your jawbone structure. They will then recommend the most suitable treatment option, such as traditional braces, clear aligners (Invisalign), or a combination of approaches.
  • Treatment Plan Development: Our orthodontist will develop a customized treatment plan outlining the treatment duration, type of braces or aligners used, and expected results. We will address any questions you may have and ensure you feel comfortable with the treatment plan .
  • Appliance Placement: Once you’ve chosen your treatment option, our team will carefully place your braces or aligners . We will provide detailed instructions on caring for your braces or aligners and maintaining good oral hygiene during treatment.
  • Regular Adjustments: Regular appointments are necessary to monitor your progress and adjust your braces or aligners as needed. These adjustments ensure your teeth move gradually into the desired positions.
  • Retention: Following treatment, a retainer will be used to maintain your new smile and prevent teeth from shifting back into their original positions.

Key Facts About Orthodontics

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. Early evaluation allows orthodontists to identify potential problems early on and intervene if necessary to prevent more complex issues later.

Types of Orthodontic Treatment at Urgentdent

  • Traditional Braces: These are the most common type of orthodontic treatment and consist of brackets bonded to your teeth and connected by wires that gradually move your teeth into their desired positions. We offer traditional metal braces and clear ceramic braces for a more discreet look.
  • Clear Aligners (Invisalign): Clear aligners are a virtually invisible alternative to traditional braces. They are a series of custom-made, removable trays that gently shift your teeth into place over time. Invisalign may be a suitable option for adults or teens seeking a more discreet treatment approach.
