Why choose orthodontic treatment as an adult? In addition to boosting your confidence, orthodontic treatments can also provide various dental and overall health benefits in the short and long term. See why the advantages of orthodontic treatments go beyond simply enhancing your smile:

Enhanced Aesthetics: A straighter smile can have a profound impact on your overall appearance. It can boost your self-esteem, increase your confidence, and leave a lasting impression in both personal and professional settings.

Improved Oral Health: Correcting misaligned teeth can significantly reduce the risk of various dental issues, including gum disease, cavities, and uneven wear on tooth enamel. A straighter smile is often a healthier smile.

Enhanced Comfort: Addressing bite problems and misalignments can alleviate discomfort, reduce the risk of jaw-related issues, and make everyday activities like eating and speaking more comfortable.

Our adult braces and Invisalign® treatment options

Invisalign Invisalign is a treatment that uses clear, custom-made plastic aligners to discreetly reposition your teeth. These aligners are virtually invisible and can be removed for easy cleaning and maintenance. You can also continue to eat your favorite foods without any restrictions. Each set of aligners is worn for 22 hours a day and replaced every two weeks, gradually moving your teeth into the desired position. Invisalign is ideal for those who want a subtle change in their appearance, especially for misaligned teeth.

Clear Braces Braces, on the other hand, have advanced significantly in technology and are now less noticeable and uncomfortable. Braces can be used to treat spacing, crowding, misalignment, overbite, and underbite, even in complex cases. At docbraces, we offer clear braces, which are made out of a transparent material that does not stain, and friction-free braces, which do not require elastic or metal ties.

Clear braces are a great solution for those who don’t want to draw unwanted attention to their orthodontic treatment. Depending on the amount of treatment that is needed, clear braces may only be used for the upper front teeth, which are the teeth that are displayed most when smiling.

Friction-free braces utilize light, high-tech wires that move teeth faster and more comfortably than conventional materials. Without the need for tightening, fewer adjustments are required during treatment, while the ceramic and stainless steel used also give the brackets a smaller appearance.
