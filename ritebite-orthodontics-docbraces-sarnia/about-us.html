iTero™ scanning system The iTero™ Scanning System is a digital technology that is an alternative to the traditional tray and putty impressions used to create a model of a patient’s teeth. The scanner captures the structure of the teeth using optical technology and a radiation-free high-speed camera that takes 6000 pictures per second. In just a few minutes, the scanner produces a digital 3D impression of your teeth and bite, which can be viewed in real-time on a computer screen. The iTero scanner is suitable for any orthodontic treatment, including Invisalign®.
