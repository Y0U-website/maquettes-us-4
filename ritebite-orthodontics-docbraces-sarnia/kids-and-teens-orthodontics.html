The Canadian Association of Orthodontists recommends that all children have an early orthodontic evaluation by age seven. Having an early consultation with a docbraces orthodontist can help your child avoid more complex and expensive treatments later in life, and in some cases, can eliminate the need for braces altogether. Patients with more complex cases or oral habits such as thumb-sucking can benefit dramatically from an early phase of treatment, preventing the need for more invasive treatment later.

Why choose orthodontics for kids & teens? In addition to better self-confidence due to improved aesthetics, there are many short-term and long-term health benefits from receiving orthodontic treatment early.

By arranging an early consultation with the specialists at docbraces, your child will be able to enjoy:

Improved Confidence: A straighter smile can boost your child s self-esteem, helping them feel more confident in social situations and throughout their daily life.

Better Oral Health: Correcting misaligned teeth early can significantly reduce the risk of dental issues like gum disease, cavities, and uneven tooth wear. A straighter smile is a healthier smile.

Enhanced Comfort: Orthodontic treatment can address bite problems and misalignments, alleviating discomfort and ensuring that everyday activities like eating and speaking are more comfortable.

Functional Benefits: Properly aligned teeth contribute to improved chewing and speech, which positively impacts your child s overall oral function and quality of life.

Straightening options for kids & teens

While Invisalign® treatments may not be suitable for young kids, we offer a range of effective treatment options that are suitable for younger patients:

Traditional Braces Best for fixing gaps in teeth, crowding, misalignment, overbite and underbite, braces are a functional solution for even the most complex cases. At docbraces, we offer friction-free braces, which do not require elastic or metal ties.

Friction-free braces utilize light, high-tech wires that move teeth faster and more comfortably than conventional materials. Without the need for tightening, fewer adjustments are required during treatment, while the ceramic and stainless steel used also give the brackets a smaller appearance. • Learn more about Braces for Kids • Learn more about Braces for Teens

Ceramic Braces Just like traditional braces, ceramic braces are perfect for fixing gaps in teeth, crowding, misalignment, overbites, and underbites. At docbraces, we offer clear braces, which are made out of a translucent material that does not stain, and friction-free braces, which do not require elastic or metal ties.

Ceramic Braces are a great solution for those who don’t want to draw unwanted attention to their orthodontic treatment. Depending on the amount of treatment that is needed, ceramic braces may only be used for the upper front teeth, which are the teeth that are displayed most when smiling.

Invisalign Teen Clear Aligners Invisalign Teen treatment uses a series of clear, plastic aligners that are custom-made to reposition a teenager’s teeth, based on a customized treatment plan created by your orthodontist. The trays are clear and can be removed at any time, which means cleaning and maintenance is simple, and your teenager can continue eating whatever they like.

Each set of Invisalign Teen trays are worn 22 hours a day and replaced every seven days, but varies by patient and doctor. Each new tray has slight tooth movements built into them and, over time, these subtle changes will cause a teenager’s teeth to slowly move into their desired position.

Best for aesthetic purposes, such as misaligned teeth, Invisalign Teen treatment is ideal for high school-age teens.

Kids and teens orthodontics FAQs

The guideline set out by the Canadian Association of Orthodontists states that children should see an Orthodontist for an early evaluation by the time they turn seven. By this point, a child’s jaw and teeth will have formed enough so that any developing issues are obvious, and an Orthodontist will be able to identify what treatment, if any, will be beneficial. For most patients, treatment won’t start until well after age seven. You don’t need a referral to have your child assessed by a docbraces Orthodontist.

The duration of treatment depends on the individual case’s complexity. On average, treatment can range from 12 to 24 months. Your child’s orthodontist will provide a more precise estimate during the consultation.

Braces may require some adjustment, but most kids and teens adapt quickly. Eating with braces may require some modification initially, but they can enjoy most of their favorite foods with a little caution. If you participate in sports or play a musical instrument, docbraces can recommend special mouthguards or provide guidance on how to protect your braces during these activities. It’s important to let your orthodontist know about your specific concerns so they can provide appropriate solutions.

Some tenderness when chewing is typically experienced for a few days following an orthodontic adjustment, or after changing to a new set of Invisalign® clear aligners. Any discomfort is typically mild and can be managed with over-the-counter pain relief, and soft foods.

A member of our docbraces Smile Team will provide specific instructions on how to care for your child’s braces or appliances, including proper cleaning and dietary guidelines. There are handy videos on how to care for your child’s braces on our Quick Tips page .
