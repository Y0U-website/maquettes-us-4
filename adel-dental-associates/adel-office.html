About Adel Dental Associates

Since 1995 we’ve been offering high quality comprehensive dental care for the whole family. We always focus on providing gentle and compassionate care and treat patients with dental anxiety.

Technology

We invest in industry leading technology for better diagnostics and more comfortable visits. Some of our technology includes: CBCT Scans, Waterlase Lasers, 3D Scanners, Digital X-Rays, and more.

Dental Anxiety

It’s completely normal to feel nervous about visiting the dentist—whether you’re 8 or 80—and our Adel dental team understands that. At our practice, we focus on two things: helping you achieve a healthy, happy smile and ensuring you feel completely at ease throughout your visit. That’s why we offer sedation dentistry, including oral conscious sedation and IV sedation, to help you relax deeply and comfortably during treatment. If you think sedation might be right for you, just let us know—we’re here to help.
