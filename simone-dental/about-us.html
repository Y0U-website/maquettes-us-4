Meet Our Doctor

Ryan Simone, DDS

Dr. Simone was born and raised in Tidewater. He attended Norfolk Academy and graduated as a Madison Scholar from Hampden-Sydney College. The next chapter in his education was spent on the West Coast, where he earned his D.D.S. from the University of Southern California.

After practicing in Los Angeles for a few years, he and his wife returned to Norfolk, where Dr. Simone began his practice - one of the best decisions he ever made. Dr. Simone has a deep passion for high-quality dentistry, and his mission is to offer an exceptional patient experience. He and his team regularly attend continuing education courses all over the country to stay at the forefront of clinical care. Dr. Simone has thoroughly enjoyed optimizing his practice by introducing advanced technology to allow for a digital workflow and seamless patient experience.

When he’s not at the office, Dr. Simone loves spending time with his wife and three children, seeing live music, playing, writing, recording his own music, or golfing.
