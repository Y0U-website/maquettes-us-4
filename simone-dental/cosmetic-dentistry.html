Cosmetic Services We Offer

Veneers are a minimally invasive procedure using thin shells of porcelain that mimic your own enamel to address and correct several possible cosmetic concerns, including: Discolored teeth, Chipped, broken, or worn teeth, Crooked, misaligned teeth or teeth with gaps, Worn teeth and Misshapen teeth.

Clear aligner therapy has revolutionized how we approach orthodontic treatment. This treatment consists of a series of clear, thin trays that are worn 22 hours a day over time to slowly move your teeth into the ideal position. The result is a smile in harmony and without all the brackets and wires!

Teeth whitening, or bleaching, is a great way to see a quick and dramatic improvement in your smile. We use top-of-the-line products to get the best results and offer in-office treatment and take-home options, like K R Whitening treatment.

Dental bonding, is often relied upon to treat various conditions in the oral cavity. When used explicitly for cosmetic procedures, we refer to this as bonding. The optical properties of today’s composites allow restorations to blend in seamlessly with the rest of the tooth, fulfilling purposes such as filling gaps between the teeth, addressing discolored patches on tooth enamel, and even hiding misaligned teeth.

Gum contouring is a cosmetic dental procedure that reshapes the gum line to improve the appearance of a "gummy smile" or uneven gums. We precisely remove excess gum tissue, creating a more balanced and aesthetically pleasing smile. This procedure can make teeth appear longer and more proportionate.

When you want to address several areas of your smile, we will likely use a combination of the treatments mentioned above to help you achieve your goals. Smile Makeovers are meticulously designed to address both the aesthetic appeal and the optimal function. Our experience, artistic eye, and passion will help us reach your vision for your perfect smile.

Teeth Whitening

A smile is one of the first things people notice, yet our teeth can unfortunately lose their luster over time, leaving our smile less bright than it once was. To address this, we offer professional whitening solutions, including both powerful, immediate results with our in-office treatments and convenient, customized at-home treatments that allow you to revitalize your smile on your own schedule.

Gum Contouring

Many patients are unaware of the dental procedure known as dental bonding, but it has been around for decades. It is often relied upon to treat various conditions in the oral cavity. Those unaware of bonding are always surprised that it can treat things such as gaps between the teeth, discolored patches on tooth enamel, and even misaligned teeth.

Gum Contouring

Crown Lengthening is a surgical procedure performed to expose more of a tooth’s surface by reshaping the gum tissue and, if necessary, the underlying bone.

Gingival grafting is a surgical procedure performed to treat gum recession by adding new gum tissue to cover and protect exposed tooth roots.

A gingivectomy is a surgical procedure that removes and reshapes excess or diseased gum tissue, often to treat gum disease or done for therapeutic reasons.

Benefits of a Smile Makeover

By enhancing the natural beauty of the teeth, a smile makeover can increase confidence, allowing patients to smile with confidence and enjoy social situations.

A smile makeover can also improve the function of the teeth, allowing for better chewing and speaking ability.

A smile makeover is customized to meet the unique needs and preferences of each patient, providing a personalized solution that is tailored to their specific needs.
