Implant dentistry addresses tooth loss and restores oral function and aesthetics. These services typically involve the surgical placement of dental implants, which are titanium posts that fuse with the jawbone to provide a stable foundation for artificial teeth.

Whether you need a single-tooth replacement or a full-mouth restoration, implant dentistry services offer tailored solutions to meet your unique dental needs.

These services are known for their durability, natural appearance, and ability to enhance both chewing ability and overall confidence in your smile.

Sedation dentistry has witnessed remarkable progress in recent years, thanks to advancements in both pharmaceuticals and dental techniques. Modern sedation methods offer patients a more comfortable and anxiety-free dental experience, making it an ideal solution for those with dental phobias or significant anxiety.

Sedation options range from minimal, such as nitrous oxide or oral sedatives, to deeper levels, like intravenous (IV) sedation. These options enable us to perform various dental procedures while ensuring patient relaxation and reducing discomfort, making dental visits a pleasant and stress-free experience.

Cosmetic dentistry has grown significantly over the past few decades thanks to advancements in technology and technique. Leading-edge equipment allows us to improve the size, shape, color, and overall appearance of an unattractive tooth.

Our cosmetic services include teeth whitening, porcelain crowns, dental sealants, dental veneers, and bonding. Teeth whitening treatment is one of our most common procedures due to its effectiveness.

We may recommend a porcelain crown for both restorative and cosmetic purposes. The same is true for dental veneers. Both restorations are fabricated using porcelain because of its unmatched aesthetic value.

Restorative dentistry is designed to fix any problems you may have with your teeth, gum tissues, jaw, and bone structure. We may recommend a dental filling to correct minor or moderate decay. If the damage is substantial, you may need a dental crown. A dental crown is a tooth-shaped cap that improves the function and aesthetics of the tooth. If the tooth is beyond saving, Dr. Simone will perform a tooth extraction.

We are proud to offer SureSmile Clear Aligners as a modern, effective orthodontic solution right here at our practice. These custom-fit aligners provide a discreet and comfortable way to gradually straighten your teeth, allowing you to achieve a beautiful, aligned smile without the look and feel of traditional metal braces. Whether you have minor crowding or more complex spacing issues, SureSmile offers a clear path to the confident smile you’ve always wanted.

A large part of our practice involves preventive dentistry. We take great pride in treating our patients! A routine appointment typically consists of a professional cleaning and check-up.

These treatments are often straightforward and designed to prevent potential problems.

We offer comprehensive periodontal care aimed at diagnosing and treating gum disease. Our goal is to reverse the disease or minimize its effects and to restore the health of your gums and supporting bone structure.
