What Are the Benefits of All-On-4 Dental Implants?

Compared to other types of implant-supported tooth replacements, All-on-4 restoration offers a range of benefits, such as:

  • Saves money :Fewer implants posts and fewer appointments means more money stays in your wallet.
  • Avoids bone grafting : With All-on-4 restoration, implants are placed in the bulkiest areas of your jaw, which helps avoid the need for a bone graft ahead of time.
  • Shorter recovery time : Fewer surgical placement sites means you will spend less time waiting for your implants to heal in place.
  • Long-lasting results: A lifetime of smiles is possible with durable All-on-4 smile restoration!

All-On-4 Dentures VS Traditional Dental Implants

Traditional dental implants in Grapevine or even traditional implant-supported dentures could require as many as ten implants to secure an upper or lower arch. In addition, a patient missing most or all their teeth for some time has probably lost a significant amount of jawbone density and may need a bone graft before undergoing dental implantation.

With the All-on-4 dental implant system, however, patients can achieve the same outstanding results as traditional implants with less than half the number of implants needed. With just four strategically placed dental implants, patients can achieve a full smile while avoiding lengthy treatment and recovery times. In addition, All-on-4 implants are usually placed at an angle that maximizes bone-to-implant ratio, helping patients avoid the need for bone grafting altogether.

All-on-4 Dental Implants FAQs

Are All-on-4 dental implants covered by insurance?

Although it is unlikely that dental insurance will cover the entire cost of your All-on-4 dental implants in Grapevine, they may cover certain steps of the process after your deductible has been met.

Dental insurance also doesn’t usually cover the dental implants themselves, but it may partially cover a portion of the dentures that will be attached to them.

What foods can you eat after getting All-on-4 dental implants?

After you’ve gotten your dental implants in Grapevine, it’s important to stay hydrated with plenty of water and to eat a soft diet, including foods like yogurt, avocado, bananas, scrambled eggs, fish, and soup.

Although your new dental implants will improve the stability of your dentures and allow you to enjoy a greater variety of foods like proteins, fresh fruits, and vegetables, it’s a good idea to be careful when consuming anything excessively crunchy like nuts and popcorn so you don’t accidentally damage your denture.

Do All-on-4 dental implants take a long time to heal?

The great news about getting All-on-4 dental implants in Grapevine is that you only need four dental implants to be surgically placed for treatment success! This means that this procedure is minimally invasive and can often be performed with just oral conscious or IV sedation in-house.

Typically, it takes around two weeks for the gums to heal around the surgical sites, which is why it’s very important to maintain a soft diet during this period. Once the first two weeks are over, your implants will begin fusing with the jawbone via the process of osseointegration, which takes about three to six months to complete and is crucial to the long-term success of your newly rebuilt smile.

Are All-on-4 dental implants removable?

No, All-on-4 dental implants in Grapevine aren’t removable like snap-on dentures. They are permanently secured in the jawbone via four strategically placed dental implants.

Because they’re not removable, patients can brush them just like they would their natural teeth. They also won’t need to remove them to store them in a glass of water overnight like they would with traditional, removable dentures!
