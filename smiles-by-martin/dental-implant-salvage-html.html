How Dental Implant Salvage Works

If you believe you have a failing implant, the first thing you should do is give Dr. Greg Martin a call, a trusted dental implant dentist in Grapevine. Acting quickly will give him the best chance of saving an implant and make sure you don’t experience more severe problems than you already are.

When you come to see Dr. Martin, the first thing he’ll do is ask you about your symptoms, and then he’ll give you an exam and take an X-ray of your jawbone. If he determines that yes, the dental implant is at risk of failing, he’ll put together a care plan.

This may involve tightening or completely replacing the restoration if it is the source of the problem. He can treat a case of peri-implantitis with a deep cleaning and antibiotics. If the bone is too thin or brittle to safely hold the implant, the post might need to be removed. He’ll then refer a patient to a specialist to have the implant reinserted into the jawbone to ensure better long-term stability, but this may be preceded by a bone graft to build up and strengthen the jaw to guard against future failure.
