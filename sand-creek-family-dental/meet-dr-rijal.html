3 Ways To Transform Your Smile

Dental Implants

We use dental implants to permanently replace missing teeth with strong, natural-looking restorations. Implants fuse with the jawbone to provide a secure foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures. Restore your smile and confidence with long-lasting dental implants!

  • Permanent, stable tooth replacement
  • Prevents bone loss
  • Looks and feels like natural teeth

Clear Aligners

We use clear aligners to gently straighten teeth without the need for metal brackets or wires. These custom-made trays fit comfortably and are virtually invisible when worn. Straighten your smile discreetly and comfortably with clear aligners!

  • Faster than Braces
  • Nearly Invisible
  • Comfortable and Easily Removable
  • Fewer appointments
  • Precise treatment tracking

Preventative Dentistry

We focus on preventative dentistry to stop dental issues before they start. Regular cleanings, exams, and early treatments help maintain a healthy mouth and reduce future dental costs. Keep your smile healthy with proactive dental care!

  • Avoid cavities and gum disease
  • Detect problems early
  • Supports lifelong oral health

DDS

Dr. Sujan Rijal

With over 20 years of dental experience, Dr. Sujan Rijal brings a skilled and compassionate approach to patient care at Sand Creek Family Dental. He is passionate about combining the science and art of dentistry to relieve pain, restore function, and create healthy, confident smiles.

Dr. Rijal earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University of Colorado Anschutz School of Dental Medicine, after completing his initial dental education at B. P. Koirala Institute of Health Sciences in Nepal. Before relocating to the U.S., he practiced dentistry in Nepal for 14 years, building a strong foundation in comprehensive patient care.

At Sand Creek Family Dental, Dr. Rijal provides a full range of general dentistry services, with additional expertise in endodontics, dental implants, and clear aligner therapy. He is a proud member of the American Dental Association and stays current with the latest dental advancements to ensure high-quality care for every patient.

Dr. Rijal grew up in Biratnagar, a small town in eastern Nepal, and now resides in Wichita, KS. He and his wife—his high school sweetheart—have been married for 22 years and are proud parents of twin boys currently attending Wichita State University. Outside of dentistry, Dr. Rijal enjoys watching soccer and spending quality time with his family.

Whether he’s helping patients find relief from dental pain or creating beautiful, confident smiles, Dr. Rijal finds deep satisfaction in the hands-on, life-changing nature of his work.
