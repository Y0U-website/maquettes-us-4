What Are Full Mouth Dental Implants?

Full mouth dental implants, also known by their proprietary names “All-on-4 dental implants” or “Hybridge implants,” are a permanent replacement for missing teeth. With these dental implants, four permanent titanium or zirconia posts are implanted into your jawbone. Through a process called osseointegration, these posts fuse with your jawbone and provide a stable surface to support your new dental implants.

Dental implants have three parts: the post, the abutment and the restoration. These parts combine to restore 99 % of your original biting and chewing power, giving you the ability to eat an unlimited diet. By replacing a full arch of teeth all at once, you can minimize the amount of time you spend in surgery and reduce your recovery time!

Why Choose All-on-4 Dental Implants?

On the day of your surgery, we will provide you with milled polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA) replacement teeth, so you’ll be able to experience an instant improvement in your smile! This immediate load solution allows us to replace all your teeth in a single day.

Unlike dentures, simply by taking care of your dental implants the same way you would your natural teeth, by brushing and flossing them daily, they can last a lifetime!

Because All-on-4 dental implants prevent jawbone recession, you don’t have to worry about your jawbone changing or receding due to missing teeth.

All-on-4 dental implants are individually crafted to match your teeth, your gum line, and even your skin tone, providing a completely natural-looking teeth replacement.
