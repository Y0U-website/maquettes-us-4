Meet Dr. Mary Walker

Experience. Integrity. Excellence.

Dr. Mary Walker is originally from the Midwest . She grew up in Kansas and received both her B.S. in Chemistry and her dental degree from Creighton University in Omaha, Nebraska.

Following dental school, Dr. Walker began practicing in Connecticut. She enjoyed treating the variety of dental needs of her patients and expanding her skills with Invisalign and complex case management , including cosmetic and implant restorations. Her Spanish also improved treating a predominantly Hispanic patient base.

Moving to Northern Virginia in 2016 allowed Dr. Walker to further expand her dental skills and raise a family with 3 young children with her husband .

Getting to know her patients and helping address dental needs and fears is a priority for Dr. Walker .

In her time outside the office, Dr. Walker likes to spend time with her family and volunteer with various school activities. She enjoys hiking, biking, and cooking - especially trying new foods.
