The practice

Nazca Dental Lab is owned and operated by a Certified Dental Technician and we have a genuine care for the health and well-being of your patients, as well as your satisfaction with our work. At NDL we look forward to exceeding your expectations and refining our laboratory doctor relationship.

Our dental technicians and staff are always willing to answer your questions and assist you when needed. We are your partners in excellence! Please take a moment to review the resources before sending in your case. If you have any questions or concerts feel free to contact us directly.

Nazca Dental Lab places specific emphasis on natural apperance and much of our work is hand crafted . We focus on every detail to ensure that our restorations feature proper tooth proportions, contact heights, apperance of the patients natural dentition in function, color and facial contours. Our laboratory pursues the beauty and aesthetic of each case.
