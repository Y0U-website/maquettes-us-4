Meet the Team at Shaw Family Smiles

Carrying on the traditions of providing exceptional dental care to her patients is important to Dr. Christina Shaw! After all, her father started these traditions nearly forty years ago.

Dr. Shaw is honored to be working with many of the same team members who assisted her father in building the excellent reputation of the practice over the years. You can take the time to learn more about our team members here so you ll feel right at home when you visit our office.

Dr. Christina Shaw

Dr. Christina Shaw holds a BA from Georgetown University, a DMD from the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine, and an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) certificate from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. She was thrilled to return to her home state of Massachusetts in 2018 after practicing for several years in northern Virginia.

Dr. Shaw was honored to carry on the tradition her father started over forty years ago in her hometown and close to her parents, siblings, nieces and nephews within the Wachusett Region. Her favorite part of general dentistry is the ability to forge strong connections with patients who become like family as well!

When she s not looking after patients, Dr. Shaw can be found running (she qualified for the Boston Marathon in 2025!), reading, and spending time with her husband and two small children.

Gene

The relationships that she s built over the years with her patients and coworkers are the most important thing to Gene and one of the best aspects of her job.

When she s not at the office, you can find Gene spending time with her family, friends, and pets. She also enjoys cooking.
