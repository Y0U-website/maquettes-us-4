About Schneider Oral Surgery

Dr. Schneider enjoys hiking, playing hockey, scuba diving, and spending time with his wife and five children and two grandchildren.

Meet the Team At Schneider Oral Surgery we are committed to providing the highest level of care for our patients. Our team members work together to create a smooth and pleasant experience for everyone who walks through our door.

We also enjoy being active in our community. Dr. Schneider and his team volunteer at the Arizona Mission of Mercy in Phoenix every year. They have traveled to Mexico with the Flying Samaritans and to Guatemala and Honduras with Smiles for Central America.
