The Implant Surgery Process

Dental implants, individually, are composed of three separate parts: the biocompatible titanium post, a lifelike dental crown, and an abutment that connects the two. The titanium implant is securely placed within your jawbone. Through a natural process called osseointegration, the jawbone will fuse to the implant post as if it were a natural tooth root. The final step of the implant process involves placing a functional and attractive crown to create a natural and seamless smile.
