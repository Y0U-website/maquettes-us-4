Payment Policy

In order to offer the substantial procedure fee courtesies available under the terms of this plan were must adhere to the following payment policies.

  • The one-year membership must be paid in full at the time of enrollment.
  • The membership will be valid for one year from the date the annual fee is remitted.
  • All treatment fees are due and payable at the time service is rendered.
  • All fees for treatment requiring multiple visits or lab fees are due upon the first visit.
  • Tolland Hill Family Dental reserves the right to refuse treatment and/or terminate this membership without notice if the member’s account becomes delinquent at any time. All other Tolland Hill Family Dental terms and conditions of service apply.

Exclusions

Procedure fee courtesies offered under this plan shall not apply to any treatment already in progress prior to enrollment and may not be combined with dental insurance benefits, any other discounts, promotions or outside financing plans (Care Credit, Chase Health Advance, etc.) This is not a dental insurance plan but a discount membership plan. Benefits are limited to treatment provided by Tolland Hill Family Dental and its affiliates.
