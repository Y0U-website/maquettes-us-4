What is Orthodontic Treatment?

Orthodontic treatment involves a variety of appliances, both fixed and removable, designed to move teeth, retrain muscles, and affect jaw growth. These appliances work by applying gentle pressure on your teeth and jaw. The severity of your issue will guide which orthodontic approach is most effective.
