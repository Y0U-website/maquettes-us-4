Tolland CT Dental Implants

Adequate bone structure is crucial for implant success. In cases where bone density is insufficient, bone grafting procedures can be performed to create a solid foundation for the implant.

  • Osseointegration: The implant integrates with the jawbone over time, providing a stable foundation.
  • Abutment: This piece connects the implant post to the restoration and sits just above the gum line.
  • Restoration: Depending on your needs, the restoration could be a dental crown, bridge, or denture.

Advantages of Dental Implants

Choosing dental implants offers numerous benefits:

  • Natural Functionality: Implants allow you to eat, speak, and laugh with confidence, just like with natural teeth.
  • Bone Health: Implants stimulate bone growth, preventing the bone loss that typically occurs with tooth loss.
  • Aesthetic Appeal: Designed to look and feel like natural teeth, implants enhance your smile’s appearance.
  • Low Maintenance: Cared for just like natural teeth, implants require no special maintenance beyond regular oral hygiene practices.
  • Longevity: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.
