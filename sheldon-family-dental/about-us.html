Meet the Team at Sheldon Family Dental

Our Michigan-trained dentists enjoy paying it forward by providing quality dentistry to patients across Canton, Plymouth, Westland, Garden City, Inkster, Livonia, and all points beyond. The combined experience of Drs. Ali Ismail and Carla Ismail represents decades of skilled dentistry, which means superior diagnostics and fantastic outcomes for patients just like you.

Dr. Ali Ismail

He enjoys caring for patients in his community, where he has been practicing his entire career. Dr. Ali enjoys getting to know each patient and developing long-lasting relationships. He prides himself on being especially good with patients who have dental anxiety. Over the years Dr. Ali has obtained advanced training in dental implants, endodontics, and cosmetic dentistry. He enjoys being able to provide patients with comprehensive treatment all under the same roof.

Outside of the office, Dr. Ali enjoys spending time with his wife, Dr. Carla, and three children. He also enjoys fishing, hunting, and all things related to being in the great outdoors as well.

Dr. Carla Ismail

She is a caring dentist who uses only the most advanced materials and procedures available. She practices comfortable, health-centered dentistry with a strong emphasis on getting to know each patient. Dr. Carla is an Invisalign certified provider and looks forward to offering cosmetic and orthodontic services to patients.

At home, she likes to spend time with her husband, Dr. Ali, and three children. She also enjoys reading, cooking, and traveling.
