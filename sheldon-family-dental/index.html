The practice

New patients of all ages are always welcome at Sheldon Family Dental, and we want you to feel at home while you are with us. Here, you will find everything you need to get ready for your first dental visit with us.

Welcome to Sheldon Family Dental, the family dental office of Dr. Ali Ismail and Dr. Carla Ismail, our husband and wife dental team.

If you are searching for a new dental home in the Canton or Plymouth areas, we hope you will book an appointment with us and see the difference a family-owned dental practice can make in how you feel about your dental visits and your oral health.

Our kind and experienced dental team values trusting, long-term relationships with the families in our community. We often see multiple generations in families and celebrate important milestones with you.

Both Dr. Ali and Dr. Carla are perfectionists, which is a great quality to have in a dentist. Their commitment to ongoing education and training, combined with their high standards of care, mean excellent results for you and your smile.

Our dentists use natural-looking and durable tooth-colored materials exclusively because they believe even the most mundane dentistry should enhance, not detract from, your smile. Their attentive care and eye for artistry give our patients outstanding, long-lasting results.

Dr. Ali and Dr. Carla have young children of their own, so they have real, hands-on experience with what it takes to help kids develop good oral hygiene habits that are the foundation of lifelong healthy smiles.

We welcome your entire family to our dental practice, including your littlest members, and pride ourselves on making everyone comfortable and happy from tiny toddlers to their cherished great-grandparents.
