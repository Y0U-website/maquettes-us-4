How is the procedure done?

The dental implant process involves several steps:

The whole process can take several months, but at Smile for Miles Dental Clinic, we make it a smooth and easy experience. You’ll end up with a durable, natural-looking tooth, proving we’re the best place for dental implants.

  • Initial Consultation: Our dentist will assess your dental health, take X-rays, and develop a treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement: During a minor surgical procedure, the implant post is inserted into your jawbone.
  • Healing Period: The jawbone integrates with the implant, a process called osseointegration, which takes a few months.
  • Abutment Placement: Once healed, the abutment is attached to the implant post.
  • Crown Placement: Finally, a custom-made crown is placed on the abutment.

How to take care of dental implants?

Taking care of dental implants is similar to maintaining your natural teeth:

  • Brush twice daily with a soft-bristle toothbrush.
  • Floss daily to remove plaque between teeth and implants.
  • Use an antimicrobial mouthwash.
  • Visit your dentist regularly for checkups and cleanings.

Should I get dental implants?

If you’re missing one or more teeth, dental implants might be the long-lasting solution you’ve been searching for. Whether you need a single tooth replaced or are considering full mouth dental implants, today’s advanced techniques make it easier than ever to restore your smile.

Most healthy adults are eligible. You’re likely a good candidate for dental implants if you have:

Our dental implant doctor will evaluate whether you’re better suited for traditional implants or specialized options like All-on-4 dental implants, which offer full-arch replacement with fewer posts and faster recovery.

  • Healthy gums and a strong jawbone support
  • No chronic conditions that affect healing
  • A commitment to good oral hygiene
  • Missing one tooth, several teeth, or a full arch of teeth

What are the advantages of dental implants?

Dental implants offer numerous benefits:

  • Improved Appearance: They look and feel like your own teeth.
  • Enhanced Oral Function: They function like natural teeth, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile confidently.
  • Durability: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.
  • Bone Preservation: They help prevent bone loss and maintain facial structure.
