Baby’s First Dental Visit

EARLY VISITS TO SUPPORT HEALTHIER SMILES

The first dental visit should ideally occur during infancy, within six months of your child getting their first tooth or by their first birthday. Early visits allow Dr. Sliger to monitor oral development and discuss oral hygiene and growth.

At infant visits, the child sits comfortably on a parent’s lap while the dentist performs a gentle evaluation knee-to-knee with the parent. This early evaluation aims to spot any functional issues, including any problems that could impact feeding, speech, or jaw development.

Visiting the dentist early in childhood helps kids become comfortable and familiar with the dental environment. That way, when they’re older, they already know what to expect, and dental visits seem less of a scary thought. These early visits are also a great chance for parents to ask questions about their child’s oral health.
