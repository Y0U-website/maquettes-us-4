What you can expect from our team

Calm Dental Visits Focused on the Individual

Our entire team is dedicated to making visits calm and relaxed so every patient, no matter their age or ability, can access the help they need.

Conservative Treatment Options Are Prioritized

At our practice, we prioritize minimally invasive treatment options wherever possible, because protecting natural tooth structure is an important part of long-term oral health.

Hospital Dentistry When Needed

Dr. Sliger has practicing privileges at Arkansas Children’s Hospital, allowing treatment to be completed in a hospital setting for patients who require sedation or more complex care.
