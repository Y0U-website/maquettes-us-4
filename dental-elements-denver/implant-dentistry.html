How long does the implant process take?

The process is phased. After surgical placement, the titanium implant requires 3 to 6 months to fuse with your bone (osseointegration) before the final permanent crown can be attached.

Is implant surgery painful?

No. The procedure is performed under profound local anesthesia and sedation, making it highly comfortable. Mild post-operative soreness is common but easily managed with standard pain relievers.

Are dental implants covered by insurance?

Dental insurance coverage varies widely. Our team will verify your specific benefits and explain flexible patient financing options to help you manage the investment.
