Third-Party Applications

Our website may use third-party add-ons or "plugins" for certain functions, such as Google Maps, social media feeds, etc. These may not work or may not work the same for every user and/or every type of disability. We do not have control over the structure of these plugins, and are unable to modify them at all or to the extent that would accommodate every user of our website, and are not responsible for those elements that we do not control.

In-Office Accessibility

We have designed our office to be accessible and comfortable for all patients. Our accessibility features include:

  • Wheelchair-Accessible Entrance & Hallways: No steps at entry and spacious treatment rooms.
  • Accessible Restrooms: ADA-compliant restrooms for patient convenience.
  • Noise-Canceling Headphones: Available upon request for patients with sensory sensitivities or anxiety.
  • Comfortable Waiting Area: Seating and space designed to accommodate mobility devices.
  • Warm Blankets: Available for added comfort during your visit.
  • Assistance Available: Our friendly staff is trained to help patients with mobility, vision, or hearing needs.
