Signs You May Need Dental Implants

Missing Tooth

A gap from a missing tooth can be permanently filled with an implant that looks and works like the real thing.

Failing Tooth

A tooth too damaged to save may be best replaced with a sturdy, long-lasting implant.

Loose Denture

A denture that slips or feels unstable can be secured and supported with implants for lasting comfort.

Jawbone Concerns

Replacing a missing tooth with an implant helps preserve the jawbone that can weaken when a tooth is gone.
