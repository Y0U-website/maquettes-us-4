Dr. Lee and Dr. Young at Radiant Smiles Family Dentistry are excited to welcome you and your family to our office and want you to know that our team will work with you on getting dental care, even if you don’t have insurance.

Questions? We encourage you to contact our dental office in Kirkland today at (425) 828-9271 for more information and to schedule a visit with our dentists. We look forward to speaking with you soon!
