Jot down any questions or concerns you’d like to discuss. Your first visit is the perfect time to ask, and if more come to mind once you’re here, we’re always happy to answer them.

We’ll have questions too, so come ready to share your medical and dental history, it helps us provide the best care possible right from the start.

What to Expect at Your First Visit

Helping kids build a foundation for lifelong oral health is at the heart of what we do. Ruby Dental & Braces is committed to making sure every child has access to quality dental care, with a focus on preventive care that reduces the need for more extensive treatment down the road, backed by community and educational programs that support healthy habits at every age.

With You From the First Visit and Beyond

We work with you to build a checkup schedule that fits, whether that’s routine visits for adults or ongoing pediatric checkups as your kids grow.

A Comprehensive Dental Exam

The dentist performs a comprehensive exam, including a complete evaluation of the teeth, gums, tongue, and oral tissues, along with an oral cancer screening and any necessary digital X-rays to provide a complete picture of your oral health.

A Personalized Treatment Plan

Based on the exam, the dentist builds a treatment plan tailored to the patient, whether that’s a child’s first cleaning or an adult’s ongoing care, and reviews it together with the family.

A Review of Health History

The dentist reviews diet, oral habits, daily activities, and, for children, developmental milestones that shape pediatric dental care.

Guidance for Everyday Habits

Every visit includes practical tips on cavity prevention, nutrition, teething, fluoride use, and good oral hygiene, tailored to the patient’s age and needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Bring a photo ID, your insurance or Medicaid card, and any past medical or dental records you have. New patients can also complete intake forms online ahead of time to save time at the office.

How early should I arrive for my first visit?

Plan to arrive about 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time to complete any remaining paperwork and check in.

What happens during my child’s first dental visit?

Your child’s first visit typically includes a gentle exam, a review of their dental development, and time for our team to answer any questions you have. We recommend scheduling your child’s first visit by age one, or within six months of their first tooth appearing.

Can I stay with my child during their appointment?

Yes. We encourage parents to be involved in their child’s care and welcome you to stay with them during treatment.

How long does a first visit typically take?

First visits usually run a bit longer than routine checkups, since we’re reviewing your history and building a care plan. Most new patient appointments take about 45 minutes to an hour.

Do you accept new patients right now?

Yes. We’re welcoming new patients at all three of our Kentucky locations, with same-week availability for routine exams and cleanings.

Do you accept Medicaid and insurance for new patients?

Yes. We accept Medicaid and CHIP and most major insurance plans , and our team will help verify your coverage before your visit.

What if I have dental anxiety, or my child is nervous about their first pediatric dental visit?

Our teams are experienced with anxious patients of all ages, including kids visiting the dentist for the very first time. We take the time to explain every step, move at your pace, and offer gentle pediatric-friendly anesthesia options when needed, so first-time visits feel calm and comfortable from the start.
