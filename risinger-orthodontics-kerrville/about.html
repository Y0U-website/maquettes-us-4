About

a Family legacy

over 50 years

Risinger Orthodontics was first started by H.L. Risinger in 1966. Dr. H.L. Risinger served families throughout the Hill Country until he retired in 1998 and passed the torch to his son Brad A. Risinger DDS, MSD. Not only does Dr. Brad Risinger carry on his father’s orthodontic practices in Kerrville, Bandera and Uvalde; but he also carries on his father’s heart for the children and the families he serves in our community.
