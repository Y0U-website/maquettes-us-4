Benefits of dental implants

When looking for a tooth replacement system, it’s important to weigh its long-term and quality-of-life benefits. The following are the reasons why dental implants are a gold standard for tooth replacement.

  • Renewed oral functionality: Missing even one tooth can affect how you eat or speak. Tooth loss limits you to easy-to-chew foods, and talking can be accompanied by a lisp or slurred words. Dental implants completely change your life! Eat, laugh, and speak with ease—no slippage or adhesives needed. Dental implants feel like having your natural teeth back.
  • Stable and secure: Conventional dentures stay put in the mouth through suction or adhesives. While effective, traditional dentures can become wobbly over time. Dental implants are secured in the jawbone with titanium posts. This unique design helps you enjoy new, stable teeth that let you talk or eat whatever you want without worrying about slippage.
  • Natural appearance: Another benefit you’ll love about dental implants is their lifelike natural appearance. We restore your implants with restorations that closely mimic the appearance of your natural teeth. Once placed, most patients (and onlookers) can’t tell the difference between their natural teeth and the implant tooth.
  • Stabilize adjacent teeth: If the toothless space is left as is, the neighboring teeth often migrate toward the gap. Thiseventually affects your bite stability and alignment. However, dental implants can fill the gap and keep everything well-aligned.
  • Durable restorations: Dental implants are created from medical-grade titanium. This biocompatible material integrates with the jawbone to provide a solid, durable foundation for your prosthetic teeth. Dental implants can last several years, or even a lifetime. Their longevity makes them a fantastic investment despite their high upfront costs.
  • Jawbone preservation: After you lose your tooth, bone loss follows if the tooth isn’t addressed in time. However, when you replace your tooth with an implant, you can prevent jawbone loss. Dental implants stimulate your jawbone just like your natural teeth, helping maintain its health and stability. Dental implants not only preserve your jawbone health but also prevent the sunken-in, aged appearance that can occur with missing teeth.
  • Versatility: Tooth loss rarely happens predictably. You can lose one tooth, several, or all teeth in your mouth because of trauma, accident, genetics, or cavities. The good news? Dental implants are equal to the task. We offer single, multiple, and full-mouth dental implants to replace any number of missing teeth.
