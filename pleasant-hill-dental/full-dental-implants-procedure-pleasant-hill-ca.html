The benefits of full mouth dental implants

All-on-4 or all-on-X dental implants are an innovative tooth-replacement option for individuals who have lost most or all of their teeth. They are a life-changing solution for restoring aesthetics and function without requiring an implant for every lost tooth.

Here are the benefits of all-on-X dental implants in Pleasant Hill, CA:

Full-mouth dental implants transform your confidence and your everyday life. Whether taking photos, enjoying dinner, or meeting a new friend, the confidence of a complete, natural-looking smile is priceless.

  • Immediate function: One of the compelling benefits of the all-on-X system is how quickly you see the results. Conventional implants often require several appointments and a lengthy healing period before the implant can be restored. In contrast, we can restore most of the all-on-X implants on the same day as the surgery. That means you leave our office with a functional smile rather than waiting months for a restoration.
  • Enjoy aesthetically appealing results: Dr. Zhu combines artistry and precision to deliver beautiful outcomes with full-mouth dental implants. Once set, no one will realize you have replacement teeth. That means you can speak, smile, and interact without feeling self-conscious about your teeth.
  • Enhanced comfort and stability: Conventional dentures are effective, but they can slip and cause irritation. The all-on-X system is secured in the jawbone, which provides better stability and comfort. This ensures you enjoy your favorite meals and speak clearly without worry or discomfort. Patients tell us that their new teeth feel and function like natural ones.
  • Bone preservation: When your jawbone lacks stimulation from your teeth, it shrinks and degrades over time. Dental implants restore that stimulation by mimicking natural teeth during chewing, helping maintain a stronger, healthier jawbone.
  • Lower costs than traditional implants: Replacing every lost tooth with an implant can be costly and time-consuming. However, the all-on-X system can replace all or most of your missing teeth with just 4-6 implants per arch. Using the fewest number of implants reduces overall treatment cost, surgery time, and recovery period.
  • Long-term value: All-on-X dental implants are created from high-strength, durable materials. Unlike dentures, your restoration will last several years or even decades with good maintenance. While dental implants have a higher upfront cost, they are cost-effective in the long run. They last for many years, and you get a set of new teeth that look and function like the real thing.

Ready for full mouth restoration with dental implants?

The all-on-X implants are an exciting advancement in restorative dentistry. They allow dentists to restore an entire smile with functional, natural-looking teeth.
