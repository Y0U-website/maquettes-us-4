DentFirst Buford Mall of Georgia

At DentFirst of Buford / Mall of Georgia, we have a team of family dentists and board-certified specialists ready to meet your dental needs! We have served patients in the Buford, Lawrenceville, Suwanee, and Flowery Branch areas for over 40 years! See us on GA 20 right across the street from the Mall of Georgia, between the FedEx Office and Arby’s.

Our state-of-the-art office offers family dental care as well as Orthodontics, Periodontics, Prosthodontics, and Oral Surgery. Avoid the hassle of going around town and joining the DentFirst family to meet your dental care needs in one convenient location!

We will be here when you need us most. Our office is open Monday through Saturday! DentFirst offers early morning and evening appointments throughout the week to accommodate your busy schedule. We encourage you to reserve your appointment with one of our friendly patient services team members. Still, walk-ins are always welcome if you are having an emergency! We also offer immediate care after hours for dental emergencies.
