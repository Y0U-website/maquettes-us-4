Dental implants are artificial roots that act as a permanent base for replacement teeth. They are a safe, standard, and highly effective option for those who have lost one or more teeth, whether it be from injury, disease, or decay. The implants themselves are titanium root replacements , which provide a strong, new foundation for crowns ( see same day crowns ) or “caps” in place of missing teeth.

Though the idea of any invasive dental procedure may seem intimidating, our team at Full Heart Dentistry works to provide the most comfortable experience for dental implants in Rochester, NY. You can trust that your smile is in good hands.

Dental implants offer a range of benefits over other traditional tooth replacement options. Not only are they strong and durable, but they’re also more hygienic than most alternatives when properly cared for. All of this makes dental implants one of the closest approximations to real teeth available today. Our titanium root replacements are strong, light, and stable, providing a permanent base for your replacement teeth.

These implants can also help to preserve your jawbone, preventing any bone damage that may pose a threat to your facial structure. And, unlike dentures, implants won’t slip or shift, giving you the confidence to smile, speak, and eat with ease.
