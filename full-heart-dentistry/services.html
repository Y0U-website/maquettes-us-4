Advanced 3D-Imaging Technology

Our doctors make use of the most advanced technology available in the field to ensure your visit with us is as comfortable and convenient as possible. We use Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) imaging technology to give us a comprehensive, three-dimensional map of your mouth. Not only does this technique give us more vital information for treatment, but it also requires significantly less time and less radiation than traditional X-ray technology.

CBCT

A CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography) machine is a specialized medical imaging device that uses X-rays to create a 3D image of a patient’s dental or facial structure. The benefits of a CBCT machine for patients include a faster and more accurate diagnosis of dental and facial conditions, improved treatment planning, and a reduced need for invasive procedures.

CEREC Prime Scan Milling Unit

A CEREC Prime Scan Milling Unit is a specialized dental technology that uses computer-aided design and manufacturing to create high-quality dental restorations such as crowns, bridges, and veneers. The benefits of a CEREC Prime Scan Milling Unit for patients include a faster and more accurate restoration process, reduced treatment time, and fewer appointments required.
