FAQs for Preventative Dental Service

A dental exam is a crucial part of preventative care. Your dentist will examine your mouth, teeth, gums, and jaws to check for any problems or signs of damage or unhealthy conditions. Your dentist may also take CBCT 3D digital imaging , digital x-rays, or digital impressions to better understand your mouth’s structure.

From there, your dentist will clean your teeth, discuss your dental health history, and offer some advice for your dental hygiene. Regular dental exams are essential for detecting and treating problems early, so they don’t become more serious.

We also perform oral cancer screening every 6 months for our patients. We also check for gum disease because at least 80 % of the population has gum disease.

A dental cleaning is a routine procedure that helps remove plaque, tartar, and stains from your teeth. During the cleaning, the dentist or a dental hygienist will use special instruments to remove any plaque, tartar, and debris from the surfaces of your teeth. They may also polish your teeth to remove stains to leave them feeling smooth and clean. This procedure will keep your teeth and gums healthy.

Not only will a professional cleaning put a new shine on your teeth, but it can help to foster improved overall oral health as well. Regular cleanings are essential for preventing cavities, gum disease, and other complications.

Fluoride treatment is a simple, professional dental procedure that helps strengthen your teeth and protect them from decay. During the treatment, a highly concentrated fluoride solution is applied directly to the teeth using a brush, tray, or rinse. Fluoride is a mineral that can help rebuild the enamel on your teeth, making them more resistant to cavities and decay.
