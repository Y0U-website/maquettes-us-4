Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 17, 2026

Catawba Creek Dental respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when using our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to website visitors.

By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact our office, request an appointment, or submit information through a website form.

Our website may also automatically collect certain technical information, including your browser type, device type, IP address, referring website, pages visited, and general website activity.

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Appointment request information
  • Questions or comments
  • Preferred method of contact
  • Other information you choose to provide

How We Use Your Information

Information collected through our website may be used to:

Catawba Creek Dental does not sell personal information submitted through our website.

  • Respond to questions and inquiries
  • Process appointment requests
  • Contact you regarding scheduling
  • Provide information about our dental services
  • Improve our website and patient experience
  • Analyze website traffic and performance
  • Maintain website security
  • Comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements

Appointment Requests and Contact Forms

Information submitted through our website forms is used to respond to your request.

Submitting an appointment request through the website does not confirm an appointment. A member of the Catawba Creek Dental team may contact you to confirm availability and scheduling details.

For urgent dental concerns, please call our office directly at 704-991-7227 .

Protected Health Information

Please avoid submitting detailed medical histories, Social Security numbers, insurance identification numbers, or other highly sensitive health information through general website contact forms unless specifically requested through an appropriate secure system.

Information considered Protected Health Information may be subject to additional protections under applicable federal and state healthcare privacy laws.

Catawba Creek Dental maintains separate privacy practices concerning protected health information. Patients may contact our office to request information about our Notice of Privacy Practices.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

Most web browsers allow you to control or disable cookies through browser settings. Certain website features may not function properly when cookies are disabled.

  • Support website functionality
  • Remember website preferences
  • Understand how visitors use the website
  • Measure website performance
  • Support analytics and marketing activities

Website Analytics and Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to support website functionality, including services for:

These third-party providers may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

  • Website hosting
  • Analytics
  • Online forms
  • Appointment requests
  • Maps
  • Advertising
  • Social media
  • Website security

Embedded Content and External Links

Our website may contain embedded content or links to other websites, such as maps, videos, social-media pages, insurance resources, or other third-party services.

Visiting or interacting with third-party content may allow those providers to collect information about your interaction according to their own privacy practices.

Catawba Creek Dental is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

How We Protect Information

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to help protect information submitted through our website from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

How Long We Keep Information

Information submitted through our website may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Respond to your request
  • Maintain appropriate business records
  • Provide requested services
  • Meet legal or regulatory obligations
  • Protect the security and operation of our website

Children’s Privacy

Our website is intended primarily for adults seeking information about dental care for themselves or their families.

We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children through general website forms without appropriate involvement from a parent or legal guardian.

Your Privacy Choices

You may contact Catawba Creek Dental if you would like to:

Certain records may need to be retained when required by law or for legitimate healthcare or business purposes.

  • Ask questions about information you submitted through the website
  • Correct or update your contact information
  • Request removal of information when legally and operationally appropriate
  • Ask questions about our privacy practices

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Catawba Creek Dental may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our website, services, technology, or legal requirements.

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
