Dr. Suruchi Warwatkar

Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Suruchi has been a dentist for 25 years and is passionate about creating positive dental experiences for children. With her warm approach and expertise in modern, minimally invasive techniques, Dr. Sue provides each child with personalized, compassionate care. Her greatest fulfillment comes from turning traditionally fearsome experiences into ones of ease and happiness.

Originally from India, Dr. Sue completed her GPR and pediatric dental certificate program from Case Western and is board-certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. She is an expert in minimally invasive dentistry, as well as in caring for medically compromised and special needs patients.
