Orthodontics for Kids in Milpitas

Orthodontics for kids focuses on aligning teeth and jaws to create healthy, confident smiles. At TeethBuddies Pediatric Dentistry, we believe early orthodontic treatment and evaluation can make a big difference in your child’s dental development. Our pediatric orthodontics care helps guide proper tooth and jaw alignment, improving both function and appearance while making it easier to maintain good oral hygiene. With gentle and personalized care, we help children achieve smiles that are both healthy and beautiful.

Why Orthodontics Matters Orthodontic treatment improves more than just the look of a smile. It also supports long-term oral health and proper bite function.

Improves Alignment: Corrects crowded, crooked, or spaced teeth for a balanced smile.

Supports Jaw Growth: Encourages proper jaw development during childhood.

Prevents Future Issues: Helps avoid uneven wear, bite problems, and dental strain.

Boosts Confidence: Gives children a straight, healthy smile they can feel proud of.

Types of Orthodontic Treatments We Provide

Metal Braces

Braces for children are the most common and reliable orthodontic treatment for correcting misaligned teeth.

Ceramic Braces

Ceramic braces offer a more discreet option while still effectively guiding tooth movement.

Lingual Braces

Lingual braces are placed behind the teeth to help improve alignment while remaining less visible.

Palatal Expanders

A palatal expander for kids helps widen the upper jaw to create more space for growing teeth and improve bite alignment.

Space Maintainers

Space maintainers are small, custom devices used to hold space for permanent teeth after a baby tooth is lost too early.

What to Expect During Orthodontic Treatment

Our orthodontic process is designed to be comfortable, effective, and encouraging for every child.

Initial Evaluation: We perform an orthodontic evaluation for kids by using advanced digital imaging.

Customized Plan: A treatment plan is created based on your child’s unique needs and growth stage.

Appliance Placement: Treatment may include kids braces, clear aligners, or space maintainers to guide proper alignment.

Ongoing Care: Regular checkups help monitor progress, comfort, and treatment success.
