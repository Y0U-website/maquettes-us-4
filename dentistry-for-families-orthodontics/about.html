Our Mission

We re a small practice trying to do right by the families of North Las Vegas. That means listening before we recommend anything, going easy when you re nervous, and keeping good dental care within reach for the neighbors who walk in. We feel fortunate to do this work, and we hope to keep helping families like yours smile for years to come.

Meet Our Doctors

Experienced, caring, and deeply committed to your family s smile.

Dr. Zachary Soard

Dr. Zac is married to the love of his life, Kelli. They have been married for 20 years. She supports him in all that he does and makes everything possible. They have five kids who are the light of their lives. The Soard family are true sword fishes and love the water. Between the pool, the beach, and the lake they seem to always be floating around. When Dr. Zac is not spending time with his family he loves working with his hands whether on his old pickup or his saltwater aquarium.

Dr. Soard is a proud member of the American Dental Association, Nevada Dental Association, and the Southern Nevada Dental Association.

Dr. Brian C.

Dr. C grew up in Berkeley, CA, the youngest of five kids — one brother and three sisters. He has a long history with the outdoors and loves to travel; he has visited five of seven continents and both sides of the equator.

He earned his undergraduate degree at Brigham Young University, where he also served a mission in France for the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. He received his DDS from UCSF in 1994 and his MDS with an orthodontics specialty certificate from the University of Connecticut in 1997.

Dr. C has been practicing in Las Vegas for over 26 years. He and his wife have five children, ages 14 to 24. Outside of work he enjoys building and fixing things, staying active in his faith community, and cars.

Dr. Thomas Hutchings

Dr. Hutchings was born and raised in Las Vegas. He grew up loving extreme sports like dirt biking, snowboarding, skateboarding, and off-roading. He comes from a family of four boys who all grew up playing sports together.

He attended Brigham Young University where he got his degree in Public Health. He then attended UNLV for dental school.

He has three children and loves spending time with them, and wrestling with his two boys. He loves the artistic nature of dentistry and helping others smile.
