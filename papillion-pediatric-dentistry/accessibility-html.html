Accessibility Assistance

Papillion Pediatric Dentistry, PC is committed to making our website accessible and usable for all patients and visitors. We want everyone to be able to obtain information about our office, our services, and how to contact us.

If you have trouble using this website, viewing any page, accessing a form, or using any website feature, please contact our office. Our staff will be happy to help you by phone or in person.

Need Help Accessing Information?

Please call our office at 402.502.1256 .

You may also visit our office and our staff will assist you with forms, appointment information, and other patient information.

Website Assistance

Different web browsers, devices, screen readers, and keyboard settings may handle websites differently. If you are having difficulty using this website, you may want to try another current browser, such as Safari, Chrome, Firefox, or Edge. You may also contact our office directly and we will assist you.

Online Forms and Website Features

If you are unable to access, read, complete, or submit an online form, please call our office at 402.502.1256. Our staff can help you obtain the information, complete forms, or make an appointment.

Ongoing Efforts

We review our website periodically and make reasonable efforts to improve accessibility, usability, keyboard navigation, link visibility, text contrast, and compatibility with assistive technologies.

Contact Us About an Accessibility Problem

If you experience an accessibility problem on this website, please contact us and describe the issue, the page where it occurred, and the browser or device you were using. This information helps us review and address the problem.
