What about bone loss?

A common reason patients are turned away for implants is jawbone loss. All-on-4 is designed to work with the bone you have — the rear implants are placed at an angle to anchor into denser bone, often avoiding the need for grafting. When grafting or added support is needed, Dr. Shepard’s surgical training lets us take on complex cases that other offices refer out. A 3D CBCT scan at your consultation shows exactly what’s possible in your case.

Who may not be a candidate?

Uncontrolled diabetes, active gum infection, heavy smoking, or certain medical conditions can affect healing and implant success. These aren’t automatic disqualifiers — they’re things we evaluate and, where possible, help you address before surgery. Age itself is rarely a barrier; many of our All-on-4 patients are in their 60s, 70s, and beyond.

Not sure if you qualify? Get an evaluation. Dr. Bublik will review your 3D scans and give you a straight answer — no pressure, no obligation.

How Long Does All-on-4 Last?

The titanium implants that anchor your arch are designed to last a lifetime — they fuse permanently with your jawbone. The visible prosthesis (the teeth themselves) typically lasts 15–20+ years and can be refreshed or replaced without redoing the implants.

Caring for All-on-4 is straightforward and a lot like caring for natural teeth: brush twice a day, clean underneath the arch with a water flosser or the tools we recommend, and keep your regular checkups. At those visits we examine the implants, professionally clean the prosthesis, and confirm everything is healthy — the single biggest factor in implants lasting decades.
