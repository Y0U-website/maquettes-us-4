Implant Solutions for Every Situation

Single Tooth Implant

Replace one missing tooth with a titanium implant post, abutment, and custom crown. No damage to adjacent teeth.

Ideal for: Single tooth loss from trauma, decay, or extraction

Implant Bridge

Replace 2-3 consecutive missing teeth using just two implants as anchors — no grinding down healthy teeth.

Ideal for: Multiple adjacent missing teeth

Implant-Supported Dentures

Secure your denture to 2-4 implants so it never slips, shifts, or embarrasses you again.

Ideal for: Current denture wearers wanting stability

All-on-4 / All-on-X

A full arch of fixed, beautiful teeth permanently anchored to 4 or more implants. Same-day results possible in selected cases.

Ideal for: Full arch replacement, failing teeth

Computer-Guided Implants

Dr. Bublik’s proprietary guided surgery technique uses CBCT scans and digital planning for sub-millimeter precision.

Ideal for: Complex cases, maximum predictability

Bone Grafting + Implants

Even if you’ve been told you have insufficient bone, Dr. Bublik can prepare your jaw with grafting before implant placement.

Ideal for: Bone loss, previously denied candidates

Your Implant Journey — Step by Step

Book Consultation

Dr. Bublik evaluates your teeth, gums, and bone density using CBCT 3D imaging. You’ll receive a complete treatment plan with all costs upfront — no surprises.

Preparation (if needed)

If extractions, bone grafting, or sinus lifts are needed to prepare your jaw, these are completed first. Dr. Bublik handles all surgical procedures in-house.

Implant Placement

Using computer-guided surgery and German Argon implants, Dr. Bublik places the titanium posts with precision. Sedation options ensure your complete comfort.

Healing Osseointegration

Over 3-6 months, your implants fuse with the jawbone. A temporary restoration keeps you looking and functioning great throughout healing.

Final Restoration — Your New Smile

Your custom-crafted final crown, bridge, or full-arch restoration is placed. Made by the world-class dental lab right here in Wausau, your new teeth are designed to blend perfectly and last for life.
