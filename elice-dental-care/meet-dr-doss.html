Anthony Doss, DMD

Born and raised in New Jersey, Dr. Anthony Doss is proud to serve his community with compassionate, high-quality dental care. He completed his undergraduate studies at Rutgers University and earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine. Following dental school, Dr. Doss completed a one-year General Practice Residency at Morristown Medical Center, where he received advanced training in comprehensive patient care and complex dental procedures.

Dr. Doss is passionate about helping patients feel comfortable and confident in their smiles. He is committed to delivering gentle, personalized care and staying up to date with the latest techniques and technologies in dentistry. Whether you’re in for a routine checkup or a more involved treatment, his goal is to make every visit a positive experience. Dr. Doss looks forward to getting to know you and supporting your oral health journey!

In his free time, he enjoys working out, traveling, trying new restaurants, and reading books.
