Frequently Asked Questions

Most dental insurance plans offer limited or no coverage for implants, since they’re often classified as cosmetic. Our Dublin, Gahanna, and Grove City offices accept many insurance plans and offer financing options — we’ll help you understand exactly what your plan covers before treatment begins.

The full process typically takes several months from implant placement to final crown, because the titanium post needs time to fuse with your jawbone before a custom crown can be placed on top. The surgical appointment itself is relatively brief — most of the timeline is healing.

Good candidates have healthy gums and enough jawbone to support the titanium post. Patients who’ve had long-term tooth loss may have some bone loss, which can sometimes be addressed before implant placement. Schedule a consultation at any daily DENTAL & bracesbar location to find out if implants are right for you.

Unlike a bridge, implants don’t require grinding down adjacent healthy teeth and carry no risk of decay underneath a crown. They also last a lifetime with proper care — bridges typically need replacement every 10–15 years and can slip or loosen over time.

Leaving a gap causes a chain reaction: adjacent teeth shift, bite alignment changes, and the jawbone beneath the gap begins to deteriorate over time. That bone loss can lead to facial sagging and make future implant placement more complex, so earlier treatment is better.
