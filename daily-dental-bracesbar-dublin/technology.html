Dental Technology

Electronic Dental Records

Our practice uses paperless documentation, allowing dentists to access patient information quickly for shorter treatment times and improved collaboration between providers.

Digital X-Rays

Advanced digital radiography enables early detection of cavities, gum disease, and other dental problems, allowing for timely intervention with significantly less radiation than traditional X-rays.

Intraoral Camera

Our high-quality imaging devices show patients their oral cavity on computer monitors in real-time, improving understanding of treatment needs and helping you make informed decisions.

VELscope

Non-invasive oral cancer screening using natural tissue fluorescence to identify abnormalities that may be invisible to the naked eye.

CEREC CAD/CAM

Same-day dental crown technology that completes restorations within two hours using computer-aided design and manufacturing no temporary crowns or second visits needed.

Microlux Transilluminator

Advanced caries detection device that identifies cavities in their earliest stages, allowing for less invasive treatment before problems grow.

Perio Protect

Custom tray-based treatment system for gum disease, delivering pathogen-eliminating medications directly to periodontal pockets for effective at-home treatment.

BioHorizons Implants

Premium dental implant system engineered with advanced surface technology for strong integration and reliable, long-lasting tooth replacement.

Wave One Endodontic Unit

Advanced technology for more comfortable, efficient root canal treatments with better outcomes.

Nitrous Oxide Sedation

Safe, effective sedation combining nitrogen and oxygen for pain and anxiety management during procedures. Effects wear off quickly after treatment.

Dental 3D Imaging (CBCT)

Cone beam computed tomography captures detailed 3D images of your teeth, bone, and nerves, enabling precise diagnosis and treatment planning for implants and more.

iTero Scanners

Digital intraoral scanning replaces messy impressions with fast, accurate 3D models for Invisalign, crowns, and other restorations.

KOR Whitening

Advanced whitening system that restores teeth to their natural brightness, delivering results even on stubborn, long-standing stains.

Curodont

Minimally invasive treatment that regenerates early enamel lesions and reverses the earliest stages of tooth decay without drilling.

Picasso Laser System

Soft-tissue dental laser for precise, comfortable gum treatments with less bleeding and faster healing.
