Our Dental Implant Services

Dental implants are a revolutionary solution for replacing missing teeth, offering both functional and aesthetic benefits. At Webster Dental Group, we specialize in various implant options tailored to your unique needs.

Single Tooth Implants

If you’re missing a single tooth, a single tooth implant can seamlessly fill the gap. This involves surgically placing a titanium post into the jawbone, acting as a sturdy foundation for a custom-designed crown. With proper care, single tooth implants can last a lifetime, providing stability and restoring your smile’s natural appearance.

Implant Bridge

For those missing multiple adjacent teeth, implant bridges offer a reliable and durable solution. This involves placing implants at either end of the gap and attaching a bridge of artificial teeth in between. Implant bridges not only restore function for chewing and speaking but also prevent bone loss and maintain facial structure.

FAQ’s About Dental Implants

What are dental implants, and how do they work?

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically inserted into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. They provide a sturdy foundation for artificial teeth such as crowns, bridges, or dentures. Over time, the implant fuses with the bone through a process called osseointegration, ensuring stability and durability.

Are dental implants suitable for everyone?

While most individuals with good oral health and sufficient bone density are candidates for dental implants, certain factors such as uncontrolled diabetes, smoking, or gum disease may affect eligibility. A comprehensive evaluation by a qualified dentist or oral surgeon is necessary to determine suitability for the procedure.

What is the process of getting dental implants like?

The dental implant process typically involves several stages. After an initial consultation and treatment plan, the implant is surgically placed into the jawbone. Following a healing period of a few months, during which osseointegration occurs, an abutment is attached to the implant. Finally, a custom-made artificial tooth or restoration is securely affixed to the abutment.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. Regular oral hygiene practices, including brushing, flossing, and routine dental check-ups, are essential to ensure the longevity of implants. Additionally, avoiding habits such as smoking and maintaining overall good health can contribute to the success and durability of dental implants.

What are the benefits of choosing dental implants over other tooth replacement options?

Dental implants offer numerous advantages, including stability, functionality, and aesthetics. Unlike traditional dentures or bridges, implants mimic the natural structure of teeth, providing a secure and comfortable fit. They also prevent bone loss in the jaw, preserve facial contours, and allow for normal chewing and speaking functions, enhancing overall quality of life.
