Meet dr smirnova

Education

Dr. Lana Smirnova grew up in Kiev, Ukraine. She graduated Kiev Medical School majoring in Dentistry with Honors and worked as a Dentist before moving to the United States. Here, Dr. Smirnova obtained her degree at New York University, College of Dentistry . She made the Dean’s List and graduated in the top ten percent of her class.

About the Practice

Her practice philosophy is to create beautiful healthy smiles that one can be proud of and to build lasting relationships with her patients by treating them with respect, kindness, and a gentle touch.

Thanks to Dr. Smirnova’s interest in the latest dental technology and her commitment to continuing dental education, she is able to offer you the highest level of dental care available. She believes that being a perpetual student helps to grow and to master her professional skills as well as to continue her love of dentistry.

The approach to your treatment starts by carefully listening to you. Then, after a complete and thorough examination, Dr. Smirnova reviews and discusses the diagnosis and treatment options with you. Each patient s treatment is customized according to the individual s wants and needs.

I am proud to provide my patients with the best in dental technology, treatment options, and patient comfort. It is my pleasure to address all your questions and concerns.

Beyond the Office

When she’s not doing dentist ry, Dr. Smirnova loves spending time with her husband and two children. Together they enjoy skiing, hiking, reading books, and traveling.
