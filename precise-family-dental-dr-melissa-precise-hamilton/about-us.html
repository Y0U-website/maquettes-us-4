Meet the Team at Precise Family Dental

At Precise Family Dental, we welcome patients of all ages and strive to make your dental appointments as comfortable as possible. Dr. Melissa Hamilton and our team know that it takes a lot of great dental visits to get rid of the common fear of the dentist! We promise to be patient and make you feel a little better about visiting the dentist.

We use dental technology because it streamlines your treatment and improves your comfort. But we haven’t skimped on personal touches that make you feel like a welcome member of our extended family. We have high standards, and we hope to prove that to you!

Our friendly team will take the time to get to know you and will greet you like an old friend every time you visit. To put you at ease, we designed our office to look more like someone’s comfortable living room than a sterile dental practice.

We love to treat patients of all ages and would like to welcome you as a patient to our Scottsboro, AL dental office. Dr. Hamilton and our dental team hope that you will give us the chance to take care of your oral health needs! Our goal is for you to be proud of your smile, and we’re not satisfied until you’re satisfied!

Our family dental office located in Scottsboro, AL on 72 in the Middleton Place shopping center (between Comfort Care and Rent a Center beside Carlile s Family Restaurant).

Please take a few moments to get to know Dr. Hamilton and her team, then call us to arrange your next dental exam and cleaning.
