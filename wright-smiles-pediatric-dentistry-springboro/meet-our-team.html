Meet Dr. Jody Wright

When I was a child, I always felt comfortable going to the dentist. I went to a pediatric dentist and loved it. While in high school and college, I worked as a dental assistant for the same pediatric dentist. I enjoyed watching him treat small children in such a kind and friendly manner. He always tried to make it fun for the staff and patients. He was my inspiration to become a pediatric dentist.

Today, I am blessed with the opportunity to make others feel the same and I love it. Having an appreciative child give me a hug and leave with a smile on their face is the most satisfying part of my practice. -Dr. Jody

Dr. Jody Wright is a board-certified pediatric dentist who has been practicing dentistry for more than 25 years. She earned her Specialty Certificate in Pediatric Dentistry from the State University of New York at Buffalo after completing her Pediatric Dental Residency at The Women’s and Children’s Hospital of Buffalo. Previously, she earned her undergraduate degree at Xavier University and her Doctorate of Dental Surgery at the Ohio State University College of Dentistry.
