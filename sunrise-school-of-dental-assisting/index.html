The practice

When you enroll at Sunrise School of Dental Assisting, you are given all the training and tools needed to become a valuable and successful member of a dental team. To learn more about our curriculum, please contact us or visit our application page to learn about the application process.

SSDA was founded by Dr. Dinah Vice and has over 30 years experience educating dental assistants. Each of our instructors also have over 20 years of experience in the dental field and we have been training Dental Assistants since 2007; making us the most tenured DAI program in the area.

Classes are held in the evening, meaning little personal or professional sacrifice is necessary in pursuing dental assistant training. And, because our program is only 14 weeks long, you can position yourself for a lifelong career in a relatively short amount of time. Our school provides students three convenient locations to choose from Raleigh, Greensboro, and Fayetteville.

Sunrise students have the opportunity to rotate through private dental offices, providing a superior, real-life training experience as compared to other schools that teach in “mock training” clinics. Our graduates also have the unique opportunity to shadow in any of our three Sunrise Dental office locations until a permanent position is secured.

Class sizes at each of our school locations are small enough that each student is afforded a high degree of individualized training. One-on-one tutoring is also available to all students if needed.

Sunrise School of Dental Assisting offers a top-quality Dental Assistant Education at an affordable price with interest-free financing and flexible payment options . Our program coordinator can assist you in selecting the best payment option for you. Tuition is only $3,295. A $300.00 discount will be given for students paying their tuition in full on the night of orientation.

Sunrise School of Dental Assisting does more than simply teach the skills of dental assisting. We teach students how to compose professional, effective resumes, how to succeed in interviews, and how to conduct successful job searches for a dental assistant career. Students are also given the first opportunity to interview for any positions available in any of our locations.

Dental assisting is a dynamic and interesting career for both men and women. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics projects that employment of dental assistants is expected to grow by 31 percent from 2010 to 2020, much faster than the average for most other occupations. Our dental assistant school is confident that ongoing research linking oral health and general health will continue to increase…
