About Our Expert Dentist in Germantown, MD

Facebook icon Instagram icon YouTube icon Google icon Meet the Doctor Dr. Adam Vitelli

Dr. Adam Vitelli, originally from Long Island, NY, has been practicing dentistry in the DC Metropolitan area since 2002. He attended Lehigh University and earned a Bachelor Science degree in biology, completing his undergraduate studies in 1997. He graduated from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine in 2001, and completed the General Practice Residency at Monmouth Medical Center the following year.

Dr. Vitelli originally practiced in Maryland from 2002 to 2008 before moving to DC to open Washington’s Choice Dental Care. He was pleased to return to Maryland in 2016 with his family, helping patients in our community to achieve lifelong oral health. He and his wife, Cindy, have four children and one beagle. Dr. Vitelli’s hobbies include racquetball, weightlifting, and golf.
