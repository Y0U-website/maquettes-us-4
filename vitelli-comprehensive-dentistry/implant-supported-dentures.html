Our Approach to Implant Denture Treatment

At Vitelli Comprehensive Dentistry, we take a comprehensive and personalized approach to tooth replacement. Dr. Vitelli works closely with each patient to develop a treatment plan that suits their dental anatomy, goals, and health needs.

Your implant-supported denture process may include:

Every case is unique, and our team ensures you are well-informed throughout the process.

  • Consultation and Imaging: Digital scans and 3D imaging to evaluate your bone health and design a treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement: Surgical placement of 2 to 6 titanium implants per arch under local anesthesia, with sedation available for your comfort.
  • Healing Period: A period of healing to allow implants to integrate with the jawbone.
  • Custom Denture Fabrication: Design and placement of your final overdenture, which snaps securely onto the implants.

FAQs About Implant-Supported Dentures

How long do implant-supported dentures last? With proper care, the implants can last decades, and the dentures themselves often last 10–15 years before needing replacement.

Will I need bone grafting? Some patients with bone loss may need grafting before implants can be placed. We’ll assess this with 3D imaging during your consultation.

Are they removable? Yes, most implant-supported dentures are removable by the patient for cleaning, but they snap firmly into place when worn.
