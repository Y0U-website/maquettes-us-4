Our Dental Implants Process

We handle your entire implant journey in our Germantown office. We use iTero digital impressions for precise, mess-free scanning at every stage:

  • Consultation: Dr. Adam Vitelli will assess your dental health with digital X-rays and discuss your goals to determine if dental implants are the right solution. Patients concerned about periodontal health will be evaluated at this stage as healthy gums are essential before placement.
  • Implant Placement: The titanium post is surgically inserted into the jawbone under local anesthesia. Nitrous oxide sedation is available for anxious patients. A healing period of 3–6 months follows to allow the implant to fully integrate with the bone.
  • Abutment Attachment: After healing, a small connector called an abutment is placed on the implant, serving as the anchor for the replacement tooth. ‍
  • Crown Placement: Finally, a custom-made dental crown is attached to the abutment, completing your natural-looking, fully functional tooth.
