About Rosenberg Family Dentistry

Dr. Joe Rosenberg graduated from UMKC School of Dentistry in May 1984. Wanting to move back to his family, he began practicing in St. John in July of 1984 and then established a second office in Larned, KS in 1990. The third Great Bend location was opened in July of 2017 when the practice was purchased from another local dentist. Doctor Rosenberg is married and has 3 daughters and 4 grandchildren. His family resides in St. John, KS.

With more than 30 years of experience in the dental industry, Dr. Rosenberg is committed to staying ahead of the curve with medical advances and investing in the latest technology to serve our patients. With a gentle touch and a compassionate attitude, our team will always maintain a calm atmosphere to make your family feel at ease - from routine cleanings to complicated procedures.

In addition to high tech diagnostic equipment, we also stay current on the latest products available to our patients. These include recommending oral health products such as 3M ESPE Clinpro 5000, MI Plus paste and Prevident 5000. We also highly recommend the CloSYSII system for oral home care for all our patients.

With three locations in Great Bend, Larned, and St. John, Kansas we are ready to help you schedule your next appointment close to home! Our staff has a reputation for friendly smiles and quality dental work, because our mantra is that everyone can "Get more out of life with a great smile!"
