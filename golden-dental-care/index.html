At Golden Dental Care, we proudly provide complete oral healthcare for patients of all ages—from toddlers to seniors—serving the communities of Golden, Wheat Ridge, Arvada…

The practice

Our story began in 1972 when Dr. John R. Burchfield, Sr. established the practice in Golden, Colorado. In 1998, his son, Dr. John M. Burchfield, Jr., joined him, and together they provided trusted, compassionate dental care for 13 years until Dr. Burchfield, Sr.’s retirement in 2011.

Today, Dr. John M. Burchfield, Jr. carries on his father’s legacy—grounded in integrity, personal care, and a commitment to excellence—while continuously incorporating the latest advancements in dental techniques to ensure a thorough and comfortable patient experience.

In July 2025, we welcomed Dr. Lauren Ballew to the team. She joins Dr. Burchfield in our ongoing mission to deliver outstanding dental care to every generation. Together with our dedicated staff, Dr. Burchfield and Dr. Ballew are honored to support you and your family’s oral health and are committed to earning your trust, respect, and long-term loyalty.

Please feel free to contact our office with any questions you may have. We look forward to meeting you and your family.

We have staff members able to communicate with Spanish-speaking patients.

Outside the office, Dr Burchfield cherishes spending time with his family; his wife Jennifer, and two daughters, Charlotte and Lydia. He also loves to travel, golf, hike and ski.

He is a member of the Metro Denver Dental Society, Colorado Dental Association, and the American Dental Association. Dr. Burchfield has been awarded the Delta Dental Certificate of Excellence from 2006 to the present. Dr. Burchfield has also been recognized with the Colorado Gerontological Society Award.

It is of utmost importance to Dr. Burchfield to maintain the highest levels of excellence in his practice by taking Continuing Education courses yearly.
