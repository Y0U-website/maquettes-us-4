What is the dental implant procedure, and how long does the dental implant process take?

The dental implant procedure includes an initial consultation, surgical placement of the implant, a healing period for integration, and final restoration placement. Depending on your needs, this may involve a single tooth implant or a full-arch solution like All-on-4.

The overall timeline varies because every patient heals differently. Some patients are candidates for immediate temporary teeth on the day of surgery, while others may require additional healing before a restoration is attached. Factors such as bone quality, gum health, and whether any preparatory treatment is needed all influence the schedule. During your consultation, you’ll receive a custom-made treatment timeline based on your specific needs.
