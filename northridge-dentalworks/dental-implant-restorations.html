Customized Implant Solutions

Our restorations are tailored to meet the individual needs of each patient, with options including:

  • Single-Tooth Replacement: An implant and a crown to replace a single missing tooth.

The Dental Implant Restoration Process

Our patient-centric approach ensures a comfortable and successful restoration experience:

  • Detailed Consultation and Planning: Assessing your oral health and planning the implant placement.
  • Surgical Placement: Inserting the implant into the jawbone with precision and care.
  • Healing and Osseointegration: Allowing time for the implant to integrate with the bone.
  • Custom Restoration: Designing and fitting the crown, bridge or denture to the implant.

Benefits of Dental Implant Restorations

  • Natural Look and Feel: Closely mimics the appearance and function of natural teeth.
  • Longevity: With proper care, implants can last a lifetime.
  • Improved Oral Health: Stimulates bone growth, maintaining jaw structure.
  • Enhanced Comfort and Confidence: Eliminates the discomfort and insecurity often associated with removable dentures.

FAQ

Dental implant restorations involve placing a crown, bridge or denture on top of a dental implant. This process completes the replacement of a missing tooth or teeth and restores both function and appearance.

A dental implant restoration is attached to a dental implant, which is surgically placed in the jawbone. In contrast, a traditional dental crown is placed over a natural tooth or a tooth that has undergone root canal therapy.

Caring for dental implant restorations involves maintaining good oral hygiene, including regular brushing and flossing and visiting our dentists for routine checkups. Proper care helps ensure the longevity and health of the restoration.

Dental implant restorations have a high success rate, with studies showing success rates of 95 % or higher. Success depends on factors such as the quality of the implant placement oral hygiene and regular dental care.
