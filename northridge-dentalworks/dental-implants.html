Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last in Northridge, California?

With proper oral hygiene and regular dental visits, implants can last 25 years or longer. The titanium post typically lasts a lifetime, while the crown may need replacement after 15 to 20 years.

Are dental implants painful to get?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort during implant placement. Local anesthesia numbs the area completely, and post-surgical pain is typically manageable with over-the-counter medications for a few days.
