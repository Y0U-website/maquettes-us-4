Frequently Asked Questions About Invisalign

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Most patients complete treatment within 12 to 18 months, though complex cases may require additional time for optimal results.

Can I eat normally with Invisalign aligners?

Yes, you remove aligners before eating and drinking anything except water, allowing you to enjoy all your favorite foods.

How often do I need appointments during Invisalign treatment?

Progress visits typically occur every six to eight weeks to monitor movement and provide new aligner sets.
