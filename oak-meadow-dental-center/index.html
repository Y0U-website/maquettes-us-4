Doctors Fantino and Dyer offer patients comfortable, high-quality appointments. Our caring dental team is here for your dental needs and we pride ourselves on the level of care…

The practice

The doctors have been members of the Santa Clara Dental Society, California Dental Association, and American Dental Association for over thirty years, following the standards and recommendations set forth.

As a family practice, we treat patients as we would our families, not compromising any of our standards. We offer care to patients ranging from one year old to over one hundred years old. Many of our patients and their families have been coming to our practice for multiple generations and we look forward to seeing patients grow from infancy to teenagers, adulthood, and beyond.

Doctors Fantino and Dyer are very involved in the community, educating local elementary school children each February during Children’s Dental Health Month.

In coordination with the local dental society, the entire office volunteers each year to help underserved children with their dental care.

They also sponsor local sports teams and school fundraisers, and participate in local health fairs.
