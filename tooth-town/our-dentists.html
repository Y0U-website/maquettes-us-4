Dr Fawaz Shihadeh

Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Fawaz Shihadeh has a deep passion for working with children, ensuring their first experiences with dentistry are positive, gentle, and fun . He understands that he plays a crucial role in shaping a child’s long-term attitude toward dental care and is committed to compassionate, trauma-free dentistry . That’s why he does not use methods like papoose boards —instead, he builds trust and confidence to make each visit a stress-free experience.

Dr. Shihadeh also has specialized training in treating patients with special healthcare needs , ensuring that every child receives the care and attention they deserve, no matter their unique requirements.

A Florida native, Dr. Shihadeh earned his B.S. in Neuroscience from the University of Pennsylvania before completing his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) at NYU College of Dentistry , where he also received a certificate in Special Patient Care . He further honed his expertise in pediatric dentistry by completing both his General Practice Residency and Pediatric Dental Residency at Montefiore Medical Center in New York City—one of the nation’s top pediatric dentistry programs.

He values building strong relationships with patients and their families and looks forward to welcoming you to Tooth Town. Beyond dentistry, Dr. Fawaz enjoys traveling, reading, and volunteering at animal shelters

Dr Chidiogo Obi

General Dentist

Dr. Chidiogo Obi brings a patient-first philosophy to Tooth Town, ensuring that every visit is comfortable, informative, and stress-free. She believes in a personalized approach to care , tailoring treatments to each patient’s unique needs while using the latest techniques to ensure excellent results.

Dr. Obi earned her Bachelor’s degree in Applied Economics with a minor in Business Administration and Management from the University of the Pacific , then went on to complete her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) at Roseman University , where she was one of only eight students in her class selected for an elite specialty program . Through this training, she gained advanced experience in endodontics, implants, and extractions , making her an expert in a wide range of restorative and surgical procedures.

Dr. Obi is passionate about continuing education and is always taking courses to stay ahead of the latest advancements in dental technology and patient care. She is known for going above and beyond to ensure her patients are comfortable, informed, and confident in their treatment plans.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Obi loves spending time with her adorable Shih Tzu, Zola .

  • Favorite Dental Device/Tool: Floss
  • Fun Fact: She and Dr. Achebe met in 7th grade and have been friends ever since!
