Meet Dr. Matt Roche

Georgia Native, Smile ‘Engineer,’ And Explorer

Dr. Roche brings a mix of enthusiasm and a love of education to patients at Innovative Orthodontics.

Dr. Roche started down the path of psychology before heading to dental school and pursuing his passion for orthodontics. He brings an energetic, thoughtful approach to orthodontics, as well as an enthusiasm for “engineering smiles.”

His passion for teaching and problem-solving contribute to his easy ability to create meaningful connections. He loves to take the time to educate his patients and their families about unique treatment options.

When he’s not creating beautiful smiles, he’s outside on another adventure — camping, hiking, kayaking, or exploring the world! If he’s not out enjoying the outdoor world, he loves exploring virtual worlds in video games.

Dr. Roche uses his knowledge on human connections and his passion for creating beautiful smiles to make his patients feel comfortable while building their enthusiasm for their future smiles.

Education & Qualifications

Dr. Roche’s Education Includes A Unique Blend Of Dentistry And Psychology

Dr. Roche started down one path before discovering his love of dentistry. He attended Mercer University in his hometown of Macon, Georgia, where he earned a bachelor’s degree in psychology.

He soon found his passion for dentistry, and traveled to Bradenton, Florida, to complete his dental training at LECOM School of Dental Medicine. He earned his doctor of medicine in dentistry (DMD) degree there before receiving his MS in Orthodontics from Seton Hill University in Greensburg, Pennsylvania.

Dr. Roche’s training in psychology and dentistry means he knows how to connect with patients and help them feel good about the journeys to their new smiles.
