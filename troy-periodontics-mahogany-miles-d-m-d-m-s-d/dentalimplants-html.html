Dental Implant FAQs

Straight answers to the questions patients ask most before choosing implants.

The titanium implant post itself is designed to last a lifetime. Clinical studies consistently show implant survival rates above 95 % at 10 years. The porcelain crown attached to the implant typically lasts 15 to 25 years depending on care and location in the mouth. With regular periodontal maintenance and good home hygiene, most patients keep their implants for the rest of their lives.

The procedure itself is performed under local anesthesia and, if desired, sedation, so most patients report feeling little to no discomfort during surgery. Many patients are surprised at how straightforward it is compared to a tooth extraction. Post-operative soreness is typically managed well with over-the-counter pain relievers and prescribed medication. Most patients return to normal activities within a day or two of the placement appointment.

The timeline varies depending on your situation. For a straightforward single implant with adequate bone, the process from placement to final crown is typically 3 to 6 months — most of which is healing time while you go about daily life normally. Cases requiring bone grafting or multiple implants take longer. Dr. Miles will outline a precise timeline during your consultation so you know exactly what to expect at every stage.

Many patients who have been told they lack sufficient bone are still eligible for implants after a bone grafting procedure. When a tooth is extracted and not replaced, the surrounding bone begins to resorb within months. The longer a tooth has been missing, the more bone may have been lost. However, bone grafting can rebuild the volume needed to place a stable implant. Dr. Miles uses 3D CBCT imaging to assess your bone precisely and will discuss all options during your evaluation.

Periodontists complete 3 additional years of surgical specialty training beyond dental school, focused specifically on the bone, gum tissue, and structures that surround teeth and support implants. This is the same tissue environment where implants are placed and must integrate. Dr. Mahogany Miles is also a specialist in treating gum disease, which must be addressed before and monitored after implant placement, giving you comprehensive, coordinated care all in one practice without the need for multiple referrals.
