Crowns

When a tooth has been weakened or fractured but it is still viable, a crown protects that tooth and extends its life many more years. Dr. Simon recommends a crown for a tooth that has received root canal therapy as the lack of blood supply to this tooth will cause it to become brittle. A shiny new crown will strengthen that tooth. With every crown placed in this office in Avon Lake, OH, Dr. Simon ensures that it is a match for the other teeth and an enhancement to the patient’s smile.

In Avon Lake OH every time Dr. Simon prepares a bridge for a patient, she takes great care to ensure that the result is not only a perfectly satisfactory fit but that it also has a perfect appearance that matches the other teeth in the mouth. When well cared for, a bridge can last for decades. We will make sure you not only receive a beautifully matching bridge, but that you also know just how to care for it to make it last.

Veneers

Veneers can benefit our patients from Avon Lake, OH in so many ways. If you’re not familiar with veneers, these are thin porcelain shells that cover the fronts and the tips of teeth. They can be used to even out the size or length of a patient’s teeth when a few of them are irregularly shaped. When one or more teeth are discolored or chipped, a few veneers can quickly restore this damaged smile. Veneers can also be used to cover all visible teeth when a patient has cosmetic problems throughout the mouth, resulting in a brilliant new smile.
