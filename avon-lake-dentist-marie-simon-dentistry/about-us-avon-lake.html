Meet Dr. Marie Simon

Dr. Simon knows Northwestern Ohio very well, having grown up in North Royalton and completed her undergraduate work at Ohio State University. She continued her education at the OSU College of Dentistry, obtaining her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 1988. Being a perfectionist with a special interest in cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Simon further completed post-doctorate training at Case Western Reserve University. As a result, Dr. Simon is fully qualified to provide beautifully designed, aesthetic restorations and cosmetic enhancements to her patients.

When Dr. Simon is not treating patients, she loves giving back to her community by supporting activities like Autism Awareness, Coats for Kids and Toys for Tots.

Our Mission

Our mission is providing the very best quality dental care in a calm, friendly and relaxing environment. We want every patient to feel confident that we will design the perfect treatment plan for them to improve or maintain their health or to help them achieve their goal of a more beautiful smile. While our primary focus is on quality care, we make every effort to keep patients comfortable throughout their treatment. We know that gentle treatment makes it so much easier for you to maintain a regular schedule of dental care.

Every staff member at Marie Simon Dentistry is here to give you a positive experience during every phase of your care. Please let us know if there is any way we can improve our service.
