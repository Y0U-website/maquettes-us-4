The practice

Regular exams are a critical part of preventive health care. During an exam, the hygienist will clean your teeth and check for cavities and gum disease.

Cleanings involve the removal of dental plaque from teeth with the intention of preventing cavities, gingivitis, and disease. A professional cleaning at least twice a year can improve your oral health.

Dental fillings restore the function and integrity of a missing tooth structure.

A dental extraction is the removal of teeth from the dental socket. Extractions are performed for a wide variety of reasons.

Dentures are prosthetic devices constructed to replace missing teeth.

A bridge is a fixed dental restoration used to replace one or more missing teeth.
