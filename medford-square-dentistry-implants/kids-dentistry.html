What to Expect at Your Child’s Dental Visit

We strive to make every visit comfortable and worry-free for both children and parents. Here’s what you can expect:

We start by warmly welcoming your child to our kid-friendly dental space and helping them feel at ease.

If age-appropriate, we’ll gently clean their teeth and perform a quick exam to check for any concerns.

We explain brushing and flossing in simple, engaging ways your child will understand—and answer any of your questions too.

From teething and early dental visits to braces and beyond—we’re here for every stage of your child’s journey.

When Should Kids Start Seeing the Dentist?

We recommend your child’s first visit to our Medford dental clinic by their first birthday, or within six months of the first tooth appearing. This early start helps us:

  • Identify concerns early on
  • Create a positive, comfortable dental experience
  • Promote lifelong healthy dental habits

Tips for Preparing Your Child for Their First Visit

Here’s how you can help your child feel confident ahead of their appointment:

Our goal is to make your child’s first dental experience in Medford something they’ll look forward to!

  • Choose morning appointments when your child is most rested
  • Talk about the dentist in happy, simple terms
  • Let them bring a comfort item or toy
  • Avoid negative words like “hurt”—we’ll handle everything with care
