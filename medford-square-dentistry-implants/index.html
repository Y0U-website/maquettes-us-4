The practice

Medford Square Dentistry & Implants provides complete dental care under one roof—from routine exams and family visits to implants and cosmetic procedures. No matter your needs, you’ll experience skilled, caring treatment in a welcoming environment.

Restore missing teeth and renew your smile with advanced implant dentistry. Dental implants provide a permanent, natural-looking solution that improves function, appearance, and confidence, helping you eat, speak, and smile with ease.

Straighten your teeth discreetly and comfortably with Invisalign clear aligners. Custom-made and virtually invisible, Invisalign helps improve your bite alignment and enhances your smile without metal brackets or wires—perfect for teens and adults alike.

Save your natural tooth and relieve dental pain with gentle root canal therapy. This procedure treats infection at the tooth’s root, preserving the structure while preventing the need for extraction or more extensive dental work.

Transform your smile with custom-made dental veneers. These thin porcelain shells cover flaws like chips, gaps, or discoloration. They create a bright, symmetrical, and confident smile with natural-looking, long-lasting results.

Prevent pain, crowding, or infection with safe and effective wisdom tooth evaluation and removal. Whether your wisdom teeth are impacted or emerging incorrectly, we offer expert care to protect your oral health and ensure a comfortable recovery.
