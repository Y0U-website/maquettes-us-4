Root Canal Treatment

Noticing these symptoms? Early intervention can help avoid complications and save your tooth.

  • Prolonged sensitivity to hot or cold
  • Swollen or sore gums near a tooth
  • Tooth darkening or discoloration
  • Small bump or boil near the tooth root

Advantages of Root Canal Treatment

  • Fast Pain Relief: Get lasting relief from sharp, throbbing pain.
  • Saves Your Tooth: Avoid extraction and keep your natural tooth structure.
  • Restores Normal Function: Eat, chew, and speak normally again.
  • Stops Spread of Infection: Prevent damage to other teeth and surrounding tissue.
  • Maintains Smile Aesthetics: After a crown, your tooth looks and functions beautifully.

How We Perform Root Canals in Medford

  • Comprehensive Exam: We start with a digital X-ray to evaluate the infection and plan treatment.
  • Comfortable Numbing: Clinical care ensures you’re relaxed and pain-free throughout.
  • Thorough Cleaning: We remove the infected pulp, clean the inner canals, and sterilize the space.
  • Sealing the Tooth: The canals are filled with a safe material to block bacteria from returning.
  • Final Restoration: A durable crown is often placed to protect the tooth and restore function.

Post-Treatment Care and Healing

  • Minor soreness may last for a day or two—easily managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.
  • Wait to chew on the treated side until we place your permanent crown.
  • Maintain brushing, flossing, and regular dental visits to keep your restored tooth healthy.
