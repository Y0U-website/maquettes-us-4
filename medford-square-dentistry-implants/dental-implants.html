Dental Implants

  • Natural Look and Feel: They mimic the look, feel, and function of real teeth so you can eat, speak, and smile confidently.
  • Preserve Jawbone Health: Implants help stimulate the jawbone and prevent bone loss, unlike bridges or dentures.
  • Protect Adjacent Teeth: They don’t require alteration of neighboring teeth for placement.
  • Enhanced Comfort: Since implants integrate with your bone, there’s no slipping or irritation.

Types of Dental Implant Solutions We Offer

  • Single-Tooth Implants: Restore a single missing tooth using a custom implant and crown for a seamless look.
  • Implant-Supported Bridges: Ideal when multiple teeth are missing—supported by implants for added stability.
  • Implant-Retained Dentures: Provide enhanced comfort and hold for those missing all upper or lower teeth.

The Dental Implant Process

  • Consultation & Planning: We perform a full evaluation using digital imaging to assess your jawbone and plan your care.
  • Implant Placement: The titanium post is placed under clinical care with minimal discomfort reported.
  • Healing & Osseointegration: The implant fuses with the bone over several months to ensure a strong foundation.
  • Restoration Placement: We place a custom crown, bridge, or denture to complete your smile naturally.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Even if you’ve experienced bone loss, our bone grafting procedures may allow you to receive implants successfully.

  • Are missing one or more teeth
  • Have healthy gums and adequate jawbone
  • Don’t have uncontrolled health conditions like diabetes
  • Maintain good oral hygiene and see your dentist regularly
