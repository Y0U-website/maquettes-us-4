The practice

At Sebring Oral Surgery, we proudly offer All-on-X dental implants a revolutionary, full-arch restoration solution designed for patients missing most or all of their teeth in the upper or lower jaw. This advanced technique uses strategically placed dental implants (typically 4 to 6 per arch, customized to your bone density and needs) to securely support a fixed, permanent set of natural-looking…

The implants fuse with your jawbone over time for long-term stability, helping preserve bone health, restore comfortable chewing and speaking, and eliminate the hassle of removable dentures and adhesives.

We don’t cut corners when it comes to your new teeth we use only the highest-quality materials and techniques for exceptional, long-lasting results.

Premium Cosmetic Zirconia Teeth Our fixed zirconia restorations are the best available: crafted in a state-of-the-art laboratory for superior durability, stain resistance, and a natural translucency, color, and lifelike beauty that closely mimics real teeth delivering a stunning, confident smile that looks and feels incredible.
