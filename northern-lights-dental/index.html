Welcome to Northern Lights Dental

We at Northern Lights Dental offer professional dental care in a warm, welcoming environment for ages 1 to 101+.

The practice

We view each visit from the mind of our patients. By staying up to date on research and education, we can correctly diagnose, assess risk, and thoroughly consider options with the patient. We strongly believe in electing for conservative approaches whenever possible.

At Northern Lights Dental, we are committed to providing excellent quality dental care to you and your families in and around Fargo, North Dakota. You can expect top of the line technology and abundant services in a comfortable, friendly environment. We treat our patients with the utmost compassion and will ensure you achieve and maintain a bright, healthy smile.
