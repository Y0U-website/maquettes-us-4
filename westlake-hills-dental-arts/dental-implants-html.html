What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are dedicated tooth replacements that rebuild not just the crown portion that makes up your smile, but the root that extends deep into the jawbone and surrounding gum tissue. They are typically made up of three components: a titanium post, a tooth-colored restoration, and an abutment to connect them together. Whether you need to replace one tooth or several, you can expect your implants to include these components.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

No two patients have the exact same experience when getting dental implants, but most treatments have the same four basic steps. We’ll go over the details in person, but for now, here are the basics of how dental implants work in Westlake.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

The dental implant process starts with a consultation, where you’ll spend some time talking to Dr. Long about the expectations and goals you have for your new teeth. Then, she’ll closely examine your mouth and jaw to find out whether you’re a good candidate for dental implants. If you are, then she’ll outline what you can expect at every stage in the treatment, including preparatory work. It’s not uncommon for patients to need one or more preliminary procedures before dental implant surgery, such as bone grafting to strengthen a jawbone that has deteriorated after tooth loss.

Dental Implant Surgery

Once you’re ready, we’ll refer you to a trusted implant dentist in Westlake for the surgical step of your treatment. By visiting a professional who has specialized in dental implant placement, you can rest easy knowing your care is in the best of hands.

The specialist will make sure your mouth is numb by administering local anesthesia. Next, they make small incisions at precise locations in the gums in order to access the jawbone. They insert the dental implant posts at predetermined positions, place caps over them to protect them during the healing process, and close up your gum tissue.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment

Next comes osseointegration. This process, which usually takes between three and six months, is essential for the long-term success of dental implants in Westlake. Immediately after your implants have been placed, your jawbone tissue begins to grow around them, to the point where they can serve as the roots of your new teeth.

When your implants have fully fused to your jaw, you’ll most likely undergo another procedure to have abutments secured on top of them. Abutments are little metal connectors that let Dr. Long attach your custom restoration and complete the visual part of your smile.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restoration(s)

Healing from your abutment surgery should only take a few weeks. At that point, you’ll return to our dental office to have your new pearly whites placed at last! Dr. Long will secure your dental crown, bridge, or denture to your abutments. Because these restorations are made from impressions taken of your smile, you can trust that they’ll look just as natural as the teeth you were missing!

Benefits of Dental Implants

Are you currently in the process of deciding which tooth-replacement solution is best for you? Day-to-Day Benefits

Below are some of the day-to-day benefits you’ll enjoy with dental implants:

  • Hassle-Free Maintenance : In short, you won’t have to buy a bunch of special oral hygiene products or adopt a cumbersome dental care regimen with dental implants. Quite the opposite since you can maintain them by simply taking care of them like you take care of your natural teeth!
  • Increased Confidence : On top of feeling natural inside the mouth, dental implants look incredible because they are custom-made for each patient. In other words, they don’t compromise on look or function.
  • Varied Diet : Since dental implants get their strength from your jawbone and are crafted from durable dental materials, they make it possible to eat your favorite foods without much concern.

Health Benefits

Another reason that dental implants are preferred by patients and dentists alike is because they benefit your oral and overall health. Here are a few examples:

  • Jawbone Preservation : Since dental implants replace the root of the tooth, they can mimic all of its previous functions that were lost when the tooth was extracted. This includes bone stimulation, which helps preserve your jawbone and maintain the strength of your bite.
  • Protection for Natural Teeth : Unlike dental bridges, you won’t need to modify existing teeth since the tooth-colored restorations sit directly on the implanted titanium post.
  • Better Overall Health : From malnutrition to indigestion, there are several ways tooth loss can negatively impact your overall health. Fortunately, dental implants can help in that aspect too since they help you chew, allow you to eat a more varied diet, and prevent dental drift!

Long-Term Benefits

Arguably one of the biggest reasons to choose dental implants over other tooth-replacement options is because of the long-term benefits they offer, including:

  • High Success Rate : Dental implants are known for having an incredibly high success rate when placed by a professional. In fact, success rates hover between 95 % and 98 %, meaning you can smile, eat, and speak with confidence.
  • Youthful Appearance : It’s common for patients who are missing teeth to have a more aged appearance. The good news is that dental implants fill the missing spaces in your mouth and preserve your jawbone, helping you look and feel more youthful.
  • Long-Lasting Results : Dental implants can remain healthy in the jaw for long periods of time, typically 30 years or longer. This cannot be said for other options, which last between 10 and 15 years, on average, before needing replacement.

Missing Single Tooth

A single tooth can easily be replaced by inserting an implant into the jaw, attaching an abutment, and covering it with a dedicated porcelain crown to ensure the best esthetics. The restoration can occur once the implant has fully integrated with the bone tissue, which is a process known as osseointegration.

Missing Multiple Teeth

While multiple implants are needed to replace multiple teeth, you don’t need as many as you might think. For example, a pair of dental implants can typically hold a bridge containing three to four crowns. Unlike traditional dental bridges, you won’t need to modify existing tooth enamel just so they can hold your restoration.

The Parts of Your Dental Implant

The cost of your dental implants can also be influenced by the following parts:

  • Material : Implants constructed out of zirconia instead of titanium often cost less.
  • Number of Implants : One implant can replace a single tooth, two for replacing a consecutive row of teeth, and 4 or more for restoring an arch. Naturally, the more implants you need, the higher the final cost will be.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

If you’ve received your dental implants, congratulations! They’re an excellent way to replace your missing teeth that are built to last! However, much of their success now depends on proper care. By maintaining a consistent oral hygiene routine and making smart lifestyle choices, you can ensure that your implants remain in excellent condition for a lifetime. Here are some great tips from Dr. Long to help you protect your investment and enjoy a healthy smile for life.

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

Keeping your dental implants clean is the best way to prevent gum disease and other complications. Brush your teeth twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush and toothpaste with the ADA seal of approval. Don’t forget to floss around your implant to remove any plaque buildup it s the same as flossing your regular teeth! If you want to go the extra mile, consider using an antimicrobial mouthwash to further reduce bacteria. A diligent oral hygiene routine helps ensure the longevity of your dental implants and keeps your whole mouth fresh and clean.

Eat a Healthy Diet

A balanced diet rich in vitamins and minerals is an essential ingredient for supporting the health of your implants and the structures of your mouth that support it. Foods high in calcium and vitamin D, such as dairy products and leafy greens, strengthen your jawbone and surrounding teeth. Try to check nutrition labels and incorporate foods rich in vitamin C, like citrus fruits and bell peppers, to promote gum health. Being smart and careful with your diet will give your dental implants the best chance to thrive!

Break Bad Habits

Certain habits are notorious for causing problems with implants. For example, smoking impairs healing and increases the risk of implant failure. Chewing on hard objects like ice, pens, or fingernails can also damage your implants or surrounding teeth. By recognizing and breaking these habits, you can keep your new smile looking and feeling good much longer.

Protect Your Dental Implants

To safeguard your dental implants, you should consider investing in protection, especially if you grind your teeth at night. A customized nightguard is a simple and effective way to shield your investment from harm. Similarly, if you play contact sports, a mouthguard will protect your implants and natural teeth from injury. Even if you purchase a simple boil and bite mouthguard from a big box store it’s still much better than nothing!

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Regular dental checkups and cleanings are the last piece of the puzzle when it comes to maintaining the health of your dental implants. During these visits, our team will check the condition of your implants and clean hard-to-reach areas. Try to visit us at least twice a year so we can catch early signs of developing issues. This is a critical part of ensuring your dental implants are successful and your overall oral health is protected.

How long do dental implants last?

Depending on how well you take care of your new teeth, dental implants are known to last a lifetime. Because they are seated within the jawbone and fuse with the remaining tissues, they are much more stable and durable than traditional dental bridges and dentures. As long as you commit to taking proper care of your teeth once they are in place, you can expect to enjoy them for the remainder of your life.

How successful are dental implants?

The success rate for initial placement is around 95 %. After 10 years, this percentage only drops to 90-95 %. It is safe to say that dental implants are considered one of the most beneficial forms of tooth replacement on the market.

The likelihood that complications will arise once your implant posts are in place is slim; however, trouble can occur if you do not maintain good oral hygiene or sustain severe facial trauma. Am I too young to get dental implants?

It is unlikely that our team will agree to put dental implants in place if you are a teenager or even someone in your early 20s.
