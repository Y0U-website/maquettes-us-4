COVID-19 Dental Office Safety Protocol West Lake Hills, TX

Our Commitment to Your Safety

Nothing matters more to us than the health and safety of our patients and that is we have always kept sterilization and cleanliness at the highest standard to protect both you and our team even more, we’ve implemented the following steps into our dental office’s protocols. If you have questions about how we’re making your visit a safe experience, feel free to reach out to us.

Patient Masks We no longer require patients to wear masks, but we will continue to encourage and support any and all patients that feel more comfortable wearing a mask while in our office.

Team Vaccinations Each member of our team has now been vaccinated against COVID-19.

Advanced Team Training Each member of our team has completed additional awareness and prevention programs to enhance their knowledge and preparedness.

Sequenced Patient Scheduling Plain and simple, we are not seeing as many patients. We are allocating more time between visits for coming, going and cleaning.

Expanded Patient Appointments In an effort to save you time and multiple exposures, we are offering extended appointments. This allows us to do more dentistry in a single visit.

Operatories Disinfected After Every Appointment We maintain exceptional cleanliness by thoroughly wiping, sanitizing, and disinfecting all instruments, equipment, and surfaces in each treatment room after every patient.

Deep Cleaning Every Evening In addition to regular cleaning and sanitizing throughout the day, we conduct an extensive and thorough cleaning every night.

CDC-Approved Instrument Sterilization This is our standard procedure, consistently followed without exception. Anything that comes into contact with your mouth undergoes thorough sterilization with heat and pressure between each use.

ASTM Level 3 Masks The beautiful smiles of our clinical team – dentists, hygienists, and assistants – will be protected behind masks while treatment is being provided to each patient.

Front Desk Masks Our friendly administrative team would be happy to wear a mask during your visit upon your request. Otherwise, you will see their smiling faces while greeting you!

HEPA Air Filtration Our air has never been cleaner. We have added HEPA-grade filters to our HVAC system along with stand-alone air purifiers in each treatment room.
