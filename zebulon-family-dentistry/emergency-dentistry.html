Emergency Dentistry in Zebulon, NC

Emergency dental care provides prompt treatment for unexpected dental problems that require immediate attention. Whether you’re dealing with a severe toothache, a broken or knocked-out tooth, swelling, or a dental injury, our team is here to help relieve pain and protect your oral health. When you call, we’ll assess your symptoms and schedule you as quickly as possible, often the same day for urgent situations.

During your visit, we’ll identify the cause of the problem, explain your treatment options, and provide the care you need to get you feeling better. Fast treatment can prevent complications, save damaged teeth, and help you return to your daily routine with comfort and confidence.

We accept emergency appointments during our office hours. You can call for an emergency visit, request an appointment online, or come into our office.

The Process

Contact our office immediately so your symptoms can be evaluated and an emergency appointment can be arranged as soon as possible. Your dentist will examine your teeth, gums, and jaw, and may take digital X-rays to identify the source of the problem. You will receive a diagnosis and a personalized treatment plan based on your dental emergency. Treatment may include relieving pain, controlling bleeding, treating infection, repairing a damaged tooth, or stabilizing or replacing a knocked-out tooth. Before you leave, you’ll receive instructions to support healing and protect your oral health.

Next Steps

Follow all home care instructions provided by your dental team to support healing. Take prescribed or recommended medications as directed. Avoid chewing on the treated area until your dentist advises it is safe. Attend any recommended follow-up appointments to monitor healing or complete additional treatment. Contact our office immediately if your symptoms worsen or new concerns develop.

FAQ

  • What qualifies as a dental emergency? Severe pain, swelling, bleeding, a knocked-out tooth, a broken tooth, or signs of infection should be evaluated as soon as possible.
  • Can I walk in for emergency dental care? Walk-ins may be accepted when available, but calling ahead helps the team prepare for your arrival and provide faster care.
  • Should I go to the emergency room for a dental emergency? If you have severe facial swelling, uncontrolled bleeding, or a life-threatening emergency, seek immediate medical care. Otherwise, contact our team first.
