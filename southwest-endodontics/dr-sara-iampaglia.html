Dr. Sara Iampaglia

After working a few years in private practice in Sao Paulo, Brazil, Dr. Iampaglia relocated to Chicago where she was accepted into the Post-Graduate Program at the University of Illinois-Chicago College of Dentistry . She earned a master’s degree (MS) in Oral Sciences while immersing herself in research projects, including extensive research in biomodification of root dentin using bioactive agents.

Her passion for endodontics led her to pursue and complete her Postdoctoral Endodontic Certification and upon completion of the program, Dr. Iampaglia received her specialty license to practice endodontics.

Dr. Iampaglia resides in Naperville with her husband, two daughters and their dog, Ruby. She is fluent in three languages and can communicate with patients in English, Portuguese and Spanish . Outside of work, she loves to cook, bake and listen to music.

You can see Dr. Iampaglia in both locations, Geneva and Orland Park.
