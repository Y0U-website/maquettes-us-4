The practice

Dr. Keith F. Hinnant , Dr. Brittney Thorne and Dr. Fraser Murphy know your family’s smiles are important to you. They’re important to our doctors and team members , too. More Topics I grew up at the office and watched my father, Dr. Willard Hinnant, care for his patients. It was incredible to see how he was able to improve a person’s life, and I admired the connections he made with each patient. For more than 20 years, I’ve carried on my father’s legacy: treating each patient with compassion and respect, and helping them achieve their healthiest smiles.

My patients feel like family to me, and I care about each person’s well-being. Not only do I want everyone to achieve a healthy, becoming smile, but I want them to have overall wellness and to enjoy life.

I particularly enjoy restorative and aesthetic work. It’s a great feeling to restore a person’s oral health, to allow them to chew properly and enjoy eating the foods they like again, and to give them a smile they love in the process.
