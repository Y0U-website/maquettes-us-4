Meet the Poppy Kids Team

Alexandra

Alexandra’s passion for dentistry began through a close family friend whose father, an oral surgeon, inspired her with his enthusiasm for the field. She brings her curiosity and energy to assisting in pediatric dentistry, where she loves learning and connecting with young patients, making each visit a positive experience.

Besides work, Alexandra enjoys practicing yoga and curling up with a good book. She takes pride in being someone children remember fondly, associating their dental visits with positive memories that build confidence in their oral health.

Emily

Emily has been immersed in dentistry since June 2005, bringing 20 years of experience to her work. She loves getting to know people and their families, creating meaningful connections with each patient that often last for many years. Emily is particularly drawn to working with children because of their cheerful nature and imaginative perspective on the world, which brightens every single day.

Outside of her professional life, Emily enjoys spending time with her husband, three boys, and three cats, and loves baking sourdough bread.

Neah

Neah has been in dentistry for two years and absolutely loves making kids feel safe and comfortable, turning every visit into a positive experience. Few things bring her more joy than seeing a child walk out of the office smiling. It’s the highlight of her day and the reason she loves this work.

Outside the office, Neah enjoys traveling, snowboarding, and discovering new restaurants. Sushi is always at the top of her list. These passions keep her energized and add warmth to every family she welcomes.

Jen

With ten years of Applied Behavior Analysis experience across a variety of settings, Jen is passionate about helping children feel supported, confident, and emotionally successful. At Poppy Kids, she brings that same warmth and care to young patients navigating the dental setting.

Outside the office, Jen enjoys hiking, spending time at the beach, and hanging out with friends. These passions keep her grounded and inspire the kindness and patience she brings to every child she meets.

Hawley

Hawley is Poppy Kids Pediatric Dentistry’s beloved pup and sweetest team member. A gentle and loving golden retriever, she has mastered the art of spreading comfort with her wagging tail and calm nature. While Hawley isn’t always at the office—when she is here, she brings love and joy, helping make visits feel warm and positive.

Outside of her “work,” Hawley enjoys rolling on the lawn and lounging in the shade at her grandparents’ house. She also loves playing with her goldendoodle cousins, Tiffany and Apple, and never turns down belly rubs, treats, or a game of tug.
