Meet Dr.Boyapati

Dr.Yasoda Boyapati started her dental journey in India where she graduated with a BDS degree from NTR University of Health Sciences. After moving to United States, she was awarded Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2012 by the prestigious New York University. Soon after graduating from dental school, Dr.Boyapati and her husband chose west Texas as their home where they are currently raising their two children.

Dr.Boyapati, has been practicing dentistry in the West Texas for more than a decade now. Dr.Boyapati, likes to keep herself updated with the latest advancements in the field of dentistry to provide the best care for her patients. She did hundreds of hours of continuing education even after graduating from dental school.

Dr. Boyapati holds Implant fellowship form Misch international Implant institute. As a Misch Fellowship dentist, Dr.Boyapati gained advanced knowledge about implants.

Bringing Bright Smiles to the Permian Basin: Dr. Yasoda Boyapati’s Mission to Serve Underserved Communities

By Kieri Sitz, THSteps Provider Relations Representative

In the vast stretches of West Texas, where access to specialized medical and dental care can often be limited, one provider is making a significant difference in the lives of children and families: Dr. Yasoda Boyapati. Through her dedicated work at Vibrant Family Dental located in Odessa, TX and Wee Care Dental located in Crane, TX, Dr. Boyapati has become a trusted name in the Permian Basin-especially among children and families who are Medicaid recipients.

Her approach blends advanced clinical skill with a deep understanding of the community’s needs. Whether it’s a routine cleaning or more complex restorative procedures, Dr. Boyapati ensures that each patient receives personalized care tailored to their health, background, and comfort level.

For medical professionals across Texas, Dr. Boyapati represents a vital referral partner-especially for pediatric patients in need of a Medicaid-friendly dental home. Her offices are well-equipped, her team is highly trained, and her commitment to rural health is unwavering. She welcomes collaboration and referrals from pediatricians, family doctors, and other specialists who are also committed to improving the health and wellness of Texas children.

As the healthcare landscape continues to evolve, it is providers like Dr. Boyapati who are leading the way in ensuring that care is equitable, accessible, and patient-centered. Her work stands as a powerful reminder that geography should never be a barrier to a healthy smile.
