What to Expect

Your first appointment typically lasts 60–90 minutes and includes everything you need to start on a great dental journey.

Step 1 Complete Your Paperwork Arrive 10 minutes early, or fill out forms online to complete a brief health history. This helps us tailor your care from the first moment. Our online forms use the same secure patient portal you&#x27;ll use for future visits, so it&#x27;s worth creating your account now.

Step 2 Comprehensive Exam & X-Rays Dr. Jain performs a thorough exam of your teeth, gums, bite, and jaw. Digital X-rays give us a precise picture of your full oral health.

Step 3 Professional Cleaning Our hygienist Michelle removes plaque and tartar buildup, polishes your teeth, and gives personalized home-care tips.

Step 4 Your Treatment Plan Dr. Jain reviews all findings with you, answers your questions, and — if needed — outlines a clear, prioritized treatment plan with no pressure.
