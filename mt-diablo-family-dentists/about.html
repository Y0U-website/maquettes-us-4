Dr. Naina Jain, DMD

Dr. Naina Jain founded Mt. Diablo Family Dentists with one goal in mind: to give every patient the kind of dental experience they actually look forward to. She earned her Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry (DMD) degree from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine, then completed advanced training in restorative and cosmetic dentistry before bringing her practice to Concord, CA — where she has spent over a decade building relationships with families across the East Bay.

What sets Dr. Jain apart is her rare combination of clinical skill and genuine warmth. She takes the time to understand each patient&#x27;s goals, history, and comfort level before recommending any treatment. For patients who experience dental anxiety, she is known for her calm, patient-centered approach that puts even the most nervous visitors at ease.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Jain is deeply committed to her community. She participates in local dental outreach programs and is a member of the American Dental Association and the California Dental Association.

Dr. Bhavana Chandradhara, DMD

Dr. Bhavana Chandradhara earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachusetts. Before relocating to the United States, she completed her Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) and Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) in Conservative Dentistry and Endodontics from Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences in India, where she practiced as an endodontist and restorative dentist and built extensive experience in advanced restorative procedures, microscopic endodontics, root canal therapy, and esthetic dentistry.

Known for her gentle chairside manner and commitment to patient education, Dr. Chandradhara believes that informed patients are empowered to make the best decisions for their oral health. She is an active member of the American Dental Association and the California Dental Association, and outside the practice she enjoys traveling with her family, reading, practicing yoga, and exploring new cultures and cuisines.
