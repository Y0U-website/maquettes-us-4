What are Dental Implants?

Implants are a screw-like structure that is placed underneath the gum in the jawbone as an artificial root. It is fixed to support the crown or dentures, which provide a natural appearance to the mouth.

Procedure For Dental Implants

Fitting implants is done surgically. In the procedure, a trained specialist provides the patient with a customized treatment plan that addresses the specific needs and instructions before the surgery.

Next, a post made of titanium is placed in the socket of the missing tooth’s bone surgically. After the surgery, the dentist waits until the jawbone is healed completely and has been grown around the implanted metal post. It might take from 6 to 12 weeks.

After it is secured in the jawbone, the dentist connects an abutment to hold the artificial tooth into the mouth securely. Your new teeth or tooth is created using the impression taken during the examination session. Lastly, a crown is attached to the abutment appropriately.

Aftercare For Dental Implants

After the surgery, your dentist will instruct you to avoid eating hard, hot, or sticky food items. Instead, eat soft or cold food during the healing process. Apart from that, you can brush and floss your mouth similarly you do with your original teeth. You will also be asked to visit the dentist for a routine dental checkup or follow-up after the treatment.

Having implants provides greater comfort and improves speech dramatically. Other than that, an enhanced smile will boost your self-esteem and make you smile a lot with being conscious about your appearance. Also, if taken care of appropriately, your implants will last longer than their actual expiry date.
