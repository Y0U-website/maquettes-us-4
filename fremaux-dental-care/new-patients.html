NEW DENTAL PATIENTS IN SLIDELL, LA

WHAT SHOULD YOU KNOW ABOUT ON YOUR FIRST VISIT?

At Fremaux Dental Care, we’re dedicated to making every patient experience comfortable and convenient. When you call to schedule your first appointment or fill out our form below, our team will send you the new patient forms via email to help get you started. Please use these forms as a guide to finding what information is required for our office.

When you arrive at our office you will be given a tablet connected to our secure network. We take pride in our digital processes that increase productivity and reduce waste. We have taken all necessary steps to ensure your information is safe and secure!

Patients invest in their dental insurance coverage, and our team is dedicated to helping them maximize their coverage. We are in-network with many major PPOs, and we’re happy to process and file insurance claims for any provider. Our knowledgeable team works to ensure patients fully understand the portion of coverage they are responsible for, and allow them to plan for financing accordingly. As insurance varies between patients, we only offer estimates, but we do everything possible to provide accurate guidance.

The Fremaux Dental Care team is dedicated to offering high quality, affordable treatment for our patients, but for those who need more complex procedures financing may be important. We are happy to work with CareCredit to offer low or no interest payment plans for qualified patients. We are not a Medicaid provider.

To learn more about using insurance and financing options with our office, head over to our Insurance & Financing page with the link below.
