The practice

The practice is conveniently located just across the street from the Fremaux Town Center. There’s plenty of parking onsite, and the exam rooms feature state-of-the-art diagnostics, including intraoral scanners and digital X-rays.

Individuals and families from all over the Northshore visit Fremaux Dental Care for preventive dentistry , including dental exams and cleanings . The practice also offers more specialized care, including cosmetic dentistry , emergency dentistry , and root canal therapy.

Fremaux Dental Care is one of the few practices in the area offering access to modern aesthetics and orthodontics. The providers use CEREC same-day crowns, dental veneers, Invisalign oral aligners, and SureSmile to help people achieve stunning smiles. For those with wrinkles and fine lines, Fremaux Dental Care administers Botox .

The practice takes a conservative and patient-centered approach to oral health care. The providers know dentistry isn’t one-size-fits-all and tailor personalized treatment plans to each individual’s needs and goals.

Britney Beard, DDS, is an experienced cosmetic, general, and family dentist at Fremaux Dental Care, providing high-quality oral health care to individuals and families in Slidell, Louisiana, and throughout St. Tammany Parish.

Dr. Beard is a proud Louisiana native. She began her career pursuits at the Louisiana State University School of Dentistry in New Orleans, earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery. After graduation, Dr. Beard pursued continuing education in aesthetic, implant, and sleep apnea dentistry, before moving to the Northshore.

At Fremaux Dental Care, Dr. Beard works with people of all ages. She diagnoses and treats most oral issues and has special interests in orthodontics, the placement of dental implants, and the management of temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders.
