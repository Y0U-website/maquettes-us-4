The practice

We offer a variety of treatments for all ages. We evaluate the health of your mouth and customize treatment to your needs and goals. We want to help you achieve a healthy, happy smile that you will be proud to show off!

We strive to better ourselves as individuals and health care providers every day. Our team works together to ensure that you receive the highest quality dental care. You’ll be treated like family the second you walk in the door!

Dr. Seth grew up in northeast Nebraska in the small town of Coleridge. True to his corn born roots he received his Bachelor of Science in Biology from the University of Nebraska.

Dr. Lauren grew up in Wichita, KS. She received her Bachelor of Science in Biology from Drake University where she also played soccer for the Bulldogs.

Drs. Seth and Lauren met during their first year of graduate school at the University of Nebraska Medical Center. After a year of intense studying and discovering their shared passion for smiling they fell in love and got married. It was quite toothfully a match made in Heaven.

After graduation they moved to Iowa, where Dr. Lauren worked in Sioux City and Dr. Seth in the small town of Moville. In the Spring of 2019 they welcomed their firstborn son, Henry, and life for them has never been the same. They knew it was then time to live out their dream of working together in their own family practice so they migrated north to Harrisburg, SD to plant some roots.
