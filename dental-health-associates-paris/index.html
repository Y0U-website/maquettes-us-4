The practice

Dr. Logan Nichols grew up in Leitchfield, Kentucky, with his parents and two younger brothers. His grandfather was a dentist and both of his parents are dentists, so from a young age, Dr. Nichols had an interest in dentistry.

He attended Grayson County High School, then pursued a Bachelor of Science degree in biology from the University of Kentucky, where he graduated in 2017. He continued his education at the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry, earning his DMD in 2021. After graduating, Dr. Nichols participated in an oral surgery internship at The Oral Surgical Institute in Nashville, Tennessee, where he…

Following the completion of his internship, Dr. Nichols returned to Lexington, where he now lives with his wife, Dr. Aliah Nichols, and their dog, Lily. He has been providing dental care in Paris since 2022.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Nichols enjoys spending time with his wife and their friends, attending UK sporting events, playing golf, and traveling.

Originally from Shelbyville, Kentucky, Dr. Claire Gibson earned both her Bachelor of Science degree and her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Kentucky . A proud UK Wildcat fan , she has deep roots in the Lexington community, where she currently resides.

Dr. Gibson joined Dental Health Associates in 2023 and is passionate about providing exceptional general dentistry to patients of all ages. Her areas of expertise include restorative procedures, root canal therapy, and extractions . She is dedicated to delivering high-quality care while building lasting relationships with both new and returning patients.

Outside of the office, Dr. Gibson enjoys spending time with her family, exploring the outdoors, and trying out new recipes.

Dr. Anna Newman is a proud graduate of the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry and brings over 35 years of experience in all aspects of general dentistry. Throughout her career, she has been dedicated to providing exceptional care while building meaningful relationships with her patients.
