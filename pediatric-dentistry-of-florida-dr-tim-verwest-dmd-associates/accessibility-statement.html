Accessibility Statement

  • Sound is opt-in: the site is silent unless you choose to turn sounds on.
  • Keyboard access: navigation, menus, and interactive features work with a keyboard, and the games include keyboard-accessible modes.
  • Screen-reader support: meaningful images carry text descriptions, controls are labeled, and pages use proper headings and landmarks.
  • Readable design: generous type sizes, strong color contrast, and touch targets sized for small (and grown-up) hands.
  • Printable alternatives: every storybook has a print-at-home PDF, and our what-to-expect storybook includes a printable social story for children who like to preview new places before visiting them.

Known limitations

Some parts of the site are visual by nature: decorative water effects (which Calm Waters turns off) and drawing-based games (which include simpler accessible modes). Videos play through YouTube’s player, which offers caption controls where captions are available. If any of this gets in your way, we want to know about it.

Need help, or found a barrier?

If you have difficulty using any part of this website, call us at (239) 482-2722 and our team will gladly provide the information you need another way, over the phone or in print. If you tell us about a barrier on the site, we will work to fix it.

Ongoing work

Accessibility is never finished. New pages are built against the same checklist, and this statement is reviewed as the site grows.
