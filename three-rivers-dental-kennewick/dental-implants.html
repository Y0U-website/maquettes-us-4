What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are permanent teeth replacements, and they’ve become a popular alternative to dentures over the past few years. While they can cost more than dentures, they last longer and save you money over time. Dental implants lead to fewer visits to the dentist because they’re easier to maintain compared to dentures.

Are Implants Right for Me?

You can use dental implants to replace a single tooth, multiple teeth or a full upper and/or lower set of teeth. If you need to restore a full arch, you may be a perfect candidate for implant therapy. Dr. Roach will place four to six implants and attach a full arch of teeth, giving the procedure its name (all teeth, only four implants).

Just as dental implants have emerged as the preferred choice of permanent teeth replacement, the all-on-four approach has become the preferred model for dental implant treatment. What Are My Dental Implant Options?

Full Mouth Dental Implants

Patients who need to replace a full upper and/or lower set of teeth can now receive full mouth implants. Dr. Roach will place 4 implants and attach a full arch of teeth, giving the procedure its name.

Implant-Retained Bridge

Occasionally a patient is missing more than one tooth, but still has healthy gums and surrounding teeth, and an implant-retained bridge is the best solution. Two implants are placed, supporting a bridge, leaving other natural teeth intact.

Single-Tooth Dental Implants

If you have lost a single tooth due to an accident or from periodontal disease, that one tooth can be replaced using a dental implant. Your new tooth will look and function just like your other natural teeth.
