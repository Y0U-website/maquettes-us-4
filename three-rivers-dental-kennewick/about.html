About Three Rivers Dental

We are committed to our community’s health by providing a relaxed, family-friendly environment. We take the time to answer questions for our patients, prior to treatment, so that we can reduce their anxiety about both the cost and process.

Innovative Technology and Transparent Treatment Plans

Our goal is for every patient to leave fully informed of their dental condition. We do not push unnecessary treatments, instead, we use intra-oral photos, x-rays as well as a one-on-one discussion to effectively communicateyour options. We always discuss costs prior to treatment to reduce anxiety over finances.

Your Total Health is Our Primary Concern

We understand the link between dental inflammatory disease and cardiovascular health. We know the effect on one’s psyche from inability to smile confidently. We know the negative effect of not having natural teeth on one’s lifespan. We are here to provide opportunity to improve your health. We desire a meaningful, long-term relationships with our patients, addressing the relationship between dental disease and systemic health. We want the children we see today to bring in their family for dental care in 20 years time.
