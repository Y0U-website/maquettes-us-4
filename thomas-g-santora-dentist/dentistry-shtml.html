Meet New Jersey’s Trusted Local Dentist

Dr. Thomas G. Santora earned his DMD degree from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in December 1972 and served in the military as a captain in the Dental Corps at Fort Knox, Kentucky for the following two years. His skillful blending of traditional dentistry with proven state-of-the-art technology and procedures has earned him the trust of the Roselle Park, NJ community for over 40 years.

4 Reasons Patients Choose Dr. Santora for Their Dental Health

1. Thorough Dental Care

Because Dr. Santora takes a long-term view of his patients’ dental health, he provides them with through oral health examinations and recommends preventative measures to help them minimize potential issues. He makes his patients feel secure by always explaining treatment choices and recommendations.

2. Expertise and Experience

Dr. Santora is dedicated to continually renewing his skills through both continuing education and his affiliation with both the NYU Dental School and Visztara Institute for Dental Implantology. His advanced dentistry skill set combined with over 40 years of experience as a dentist enables him to select the most appropriate treatments for each patient adroitly.

3. State-of-the-Art Equipment and Procedures

Dr. Santora keeps abreast of advances in technology and uses only the most advanced dental techniques and equipment that have been proven effective in dental diagnostics and care.

4. Safe, Durable Materials

Dr. Santora uses only the safest, most durable substances for your dental care, sourcing ceramics and other restorative dental materials exclusively from the most established, trustworthy laboratories.
