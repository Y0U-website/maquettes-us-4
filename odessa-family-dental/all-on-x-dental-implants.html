The True Smile Forever Care Plan

The final teeth begin a new phase of care. Recall intervals are personalized to your health, hygiene, implant history, and risk—not automatically the same for every patient. Continuing visits may include:

The care plan is an ongoing maintenance relationship. It does not mean unlimited coverage or that every future service is included without charge.

  • Checking the gums, implants, prosthetic components, bite, and access for cleaning.

  • Addressing inflammation, wear, loosening, fracture, or bite changes early.

The True Smile Guarantee

The final restoration is supported by The True Smile Guarantee, provided in writing with treatment. Before treatment, we explain the coverage, length of protection, maintenance requirements, and exclusions so you know what the commitment includes.

The written terms—not the name of the protocol or care plan—define the protection for your restoration. This is not a guarantee of a particular clinical outcome or a promise of unlimited lifetime coverage or free repairs. Results still depend on individual health, healing, home care, attendance, habits, and other patient-specific factors.
