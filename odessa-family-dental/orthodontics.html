Orthodontic Treatment FAQs

Who can benefit from orthodontic treatment?

Children, teens, and adults may benefit from orthodontic treatment if teeth are crowded, spaced, rotated, or if the bite causes uneven wear, cleaning difficulty, or esthetic concerns.

Are clear aligners an option?

Clear aligners may be an option for mild to moderate tooth movement when the teeth and bite fit the treatment goals. An exam is needed to determine whether aligners or braces are more appropriate.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment time varies based on the complexity of movement, the appliance used, patient cooperation, and biological response. Some limited cases are shorter, while comprehensive cases may take longer.

Is orthodontics only cosmetic?

No. Orthodontics can improve smile appearance, but it can also make teeth easier to clean, improve bite function, and reduce uneven wear in certain cases.

Do adults get orthodontic treatment?

Yes. Many adults choose orthodontics to improve crowding, spacing, bite concerns, or smile appearance, as long as the teeth and gums are healthy enough for movement.
