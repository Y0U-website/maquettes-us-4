What is All-on-4?

All-on-4 replaces a full upper or lower arch using four implants placed at angles that make the most of your available bone. A complete set of teeth is screwed to those implants rather than resting on your gums, so nothing slips and no adhesive is needed. The teeth are fixed for you, but your prosthodontist can remove them when needed for cleaning or repair.

How the timeline works

  • Hopeless teeth are removed and four implants are placed in one visit
  • A fixed temporary arch is attached the same day, so you are never without teeth
  • The implants integrate with your jawbone over about four months
  • Your permanent set is then made, tried in, and screwed on for good
  • Sedation is available to keep you comfortable throughout surgery

Consider All-on-4 if

  • You are tired of perpetual dental problems and want a longer-lasting solution
  • You want to enjoy the foods you love again
  • You want teeth that look and feel natural, without adhesives
  • You are missing all or most of your teeth on one or both arches
  • You want a fixed solution rather than a removable denture

How All-on-4 compares

A denture rests on your gums. An All-on-4 arch is anchored to implants in the jawbone.

Your All-on-4 journey, step by step

From your first appointment to your final permanent smile.

Consultation

We evaluate your bone density, remaining teeth, and goals, then give you a clear treatment plan with pricing. Sedation is prescribed as needed to keep you comfortable during the procedure.

Surgery day

Hopeless teeth are removed, four implants are placed into the jawbone, and a full temporary arch is fixed to those implants in the same visit, so you leave with a complete smile.

Four-month healing

The implants integrate with your jawbone over about four months. You wear your fixed temporary teeth throughout and attend follow-up appointments so we can monitor healing.

Permanent teeth

Your permanent set is fabricated, tried in for your approval, then screwed onto the implants. We evaluate and adjust the fit and bite until it is right.
