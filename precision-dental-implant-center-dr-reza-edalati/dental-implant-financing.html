How dental implant financing works

Dental implant financing lets you split the cost of care into easy monthly payments, so you do not have to wait or pay everything up front. We work with three trusted lenders, and each one covers dental implants.

Choose a plan

Pick the partner and plan that fit your budget. Our team can help you compare.

Apply in minutes

Start treatment

Get approved, start your care right away, and pay over time.

Yes, you can finance your dental implants. Whether you need one implant or a full arch, every partner covers it, including All-on-4 and full mouth restoration.

Three ways to pay over time

Each lender has its own strengths. Compare them below, then apply with the one that fits your treatment and budget.

CareCredit

Apply once and use it for your whole family, from a single implant to a complete smile makeover. Covers dental implants and All-on-4, crowns and bridges, veneers, dentures, root canals, teeth whitening, and routine care.

For implants: Great for a single implant or crown. You pay no interest when you pay within the promotional period.

  • Longer reduced rate plans up to 60 months for larger treatment
  • Pre-qualify with a soft credit check that does not affect your score

Cherry

Apply in about 30 seconds and most patients get an instant decision. Covers dental implants and full arch work, veneers, crowns, dentures, clear aligners, and more.

For implants: A strong choice for same day implant treatment, so you can begin without waiting.

  • Pay in 4 option that is always 0 % interest
  • Monthly plans up to 60 months with true 0 % APR for qualified borrowers
  • Financing up to $50,000 with no hidden fees and no deferred interest

LendingClub

Your payment stays the same each month, so your budget is easy to plan. Covers everything from a single tooth implant up to a full mouth of implants, including All-on-4 and full mouth restoration.

For implants: Fits full arch and full mouth cases best, since you can borrow more and choose a longer term.

  • Loans from $500 up to $65,000
  • Terms up to 144 months for comfortable monthly payments
  • True no interest plans for 6, 12, 18, or 24 months, plus extended reduced rate plans
  • No loan origination fee
