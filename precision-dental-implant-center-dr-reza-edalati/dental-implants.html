What are dental implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone to replace the root of a missing tooth. Once it integrates with the bone a process called osseointegration a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached on top. Implants are the only tooth replacement option that preserves jawbone and prevents bone loss, so they look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

Types of implants we offer

  • Single tooth implants replace one missing tooth with a natural-looking crown
  • Multiple tooth implants replace several teeth with implant-supported bridges
  • Full arch / All-on-4 replace a full upper or lower set with four to six implants
  • Implant-supported dentures a stable, permanent alternative to traditional dentures

Am I a candidate?

Patients with some bone loss can often still qualify after a bone graft, or by choosing All-on-4, which uses the denser bone at the front of the jaw. A 3D scan at your consultation determines which approach fits your case.

  • You are missing one or more teeth
  • You have a tooth that needs to be extracted
  • You wear loose or uncomfortable dentures
  • You want a permanent, natural-feeling tooth replacement
  • Your jawbone is fully developed and your gums are healthy

How implants compare

Bridges and dentures replace what you can see. An implant also replaces the root, which is what protects the bone underneath.

The dental implant process, step by step

Consultation

We take X-rays and a CBCT 3D scan of your jaw. Dr. Edalati evaluates your bone density and gum health, then builds a personalized treatment plan no pressure, no obligation.

Digital planning

Using 3D digital planning software, we design your new smile before any procedure begins, so you can preview the result and make decisions with confidence.

Implant placement

The titanium posts are surgically placed into your jawbone under local anesthesia. Sedation options are available for patients with dental anxiety.

Final restoration

Once the implant integrates with your bone, your custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached and your bite is perfected.
