What are snap-in dentures?

Snap-in dentures also called snap-on dentures or implant-retained overdentures are complete dentures that snap onto dental implants instead of resting on your gums. They stay secure all day, then come out for nightly cleaning. Every one is custom-designed by a prosthodontist for the best possible fit and appearance.

How they work

  • The denture clicks onto attachments fixed to your implants
  • Implants hold it in place, so it does not rest on your gums
  • You remove it yourself each night for cleaning
  • An existing well-fitting denture can sometimes be converted
  • Custom-designed by a specialist prosthodontist, not a lab template

Consider snap-in dentures if

  • You want a denture that will not move while eating or talking
  • You are unhappy with loose or ill-fitting traditional dentures
  • You want implant security with the convenience of a removable denture
  • You are missing all or most teeth on one or both arches
  • You want a cost-effective implant-supported solution

How snap-in dentures compare

A traditional denture rests on the gums and relies on suction or adhesive. A snap-in denture is anchored by implants.

Your snap-in denture journey, step by step

A carefully planned process from your first consultation to a confident, secure smile.

Implant placement

If you do not have dental implants yet, they are placed first and allowed to heal for several months. A well-fitting existing denture may sometimes be converted.

Impressions & design

A thorough impression appointment captures your muscle movements and gum tissue. A wax rim is tried in as a template, and you choose the shape, color, and size of your new teeth.

Wax try-in

The new teeth are tried in at a wax stage so you can see how they look and how you speak with them. After your approval, the denture is finalized in acrylic resin.

Snap in & go

Snaps are fitted to the implants and the denture. It clicks securely into place, and before you leave we make sure you can insert and remove it confidently on your own.
