How to Care for Your Dental Implants:

Caring for dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth:

  • Brush and floss daily
  • Avoid biting down on hard objects like ice or pencils
  • Schedule regular check-ups at Prosper Dental Studio to ensure the health and longevity of your implants Proper care helps maintain the health and appearance of your implant restoration, contributing to its long-lasting success.
