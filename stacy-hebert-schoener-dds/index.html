With Dr. Stacy, you never have to worry about getting what you and your loved ones need. She can provide proactive preventive dentistry for children and adults as well as more advanced services to restore and uplift your smile. Every piece of technology we have will make your life easier. We can design and place custom-dental crowns in a single visit to cut down on appointments, take putty-free dental impressions, use lasers to scan for cavities without poking your teeth, and much more.

Our practice is in-network with most PPO dental insurance providers, meaning you can rely on us to accept your plan, process the claim quickly, and help you get the most out of your benefits.

With our in-house membership, you can spend less compared to insurance while enjoying a wider variety of benefits, including a 30 % discount on services typically not covered by regular plans.
