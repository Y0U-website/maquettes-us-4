Braces

Braces are an exceptionally effective orthodontic treatment, designed to straighten teeth, correct bite issues, and enhance overall dental health. Ideal for patients of all ages, these devices transform smiles by gradually and precisely aligning teeth into their optimal positions, resulting in a beautiful, healthy smile. If you’re needing braces in the Prosper, Aubrey, Frisco, Celina, Little Elm, or nearby areas, we’ve got a few options for you!

Traditional braces use refined metal brackets and wires to gradually and precisely straighten teeth, while correcting various bite issues. With the latest advancements in orthodontic technology, modern traditional braces are now more comfortable and discreet than ever before, providing an efficient and sophisticated path to a beautifully aligned smile.

Clear braces, also known as ceramic braces, offer a sophisticated alternative to traditional metal braces by utilizing tooth-colored or clear brackets. This aesthetic enhancement makes them a highly desirable choice for those seeking a more discreet and elegant orthodontic treatment option.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners present an exceptionally discreet and convenient solution for achieving a flawlessly aligned smile. Made from smooth, transparent plastic, these aligners gently and precisely shift teeth into their desired positions, replacing the need for metal brackets or wires.

Patients enjoy the freedom of removing aligners for dining, oral hygiene, and special occasions, making them an appealing choice for those wanting a flexible and simple method to enhance their smile. With clear aligners, you can live life with a straighter, beautiful smile. If you’re looking for clear aligners, in Prosper, Aubrey, Frisco, Celina, Little Elm, or a surrounding area we’ve got you covered!

Habit Appliances

Habit correction appliances help break detrimental oral habits like thumb sucking and tongue thrusting, which can lead to dental issues such as misaligned teeth and improper bites. These appliances gently discourage these behaviors, promoting healthier oral growth.

Dr. Bellan will help you find the right habit correction appliance designed to meet your child’s individual needs. Custom-made for comfort and effectiveness, these appliances play a crucial role in early orthodontic intervention, guiding proper dental growth and development.

Airway Focused Orthodontics

At Truly Yours Orthodontics, we believe that great orthodontic care goes beyond straight teeth, it includes supporting proper breathing and overall health. Airway orthodontics focuses on identifying and treating issues that affect how you or your child breath, especially during sleep. As part of every consultation, we take a 3D X-ray to evaluate the airway and assess jaw structure. Conditions like narrow arches, mouth breathing, and jaw misalignment can contribute to snoring, sleep-disordered breathing, and even daytime fatigue or behavioral concerns.

Treatment options are personalized and may include palatal expansion to improve airflow, jaw growth modification for children, custom sleep appliances for adults, or surgical collaboration for more complex cases. We often work alongside ENTs and myofunctional therapists to ensure a complete approach to care. By addressing airway issues early and effectively, we help our patients breathe better, sleep better, and feel better because your overall health is truly our priority.

Dentofacial Orthopedics

Dentofacial orthopedics focuses on jaw and facial growth for balanced appearance and function.

Using advanced diagnostics, Dr. Bellan creates custom treatment plans with orthopedic appliances or surgery for complex cases.

This approach not only enhances aesthetics but also improves bite function, airway, speech clarity, and reduces TMJ disorder risks, significantly improving oral health and quality of life.

Retainers

Retainers are essential in maintaining the results following braces or clear aligners. Following the active phase of orthodontic treatment, when teeth have been aligned, retainers ensure the stability of your newly perfected smile by preventing any regression to their original positions.

These appliances are typically worn for a designated period, as directed by Dr. Bellan. Available in a variety of types, including removable and fixed options, retainers offer a tailored solution to suit individual preferences and treatment requirements, ensuring optimal dental health and smile maintenance.

Technology

Utilizing cutting-edge technology, our practice ensures precision, efficiency, and comfort throughout every step of your journey.

Elevating precision and comfort to unparalleled heights, our cutting-edge digital scanner captures detailed 3D images of the patient’s teeth and gums in minutes, eliminating the discomfort and mess typically associated with traditional impressions. Instantly displayed on a computer, these images allow Dr. Bellan to tailor treatment strategies with accuracy, resulting in expedited turnaround times and impeccably accurate outcomes.

Our digital X-rays capture high-resolution images of the teeth, jaws, and surrounding structures with unmatched clarity and precision. Unlike conventional X-rays, these digital X-rays can be viewed immediately on a computer, expediting treatment planning. We utilize CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography) scans, which provide 3D images for a detailed view of dental structures and airway evaluation. This revolutionary technology underscores our commitment to patient safety, using lower radiation levels compared to standard film-based techniques.
