Dr. Manisha Singh, DMD

Meet Dr. Singh

Dr. Manisha Singh is a dedicated family and general dentist at A&M Dental Arts Somerset in Somerset, New Jersey. With a rich background in dentistry, Dr. Singh has been serving the community with compassion and expertise since opening her own practice in February 2020. Prior to this, she practiced in Bridgewater, NJ, beginning in 2016. During the COVID-19 shutdown, Dr. Singh distinguished herself by welcoming and treating emergency patients , providing critical support during challenging times.

Born in Chandigarh, India, Dr. Singh completed her Bachelor of Dental Surgery before moving to the United States. She launched her dental career in 2010, successfully managing her own practice in India for several years. Following her passion for dentistry, she earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine from the prestigious Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine. Dr. Singh initially practiced in Philadelphia before settling in New Jersey.

Certified in Invisalign and proficient in general dentistry, Dr. Singh is dedicated to delivering exceptional care to enhance her patients’ smiles. She is an active member of the American Dental Association, New Jersey Dental Association, and Central Jersey Dental Association. Her enthusiasm for her profession is evident in her patient interactions and her commitment to continuous learning.

Dr. Singh goes above and beyond the standard requirements, completing well over forty hours of continuing education annually. She regularly participates in advanced courses in both dentistry and practice management to stay abreast of the latest technologies and techniques, ensuring her patients receive the most comfortable and effective treatments. Her dedication extends to being available 24/7 for emergencies, emphasizing her commitment to patient care.

Under Dr. Singh’s leadership, A&M Dental Arts is devoted to providing state-of-the-art, relationship-based, comprehensive care tailored to the unique needs and budgets of each patient. What she loves most about dentistry is the opportunity to improve her patients’ quality of life, ensuring they leave her practice with a confident smile.

Residing in Manalapan, New Jersey, with her husband and two children, Dr. Singh enjoys traveling with her family and continuously enhancing her skills through new courses in dental surgery and management.
