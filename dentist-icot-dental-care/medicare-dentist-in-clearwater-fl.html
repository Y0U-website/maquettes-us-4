Each plan differs, so it’s important to review your specific plan’s dental coverage.

At ICOT Dental Care, we’ll help you review your plan to see what’s covered and provide affordable financing options if needed.

  • What dental services are covered by Medicare Advantage plans? Medicare Advantage (Part C) plans often include dental benefits, depending on the plan. These may cover: Routine exams and cleanings
  • X-rays
  • Fillings
  • Crowns, bridges, and dentures
  • Periodontal (gum) care
  • How do I know if my Medicare plan covers treatment? Check your Medicare Advantage plan documentation or call the number on your plan’s member ID card. You can also contact Icot Dental Care directly we accept Medicare and can help verify your coverage and explain what treatments are included.
  • Will Medicare cover dentures or other dental prosthetics? Original Medicare does not cover dentures or prosthetics. However, many Medicare Advantage plans do include partial or full coverage for: Dentures
  • Bridges
  • Implant-supported prosthetics
  • How often does Medicare cover cleanings and dental check-ups? Only Medicare Advantage plans offer routine dental cleanings and check-ups often twice per year, but this depends on the plan. Original Medicare (Part A & B) does not cover preventive dental visits.
