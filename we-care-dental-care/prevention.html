What to Expect During Your Child&#x27;s First Dentist Visit

What to expect during an initial visit depends on the age of your child. You should bring your child to the dentist in Roanoke for the first time at the age of 1, or when their first tooth has erupted. For infants and toddlers between ages 1-3, Dr. Burkitt will perform a basic oral exam to help you understand your child’s oral health and what steps should be taken to keep their smile strong. Children over the age of 3 will usually have their teeth cleaned and polished. X-rays are usually taken at the age of 5, though Dr. Burkitt my suggest them earlier if necessary.

Dental Sealants For Cavity Protection

Dental sealants consist of a special type of resin, which is applied directly to your child’s molars and rear teeth after their mouth has been cleaned. This resin hardens into a protective shield that keeps bacteria, acids, and sugar from direct contact with the teeth. In turn, this prevents the formation of cavities. Dental sealants can last up to a decade, giving you and your family peace of mind and protection from tooth decay for years.
