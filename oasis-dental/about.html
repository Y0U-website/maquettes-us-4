Meet the Oasis Dental Team

Each member of our team is entirely dedicated to providing the best patient care and customer service. Their loyalty, friendliness, efficiency, and professionalism are unmatched. Our dental assistants make our back office run smoothly, making sure patients have everything they need to be comfortable; our hygienists are thorough and have over 30 years of combined experience; our front office team is knowledgeable and excellent at navigating insurance and financing.
