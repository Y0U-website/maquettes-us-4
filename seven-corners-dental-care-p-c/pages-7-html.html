Digital X-Rays & Intra-oral Camera

Our office utilizes a Dexis digital radiography unit. These computerized X-ray imaging systems use electronic sensors in lieu of X-ray film. This technology provides high-quality images almost immediately, allowing fast and comprehensive diagnosis of dental health problems. Digital X-ray’s are also significantly safer for patients as 90 % less radiation is used when compared to conventional film X-ray technology. Our dentists also use an intra-oral camera; this technology gives a close-up and in-depth view allowing small problems to be spotted and treated before they become more serious.
