Signs You May Need Multiple-Tooth Implant-Supported Bridges

Adjacent Gaps You are missing multiple adjacent teeth in a row rather than one single isolated gap sitting somewhere in the middle of your smile line today.

Adequate Bone You possess adequate bone density in the jaw for implant placement, or else a bone graft can build up that foundation for you well beforehand.

Good Health Your overall oral health is good, with healthy gum tissue and with no untreated tooth decay anywhere in the areas surrounding that open space.

Fixed Solution You want a long-term and fixed tooth replacement rather than a partial denture that has to come back out of your mouth again every single night.

How Gardena Dental Group Is Changing Lives

Up to four teeth in a row can typically be restored using only two dental implants. Our dentist confirms the exact configuration after reviewing your imaging.

A traditional bridge anchors to the natural teeth on either side, which means reshaping them. An implant bridge anchors to implants instead, leaving your healthy teeth untouched.

The implants need several months to integrate with the bone before the bridge is attached. Our dentist provides a timeline specific to your case at the consultation.
