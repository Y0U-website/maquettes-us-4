Signs You May Need All-on-4 Dental Implants

Missing Teeth You have lost most or all of your own teeth and want a permanent full-arch replacement rather than a removable appliance of any kind at all.

Failing Teeth Your remaining teeth are failing from decay or gum disease and will need removing first before any lasting restoration can happen for you at all.

Denture Frustration Traditional dentures slip, shift, and make eating difficult, and you are ready for something that stays firmly in place all day long instead.

Limited Bone Bone density in your jaw is fairly limited, and angled implant placement can work around that without any extensive bone grafting in most cases.
