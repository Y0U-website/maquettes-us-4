Signs You May Need Implant-Supported Dentures

Loose Dentures Your current dentures slip or shift while you talk and eat, and adhesives are no longer solving that problem for you on a day-to-day basis.

Sore Spots Sore spots and constant irritation from a removable denture have made wearing it uncomfortable throughout the entire day at home and at work.

Chewing Difficulty Eating has become difficult enough that you now avoid certain foods entirely rather than risk trouble with your current removable appliance.

Bone Loss Bone loss from your missing teeth has begun changing your facial shape, and dental implants can help slow that whole process down considerably.
