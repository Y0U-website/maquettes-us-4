Signs You May Need Single-Tooth Implant

Missing Tooth You have one missing tooth and want a permanent replacement that will not affect any of the healthy teeth standing on either side of the space.

Adequate Bone You have adequate bone density in your jaw to support an implant, or a bone graft can build up that foundation before the implant ever goes in.

Good Health Your overall oral health is good, and you either avoid smoking altogether or are willing to quit for as long as the implant needs in order to heal.

Long-Term Solution You have a fully developed jawbone and want a long-term tooth replacement rather than a removable appliance that has to come out each night.
