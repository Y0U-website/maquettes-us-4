Are interceptive orthodontics covered by dental insurance?

The only way to know whether your dental insurance will cover the cost of interceptive orthodontics for your child is to review your plan. Each one is different, but if you need help, you can always ask a member of our team to work on your behalf to navigate the details of your plan and determine how much you can save on out-of-pocket costs.

If coverage is available, they may offer to pay for up to 50 % of the total cost, but this range can vary. If you need help paying any of the remaining costs, you can apply for CareCredit. This third-party financier offers payment plans with low or no interest attached. Instead of paying the full balance upfront, you pay it off over time.

What will happen after my child completes interceptive orthodontic treatment?

Once your child completes interceptive orthodontic treatment, they may require regular monitoring by our dental team to ensure their teeth and jaw continue to develop and grow properly. If additional orthodontic treatment is needed, we will discuss the next phase, which will likely include full braces. This will ensure that their teeth move into proper alignment for a straighter, healthier, fully functional smile.
