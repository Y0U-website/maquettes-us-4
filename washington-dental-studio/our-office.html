Our Mission

When you’re at Washington Dental Studio, you’re family. Cultivating a close-knit community is what we’re all about because Naperville is our home. Whether it’s building lasting friendships with patients, investing in advanced technology, or uplifting our neighbors through active community outreach, it’s our mission to champion flourishing oral health for our vibrant city of Naperville with dental excellence.

Before your appointment, feel free to make yourself at home and enjoy a complimentary refreshment served by our team! With a fresh cup of coffee or tea in hand, our relaxing patient lounge will feel like visiting a local cafe.

As a local practice on the leading edge of care, we use the latest dental 3D printers to provide our patient family with the most customized, efficient, and comfortable mouthguards, whitening trays, aligners, and more!

Our warm and welcoming office features an array of modern comforts for the whole family to feel at home. From noise-cancelling headphones to our iPad bar, Washington Dental Studio has everything you need for a soothing stay.

Our office features intraoral scanners that seamlessly capture thousands of detailed images of the inside of your smile within seconds, giving you and Dr. Shah an accurate 3D view of your mouth, teeth, and gums.

Optimized with your relaxation in mind, every treatment room in our office is equipped with mounted TVs and Netflix streaming, so you can catch up on your favorite movies and shows while we care for your grin with top-notch treatment!

Recognized as the future of x-rays, cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) helps our team to safely, efficiently, and accurately examine 3D images of your facial bones, oral tissues, teeth, and gums, in just one easy scan.

Say goodbye to noisy treatments and hello to seamless, virtually silent dental care. With less noise and more comfort, electric handpieces allow Dr. Shah to provide the smoothest, most relaxing treatments for your smile.

Whether you have dental anxiety or you’re feeling nervous about an upcoming appointment, Dr. Shah is here to help ease your fears, provide peace of mind, and support a calming stay with minimal inhaled nitrous sedation.
