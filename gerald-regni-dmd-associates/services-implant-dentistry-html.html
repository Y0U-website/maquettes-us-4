Implant Dentistry

A dental implant replaces the root itself, keeping the jawbone active and the neighbouring teeth untouched.

From bone preparation to the final restoration, every step is planned with 3D imaging around your anatomy, and you choose the material, titanium or metal-free, that your body will tolerate best.

How implants are utilized

The same implant foundation can replace a single tooth or an entire arch. How we use it depends on how many teeth are missing, and where.

Design and elements of implant utilization

A dental implant is really three parts working together, placed and assembled in a careful sequence.

Signature treatments

Bone Regeneration & Sinus Lifts

When there isn’t enough bone to anchor an implant, we rebuild the foundation first, often supported by your own platelet-rich plasma to speed healing. A sinus lift creates room in the upper jaw where the sinus sits close to the ridge.

Titanium Implants

A well-established, durable implant option with the longest track record, suitable for a wide range of cases. The post fuses with your jawbone (osseointegration) to form a stable base for a natural-looking crown.

Ceramic Zirconium Implants

A metal-free implant for patients who prefer to avoid metal entirely or whose history suggests sensitivity. Zirconia is strong, biocompatible, and tooth-coloured, with no dark line at the gumline.
