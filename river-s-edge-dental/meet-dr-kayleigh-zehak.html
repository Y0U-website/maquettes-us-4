(704) 230 1470 image Contact Us A Passion for Excellence in Patient-Centered Dentistry Dr. Kayleigh Zehak is a compassionate and dedicated dentist committed to delivering exceptional, patient-centered care. She is passionate about helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles and provides a wide range of services, including cosmetic dentistry, implant restorations, tooth extractions, and dentures. With a focus on individualized treatment and attention to detail, Dr. Zehak strives to create a comfortable experience while achieving outstanding results for every patient.

Outside of the office, Dr. Zehak enjoys spending quality time with her husband, son, and pets, including her dogs and birds. She also has a love for the outdoors and enjoys exploring new destinations through travel. Whether she is caring for patients, adventuring with her family, or discovering new places, Dr. Zehak brings the same enthusiasm, compassion, and commitment to excellence that define her approach to dentistry.
