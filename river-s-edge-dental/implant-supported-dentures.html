We perform a comprehensive evaluation and take imaging to plan implant placement.

Small titanium posts are placed into the jawbone where they integrate to form a stable foundation.

The implants fuse with the bone to create long term stability.

Your custom denture is designed, crafted, and secured to the implants for a natural, comfortable fit.
