Meet Dr. Ann Marie Moshos

(704) 230 1470 image Contact Us A Leader in Modern, Patient Centered Dentistry Dr. Ann Marie Moshos brings a rare combination of advanced training, clinical precision, and genuine compassion to River’s Edge Dental. Her approach to dentistry is guided by a belief that exceptional outcomes begin with trust, clear communication, and a commitment to the well being of every patient. She provides care that is thorough, gentle, and grounded in extensive experience.

Patients who choose Dr. Moshos consistently share that they appreciate her calm presence, her attention to detail, and the comfort they feel under her care. She treats each patient as an individual, taking time to understand their concerns, goals, and expectations to create tailored treatment plans that deliver long lasting, natural looking results.

Dr. Moshos graduated summa cum laude with a degree in Biology before earning her Doctor of Dental Medicine from Nova Southeastern University. She graduated second in her class, a reflection of her dedication, discipline, and excellence. Her academic achievements demonstrate her ability to master complex material, think critically, and perform with precision.

Her high level education prepared her for every aspect of modern general dentistry, from preventive care to advanced restorative treatment.

Service and Advanced Training in the United States Air Force

Dr. Moshos began her career serving as a dentist in the United States Air Force. During her service, she completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at Langley Air Force Base. This competitive program provided intensive training in complex procedures, surgical techniques, treatment planning, and emergency care.

Her service also earned her multiple Officer of the Quarter awards, highlighting her leadership, professionalism, and dedication to excellence.

Dentistry is always evolving, and Dr. Moshos has continued to expand her skills through ongoing advanced education. In 2018, she completed the MaxI Implantology course, gaining specialized training in implant placement and restoration. She remains active in continuing education, staying current with the latest techniques and technologies in modern dentistry.

Her commitment to growth ensures that patients receive the highest standard of care using evidence based methods.

A Compassionate, Patient First Approach

Life Beyond Dentistry

Outside the office, Dr. Moshos enjoys an active and fulfilling life with her family. She has visited 37 countries and values travel for the perspective and connection it brings. She enjoys snowboarding, hiking, and wake surfing with her husband and daughters.

Her sense of adventure, curiosity, and balance enhances her ability to connect with patients on a personal level and brings joy and vitality to her work.
