The practice

Dr. Charng is honored to follow in the footsteps of Craig Potter, DDS, who served Arcadia and the surrounding communities for 40 years. He takes immense pride in serving the community and offering high-quality dental care to anyone who sets foot in his office.

After graduating from Arcadia High School, he earned a Bachelor of Science (BS) degree in neuroscience at UCLA, and then his Doctorate of Dental Surgery (DDS) from New York University College of Dentistry.

In his practice, Dr. Charng performs tooth-saving treatments such as root canals, as well as a variety of appearance enhancements like placing dental implants, porcelain veneers, and professional in-office teeth whitenings.

Dr. Charng is a proud member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, and San Gabriel Valley Dental Society.

In his free time, Dr. Charng enjoys hiking with his family and playing basketball with his friends. He also delights in trying new restaurants and catching up on the news.
