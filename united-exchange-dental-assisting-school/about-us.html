(402) 506-7058 About United Exchange Dental Assisting School in La Vista, NE Where Dental Education Meets Real-World Career Preparation When you’re ready to start a rewarding career in dental assisting, you need training that prepares you for real clinical settings from day one. United Exchange Dental Assisting School provides hands-on education designed to give you the skills, confidence, and experience that dental practices across La Vista, Omaha, Council Bluffs, Gretna, Papillion, Ralston, Benson, and Elk Horn are actively seeking.

Our approach is built on practical learning, personalized instruction, and genuine support for every student who walks through our doors.

We believe in reliability, honesty, and putting your success first because when you succeed, our community thrives.

Established to Serve Students Across La Vista and the Greater Omaha Area

We founded our school with a clear purpose: to offer aspiring dental assistants a straightforward path to a fulfilling career without unnecessary barriers or delays. Every course we teach reflects the real demands of modern dental offices, from patient care and radiography to infection control and chairside procedures.

Our location in La Vista puts you at the center of a vibrant metro area where dental practices are growing and hiring. You’ll train in a region known for its welcoming neighborhoods, strong work ethic, and commitment to quality healthcare values that shape everything we do.

We’re proud to support students and dental professionals throughout the community, helping local practices find skilled, job-ready team members who are prepared to contribute from their first day.

Learn more about our courses and see how we prepare you for success.

Guided by Experience and Committed to Your Growth

Our experienced instructors know what it takes to succeed in a busy dental office, and they bring that knowledge into every lesson. We treat each student as an individual, offering the guidance and encouragement you need to build both competence and confidence in your new career.
