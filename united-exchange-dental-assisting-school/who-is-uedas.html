Our Mission

We will empower our students to reach their full potential through our uniquely designed, evidence-based educational program, working with them to provide hands-on experience so they can develop the skills and confidence needed to be leaders in the dental community.

UEDAS Vision

We are driven by our passion for education, our hunger for education and our commitment to excellence. We aim to foster a community for students and dental assistants that encourages them to develop the skills, confidence and knowledge to become the best in their field.

Our Core Values

At the center of United Exchange Dental Assisting School are our core values Service, Team Player and Innovation.

Program Objective

Our objective is to thoroughly and efficiently train and prepare our students in our dental assisting program for a position as a dental assistant in a general dental office. We are committed to helping our students continue to grow professionally after achieving their certification so they can enjoy a fulfilling and successful career.

For more information about our dental assisting program in La Vista, Nebraska, or to learn how you can enroll in private secondary schooling, please contact our office at 402-506-7058.
