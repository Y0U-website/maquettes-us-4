What are dental implants?

Dental implants, as described by our dental team, are small titanium posts surgically placed in the jawbone to act as artificial tooth roots. After placement, a natural-looking crown is attached to the implant to replace your missing tooth. Dental implants restore both the functionality and aesthetic appeal of your smile.

Are dental implants considered safe?

Absolutely! Dental implants have been used for decades and are considered a safe and reliable treatment for replacing missing teeth. When the treatment is performed by an experienced dental professional, such as those at our office, the procedure has a high success rate and long-term benefits.

What are the benefits of quality teeth implants?

The benefits of teeth implants are numerous. They look, feel, and function like natural teeth, allowing you to chew and speak comfortably. They also preserve your jawbone, preventing the bone loss that often occurs with missing teeth. Additionally, they provide a permanent and durable solution that, with proper care, often lasts most patients a lifetime.

Dental Implants – Remarkable Patient Results

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Most of our adult patients who have good oral and overall health are the best candidates for dental implants. If your jawbone is healthy and you are free from gum disease, you are likely eligible for the procedure. A consultation with our skilled team can determine your suitability.

Is the procedure painful?

While the idea of oral surgery may seem intimidating, many patients report minimal discomfort during dental implant placement. Your dentist uses local anesthesia to ensure you are comfortable throughout the procedure. Afterward, mild soreness can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.

How long do dental implants last?

One of the most remarkable aspects of getting dental implants is their longevity. With both proper oral hygiene and routine dental check-ups, most quality dental implants often last decades or even a lifetime, making them a long-term investment in your oral health.

How do I care for my new dental implants?

Caring for your new dental implants is actually quite simple and straightforward. You must brush and floss daily, just as you would with natural teeth. Routine visits to our office will help ensure your implants remain in excellent condition.
