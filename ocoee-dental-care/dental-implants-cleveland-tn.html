Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants are considered the best option for replacing missing teeth due to their many advantages over other options. One of the biggest advantages is the preservation of the jawbone. Dental implants, unlike dentures and bridges, which sit on top of the gums, are fixed directly into the jawbone. They mimic the roots of natural teeth. This integration stimulates the jawbone and prevents bone loss, which is common with missing teeth.

Durability and longevity are two other key benefits of dental implants. They are a long-lasting solution that can ultimately be cost-effective. The implants are incredibly stable, so you can eat and speak without fear of your teeth shifting.

Dental implants also have a natural appearance and feel. Custom-made bridges and crowns are designed to match your existing teeth to ensure your smile appears as natural as possible. This is a major advantage for patients who want to regain their confidence after tooth loss.

The Dental Implant Process At Ocoee Dental Care

Understanding the process of dental implants can ease any fears you may have. We at Ocoee Dental Care prioritize patient comfort, satisfaction, and care, guiding you through each step with transparency and care.

The first step is a consultation with Dr. An Ta, who will evaluate your oral health to determine if you are a good candidate for dental implants. If you are a suitable candidate, the next step is to have the implant surgically placed. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia to ensure that you remain comfortable. The implant is carefully placed into the jawbone, where it needs time to integrate and heal. This period of healing, also known as osseointegration, takes about five months.

After the implant has integrated fully with your jawbone the next step will be to attach the abutment. This acts as a connection between the implant, and the final restoration. The abutment will be attached to the custom-made denture, bridge, or crown after the abutment has been placed. The result is an entirely functional tooth with a natural appearance that blends seamlessly into your existing teeth.
