Education

Bachelor of Science at San Diego State University, CA. Major in Microbiology, Minor in Business

Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) at Case Western in Cleveland, OH

Dr. Ta spent an additional year doing a General Practice Residency at St. Vincent Hospital in Cleveland, OH. He spent half his time doing rotations in various departments including emergency, anesthesiology, and addiction services. In addition to learning about the human body, Dr. Ta fine tuned his skills in oral surgery doing implants, lateral sinus lifts, and impacted wisdom teeth extractions. Dr. Ta also worked closely with an Endodontist.

Dr. Ta received his certificate in life coaching at IAP Career College. He has a passion for helping others find their purpose in life.
