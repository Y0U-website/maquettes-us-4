Dr. Parker Long

Dr. Long is a member of several professional dental organizations, including:

As a dentist, Dr. Long is committed to making his patients feel like they are at home from the moment they walk in the door. During an appointment with him, patients can expect to receive undivided attention from him and the office team to provide them with the best possible dental care.

Dr. Long grew up in Maurice, LA, just outside of Lafayette. He moved to Sugar Land, TX, in 2001 and has been in Texas ever since. Dr. Long and his wife, Taylor, have two children, Timothy and Virginia. Outside of the office, Dr. Long enjoys cooking, hunting, yard work and being outdoors. In the fall he attends every Texas A&M home football game, and enjoys college football in general.
