Stephanie

Stephanie is a Registered Dental Hygienist that graduated in 2019 and also, has her bachelors in biology. Her focus is making sure everyone feels comfortable and leaves their appointment feeling confident. She enjoys reading, being in the sun and spending time with her husband and daughter.

Karen Lowe

Karen is a Registered Dental Assistant and has been with the practice for 30 years acting as the Office Manager. She is known for treating everyone with kindness and strives to make the patients feel special, loved and comfortable during their visit. Karen loves fishing, going to the beach and spending time with her husband and family.

Kara Vance

Kara is a Registered Dental Assistant who works in the front office, as well. She has been in the dental field for 8 years and is known for her positive attitude and kindness. She strives to treat every patient with respect and grace. She enjoys volunteering with her church, the outdoors, traveling and spending time with her husband and two daughters.

Dr. Parker Long

Dr. Long is an Aggie that graduated from the University of Texas for Dentistry in 2014. He is a member of the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry and Brazos Valley Dental Society. Dr. Long focuses on providing a family like atmosphere while giving exemplary care to his patients. He is an avid A&M sports fan, loves hunting and cooking, as well as spending time with his wife and three children.

She likes being outdoors, enjoys her chicken coop and spending time with her husband, kids and grandkids.

Daniella
