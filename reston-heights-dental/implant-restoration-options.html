Implant Restoration Options

  • Implant-Supported Bridge Restores 2–4 teeth in a row using fewer implants than “one per tooth.” Great for: adjacent gaps.
  • Implant-Supported Denture Choose fixed (non-removable “hybrid”) or snap-on for added security. Great for: full upper or lower arch replacement.
  • All-on-X Full Arch A stable full arch on 4–6 implants with fewer surgeries. Great for: a streamlined, same-day full-arch solution.
  • Immediate-Load Temporaries In select cases, leave with a temporary tooth the same day. Great for: restoring appearance during healing.

Technology We Use for Planning & Precision

  • Cone Beam CT (CBCT) — 3D imaging for accurate implant placement
  • Digital Impressions — Mess-free, precise fit
  • Intraoral Cameras — See what we see to co-plan your care
  • CEREC Same-Day Crowns — Available for select implant restorations
  • 3D Printing — Surgical guides and prototype design
  • T-Scan Digital Bite — Balances your bite for long-term success

Am I a Candidate?

You may be a great candidate if you have missing or failing teeth, want a fixed solution, and have sufficient bone (or are open to grafting). Dr. Karbasi will review your health history, scan your jaw with CBCT, and discuss your goals to recommend the right path.
