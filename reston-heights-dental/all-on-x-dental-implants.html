How the Process Works

Consultation & Digital Planning: We take a 3D Cone Beam CT and digital impressions to map bone and design your new smile.

Guided Implant Placement: Four to six implants are placed at precise angles for maximum support; sedation is available for comfort.

Provisional Teeth: Many patients receive a temporary fixed set the same day so you don’t go without a smile.

Final Restoration: After healing (about 3–6 months), we deliver a custom hybrid denture or zirconia bridge for lasting strength and beauty.

Technology We Use

  • Cone Beam CT for 3D surgical planning
  • iTero digital impressions for fast, accurate scans
  • 3D printing / CEREC for custom provisional and final prosthetics
  • T-Scan occlusal analysis for balanced bite
  • Soft-tissue lasers for gentle healing
  • Electric handpieces & piezo surgery tools to reduce trauma
