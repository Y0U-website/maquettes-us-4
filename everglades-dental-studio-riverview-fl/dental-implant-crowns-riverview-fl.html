What Are Dental Implant Crowns?

A dental implant crown is a permanent restoration that replaces one missing tooth. It has the titanium implant placed into the jawbone, combined with a custom crown, which looks and feels like a natural tooth. The function of the implant is to represent the root of the tooth, while the crown is an imitation of the lost tooth in appearance and function. If you’re looking to restore your smile, consider getting a dental implant crowns for a natural and long-lasting solution.

Dental implant crowns are durable, long-lasting, and can improve both the function and appearance of your smile.

Benefits of Dental Implant Crowns

  • Long-Lasting and Durable: The implant crowns will last as long as you maintain them.
  • Natural Appearance: The crowns are designed to blend with the colour, shape, and size of your natural teeth.
  • Improved Function: Dental implant crowns in Florida can deliver adequate chewing and speaking function.
  • Maintains Jawbone Health: It maintains jawbone health by keeping the bone structure intact and avoiding bone loss that occurs after tooth loss.
  • No Harm to Nearby Teeth: Dental implants do not involve the alteration of nearby teeth, unlike other bridges.
