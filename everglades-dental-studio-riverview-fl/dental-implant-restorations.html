All-On-4 Implant Restorations

All-on-4 implant restorations are a great option for patients who need full-arch tooth replacement. This method uses four strategically placed by a professional dentist in Riverview, FL , to support a full set of teeth, offering a secure and long-lasting solution.

Benefits of All-On-4:

  • Requires only four implants per arch
  • Restores full functionality and attractiveness.
  • No need for detachable dentures.
  • Faster healing than typical implants.
  • Provides powerful biting force.

All-On-X Implant Restorations

Why Choose All-On-X?
