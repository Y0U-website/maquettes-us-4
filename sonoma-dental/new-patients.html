Our Approach to Care

At Sonoma Dental, we practice evidence-based dentistry focused on long-term health and stability. That means recommending care thoughtfully, prioritizing prevention, and helping patients understand why a treatment may or may not be needed.

You can always expect honesty, transparency, and respect for your comfort and preferences.

Insurance & Payment

We believe patients should understand their financial options clearly. Our team will help you navigate insurance benefits and discuss fees upfront so there are no surprises.

We’re happy to answer questions and help you make informed decisions about your care.

A Calm, Comfortable Experience

We understand that dental visits can be stressful. Our office is designed to feel calm and welcoming, and we take a gentle, unrushed approach to care.

If you have dental anxiety or past experiences you’d like us to know about, please let us know. We’re here to help.
