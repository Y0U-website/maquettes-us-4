Our Dental Bridge Process

  • Consultation & Planning We assess your oral health and discuss the best bridge options for your unique needs.
  • Preparation of Supporting Teeth The anchor teeth (abutments) are gently reshaped and prepared for crowns.
  • Impressions & Custom Design Precise impressions are taken to create a bridge that fits and looks natural.
  • Bridge Placement Your custom bridge is securely fitted and adjusted for comfort and function.
