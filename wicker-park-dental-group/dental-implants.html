Types of Dental Implants We Provide

Every patient is unique, so we take a personalized approach to dental implant treatments. That’s why we offer several ways to rebuild your smile, no matter how many teeth you’re missing.

Our practice delivers versatile, customized tooth replacement options tailored to your specific clinical needs and cosmetic preferences:

Single Tooth Implant

It consists of a biocompatible post placed directly into the jawbone, capped with a custom-crafted porcelain crown via an abutment. It replaces missing teeth seamlessly without compromising or filing down adjacent healthy teeth.

Implant-Supported Bridge

An implant-supported bridge is the ideal choice for replacing several consecutive missing teeth. Anchored securely by dental implants rather than natural teeth, it offers unmatched stability and prevents remaining teeth from shifting out of alignment.

Snap-In Implant Dentures (Overdenture)

Combining the affordability of traditional prosthetics with the stability of modern implantology, an implant-supported denture/overdenture snaps securely onto strategically placed implants. Say goodbye to messy adhesives, slipping plates, and sore spots.

Full Mouth Implants (All-on-4)

For complete arch restoration, All-on-4 / full-mouth dental implants utilize four to six strategically angled implants to support a full set of permanent fixed teeth. In select cases, immediate/same-day implants or mini dental implants allow you to leave our office with a brand-new, functional smile in just one visit.

Dental Implant Procedure

  • Consultation & Imaging: We begin with a thorough exam and detailed imaging to evaluate your jawbone, gum health, and the placement of surrounding teeth and nerves.
  • Preparatory Care (if needed): Some patients need bone grafting or a sinus lift to build up the jaw before an implant can be placed securely. We’ll walk you through whether this step applies to you.
  • Implant Placement: Using sedation dentistry for a relaxed, comfortable experience, we surgically place your implant post into the jawbone, where it will begin to fuse with the bone through a natural process called osseointegration.
  • Healing Period: Over the following weeks to months, your implant integrates with your jawbone, forming a stable foundation for your new tooth. Depending on your case, you may qualify for immediate/same-day implants with temporary teeth placed the same day as surgery.
  • Abutment & Final Restoration: Once healing is complete, we attach a small connector piece, known as an abutment, and place your custom-crafted final crown, bridge, or denture designed in-house for a precise, natural fit.
