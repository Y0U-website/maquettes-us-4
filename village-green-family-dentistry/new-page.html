About the Office

ABOUT THE PRACTICE

We are a new full-service dental practice conveniently located at the Village Green plaza on Route 111 in Windham, New Hampshire. Our practice may be new, but be assured that Dr. Levine brings over a decade of experience in both private practice and public dental care to her practice

Village Green Family Dentistry offers a full complement of services, including family dentistry, cosmetic dentistry and a comprehensive range of preventive care and dental treatments, all in a comfortable atmosphere. Dr. Levine and her team of dental professionals provide highly personalized care to families and individuals of all ages, including those with special needs. They also attend on-going training so they can continue to offer the most up-to-date procedures and services to their patients.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Village Green Family Dentistry is proud of its “green” philosophy. Not only is it good for the environment, but it is also beneficial to our patients. We use state-of-the-art, environmentally friendly equipment and technologies that help to provide safe and efficient care without the pain and time associated with old-fashioned dentistry.

At Village Green Family Dentistry, we are dedicated to providing quality dental care to all our patients. We accept most dental insurance plans, credit cards and offer payment plans to help patients benefit from the highest level of dental care.

GOING GREEN FOR THE ENVIRONMENT AND OUR PATIENTS

Village Green Family Dentistry is completely digital with advanced imaging equipment and chartless patient records. Not only does this mean that we’re using less paper and chemicals, but that there is also less radiation exposure, shorter appointments and higher quality images for our patients.
