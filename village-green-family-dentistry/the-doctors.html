DR. DONALD HUDSON

Dr Hudson has been practicing dentistry for over 25 years. Originally from Kansas City, MIssouri, Don spent most of his formative years in Alabama and Georgia, graduating from the University of Georgia and getting his DMD from the Medical College of Georgia in 1999.

After graduation, he served in the US Air Force for 5 years then had his own practice in Keene, New Hampshire for 14 years during which he was elected to the Board of Trustees for the New Hampshire Dental Society.

A huge music and sports fan, Dr Hudson likes to spend his free time playing guitar and going to music or sporting events.

DR. HEATHER STROCK

Dr. Strock discovered her passion for healthcare when she was young, knowing that she wanted to do meaningful work that helped people. While earning her DMD from Boston University’s Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine, she was able to serve in community health centers around Boston and volunteer in Honduras through MEDICO- a dental mission trip program which provides emergency and restorative care for the underserved population. These experiences solidified Dr. Strock’s mentality that her goal is to always provide quality care with compassion.

Dr. Strock focuses on delivering personalized care, with commitment to high-quality, patient-centered treatment.

When she is not practicing dentistry, Dr.Strock enjoys spending time with her husband and their three boys who keep them very busy! They are avid hockey fans (Go Bruins!) and love being outdoors.

A LASER FOCUS ON PROVIDING THE BEST CARE

Dr. Levine uses the latest in laser technology for select procedures. She is a member of the Academy of Laser Dentistry, which requires specific courses and standardization tests to become a member. The Academy makes the distinction that as a member, Dr. Levine has qualified herself to be a Laser Dentist, and not just a dentist who uses a laser.
