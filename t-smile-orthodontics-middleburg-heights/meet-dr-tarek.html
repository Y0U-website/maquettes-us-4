Customized Treatment

Not all orthodontic treatments are the same. Dr. Elshebiny’s patients are guaranteed a customized treatment approach, including computer-designed appliances that provide precise and faster results than conventional orthodontics.

Three-dimensional diagnosis is standard at T Smile Orthodontics. By combining low-radiation 3D Conebeam CT technology with the latest imaging software, Dr. Elshebiny is able to visualize the whole of each patient’s facial structure. This allows him to design a unique treatment plan for every person.

The combination of comprehensive diagnosis and computer-built appliances offers the most precise, efficient, and aesthetic orthodontic solution. For patients, this means fewer office visits and exceptional comfort throughout treatment!

Facially-Guided Orthodontics

No longer is orthodontic treatment just treating the smile by straightening teeth. Dr. Elshebiny’s unique approach to orthodontics looks at the whole of the person’s head and face. He analyzes the position of the patient’s teeth in relation to their face and then designs treatment to improve the individual’s bite, along with their smile. Not only are the smile results stunning, the improvements in the individual’s health and function are equally lasting and impactful.
