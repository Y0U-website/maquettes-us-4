How Invisalign Clear Aligners Work

Who Can Invisalign Help?

Invisalign can help teenagers and adults alike achieve more properly aligned smiles. To find out whether clear aligners are the best option in your particular case, you will need to visit our office and have our team examine your mouth. We’re able to use Invisalign to correct a wide range of orthodontic issues; here’s a closer look at some of the problems that the treatment can potentially help with.

Crowded Teeth

Teeth that overlap can draw attention that you don’t want. On top of that, they’re harder to clean and can easily trap food particles as well as harmful plaque. Fortunately, Invisalign can often help you improve your appearance and your overall oral health by repositioning your teeth so that they’re lined up properly.

Gaps Between Teeth

While having teeth crowded too closely together is problematic, you also don’t want them to be spaced too far apart. In addition to aesthetic concerns, the empty space can make it all too easy for debris to get caught between your teeth, which can ultimately increase your chances of suffering from oral health issues like cavities and gum disease. Fortunately, it’s often possible to close gaps in your grin with the help of Invisalign.

Overbite

Having an overbite means that the upper teeth come down in front of the lower teeth. A slight overbite is completely normal and nothing to worry about. But if it’s too pronounced, it can cause some of your teeth to wear down faster than normal, and it can lead to serious discomfort in your jaw. Invisalign can correct your overbite in order to help you avoid the worst of the long-term consequences.

Underbite

As you can probably guess, an underbite is the opposite of an overbite. It occurs when the lower teeth overlap the upper teeth when you close your mouth. This type of bite issue can happen for a wide variety of reasons (such as thumb-sucking during childhood), and it can make it difficult to chew properly. In some circumstances, it can even lead to mouth breathing and sleep apnea. It may be possible to address an underbite with Invisalign depending on how severe it is.

Crossbite

If your teeth erupt at the wrong angles, you might end up with some of your bottom teeth jutting out in front of your upper teeth. This is known as a crossbite. With Invisalign, it’s possible to reposition the teeth in question so that they are aligned properly, thus improving the appearance and function of your smile.

Open Bite

Do your upper and lower teeth not touch at all when you bite down on something? This is a less common type of bite problem known as an open bite, and it can have a number of consequences; notably, your teeth may have a higher chance of being chipped in an accident. Invisalign can be used to adjust your two arches of teeth in order to help you overcome your open bite.

Benefits of Invisalign

Invisalign continues to be one of the most sought-after orthodontic treatments. In fact, more than 17 million patients have straightened their teeth with clear aligners! While you might assume the fact that they are basically invisible is the biggest perk, that’s actually just one of many. Below, you will find several noteworthy reasons Invisalign continues to be so popular.

Improved Comfort

Instead of using metal brackets and wires to move your teeth into their preferred positions, Invisalign uses a custom series of removable aligners. As a result, you won’t have to worry about soft tissue irritation and mouth abrasions like you would with traditional braces. Not only that, but you won’t have to schedule an emergency appointment to fix a broken bracket or loose wire!

Freedom to Eat Your Favorite Foods

In addition to the clear aligners being comfortable and clear, Invisalign aligners are also removable. That means that, no matter what your favorite foods might be, you can still enjoy them when completing treatment. Just make sure that you don’t overindulge on sugary sweets and that you exercise caution with extremely crunchy foods. That way, you’re less likely to develop a cavity or a crack in your enamel during the teeth-straightening process.

Hassle-Free Dental Care

Another perk: your oral care doesn’t have to change either. This is noteworthy because patients with traditional braces often need to purchase interproximal toothbrushes, pre-threaded floss, and other special dental care products. With Invisalign , all you need to do is remove the aligners, rinse them with clean water, and store them in their designated case first. Then, you can continue your dental hygiene routine as usual.

Fewer Check-In Visits

Invisalign uses zero metal hardware to straighten teeth. As a result, you won’t have to visit us as often. Instead, you’ll progress through portions of your treatment plan at home. Then, every 6-8 weeks, you’ll come to our office so we can confirm your progress. To put it simply, you spend less time thinking about orthodontic treatment and more time enjoying your smile.

Basically Invisible Braces

Of course, one of the biggest benefits of Invisalign is that the aligners are basically invisible, which is a huge upgrade from noticeable, metal brackets and wires. The key to keeping your orthodontic treatment discreet is taking great care of your trays. That starts with gently rinsing them with clean, cool water when you remove them and extends to brushing them with a damp, soft-bristled toothbrush before putting them back on.

Shorter Average Treatment Timeline

Typically, patients with traditional braces achieve the desired results in 2-3 years. Patients with Invisalign , on the other hand, usually complete their orthodontic treatment in just 12-18 months. Living with Invisalign Aligners

When you commit to getting Invisalign in Reynoldsburg to straighten your smile, you should make sure that you have an accurate idea of what to expect. There are a few steps that you’ll need to follow in order to make sure everything goes smoothly throughout the orthodontic process. Below is a quick look at the basics that you should be aware of when it comes to living with Invisalign .

Wearing Your Aligners

Your Invisalign aligners can only do their job if you wear them for the requisite amount of time. If you remove them too often, your treatment plan may be delayed. As such, you will need to make a point of keeping your aligners in your mouth for at least 20 to 22 hours per day. You should only remove them when it’s time to eat or carry out your oral hygiene routine.

Cleaning Your Aligners

Cleaning your Invisalign aligners is essential for preventing bacteria buildup. Whenever you take your aligners out of your mouth, you should rinse them off with lukewarm water. (Avoid using hot water, as it could cause the plastic to lose its shape.) Additionally, you can brush your aligners daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush; be sure to use a nonabrasive cleaning agent such as hand soap.

Eating & Drinking

During your Invisalign treatment, you’ll be able to eat and drink whatever you want. That said, if you try to bite and chew food while your aligners are in your mouth, you could easily end up damaging them. Furthermore, many foods and beverages can leave stains on your aligners, causing them to stand out while you’re wearing them.

To keep your aligners safe, you should remove them when it’s time for a meal or snack. Be sure to store your aligners in their protective case while you’re eating; if you put them on the table or wrap them in a napkin, they might get left behind or thrown away by mistake.

Losing or Damaging an Aligner

When an aligner is lost or significantly damaged, you should call our office right away. Our team will consider your situation, then explain what steps should be taken to keep your treatment on track. Oftentimes, you may need to wear a previous set of aligners until we can provide you with a replacement. However, if you were about to switch to the next set of aligners anyway, then simply making the change early could be a viable option.

Routine Check-Ins

Roughly every six to eight weeks, you will visit our office for an Invisalign progress check. These visits are typically quite brief; in many cases, they only take around 15 minutes. During your appointment, we’ll examine your teeth to make sure that they’re moving as expected. We will also give you the next set of aligners in your treatment plan.

Understanding the Cost of Invisalign

Before you begin the Invisalign process, you should have a clear idea of how much it will cost. Our team aims to be transparent when it comes to the financial aspect of your care; we will give you a reliable estimate during your initial consultation, and we will answer any cost-related questions you might have. Below are a few things to keep in mind when it comes to the potential price tag of Invisalign.

Factors That Affect the Cost of Invisalign

First of all, there’s the question of how many arches are involved in your treatment. While Invisalign is typically used to straighten the upper and lower rows of teeth at the same time, some patients only have it performed for one arch. As you can probably guess, a single-arch treatment tends to have a lower price tag than a two-arch treatment.

Additionally, the complexity of your case also needs to be taken into account. People with more severe misalignment issues can normally expect to pay more for Invisalign. On top of that, the longer your Invisalign treatment takes, the more it will likely cost.

Invisalign vs. Mail-In Aligners: Which Costs More?

On paper, mail-in aligners tend to have a lower cost than Invisalign. However, one of the downsides of mail-in aligners is that there’s no dental professional monitoring your progress. As such, there’s a higher chance of problems occurring and not being caught until it’s too late. In the worst-case scenario, you could end up with serious dental issues that require additional (and potentially costly) treatment.

Does Dental Insurance Cover Invisalign ?

Dental insurance plans that include coverage for orthodontic treatments will often help pay for Invisalign. Of course, not every plan is the same; a service that’s covered under one policy may not necessarily be covered under another.

It’s best to take the time to review your dental insurance policy to confirm whether you can use your benefits to cover the cost of Invisalign. Remember that you can always call your insurance company to confirm the details of your benefits. Additionally, our team can go over your plan with you and clarify any details that you don’t understand.

Options for Making Invisalign Affordable

Even if you don’t have a dental insurance plan that will help pay for Invisalign, you can still make the cost of the treatment more affordable via financing. Our practice is happy to work with CareCredit and Lending Club, two third-party financing companies that are trusted by dental offices across the country. Signing up for a third-party financing plan lets you break up the cost of your care into smaller installments, which can make paying for Invisalign significantly easier on your budget.

If you’re interested in pursuing Invisalign but are worried about the costs involved, don’t hesitate to let our team know during your initial consultation. We can review your payment options in more detail with you and help you put together a plan.

Invisalign FAQs

Does Invisalign Hurt?

As a matter of fact, Invisalign is one of the comfiest orthodontic treatments. Its trays use a smooth plastic design that makes the alignment process feel easy and mild.

The only point to note is that Invisalign has an adjustment period. As you start wearing each set of trays, you may experience slight soreness or discomfort. Even so, please rest assured; this ache (if or when it occurs) is temporary and fades within a few days. The most you’ll feel afterward is the small pressure that straightens your teeth.

At the same time, you can always manage any Invisalign discomfort you have. Great ways to do so include taking over-the-counter medicines, using ice packs, etc.

How Long Will I Need to Wear My Aligners Per Day?

To ensure a successful treatment, you should wear your aligners 20-22 hours a day. Not doing so will delay the alignment work, making your care take longer (and cost more) than it should.

Remember: the trays only straighten your smile while worn. When they’re outside your mouth, your teeth are at risk of going back to their earlier positions. You should thus wear your aligners as much as possible, only removing them for meals and oral care. That arrangement leaves most patients with a suggested wear time of 20-22 hours daily.

Does Invisalign Give You a Lisp?

Invisalign can give some patients a temporary lisp. That said, whether it gives one to you will depend on your situation.

Note that lisps are caused by poor tongue placement in your mouth. If you get one from Invisalign, it’s because your aligners have altered the space near and around your teeth. This shift can cause your tongue to move differently, resulting in problems with your pronunciation.

Of course, an Invisalign lisp is typically minor and fades with time. This sort of speech impediment goes away within a few weeks at most, allowing you to talk normally again.

What Happens at Invisalign Checkups?

Invisalign checkups are a chance for your dentist (or orthodontist) to monitor your treatment progress. As such, they involve thorough oral exams and the opportunity to receive your next trays.

While effective, aligners can cause (or face) problems during treatment. A dentist uses Invisalign checkups to see whether such issues have occurred or if things are on track. In the latter case, they’ll provide your next set of aligners so your smile can keep getting straighter.
