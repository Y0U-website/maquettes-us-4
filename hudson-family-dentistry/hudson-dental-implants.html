Who Needs Dental Implants?

Dental implants are designed for adults who want a permanent, reliable solution to missing teeth. Whether tooth loss has occurred from injury, decay, or gum disease, implants can restore both function and appearance. They’re especially valued by individuals who may have lived with gaps or removable dentures for years and are now ready for a secure, lasting option. Since implants require a fully developed jaw, they’re best suited for adults whose smiles have stopped changing.

Many people who choose implants appreciate the freedom of enjoying meals without worry, speaking with clarity, and smiling with confidence. At Hudson Family Dentistry, our Hudson dentist team carefully evaluates bone health, lifestyle, and long-term goals to ensure every patient receives a solution tailored to their stage of life and dental needs.

The Dental Implant Process

  • Consultation – X-rays, exam, and treatment planning with Dr. Crawford or Dr. Powelson.
  • Surgical Placement – The titanium post is placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia.
  • Healing – The bone fuses with the implant (osseointegration) over a few months.
  • Abutment Placement – A connector is attached to secure the crown.
  • Final Restoration – A custom crown or bridge completes your natural smile.
