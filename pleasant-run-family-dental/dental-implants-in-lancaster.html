Is This Restoration Right For You?

If you’re wondering whether you’re a candidate for dental implants, the answer is often yes. Most patients with healthy gums and sufficient bone can get dental implants. And even if you’re not immediately eligible, procedures like bone grafting can help you get there. We’ve helped many patients, including seniors and those with past complications, become candidates through careful planning.

The Process: Simplified

Healing may take a few months, but the results are permanent and life-changing.

  • Initial Consultation – We evaluate your mouth and discuss your options.
  • Surgical Placement – The implant is inserted into your jaw and allowed to heal.
  • Post & Crown Placement – Once healed, a post is added to support your custom crown or denture.
  • Final Restoration – Your new tooth or denture is placed, completing your functional, natural-looking smile.
