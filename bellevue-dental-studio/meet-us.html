About Us

Drs. Jonathan & Christine are a husband and wife team that met while in dental school at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, MA.

Dr. Jonathan knows that the smallest thing can make the biggest difference when it comes to a patient’s smile. That’s why Dr. Jonathan takes extra time to completely personalize each patient’s care based on their unique goals, needs, and wants.

Both doctors have completed additional training at advanced hospital residencies in Los Angeles.

Starting at your first appointment, Dr. Jonathan will fully tailor your experience, so you’re guaranteed to walk out of our dental office with a healthy smile that fills you with confidence every time you see it.
