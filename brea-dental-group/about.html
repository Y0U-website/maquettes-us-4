Dr. Clark Schneekloth, DDS, MS Orthodontist

Dr. Clark Schneekluth, a California native, was born in Los Angeles. In 1977, he graduated with high honors from Point Loma Nazarene College with a BS in biology. Following his undergraduate studies, he attended Loma Linda University, where he received his dental degree in 1981 and his orthodontic certification in 1983. Since that time, he has continuously attended continuing education classes in order to stay up to date on the latest in orthodontics and offer his patients the best care possible.

Dr. Schneekluth has provided exceptional orthodontic treatment to families in Garden Grove, Seal Beach, and the surrounding areas in Southern California since 1983. With his talent, skill, and experience, Dr. Schneekluth ensures that everyone who receives orthodontic treatment from him leaves with an aesthetically pleasing, harmonious, and functional smile that complements their face and will last a lifetime. He feels that everyone deserves a smile they can be proud to wear.

Dr. Schneekluth is happily married to his wife Sherilyn and has two sons. When he’s not improving your smile or learning about the latest orthodontic advancements, Dr. Schneekluth enjoys participating in and watching sports, hiking, biking, and watching both movies and live entertainment. He also enjoys fine dining and traveling.
