Repairs/Relines

We offer repairs for every type of removable (i.e., metal, acrylic and flexible). We can restore both fractures and tooth replacements, regardless of who fabricated the appliance. Depending on the tissue sensitivity of the patient, we offer hard or soft relines for acrylic dentures or partials as needed.

As with age, mouth tissue continually changes. Relines and repairs are beneficial to individuals because they promote good hygiene, function better and fit more comfortably. They limit infections, sores, and minimize trapped food particles under the appliance. After working with you to establish your patient’s plan of care, we will repair or reline the removable appliance as prescribed.

Our highly qualified technicians are available for all your dental needs.

Intraoral Scans

As we move into the digital dentistry world, technology has transformed in the dental industry.

Starting with a digital impression of the patient’s mouth from the dentist, the images are captured by an intraoral scanner and processed by your scanning software. SmartLab accepts scans from most chairside scanners including Dentsply, 3shape, iTero and many more! With the array of 3D printers, we will generate any printed model processed by your scans.
