Porcelain Fused To Metal

Crown & Bridge

Porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM) crowns and bridges have been used in dentistry for years. We are proud to offer a wide range of options in both porcelain and metal. Our accurate production process ensures great aesthetics and optimum functional occlusal surfaces on all our PFM cases.

Full-cast restorations are the standard for long-term durability of posterior crowns. Used for over one hundred years in dentistry with an exceptional success rate, the advantages of a full-cast crown and bridge are: Minimum preparation Great for limited occlusal spaces Excellent marginal fit Ease of polishing and adjustment Offers beauty and durability for crown choice

For more information about Crowns & Bridges, please contact us .

E.Max

Porcelain-fused-to-metal layered zirconia restorations have an extraordinarily strong substructure covered by a weaker porcelain to increase the esthetics. In most cases, porcelain can chip or fracture away from the substructure and will cause the failure of the restoration; monolithic lithium disilicate has no such weakness.

IPS E.Max lithium disilicate is: Strong, durable and completely esthetic Proved to be more durable than layered zirconia in chewing simulated tests Most robust ceramic system evaluated to date Made of a glass-ceramic material, as durable as natural teeth, making them less likely to crack, chip or break

For more information about E.Max, please contact us .

Veneers

Veneers are thin ceramic shells that cement to the front surface of the tooth.

Porcelain veneers and laminates have been a specialty of SmartLab for years. We offer the IPS E.Max as well as other materials. Custom shading and photo shade matching is available to our clients as well. We can do ultra-thin veneers as well as the conventional thickness.

Based on an individual’s needs, ceramic veneers can be created to disguise many superficial concerns and designed for custom fit which requires significantly less preparation of the tooth.

Zirconia

Zirconia is an innovative system for aesthetic metal-free crowns and bridges and state-of-the-art material for dental restorative treatments. The reliability of zirconia restorations is backed by years of proven clinical success. It is a type of ceramic, or in more specific terms, a zirconium oxide that is stronger than porcelain and some metal alloys. Additionally, it gets fewer sharp edges from the normal "wear and tear" that happens over time.

The system is used to fabricate anterior or posterior single units or multi-unit bridge restorations out of translucent, biocompatible zirconia, the strongest and toughest dental ceramic. Zirconia restorations can be placed with conventional cementation or adhesive bonding techniques. Millions of units have been placed worldwide since its introduction. With our digital scanning design and milling system, the results are an excellent fit and marginal accuracy, for all your zirconia restorations needs.

Much like gold crowns, zirconia crowns may last past 20 years, thus allowing it to rate among the strongest and most durable products for tooth restoration.

For more information about Zirconia, please contact us .

Implants

We work with most major brands of implants including but not limited to: Zimmer, Implant Direct, Nobel Biocare, Straumann, IBSA, BioHorizons, Hiossen, and many more! We can also accept IOS files for most implant cases and then fabricate custom milled abutments and restorations. Please contact us to receive the appropriate scan bodies for your case prior to scanning.

Many patients who have selected dental implants describe a quality of life that is much more comfortable and secure than the lifestyle endured by those with fixed bridges or removable dentures. Dentures may often make a person feel and look older, may cause embarrassing social situations if they slip, and may restrict the everyday pleasure of eating certain foods.

Partials

SmartLab offers the latest partials, including: Cast-metal partials Flexible partials - including Valplast, TCS and DuraFlex Acrylic partials and flippers

Our removable partial dentures are fabricated to fill in the space created by missing teeth and restore natural function. We use the best material and process to minimize distortion and create great fit and aesthetics.

Valplast, TCS and DuraFlex are flexible, nylon thermoplastic partials that are thinner and more translucent than traditional partials. Doctors appreciate the minimal prep, easy insertion and long-term strength; while patients love the metal-free comfort and natural appearance.

SmartLab offers quick turnaround and precise fabrication of all partial dentures.
