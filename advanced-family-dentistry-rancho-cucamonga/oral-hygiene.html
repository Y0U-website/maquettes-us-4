Oral Hygiene services offered in Rancho Cucamonga, CA

Good oral hygiene preserves your teeth and gums, which is essential for your overall wellness. Reza Parhizkar, DDS, at Advanced Family Dentistry in Rancho Cucamonga, California, supports patients’ oral hygiene with expert advice and routine exams. Poor oral hygiene increases your risk of problems like heart disease and can cause dental issues, so don’t take it lightly. Call Advanced Family Dentistry to arrange an oral hygiene evaluation or schedule a consultation online today.

Why is oral hygiene important?

Brushing and flossing daily removes plaque a sticky film of bacteria that develops on everyone’s teeth throughout each day. If you don’t practice good oral hygiene, plaque builds up and hardens into tartar.

Plaque and tartar raise your risk of tooth decay, gum disease, and even tooth loss significantly. Some people may also experience pain, bad breath, and difficulty eating.

A healthy mouth ensures you can eat and speak comfortably and confidently. Gum disease also has links to other health problems like heart disease and diabetes, so oral hygiene should be a priority.

What does good oral hygiene involve?

For optimal oral hygiene, Advanced Family Dentistry recommends brushing your teeth twice every day once in the morning and once before bed. Use a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste.

Flossing once a day is just as important. Floss and interdental brushes help clean between your teeth, where your toothbrush can’t reach.

How is oral hygiene affected by braces or other dental work?

Keeping your mouth clean is critical if you have braces or other dental appliances. Plaque can build up around brackets or dental work, so take extra care with brushing and flossing. Your oral hygienist shows you the best tools and techniques to use.
