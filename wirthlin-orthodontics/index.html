The practice

On your first visit , we’ll take x-rays and digital impressions, discuss your treatment options, create a clear plan, and lay out your total costs & payment preferences. You’ll have all the information you need to get started.

No more gooey trays in your mouth! All impressions are taken digitally for a goop-free, gag-free experience. No mess . No stress.

We know happy kids = happy parents. So we create an environment where kids (and adults!) feel safe and comfortable.
