What are All-on-4 dental implants?

All-on-4 is a full-arch restoration technique where four implants are used to support a complete set of upper or lower teeth. These implants act as a strong foundation for a fixed prosthetic, providing a stable and natural-looking smile.

This solution is ideal for patients who:

  • Are missing most or all teeth
  • Currently wear dentures
  • Want a more permanent alternative
  • Have experienced bone loss

Who is a candidate for All-on-4?

  • Are missing most or all of your teeth
  • Are tired of removable dentures
  • Want a stable, long-term solution
  • Have sufficient bone structure for implant support

How All-on-4 can transform your life

All-on-4 dental implants can dramatically improve your daily life.

  • Restore your ability to eat comfortably
  • Speak clearly and confidently
  • Eliminate the inconvenience of removable dentures
  • Enjoy a natural-looking, secure smile

The All-on-4 treatment process

  • 01 Consultation & evaluation We assess your oral health and determine if All-on-4 is right for you.
  • 02 Digital treatment planning Advanced imaging is used to map precise implant placement.
  • 03 Implant placement Four implants are strategically placed to support your new smile, asleep under in-house IV sedation.
  • 04 Full-arch restoration A custom prosthetic is securely attached, restoring function and appearance — often the same day.
