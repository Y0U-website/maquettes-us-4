DENTAL IMPLANT FAQ&#x27;S

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of titanium that are surgically placed into the jawbone to support replacement teeth.

Are dental implants permanent?

Yes, dental implants are designed to be a permanent solution for missing teeth, offering long-term stability and durability.

Is everyone a candidate for dental implants?

How long does the dental implant process take?

The duration varies but generally involves a few months, including healing time after implant placement and the attachment of the final restoration.

Can I eat normally with dental implants?

Yes, dental implants allow for normal eating habits as they function like natural teeth, providing stability and strength.

How do I care for dental implants?

Dental implants require regular oral hygiene practices, such as brushing, flossing, and routine dental check-ups, similar to natural teeth.

Can smokers get dental implants?

While smoking can impact healing, it doesn&#x27;t necessarily disqualify someone from getting dental implants. However, smokers may be advised to quit or reduce smoking to enhance the success of the procedure.
