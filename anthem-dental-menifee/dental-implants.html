Permanent, natural-looking replacement teeth

A dental implant is the closest modern dentistry comes to regrowing a tooth: a biocompatible titanium root, topped with a custom porcelain crown, that restores 100 % of natural function and preserves the jawbone beneath it.

Anthem Dental places premium titanium implants — a system with decades of documented success — planned digitally on cone-beam CT imaging for precision placement. From single teeth to implant-supported bridges, the entire process happens under one roof.

Not every patient arrives with enough bone to support an implant right away — years of missing teeth can cause the jawbone to resorb. When that’s the case, bone grafting rebuilds the foundation first, so an implant that once seemed out of reach becomes possible again.

What a dental implant involves, from first scan to final crown

An implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone, where it takes the place of a tooth’s root. Once the bone has healed around it, a crown is attached. Because the process depends on healing, it is measured in months rather than visits. A typical course:

  • Consultation and 3D scan. A cone-beam CT scan shows bone height and width, nerves and sinuses, so the implant can be planned precisely.
  • Preparation, if needed. A failing tooth is removed, and bone grafting rebuilds areas where bone has been lost.
  • Placement. The implant is placed under local anesthetic.
  • Healing. The bone bonds to the implant over a period of months. A temporary replacement can often fill the gap in the meantime.
  • The crown. A custom porcelain crown is made to match the teeth beside it, and attached.

Is an implant always the answer?

No. A bridge can be a good choice when the teeth on either side already need crowns, and some health conditions or habits — uncontrolled diabetes, heavy smoking — affect how well bone heals around an implant. The consultation covers the alternatives honestly, with costs for each in writing.
