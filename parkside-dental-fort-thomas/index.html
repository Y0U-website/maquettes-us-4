0 Our Services We offer a full suite of dental services designed to meet the needs of every stage of life. From preventive maintenance to restorative and cosmetic procedures, we have you covered.

Routine cleanings and exams are an important part of maintaining a healthy smile. During these visits, we remove plaque and tartar buildup, perform an oral cancer screening, check for cavities and other concerns, and provide personalized home-care instructions to support long-term oral health.

Tooth-colored fillings restore teeth affected by decay, while helping preserve strength, function, and appearance.

Dental sealants provide a protective barrier on the chewing surfaces of back teeth to help reduce the risk of cavities.

Dental bridges are fixed restorations used to replace one or more missing teeth, helping restore your smile, bite, and ability to chew comfortably.

Custom occlusal guards are designed to protect teeth and support the jaw joints during nighttime clenching and grinding.

Root canals remove infection from inside the tooth to ease pain and help preserve your natural tooth.

Scaling and root planing is a deep cleaning treatment used to help treat gum disease. It removes plaque and tartar from below the gumline and smooths the tooth root surfaces to help gums heal and reattach more securely.

Dental implants are a long-lasting solution for replacing missing teeth, designed to restore the look, feel, and function of your natural smile.

Invisalign offers a discreet way to straighten teeth with clear, removable aligners customized to your smile.

Custom whitening trays offer a comfortable and effective way to brighten your smile at home. Made to fit your teeth precisely, they help ensure even whitening results while allowing you to achieve a noticeably brighter smile over time.
