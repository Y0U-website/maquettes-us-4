Why Choose Dr. Q Dental

At Dr. Q Dental, we believe exceptional dentistry starts with a foundation of trust, compassion, and lifelong relationships. For over 20 years, our team has cared for families throughout Monrovia and Arcadia, offering dental care that’s rooted in integrity, comfort, and long-term health for every stage of life.

Care for the Whole Family, All in One Place

At Dr. Q Dental, we make it easy for families to get the care they need. Whether it’s a child’s first dental visit, routine cleanings for parents, or ongoing care for seniors, our team provides comprehensive treatment for all ages under one roof. We take a thoughtful approach tailored to each stage of life, focusing on what’s necessary, explaining every option clearly, and making sure each visit supports your long-term dental health and comfort.

Technology That Puts You at Ease

Your comfort is always our top priority. That’s why we’ve invested in advanced dental technology designed to make every visit smoother, more precise, and more comfortable from start to finish. For your ease, we use:

Intraoral Scanner

This advanced imaging technology allows us to capture highly detailed, 3D images of your teeth without messy impressions. The process is quick, non-invasive, and far more comfortable, with no gagging, unpleasant taste, or bulky materials. It is especially helpful for patients with a sensitive gag reflex or dental anxiety, making the experience feel easier and more relaxed. It also improves accuracy in diagnosis and treatment planning.

Solea® Laser Dentistry

This cutting-edge laser allows us to perform many procedures with greater precision and less discomfort. In many cases, it eliminates the need for drills or anesthesia, helping you feel more relaxed throughout your visit and supporting faster healing.

Trusted by Monrovia Families for Over 20 Years

Dr. Q Dental has earned a lasting reputation in the Monrovia area by providing dependable, high-quality care with a personal touch. Our team is known for being thorough and consistent, dedicated to making every visit count. With more than 200 five-star reviews and a long history of serving local families, we’re here to help you maintain a healthy smile for years to come.
