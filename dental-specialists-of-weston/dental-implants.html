What to Expect During Your Visit

  • Initial Consultation : Your journey begins with a thorough examination by our skilled dentist. We’ll discuss your dental history, assess your oral health, and determine if you’re a good candidate for implants.
  • Treatment Planning : If you’re a candidate, we’ll create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs. This may include imaging tests to evaluate your jawbone and surrounding teeth.
  • Surgical Procedure : The implant placement is a straightforward procedure performed under local anesthesia. You’ll feel minimal discomfort, and our team will ensure you’re comfortable throughout the process.
  • Healing Period : Once an implant is placed, some healing time is required in the jawbone to bond the bone with the implant. It may take a few months to heal, but this helps for the long-term success of your implant.
  • Abutment Placement : Once healed, our dentist near you will attach a small connector (abutment) to the implant. This will hold your new crown securely in place.
  • Crown Placement : Finally, we’ll place your custom-made crown, completing your new smile!

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Implants mimic the look and function of your natural teeth.
  • With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.
  • They help maintain jawbone structure and prevent further dental issues.
  • No slipping or discomfort like traditional dentures.
  • Easy to care for—just brush and floss like your natural teeth!
