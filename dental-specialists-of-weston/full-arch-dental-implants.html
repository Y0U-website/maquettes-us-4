Who Is a Good Candidate for Full Arch Implants

  • Are missing most or all of your teeth
  • Have failing dental work or loose dentures
  • Want fixed teeth that look and feel natural
  • Have sufficient bone or are open to grafting if needed
  • Are in good general health or able to manage chronic conditions

What to Expect During Treatment

  • Consultation and scans
  • Digital planning and implant design
  • Implant placement and same-day temporary teeth
  • Healing and bone integration (3 to 6 months)
  • Delivery of your final prosthesis
  • Long-term care and follow-up appointments

Benefits of Full Arch Dental Implants

  • Permanent teeth that do not shift or fall out
  • More than 90 percent of natural biting and chewing ability
  • Better speech, appearance and confidence
  • Helps prevent further bone loss in the jaw
  • No adhesives, soaking or removable parts
  • Long-lasting results with proper maintenance
