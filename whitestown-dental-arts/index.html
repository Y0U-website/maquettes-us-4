The practice

Dental implant services at Whitestown Dental Arts offer a durable, natural-feeling solution for patients seeking to replace missing teeth and restore everyday function. Implants help support chewing, speech, and jaw stability by acting as artificial tooth roots anchored securely within the bone. From the initial consultation to final restoration, our team offers a supportive environment where you can ask questions and feel confident…

We provide clear explanations about implant benefits, treatment timelines, healing expectations, and long-term maintenance. This guidance helps you understand each step of the process and make an informed decision that aligns with your oral health goals.

A single-tooth implant replaces one missing tooth using an implant post, abutment, and custom crown. This solution restores function and appearance without affecting neighboring teeth, offering a stable, long-lasting alternative to traditional bridges.

Multiple implant-supported restorations are designed to replace several missing teeth with improved stability, strength, and long-term support. These options help maintain bite alignment and prevent shifting while restoring comfortable chewing.

Implant restoration and repair services address worn, loose, or damaged implant-supported crowns, bridges, or denture attachments. These treatments help maintain proper function, comfort, and long-term stability by ensuring each component fits securely.

Advanced 3D imaging provides detailed views of bone structure, anatomy, and implant positioning needs. This technology supports accurate planning, helping ensure implant placement aligns with safety, comfort, and long-term function.

Bone grafting strengthens areas where bone may be insufficient for implant placement. This treatment helps ensure implants have the stable foundation they need for long-term success, supporting both function and overall oral health.

Les soins
