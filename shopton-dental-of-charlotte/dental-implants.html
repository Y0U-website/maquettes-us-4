Frequently Asked Questions

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are a tooth replacement option that offers realistic and durable results. The synthetic structure consists of an implant, an abutment, and a crown, and it is fixed in the jawbone to permanently replace severely damaged, decayed, or missing teeth.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

Implants permanently replace damaged or missing teeth, enhance the appearance of your smile, and provide life-long retention because they are fixed in the jawbone. They allow you to eat and speak comfortably, are biocompatible so they adapt well to your jawbone and gum tissues, and integrate with the surrounding bone to help prevent future bone loss.

Can dental implants help prevent bone loss?

Yes. Because implants integrate with the surrounding jawbone, they help prevent future bone loss—something that often occurs after teeth are lost. This makes them a valuable long-term solution for your oral health.
