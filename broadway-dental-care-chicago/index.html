The practice

Our goal is to provide you with the highest standard of treatment and an enjoyable experience. We are dedicated to providing the finest quality orthodontic treatment in a friendly, enjoyable and caring environment.

Dr. Nguyen earned his doctorate in dental surgery from the University of Illinois in 1992. While attending the University, Dr. Nguyen earned the highest levels of academic and clinical achievements.

With a commitment to delivering the highest level of comprehensive, contemporary and compassionate dental care, Dr. Nguyen established his own practice in 1994. Dedicated to staying current and proficient with leading edge dentistry, he has incorporated some of the most advanced dental technology and techniques into his practice while providing gentle dental care.
