Surgically Guided Implants

Dental implant surgery replaces missing teeth with natural-looking and feeling prosthetics. Dr. Monica Crooks specializes in guided implant surgery, providing precise and accurate results when creating and placing dental implants in Sacramento, CA

Guided implant surgery involves the use of an advanced surgical guide, minimizing operator error and ensuring the most precise surgical placement possible. These guided implants not only save time and money but also help prevent complications.

If you’re looking for dental implant surgery in Sacramento, CA, Trust Dr. Monica Crooks and call (916) 922-2207 for an expert in guided implant surgery for the best results.

Benefits Of Using A Surgical Guide Dental Implants

Surgically guided dental implants are safer and more predictable than standard dental implants and offer the following benefits:

  • Increased accuracy
  • Reduced risk of complications
  • More predictable results
  • Enhanced comfort
  • Long-term restoration
  • Minimized treatment time
  • Lower cost of treatment

How Does Surgical Guide Dental Implants Work?

Dr. Monica Crooks captures a 3D image of your teeth that guides her in designing and placing your dental implants. The 3D implant planning software helps her view the intricate details of your bone structure, gums, and teeth so that she can create an incredibly precise implant. Ultimately, this surgical guide allows Dr. Monica Crooks to provide a quicker treatment and recovery time, and results that look and feel just like your natural teeth.
