Implant Supported Dentures

Dental Implants are used to replace natural teeth lost from extensive decay, gum disease, or a traumatic injury. They are realistic and permanent, providing the most advanced dental solution to missing teeth. If you have lost a tooth, Dr. Monica Crooks may suggest a dental implant to restore the appearance of your smile as well as the function of your teeth.

Dr. Monica Crooks Sacramento office provides patients suffering from tooth loss with natural-looking and permanent dental implant solutions. Dental implants are solidly anchored in the bone, just like your teeth so you can chew and speak with confidence!

Don’t let missing teeth prevent you from living your best, most confident life!

What Are The Main Benefits Of Implant Supported Dentures

Anchored dentures can be an effective treatment if you are looking for an alternative to traditional dentures. There are many benefits to implant-supported dentures, including:

  • Eliminating messy adhesives
  • Preventing dentures from slipping out of place
  • Increased comfort and stability
  • Easy maintenance
  • Restoring teeth functionality
  • Minimizing gum irritation
  • Providing a more natural look and feel

Everything You Need To Know About Implant Supported Dentures

Dr. Crooks will start by assessing your jawbone density and overall oral health. You must have a strong enough jawbone to support the implants to be a candidate for this procedure. If you do, she creates a mold of your mouth to build a unique, custom prosthesis.

Titanium posts are then surgically placed into your gum tissue, and over the next few months, they fuse to your jawbone, creating a permanent, stable support for the denture. After this process is complete, Dr. Monica Crooks will construct a durable restoration that screws into the dental implant. Dental implants have a very high success rate, making this option one of the most popular for restoring missing teeth.

The procedure results in new teeth that look and function much like your natural ones. You can also clean and maintain your implants just like your natural teeth through regular brushing, flossing, and routine check-ups with your dentist.
