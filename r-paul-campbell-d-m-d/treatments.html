Early Treatment

Braces and teeth aligners are essential orthodontic appliances. While some people are blessed with perfectly aligned teeth, others may need dental alignment treatment as early as seven years old. Though your child may not need them at that age, The American Association of Orthodontists recommends children should be screened by an orthodontist for potential orthodontic problems in their tooth alignment, gums, jaw, and faced problems, reverse swallow, tongue thrust habits, and correcting them with the necessary orthodontic appliances before becoming more serious. Adolescent Treatment

For many teenagers, wearing braces is almost a rite of passage into their adult years. Adolescents are growing and changing at a rapid rate, which is when crooked teeth, gaps, and bad bites are detected. Since these problems don’t correct themselves, most parents will seek alignment treatment. Braces will be placed on the patient’s teeth to straighten and correct their bite, offering a confident and healthy smile.

Retention

It’s crucial to establish a routine of wearing your orthodontic retainer to maintain the results you achieved during your treatment phase.

Hawley Retainer

The removable Hawley is considered the “traditional” retainer. Its construction includes plastic (acrylic) that covers the roof of the mouth or lies along the tongue side of the lower teeth and comes in a variety of colors. Wire clasps grasp the back molar teeth to anchor the retainer while a metal bow wire runs across the front of the teeth to help maintain their orthodontic position.

The Essix retainer is a clear, plastic removable appliance that fully covers the teeth to help maintain a straighter position.

The Essix retainer is a clear, plastic removable aligner that fully covers the teeth to help maintain straight teeth. It’s virtually invisible, durable, easy-to-wear and normally less expensive than the traditional retainers.
