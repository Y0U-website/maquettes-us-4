Introducing the latest orthodontic technology DentalMonitoring!

DentalMonitoring allows you to take scheduled scans of your teeth & communicate with our practice securely from the comfort of your home.

We will provide you with a Dental Monitoring Scan box that enables you to take quality teeth selfies using your smartphone, in only 5 minutes! These pictures will be sent to our clinic so we can closely monitor how to proceed with your aligner treatment, suggestions to improve your treatment, and evaluate your brushing.

The team at R. Paul Campbell, D.M.D. brings their own unique skill set, experience and knowledge to the practice. Collectively, they work together to help create the straightest, healthiest smiles for patients in Lewistown and Bellefonte , PA. They do what they love to do, so you can enjoy what you love to do.

Dr. Campbell and his staff have each earned a quality education and developed a wealth of experience both in the dentistry and at the practice. Their dedication to the industry is exemplified by their consistent improvements in techniques through continued educational courses.

Their commitment to the people of the community isn’t just within the walls of the practice. Dr. Campbell and the team volunteer with the National Foundation for Dentistry for the Handicapped and participate in the job shadowing program associated with Central Pennsylvania Institute of Science and Technology’s dental assisting program.

Dr. Campbell grew up in the State College area and graduated from State College High School in 1981. After graduating from Tufts University School of Medicine in Boston, MA in 1993, he received his Advanced Graduate Study in Orthodontics From Boston University Dental School in 2000. He then took over the practice from his father, Dr. Robert Campbell which was established in 1968.

Dr. Campbell is involved with these professional memberships: American Dental Association Pennsylvania Dental Association Middle-Atlantic Society of Orthodontists Centre Dental Society
