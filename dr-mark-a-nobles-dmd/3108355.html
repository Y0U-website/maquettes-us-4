Meet Dr. Mark A. Nobles, DMD

Dr. Nobles grew up in nearby Starkville, MS and attended Mississippi State University where he graduated with a Bachelor of Arts and a Bachelor of Science degree in 1997.

That same year, he was admitted to the University of Mississippi of Dentistry in Jackson graduating with a Doctor of Dental Medicine degree in 2001.

During his time in Jackson, he married his wife, Dena, who grew up in Russellville, Alabama and was teaching in the Jackson area.

Dena, is on faculty @ Mississippi State University and they have four children.

Hobbies include hunting, fishing, exercise, and chauffeuring their children to practices, games, and many activities.

Meet Dr. Ben Harrelson, DMD

Dr. Harrelson grew up in West Point Mississippi, and attended Mississippi State University where he graduated with a Bachelor of Science degree in Microbiology, Magna Cum Laude in 1999. He went on to attend the University of Mississippi in Jackson graduating with a Doctor of Dental Medicine degree in 2003.

After graduation he took his first position with a group practice in Nashville, TN. He lived in Nashville one year before returning to his home state to settle in to establish his practice.

He joined WiIllowbrook Dental Center in the summer of 2004. Since that time he has been blessed with three children, two boys and a girl (Davis, Baker, and Lyla). He enjoys traveling, anything outdoors, and spending time with his family.
