Frequently Asked Questions.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes! We’re always welcoming new patients and would love the opportunity to care for you and your smile. Whether you’re due for a checkup or looking for a new dental home, we’re here to help.

What should I expect during my first visit?

Your first visit includes a warm welcome from our team, a comprehensive exam, digital X-rays if needed, and a conversation with the doctor about your oral health goals. It’s a great time to ask questions and get to know us.

Do you treat children and adults?

Yes, we provide care for patients of all ages—kids, teens, and adults. From first visits to ongoing family care, we tailor treatment to meet each patient’s unique needs.

Where are you located and is there parking available?

We’re located at 3512 Main St. Vancouver, WA 98663. Our office is easy to find, and we offer convenient, free parking right outside the building.

Do you accept my insurance?

We work with many major insurance providers and are happy to help you understand your coverage. Just give us a call with your insurance details, and we’ll gladly verify your benefits before your appointment.

What if I don’t have dental insurance?

No insurance? No problem. We offer affordable options for uninsured patients, including our in-house membership plan . Quality care should always be within reach.

I have dental anxiety. Can you help?

Yes, and you’re not alone. Many patients feel nervous about dental visits, and we’re here to make your experience as calm and comfortable as possible. From a welcoming environment to gentle care and clear communication, we go the extra mile to help you feel at ease.
