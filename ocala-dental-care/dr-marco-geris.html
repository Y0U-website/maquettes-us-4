Dr. Marco Geris, DDS

Dr. Marco Geris is a dedicated and skilled dentist who received his education in dentistry from Nova Southeastern University in Fort Lauderdale, Florida. He is passionate about providing comprehensive dental care to his patients and is committed to staying current with the latest advancements in dentistry. His passion for providing exceptional dental care led him to earn the prestigious achievement award from the American Association of Endodontics.

Dr. Geris actively engages in continuing education courses in the fields of endodontics, implants, full mouth rehabilitation, and the latest advancements in dental technology. He stays updated with the latest techniques and tools, which allow for accurate and efficient diagnoses and treatment plans. Dr. Geris is most passionate about tackling challenging and complex root canals, as he finds immense satisfaction in saving teeth and providing relief from patients’ pain.

Outside dentistry, Dr. Geris enjoys playing and watching soccer. He cherishes his time spent with his family and fiancée. Patients can trust Dr. Marco Geris’s commitment to their oral health and overall well-being.
