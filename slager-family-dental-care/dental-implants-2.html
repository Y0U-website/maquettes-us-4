The Dental Implant Procedure

Our streamlined process ensures efficiency and comfort:

Consultation Imaging: Detailed 3D scans to evaluate bone and plan placement.

Treatment Planning: Customized strategy tailored to your needs.

Implant Placement: Precise surgical placement using guided technology.

Healing Phase: The implant integrates with your jawbone.

Final Restoration: Placement of a custom crown, bridge, or denture.

Dental Implants FAQs

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants can last 20 years or more , and in many cases, a lifetime with proper care and regular dental visits.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The full dental implant process typically takes 3 to 6 months , depending on healing time and individual needs.

What is the success rate of dental implants?

Dental implants have a success rate of over 95 % , making them one of the most reliable dental procedures available.

Can I replace multiple teeth with dental implants?

Yes, implant-supported bridges and full-arch implants allow us to replace several or all missing teeth efficiently.

Do dental implants look natural?

Yes, implants are designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth , blending seamlessly with your smile.

What if I don’t have enough bone for dental implants?

Bone grafting procedures can often rebuild the jawbone to support successful implant placement.

Are dental implants better than dentures?

Dental implants provide greater stability, comfort, and durability compared to traditional dentures.

How do I care for dental implants?

Maintain good oral hygiene by brushing, flossing, and visiting your dentist regularly to keep your implants healthy.
