For more complex cases, we may refer you to a trusted oral surgeon, also known as an oral and maxillofacial surgeon. These specialists receive advanced training in surgical procedures involving the teeth, jaw, and facial structures.

No matter your needs, we will guide you through every step of the process and ensure you receive the right level of care for the best possible outcome.

How do I know if I need oral surgery?

You may need oral surgery if you are experiencing persistent tooth pain, swelling, infection , or have a severely damaged or missing tooth. A consultation at Village Green Dentistry will help determine the best treatment based on your condition.

What is the recovery time after oral surgery?

Recovery time depends on the type of procedure, but most patients begin to feel better within a few days. For treatments such as extractions or root canals, normal activities can often be resumed quickly, while more complex procedures like dental implants may require a longer healing period.

What is the difference between All-On-4 and traditional implants?

All-On-4 implants use fewer implants to support a full arch of teeth, making them a more efficient option for patients missing multiple teeth. Traditional implants replace teeth individually. Our team will help you choose the best option based on your needs.

Don’t Wait to Treat Dental Pain or Infection If you’re experiencing pain, swelling, or signs of infection, delaying treatment can worsen the problem. Getting the right care early can relieve discomfort, prevent complications, and protect your long-term oral health.
