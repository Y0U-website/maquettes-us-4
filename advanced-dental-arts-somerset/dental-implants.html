Our Dental Implant Process

Step 1: Consultation and Imaging

We begin with a thorough exam, including 3D CT imaging , to assess bone density and create a precise treatment plan tailored to your smile.

Step 2: Implant Placement

Dr. Afshar or Dr. Haddad places the titanium implant into the jawbone using guided surgical techniques for accuracy and comfort. Sedation options are available for anxious patients.

Step 3: Healing and Osseointegration

The bone heals around the implant over several months, securely anchoring it in place.

Step 4: Restoration

Once healing is complete, we attach a ceramic crown , bridge, or denture to the implant, custom-matched to your surrounding teeth for a seamless look.

Types of Implant Solutions

Single Tooth Implants

Ideal for replacing one missing tooth with a crown supported by a single implant.

Implant-Supported Bridges

Used to replace several adjacent missing teeth. Anchored to two or more implants without affecting nearby healthy teeth.

Implant-Retained Dentures

Offer a better fit, comfort, and chewing function than traditional dentures. Perfect for full-arch restoration.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants integrate beautifully into full smile makeovers and are often combined with teeth whitening or Invisalign for total smile enhancement.

  • Prevent jawbone loss and preserve facial structure
  • Look, feel, and function like natural teeth
  • Do not affect adjacent teeth like traditional bridges
  • Offer permanent support with proper care
  • Restore up to 90 % of natural bite force
  • Easy to clean and maintain like real teeth

Most patients report minimal discomfort. Local anesthesia completely numbs the area, and mildsoreness afterward is typically managed with OTC pain medication.

From start to finish, it usually takes 3–6 months, depending on healing time and whether bone graftingis required.
