0 Our “Home” Team At Rest Dentistry, we’re a small biological , airway, and sleep -focused office in smallish Battle Ground, WA—a couple steps away from Vancouver, WA and Portland, OR. Our “home” team is small too. But our purpose is big.

These are the people you will meet in person when you come to visit our office. They handle all the details around the comprehensive dental care we provide. While we have a remote “away” team that handles medical insurance and billing for Obstructive Sleep Apnea and TMJ oral appliances, our “home” team will be the ones you meet every time you see us.

I don’t believe in coincidences or accidents, so none of these ladies are here by chance. They were each meant to be here. And each one is a blessing to work with. Come see for yourself.
