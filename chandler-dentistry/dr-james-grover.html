Send A Message Get To Know Dr. Grover Clinical Precision. Artistic Excellence. Dr. James Grover is a highly accomplished and compassionate dentist known for blending clinical precision with artistic excellence. A distinguished graduate of the University of Texas School of Dentistry, Dr. Grover earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree with honors — setting himself apart through outstanding academic achievement, clinical mastery, and a deep commitment to patient-centered care.

He earned his reputation in Houston before relocating to Arizona, where he continues to deliver exceptional results. Driven by an unwavering dedication to excellence, Dr. Grover has completed hundreds of hours of advanced continuing education in cosmetic, restorative, and implant dentistry.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Grover is an avid traveler and outdoorsman who enjoys exploring new cultures and spending time in nature — a well-rounded lifestyle that shapes the thoughtful, empathetic care he brings to every patient.

A Foundation Built on Decades of Excellence

Dr. Grover graduated Magna Cum Laude in Biochemistry from Texas A&M University before earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Texas School of Dentistry — where his clinical work contributed to peer-reviewed scientific publications.

He began his professional journey as a partner in an extensive Houston group practice before relocating to Arizona with his girlfriend and their two rescue dogs, Brad and Ghost. His passion for dentistry runs deep — rooted in a familial connection to his uncle, who has practiced for over 40 years. That legacy drives his love for the profession every day.

Your Comfort. Our Priority.

Dr. Grover is committed to ensuring that each patient feels comfortable and at ease throughout their treatment journey. His dedication to achieving natural-looking results is a testament to his commitment to enhancing aesthetics while preserving authenticity.
