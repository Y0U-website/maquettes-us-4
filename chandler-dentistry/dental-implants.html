Single Tooth Implants

Replace One Missing Tooth

A single dental implant is the ideal solution for replacing one missing tooth without affecting the adjacent teeth. Unlike a bridge, which requires grinding down neighboring teeth, an implant stands on its own with a titanium post, abutment, and custom crown.

The result is a restoration that looks, feels, and functions exactly like a natural tooth. With proper care, a single dental implant can last a lifetime.

Send A Message Full-Arch Implants Replace an Entire Arch of Teeth Full-arch dental implants (also known as All-on-4 or All-on-6) can replace an entire arch of missing teeth with as few as four to six implants. This innovative approach provides a permanent, fixed set of teeth that look and function like natural teeth.

Unlike traditional dentures, full-arch implants are securely anchored in your jawbone, eliminating the need for adhesives and the worry of slipping dentures.

Send A Message The Implant Process What to Expect The dental implant process typically involves several stages. First, a comprehensive evaluation including digital imaging to assess your bone density and plan your treatment. Then, the titanium implant post is surgically placed in your jawbone.

After a healing period (usually 3-6 months) during which the implant fuses with your bone, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to complete your restoration. Dr. Grover will guide you through every step of the process.

All-On-4 dental implant retained dentures are a revolutionary solution for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth. This innovative technique uses just four strategically placed dental implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth, providing a permanent, stable, and natural-looking smile.

Our All-On-4 Dental Implant Retained Dentures Process At Chandler Dentistry

Our process begins with a comprehensive evaluation, including 3D imaging to assess your bone structure and determine the optimal implant placement. On the day of surgery, four titanium implants are placed at precise angles to maximize bone contact and stability. A temporary set of teeth is attached the same day, so you never have to go without a smile.

How Long Do All-On-4 Dental Implant Retained Dentures Take?

The initial procedure typically takes a few hours, and you’ll leave with a temporary set of functional teeth. After a healing period of 3-6 months, your permanent custom prosthesis is fabricated and placed.
