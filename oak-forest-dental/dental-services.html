Dental Services

At Oak Forest Dental we are proud to work with all our patients as partners to choose the best course for their dental care. In our comfortable, relaxing office environment, we give every patient the highest quality of care and offer a full range of professional services:

  • Comprehensive preventative care
  • Non-surgical preventative gum therapy
  • Dental bonding
  • TMD occlusal splint therapy
  • Tooth-colored fillings
  • All-porcelain crowns and bridges
  • Porcelain veneers/smile designs
  • Implant crowns
  • Dentures and partial dentures
  • Traumatic dental injuries
