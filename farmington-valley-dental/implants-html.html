How Are Dental Implants Different?

Dental implants are far different than other types of tooth replacement like dental bridges or dentures. This is because dental implants involve the use of a dental implant post, which is a small, titanium screw that is surgically placed into the jawbone. This replaces the root structure of the missing tooth, providing a secure and highly reliable foundation for the prosthetic tooth, whether that be a crown, bridge, partial, or full denture. This means you will never have to worry about slipping, irritation, or discomfort.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

Dental implants require a multi-step process that spans several months, but the result is a long-lasting solution for tooth loss. Many patients find the benefits to be well worth the time and effort. Fortunately, our team at Farmington Valley Dental is qualified to carry out the entire treatment from start to finish right here in one convenient location. Although every patient’s treatment plan will vary slightly, below are the four main stages you can expect.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

Your dental implant journey begins with an in-depth consultation with Dr. Peters. He’ll conduct a thorough oral examination, inquire about your medical history, and overall assess your candidacy for implant placement. Based on our evaluation, we may recommend preliminary procedures (i.e., bone grafting, gum disease treatment, tooth extractions) to prepare you for the procedure.

Once we’ve confirmed you’re free of any growing complications, such as decay or infection, we can move forward to the next step of the process.

Dental Implant Surgery

After you’ve agreed to our treatment plan and Dr. Peters has determined you’re a suitable candidate, we’ll schedule your in-house surgery. Before starting the procedure, he’ll administer a local anesthetic to minimize discomfort. Once you’re comfortable, the implants will be surgically placed throughout your jawbone at precise, pre-planned positions. Your gums will then be sutured closed, and dental caps will be placed on top to keep the posts protected while your mouth heals.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment Placement

Following dental implant surgery, the titanium posts will bond with your body via a natural process known as osseointegration. This can take anywhere from three to six months to occur, creating a stable foundation for your new smile. Once completed, this will give your implants the lifelike qualities of a natural tooth, such as its incredible functionality and longevity.

You’ll then return to our office for a second minor surgery to receive your abutments, which are small connector pieces that are attached to your implants. This allows us to secure a personalized restoration (i.e., crown, bridge, or denture) in place.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restorations

While you heal from dental implant surgery, our trusted dental laboratory will craft your custom restoration. Once it’s ready, and your gums have healed, we’ll attach it to the titanium posts. We’ll double-check that your bite is correct and you’re happy with the results before we send you on your way. Now you can enjoy your brand-new smile!

What Do Dental Implants Cost?

During an implant consultation with your dentist in Farmington, the following factors will be considered to determine what your dental implants will cost: the number of teeth you’re missing, where they are missing, the health of your jawbone, and other special considerations.

How Many Teeth Are Missing?

Dental implant posts are individually priced, which means this factor will usually have the greatest influence over the total cost of your procedure. A single missing tooth is less costly to replace than a full arch, for example. However, an individual post is not needed for cases of multiple or complete tooth loss. We can be strategic while still achieving a secure fit for the prosthetic.

Where In the Jaw Are The Missing Teeth Located?

Your dentist will have a different approach for dental implants depending on their location in the jaw. Front teeth may be more costly to replace than molars or premolars due to aesthetic concerns which result in a more complex procedure.

How Healthy is the Jawbone?

Patients who have been missing teeth for a while or who have had severe gum disease (periodontitis) may need to have a bone graft or sinus lift to create the right environment for dental implants. This initial procedure increases the cost of an implant procedure.

Are There Any Other Special Considerations?

Other factors that may influence the cost of a dental implant procedure include what type of anesthesia/sedation are used, how many preparatory appointments are needed, and others.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

Dental implants can be a long-term investment that keeps your smile strong and healthy for life if you take excellent care of them. Thankfully, these amazing prosthetic devices require only the same oral hygiene habits as real teeth to stay in great shape. Here’s a quick guide to how you can keep your dental implants in excellent condition from the friendly team at Farmington Valley Dental.

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

Dental implants cannot develop cavities, but the gum tissue supporting them can still be compromised by infections if you don’t practice proper hygiene. To keep your implants in great shape, take care to brush, floss, and rinse with antibacterial mouthwash every day. It’s also a great idea to drink plenty of water to help flush harmful agents away from your mouth and keep you hydrated for ample saliva flow.

Eat a Healthy Diet

Dental implants restore almost all of your original chewing power, so you’ll be able to eat anything you like after receiving them. However, it’s important to make smart eating decisions to avoid accumulations of harmful oral bacteria and provide your gums with the nutrients they need to stay healthy. Be sure to limit your sugar consumption while enjoying plenty of foods that are rich in calcium, vitamins C and D, and protein.

Break Bad Habits

Tobacco use and excessive alcohol consumption can erode the health of your gums and set your dental implants up for early failure, so it’s best to quit these destructive vices after receiving your restorations. It’s also important to refrain from chewing on inedible materials like ice, wood, or plastic as well as using your teeth to open packaging.

Protect Your Dental Implants

Dental implants are incredibly strong, but they are not indestructible and can be compromised due to trauma. When playing sports, wear an athletic mouthguard to keep your natural and artificial teeth safer from injury. If you grind your teeth at night, be sure to wear a protective nightguard when you go to bed. The team at Farmington Valley Dental will be happy to fix you up with customized appliances that provide superior protection.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

People with dental implants still need to see their dentists every six months for routine checkups. These appointments allow our team to monitor your oral health, clean the hard-to-reach areas of your mouth, and address problems in their early stages, which can help keep your implants in beautiful condition and prevent early failure.

Dental Implant FAQs

Are All Dental Implants The Same?

There are different types of dental implants that are used in different situations. Mini-implants, which are smaller and shorter than traditional implants, can be used to anchor full dentures.

They’re often a great choice for patients who have experienced bone loss in their jaws and, in many cases, just 2-6 mini-implants are necessary per arch.

Does It Hurt To Get Dental Implants?

It’s understandable that implants might seem like they would be painful, but they essentially act as replacement roots, so you won’t feel them any more than you feel the roots of your natural teeth.

Are Dental Implants Noticeable?

An implant sits under the gumline, so it’s completely invisible. And since it looks, feels, and functions so naturally, no one will know it’s there. In fact, the only way for anyone to see your implant is to take an X-ray showing the titanium post in the jaw.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Dental implants stimulate new bone growth, allowing the jawbone to fuse to the post. As a result, they offer a 95 % 10-year success rate. With the right aftercare, the implant post can potentially last for a lifetime. To promote its longevity, it is best to maintain your oral hygiene and regular appointments with your dentist in Farmington. While the post can last forever, your dental crown will suffer from daily wear and tear. On average, it will need to be replaced about every 10 years.

Do Dental Implants Feel Natural?

With the use of the dental implant post, your smile will closely look and feel natural as they are securely held in place. In fact, when compared to traditional dentures, dental implants offer an increased biting force of over 70 %.

How Much Do Dental Implants Cost?

There is no set price for the procedure because it varies from person to person. There are several factors that influence the cost, such as how many teeth need to be replaced, if additional procedures are needed, and your insurance coverage. While you can expect to pay more upfront, they offer a long-term investment and will be more cost-effective in the future because you do not need special cleaning products, adjustments, or new prosthetics made every few years.

Can Dental Implants Stop Bone Recession?

Dental implants are the only form of tooth replacement that stops bone recession from missing teeth. The implant post stimulates the jawbone, encouraging new bone growth. This helps to preserve your bone density to protect any remaining natural teeth and your facial structures.
