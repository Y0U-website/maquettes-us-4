Dr. Raymond Duong was born and raised in Brooklyn, New York, and is a board-certified pediatric dentist in Hackensack, New Jersey. Dr. Duong is a skilled dental specialist with a passion for staying at the forefront of his field. Combining compassionate care with extensive knowledge, he offers pediatric dentistry services to patients in Hackensack.

Dr. Ray learned first-hand the importance of establishing early dental care, prevention, and positive dental experiences during childhood. He recognizes that every child is unique and strives to provide compassionate dental care that is tailored to a child’s personal needs. Dr. Ray is known for his energetic and relatable personality.

His unique education and experience enables him to provide outstanding care for infants, children, and adolescents in a family-friendly environment. Dr. Ray is a strong believer in evidence-based dentistry and keeps up to date with the latest scientific literature.

Educational Background and Experience

Dr. Ray graduated cum laude from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine with his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree. During his last two years of dental school, his passion for helping others led him to complete his degree with honors focusing on community service and medically complex care.

His exposure solidified his passion for pediatrics and helping those with special needs. Following dental school, he completed an intensive specialty-training program in pediatric dentistry at Interfaith Medical Center in Brooklyn.

During residency, he obtained invaluable clinical experience in sedation dentistry and provided comprehensive dental care to underserved areas in Brooklyn.

About Dr. Duong

In his free time, Dr. Ray loves to spend quality time with his partner, family, and friends. He enjoys his life and hobbies include fitness, singing along to Broadway hits, and going to food adventures.
