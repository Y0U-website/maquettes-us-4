Dental implants offer the look, feel, and function of natural teeth because they provide the security of your natural tooth roots.

Dental implants offer many benefits compared to more temporary replacement options, such as:

Implants provide a stable foundation for replacement teeth, allowing them to blend in seamlessly with your smile.

Dental implants, made of biocompatible titanium, integrate with your natural jawbone through osseointegration. The surrounding bone heals around the implant after surgery, becoming a natural part of your smile.

Unlike dental bridges, implants don’t rely on your surrounding teeth for support, allowing you to maintain the natural structure of your healthy teeth. Implants also stimulate the underlying bone to prevent it from breaking down.

Loose or ill-fitting dentures can affect your speech and cause sores on your gums. Dental implants hold your replacement teeth securely in place to ensure clear speaking and improve overall comfort.
