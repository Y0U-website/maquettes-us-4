Meet the Team

Carla Kucirek, DDS

Dr. Kucirek has been practicing dentistry since 2014 after graduating from Creighton University School of Dentistry. Originally from Idaho, she fell in love with the Midwest and chose to grow roots in Southwest Iowa. Being a dentist allows her to fulfill her passion for serving others, challenge her mind and hone her artistic skills on her favorite medium—teeth! She and her husband Adam love to be active outdoors, support local artists and musicians, and keep busy with their two school-aged daughters. (Go Tigers!)

Kira

Kira

Kira grew up in Henderson and graduated from Nishna Valley. She graduated in 2012 with a degree in Dental Hygiene from Iowa Western and has been working here since then. Presently, she lives in Bennington, Nebraska with her husband and dog. Kira enjoys crafting, reading, and rooting for Nebraska volleyball.

Melissa

Melissa

Melissa graduated from Dental Hygiene at Ozarks Technical Community College in Springfield, MO in 2010. She and her husband live in Elliot with their three boys. They enjoy the outdoors, spending summers in the Ozarks, fishing and hanging out with friends and family. Melissa is passionate about helping her patients maintain their teeth for life!

Karen

Karen

Karen has lived in Southwest Iowa all of her life, graduating from school in Stanton, Iowa. She has been working in the dental field since 2006. She enjoys helping others with any questions or concerns they may have while in or out of the office. She and her husband Dan have three grown children. She stays busy with the grandchildren, church, the Five-One-8, the Wilson Performing Arts Center and baking for others as the “Sweet Swede.”

Brenda

Brenda

Brenda started her dental career in 1994 and continued classes to expand her skills. She has a very strong work ethic and provides a welcoming environment that comforts and relaxes patients. She grew up in the countryside East of Clarinda and currently lives by Viking Lake with her husband and daughter. She thoroughly enjoys walks in nature and spending time with her family.
