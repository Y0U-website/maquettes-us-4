Meet Your Orthodontist, Dr. Adina Wolfe!

Come meet our orthodontist, Adina Wolfe, DMD! Dr. Wolfe earned her dental degree from Temple University. After graduating from Dental School she spent an additional Three Years training at the University of Buffalo’s Orthodontic Residency Program.

Not only is she the only practicing Orthodontist at Quaker Pointe Orthodontics, she is the owner. What that means is you’ll never see multiple doctors, never have a doctor leave mid-treatment, and you won’t feel like you’re just a number. Come experience the difference!

Dr. Wolfe is married with two children and has a large, lovable pup. She understands the importance of family, which is why she creates a welcoming environment for her patients. She strives to make each visit to Quaker Pointe Orthodontics a place that feels like home.

When she not in the office you can find her running, reading, wood working, hiking, or baking!
