Who We Are

Dr’s Anna & Kyle have been together since they first met in dental school, and were married shortly thereafter. They try to ensure that their passion for their work is apparent to each and every patient!

Dr. anna shoenberger, dmd pediatrics

dr. kyle shoenberger, dmd orthodontics

Kyle Shoenberger, DMD is an orthodontist. He grew up in Allentown, PA and received his dental degree from the University of Pennsylvania, School of Dental Medicine, and continued his training at Woodhull Medical Center where he went on to complete a one-year intense hospital-based residency involving emergency and operating room treatment. Dr Kyle then completed a fellowship and residency program in orthodontics at the Roseman University College of Dental Medicine. He enjoys spending time with his wife, and loves helping people smile!

We treat each patient (and tooth!) with the care and consideration they deserve. We make sure to spend the time to answer all of your questions.

We check to make sure you keep that perfect healthy smile.
