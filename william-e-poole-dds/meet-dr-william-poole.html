Meet Dr. William Poole Your Local Family Dentist

D r. William Poole has been faithfully servicing patients from Leola, New Holland, Ephrata, Akron and the surrounding Lancaster community since 2001.

In caring for their patients, Dr. Poole and his friendly team strive to grow relationships built on trust and openness.

As a patient of Dr. Poole, you can be confident in knowing that you will not be pressured into extra services or treatments. You will only receive the highest quality care based on your wants and needs.

Dr. Poole grew up in Leola and graduated from Conestoga Valley High School. He holds his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Case Western Reserve University, Cleveland, OH.

When not practicing dentistry, Dr. Poole enjoys spending time with his wife Alison and their three sons Jackson, Aaron, and Chase.
