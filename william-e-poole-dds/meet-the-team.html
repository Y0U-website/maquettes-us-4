Dr. William Poole

Dr. William Poole has been in practice in Leola since 2001. After growing up in Leola and graduating from Conestoga Valley High School, Dr. Poole focused on pursuing his dental degree. This led him to Philadelphia to study Biology at La Salle University and obtain his B.A. degree in 4 years. Dr. Poole then completed four years of rigorous dental education at Case Western Reserve University in Cleveland where he earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree.

He has completed numerous continuing education courses in areas such as cosmetic dentistry and restorative dentistry. Meet The Doctor Meet The Team The staff at William E. Poole, D.D.S. , P.C. is comprised of many talented individuals with over 50 years of collective experience in the dental field. Our team consists of receptionists, dental hygienists, and assistants that are highly skilled in their field, and are held to the highest standards of patient care. With a focus on dental excellence and patient care, our staff is here to make your visit both efficient and personable.
