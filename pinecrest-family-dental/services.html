Dental Implants

Read More

The use of dental implants represents one of the most significant advancements in dentistry over the past four decades. These implants are composed of titanium and other biocompatible materials, ensuring they harmoniously integrate with the human body. Surgically placed into the upper or lower jaw, dental implants serve as sturdy anchors for replacement teeth, providing security and stability.

The dental implant procedure generally involves three phases. Firstly, your dentist will surgically insert the implant into the jawbone. During the healing process, a diet of soft, cold foods, and warm soup may be recommended to aid in recovery. The second phase is osseointegration, where the bone fuses with the implant, creating a remarkably strong bond. This process may take several months, but it’s crucial for the overall success of the implant.

Once the implant is fully integrated, the final phase involves placing the artificial tooth or teeth. If it’s a single-tooth implant, a custom dental crown will be meticulously designed to match your existing teeth in size, shape, color, and fit. For multiple missing teeth, personalized bridges or dentures will be created to perfectly complement your mouth and implants.

We understand that the process of crafting replacement teeth takes time. Therefore, while you wait for the permanent solution, your dentist may provide a temporary crown, bridge, or denture to ensure you can eat and speak comfortably.

Experience the transformative benefits of dental implants at Pinecrest Family Dental, where our expertise and compassionate care will guide you on the journey to a revitalized smile.

1. Consultation: During your initial consultation, our experienced Invisalign providers will evaluate your smile and discuss your goals. We’ll create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs.

2. Custom Aligners: Based on your treatment plan, a series of custom aligners will be created for you. Each set of aligners is worn for about 1-2 weeks before progressing to the next set in the series.

3. Wear and Care: You’ll wear your aligners for 20–22 hours a day, removing them only to eat, drink, brush, and floss. This flexibility means you can maintain your regular oral hygiene routine and enjoy your favorite foods.

4. Gradual Progress: As you progress through each set of aligners, you’ll notice your teeth gradually shifting into alignment. You’ll have occasional check-ins with us to ensure your treatment is on track.

5. Completion: Once you’ve completed your series of aligners, you’ll be amazed by your beautifully aligned smile. To maintain your results, we may recommend a retainer be worn as instructed.

-Convenience: The ability to remove the aligners makes eating, brushing, and flossing easy and hassle-free.

-Predictable: Invisalign’s advanced technology allows you to see a virtual representation of your treatment plan and expected results before you even begin.

Deep within the protective layers of your tooth lies the pulp, a vital part containing blood vessels, nerves, and connective tissue. This pulp extends from the crown of the tooth to the root tip. Unfortunately, the pulp can become inflamed or infected due to factors like dental decay, trauma, or gum disease. Inflammation of the pulp’s sensitive nerves often results in discomfort, while chronic infection may lead to a tooth abscess.

The most common endodontic treatment is the renowned root canal procedure. This procedure involves the removal of the infected or damaged pulp from the tooth. The inner chamber and canals of the tooth are carefully cleaned and shaped using specialized instruments. Once thoroughly cleaned, the root is sealed with a filling to prevent further infection.

It’s important to note that following a root canal, although the tooth may no longer respond to temperature changes or sensations, it can function well for many years. For the best longevity, especially for back teeth, a crown or cap is often recommended after endodontic treatment. This helps restore the tooth’s strength and protect it from future damage.

Crown & Bridge

We offer comprehensive Crown and Bridge services to restore the function and aesthetics of your smile. Our skilled team is dedicated to providing you with top-quality dental care that ensures your teeth remain healthy and beautiful.

Read More

Dental crowns are versatile restorations that effectively cover or cap a tooth, allowing us to restore its normal function and appearance. Crowns are crafted from various materials, including all metal, porcelain fused to metal, or all-ceramic (porcelain). We recommend crowns for a range of dental issues, such as teeth with extensive fillings, those that have undergone root canals, fractured or worn teeth, and teeth that are misshapen or discolored. Our goal is to provide you with a durable and natural-looking crown that enhances your smile’s integrity and beauty.

When it comes to replacing missing teeth, traditional methods like dental bridges offer reliable solutions. A bridge consists of a false tooth, known as a pontic, which is anchored between two dental crowns. These crowns are placed on the teeth adjacent to the gap created by the missing tooth. The crowns and pontic work together to create a seamless replacement for the missing tooth. Dental bridges are fixed restorations, meaning they are securely cemented into place. While you can’t remove the bridge yourself, we can provide you with special aids to ensure proper maintenance and hygiene.
