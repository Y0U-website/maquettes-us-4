Dental Crown Bridges Treatment in Pinecrest, FL

A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring an artificial tooth to nearby natural teeth or dental implants. This option helps restore chewing ability, support facial structure, and prevent surrounding teeth from shifting.

FAQs

How long do dental crowns and bridges last? With proper care, many crowns and bridges can last 10–15 years or longer.

Is crown treatment painful?

Local anesthesia is used to keep the treatment comfortable during tooth preparation.

Can a broken crown be repaired?

Some crowns can be repaired, while others may need replacement depending on the damage.

Are bridges good for missing teeth?

Yes, bridges can replace missing teeth and help restore chewing and smile balance.

How do I care for a crown or bridge?

Brush twice daily, floss carefully, and schedule regular dental checkups.
