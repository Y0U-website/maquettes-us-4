FAQs

How long do dental veneers last? Porcelain veneers can last 10–15 years or longer with proper oral hygiene and regular dental visits.

Are veneers permanent?

Because a small amount of enamel is removed during preparation, veneers are considered a permanent cosmetic treatment.

Do veneers look natural?

Yes. Modern veneers are customized to match the color, texture, and translucency of natural teeth.

Can veneers fix crooked teeth?

Veneers can improve the appearance of minor misalignment or uneven spacing, though severe orthodontic issues may require braces or aligners.

Is getting veneers painful?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort during the procedure. Local anesthesia may be used during tooth preparation for added comfort.
