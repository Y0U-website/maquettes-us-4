Boe Simmons PA-C

Owner / Founder Sleep Better Idaho

Boe Simmons is a physician assistant who has been practicing for over 22 years. He has focused on sports medicine injuries, general orthopedics, and total joint arthroplasties. He has always been exemplary at patient care and jumped at the chance to work with patients with sleep apnea as there is such a need to screen and treat this underserved area of medicine. He enjoys golf, boating and spending time with his family camping. He is married and has two children.

Crystal Semmler, EFDA

Clinic Director

Crystal is a born and raised Idahoan. She lives in Meridian with her husband, children, and dogs.

Crystal has enjoyed a career in dentistry for more than twelve years. Her passion is patient care. She is committed to educating patients and physicians to ensure the successful treatment of obstructive sleep apnea. She has undergone extensive training in the field of dental sleep medicine with highly experienced doctors around the country. She has studied sleep disordered breathing and obstructive sleep apnea in great depth.

Crystal enjoys cooking, camping, hiking, fishing, and being outdoors. She enjoys music, and is a classically trained violinist. Her family, friends, and career are the highest priorities in Crystal’s life. She is proud to be part of the Sleep Better Idaho team!
