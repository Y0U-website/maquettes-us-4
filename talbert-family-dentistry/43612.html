Meet Dr. Grant Talbert, DMD

Dr. Grant Talbert

Dr. Grant Talbert started Talbert Family Dentistry in May 2022 with a clear vision: to create a practice where high-energy teamwork and genuine care transform smiles and lives. After graduating from Birmingham Southern College in 2014 with a biology major and chemistry minor, he earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Alabama at Birmingham School of Dentistry in 2018.

A Passion for Life-Changing Dentistry

What inspired Dr. Talbert to pursue dentistry was witnessing how transformative dental care could be. He watched friends and family members gain new confidence and joy simply from improving their smiles, and he knew he wanted to create those life-changing moments for others. This passion for smile transformation naturally led him toward cosmetic dentistry, where he can combine technical precision with artistic vision to help patients rediscover their confidence.

An Exceptional Team Environment

Advanced Technology Meets Patient Care

At Talbert Family Dentistry, Dr. Talbert combines advanced technology, including digital smile design software and same-day 3D printing capabilities, with personalized care to deliver outstanding results . Whether performing routine general dentistry or complex cosmetic transformations, his focus remains on the profound impact a beautiful, healthy smile can have on someone s entire life.

When he s not creating confident smiles, Dr. Talbert finds his happy place on the water. He s also passionate about hunting, fishing, and working out, and he s a huge music fan.
