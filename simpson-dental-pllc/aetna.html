Aetna Dental Patients at Simpson Dental PLLC

If you have Aetna dental coverage and are looking for care in Charleston, WV, you may be wondering how a fee-for-service practice works. At Simpson Dental PLLC, we do not accept dental insurance — which means no carrier restrictions and no claim-driven treatment decisions.

At Simpson Dental PLLC, we are a fee-for-service practice that does not accept dental insurance. Dr. Christen Simpson Raynes and Dr. Chase Preston focus entirely on delivering the highest quality care — without insurance restrictions — while offering flexible financing to make treatment accessible for every patient.

Step One: Benefit Verification and Treatment Planning

Your journey begins with a thorough consultation where we assess your needs and provide a clear, upfront cost estimate. We review all treatment options with you before your appointment so you have complete information and no surprises.

We build your treatment plan based on clinical necessity and your goals — never on insurance coverage or limitations. Our general dentistry approach ensures comprehensive care that addresses your immediate needs while planning for long-term oral health.

Transparent Cost Estimates

For all procedures, we provide written cost estimates upfront. You’ll know exactly what you’re paying before treatment begins — no surprises, no claim-dependent costs. We explain all treatment options and associated costs in plain English so you can make fully informed decisions.

Step Two: Treatment Planning and Cost Estimation

For major procedures, we provide written cost estimates so you have a clear picture of your investment before treatment begins. Our financial coordinators work directly with you to identify the right financing plan, keeping you informed at every step.

Coordinating Multiple Procedures

When you need extensive dental work, we strategically coordinate procedures to minimize your overall costs and maximize efficiency — using our in-office lab to complete many restorations in a single visit.

Step Three: Treatment Delivery

During treatment, we focus entirely on delivering exceptional dental care. Our advanced technology, including digital scanners and our on-site laboratory, allows us to provide efficient, high-quality care. You receive detailed statements after each visit showing exactly what was completed and what you paid — full transparency at every step.

Advanced Procedures at Simpson Dental

Our comprehensive services include dental implants , hybrid ceramic zirconia implants, and same-day teeth options for full mouth rehabilitation. We provide upfront cost estimates for all advanced cases so you know your full investment before treatment begins.

Step Four: Ongoing Care

After completing major treatments, we help you maintain your results with regular preventive care. We stay proactive about your care schedule, helping you plan ahead so treatment aligns with your timeline and budget.

Additional Services and Payment Options

Dr. Christen Simpson Raynes provides facial aesthetics treatments using our Fraxel CO2 laser, offering comprehensive enhancements that complement your dental work. We offer flexible financing through CareCredit , Cherry , and Proceed Finance, with interest-free promotional periods and extended payment plans. We also work with Health Savings Accounts and Flexible Spending Accounts.
