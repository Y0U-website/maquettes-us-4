Dr. Tim Stirneman

Dr. Stirneman has been working in dentistry for 31 years now, and there’s nothing he enjoys more about his job than meeting new patients and helping them improve their lives.

Dr. Stirneman graduated from Creighton University with a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics. He is a Diplomate of the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine, he is also an accredited member of the Academy of General Dentistry, the American Dental Association, etc. He then went on to achieve his Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree at Creighton University Dental School.

Hundreds of hours of continuing education each year keep Dr. Tim Stirneman among an elite class of dentists who provide exceptional sleep apnea treatments. He is also accredited member of the Academy of General Dentistry, the American Dental Association, Illinois State Dental Society and several sleep organizations.

An award winning sleep dentist, recognized both nationally and across the Chicago area, Dr. Stirneman has also been voted to several patient choice awards, including Practice of the Year. He has also been featured on numerous television programs, radio and print publications, including the Wall Street Journal, Chicago Sun Times, NBC, ABC and WGN.

Dr. Stirneman passion is improving lives by treating sleep apnea. A lifelong Cubs fan, Dr, Stirneman has three grown children: Kenny, Katie, and Kurtis.
