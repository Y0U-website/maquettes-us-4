Our Process at Smile Glen Ellyn

At Smile Glen Ellyn, we use advanced technology and personalized care to deliver exceptional results:

  • Consultation and Planning : We begin with a comprehensive exam, including 3D imaging using CT cone beam technology, to ensure precise planning and placement.
  • Implant Placement : The implant, which serves as the tooth root, is surgically placed in the jawbone to provide a stable foundation.
  • Custom Crown Fabrication : After healing, we design a custom implant crown that matches your natural teeth in both appearance and function.
  • Placement and Adjustment : The crown is securely attached to the implant, completing your smile restoration.
