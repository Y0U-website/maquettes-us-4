Implant Retained Dentures

Why would you need Implant Retained Dentures?

The reason you might need this type of denture is because you&#x27;re tired of the appliance constantly falling out of place. Rather than have to worry that it&#x27;s becoming a problem, you simply need to have implants placed that will fit your mouth perfectly. There are many people who have benefited from these types of dentures. They are easy to make and can snap right onto the implants themselves.

The best way to determine if you&#x27;d need implant supported dentures is with an exam. Our doctors will evaluate your smile and gums to see if these implants are right for you. You will find that the process to place the new implants is very easy and quick. You can benefit from having the implants placed as needed.

What happens during the Implant Retained Denture process?

The first step involves placing the actual implants. This is done through oral surgery that can be performed right in our office. We will then work to get those implants to heal, which takes about six months. Once the implants are healed completely, our goal is to make a new denture for you. This denture is made using impressions of your implants and gums. You will come back in to have the new denture placed and we can make adjustments to the appliance as necessary. You can feel confident in how you look thanks to these types of implants and subsequent dentures.
