Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Kyle Foutz

Dr. Kyle Foutz is proud to return to his home town as a Clinical Partner at Integrity Dental in Farmington, New Mexico. Passionate about patient-centered care and committed to long-term service in his community, Dr. Foutz is excited to begin his professional journey in a place that shaped him. He looks forward to making a meaningful contribution to a region with a growing need for high-quality dental care.

Current Role and Practice

The opportunity to work with Dr. Cole and his wife, Robyn, stood out immediately to Dr. Foutz. Their warmth, professionalism, and shared vision for the practice’s future made the decision to join Integrity Dental an easy one. With a deep understanding of the Four Corner’s area and its wonderful people, Dr. Foutz is eager to bring his energy, empathy, and dedication to a team focused on compassionate dentistry. He looks forward to learning from a seasoned mentor like Dr. Cole while laying the foundation for growth in the practice.

Professional Journey

Philosophy and Passion

Dr. Foutz believes in dentistry that is personal, not procedural. He is known for taking time with patients, particularly during exam appointments, to understand each patient’s needs, ease any fears, and build meaningful connections. He understands that many patients experience anxiety and makes it a priority to create a comfortable and informative experience from the very first visit.

One of the most rewarding aspects of his journey has been seeing patients go from pain or self-consciousness to pride in their smile, especially in smile transformations. He also finds fulfillment in providing immediate relief, whether through emergency care or simple, effective interventions.

Personal Life

Looking Ahead

For Dr. Foutz dentistry isn’t a profession; it is a longterm scalling. He is excited to help grow a practice that delivers excellent care while remaining deeply rooted in the values of service, respect, and trust. With mentorship from Dr. Cole, and the superb work of the entire Integrity Dental team, he looks forward to helping patients across generations smile with confidence.
