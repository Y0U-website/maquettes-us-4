Patient 2

Before: The patient had a full set of upper teeth, but was unhappy with how the smile looked. The upper teeth and gums were too prominent when the patient smiled, affecting the patient’s confidence.

After (All-on-4): Using the All-on-4 treatment concept, we created a new full-arch prosthesis that improved the appearance of the patient’s upper teeth. 4–6 dental implants were placed to anchor the prosthesis securely. The new setup balanced tooth size, shape, and positioning, giving the patient a natural-looking, confident smile that they feel proud to show.

Patient 3

Before: The patient had severe gum disease and multiple failing teeth in both the upper and lower jaw. Chewing, speaking, and smiling were difficult, and her overall oral health was compromised.

After: We removed the non-restorable teeth and treated the gums and jawbone as needed with alveoloplasty and bone grafting. On the lower jaw, 4–6 dental implants were placed to support a permanent All-on-4 prosthesis. On the upper jaw, a complete overdenture was fitted for comfort, function, and aesthetics. The result is a fully restored smile, healthier gums, and the ability to eat and speak confidently.
