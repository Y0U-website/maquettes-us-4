Meet Alexander Doye, DDS

Dr. Doye completed his dental training at Virginia Commonwealth University School of Dentistry, followed by advanced oral and maxillofacial surgery training through an internship at The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center and residency at the University of Cincinnati.

Dr. Doye brings extensive experience in dentoalveolar surgery, facial trauma, anesthesia, and benign pathology. His clinical approach is grounded in precision, evidence-based care, and a commitment to excellent patient outcomes.

He is proud to be practicing in Chapel Hill, returning to an area that feels like home after spending part of his youth in nearby Apex, North Carolina. Dr. Doye is honored to serve patients across the Triangle with compassionate, thoughtful, and highly skilled surgical care.
