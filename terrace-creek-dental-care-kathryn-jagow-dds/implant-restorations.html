The Benefits of Implant Restorations

Implant restorations are custom-designed to match the shape, color, and function of your natural teeth.

Implants integrate with your jawbone for unmatched durability and stability.

Unlike traditional bridges, implants don’t rely on adjacent teeth, preserving their health and integrity.

Our Treatment Process

Personalized Consultation & Treatment Plan

Every smile is unique, which is why your journey begins with a comprehensive evaluation. Dr. Jagow will assess your dental health, discuss your goals, and determine the best restoration option for your needs. We’ll walk you through each step so you feel comfortable and informed.

Precision Placement & Healing

If you don’t already have a dental implant in place, we’ll coordinate the placement with a trusted specialist. After the implant is surgically placed into the jawbone, a healing period of a few months allows the bone to fuse with the implant, creating a solid foundation for your restoration.

Custom Restoration for a Natural Fit

Once healing is complete, we’ll attach a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture to your implant. Our restorations are crafted for a seamless, natural look. With proper care, your implant restoration can last for decades.

If you have missing or severely damaged teeth and want a long-term, natural-looking solution, implant restorations may be the best option. Ideal candidates have good overall oral health and sufficient jawbone density to support an implant. During your consultation, Dr. Jagow will determine if an implant restoration is right for you.

With proper care, implant restorations can last 15 years or more, and the implant itself can last a lifetime. Maintaining good oral hygiene and attending regular dental checkups will help keep your restoration in top shape.
