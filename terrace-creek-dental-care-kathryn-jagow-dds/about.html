Our Technology

CEREC® Same-Day Crowns and Bridges Embrace convenience. CEREC® technology allows us to create and place high-quality, custom-fitted crowns and bridges in just one visit. This innovative system uses digital impressions and precise milling to craft your restoration right in our office, saving you time.

Intraoral Cameras Stay informed and involved in your dental care. Intraoral cameras capture detailed images of your teeth and gums, which we can display on a screen for you to see. This technology enables us to better explain your oral health condition, discuss treatment options, and keep you engaged in the decision-making process.

Digital Impressions No more messy, uncomfortable traditional impressions. Our practice utilizes digital impression technology to create highly accurate, 3D images of your teeth. This process is quick, comfortable, and eliminates the need for physical impressions. Digital impressions also allow for more precise treatment planning and better-fitting restorations.

Digital Smile Design Want to see your smile outcome before treatment starts? With digital smile design technology, we can create a virtual simulation of your proposed smile makeover. This allows you to see the potential results, provide feedback, and collaborate with our team to design a smile that aligns with your unique preferences and facial features.

Digital X-Rays Our practice uses digital x-rays to capture detailed images of your teeth, gums, and jaw. Compared to traditional film x-rays, digital x-rays emit significantly less radiation and provide instant, high-resolution images. This technology allows us to detect dental issues early, monitor your oral health over time, and plan treatments with greater precision.

Sedation We understand that dental anxiety is a common concern for many patients. To help you relax and feel comfortable during your appointments, we offer sedation options. Whether you prefer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) or oral sedation, our team works closely with you to determine the best approach for your needs. Your comfort and peace of mind are our top priorities.

Metal-Free Dentistry We prioritize the use of biocompatible, metal-free materials in our dental treatments. From tooth-colored fillings to ceramic crowns and implants, our practice offers a range of aesthetic and health-conscious options. By avoiding metal-based restorations, we reduce the risk of allergic reactions, enhance the natural appearance of your smile, and promote long-term oral health.
