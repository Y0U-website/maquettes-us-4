Dental Implants That Fits Your Budget

A single-tooth dental implant at our office runs $3,500–$4,200 — that includes the implant, abutment, and crown per tooth. An implant bridge (2 implants supporting 3 crowns) runs $8,000–$9,000 . Final pricing varies based on materials — titanium vs. ceramic implants, zirconia vs. porcelain-fused-to-metal crowns, and other component choices all affect the final quote. Most insured patients have 50–80 % coverage for this procedure. We verify your benefits before treatment and give you an accurate estimate — and we work to maximize every dollar of coverage you’re entitled to.
