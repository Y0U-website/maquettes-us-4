How much does it cost to restore a tooth implant? Costs vary based on the type of restoration needed. A single crown replacement may cost less than a full arch restoration. We provide detailed cost estimates and help you maximize your insurance benefits.

Can you get a dental implant redone? Yes. If an implant fails or no longer functions properly, it can often be replaced with a new implant or restored with advanced treatments like bone grafting.

Can a failing dental implant be fixed? In many cases, yes. Depending on the severity, we may be able to treat inflammation, adjust the abutment, or replace the restoration without removing the implant post.

Is teeth restoration worth it? Absolutely. Restoring an implant helps maintain oral function, prevent further damage, and preserve your smile’s appearance and alignment. It is a worthwhile investment in your long-term oral health.
