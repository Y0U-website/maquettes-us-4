Sugandh P. Relan, DDS, MDS, BDS

I was born and raised in India. I grew up in a nurturing family environment, that instilled the importance of kindness, family values, loyalty and continued personal growth. As a child, I found such reward in making people smile that I often thought of becoming a dentist when I got older.

I earned by Bachelors and Masters of Dental Surgery degrees with Gold Medal from the Maharashtra University of Health Sciences, India. After moving to United States to continue with my passion, I went to the University of Washington School of Dentistry, one of the top dental school in the country and got honored with Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

Here I am today, with a passion for dentistry and a continual commitment to making people smile. My approach towards providing care is comprehensive and preventative and I strongly believe in the importance of listening to you and understanding your needs. My relationship with you, I hope, will be long term. As your Dentist, I would like you to know that I will continue to educate myself, keeping abreast with the most current advances dentistry has to offer.

I would like to say thank you, for putting your trust and confidence in me. I am really excited to know you and I am looking forward to serving you and your family!

Dr. Dipti Soni, DDS, MDS, BDS

Dr. Dipti Soni earned her DDS degree with cum laude honors from the International Dental Program at the University of Nevada. In addition, Dr. Soni completed her undergraduate degree in Dental Surgery and postgraduate degree in Orthodontics from India. Dr. Soni provides patient-focused, high-quality dental care, with an emphasis on ethical practices, building a rapport with patients, and continuous learning.

Outside of her work life, Dr. Soni enjoys spending time with her family and exploring the natural beauty of the Pacific Northwest with her husband and two daughters.
