Dr. Damera

Dr. Damera graduated from New York University School of Dentistry, New York. She has over 15 years of experience in the field of dentistry, and stays up to date with all the latest advances and treatment.

Dr. Damera treats every patient with care and compassion, and has a gentle touch ensuring a very comfortable patient experience. Her detail oriented approach ensures each patient is treated with the best care possible.

She is very happy to be part of the community to provide excellent dental care in a state of the art modern environment. Prior to going to NYU, Dr. Damera volunteered for American Red Cross in Chicago as an Office Assistant.

Dr. Damera has been a proud resident of Westford, MA for the last 12 years. She has two lovely daughters and enjoys spending time with family and friends.
