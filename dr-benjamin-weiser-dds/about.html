A Personal Approach to Dental Care in Brunswick, MD

Dr. Weiser helps patients of all ages to maintain the health, comfort, and stability of their smiles. We provide comprehensive dental care, including treatment for complex dental concerns. If you have gum disease, damaged teeth, or missing teeth, we can help you restore your smile. We want all patients to enjoy the benefits of optimal oral health. A healthy smile plays an important role in enjoying good physical wellness, especially as we age.

Routine dental care visits are important and are the foundation of our practice. When patients visit regularly, we can help them minimize their risk of developing common problems such as tooth decay and gum disease. These can start as simple issues but, left untreated, can threaten the health and stability of your teeth and gums. Dr. Weiser is committed to the health of your smile and takes time with every patient to fully understand their needs and concerns. We want you to leave your visit feeling confident in your smile and empowered to maintain your oral health between visits.

We understand that visiting the dentist can make you feel anxious or uneasy. Our staff is welcoming, friendly, and compassionate. We don’t want a fear of the dentist to keep you from receiving the routine care you need, often leading to complex dental problems. Let us know if you feel anxious, and we will take the time to educate you on each step in the process and find ways to help you relax in the dentist’s chair.

Welcoming New Patients

We welcome children and adults. Our team can meet the changing needs of growing and aging smiles. We welcome new patients to join our dental care family and enjoy patient-focused dentistry.
