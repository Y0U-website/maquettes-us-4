Dr. Georgia Cullington, DMD

Dr. Georgia May Cullington is a proud alumni of University of New Hampshire 2021 and University of New England College of Dental Medicine 2026. She has experience in restorative, endodontic, periodontics procedures, extractions, and radiographic interpretations. She worked with underserved communities in Conway, NH. Dr. Cullington enjoys treating patients with special needs and making everyone feel comfortable at the dentist! S he served as President of the Endodontics Club , organizing meetings, lectures, and workshops. She is a member of the American Dental Association (ADA) .

She participated in Give Kids a Smile Program, which helps children from low income families receive needed dental care, and worked with the Maine Behavioral Health Center of Excellence in Autism and Developmental Disorders .

Dr. Cullington is originally from Framingham, MA and her family lives on a farm in Hooksett, NH! When not treating patients, she loves to travel, read (she has read over 30 books in one summer!) and be around her animals. She has had a pet parrot and rabbit, and her family takes care of goats and chickens on the farm. They also take care of honeybees!
