Why are Dental Implants Better Than Dentures?

Dental implants are fixed in the jawbone, helping preserve bone, improve chewing ability, and provide a more natural feel than removable dentures.

How much does a dental implant and implant crown cost?

The total cost of dental implant treatment varies because every patient’s needs are different. Fees may include the diagnostic imaging, tooth extraction, bone grafting, surgical implant placement, abutment, and custom implant crown.

At this time, because implant placement is generally completed by a dental specialist and the final implant crown is restored at Harte Dental, different portions of treatment may be estimated or billed separately. Dental insurance coverage also varies by plan. Our team can review the recommended treatment, explain the Harte Dental portion of the cost, and help obtain insurance estimates when appropriate so you can make an informed decision.
