What is the Procedure for Dental Implant Surgeries?

The process of replacing a damaged tooth involves the following steps:

Extraction

First, the affected tooth is carefully extracted, after which the socket is filled with a bone graft. This procedure takes about 1 to 1.5 hours to complete, after which you will receive detailed post-operative instructions.

Scan For Bone Health

Three months after the extraction, your dentist will advise you to take a CBCT Scan (cone-beam computed tomography) to check bone health. This appointment lasts 10 to 15 minutes, and there are no additional charges for it.

Implant Surgery

Once the CBCT scan results are reviewed and the bone health is confirmed to be satisfactory, surgery will be scheduled. The procedure typically lasts between 1 to 1.5 hours and is performed using an implant motor and drills. The surgery can be conducted in one of two ways: either as a two-step process, with a separate surgery for abutment placement, or as a single-step procedure where abutment placement occurs during the same surgery.

Scanning Abutment

Three months after the implant surgery, an abutment scan is done using a gold-standard 3D scanner (Ivoclar Itero) to begin the fabrication of the implant crown and send it to the lab. The Itero 3D scan is known for its accuracy, minimizing the chances o ferrors. This appointment typically lasts between 1 to 1.5 hours.

Placing Of Implant Crown

About two weeks later, the implant crown and abutment are placed after the case returns from the lab. The crown is fixed either by screw (screw retained) or using cement (cement-retained crown).

Benefits Of Dental Implants

The benefits of implants over removable dentures and fixed crowns are:

  • They give a better final result.
  • The condition of adjacent teeth is not affected as in the case of crowns and bridges.
  • Implants handle chewing forces better, significantly enhancing your ability to eat.
  • Individuals with removable dentures often experience discomfort and feel self-conscious while wearing them. They also have problems chewing and speaking, worrying about their dentures slipping and muffling their voices in public.
  • The functionality of implants is 90 % like a natural tooth.
  • The success rate of the treatment is 96 %.
  • People with dental implants do not struggle with digestive problems as with dentures. They can chew better, so the digestive tract remains healthy.
  • Dental implants improve the overall quality of life, which might not be the case with removable and fixed crowns and bridges.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How long do dental implants last? With proper oral hygiene and regular follow-up, dental implants can last a minimum of 10 years and often much longer. However, the crown may need replacement every 8 to 9 years.

How do I get started with dental implants? Book an appointment with our dental experts. During your visit, they will recommend a CBCT scan and order some baseline investigations. A 3D scan will also be performed to identify the hard and soft tissues inside your mouth.

Where will dental implant surgery be performed? Dental implant surgery can be performed in different settings depending on the complexity of the procedure. For single implants or basic procedures, the surgery can typically be carried out chairside in the dentist’s operation room under local anesthesia. However, for more extensive procedures such as full-mouth rehabilitation, it’s recommended to perform the surgery in a general operatory under general anesthesia.

Do dental implants need special care? Yes, they need extra care in terms of oral hygiene and chewing. While they allow for better chewing, it’s best to avoid biting into complex substances such as nuts, bottle caps or hard bones.

How much does a dental implant cost? The cost of dental implants varies depending on the procedure and type of dental implants to be placed. Ceramic implants are generally costlier than titanium implants, and more advanced techniques may incur additional expenses due to the specialized equipment and materials required.
