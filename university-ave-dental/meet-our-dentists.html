Meet Our Dentists

Our compassionate dentists and dedicated staff are here to ensure your utmost comfort during your dental visit. You’re in excellent hands with our team!

Dr. Logan Connor

Welcome to the office, Dr. Logan Connor! Dr. Connor is passionate about dentistry and providing exceptional care. He advocates for preventative care and uses the latest advancements in dentistry. Dr. Connor combines his skills in cosmetic dentistry to help you achieve your desired smile. With digital dentistry and AI software, he can provide same-day crowns, allowing you to leave your appointment with your permanent crown in place.

Dr. Connor was born and raised in Minnesota. He grew up in Saint Peter, MN, attended Saint John’s University, and earned his master’s degree in Arizona. After that, Dr. Connor moved to Illinois and completed his education at Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine. Following dental school, he returned to Minneapolis to pursue his passion for dentistry and to be a part of the Twin Cities community.

In his spare time, you can find him soaking up the sun on a lake, shanking golf balls into the woods at a local golf course, or enjoying a brewery with some friends. He’s slowly becoming a foodie and loves finding new foods to cook and restaurants to indulge in. Let him know your recommendations!

Dr. Connor’s goal is to make your dental experience comfortable and stress-free. Dentistry can be pretty complex and overwhelming, so Dr. Connor likes to take the time to explain everything about your smile and create a customized plan to keep you healthy and smiling. He’s excited to work with you!

Dr. Ryan Lembke

Dr. Ryan Lembke was born and raised in Chaska, Minnesota. He received a bachelor’s degree from St. John’s University in Collegeville, MN. Then, he graduated with honors from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry.

Dr. Lembke appreciates all aspects of general dentistry, including tooth-colored fillings, same-day crowns, root canals, oral surgery, and implant restoration and placement. He is motivated to get to know patients individually, working with you to achieve and maintain a healthy, beautiful smile.

Dr. Lembke, a true Minnesotan at heart, enjoys spending time outdoors with friends and family. His hobbies include running, fishing, camping, pickleball, and cross-country and downhill skiing.

Dr. Mikaela Maier

Hey there! I’m Mikaela Maier, a dentist with a passion for creating smiles and improving oral health. Originally from Brooklyn Park, MN, I pursued my undergraduate degree and Doctorate of Dental Surgery at the University of Minnesota. Afterward, I delved into an Advanced Education of General Dentistry program with Health Partners and Regions Hospital, honing my skills to provide top-notch care.

When I’m not in the dental chair, you can find me cherishing moments with my niece, nephew, and loyal dog. I thrive on adventures, whether it’s traveling, diving into a good book, belting out tunes at karaoke, or soaking up the sun while boating on the lake.

Meeting new people every day is a highlight of my job. It’s a privilege to connect with patients, understand their needs, and help them achieve optimal oral health. Let’s embark on a journey to a healthier, happier smile together!
