Why Choose CandidPro ?

Convenience: Remote monitoring through the ProMonitoring app reduces the number of in-office visits.

Versatility: Effective for mild, moderate, and even complex orthodontic cases in patients of all ages.

Discretion: Clear, comfortable trays blend into your smile and can often work with fewer attachments than other aligner systems.

Trusted Results: Backed by FDA-cleared technology and crafted from Zendura FLX , a safe, BPA-free, medical-grade plastic designed for comfort and durability.

CandidPro vs. Invisalign

Both Invisalign and CandidPro use clear aligners to gently move your teeth. The main difference? CandidPro offers the convenience of remote check-ins, giving you flexibility without sacrificing safety or results.

Treatment Timeline

Every smile is unique. Some patients start to see results in just a few months, while more advanced cases may take a year or longer. With consistent wear and our team’s guidance, you’ll be on track toward the smile you’ve been waiting for.
