The Dental Implant Process: The process of getting dental implants involves several steps, typically completed over a few months. After a thorough evaluation and planning, the implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone. There is then a healing period, allowing the implant to integrate with the bone. Once healed, an abutment is placed on the implant, and finally, a custom-crafted crown is attached, completing your new smile.

Discover the life-changing benefits of dental implants at Restore Dental Studio. Contact us today to schedule a consultation and take the first step towards a restored, confident smile.

  • Durability and Stability: Because they fuse to your jawbone, implants offer unmatched stability and can last a lifetime with proper care.
  • Improved Oral Health: Unlike other tooth replacement options, implants do not require altering adjacent teeth, preserving more of your natural tooth structure.
  • Prevention of Bone Loss: Implants help maintain jawbone density and prevent the bone loss that occurs when teeth are missing.
  • Enhanced Confidence: Dental implants can restore your smile and confidence, allowing you to eat, speak, and laugh without worry.
