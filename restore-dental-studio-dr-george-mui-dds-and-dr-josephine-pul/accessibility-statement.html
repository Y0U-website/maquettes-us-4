Accessibility Statement

Continuous Improvement: We are continually working to improve the accessibility of our website. Our team regularly reviews the site to implement improvements and ensure compliance with the latest accessibility standards.

Feedback and Contact Information: We welcome feedback from our users on how we can improve the accessibility of our website. If you encounter any issues or have suggestions, please contact us. We value your input and are committed to making our website as accessible as possible.

Training and Awareness: Our staff is informed about the importance of digital accessibility and best practices for maintaining an accessible online presence.

At Restore Dental Studio, we believe that everyone should have equal access to online information and services. We are dedicated to ensuring that our website is accessible to all users, and we strive to provide a digital experience that is inclusive and effective for everyone. If you need assistance or have any questions about our website’s accessibility, please do not hesitate to contact us.

  • Keyboard Navigation: Our site is navigable by keyboard for users who are unable to use a mouse. This includes accessible drop-down menus, forms, and other interactive elements.
  • Text Alternatives for Non-Text Content: We provide text alternatives for non-text content, such as images, to ensure that all information is accessible to users who rely on assistive technologies.
  • Resizable Text and Contrast Options: Users can adjust text size and contrast settings for enhanced readability and visual comfort.
  • Accessible Forms and Input Fields: Forms and input fields on our website are designed to be accessible, with clear labels and instructions.
