Michael Spencer, DDS

Dr. Michael Spencer attended Jacksonville University for his undergraduate degree, graduating cum laude in 1981. Then went to Emory University, where he earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree. His professional goal is to be continuously seeking the best and most technologically advanced materials, techniques and equipment to diagnose and prevent dental disease and to restore teeth so that they look, feel and function like natural teeth.

“Each and every member of our staff has a true commitment and passion to do and be the best for each patient every day. We take great pride in the office mission of preventing disease and showing each patient how to keep their teeth for a lifetime. I love what I do and am honored to serve the dental needs of our patients.”

Dr. Spencer has additional specific training in the diagnosis and treatment of TMJ disorders from the University of Florida. He is a member of the Florida Dental Association, American Dental Association and Pierre Fauchard Academy. He is an avid golfer and football fan and is proud of his beautiful wife of 40 years, mother of their four children, and grandmother to six grandchildren.
