Meet Dr. Chuck Miller

Dr. Chuck Miller has been a cornerstone of Raintree Dental since January 2017 and proudly assumed ownership of the practice in 2019. With a deep-seated passion for dentistry, Dr. Miller finds great fulfillment in helping people achieve optimal oral health and enjoys the intricate, hands-on nature of the work. His commitment to patient care and excellence in dental practice is evident in every aspect of his role.

Outside the office, Dr. Miller leads an active and fulfilling life. He finds joy in boating, tackling home renovation projects, and spending quality time with his wife Sara and their two children, Evan and Ava.

Among his many accomplishments, Dr. Miller is proud to have been a part of two Drum Corps International World Champion teams, winning first place in 1996 and 1999.
