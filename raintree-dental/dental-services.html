Clear Orthodontic Aligners

Want a straighter smile without metal braces? Clear aligner therapy at Raintree Dental offers a comfortable, discreet way to align your teeth using a series of custom-made, removable trays. Clear aligners gradually move your teeth into a better position over time—helping improve your smile, bite, and confidence. They’re designed to fit your lifestyle, so you can take them out to eat, brush, floss, or for special occasions. Benefits of clear aligners:

  • Nearly invisible and easy to wear
  • Comfortable, smooth trays (no brackets or wires)
  • Removable for eating and oral hygiene
  • Fewer office visits than traditional braces
  • Great for teens and adults
  • SureSmile & Candid Pro

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a permanent solution for missing teeth. They provide a strong foundation for fixed or removable replacement teeth that are made to match your natural teeth.

Veneers

Veneers are thin, custom-made shells crafted of tooth-colored materials designed to cover the front side of teeth. They can improve the appearance of teeth that are worn, chipped, discolored, or misaligned.

Dental Bridges

Dental bridges are used to fill the gap created by one or more missing teeth. A bridge is made up of two or more crowns for the teeth on either side of the gap and a false tooth/teeth in between.

Dentures

Complete or partial dentures are removable appliances that can replace missing teeth and help restore your smile. They make it easier to eat and speak better than you could without teeth.
