Dr. Marisa Chanin

She continued her education and received her doctorate from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine in Downers Grove, Illinois. In her senior year, she was honored to receive the International College of Dentist’s Humanitarian Award for her service throughout dental school. She then completed her pediatric dental training at Nova Southeastern University. Dr. Chanin is currently a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the Florida Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, and the American Dental Association.

Dr. Chanin loves spending her free time with her husband, Tai and her dog, Riley. She also loves trying new food, traveling, and spending time with her family and friends.
