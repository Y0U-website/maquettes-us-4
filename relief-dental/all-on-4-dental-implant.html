The All-on-4 Procedure at Relief Dental

Your journey to a full, functional smile begins with a consultation at Relief Dental. Here’s what you can expect:

Step 1: Personalized Consultation

We’ll evaluate your oral health, take 3D images of your jaw, and discuss your smile goals. Dr. Washington will create a customized treatment plan just for you.

Step 2: Implant Placement

On the day of the procedure, four titanium implants are carefully placed into your jawbone. These implants act as sturdy anchors for your new teeth.

Step 3: Immediate Restoration

Unlike traditional implants that require months of healing before placing the final restoration, All-on-4 allows you to leave our office with a temporary, natural-looking arch of teeth the same day!

Step 4: Final Smile

Once your implants fully integrate with your jawbone (typically within a few months), we’ll replace the temporary teeth with a custom-designed, durable permanent set of teeth.

FAQs About All-on-4 Dental Implants

Am I a candidate for All-on-4 implants?

Most patients missing multiple teeth are excellent candidates. We’ll evaluate your oral health and jawbone density during your consultation.

How long does the All-on-4 procedure take?

The implant placement and temporary teeth restoration can often be completed in a single day.

Are All-on-4 implants better than traditional dentures?

Yes! All-on-4 provides a secure, permanent solution that looks, feels, and functions like natural teeth, unlike traditional dentures that can slip or require adhesives.

How long do All-on-4 implants last?
