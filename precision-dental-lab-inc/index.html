Selecting a dental laboratory is an important decision. When a lab fails to deliver on time, or makes errors that require adjustments and remakes, the patient naturally assumes the fault lies with the dentist. Even cases that arrive on time and fit satisfactorily do not showcase a dentist’s talents unless the finished restoration appears life-like in the mouth.

Precision Dental Laboratory has been delighting patients and practitioners for over 30 years with prosthetics that combine our precision fit with a high degree of artistry. There is really no secret to how we consistently achieve this level of quality! Simply put, it comes down to employing highly qualified people, using the best materials, and maintaining meticulous attention to detail.
