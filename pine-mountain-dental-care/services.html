Comprehensive Dental Services

From routine checkups to full smile makeovers, everything under one roof.

General & Preventive Services

Exams & Cleanings Routine dental exams and professional cleanings to keep your smile healthy and catch issues early.

Composite Fillings Tooth-colored composite fillings that restore decayed teeth naturally and blend seamlessly with your smile.

Dental Crowns Custom dental crowns that restore damaged or weakened teeth to their full strength, shape, and appearance.

Dental Bridges Fixed dental bridges that replace one or more missing teeth, restoring your bite and smile without surgery.

Dentures Custom-fitted full and partial dentures that restore your smile and chewing function when multiple teeth are missing.

Sleep Dental Appliances Custom oral appliances including the EMA (Elastic Mandibular Advancement) device that treat snoring and mild to moderate sleep apnea.

Cosmetic & Smile Makeover Services

Veneers Porcelain veneers that transform chipped, stained, or misshapen teeth into a stunning, natural-looking smile.

Implant-Supported Dentures Stable, implant-supported dentures that stay firmly in place no adhesives, no slipping, no compromises.

Invisalign Clear, removable Invisalign aligners that straighten your teeth discreetly without metal brackets or wires.

Botox Expertly administered Botox to smooth fine lines and relieve jaw tension, TMJ discomfort, and teeth-grinding from dentists who know facial anatomy.

Microneedling A minimally invasive treatment that boosts collagen to improve skin tone, texture, and radiance with little to no downtime.

Microneedling with PRF Microneedling enhanced with Platelet-Rich Fibrin from your own blood for faster healing and a naturally refreshed, radiant glow.

All-on-X Implants A complete arch of fixed teeth supported by four to six implants secured in place and never removed.

3 on 6 Implants A fixed full arch built as three separate bridges over six implants permanent, and simpler to service one section at a time.

Oral Surgery & Implants Services

Dental Implants Permanent dental implants that replace missing teeth from the root up, looking and functioning like natural teeth.

Extractions & PRF Bone Grafting Gentle tooth extractions paired with PRF bone grafting to preserve your jaw and set the stage for future implants.

Sinus Lift A bone graft that rebuilds height in the upper jaw, making dental implants possible where there was not enough bone.

Advanced Technology

iTero Scanner Fast, comfortable digital impressions with no messy putty for crowns, Invisalign, and more.

SIRIOS X3 Scanner High-precision intraoral scanning for better-fitting restorations and clear aligner planning.

Rayface 3D facial scanning that helps us plan esthetic treatments around your unique features.

Rayscan CBCT Low-dose 3D cone-beam imaging for precise implant placement and safe oral surgery.

Biolase Laser Dentistry Laser-assisted treatment that often means less bleeding, fewer stitches, and a more comfortable recovery than a scalpel.
