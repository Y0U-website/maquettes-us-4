0 Dr. Justen Toner DDS Compassionate. Loyal. Driven.

Dr. Toner takes pride in providing top tier dentistry for his patients through advanced techniques and with the aid of state-of-the art technology. He is recognized by colleagues and specialists across southeast Wisconsin for his thorough and gentle care. Dr. Toner is a member of the American Dental Association and Wisconsin Dental Association.

Dr. Toner is a graduate of Augustana University of Sioux Falls, SD with a degree in Biology. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Marquette University in 2004 and has served the Milwaukee community every since!

Dr. Toner enjoys spending time with Jax in his free time they enjoy many outdoor activities including fishing, boating, riding ATV’s, jet skis, and snowmobiles.
