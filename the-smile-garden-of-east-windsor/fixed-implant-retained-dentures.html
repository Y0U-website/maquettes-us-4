The Benefits of Fixed Implant-Retained Dentures

Improved Comfort and Function With fixed implant-supported dentures, you’ll experience enhanced comfort. Unlike traditional dentures that can shift or rub, these implants stay firmly in place, allowing you to chew and speak without discomfort.

Enhanced Confidence Knowing your dentures are securely anchored in your mouth, you’ll feel more confident in social situations. You won’t have to worry about your dentures slipping or making you self-conscious while speaking or eating.

Long-Term Durability Fixed implants are designed to last for years, offering you a long-term solution for tooth replacement. With proper care, your new dentures will function like natural teeth, helping you avoid frequent adjustments or replacements.

What to Expect From Fixed Implant-Retained Dentures

Consultation and 3D Scan

The first step is to schedule a consultation with Dr. Garfinkle. During this appointment, we’ll use our state-of-the-art 3D imaging system to assess your bone quality and determine the ideal placement for the implants. This ensures the best fit for your new dentures and helps us plan the procedure precisely.

Implant Placement

Once we’ve determined you’re a good candidate, the next step is placing the dental implants. Dr. Garfinkle will carefully insert the implants into your jawbone, ensuring they’re positioned for optimal function and durability. Most patients experience only mild discomfort during this procedure.

Healing and Final Denture Placement

After your implants are placed, it’s important to allow time for healing. Over the next few months, the implants will fuse with your bone, creating a solid foundation for your dentures. Once healed, your permanent fixed dentures will be attached, and you’ll be ready to enjoy your new smile with full chewing power.
