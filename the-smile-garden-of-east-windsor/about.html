Get to Know Your Dentist

Dr. Arielle Garfinkle

Dr. Garfinkle is dedicated to changing the way you view dental visits. Her approach is judgment-free, wellness-focused, and completely personalized.

Originally from Philadelphia, Dr. G knew from a young age that she wanted to be a dentist—one who truly listens to patients and makes their visits relaxed, comfortable, and even enjoyable. After earning her dental degree from Temple University, she completed her residency in Grand Junction, Colorado, before making Windsor her home.

Dr. G blends advanced technology with a holistic perspective, ensuring her patients receive high-quality care with an extra layer of attention to their overall health. She is married to her childhood friend, Isaac, and they are having the best time raising their two boys, William and David.

Giving Back With Purpose

Supporting Our Community’s Wellness

We believe oral health and whole-body wellness should be accessible to everyone. That’s why we’ve partnered with Vegetable Connection , an organization that provides CSA shares, cooking lessons, and nutrition education to families in need. With every new patient, we&#x27;ll be donating a portion to the organization. Your visit with us makes a difference!

We also support local farmers that use sustainable growing methods to uplift our agricultural community.

Through these initiatives, we’re helping people make healthier choices, learn more about their nutrition, and build strong, vibrant smiles. Want to get involved? Ask us how!
