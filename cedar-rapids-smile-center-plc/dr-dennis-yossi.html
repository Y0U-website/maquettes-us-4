What Is the MAGD Designation?

The Mastership Award (MAGD) is the highest honor a general dentist can achieve from the Academy of General Dentistry. It requires completion of more than 1,100 hours of continuing education courses in 16 dental disciplines. Fewer than 2 % of general dentists nationwide hold this award. For you, it means your care is guided by someone who has invested deeply in staying current with the best techniques and materials available.
