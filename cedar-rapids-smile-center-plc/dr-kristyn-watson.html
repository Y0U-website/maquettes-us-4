Children s & Family Dentistry

Parents appreciate Dr. Watson’s gentle approach with kids. She provides children’s and family dentistry for toddlers through teens, including exams, cleanings, sealants, and guidance on habits. Her goal is to make each visit positive so children grow up comfortable at the dentist.

Cosmetic Dentistry & Dentures

Dr. Watson also offers cosmetic improvements like Invisalign, veneers, and professional whitening. For patients needing dentures, she designs both partial and full dentures plus implant-supported options. She works with you to find a solution that fits your budget and lifestyle.

Dr. Watson takes time to listen. She knows that dental visits can feel stressful, especially for new patients or those who have had difficult experiences before.
