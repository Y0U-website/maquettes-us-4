What Makes a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most people with good general health and enough jawbone density can be candidates for dental implants. Even if you have experienced bone loss, we may be able to rebuild the area with a bone graft. Our team will evaluate your oral health, medical history, and goals during a consultation to determine if implants are the right fit for you. We welcome patients of all ages, and we are happy to answer any concerns you have about the process.

What Happens If You Don t Replace a Missing Tooth?

Leaving a missing tooth unfilled can lead to problems that go beyond appearance. The jawbone beneath the gap begins to shrink because it no longer has a tooth root to stimulate it. Neighboring teeth may tilt into the empty space, throwing off your bite and making cleaning more difficult. Over time, this can increase the risk of decay, gum disease, and even further tooth loss. Replacing the tooth early helps preserve your oral health and avoid more complex treatment down the road.

Your Dental Implant Process at Cedar Rapids Smile Center

We know that the thought of a surgical procedure can feel overwhelming. That is why we take the time to explain every step and make sure you are comfortable before, during, and after treatment.

Initial Consultation and Treatment Planning

Your first visit is all about you. We will examine your mouth, take digital images using our iTero scanner, and discuss your smile goals. We will also talk about your health history and any concerns you have. If you are a candidate, we will map out a custom treatment plan and review costs and financing options so there are no surprises.

Implant Placement and Healing

The implant placement is done in our office under local anesthesia. Many patients describe the experience as easier than they expected. After the implant is placed, a healing period of a few months allows the bone to grow around the post — a process called osseointegration. During this time you can wear a temporary restoration so you are never without a tooth.

Placing Your Crown and Final Restoration

Once the implant is fully integrated, we take a final impression and create your permanent crown. The crown is attached to the implant with a small connector called an abutment. The result is a tooth that looks, feels, and functions like the real thing. You can brush, floss, and eat normally again.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants in Cedar Rapids

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The crown may need replacement after 10–15 years of normal wear, but the implant itself is designed to be permanent.

Does the dental implant procedure hurt?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during and after the procedure. We use local anesthesia to keep you comfortable, and many patients describe the recovery as easier than expected.

How many appointments does the implant process take?

Typically the entire process takes three to four appointments spread over several months: a consultation, the implant placement, a healing check, and finally placement of the crown. The timeline depends on how quickly your bone heals.

Do you accept dental insurance for implants?

Yes, we welcome all dental insurance and provide courtesy billing to help you maximize your benefits. Coverage varies by plan; we can verify your specific benefits when you schedule a consultation.

Can I get a single tooth implant, or do you only do full-arch cases?

Yes. We place single-tooth implants for patients who are missing just one tooth. We also offer implant-supported dentures for those needing multiple teeth or full-arch replacement.
