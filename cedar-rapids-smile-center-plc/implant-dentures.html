An implant-supported denture is a replacement for a full arch of missing teeth that is secured by dental implants rather than resting on the gums. Unlike conventional dentures, which can slip or shift, these dentures attach to implants placed in the jawbone. This creates a stable foundation and a secure fit. The result is a more confident smile and better chewing ability.

How Snap-On and Full-Arch Options Work

There are two main ways implant-supported dentures attach. With snap-on dentures (often called overdentures), the denture clicks onto attachments on the implants. You can remove them for cleaning, but they stay firmly in place during daily use. Full arch implants use a fixed bridge or denture that is screwed directly onto four or more implants. This option is not removable by the patient and feels most like natural teeth. Both methods provide strong stability and help preserve jawbone density, unlike traditional dentures.

Why Implant Dentures May Be Right for You — Benefits & Value

Stability, Comfort, and Long-Term Oral Health

Implant-supported dentures are designed to stay put. Because they are anchored to the jawbone, you can bite into an apple, laugh, or speak without worry. The implants also stimulate the bone, which helps prevent the bone loss that often occurs after tooth loss. Your facial structure stays more natural, and you avoid sores or irritation caused by dentures rubbing on gums.

What Happens If You Leave Missing Teeth Untreated

We understand that losing teeth is hard. If you have been living with missing teeth or struggling with loose dentures, you may notice changes over time. The jawbone can shrink, causing your face to look older. Remaining teeth may shift, making chewing difficult. These changes can affect your nutrition and confidence. Implant-supported dentures help stop that cycle. They give you back a full smile and the ability to eat the foods you enjoy, without the discomfort of slipping dentures.

Implant-Supported Dentures vs. Your Other Options

To help you compare, here is how implant-supported dentures stack up against traditional dentures and individual dental implants.

Traditional dentures are a more affordable starting point, but they do not preserve bone or offer the same chewing power. Single dental implants are ideal for one or a few missing teeth, but for a full arch, implant-supported dentures provide an efficient, stable solution. If you are unsure which path fits you, we will review your goals and oral health together. For more detail, visit our pages on dentures and dental implants .

Frequently Asked Questions About Implant Dentures in Cedar Rapids

Am I a candidate for implant-supported dentures?

Most patients with enough jawbone density and healthy gum tissue are candidates. Even if you have experienced some bone loss, grafting may expand your options. A consultation with our team gives you a clear answer.

How do snap-on dentures differ from fixed implant dentures?

Snap-on dentures are removable and attach to the implants with connectors. Fixed implant dentures are permanently screwed in and cannot be removed at home. Fixed dentures feel more like natural teeth, while snap-on dentures are easier to clean.

Does insurance cover implant-supported dentures?

Coverage varies by plan. We accept all dental insurance and file claims as a courtesy. Our front office can check your benefits and explain what is covered before you commit to treatment.

How long do implant-supported dentures last?

The implants themselves can last a lifetime with good oral hygiene and regular checkups. The denture part typically needs replacement every 10 to 15 years. If you already have a denture that needs repair, we can help with that too at our denture repairs service.
