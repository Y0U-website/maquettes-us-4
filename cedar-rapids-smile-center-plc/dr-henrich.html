Dr. Daniel Henrich, DDS

Conservative Dentistry — What It Means for You

Conservative dentistry means doing only what is necessary to keep your smile healthy and functional. Dr. Henrich focuses on minimally invasive treatments, recommending the smallest filling, the gentlest cleaning, or the most appropriate preventive measure for your specific situation. You will never feel pressured into a treatment plan you are not ready for.

A Focus on Patient Education

Dr. Henrich is committed to patient education because informed patients make better decisions. Expect clear explanations of what we find, what it means, and what your options are. We want you to feel confident about your care, not confused by jargon. It is a core part of how Dr. Henrich practices.

Pediatric Screenings & Family-Friendly Care

Dr. Henrich has a special interest in pediatric screenings and creating a positive dental experience for children. The team at Cedar Rapids Smile Center is trained to help kids feel comfortable and safe. Routine exams and cleanings for children include gentle screenings to catch small issues early. For more on how we care for young smiles, visit our Children’s Dentistry page.

Dr. Henrich s Background & Credentials

Dr. Henrich earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Iowa College of Dentistry and joined Cedar Rapids Smile Center in 2014. He practices alongside Dr. Dennis Yossi, DDS, MAGD and Dr. Kristyn Watson, DDS , focusing on conservative dentistry, patient education, and pediatric screenings as a key part of the practice’s whole-family care approach.
