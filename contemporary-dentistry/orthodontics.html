Frequently Asked Questions

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment time varies depending on your needs, but many patients complete Invisalign treatment within 6 to 18 months, while traditional braces may take significantly longer. It just depends on how severe your case is and your personal preferences and willingness to comply with the wearing regimen.

Is Invisalign as effective as braces?

For many cases, yes. Invisalign is highly effective for mild to moderate alignment issues. But again, every case is different and there is a number of factors that need to be considered. At Contemporary Dentistry we help you weigh your options and make most effective decision, both from your oral health, overall well-being and, of course, the cost perspective.

Can adults get orthodontic treatment?

Yes, orthodontic treatment is suitable for both teens and adults. Yes, you probably see more teenagers and young adults with orthodontic braces. But the technology, the accessibility and cultural norms are shifting and you might actually see quite a few adults, sometimes middle-aged adults wearing braces. It&#x27;s even more common for Invisalign, except that because these aligners are clear and practically invisible, you can&#x27;t really tell how many people are wearing them. But believe us, there are plenty.

Will orthodontic treatment be uncomfortable?

Traditional braces may cause more lasting discomfort, while Invisalign aligners may cause some discomfort for a couple of days after you switch to a new tray. You may experience mild pressure as teeth shift, but Invisalign is generally more comfortable than traditional braces.
