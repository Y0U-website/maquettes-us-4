Dental implants are small, biocompatible posts placed in the jawbone to act as artificial tooth roots. Once integrated with the bone, they provide a secure foundation for a custom restoration. Dental implants can be used to: Replace a single missing tooth
  • Support multiple teeth or bridges
  • Stabilize full-arch restorations
  • Restore both function and appearance

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants are widely considered the most advanced tooth replacement option due to their durability and functionality.

Compared to alternatives like dentures , implants offer greater stability and convenience.

  • Long-lasting, stable solution for missing teeth
  • Preservation of jawbone structure and density
  • Natural look and feel
  • No reliance on neighboring teeth for support
  • Improved chewing, speech, and comfort

The Dental Implant Process

The implant process is carefully planned to ensure precision and long-term success.

Why dental implants?

If you are missing teeth or frustrated by loose dentures, dental implants offer a permanent solution to restore your chewing and confidence. These restorations function like natural teeth and support your long-term oral health. A dental implant serves as a replacement tooth root that provides a stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture. These biocompatible posts restore essential functions like chewing and speaking while preventing the bone loss that often follows tooth removal. By anchoring directly into the jaw, they function as a permanent part of your anatomy.

How are holistic dentistry techniques applied during dental implant procedures?

Our holistic philosophy addresses material concerns by prioritizing biocompatible, metal-free options, where appropriate. We use ozone therapy to disinfect surgical sites and support natural healing, also where clinically appropriate.

How long does the dental implant process take from start to finish?

The dental implant process typically spans three to nine months, depending on your body’s specific healing rate and the complexity of your case. This timeline is necessary for osseointegration, which is the process where the implant post fuses permanently with your natural jawbone. If your treatment plan requires preparatory steps like bone grafting or extractions, the total timeline may be extended to ensure a stable and healthy foundation for the final crown.

What happens if I need a tooth extraction before the implant?

If a damaged or decayed tooth is still in place, it must be removed before the implant process can proceed. In many cases, we can perform the extraction and place the implant during the same appointment to minimize your recovery time. This include services for wisdom teeth removal if those teeth are causing crowding or bone issues that impact the success of your new dental implants.

Are ceramic (aka zirconia) implants right for me?

Ceramic zirconia implants are an excellent metal-free alternative for patients with metal sensitivities or those who prefer a holistic approach to their dental care. These implants are highly biocompatible and provide a natural white appearance at the gum line, which is ideal for patients with thin or recessed gums. We will evaluate your specific bite force and the location of the missing tooth to determine if zirconia or traditional titanium is the most durable choice for your long-term health.

How do I know if I am a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with missing teeth are candidates for dental implants if they have sufficient jawbone density and healthy gum tissue to support the restoration. During your initial consultation, we will use 3D imaging to evaluate your bone structure and discuss your medical history, as certain chronic conditions or smoking can affect the healing process. Even if you have experienced bone loss, preparatory treatments like bone grafting can often restore your candidacy for a successful implant.
