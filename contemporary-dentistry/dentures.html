Dentures are custom-made appliances that replace missing teeth and surrounding tissue. They are designed to restore both function and appearance, helping you eat, speak, and smile with confidence. Dentures can be used to: Replace multiple missing teeth
  • Restore full arches of teeth
  • Improve facial structure and support
  • Enhance overall smile aesthetics

Types of Dentures

We offer several types of dentures to meet different levels of tooth loss and patient preferences.

Each option is customized to ensure a secure fit and natural appearance.

  • Full dentures for complete tooth replacement
  • Partial dentures for patients with some remaining natural teeth
  • Implant-supported dentures for added stability using dental implants

FAQs

How long do dentures last?

Dentures typically last several years, but may need adjustments or replacement over time due to natural changes in your mouth.

Will dentures look natural?

Yes, modern dentures are designed to closely resemble natural teeth in both appearance and fit.

Are dentures comfortable?

With proper fitting and adjustments, dentures can be very comfortable. Implant-supported options offer additional stability.

Can dentures be secured more firmly?

Yes, implant-supported dentures provide increased stability and help prevent movement.
