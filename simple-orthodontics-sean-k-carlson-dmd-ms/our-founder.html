Our Founder — Sean K. Carlson, DMD, MS

Sean K. Carlson, DMD, MS

Why he built Simple.

After more than 25 years in orthodontics, Dr. Carlson had a clear view of what was working — and what wasn&#x27;t.

The standard model was built around frequent visits, reactive adjustments, and a process that asked families to organize their lives around appointments.

Not just better technology. Better thinking.

That belief became the foundation for a new care model, called SmartCare™. Simple Orthodontics was the first practice built on it. A practice built from the beginning around planning more carefully, monitoring more thoughtfully, and designing an experience that fits into real life.

Fewer visits. More precision. A schedule built around a family&#x27;s life, not the other way around.

His background.

Dr. Carlson earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine from Harvard University and his Orthodontic Degree and Master of Science in Oral Biology from the University of California, San Francisco.

He joined his first private practice in Mill Valley in 1998, eventually acquiring and growing it into a recognized regional brand over two decades.

He has lectured globally — from California to Russia, Europe, and Brazil — and created the first course of its kind on CBCT imaging in orthodontics, teaching more than 500 orthodontists how to integrate 3D technology into clinical practice.

Before orthodontics.

He struggled early in school, but found his footing in art, music, and creative work.

In high school, he played in a punk band called Unskilled Labor with close friends Tom Hamma and Richie Bucher. They shared stages with a young Billie Joe Armstrong — long before Green Day was Green Day. Richie Bucher later drew the cover art for Green Day&#x27;s debut album Dookie.

He did not start out planning to be a doctor. He started out making things.
