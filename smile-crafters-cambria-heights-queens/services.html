Dental Cleanings

At Smile Crafters, our dentists, hygienists, and periodontists deliver superior in-house treatment and provide patients of all ages with advice on best at-home cleaning practices. With the advice and treatment of our dental hygienists, we strive to keep your insurance costs down, taking a preventative approach and eliminating concerns before they have the chance to create further issues leading to potentially costly dental treatment.

3D Dental X-ray Although digital dental x-rays can capture two-dimensional pictures of your teeth, 3D oral imaging provides a full 360-degree panoramic view. 3D imaging allows our dental professionals at Smile Crafters to assess the full extent of potential oral health conditions or possible damage to your teeth, mouth, skull, and jaws.

Laser Dentistry Laser dentistry care in New York City provided by Smile Crafters, revolutionizes dental procedures, making them quicker and less painful. This includes a range of treatments from cosmetic laser dentistry to advanced laser cosmetic dentistry, showcasing the versatility and efficacy of laser technology in dental care.

Digital Dental Scanning The digital scanner uses a laser-emitting wand to capture hundreds of images per second, which are then stitched together to create a 3D model of the patient s mouth. Digital scanner is designed to be small enough to reach the patient s molars, even if their mouth doesn t open very wide. The scanner can generate a full arch scan in as little as 30 seconds.

Full Arch Implants With a full arch dental implant, the dental implants are surgically placed into the bone so that a prosthetic crown and bridge (the visible part of the tooth) can be firmly attached to it. Using state-of-the-art technology, the crowns and bridges are made to fit the exact opening in your smile.

Teeth Whitening We use ZOOM teeth whitening to change the color of your teeth from three to four shades, soemtimes more. Professional, in-office Power Teeth Bleaching technology is the most researched and scientifically proven way to whiten the teeth in the shortest time and the greatest number of shades.
