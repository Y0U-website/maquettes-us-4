Benefits of sedation:

  • Eliminates pain, anxiety and stress
  • Allows for total comfort during lengthy procedures
  • Makes the patient unaware of the surrounding and ongoing surgery
  • Is safe when administered by a trained professional
  • Allows for the oral surgery procedure to be performed more safely
  • Allows for fewer appointments since the patient is comfortable and can tolerate the needed procedure.

Sedation and Anesthesia Safety

IV Conscious Sedation

Most people would prefer not to remember their wisdom teeth removal surgery. Sedation is a great way to accomplish this. Wisdom teeth are in the back of the mouth and it can be difficult for many patients to tolerate having them removed while fully conscious. To help patients forget or “sleep” through the experience, we provide the option of IV sedation. This helps our patients feel as if they completely slept through the whole procedure. IV sedation is especially useful for patients who are young, fearful or anxious.
