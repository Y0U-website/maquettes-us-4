The practice

She is prepared to treat a wide variety of children’s dental problems and is also trained and qualified to care for patients with medical, physical or mental disabilities.

Our practice is located in Beverly Hills, Michigan and provides dental care for children from infancy through adolescence. We offer elevator access at the side of the building to accomodate strollers and wheelchairs.

Children are very adaptable and can deal with oral pain on an everyday basis if it’s what they’ve become accustomed to. Dr. Cari is an expert at diagnosing dental problems that could or may already be causing your child pain. A healthy mouth enhances your child’s ability to maintain overall general good health.

You’ll find our office to be a safe, caring and comfortable environment for our patients and their parents. Our caring and highly trained staff will provide a positive dental experience for your child.

Our Mission is to provide a safe environment and quality dental care to keep your child as happy as possible. Also, to teach you and them about proper oral hygiene procedures and how to prevent cavities.

We will ensure that your child’s dental visits are a positive experience, and will help to enable their ability to eat, chew, smile, learn and play.
