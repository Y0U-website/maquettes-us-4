Meet the Doctor

Dentist in Littleton, CO Meet the Doctor Contact Us Our Doctor Dr. Walt Vogl, General Dentist Before earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2004 from the University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine and beginning his private practice career, Dr. Vogl worked as a mechanic at United Airlines in San Francisco. During that time, he began attending the University of California at Davis and received his Bachelor of Science degree in molecular genetics in 1999.

Dr. Vogl is dedicated to providing uncompromising care to each of his patients. He chose to pursue dentistry because of his deep desire to positively impact the oral and overall health of those in his care and to make a difference in their lives. No matter the aspect of general dentistry he’s performing (preventive, restorative, or cosmetic-enhancement care), his focus is on delivering service that’s compassionate, patient-centric, and effective.

Outside the Office When he’s not caring for smiles, Dr. Vogl and his wife Patti enjoy kite surfing, biking, and spending time with Killer, their 115-pound Rhodesian Ridgeback/Coonhound/Boxer mix. Dr. Vogl also loves to fly airplanes and ride and race superbikes!
