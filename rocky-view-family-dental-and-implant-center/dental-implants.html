What to Expect from the Dental Implant Process

Getting dental implants in Littleton is a multi-step process designed to deliver optimal results. Here’s a basic rundown of what you can expect:

Initial Consultation

During this visit, we’ll evaluate your oral health, take X-rays, and discuss your specific needs and goals. If you require additional preparatory procedures like bone grafting, we’ll include that in your treatment plan.

Implant Placement

Dr. Walt Vogl will surgically place the titanium implant into your jawbone. This procedure is carried out with precision and care to ensure minimal discomfort and a smooth recovery.

Healing Period

Your jawbone will fuse with the implant over the next few months. This ensures stability and a strong foundation for the replacement tooth.

Final Restoration

Once healed, we will attach the custom-made crown, bridge, or denture to the implant post. The result? A flawless smile that looks and feels completely natural.

How to Care for Your Dental Implants

After your procedure, proper care is essential for ensuring long-term success. Here are a few tips to keep your implants in great shape:

Following these simple steps helps ensure your implants last a lifetime!

  • Maintain Good Oral Hygiene: Brush twice a day, floss daily, and use an antibacterial mouthwash to prevent plaque build-up around the implant.
  • Regular Check-ups: Scheduling routine visits to Rocky View Family Dental and Implant Center helps us monitor your implants and overall oral health.
  • Avoid Hard Foods: While implants are intense, excessive pressure from ice or hard candies can cause damage over time.
