Meet Our Skilled Dentist

Alexander Gatten , DMD

Dr. Gatten completed his periodontics residency at the VA Richard L. Roudebush Medical Center in Indianapolis. During his specialty training, he developed a strong passion for oral surgery through extensive hands-on experience with surgical procedures. He’s excited to bring his expertise and steady hand to our team, supporting patients with comprehensive surgical care.

Before pursuing a career in dentistry, Dr. Gatten served for eight years as an aircraft operator in the U.S. Navy. His dedication to service, precision, and discipline now carries over into his work with patients. A proud graduate of the University of Louisville, Dr. Gatten has a special interest in sedation dentistry, dental implant placement, and complex tooth extractions.

He brings a compassionate approach and a deep commitment to clinical excellence. Dr. Gatten is known for building strong, personal connections with patients and delivering care that is both skillful and empathetic. Outside the office, Dr. Gatten enjoys spending time with his wife, Kelsie, and their dog, Irish. Together, they love tackling CrossFit workouts, experimenting with new recipes, hitting the slopes for snowboarding adventures, and cheering on Notre Dame football in the fall.

Dr. Corrine Molter , DMD

Outside the office, Dr. Molter enjoys spending time with her partner, Ryan, who is starting his pediatric dental residency. When not in their scrubs, the two can often be found dominating the pickleball courts, visiting with their friends and family, or on stage at karaoke night! Dr. Molter is an avid reader, loves challenging herself to try new workouts and recipes, and is a self-proclaimed trivia enthusiast.
