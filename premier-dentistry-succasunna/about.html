Get to know

Dr. Yvonne Kiernan

Dr. Yvonne Kiernan grew up in New Jersey, where her passion for dentistry began with her first job as a dental assistant and later expanded through a career in dental hygiene. Driven by a desire to continually grow and advance her skills, she returned to school and graduated summa cum laude from Montclair State University with a Bachelor of Science in Biology. She then earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in Newark in 2017.

Following dental school, Dr. Kiernan completed a one-year General Practice Residency at the Lyons VA Medical Center, where she received advanced training in all aspects of general dentistry. She found it especially meaningful to care for our nation’s Veterans and remains grateful for that experience.

Dr. Kiernan believes in giving back to the community and supporting meaningful causes. She has participated in charitable fundraising efforts with the Tunnel to Towers Foundation and the 403 Reasons to Run chapter. The Tunnel to Towers Foundation supports veterans, first responders, and their families through programs that provide mortgage-free smart homes, pay off mortgages for families of fallen heroes, and offer housing assistance to those who have sacrificed in service to our country and communities.

Dr. Kiernan resides in Morris County with her husband and two teenage sons. Outside the office, she enjoys fitness training, gardening, and traveling with her family. She also enjoys practicing Taekwondo and is currently working toward her black belt. Her training includes forms, sparring, self-defense, and traditional weapons training. She values the discipline, focus, and perseverance that martial arts instill and believes these qualities complement her commitment to excellence in dentistry and patient care.

Your first visit

Personalized care in a relaxing setting

From the moment you step into our peaceful office, you’ll be greeted by our friendly team, ready to make you feel right at home. Enhancing your experience

Same-day crowns

With our VHF 4 same-day mill, we make it possible to craft and place crowns in just one visit. Say goodbye to temporary crowns and multiple appointments!

Digital scanner

No more goopy impressions! Our digital scanner provides precise, comfortable imaging for more accurate restorations and a better patient experience.

Soft tissue and all-tissue lasers

We use Fotona laser technology for treatments that are quicker, more comfortable, and require less healing time. Whether addressing TMD discomfort or providing soft-tissue care, our lasers bring advanced solutions to your dental needs.

Comfort amenities

Enjoy TVs in every operatory, cozy blankets, and neck pillows to help you feel more relaxed during your visit. Headphones are also available upon request for a more personalized and enjoyable experience.
