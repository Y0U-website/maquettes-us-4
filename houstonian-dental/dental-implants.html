Who Can Get Dental Implants?

Dental implants are the right choice for individuals who have experienced tooth loss. To get these prostheses, a person should have good oral and general health. Additionally, patients should have acceptable bone mass capable of giving strong anchorage of the implant screws. The gum tissue should also be free of gum disease to prevent issues such as peri-implantitis that could result in failed implants.

Dental Implants Procedure

Dental implants can be complex; however, dental technology is changing things today. Typically, a tooth implant procedure would involve:

The preparation part – An exam is done that provides information to aid with creating the treatment plan. X-rays also help see if there could be issues of concern, like gum disease and decay hiding underneath the gumline. Extractions are done, if required, before the implant surgery.

Implant screw placement – A sedative drug is given to help with anxiety and smooth the procedure. Our dentists insert the implant into the jawbone. A healing time of about 4 to 6 months is allowed for osseointegration.

Placing abutments – A piece that connects the artificial tooth to the implant is fitted. You also get temporary crowns for the teeth. After about 14 days, you check in for placement of the final implant crowns.

Fitting the crowns – The temporary crowns are taken out, and the permanent ones fitted.
