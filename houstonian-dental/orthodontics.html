Orthodontics in Houston, TX

Orthodontics is dedicated to the diagnosis, prevention, management, and correction of misaligned teeth and jaws. At our dentist office near you , we recognize that attaining a stunning, healthy smile involves more than just appearance. Well-aligned teeth contribute to enhanced oral health, facilitating the maintenance of good hygiene and helping to prevent potential dental problems in the future.

Common Orthodontic Problems

If you are contemplating orthodontic treatment in your vicinity, you are certainly in good company. A variety of individuals encounter prevalent orthodontic concerns such as crowded teeth, gaps between teeth, overbites, underbites, and crossbites. These issues may result in challenges related to chewing, speaking, and upholding oral hygiene. Clear Aligners provide an effective response to these issues, presenting a discreet and convenient option for both teens and adults.

Orthodontic Treatment Options

Our Houston dental clinic provides a variety of orthodontic treatments in your vicinity to cater to your specific requirements. Clear aligners, such as Invisalign, are favored by patients looking for a discreet option compared to conventional braces. These bespoke, transparent plastic aligners progressively move your teeth into their correct alignment without the necessity for brackets and wires. We offer traditional braces for individuals who may need more extensive treatment options.

Treatment Process

Treatment process starts with a consultation, during which our skilled dentists will assess your orodental condition and develop a personalized treatment plan. Once your custom aligners are ready, you will wear them as prescribed, changing them every few weeks. Over time, you’ll witness the transformation of your smile as your teeth gradually align.

Benefits of Orthodontics

The benefits of orthodontic treatment to your oral health extend beyond a radiant smile. Properly aligned teeth can enhance your self-esteem and boost oral health. You’ll find cleaning your teeth and gums easier, reducing the exposure of tooth decay and gum disease. Additionally, a well-aligned bite can alleviate issues such as jaw pain, headaches, and speech difficulties.
