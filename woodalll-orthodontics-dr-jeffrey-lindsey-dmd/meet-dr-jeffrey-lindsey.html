Beyond the Practice

I grew up in Georgia and South Carolina. My father was in the textile industry and we moved around a lot: I was in 12 different school systems from kindergarten to 12 th grade!

Settling in Carrollton in 1986, and making it my home; has been the most wonderful experience. My wife Lori and I have been married for almost 40 years. We raised our two sons, Daniel and David who are both happily married and reside in Carrollton with their families. In our spare time we are loving being Grandparents to our four wonderful Grandchildren. Lori and I also have three dogs: Cooper, who is a Pomeranian, and Flurry & Dash who are Icelandic Sheepdogs. They bring us so much joy every day.

My favorite pastime, outside of being with family and friends, is working on my farm and driving the tractor. I also dabble in beekeeping and enjoy working in my garden where I’m creating a natural environment for the deer, turkey, and other wildlife.

Lori and I enjoy traveling and continue to explore new-to-us areas every year. When I have down time, I’m an avid reader with an interest in historical and biographical works.
