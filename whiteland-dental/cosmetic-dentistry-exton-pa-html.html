Professional Teeth Whitening

We offer two whitening options: in-office whitening that delivers 6–8 shades of brightness in a single 90-minute appointment, and custom take-home trays for gradual whitening over 1–2 weeks. In-office is best when you have a wedding, presentation, or photo opportunity coming up. Take-home trays are gentler for sensitive teeth and easier to maintain long-term. Both use medical-grade hydrogen peroxide gel concentrations not available over the counter, supervised by our doctors to protect your enamel and gums. Results last 1–2 years on average, longer with maintenance.

Digital Smile Design (DSD) Technology

Before any cosmetic work begins, we use Digital Smile Design to preview your new smile. We capture facial photos, intraoral scans, and bite registrations, then digitally design the proportions, shapes, and shades that complement your unique facial features. You see a 3D rendering of your projected results before approving any treatment, eliminating the guesswork and giving you complete creative input into your own smile. This same technology lets us mock up veneers in temporary acrylic so you can wear and approve the design before we craft your final porcelain.

Common Questions

Porcelain veneers at Whiteland Dental range from $1,500 to $2,500 per tooth. A full smile makeover (typically 6–10 veneers) ranges from $9,000 to $25,000 total. We offer flexible financing and will design treatment plans that fit a range of budgets.

Veneers require minimal but irreversible preparation of the front tooth surface (about 0.3mm of enamel is removed). Once placed, they typically last 15–25 years with proper care. When they eventually need replacement, you&#x27;ll need new veneers (you can&#x27;t return to bare tooth structure).

It depends on your starting shade and tooth structure. Most patients can lift 6–10 shades through professional whitening. For dramatically brighter results beyond what whitening achieves, porcelain veneers can deliver any shade you choose, including the brightest &#x27;Hollywood white&#x27; tones.

Professional whitening supervised by our doctors is safe for enamel. We use the lowest effective concentration of hydrogen peroxide and apply protective barriers to your gums during in-office treatment. Some patients experience temporary sensitivity during whitening, but it resolves within 24–48 hours of completing treatment.

Most smile makeovers are completed in 2–4 visits over 3–6 weeks. Visit 1 is consultation, photos, and 3D scans. Visit 2 is preparation and temporary placement.
