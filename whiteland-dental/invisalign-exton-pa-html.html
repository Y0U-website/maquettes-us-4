How Invisalign Works

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, clear plastic aligners that gradually shift your teeth into their ideal position. Each aligner is worn for one week, then swapped for the next in the series. You wear them 20–22 hours a day and remove them only to eat, drink anything other than water, brush, and floss. Unlike traditional braces, there are no metal brackets, no wires to tighten, and no food restrictions. Most patients report the aligners are comfortable within 2–3 days of starting each new set.
