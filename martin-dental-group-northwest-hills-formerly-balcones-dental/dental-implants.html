The Dental Implant Process

Consultation

Your journey begins with a comprehensive consultation where our dentists in Round Rock, TX will discuss your unique needs, evaluate your oral health and determine if dental implants are the best option for you.

Implant Placement

Following your consultation, the implant is precisely placed within your jawbone, serving as the foundation for your artificial tooth. Over the next few months, the implant will fuse with your jawbone, forming a lasting bond. Once the implant has fully integrated with the jawbone, the abutment is then placed on top of the implant. This serves as the connector piece for the crown.

Final Restoration

Once the implant has successfully integrated with your jawbone, our skilled team will create and place a customized crown and attach it to the abutment. Blending with your natural teeth, this will leave you with a beautiful, functional smile.

What are the types of implants?

Dental implants come in several types, each designed to meet specific needs and conditions. The most common type is the endosteal implant, which is surgically placed directly into the jawbone. Endosteal implants are typically shaped like small screws and are the most widely used type due to their stability and effectiveness.

Another type is the subperiosteal implant, which is placed under the gum but above the jawbone. This type is used for patients who do not have enough healthy jawbone and cannot undergo a bone augmentation procedure.

Zygomatic implants are a less common option, used when there is insufficient bone in the upper jaw. These implants are anchored in the cheekbone (zygoma) rather than the jawbone.

Each type of implant has its own advantages and is selected based on the patient&#x27;s specific anatomical and health considerations. How long do implants last?

Dental implants are renowned for their durability and longevity. With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. The implant itself, which is made of biocompatible titanium, is designed to integrate with the jawbone and become a permanent part of your oral structure. The crown, or the visible part of the implant, typically lasts between 10 to 15 years before it may need replacement due to normal wear and tear.

Maintaining good oral hygiene is key to ensuring the longevity of your dental implants. Regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups can help prevent gum disease and other issues that could compromise the stability of your implants. At Martin Dental Group, Dr. Nick Martin emphasizes the importance of routine dental visits to monitor the health of your implants and address any concerns promptly.

Are dental implants painful? (Healing timeline)

The thought of undergoing a dental implant procedure can be daunting, especially when it comes to concerns about pain. However, most patients find that the discomfort associated with dental implants is minimal. During the procedure, local anesthesia is used to numb the area, ensuring that you feel little to no pain. Some patients may experience mild discomfort or swelling following the surgery, but this can usually be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers and will subside within a few days.

The healing process, known as osseointegration, is crucial for the success of dental implants. During this time, the implant fuses with the jawbone, providing a stable foundation for the replacement tooth. This process can take anywhere from three to six months, depending on individual healing rates and the quality of the bone. Throughout this period, it is essential to follow your dentist&#x27;s aftercare instructions carefully to ensure optimal healing and integration.

How much do implants cost?

When considering dental implants, one of the most common questions is about the cost. Dental implants are a significant investment in your oral health and quality of life, offering a long-term solution for missing teeth. The cost of dental implants can vary widely depending on several factors including the number of implants needed, the complexity of the procedure, and any additional treatments that might be required, such as bone grafting or sinus lifts.

On average, the cost of a single dental implant in the United States can range from $3,000 to $5,000. This price typically includes the implant post, the abutment, and the crown. It&#x27;s important to remember that while the initial outlay might seem high, dental implants are designed to be a durable, long-lasting solution, often lasting a lifetime with proper care. Many dental practices, including Martin Dental Group in Round Rock, offer financing options or payment plans to help make this life-changing investment more affordable.

How do I take care of my dental implants?

Caring for your dental implants is similar to maintaining your natural teeth. Regular oral hygiene practices like brushing twice daily and flossing, along with professional dental check-ups and cleanings, will help ensure your implants remain healthy and long-lasting.
