Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Martin

Dr. Martin is a native of Texas and grew up in Austin. He knew from a young age that he wanted to work in healthcare and after spending time with his uncle at his dental office he knew he had found the perfect profession for him.

Dr. Martin’s favorite part of his job is getting to get to know his patients over the course of their life. He truly enjoys being able to develop long term relationships with patients and help them feel comfortable in his care. It’s extremely rewarding when a nervous or skeptical patient puts their trust in our office and we’re able to deliver on our promise to provide an exceptional and comfortable experience.

Dr. Montalvo

Dr. Gavin Montalvo leads our Round Rocklocation with a focus on creating meaningful connections and deliveringexceptional patient care. Originally from Kansas City, he earned his bachelor’sdegree from Marquette University before completing his Doctor of Dental Surgeryat the University of Missouri–Kansas City School of Dentistry. He continues toexpand his expertise through advanced training in Sleep Apnea and Invisaligntherapy and has a special interest in Endodontics and Cosmetic Dentistry.

Dr. Montalvo believes that greatdentistry starts with great relationships. He takes time to listen, understandeach patient’s goals, and makes every visit comfortable and stress-free.Inspired by his own childhood dentist who made dental visits something to lookforward to, he creates a calm and welcoming environment where patients feelcared for, confident, and at ease.

Outside the office, Dr. Montalvo enjoysexploring Austin’s restaurant scene, spending time with friends and family, andrelaxing on Lake Travis. He is the proud owner of two French bulldogs and givesback to the community through volunteer work with the Texas Mission of Mercyand the Capital Area Dental Society’s free clinics.

Dr. Montalvo is committed to providingcompassionate, high-quality care and helping every patient enjoy a healthy,confident smile.

Dentistry Made Easy

Dentistry Made Easy

Office Tour

Exam

As the first step, Dr. Martin and our team will take x-rays and evaluate your mouth to look for signs of cavities and other oral health issues like gingivitis. This ensures that your mouth is healthy, and helps us choose the right kind of dental cleaning for you. If Dr. Martin notices any issues, he’ll work with you to schedule any necessary follow-up treatment.

Cleaning

Once we’ve gotten a better look at your teeth, it’s time to clean those pearly whites! Sit back, relax, and let our friendly dental team remove plaque, clean your teeth, and keep your grin looking its best. We’ll send you home with a dental “goodie bag,” and you’ll schedule any future cleanings or other appointments with our team before you head out.
