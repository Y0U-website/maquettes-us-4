  • Porcelain Veneers Veneers are very thin pieces of durable, tooth-shaped porcelain that are custom made to match the shape and color of your teeth and then bonded to the front of your teeth to create a beautiful, attractive smile. They can reshape your teeth and your smile and are an extremely durable alternative to crowns. They are the optimal solution for a number of dental conditions. Porcelain veneers are used for cosmetic purposes, especially those associated with crooked, misshapen, discolored, stained, worn, or chipped teeth.
  • Teeth Whitening Teeth whitening is a simple, non-invasive dental treatment that can change the color of natural teeth enamel. Having whiter teeth is one of the major aesthetic concerns of most patients, and teeth whitening, or bleaching, is an ideal way to brighten your smile. When considering teeth whitening, it is important to consider replacing any old fillings and crowns because teeth whitening only works on natural teeth enamel. The replacement of any restorations will take place after the whitening treatment so we can match the newly bleached teeth.
