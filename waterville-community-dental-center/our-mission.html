OUR MISSION

The mission of the Waterville Community Dental Center is to improve access to quality oral health care for low income, uninsured, underinsured and Medicaid-eligible residents of central Maine.

OUR WORK

Access to affordable oral health care is a critical resource in maintaining the health of our community. WCDC delivers a full range of oral health services including education, cleanings, oral examinations, extractions, fillings, crowns and other restorative care.

WCDC welcomes all forms of payment including MaineCare and private insurance. For income-qualified individuals it offers sliding fee reduced dental rates.
