All On X Dental Implants

All-On-X is a dental implant solution designed for individuals with severely compromised or no teeth. If you are dissatisfied with dentures or the pain of failing teeth and wish to start over with a clean slate, this treatment may be the ideal solution for you. By utilizing four to six dental implants, the All-On-X concept can replace missing or broken teeth with a fixed solution.

All-On-X implants are securely fixed in place and function much like natural teeth, providing stability and eliminating worries of teeth slipping, breaking or shifting. This allows patients to speak, laugh, and eat without any concerns.

In many cases, patients may leave with temporary teeth placed during their initial procedure. This would not be possible without our in-house digital lab. It is highly appreciated by patients, as you can heal with ideal teeth that are 100 % functional.

Snap-In Implant Dentures

Snap-In Dentures are removable by the patient for easy cleaning, but offer superior stability when compared to conventional dentures.

Dental implants can eliminate the need for the palate portion of top dentures (the part that covers the roof of the mouth), thus helping to reduce gagging sensations and diminished ability to taste food. Snap-In Dentures can also make a loose lower denture more stable, keeping it from floating around in your mouth.

Single Tooth Implants & Bridges

Dental implant restorations are prosthetic teeth that are designed to attach to your implant, and restore your smile. For Single Tooth Implants, a dental crown is usually used. This crown functions just like a real tooth and is crafted to fit into your smile perfectly. In conjunction with the titanium implant, your new tooth will function just like your natural tooth did.

A Multi-Tooth Implant Bridge is similar to a Single Tooth Implant, but is used to replace a series of failing or missing teeth. Two dental implant screws are placed into the jaw bone and a series of strong, custom teeth bridge the gap.

Single Tooth Implants and Multi-Tooth Implant Bridges can be treated just like natural teeth. Simply brush and floss your teeth normally, and your implant(s) will remain healthy and strong for years to come.

Dental implants typically last decades with proper care.
