Single Tooth Implants or Multi-Tooth Implant Bridges repair failing or missing teeth in a localized area.

If you have one or more missing teeth, you may be struggling to chew food or feel embarrassed to talk, laugh, and socialize. Missing most or all your teeth can result in changes in your facial appearance, a decline in your health, and increased discomfort and/or self-consciousness. No matter how many teeth you are missing, dental implants offer a solution to these problems.

  • Stable and natural chewing | Enjoy your favorite foods with the strength and stability of a natural tooth.

How It Works

A dental implant is a titanium screw that’s placed into your jawbone which serves as the foundation to replace a tooth or teeth.

  • Implant post (or body) | This is the foundation. It is placed in the jaw and acts as an anchor.
  • Abutment | This piece connects the implant post and crown.
  • Crown | This is the visible portion that looks like a natural tooth. This could also be an implant bridge across multiple implants.
