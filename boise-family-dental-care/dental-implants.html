Common Questions about Dental Implant

How Do Dental Implants Work?

  • Post: This component simulating the root is constructed from medical-grade titanium drilled into your jaw before fusing with surrounding bone through osseointegration.
  • Abutment: The metal connector inserts into the post and securely attaches it to the crown.
  • Crown: The final piece to your implant supplies a real tooth’s physical look and feel.
