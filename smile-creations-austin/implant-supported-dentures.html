  • Long-Lasting Solution: With proper care, implant-supported dentures can provide a permanent, durable solution that lasts for many years.

Types of Implant-Supported Dentures

At Smile Creations, we offer two main types of implant-supported dentures to meet your needs:

1. Bar-Retained Implant-Supported Dentures

In this type of implant-supported denture, a thin metal bar is attached to the dental implants in your jawbone. The denture is designed to snap onto the bar, providing a secure and stable fit. Bar-retained dentures offer excellent support while allowing for easy removal for cleaning and maintenance.

2. Ball-Retained Implant-Supported Dentures

Also known as stud-attachment dentures, ball-retained dentures use ball-shaped connectors on the dental implants that fit into sockets on the underside of the denture. This design provides a firm attachment, allowing the denture to remain securely in place while still being removable for cleaning.

Both options provide increased stability and comfort compared to traditional dentures, and Dr. Heck will work with you to determine the best option for your specific needs.

The Implant-Supported Denture Process at Smile Creations

At Smile Creations, we take a comprehensive and personalized approach to your implant-supported denture treatment to ensure the best results. Here’s what you can expect during the process:

1. Consultation and Evaluation

Your journey to implant-supported dentures begins with a thorough consultation with Dr. Matthew Heck or Dr. Annalisa Heck:

  • Oral Examination: We will evaluate your oral health, discuss your goals, and determine if implant-supported dentures are the best solution for you. Digital X-rays and 3D imaging may be used to assess the health of your jawbone and determine the ideal placement for your implants.
  • Treatment Plan: Based on your evaluation, we’ll create a customized treatment plan that outlines the steps of your procedure, including the type of implant-supported dentures that will work best for your needs.

2. Implant Placement

During the next phase of your treatment:

  • Implant Surgery: Dental implants are surgically placed into the jawbone. These implants act as the foundation for your dentures, providing stability and support.
  • Healing Period: Over the next few months, the implants will fuse with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration, creating a strong and durable base for your dentures. In some cases, temporary dentures may be placed during this healing period.

3. Attaching the Dentures

Once the implants have fully integrated with your jawbone:

  • Fitting Your Dentures: Your custom implant-supported dentures will be attached to the dental implants using either a bar or ball-retained system. Dr. Heck will ensure that the fit is secure and comfortable, providing you with a functional and natural-looking smile.
  • Final Adjustments: We will make any necessary adjustments to ensure your dentures fit properly and provide maximum comfort and function.

Caring for Your Implant-Supported Dentures

Proper care is essential to maintaining the longevity of your implant-supported dentures and ensuring your oral health remains in top condition:

  • Oral Hygiene: Just like with natural teeth, it’s important to brush and floss around your implants and clean your dentures regularly to prevent plaque buildup and ensure gum health.
  • Regular Check-Ups: Schedule regular check-ups and cleanings with Smile Creations to monitor the health of your implants and surrounding tissues.
  • Careful Cleaning: If you have removable dentures, clean them daily using a denture brush and denture cleaner, and soak them overnight. For fixed implant-supported dentures, special tools like water flossers or interdental brushes may be recommended.
