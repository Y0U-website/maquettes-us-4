Originally from Orange City, Iowa, he now calls Sioux Falls home, where he lives with his wife and their three children and enjoys helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through…

Dr. Schuiteman earned his Bachelor of Musical Arts degree from the University of South Dakota, with emphases in saxophone and choral music and minors in biology and chemistry. He went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Iowa College of Dentistry, where he received the American Academy of Oral Medicine Certificate of Merit.

Dr. Schuiteman knew early on he wanted to pursue a career in healthcare. After shadowing dentists in high school, he quickly realized dentistry combined everything he loved: science, artistry, and hands-on patient care.
