Payments

We want to make your experience as smooth as possible, which is why we gladly accept cash and checks for all our services. Whether you prefer the simplicity of paying with exact change or the traditional method of writing a check, we ve got you covered. Our team will provide you with a detailed invoice, and you can settle your payment directly with us at the time of service or according to your agreed-upon terms.

​We make it easy to pay for your treatment! We accept major credit cards, including Visa, Mastercard, and Discover.

We understand that prioritizing your dental health is important, and financial concerns shouldn t stand in the way of a healthy, beautiful smile. That s why we re proud to offer CareCredit as a flexible payment solution in our office. CareCredit is a healthcare credit card designed specifically for health and wellness expenses, allowing you to receive the treatment you need now and pay over time with convenient monthly payments. Applying is quick and easy, often with an instant decision, empowering you to proceed with essential dental care without delay.
