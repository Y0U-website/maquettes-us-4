Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults with good overall health and sufficient jawbone density are excellent candidates for implants. We evaluate your oral condition through digital imaging and a comprehensive exam. If you’ve experienced bone loss, bone grafting or ridge augmentation may be recommended to prepare the site for implant placement.

The Dental Implant Process at Foothills Family Dental

Under the skilled care of Dr. Richard J. Pass , your implant journey is guided with precision, experience, and compassion. Here’s what to expect:

We also offer implant-supported dentures for those seeking full arch restoration with added stability.

  • Consultation & Exam: We evaluate your oral health, take digital X-rays, and review your goals.
  • Treatment Planning: If necessary, we’ll recommend bone grafting before placing the implant.
  • Implant Placement: The titanium post is surgically placed into your jawbone.
  • Healing & Osseointegration: Over 3–6 months, the implant fuses with your jawbone.
  • Restoration: A natural-looking dental crown or bridge is placed to complete your smile.
