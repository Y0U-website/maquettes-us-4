Our Story and Philosophy

At Foothills Family Dental, we pride ourselves on offering a wide range of outstanding dental procedures to patients of all ages. Nestled in Old Town Fort Collins just south of Mulberry on College Avenue, our practice is built on the pillars of quality dental care and excellent service. Our mission is to make top-tier dental care accessible to everyone, integrating advanced technology and creating tailored treatment plans for a uniquely comfortable patient experience.

Our Expert Dental Team

Dr. Richard J. Pass

With over 20 years of experience in the dental field, Dr. Pass is not just a skilled dentist but also a compassionate caregiver. His journey from Yorkshire, England, to Fort Collins, CO, has been filled with academic and professional achievements. His expertise in implant dentistry and gum grafting, coupled with his dedication to global dental health, makes him an exceptional leader in our practice.

Elizabeth Fitch Beckner, D.D.S. Dr. Elizabeth Beckner is a compassionate and experienced general dentist with over a decade of clinical expertise. She is dedicated to providing comprehensive, patient-centered care for individuals and families of all ages—from children visiting the dentist for the first time to seniors needing restorative treatment.

Our Commitment to the Community As a family-oriented practice, we focus on educating our patients, from children to adults, about oral hygiene and preventative care. Our community-centric approach extends beyond the clinic, as we actively participate in local health initiatives and charity events.

Our Mission

Our goal is to ensure that every individual who walks through our doors receives comprehensive and compassionate dental care. Through our in-office dental benefits plan, we strive to make dental care affordable and accessible, especially for those without insurance.
