MEET DR. T

DR. ANTHONY TRENTACOSTE, JR., affectionately known as Dr. T, has been providing exceptional dental care to patients in Metairie, Louisiana for over three decades. With a career spanning 30 years, his passion for dentistry shines through in his gentle touch, genuine concern, and unwavering dedication to every patient who walks through the doors of Causeway Dental.

From the youngest of smiles to the most experienced grins, Dr. T treats each patient with the utmost care and compassion. His philosophy extends beyond simply delivering premium dental services; he is committed to empowering his patients with the knowledge and tools necessary to maintain a lifetime of optimal oral health and radiant smiles.

At Causeway Dental, the team believes in a holistic approach to dentistry, focusing not only on clinical excellence but also on patient education. Dr. T and his staff take the time to ensure that every individual understands the importance of dental hygiene and is equipped with the resources to sustain a beautiful, healthy smile long after leaving the office.

Dr. T’s unwavering commitment to his craft is evident in his prestigious educational background and professional affiliations. He holds a Bachelor of Science degree from Loyola University and received his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the renowned LSU School of Dentistry in 1980. Moreover, he is an active member of the American Dental Association, staying abreast of the latest advancements and best practices in the field.

With a perfect blend of expertise, compassion, and a genuine love for his profession, Dr. Anthony Trentacoste, Jr. continues to redefine the standard of dental care in Metairie, ensuring that every patient receives the highest quality of service and leaves with a renewed confidence in their smile.

CERTIFICATIONS: B.S. Loyola LSU School of Dentistry 1980
