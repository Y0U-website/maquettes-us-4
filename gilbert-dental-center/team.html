Meet the Team Gilbert Dental Center Dr. Zach Zapata Dr. Zach Zapata is an Arizona native who is passionate about helping patients feel comfortable, informed, and confident in their dental care. He earned his Doctorate of Dental Medicine from the Arizona School of Dentistry & Oral Health and is committed to providing high-quality treatment with a personal touch.

Fluent in Spanish, Dr. Zapata learned the language while serving a mission for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, allowing him to connect with and serve a broader range of patients. Outside the office, he enjoys spending time with his wife and children, cooking for family and friends, and exploring Arizona’s trails on his mountain bike.

He feels grateful to serve the community he calls home and looks forward to building lasting relationships with his patients.

Dr. Marissa Olmos

Meet Dr. Marissa Olmos! Dr. Olmos is an Arizona native with a deep passion for dentistry and her community. She completed her undergraduate studies at Arizona State University and went on to earn her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from AT Still University’s Arizona School of Dentistry and Oral Health.

Dr. Olmos believes dentistry should be an empowering and joyful experience, and she is dedicated to providing the highest level of care to every patient she meets. She understands that every smile is unique and approaches each patient with that individuality in mind. She also knows that the dental office is not everyone’s favorite place, which is why she is committed to creating a comfortable and relaxing environment from the moment you walk through the door.

When she is not caring for patients, you can find Dr. Olmos on the tennis court, hitting the ski slopes, spending time with her golden retrievers Levi and Murphy, or traveling with her fiancé Jesse.

We look forward to you meeting the newest addition to our team!

Dr. Kramer

Dr. Kramer was born in Tomah, Wisconsin and moved to Chandler, Arizona at an early age where she was raised and ultimately calls home. She grew to love Arizona and has remained here throughout her education. Dr. Kramer completed her undergraduate degree in Biology at Grand Canyon University then simultaneously gained her Masters in Public Health and Doctorate of Dental Medicine from A.T. Still University - Arizona School of Dentistry and Oral Health in Mesa, Arizona.

Dr. Kramer believes in whole person healthcare and that various dental diseases should be treated to prevent and treat systemic health.

Her approach with dental care is gentle, communicative, and patient-focused. Dr. Kramer understands that every patient’s smile is unique and therefore should be treated individually.

When Dr. Kramer isn’t treating patients, she enjoys workout out, being outdoors with her mini australian shepard, Winnie, traveling, and trying new restaurants
