The practice

We never compromise on delivering exceptional care. From precise digital imaging to self-ligating brackets, we utilize the most current treatment techniques and equipment available today. This means shorter treatment times, fewer appointments, and superior comfort for our patients from start to finish.

We are more than just an orthodontic office; we are your neighbors. We are proud to actively support the families we serve, partiipating in everything from local school fairs to ice cream socials. This community spirit extends inside our doors, where you will find a warm, energetic atmosphere of being among friends.

Dr. Spivak combines elite academic training from Harvard and Tufts with decades of diverse clinical experience. Because learning never stops, our team’s unwavering dedication to rigorous continuing education ensures your personalized treatment plan is built on a deep foundation of knowledge and proven results.

Behind every beautiful smile we create is a team of dedicated, fun-loving, and highly skilled professionals. We work together to make sure your visits are seamless, from the front desk to the clinic chair.
