A Clean, Modern, Low-Stress Environment

Dental anxiety is real, and a lot of it comes from not knowing what to expect. Swift Creek Dental Center is a clean, modern office where the goal is to make every visit feel as low-stress as possible. From the front desk to the treatment chair, the team is friendly, unhurried, and focused on keeping you informed and comfortable throughout.

Transparent Communication About Treatment and Costs

Before any treatment begins, patients know what’s being recommended and why. Swift Creek Dental Center believes that clear explanations lead to better decisions — and that no one should leave an appointment feeling confused about what happened or what comes next. Insurance coverage, out-of-pocket estimates, and treatment options are discussed openly so there are no surprises.

Insurance Filed on Your Behalf

Swift Creek Dental Center accepts dental insurance and handles the filing process for every patient. Medicare is accepted. A complimentary benefits check is offered before treatment begins so patients have a realistic picture of their coverage.

Same-Day Care When You Need It

Urgent dental needs don’t wait for a convenient opening in the schedule. Same-day care may be available for both new and existing patients when the schedule allows — call the office as early in the day as possible for the best chance of being seen. Whether it’s a toothache, a broken tooth, or another unexpected concern, Swift Creek Dental Center takes urgent needs seriously.

A Team With the Training to Back It Up

Technology and philosophy matter — but so does clinical skill. The hygienists at Swift Creek Dental Center are certified to provide local anesthetic for periodontal treatment, which means patients receive thorough, comfortable care without unnecessary handoffs or delays.

It’s the kind of in-house capability that makes appointments more efficient and the experience more consistent for every patient.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Swift Creek Dental Center different from other dental offices in Midlothian?

Swift Creek Dental Center is built around long-term patient relationships rather than short-term treatment. Dr. Estes and Dr. Koelmel take time to explain findings clearly, discuss options honestly, and provide recommendations based on what’s actually best for the patient — not what fills the schedule. For families looking for a dental home they can rely on year after year, that distinction matters.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes. Swift Creek Dental Center accepts dental insurance and files claims on behalf of every patient. Medicare is also accepted. Before treatment begins, we offer a complimentary benefits check so patients have a clear picture of their estimated coverage.

Is same-day care available?

Same-day care may be available for new and existing patients depending on appointment availability. Call the office as early in the day as possible — our team will do everything we can to get you seen quickly for toothaches, broken teeth, or other urgent concerns.

Do you treat children and adults?

Yes. Swift Creek Dental Center provides dental care for adults and children under one roof. We are a family dental practice — not a pediatric specialist — but experienced and comfortable caring for patients of all ages.

How do I get started as a new patient?

Call the office or submit an appointment request online. We’ll walk you through scheduling, answer any insurance questions, and offer a complimentary benefits check before your first visit. New patients are always welcome at Swift Creek Dental Center.

New patients are welcome, and the first visit is designed to be straightforward. We’ll check your benefits, answer your questions, and take the time to understand what you’re looking for in a dental home. Swift Creek Dental Center serves families throughout Midlothian, Swift Creek, Woodlake, Brandermill, Hampton Park, and Chesterfield County.
