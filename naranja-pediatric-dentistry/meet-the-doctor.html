Meet the Doctor

Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Fergie Alexa Molina is a compassionate and highly skilled board-certified pediatric dentist who is dedicated to creating positive dental experiences for children of all ages and needs.

With a calm demeanor and a natural ability to connect with young patients, Dr. Molina is especially passionate about caring for children with special needs and those who require hospital-based dentistry.

Originally from Colombia, Dr. Molina completed her undergraduate studies at Florida State University before earning her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the Kornberg School of Dentistry. She went on to complete a General Practice Residency at Montefiore Medical Center, where she honed her skills in comprehensive care.

Pursuing her passion for pediatrics, she further specialized through a Pediatric Dentistry Residency at Howard University College of Dentistry.

Dr. Molina has been practicing since 2019 and brings both technical expertise and genuine empathy to her work. Her calm and patient-centered approach is particularly effective in treating children with dental anxiety, making her a trusted provider among families seeking gentle, specialized care.

In addition to her civilian practice, Dr. Molina proudly serves as a dentist in the United States Army Reserves, reflecting her commitment to service and excellence in oral healthcare.

Outside the office, Dr. Molina enjoys horseback riding and spending quality time with her German Shepherd, Abby, as well as her family and friends. She is a strong believer in the power of preventive care, education, and creating a fun, welcoming environment for every child she treats.
