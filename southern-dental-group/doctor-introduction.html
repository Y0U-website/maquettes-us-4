About Doctor

Dr. Clayton Wright and his team at Southern Dental Group pride themselves in offering excellent patient care and the latest in advanced dental treatment. Dr. Wright has been working in Dothan since 2001 and has one of the premiere dental treatment offices in south Alabama. Dr. Wright received his D.M.D. degree from the University of Alabama School of Dentistry and continues to enhance his knowledge with courses and learning experiences to better serve his patients.

The team at Southern Dental Group uses the latest technology in preventive care and cosmetic procedures. The oral health of the patients at Southern Dental Group is not taken lightly. The hygiene team is diligent in diagnosing and treating gum disease, while Dr. Wright works to meet patients’ cosmetic and restorative needs. This office has an excellent reputation for providing quality dental work, performed in a comfortable and professional environment.

Dr. Wright is also affiliated with the American Academy of Facial Esthetics and administers Botulinum Toxin (Botox & Xeomin) , Dermal Fillers, and TMJ Pain injections. Dr. Wright ensures that each patient he cares for receives the healthy and happy smile they deserve!
