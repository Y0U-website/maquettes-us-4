The practice

Our team customizes each treatment plan for comfort and precision, helping you achieve the confident, natural smile you’ve always wanted.

We provide periodontal maintenance including plaque and tartar removal, scaling, root planing, and polishing to keep your gums healthy.

For patients who have delayed dental care due to anxiety, we offer sedation solutions so major procedures can be performed comfortably in fewer visits.

Veneers are ultra-thin ceramic shells bonded to your teeth to redesign and reshape your smile in a natural, durable way.

We also offer a full spectrum of general and cosmetic care: bleaching (teeth whitening), bonding, composite fillings, crowns, dentures & partials, inlays/onlays, and more.

Schedule your appointment Ready for your next dental appointment? Our team makes scheduling simple and stress-free. Whether you’re due for a routine cleaning, have a question about treatment, or want to book a family visit, we’re here to help.

You can request an appointment online anytime, or call our Davis office to find a time that fits your schedule. We’ll confirm your visit quickly and make sure you feel prepared before you arrive.

Les soins

Bridges offer an effective way to replace missing teeth by using your adjacent natural teeth as anchors, creating a "bridge" over the gap.

Straighten your smile the modern way with Invisalign® at Coral Bay Family Dentistry. These nearly invisible aligners gently move your teeth into alignment — no metal brackets, no tightening appointments, and no interruptions to your lifestyle.
