0 The Team you can Trust Rhee Dentistry Palm Coast

Dr. Rhee was born in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and raised in Monroeville, just east of the city. He earned his Bachelor of Science degree from the Pennsylvania State University and went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of North Carolina School of Dentistry at Chapel Hill. He then completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at the University of Florida College of Dentistry.

Committed to lifelong learning, Dr. Rhee has completed Levels I–VI at the prestigious L.D. Pankey Institute in Key Biscayne, Florida, and has participated in over 2,000 hours of continuing education since graduating from dental school. He established his dental practice in Palm Coast in 1993 with a focus on comprehensive, interdisciplinary care in aesthetic, restorative, and implant dentistry.

Dr. Rhee is a past president of the Volusia-Flagler Dental Association and has served as a delegate for the Florida Dental Association. He is a proud member of the American Dental Association, the Florida Dental Association, and the Volusia-Flagler Dental Association. In addition to his practice, he holds a faculty appointment as a Courtesy Clinical Assistant Professor in the Department of Restorative Dental Sciences at the University of Florida College of Dentistry in Gainesville.

Outside the office, Dr. Rhee enjoys spending quality time with his wife and their two daughters; one who attends Matanzas High School and one who attends FSU. He enjoys all outdoor activities and is an avid African cichlid aquarium hobbyist.
