  • What types of dental insurance do you accept? We accept most major dental insurance plans, including PPO providers. If you’re unsure about your coverage, feel free to contact us, and we’ll verify your benefits before your visit.
  • How often should I schedule a dental cleaning? We recommend a cleaning and exam every six months to prevent dental issues and maintain optimal oral health. Regular checkups help us catch potential problems early, saving you time and money.
  • What is the difference between a crown and a filling? A filling is used for smaller cavities, while a crown is needed for larger or severely damaged teeth. Crowns provide more extensive restoration for teeth that need added strength.
  • Can I get same-day care for a dental emergency? Yes! We offer same-day emergency appointments to handle dental emergencies like severe pain, broken teeth, or other urgent concerns. If you’re in need of immediate care, call us, and we’ll get you seen as soon as possible.
  • How does Invisalign® work? Invisalign uses clear aligners to gradually straighten your teeth, providing a more discreet and comfortable alternative to traditional metal braces. It’s perfect for teens and adults in Sacramento, CA looking for a nearly invisible way to enhance their smile.
  • Do you offer sedation dentistry for anxious patients? Yes! We offer a variety of sedation options, including nitrous oxide (laughing gas) and oral sedation, to help you relax during your procedures. Our goal is to make your visit as stress-free as possible.
  • What is a dental implant and how does it work? A dental implant is a titanium post surgically placed in the jawbone to replace a missing tooth. It provides a permanent solution for tooth replacement and can restore both the function and appearance of your smile.
  • Do you offer cosmetic treatments for smile makeovers? Yes! We offer a range of cosmetic dentistry services, including porcelain veneers, professional teeth whitening, and Invisalign to help you achieve the smile you’ve always dreamed of.
  • How can I pay my dental bill? We offer multiple payment options, including online payments and in-office payment plans. We also accept most dental insurance plans and can help you navigate your coverage to make payments more manageable.
  • What should I do if I have a dental emergency after hours? If you have a dental emergency after hours, call our office for immediate assistance. We offer emergency care outside regular business hours to provide fast relief when you need it most.
