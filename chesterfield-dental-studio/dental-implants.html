Do I Need a Bone Graft Before Getting Dental Implants ?

In some cases, patients may need a bone graft to ensure there is enough bone structure to support the implant. If you’ve experienced bone loss due to missing teeth, injury, or periodontal disease, our imaging technology helps us determine whether bone grafting or a sinus lift is necessary before placement.

How Long Do Tooth Implants Last?

With proper care, dental implants can last 20 years or more. Compared to bridges or dentures, implants provide unmatched durability and long-term value. Routine checkups and good oral hygiene are essential to maintaining their longevity.

What Is the Recovery Time After Dental Implant Surgery ?

While healing time can vary, most patients experience only mild discomfort for 1–2 days after implant placement. Full osseointegration, the process where the implant bonds with your bone, typically takes 3–6 months.

We’ll provide detailed aftercare instructions and check in as you heal to ensure everything is progressing smoothly.

How Do I Care for My Dental Implants After They Are Placed?

Taking care of your dental implants is simple:

With consistent care, your implant can serve you reliably for decades.

  • Brush at least twice a day
  • Floss daily with implant-safe tools
  • Schedule regular dental checkups and cleanings
  • Avoid tobacco products, which can interfere with healing
