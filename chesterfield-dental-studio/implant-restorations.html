Can Dental Implants Replace Multiple Missing Teeth?

Yes. Implant-supported bridges and full-arch restorations allow us to replace several or all missing teeth without a separate post for every gap. By sharing the load across strategically placed implants, you gain superior bite force and comfort compared with traditional removable dentures.

How Long Do Implant Restorations For Teeth Last?

With good hygiene and routine check-ups, implant restorations commonly last 15–25 years or more. Ceramic materials resist staining and fracture, while the titanium post can last a lifetime. Factors affecting longevity include:

  • Daily oral care habits
  • Regular professional cleanings
  • Overall health and bone quality
  • Avoiding habits like smoking or chewing ice

How Do I Care for My Dental Implant Restoration ?

Maintaining healthy habits and preventive dental care is key to protecting your tooth implant .

  • Brush twice daily with a soft-bristle toothbrush
  • Floss or use interdental cleaners around the implant crown
  • Visit us twice a year for exams, digital X-rays, and professional cleanings
  • Wear a night guard if you clench or grind your teeth
  • Avoid biting pens, ice, or hard candies

How Long Does It Take to Heal After Tooth Implant Restoration ?

Soft-tissue healing takes a few weeks, while bone integration typically finishes within three to six months. Variables such as bone density, smoking, and overall health can affect the duration of this timeline. For suitable cases, immediate-load implants offer a temporary crown on the same day of surgery.
