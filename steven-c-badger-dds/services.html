Restoring Implants

If you have dental implants and have cracked, loose, or missing crowns, please call 928-368-7528 for implant restoration. We will schedule you for a visit to examine the issue and repair or replace the crown as needed. If we discover that the problem is with the dental implant (not the crown), we will refer you to a specialist in dental implant repair and replacement.
