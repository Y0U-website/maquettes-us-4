Dentistry Services Birmingham, MI

Services

General Dentistry

We provide comprehensive dental care services that prevent, diagnose, and treat oral health concerns. Our general dentistry services include standard oral exams, cleanings, and other dental treatments to help you achieve optimal dental hygiene.

Cosmetic Dentistry

We specialize in enhancing the natural appearance of your smile by correcting misaligned, discolored, or aging teeth. From teeth whitening and bonding to dental implants and veneers, our cosmetic solutions will enhance the quality of your smile while improving overall oral health.

Surgical Dentistry

Our surgical dentistry services are focused on restoring your beautiful smile. Dental implants provide secure, permanent solutions for patients who want natural-looking teeth replacements that offer both comfort and function.

Laser Dentistry

All Smiles offers a variety of laser dentistry services, including gingival recontouring, treatments for gummy smile, biopsies, and frenectomies.
