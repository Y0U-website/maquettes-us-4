About All Smiles Dentistry

Dr. Daniel Mashni, DDS and his staff at All Smiles Dentistry have proudly served patients in the Birmingham area with exceptional dental services for many years. We deliver personalized education, prevention, and treatment for your oral health concerns.

We are dedicated to nurturing long-term patient relationships that help you achieve optimal tooth and gum health. While we are focused on lifelong preventative care, we also offer restorative services that enhance the natural appearance of your smile.

Visit our Services page for a full list of our dental services.

Our mission is to provide the best care possible by using proven leading edge technology and educating our patients about their oral health. To develop relationships with our patients and to improve quality of life by helping our patients smile more!

Dr. Daniel D. Mashni, DDS, and his knowledgeable staff are passionate about delivering exceptional, long-term dental care to patients in and around the Birmingham area.

We take pride in creating a relaxing environment for treating our patients. It is our goal to enhance the appearance of your smile while advancing your overall health and well-being.
