Sometimes, brushing and flossing are not enough to create the brilliant white look that you are after. From age and medications, to what you eat and drink, there are many reasons why your teeth may need a whitening boost.

At All Smiles Dentistry, we offer in-office teeth whitening services to reduce the signs of stains or discoloration. As with other dental services, we will talk with you about your unique goals, interests, and concerns before committing to teeth whitening treatments as a means of brightening your smile.

Our professional-grade teeth whitening services can deliver a visibly whiter smile with a relatively short in-office visit. Using state-of-the-art technology, we safely activate peroxide, a bleaching agent, that will be applied to your teeth for a short duration of time that is determined by the shade of white that is desired. Fluoride is typically applied as a desensitizer around the gums or teeth.

We are dedicated to ensuring your bright smile is maintained at home. Our caring staff will provide guidance on how to maximize the results of our teeth whitening services. Additional visits may be scheduled in order to achieve and maintain the desired shade of white.

Invisalign is a clinically-proven, modern alternative to costly conventional braces. This highly effective orthodontic treatment corrects misaligned teeth with a series of clear, plastic, removable aligners. By slowly and gently moving your teeth into their optimal position, Invisalign offers a convenient means to achieve a more confident and beautiful smile.

As an Invisalign-certified dental practice, we will conduct an initial consultation to understand your treatment goals and we take teeth impressions that are sent to a dental lab. The number of aligners and the amount of time each must be worn is unique to your specific case. Typically, patients switch aligners weekly and treatment plans last an average of one year. Because they are removable, Invisalign aligners provide a convenient way to go about your day-to-day activities while simultaneously straightening your teeth.

Invisalign provides a comfortable, practical alternative to the wires and brackets that come with traditional braces. This pioneering treatment system has no significant impact on your daily routine so you can feel confident that your smile is improving while you enjoy the things that matter most to you. The removable aligners are virtually invisible so no one has to know you are wearing them. They also allow you to eat and drink without limitation as long as they are worn for the recommended number of daily hours.

At All Smiles Dentistry, we offer this innovative alternative to conventional braces that straighten teeth and improve overall tooth and gum health. Contact us today to schedule a consultation or to learn more about our Invisalign treatment options.
