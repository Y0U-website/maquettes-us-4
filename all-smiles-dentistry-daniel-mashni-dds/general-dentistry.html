Our dental services include custom crowns and bridgework that help to restore your natural-looking smile in the case of missing or damaged teeth. Dental crowns are made from different materials like porcelain and cast gold that offer varying degrees of durability and aesthetic appeal.

They can either fit over a decayed or aesthetically unsatisfactory tooth, or take the place of one as part of a series of bridgework procedures. Long-term self-care is necessary to protect your investment and maintain the overall health and appearance of your smile.

There are two different types of dental crowns and both serve to restore chewing power, alignment, or the natural appearance of your smile. One type of custom-made dental crown is permanently placed over your damaged or discolored teeth. If your tooth is missing, bridgework will effectively connect two custom crowns on either side of the missing space in which the second type of crown, known as the pontic, is placed in the empty space thus replacing your missing tooth.

Standard dental hygiene practices are important in maintaining long-term efficiency of your dental crowns. Brushing, flossing, and regular visits to our office will ensure the enduring comfort, function, and appearance of your crowns and bridgework. Our experienced staff will guide you through long-term self care and proper maintenance for lifelong dental health.
