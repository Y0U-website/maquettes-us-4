Dental Implants Brentwood

During your consultation, we will help you determine which type of dental implant is best for your oral health and lifestyle.

  • Implant-Supported Bridges : Perfect for replacing multiple missing teeth in a row without impacting surrounding natural teeth.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures : A secure and stable option for those missing most or all of their teeth.
  • All-On-4® Implants : A revolutionary full-arch restoration method using only four implants to support a row of teeth.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer unparalleled advantages when compared to other tooth replacement options:

  • Improved Functionality : Dental implants integrate fully with your jawbone for maximum stability, allowing you to speak, eat, and chew easily.
  • Natural Appearance : Implant-supported teeth are designed to match your natural teeth, ensuring your smile looks seamless.
  • Lasting Durability : Dental implants can last decades or even a lifetime with proper care.
  • Enhanced Bone Health : Implants prevent bone loss by stimulating the jawbone, maintaining its integrity and structure.
  • No Impact on Healthy Teeth : Unlike bridges, dental implants don’t rely on adjacent teeth for support, preserving your natural teeth.

The Dental Implant Process

We follow a meticulous, patient-focused process to ensure the success of your dental implants:

Consultation and Planning

The process begins with a detailed consultation. Using advanced imaging technology such as 3D Imaging and Digital X-rays, we thoroughly evaluate your oral health and jawbone density. This helps us develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs.

Surgical Placement of Implants

During this step, we surgically place the titanium implants into your jawbone. The procedure is precise and performed with your comfort in mind, utilizing state-of-the-art tools like our Soft Tissue Laser for optimal results.

Healing and Osseointegration

After placement, the implants require time to bond with your jawbone in osseointegration. This phase ensures a stable and secure foundation for your new teeth.

Abutment and Restoration Placement

Once healing is complete, we attach an abutment to the implant, which serves as the connector for the restoration. Finally, we place a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture to complete your new smile.
