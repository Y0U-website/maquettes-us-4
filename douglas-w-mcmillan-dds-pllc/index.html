Dr. McMillan was born in Hendersonville, NC, and has always had an affection for the mountains of Western North Carolina. After high school, he earned a degree in Biochemistry from Lipscomb University in Nashville, TN. After his undergraduate training, he went to Clemson University, where he earned a Master’s Degree in Chemistry.

After his Master’s Degree, he attended the University of Michigan School of Dentistry, where he graduated in 1999 with his DDS degree. Since graduation, Dr. McMillan has completed the Maxi Course in Implant Dentistry and also completed Advanced training in Botox and Facial Aesthetics, Cosmetic Dentistry, and Fixed Prosthodontics.
