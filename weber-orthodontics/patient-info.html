Making Orthodontics Affordable

Financial & Insurance Details

We’re committed to offering top-notch treatment without breaking the bank. During your first visit, we’ll check if your insurance covers orthodontic care. While coverage varies, we aim to maximize your benefits. Please note that orthodontic benefits differ from regular dental benefits. For any insurance queries, feel free to call us.

Before your visit, be sure to fill out your Orthodontic Insurance Information form in order to allow us to give you all the information necessary to make an educated decision about your orthodontic treatment plan. We also offer flexible payment options, including auto-drafts and personalized payment plans.

New Patient FAQs

Starting Your Orthodontic Journey? Dive into Our New Patient FAQs.

Our team will be glad to answer any questions you may have, and we will provide all of the information that you need to determine which treatment is right for you.

Please contact our practice to schedule your initial consultation.

  • What happens during the initial consultation? Your initial consultation will give you the opportunity to meet our team and find out which treatment options will best meet your needs. During your initial consultation, we will: Review your dental and medical history forms.
  • Take X-rays and photos of your teeth.
  • Help you create a customized treatment plan.
  • Discuss all financial information, insurance options, and payment plans.
  • What happens after the initial consultation? If Dr. Weber determines that you are ready for orthodontic treatment we will email you a link with all the financial and insurance information. Once you decide to move forward with treatment, please click ’START SMILING.’ We will then contact you to schedule your next visit. Dr. Weber may determine that she would like to wait on orthodontic treatment. If this is the case Dr. Weber will give a timeline for when she would like a follow-up appointment.
  • Will I receive a detailed plan and cost estimate during my initial consultation? Dr. Weber and our team will thoroughly discuss the orthodontic plan recommended. As long as the new patient forms have been filled out in advance with insurance information, you will leave our office knowing the cost of your orthodontic treatment.
  • How does Weber Orthodontics handle insurance and payment options? On your first visit, we will verify your insurance benefits. We aim to maximize your benefits and can file your claim once treatment starts. We also offer flexible payment options, including auto-drafts for credit cards, bank drafts, and personalized payment plans.
  • Are there any forms I need to complete before my first visit? Once you have scheduled your first visit, you will recieve a link to our new patient paperwork. Please complete all forms in order for our team to be able to give you the most accurate information at your new patient exam.
  • How often will I need to visit the orthodontist during my treatment? Regular visits are essential, typically every six to eight weeks is recommended. These appointments ensure your treatment is progressing as planned.
  • Do I still need to see my family dentist during orthodontic treatment? Absolutely! It’s crucial to continue visiting your family dentist every six months for routine checkups and teeth cleanings, even during your orthodontic treatment.
