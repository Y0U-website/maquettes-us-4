About Us

Behind Every Great Smile, There’s Our Team.

Meet Dr. Weber and the devoted professionals behind those brilliant smiles, and discover what truly sets Weber Orthodontics apart in the heart of Kansas City.

At Weber Orthodontics in Kansas City, we’re more than just braces and Invisalign; we are your premier destination for top-tier orthodontic care. Led by Dr. Ashlee Weber, our family-owned practice specializes in crafting radiant smiles for both kids and adults. With a unique blend of advanced techniques, personalized treatments, and a warm, patient-centric approach, we’re redefining the orthodontic experience. Choose Weber Orthodontics where every smile is a masterpiece waiting to be unveiled.

What Sets Us Apart

Family-Owned & Deeply Rooted

At the heart of Kansas City lies Weber Orthodontics, a family-owned practice that’s as much a part of the community as the smiles we’ve transformed. Our clinic embodies the warmth, trust, and personalized care that only a family-oriented approach can offer.

Every Smile is Personal

At Weber Orthodontics, you’re never "just another patient." To us, every individual who walks through our doors has a unique story. With Dr. Weber and our dedicated team, rest assured you’re always in the hands of those who genuinely care.

Transformative, Transparent Care

At Weber Orthodontics, we believe in clear communication and transformative treatments. Whether braces, Invisalign, or a simple consultation, our commitment is to guide you transparently, ensuring you’re informed and confident at every step of your smile journey.
