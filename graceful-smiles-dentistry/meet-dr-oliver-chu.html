Meet Dr. Chu Dr. Oliver Chu located in Springfield, VA About Dr. Oliver Chu Dr. Oliver Chu brings nearly two decades of dental expertise to Graceful Smiles Dentistry. A proud graduate of Boston University, where he earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree in 2008, He is an active member of the American Dental Association, the Virginia Dental Association, and the Northern Virginia Dental Association.

Dr. Chu is fluent in both English and Mandarin Chinese. Known for his warm sense of humor, he ensures patients feel at ease during their visits. Dedicated to giving back, he works at a volunteer dental clinic, prioritizing patients’ oral health and happiness.

Outside the office, Dr. Chu enjoys spending quality time with his wife and daughter, cherishing family moments and laughter.
