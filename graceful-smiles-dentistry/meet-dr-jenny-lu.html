Meet Dr. Lu Dr. Jenny Lu located in Springfield, VA About Dr. Jenny Lu Dr. Jenny Lu is a proud graduate of the Virginia Commonwealth University School of Dentistry, where she earned her degree summa cum laude. She excelled academically and was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society, an honor reserved for the top 10 % of her class.

She is an active member of the American Dental Association, Virginia Dental Association, and Northern Virginia Dental Society. Dr. Lu is dedicated to helping patients feel more confident in their smiles. With a genuine passion for dentistry and patient-centered care, she takes great joy in seeing the transformation and happiness a new smile can bring.

Dr. Lu values clear communication and cultural understanding, making sure every patient feels comfortable and well-informed. She is fluent in English and Vietnamese.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Lu is a devoted cat lover. In her free time, she feeds str ay cat colonies. Additionally, Dr. Lu likes attending NBA games and playing Pokémon Go .
