Young Teen Dentistry

(Ages 6–17)

The transition from childhood to adolescence brings unique dental challenges. This is a crucial time for your child’s oral health as their permanent teeth come in and their bite begins to change. Regular check-ups during these years are essential to ensure healthy teeth and gums as they grow and develop.

What to Expect Young teens are often excited about maintaining their smile and learning more about their dental health. We focus on educating both parents and children on the importance of preventive care during this phase. We’ll also assess how your child’s teeth are coming in and check for any potential orthodontic needs.

Teens also face an increased risk of cavities due to lifestyle changes, so we work with them to make sure they are maintaining good oral hygiene at home and can easily navigate the challenges of eating, drinking, and other habits.

  • Orthodontic Evaluations If necessary, we’ll help you determine the best time to begin orthodontic treatment, whether it’s braces or clear aligners.
  • Sealants Protecting molars with sealants to prevent cavities.
  • Fluoride Treatments Boosting cavity resistance during these years of rapid tooth development.
  • Routine Cleanings and Exams Helping your teen establish good brushing habits while managing any developing dental concerns.
  • Tooth Sensitivity Treatment Addressing any concerns related to developing or sensitive teeth.

Adult Dentistry

(Ages 18+)

As we enter adulthood, our dental needs change as our bodies and lifestyles evolve. Whether you’re managing the demands of work and family, handling stress, or simply aging, Maenza Family Dentistry offers comprehensive services to keep your smile healthy and beautiful throughout your adult years.

What to Expect Regular check-ups during adulthood are vital for maintaining good oral health, preventing gum disease, and identifying issues like cavities, cracked teeth, or problems with existing restorations. Adults also face a greater risk of gum disease, tooth decay, and other concerns due to lifestyle choices, such as smoking, diet, and oral hygiene habits.

Taking care of your teeth and gums during adulthood not only enhances your smile but also helps with overall health, as oral issues can sometimes affect other parts of your body.

  • Comprehensive Exams and Cleanings Assessing oral health, checking for decay or gum disease, and offering professional cleanings to prevent future problems.
  • Cavity Prevention Addressing issues before they become more serious, including fillings, crowns, or other restorations.
  • Teeth Whitening Brightening your smile with professional whitening treatments.
  • Restorative Dentistry Repairing and restoring teeth with fillings, crowns, bridges, and dentures.
  • Periodontal Care Treating and preventing gum disease with scaling and root planing, or more advanced treatments if necessary.
  • Cosmetic Dentistry Improving the appearance of your smile with veneers, bonding, and other treatments.
  • Oral Cancer Screenings Regular screenings to detect any signs of oral cancer early, when it’s most treatable.

Senior Dentistry

(Ages 65+)

As we age, our dental needs evolve. Seniors may face challenges such as tooth loss, gum disease, or difficulty maintaining oral health due to physical limitations. At Maenza Family Dentistry, we offer compassionate care that helps seniors keep their smiles healthy, beautiful, and functional well into their later years.

What to Expect Dental health plays a major role in your overall well-being as you age. Regular visits become even more important for detecting early signs of oral health issues that can affect your quality of life. Whether you’re dealing with the effects of aging, managing dental restorations, or navigating changes in your ability to maintain oral hygiene, we’re here to support you every step of the way.

Maintaining oral health as a senior is essential not only for your smile but also for your general health. Regular check-ups allow us to address the unique challenges you may face and ensure your dental needs are met with the utmost care and respect.

  • Routine Exams and Cleanings Keeping gums and teeth healthy, preventing cavities, and detecting issues early.
  • Restorative Care Solutions for tooth loss such as dentures, implants, or bridges.
  • Gum Disease Treatment Addressing periodontal disease, which becomes more common as we age.
  • Oral Cancer Screenings Detecting signs of oral cancer early, especially as risk factors increase with age.
  • Dry Mouth Treatment A common concern for seniors, often caused by medications, which can lead to increased decay or discomfort.
  • Dentures and Implants Restoring function and aesthetics with dentures or dental implants.
