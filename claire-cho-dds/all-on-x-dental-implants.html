What are all on X implants?

With all on X dental implants, patients have the opportunity to stabilize a full denture or bridge with just four to six implants along the arch. This means that the entire prosthesis is supported by and attached to the implants, providing a much more secure and natural feeling compared to traditional full or complete dentures.

How are All-on-X dental implants considered different from conventional dentures?

Unlike traditional types of dentures, which rest on the gums, All-on-X implants are anchored directly into the jawbone. This means they’re super stable, won’t slip or click, and help maintain jawbone health over time. Talk about a game changer!
