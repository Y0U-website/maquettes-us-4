Invisalign

For patients who are interested in options to straighten their teeth without braces, Invisalign can be a great choice. Using a series of custom-designed, clear aligners, your teeth are gradually shifted into a straight and healthy position—without anyone knowing you are undergoing orthodontic treatment. The aligners are switched out every two weeks for the next in the series, and we perform periodic check-ups to monitor your progress. Many patients can complete their Invisalign treatment in just 12 months.

Frequently Asked Questions About Orthodontics

Which orthodontic option is best for adults who want discreet treatment?

For adults seeking a subtle approach, Invisalign and Six Month Smiles are excellent options. Invisalign uses clear, removable aligners that are nearly invisible, while Six Month Smiles uses clear brackets and tooth-colored wires to blend with your natural teeth. Both options allow you to maintain a professional appearance while gradually correcting alignment issues without the look of traditional braces.

Can orthodontic treatment correct bite problems as well as straighten teeth?

Yes. Orthodontic appliances can address bite issues such as overbites, underbites, and crossbites while aligning teeth. Correcting bite problems not only improves appearance but also enhances chewing efficiency and reduces the risk of jaw pain or temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders. Our Sun City dentists evaluate both cosmetic and functional needs to recommend the right treatment for your smile.

How long does orthodontic treatment typically take?

Treatment duration varies depending on the severity of misalignment and the type of orthodontic appliance used. Six Month Smiles generally focuses on cosmetic corrections and may take, as the name suggests, around six months. Invisalign and traditional braces may require 12 24 months for comprehensive realignment. Regular check-ins help ensure progress stays on track and adjustments are made as needed.

What should I expect in terms of maintenance during orthodontic treatment?

Proper care during treatment is essential for optimal results. Brushing and flossing around brackets or aligners is critical to prevent plaque buildup and staining. Invisalign aligners should be worn 20 22 hours per day and cleaned regularly. Additionally, attending scheduled adjustment appointments allows the dentist to monitor progress, make necessary corrections, and ensure your teeth and bite are moving as planned.

How much should I budget for orthodontic treatment at your office?

The cost of orthodontic treatment depends on the type of braces or aligners you choose, the complexity of your case, and the estimated treatment duration. At Wildflower Dental in Sun City, Arizona, we provide general cost ranges for Six Month Smiles, Invisalign, and traditional braces during your consultation. Additional factors such as follow-up visits, retainers, or any preparatory work may also influence the final price, allowing us to create a personalized plan that fits your budget.

How can orthodontics improve my oral health or smile?

Orthodontic treatment aligns teeth properly, which can make brushing and flossing more effective, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease . Properly aligned teeth also help prevent excessive wear on enamel and reduce strain on your jaw joints. Beyond health benefits, straightened teeth enhance the aesthetics of your smile, boost confidence, and can even improve speech clarity and bite function.

After an initial consultation and evaluation, many patients can begin treatment within a few weeks. We coordinate appointments to minimize waiting times, and our team can guide you on the best starting date for Six Month Smiles, Invisalign, or traditional braces based on your treatment goals.

Traditional braces

Traditional metal bracket-and-wire braces are one of the most versatile orthodontic treatments available. They can be used even in severe cases of crowding, misalignment, gaps, or bite problems, and can be a great solution for many patients. Braces work by placing a gentle, yet persistent pressure on the teeth in a strategic direction to gradually move them into their optimal position. Depending on the severity of the misalignment, treatment with traditional braces generally takes between 12 and 30 months.
