Our Technology

DIGITAL X-Ray: We use digital x-ray sensors at our practice to provide a more comfortable experience for the patient while allowing us to take more precise x-rays without using film.

3D X-Ray (CBCT): X-Rays only provide a 2D image of your teeth, but sometimes, particularly when placing an implant or removing wisdom teeth, we need a more accurate, 3D image of the jaw and teeth, to make sure that those procedures do not harm nearby structures like nerves and sinuses.

3D Scanner: Allows for more accurate measurements than standard dental impressions and a more comfortable experience over the traditional materials used to make molds of teeth. Many patients the 3D scanner to standard impressions.

Laser Dentistry: We offer Laser for surgical Procedures such as Frenectomy .There are several benefits of Laser over Surgery :

  • Laser treatments require less anesthesia/numbing, resulting in a more comfortable treatment
  • Recovery time is much quicker than with traditional treatment options
  • There is less potential for complications because laser treatments are so precise

Sleep Apnea and TMD/TMJ Pain Solutions

Homeoblock Appliance For Sleep Apnea

We are proud to be one of the first facilities that offer the Homeoblock appliance as a treatment option in the DFW area. Dr. Maftoun has been trained by the inventor of the Homeoblock device, Dr. Belfor.

This device is a patented, revolutionary oral device that remodels the jawbone and stimulates jaw growth. Clinically we can expect to see a wider smile and greater facial symmetry. Since the changes are physiological and allow the head and jaw to develop proper symmetry, the result is a proper alignment of the jaw which in turn improves our head posture. Worn only at night, Homeoblock is ideal for patients who want to improve their appearance and get a better night’s sleep due to improved breathing.

The POD is a unique and comfortable appliance on the lower jaw designed to aid in treating clenching grinding (Bruxism) and TMJ. The clever design makes it very comfortable compared to commonly used night guards and can be worn during the day or night. The device serves to protect the teeth, crowns, veneers, and restorations from the destructive forces of Bruxism and will help alleviate headaches, TMJ and muscle pain.
