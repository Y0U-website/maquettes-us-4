Frequently Asked Questions About Invisalign

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Most Invisalign treatment plans at Premier Arts Dental take between 6 and 18 months, depending on the complexity of your case. Mild crowding or spacing may resolve in as little as six months, while more involved corrections may take longer. Dr. Shah will provide a personalized timeline during your consultation based on your iTero digital scan.

Many dental insurance plans that include orthodontic benefits may cover a portion of Invisalign treatment. Premier Arts Dental accepts major insurance providers including Aetna, Cigna, Delta Dental, Guardian, Horizon, MetLife, and UnitedHealthcare. Our team will review your specific plan and explain your coverage before treatment begins.

Is Invisalign painful?

Most patients experience mild pressure or tightness when they switch to a new set of aligners, which typically subsides within a day or two. This sensation is a sign that your teeth are moving as planned. Invisalign aligners have smooth edges and no metal brackets or wires, so they tend to be more comfortable than traditional braces.

Can I eat and drink normally with Invisalign?

Yes. Because Invisalign aligners are removable, you can take them out before meals and snacks and eat whatever you enjoy. Simply brush your teeth before placing the aligners back in. You should avoid drinking anything other than water while wearing your aligners to prevent staining.

How is Invisalign different from mail-order aligners?

Invisalign treatment at Premier Arts Dental includes in-person exams, digital imaging, and ongoing monitoring by Dr. Shah throughout your entire treatment. Mail-order aligners typically rely on at-home impressions and remote oversight, which may not catch complications early. Having a certified provider supervise your care helps ensure your teeth move safely and predictably.
