Frequently Asked Questions About Teen Invisalign

What age is right to start Invisalign for teens?

Can my teen play sports while wearing Invisalign?

Yes. Because Invisalign aligners are removable, your teen can take them out for practices and games and wear a traditional mouthguard instead. This is a significant advantage over fixed braces, which can cause soft-tissue injuries during contact sports.

How long does Invisalign treatment take for teens?

Treatment length depends on the complexity of your teen&#x27;s alignment needs. Many teen Invisalign cases are completed in 12 to 18 months, though Dr. Shah will provide a personalized timeline during the initial consultation based on the iTero digital scan results.

What if my teen loses an aligner?

The Invisalign teen system includes replacement aligners to account for the realities of teenage life. If your teen loses or damages a tray, contact our office and we will coordinate a replacement so treatment stays on track.

Is Invisalign as effective as braces for teens?

For many common orthodontic concerns — including crowding, spacing, and mild to moderate bite issues — Invisalign may be just as effective as traditional braces. Dr. Shah will assess your teen&#x27;s specific needs and recommend the approach designed to deliver the best results. For more answers, visit our Invisalign FAQ page.
