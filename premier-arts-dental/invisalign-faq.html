Frequently Asked Invisalign Questions

How Long Does Invisalign Treatment Take?

Most Invisalign treatments at Premier Arts Dental take between 12 and 18 months, though some mild cases may be completed in as few as six months. Your exact timeline depends on the complexity of your alignment needs. Dr. Shah will provide a personalized estimate during your consultation using digital treatment planning.

Does Invisalign Hurt?

Most patients experience mild pressure or tightness for the first day or two after switching to a new set of aligners. This is a normal sign that your teeth are moving. The discomfort is generally much less than what patients report with traditional braces, and it typically fades quickly.

How Much Does Invisalign Cost?

Invisalign cost varies based on the complexity and length of your treatment plan. At Premier Arts Dental, we provide a detailed cost breakdown before you commit so there are no surprises. We also offer flexible financing through CareCredit, Cherry, and in-house payment plans to help make treatment affordable.

Does Insurance Cover Invisalign?

Many dental insurance plans include orthodontic benefits that may apply to Invisalign. Premier Arts Dental accepts major insurers including Aetna, Cigna, Delta Dental, Guardian, Horizon, MetLife, and UnitedHealthcare. Our team will verify your coverage and walk you through your out-of-pocket responsibility before treatment begins. Visit our Insurance & Financing page for more details.

Can I Eat and Drink with Invisalign Aligners?

You should remove your aligners before eating or drinking anything other than plain water. This protects the aligners from staining and damage. Simply brush your teeth before placing them back in, and you can enjoy all the foods you love without restrictions.

How Often Do I Wear My Aligners?

For the best results, you should wear your Invisalign aligners 20 to 22 hours per day. Remove them only for eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing. Consistent wear helps keep your treatment on schedule and may even help you finish sooner.

Am I a Good Candidate for Invisalign?

Invisalign is designed to treat a wide range of alignment concerns, including crowding, gaps, overbites, underbites, and crossbites. The best way to find out if Invisalign is right for you is to schedule a consultation. Dr. Shah will evaluate your smile and recommend the most effective treatment approach.

Is Invisalign Available for Teens?

Yes. Invisalign for Teens is specifically designed with features like compliance indicators that help parents and our team confirm the aligners are being worn enough each day. It is a popular choice for teens who want a discreet alternative to metal braces.

What Happens After Invisalign Treatment?

After your final set of aligners, you will transition to a retainer to help maintain your new smile. Retainers prevent your teeth from gradually shifting back toward their original positions. Dr. Shah will recommend a retainer schedule tailored to your needs during your final visits.
