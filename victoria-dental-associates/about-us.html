Dr. Clayton W. Sciba D.D.S.

Dr. Sciba completed his education at Stephen F. Austin University and UT Health Science Center School of Dentistry at San Antonio. He is a member of the American Dental Association, Texas Dental Association, and Guadalupe Valley District Dental Society. Dr. Sciba has been in practice for more than 20 years and is more than qualified to perform any procedures to match your dental needs.

Dr. Sciba married his lovely wife, Mattea in 2002 and they have 4 children. When he’s not working to make you smile, he can be found doing volunteer work at Upward Sports and many youth rodeo associations. He also enjoys camping, jogging and rodeo. Dr. Sciba is a member of Northside Baptist Church, a Dallas Cowboy and Houston Astros sports fan.

Dr. Aaron S. Muegge D.D.S

Dr. Muegge completed his education at Texas A&M University and UT Health Science Center School of Dentistry at San Antonio. He is a member of the American Dental Association, Texas Dental Association, and Guadalupe Valley District Dental Society. Dr. Muegge serves as the Guadalupe Valley District Dental Society, Secretary/Treasurer and is in charge of coordinating and organizing the dental continuing education courses for the local dental community. He has been in practice for more than 18 years and is more than qualified to perform any procedures to match your dental needs.

When Dr. Muegge isn’t working to make you smile, he can be found doing volunteer work at First English Lutheran Church Heritage Day and Theater Victoria. He also enjoys comic books, crossword puzzles and video games. Dr. Muegge is a member of First English Lutheran Church, a Texas A&M Aggie, Houston Astros and Philadelphia Eagles sports fan.

Dr. Danielle R. Hill, D.D.S.

Dr. Hill completed her education at Texas A&M University and UT Health Science Center School of Dentistry at San Antonio. She is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry. Dr. Hill has been in practice for more than 7 years, and is more than qualified to perform any procedures to match your dental needs.

When Dr.Hill is not working to make your smile, she can be found with her family including her beloved golden retriever. She also enjoys fishing, traveling, and sewing. She is a member of Parkway Baptist Church, a Texas A&M Aggie and Houston Astros sports fan.
