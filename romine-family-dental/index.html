As a Topeka native, Dr. Jarrett Romine feels truly blessed to practice in the community he calls home. He graduated from Washburn Rural High School, as did his parents, Jeff and Jill Romine (Carpenter), and his sister, Karlee Judds (Romine). Dr. Romine went on to earn a degree in Biology from Kansas State University before completing dental school in Lincoln, Nebraska.

Dentistry was not always the plan. In his early years, Dr. Romine spent his summers pouring concrete, mowing lawns, and taking on small construction jobs. During college, he worked in a range of unique roles that included helping on a horse ranch, working in a door factory, conducting largemouth bass research for the Kansas Department of Wildlife and Parks, and serving as a camp counselor in…

Eventually, he discovered his passion for dentistry, a field that combines engineering, biology, art, and meaningful human connection. Dr. Romine values the chance to continually grow in his profession and is committed to helping patients feel at ease in the dental chair and confident in their smiles.
