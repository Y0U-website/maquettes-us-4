The Gold Standard for Tooth Replacement

A missing tooth affects more than just your appearance; it impacts your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. Dental implants are widely regarded as the premier solution for tooth loss, serving as a complete replacement for both the root and the crown of a natural tooth. At Tava Dental NY, we specialize in the art of implant restoration, crafting custom-made crowns that blend seamlessly with your smile to restore full function and aesthetics.

The Implant Journey

Strategic Planning

We begin with a comprehensive evaluation of your bone health and bite to ensure you are an ideal candidate for a long-lasting restoration.

The Foundation (Implant)

A titanium post is gently placed into the jawbone, acting as a sturdy artificial root that stimulates the bone and provides unshakeable stability.

The Connector (Abutment)

Once the implant has integrated with the jaw, a connector piece called an abutment is attached to the post to securely hold your new tooth in place.

The Restorative Transformation

Dr. Tavarez designs and places the final custom crown, ensuring the shape, shade, and fit match your natural teeth so perfectly that no one will know it is an implant.

  • Precision Fit: We use advanced impression techniques to create a restoration that fits your bite comfortably, preventing jaw strain or discomfort.

  • Long-Term Focus: Our practice prioritizes high-quality materials that are designed to withstand the forces of chewing for decades.

The Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Bone Preservation: Implants stimulate the jawbone just like natural roots, preventing the bone loss and "sunken" facial appearance that often follows tooth loss.
  • Unmatched Stability: Unlike dentures that can slip or click, implants are anchored securely in place, allowing you to eat your favorite foods without worry.
  • Protection of Neighbors: Unlike bridges, which require grinding down adjacent healthy teeth for support, implants stand independently.
  • Natural Feel: Because they are fused to the bone, implants feel and function exactly like your own teeth.
  • Durability: With proper care and hygiene, dental implants have a high success rate and can last a lifetime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults are good candidates for implants. The primary requirement is having enough healthy jawbone to support the implant post. If you have experienced bone loss, procedures such as bone grafting may be necessary to build a solid foundation before placement. Certain medical conditions, such as uncontrolled diabetes or active gum disease, may need to be managed before treatment begins.

Is the dental implant process painful?

Patient comfort is our top priority. The surgical placement is typically performed under local anesthesia, meaning you should not feel pain during the procedure. Most patients report that the post-operative discomfort is mild—often less than a tooth extraction—and can be easily managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.

How long does the entire process take?

The implant process is a journey that prioritizes healing. After the titanium post is placed, it typically takes 3 to 6 months for "osseointegration" to occur—the process where the bone fuses to the implant. Once this bond is strong, Dr. Tavarez can place the final custom crown. While it requires patience, this healing time is critical for the long-term success of the new tooth.

How do I care for my dental implant?

One of the best features of a dental implant is that it requires the same care as a natural tooth. You should brush twice a day, floss daily (paying special attention to the gum line around the implant), and visit Dr. Tavarez for regular cleanings. Keeping the gum tissue around the implant healthy is vital to preventing infection and ensuring the implant lasts a lifetime.
