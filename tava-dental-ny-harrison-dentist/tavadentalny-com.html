About Us

Under the leadership of Dr. Chantal Tavarez, Tava Dental NY continues its long-standing tradition of serving the community with personalized, patient-first dentistry. Whether you’re looking for preventive care, cosmetic dentistry, restorative treatments, or emergency dental services, our team is here to help you achieve and maintain a healthy, confident smile.

We proudly welcome patients of all ages and accept most major PPO insurance plans, making quality dental care accessible for individuals and families throughout Westchester County.
