Dental Implant Failure and Salvage

It’s worth noting that there can be multiple causes for the symptoms above. However, should you notice any of these signs, the best course of action is to schedule an appointment with our team. We’ll perform a thorough examination to determine if dental implant failure and salvage is the best solution.

  • A loose dental implant
  • Bleeding, swelling, pus, or other signs of infection
  • Difficulty chewing with your implants

How Dental Implant Salvage Works

At your initial appointment, we’ll examine your mouth and inquire about your symptoms. Once we’ve identified the underlying cause, we’ll discuss possible treatment options. If there’s an infection, we may recommend a deep cleaning or antibiotics. For patients with severe damage, we may need to remove the dental implant post entirely to address the problem. After that’s been dealt with, we can place a new implant so you can enjoy a fully functioning grin!
