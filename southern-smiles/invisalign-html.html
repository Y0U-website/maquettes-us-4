A Smarter Way to Straighten Your Smile

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, virtually invisible aligners to gradually straighten your teeth without brackets or wires. Each set of aligners is precisely calibrated to shift your teeth into their ideal position, giving you a confident smile with minimal disruption to your daily life. Whether you have crowding, gaps, overbite, underbite, or crossbite, Invisalign offers a comfortable and discreet path to the smile you deserve.

Your Journey to a Straighter Smile

From consultation to your final reveal, here is what to expect every step of the way.

Initial Consultation

Meet with Dr. Canham for a comprehensive evaluation. We use digital scanning technology to create a precise 3D map of your teeth and discuss your smile goals.

Custom Treatment Plan

Using Invisalign’s advanced ClinCheck software, Dr. Canham designs your personalized treatment plan. You will see a digital preview of how your teeth will move at each stage and the projected final result.

Receive Your Aligners

Your custom aligners are fabricated using SmartTrack material for a comfortable, precise fit. You will receive several sets of aligners to wear in sequence, each for about one to two weeks.

Wear & Progress

Wear your aligners 20 to 22 hours per day, removing them only to eat, drink, brush, and floss. Switch to the next set of aligners on schedule as your teeth gradually shift into place.

Regular Check-Ins

Visit our office every six to eight weeks so Dr. Canham can monitor your progress, make any necessary adjustments, and provide your next sets of aligners.

Reveal Your New Smile

Once your treatment is complete, enjoy your beautifully aligned smile. We will fit you with a custom retainer to maintain your results for years to come.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about Invisalign treatment.

Treatment time varies depending on the complexity of your case. Most patients complete treatment in 12 to 18 months, though some simpler cases can be finished in as few as 6 months. Dr. Canham will provide a personalized timeline during your consultation.

Most patients experience mild pressure or discomfort for the first day or two after switching to a new set of aligners. This is normal and is a sign that the aligners are working. Any discomfort is typically much less than with traditional braces and subsides quickly.

The cost of Invisalign varies based on the complexity and duration of your treatment. Many dental insurance plans cover Invisalign just as they would traditional braces. We also offer flexible payment plans to make treatment affordable. Contact our office for a personalized estimate.

You should remove your aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water. This means you can enjoy all your favorite foods without restriction. Simply brush your teeth before putting the aligners back in.

Invisalign can treat a wide range of orthodontic issues including crowding, spacing, overbite, underbite, crossbite, and open bite. The best way to find out if Invisalign is right for you is to schedule a consultation with Dr. Canham for a personalized evaluation.

Rinse your aligners every time you remove them and clean them daily with a soft toothbrush and lukewarm water. Avoid using hot water, which can warp the material. The Invisalign cleaning system is also available for a deeper clean.
