Dr. James Canham, DDS

Dr. Canham graduated from the University of Illinois College of Dentistry in 1993. After practicing for seven years in suburban Chicago, he relocated to Hilton Head Island in 2000 and purchased the Southern Smiles practice.

With nearly 1,000 hours of advanced training, Dr. Canham specializes in cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, implants, endodontics, sedation dentistry, TMJ dysfunction, sleep apnea, and cosmetic dentures.

Dr. Canham is married to Jeanie and they have four children. In his free time, he enjoys golfing, coaching, spending time at the beach, and cheering on his favorite sports teams.
