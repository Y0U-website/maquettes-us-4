At Synergy Dental, we care about your smile and provide the highest quality comprehensive dental care that exceeds every expectation.

Dr. Danielle Pike and our experienced dental team are committed to providing exceptional, personalized dental care in a comfortable, stress-free, and friendly atmosphere. As skilled family dentistry professionals, with a personal commitment to our community, our team works diligently to ensure that every patient receives the dental care they deserve.

We go above and beyond standards for cleanliness and safety, as recommended by the American Dental Association (ADA) and OSHA.

We pride ourselves in offering our patients an exceptional environment where they can feel comfortable knowing their oral health is in good hands. We develop an individualized plan for each patient. From regular cleanings and exams to advanced restorative treatments and cosmetic dentistry, we are committed to you.

Visiting your dentist at least twice a year is critical to your dental and overall health. Good dental hygiene is incredibly important in preventing conditions such as tooth decay, gum disease, and other problems. We offer regular and periodontal cleanings, imaging, exams, and oral cancer screenings for both adults and children. Other preventative care services include athletic mouthguards…

We provide a variety of restorative services, including tooth-colored restorations, crowns, veneers, implant restorations, bridges, partials, and dentures. We use advanced technology and high-quality materials to give you the most effective, comfortable experience possible.

Dr. Pike was raised in Kiel, WI and studied at UW-Madison. After earning her undergraduate degree in Natural Sciences-Genetics, she went on to attend Marquette University School of Dentistry.
