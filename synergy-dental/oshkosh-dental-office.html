Meet Your Dentist

Skilled and Compassionate Professional Committed to Your Optimal Dental Health

Dr. Danielle Pike

Background

Dr. Pike was raised in Kiel, WI and studied at UW-Madison. After earning her undergraduate degree in Natural Sciences-Genetics, she went on to attend Marquette University School of Dentistry.

Dental Style

Since graduating from dental school, Dr. Pike has earned a great reputation for practicing high-quality dentistry and developing strong relationships. Her gentle nature puts patients at ease and helps them to enjoy their dental visits. A member of the Wisconsin Dental Association and the American Dental Association, Dr. Pike is deeply committed to providing patients with compassionate and comprehensive care.

Fun Facts

Dr. Pike, her husband Joe, and their two sons enjoy exploring Wisconsin’s beautiful parks and cheering for the Brewers at Miller Park. She is an avid reader, enjoys cooking, gardening, crafting, and board games (although her sons aren’t quite old enough to take her on).
