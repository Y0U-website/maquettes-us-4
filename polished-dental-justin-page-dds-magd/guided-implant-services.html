Guided Implant Services empower patients to embark on a transformative dental journey, where cutting-edge technology and precision come together to create a smile that radiates confidence and beauty. By choosing this innovative approach, you are not only investing in a dental solution but also embracing a path of personalized care and exceptional results.   • Digital Planning: Using specialized software, the dental team crafts a detailed treatment plan, considering the optimal positioning of the dental implants. This digital plan acts as a virtual guide throughout the procedure.
  • Fabrication of Surgical Guide: Based on the digital plan, a surgical guide is meticulously designed. This guide serves as a template for the implant placement, ensuring unparalleled accuracy during the surgery.
  • Implant Placement: With the surgical guide in place, the dental professional skillfully places the dental implants into the predetermined locations. The use of the guide guarantees that the implants are inserted with utmost precision, enhancing the overall success of the procedure.
  • Healing and Restoration: Following the implant placement, the patient undergoes a healing period during which the implants fuse with the jawbone through osseointegration. Once this process is complete, custom dental restorations, such as crowns or bridges, are fabricated and attached to the implants.
  • Follow-up Care: Regular follow-up visits are scheduled to monitor the healing progress and ensure the long-term success of the implant restoration. The dental team provides ongoing support and guidance to maintain the health and longevity of the implant-supported restoration.
