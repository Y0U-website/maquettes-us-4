Technology

At Shank Dental Care, advanced technology serves two essential purposes: It makes dental care more precise, comfortable, and effective for every patient, and it allows us to practice integrative dentistry at the highest level. The right tools enable us to detect problems earlier, treat more conservatively, minimize radiation and chemical exposure, and support your body s natural healing processes.

From digital imaging to dental lasers, our technology reflects our commitment to providing care that s scientifically advanced and aligned with biological dentistry principles. Please take a moment to learn more about what we offer and how it benefits you and your dental care.

Digital X-Rays & Sensors

Digital radiography uses up to 90 percent less radiation than traditional film x-rays while providing instant, high-resolution images. This technology allows us to detect problems earlier and more accurately while minimizing your radiation exposure. This is a crucial consideration in biological dentistry, where we prioritize reducing unnecessary stress on your body.

CBCT Cone Beam Technology

Digital Impressions

Digital scanning eliminates uncomfortable putty impressions by capturing precise 3-D images of your teeth in real time. More accurate than traditional impressions and more comfortable for patients, this technology allows us to design better-fitting restorations, including crowns, bridges, and oral appliances, without the gagging and discomfort of conventional impression materials.

Intraoral Cameras

These tiny cameras capture detailed images inside your mouth, allowing you to see exactly what Dr. Shank sees. Visualizing the situation helps you understand your diagnosis and treatment recommendations more clearly, supports better communication about your care, and creates a detailed record of your oral health over time.

Laser Dentistry

Our Fotona Lightwalker laser system enables drill-free cavity removal, gentle soft tissue procedures, and therapeutic treatments that promote healing. Lasers reduce the need for anesthesia, minimize bleeding and discomfort, and create cleaner treatment sites with less trauma to surrounding tissue, supporting faster recovery and better outcomes while reducing anxiety for sensitive patients.

Laser Cavity Detection

Advanced laser technology detects tooth decay in its earliest stages, often before it s visible on x-rays or to the naked eye. Early detection means we can treat cavities when they re small, preserving more healthy tooth structure and often avoiding the need for anesthesia, supporting our conservative, minimally invasive approach.

CEREC® Same-Day Restorations

CEREC technology allows us to design, mill, and place ceramic crowns in a single appointment. There are no multiple trips to our office, no temporary crowns to deal with, and no messy, uncomfortable impressions. Using all-ceramic materials means biocompatible, metal-free restorations that look natural and function beautifully, completed while you wait

Ozone Generators

These systems produce ozonated water and ozone gas used throughout treatment to disinfect cavities, irrigate gum pockets, and sterilize surgical sites. Ozone s natural antimicrobial properties reduce harmful bacteria without harsh chemicals or antibiotics, supporting cleaner treatment sites, faster healing, and better integration with your body s natural immune response.

Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas)

This safe, mild sedation helps anxious patients feel relaxed and comfortable during treatment. Nitrous oxide takes effect quickly, wears off completely within minutes after your appointment, and allows you to drive yourself home. It s an excellent option for patients with dental anxiety or sensory sensitivities.

Digital Records

Comprehensive digital charting, imaging, and treatment records allow us to track your oral health over time, share information securely with other healthcare providers when needed, and access your complete history instantly. Digital records also eliminate paper waste and support more efficient, environmentally conscious practice operations.
