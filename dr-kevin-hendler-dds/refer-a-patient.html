Referring Provider Support Line

Our Referring Provider Support Line is easy to use, just dial our toll-free number 800-22-EMORY or 404-778-5050 . You will be connected with an Emory representative who can help you identify and schedule an appointment with an Emory specialist that meets your patient’s needs.

If you need to speak directly with an Emory physician, our representatives can have one of our physicians contact you within minutes.

If you have Epic workflow questions or need assistance resolving other concerns, our representatives can also connect you with "physician at-the-elbow" support.
