Media Contacts

The media relations team works with local, national and international print and broadcast media and other external audiences to showcase Emory Healthcare’s physicians, staff, research, and medical services. The team also works reactively, responding to media inquiries on a variety of medical-related issues.

The Emory Health Sciences Communications office is responsible for handling all external media relations for the medical enterprise. The office handles both general media relations and research communications.

Direct all other requests to the Emory Healthcare Market.
