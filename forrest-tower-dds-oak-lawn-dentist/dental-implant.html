What Are Implants?

Dental implants in Oak Lawn are permanent replacement teeth made from long-lasting compounds. Implants look and feel like real teeth. Unlike dentures, implants are affixed to your jaw. With implants, you will get back the full function of having a full set of teeth.

How Do I Get Implants?

You will need to have 2 surgeries for implants. During the first surgical procedure, Dr. Tower will affix metal posts to your jawbone. It takes between 3 and 6 months to fully heal from the first surgery. After your healing is complete, you will need to come back for a second procedure.

This surgery consists of fastening tony extensions to the metal posts. It is on these extensions that your implants will be affixed. Following a brief healing period, you can resume your normal diet.
