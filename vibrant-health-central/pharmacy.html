PHARMACY FAQ

Who can use the pharmacy?

Established patients can use the Vibrant Health Pharmacy. An established patient is an individual who has completed our Yearly Consent to Treat registration form and been seen by a Vibrant Health provider.

How much will my medications cost?

Medication prices will vary, but Vibrant Health strives to ensure patients have access to the medicine they need at affordable prices. Patients that have successfully applied for our Sliding Fee Discount Program at their appointment will also be eligible for pharmacy discounts. Discounts are available to insured patients will depend on whether they’ve successfully applied for the Sliding Fee Discount Program, their insurance plan, and the plan’s payment requirements. We encourage our patients to speak with a provider or call us directly to explore their potential savings.

Can I get my medications delivered?

Yes! We offer delivery services for all medications to Kansas City area residents.

How do I start using this pharmacy?

An established Vibrant Health patient can have their prescriptions sent to our pharmacy by:

  • Asking Vibrant Health staff, at check-in, to update their preferred pharmacy to ours in their patient account;
  • Asking their provider to send their prescriptions to our pharmacy during a visit;
  • If there is no upcoming appointment, call 913-233-4452 for our Meeks Pharmacy or call 913-222-8838 for our Central Pharmacy.
