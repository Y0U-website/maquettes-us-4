Behavioral Health

Vibrant Health’s team of behavioral health specialists are here to help with a variety of mental health needs for patients of all ages.

  • Depression
  • Anxiety
  • Trauma
  • Isolation
  • Behavioral Issues
  • Self-Harm
  • Hyperactivity

Vibrant Health offers telehealth visits for new and established behavioral health patients.

Telehealth visits are a great option to meet with a behavioral health specialist from the comfort of your own home. Behavioral health televisits use a patient portal technology called Zoom. For more information on this technology, see the patient guides available in English and Spanish.
