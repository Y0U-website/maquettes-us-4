What Can Implant Dentistry Provide You With?

Implant dentistry has advanced to provide patients with a durable, convenient, and superior alternative to their original tooth with the help of artificial implants. Artificial implants will help to restore the natural look and aesthetics of the teeth and facial structure.

Here are some of the implant dentistry options that we offer at our facility.

A dental implant is a small Titanium screw that is implanted into the jawbone where the tooth has been extracted from. This will provide a firm base to place an artificial tooth or a set of artificial teeth that would appear exactly like natural ones.

An implant is a metal stud that has screw-like grooves on its surface and will be inserted into the jawbone. The crown will then be fixed over the stud that will act as the replacement tooth. It is attached to the metal stud with the help of an abutment (a connecting element) and dental adhesives.
