ABOUT DR. LEE

Facts About Your Next Dentist

Dedicated to Patient-Centered Care

Dr. Lee prioritizes a patient-first approach, ensuring comfort and satisfaction in every aspect of dental care. He believes that patient education is essential, empowering individuals to make informed decisions about their oral health. He takes the time to discuss treatment options, answer questions, and ensure patients fully understand their dental needs. His goal is to provide compassionate, comprehensive care while fostering a trusting and open relationship with every patient.

EDUCATION AND EXPERTISE

Dr. Lee earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry and further honed his skills through an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency on Maryland’s Eastern Shore. He spent five years practicing near Hagerstown, MD, where he gained extensive experience in oral surgery, @Invisalign, and dental implants. A dedicated lifelong learner, Dr. Lee continually advances his expertise through continuing education courses to provide the latest and most effective treatments for his patients.

LIFE IN FREDERICK

Dr. Lee is a proud resident of Frederick, MD , where he enjoys staying active and exploring the local area with his dog, Lucy. He prioritizes fitness and takes advantage of the region’s outdoor activities. As a passionate sports fan, he loves cheering on the Washington Capitals and Nationals , attending as many games as possible.
