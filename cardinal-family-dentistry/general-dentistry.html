At Cardinal Family Dentistry

At Cardinal Family Dentistry , we focus on preventive care and education to ensure that our patients have healthy mouths and strong teeth. We offer preventive services like sealants and periodontal care, and we also offer solutions like crowns and fillings for common dental issues.

The American Dental Association recommends that you see a dentist for regular checkups every 6 months. During these appointments, your teeth will be thoroughly cleaned and checked for any dental issues, and this is the most important step in maintaining a healthy smile.

During your regular checkups, Dr. Barker will assess your dental health to make sure that you are free from dental issues such as cavities, periodontal disease, or damaged teeth. Dr. Barker will also thoroughly clean your teeth with professional grade tools to make sure that no plaque or tartar remain under your gums in order to avoid issues in the future.

Tooth decay, also known as a cavity, occurs when bacteria living in your mouth begins to eat away at your teeth. Untreated tooth decay can lead to an infection and extreme pain that can only be treated with a root canal or extraction.

Root canals are a fairly common procedure, but the extent of dental work can vary from case to case. This means that the cost of a root canal is dependent on the specific treatment you need. At Cardinal Family Dentistry, we aim to help all of our patients keep the cost of treatment within your budget.
