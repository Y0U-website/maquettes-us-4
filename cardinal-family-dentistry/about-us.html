Meet Dr. Jowza

Dr. Marmar Jowza, dds

Dr. Marmar Jowza grew up in New Jersey. She received her undergraduate degree in Chemistry with minors in Biology and Psychology from The College of New Jersey and later earned her Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree (D.D.S) from New York University College of Dentistry. Dr. Jowza is highly committed to clinical excellence and passionate about providing the highest quality care to her patients. She currently resides in Raleigh with her husband, Billy, and their children, William and Ella.
