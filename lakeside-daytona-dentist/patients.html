Financing from our partners

Some of our patients want the ability to chew and taste amazing food again. Some of our patients want that smile that everyone compliments. Some of our patients simply want to feel better about themselves with a beautiful smile. Whatever your smile goals are, you can afford to get what you want.

CareCredit Financing

CareCredit is a third-party financier that works like a credit card reserved solely for your medical and dental expenses. Simply swipe it at the time of your appointment and then make payments at the previously-determined rate that suits you and your budget best. Most people are approved for CareCredit!

Happen Financing

With personalized loan options from Happen, you can borrow thousands in order to cover the costs of your next dental treatment. Whether your dental insurance doesn’t cover all of your costs or you don’t have insurance at all, Happen makes it easy to select your rate, term and payment option that fits your budget best. To apply and see if you qualify, feel free to follow the link below to learn more!

Proceed Finance Financing

Attractive extended repayment terms put affordable payments in reach. Get pre-qualified without impacting your credit score.

Cherry Financing

Treat Now. Pay Monthly. Let your money go further and take better control of your cash flow when you pay in smaller, more manageable monthly installments with Cherry.
