A post is placed into the jawbone, bone integrates with its surface over several months, and a crown is fitted onto it. Unlike a bridge, neighbouring teeth are left untouched. The stages of implant treatment Implant treatment is a sequence rather than a single appointment, and the timeline is driven by healing rather than scheduling. Assessment, including imaging to evaluate bone and nearby structures
  • Any preparatory treatment, such as an extraction or bone grafting
  • Placement of the implant under local anesthetic
  • A healing period while bone integrates with the implant
  • Fitting of the abutment and the final crown

Why the healing stage cannot be rushed

The bond between bone and implant is what allows the restoration to function long term, and it forms at a biological pace. Where the gap is visible, a temporary replacement can usually be provided during this period.

Factors that affect candidacy

Most adults in reasonable health may be candidates. Bone volume, controlled gum disease, smoking, uncontrolled diabetes, and certain medications affecting bone all influence suitability and are reviewed before treatment is recommended.

Treatment usually spans several months because bone needs time to integrate with the implant. Dr. Zou will outline an expected timeline for your case after evaluating your bone and gum health.

Candidacy depends on bone volume, gum health, smoking, and certain medical conditions and medications. An assessment with appropriate imaging is what determines whether an implant is suitable for you.

An implant replaces the tooth root and does not involve the neighbouring teeth. A bridge is supported by the adjacent teeth, which are prepared to hold it. Which is appropriate depends on those teeth and the bone present.
