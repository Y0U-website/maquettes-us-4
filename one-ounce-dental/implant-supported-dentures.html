What treatment involves

Two or more implants are placed and left to integrate with the bone over several months. Attachments are then fitted and the denture is made or adapted to clip onto them. A denture is generally worn throughout treatment.

Ongoing maintenance

The denture still comes out for cleaning. The attachments include small wearing components that are replaced periodically as routine maintenance, and the fit is reviewed at your visits.
