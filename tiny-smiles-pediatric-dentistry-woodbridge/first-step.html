Recommended Scheduling

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends scheduling your baby’s first dental visit when the first tooth erupts, usually around one to two years of age. Early visits to a skilled pediatric dentist can prevent future dental issues that might require restorative treatments or orthodontic care.

Services

Regular dentist appointments, but especially your baby’s first dental visit, provides the best care and prevents future oral health problems. At the best New York City or New Jersey dental practice, the staff uses the best technology to provide an effective yet comfortable first dentist visit for your child. Your dentist usually performs an oral examination to identify any issues with erupting baby teeth, as well as the jaws, bite, gums, tongue and cheek.

The pediatric team may take x-rays of your child’s mouth to check for any problems below the gum line in the growth and development of your child’s teeth.

A top pediatric dentist may carry out gentle dental cleaning to remove any plaque or tartar. The cleaning prevents tooth decay and other oral health issues.

Your child’s dentist takes time to explain and demonstrate the best oral care procedures to your child, including proper brushing and flossing techniques.
