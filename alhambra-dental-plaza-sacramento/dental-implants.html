Implant-Supported Bridges

For patients missing several teeth in a row, our implant experts can design a custom implant-supported bridge to close the gap and restore your smile. Unlike partial dentures, this solution is fixed in place and cannot be removed.

To create an implant-supported bridge, our Periodontal Specialist places a dental implant at each end of the gap to provide a strong, solid base for your bridge. The bridge itself consists of a crown at either end and false teeth in the middle. Our specialist will bond the crowns to each implant, giving you a permanent tooth replacement solution that looks beautiful and functions just like your other healthy teeth.
