  • Higher costs and more complex treatment later: Postponing orthodontic treatment can result in more severe dental issues that require complex and costly procedures in the future. Early intervention can help prevent these problems, making treatment more manageable and potentially less expensive.

To begin your treatment, you’ll visit our Alhambra Dental Plaza team to determine your needs and goals for your smile. Treatment with Invisalign at Alhambra Dental Plaza takes, on average, 12 months, although your treatment time may differ depending on the corrections needed. So after only about a year, you’ll have a beautiful new smile that you will love showing off.

Traditional Orthodontics

Dr. Do and the team at Alhambra Dental Plaza are also proud to offer orthodontic treatment with traditional metal braces. Traditional metal braces are the ideal treatment for many people in order to correct a variety of alignment and bite issues. With advances in technology, braces are now more comfortable and discreet than ever.

Traditional orthodontics work through a combination of brackets, wires, and bands that put tension on the teeth and slowly move them into proper positions. Our team of orthodontic specialists will develop a personalized treatment plan, adjusting your braces at each appointment to gradually bring your teeth into perfect alignment. Within an average of 24 months, you’ll witness your smile transform right before your eyes.

Retainers No matter what orthodontic treatment is best for you, Dr. Do always recommends a retainer for post-treatment care. Wearing a retainer after your orthodontic treatment helps ensure that teeth don’t shift over time and that your treatment continues to be successful for years, even decades. As part of your post-treatment follow-up, Alhambra Dental Plaza will fit you for a retainer that meets your needs. Wear it on the recommended schedule to keep your smile looking and functioning at its best.

The Importance Of Early Orthodontics

Dr. Do and the Alhambra Dental Plaza team believe that early orthodontic care is crucial for children and teens. During these developmental stages, the jaw and dental arch are more receptive to treatment—allowing for less invasive methods to achieve greater outcomes; additionally, early intervention can prevent more severe problems and reduce treatment duration down the line.

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have their first orthodontic evaluation by the age of seven.
