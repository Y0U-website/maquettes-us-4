Accessibility Statement

Physical Accessibility

Our dental office is designed to be accessible to all patients:

  • Wheelchair accessible entrance and facilities
  • Accessible parking spaces available
  • Wide doorways and hallways
  • Accessible restroom facilities
  • Adjustable dental chairs for patient comfort
  • Clear pathways throughout the office
  • Visual and auditory accommodations available upon request

Assistive Technologies

Our website is designed to work with assistive technologies, including:

  • Screen readers (JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver)
  • Voice recognition software
  • Keyboard-only navigation
  • Screen magnification software
  • Alternative input devices

Ongoing Efforts

We are continuously working to improve accessibility by:

  • Regular accessibility audits and testing
  • Staff training on accessibility best practices
  • Incorporating accessibility into our design process
  • Staying current with accessibility standards and guidelines
  • Gathering feedback from users with disabilities
  • Making necessary improvements and updates

Third-Party Content

Some content on our website may be provided by third-party services. While we strive to ensure all content meets accessibility standards, we cannot guarantee the accessibility of third-party content. We work with our vendors to address accessibility concerns when possible.

Alternative Formats

If you need information from our website in an alternative format, such as large print, audio, or Braille, please contact our office. We will work to provide the information in a format that meets your needs within a reasonable timeframe.

Compliance Status

We aim to conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards. While we strive for full compliance, we recognize that accessibility is an ongoing process. We are committed to making continuous improvements to ensure our website and services are accessible to all users.

Updates to This Statement

This accessibility statement will be updated as we make improvements to our website and services. We review and update this statement regularly to reflect our current accessibility efforts and compliance status.
