Dr. Eli Shteingart

Dr. Shteingart is passionate about helping people and working with his hands, so dentistry was the perfect profession for him! He finds helping patients in pain feel better and changing patients lives through dentistry extremely rewarding and one of his favorite parts of the job.

He also takes pride in working with an efficient and dedicated team because the dental office runs smoothly, which improves the patient experience.

When he s not caring for patients, Dr. Shteingart enjoys playing basketball, running along the river in Fair Lawn, and especially playing catch with his kids. He is also highly involved in community work, working for his local synagogue, from the youth committee to the building committee.

Dr. Uliana Stomachenko

Dr. Uliana is a highly skilled dentist who earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the UMDNJ/Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in New Jersey. She further advanced her expertise through an Advanced Education in General Dentistry program at NYU Langone Health and participated in the Comprehensive Dentistry program at NYU College of Dentistry. This combination of education and hands-on experience enables Dr. Uliana to provide high-quality dental care, enhancing her patients’ quality of life.

Dr. Uliana also holds a degree in dentistry from Ukraine, where she specialized in high-end aesthetic dentistry. She stays current with modern technology and techniques by attending continuing education courses each year, focusing on functional and aesthetic solutions.

Dr. Uliana brings both professionalism and warmth to her practice. Her genuine and humble personality helps build trust and empathy with her patients. Her goal is to alleviate pain and discomfort, improve function and the aesthetics of smiles, and enhance her patients’ lives through high-quality dental care.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Uliana enjoys spending time with family and friends, participating in CrossFit classes, hiking, and traveling.
