Can Dental Implants Help with Missing Teeth?

Dental implants are designed to serve as a long-lasting and reliable solution for missing teeth. They are thin, titanium posts that act as tooth roots, which are surgically inserted into the jawbone.

Over time, these implants fuse with the bone, providing a stable foundation for artificial teeth, such as dental crowns, bridges, or dentures.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Natural Look and Feel : Dental implants are crafted to look, feel, and function like natural teeth.
  • Preventing Bone Loss : Unlike other tooth replacement options, dental implants stimulate bone growth, preventing jawbone deterioration.
  • Durability : With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.
