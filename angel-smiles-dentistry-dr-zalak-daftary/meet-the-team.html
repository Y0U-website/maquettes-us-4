OUR DENTIST

Dr. Zalak Daftary

Dr. Zalak Daftary received her undergraduate degree from East Carolina University where she graduated with honors and completed her dental training at University of southern California.

Dr. Daftary performs a wide variety of dental services but is best known for her gentle touch and a calm, empathetic demeanor. Her highest priority is making sure that the needs and comfort of her patients are always met. She stays updated with the dental technology by routinely taking Continuing Education courses and is a member of the American Dental Association, Washington State Dental Association, and Seattle-King County Dental society.

When she s not busy with dentistry, she likes to practice yoga, meditate, go on a road trip, snow board, hike and kayak.
