About Smile 5 Family Dentistry

Smile 5 Family Dentistry was founded in 2009 with a simple mission: to provide exceptional dental care in a warm, welcoming environment where families feel comfortable and confident in their oral health journey.

What started as a small practice has grown into a comprehensive dental center, but our core values remain the same. We believe that quality dental care should be accessible, comfortable, and tailored to each patient’s unique needs.

Today, we’re proud to serve thousands of families in our community, providing everything from routine cleanings to complex procedures with the same level of care and attention we’d want for our own families

Dedicated to providing exceptional dental care for families in our community for over 15 years.
