Alexi Eyvazi, DDS

Dr. Alexi Eyvazi has been in the field of dentistry for over 20 years. He took his undergraduate studies in Physiological Science at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA). In 2005, Dr. Eyvazi, graduated from University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) School of Dentistry.

His passion for his field of study and patient care has shown time and again through his continued dedication to volunteerism and using his expertise to provide care for underserved patients both here and abroad. His passion and dedication spur him to seek continuing education opportunities in order to improve his skills and to keep abreast of new developments in the exciting field of dentistry.

Dr. Eyvazi is an active member of several dental and medical organizations including the American Dental Association (ADA), the California Dental Association (CDA), the San Fernando Valley Dental Society , the Armenian American Medical Society and the Glendale Academy of Dentists.

With a sympathetic ear, Dr. Eyvazi takes pride in being a good listener for patients and their concerns and strives to provide the most conservative and cost-effective treatment in a family-friendly atmosphere.

Dr. Eyvazi is married to Dr. Arina Hartunian. In their spare time, they enjoy traveling, reading, dancing and watching competitive sporting events.

Arina Hartunian, DDS

Dr. Arina Hartunian is a California native and a true Bruin. She received her bachelor’s degree, her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree, and her certificate in pediatric dentistry from UCLA. She has also completed advanced training at the Veteran’s Affairs Medical Center in West Los Angeles and at the Rancho Los Amigos Rehabilitation Center, where she worked with children with special needs. Dr. Arina is on the Council of the Southern California Society of Dentistry for Children and teaches part-time at one of UCLA’s Pediatric Dental Clinics.

Dr. Arina is not only committed to providing superior care to her patients, but as a parent, she also understands the joys and challenges of caring for children and their teeth. She uses her skills as a pediatric dentist and a mom to provide a playful and loving environment that makes visiting the dentist fun.

When not treating our amazing patients, Dr. Arina loves spending time with her husband, Dr. Alexi Eyvazi, and their son, Noah. She also enjoys traveling, dancing, listening to music and playing tennis.

Allison Yen, DDS, MS

Dr. Allison Yen graduated from the University of the Pacific, Dugoni School of Dentistry, where she graduated top 10 of her classes and was honored with many awards for academic excellence. Following her dental training, she completed a three-year postgraduate residency program in periodontics at the University of Illinois, at Chicago. During her training, Dr. Yen obtained a Master of Science (MS) degree in oral sciences from the University of Illinois where she conducted research in wound healing. She is trained to deliver oral conscious and intravenous (IV) sedation for patient comfort.

Dr. Yen is actively involved in numerous professional organizations, including the American Academy of Periodontology, the American Dental Association, and the California Dental Association.

Dr. Yen keeps up to date through many continuing educational classes, ensuring that her patients receive the most comprehensive and advanced dental care. In her spare time, she enjoys traveling, playing volleyball and tennis, watching movies, and spending time with her family.

Emin Hartunian, DDS

Dr. Hart, a Board-Certified Orthodontist, has cultivated a strong passion for creating beautiful smiles. Dr. Hart is a proud double Trojan. He began his dental education at USC’s Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry, and ultimately went on to obtain his specialty in Orthodontics by completing his training at USC Graduate Orthodontics Program where he served as Chief Resident. During his residency, he received several renowned scholarships with the most notable achievement being a technology scholar. He was also provided the opportunity to present his research both locally and internationally.
