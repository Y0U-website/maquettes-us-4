(512) 540-4644 Dental Implant Restoration 101 Frequently Asked Questions How long does a dental implant restoration typically last? Most dental implant restorations last 15 to 25 years, and many last a lifetime. The implant post itself rarely fails. The crown on top may need replacement after 15 or so years due to normal wear, similar to any dental crown.

It depends on how much bone remains. Your dentist will take a scan to measure bone density at the implant site. Some patients with mild bone loss still qualify. Others may need grafting first to build enough support for the post.

Twice-a-year checkups are standard, the same schedule as natural teeth. Your dentist checks the restoration for fit, examines the gum tissue around the implant, and takes periodic X-rays to confirm the bone is staying healthy.
