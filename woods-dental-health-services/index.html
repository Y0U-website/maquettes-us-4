The practice

We recognize the uniqueness of every person and are committed to advocating for and delivering best practice and high-quality integrated care that champions every individual’s potential for growth, well-being, dignity, and purpose.

0 K individuals served We are the largest network of providers in PA and NJ offering highly specialized services to people with challenges or disabilities.

Our affiliates and partners offer the widest range of integrated healthcare treatment options at all stages of life.

WSOC’s remarkable accumulation of knowledge and expertise demonstrates our rich history, depth of understanding, and a legacy of success that few organizations can rival. It speaks to a wealth of institutional wisdom and a capacity to adapt and thrive across generations.

National and Global Impact Through research, advocacy, and education, WSOC is helping reshape integrated healthcare across the nation and internationally to improve care for all people facing challenges around the world.

Meet Our Partners and Affiliates Each affiliate and partner plays a significant role in enabling Woods System of Care to provide a seamless continuum of integrated care throughout an individual’s lifespan.

WSOC is training the next generation of integrated healthcare providers to ensure that they have the specialized knowledge needed to provide world-class care to individuals with intellectual, developmental, and behavioral challenges.

Psychiatrists Universities and health systems that want to elevate the care their psychiatrists provide to people with disabilities turn to the Medical Center at Woods to develop fellowships and residencies for psychiatry students.
