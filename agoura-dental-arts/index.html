0 Dental Implants at Agoura Dental Arts A long-lasting solution for missing teeth that looks and feels completely natural.

A dental implant is a small titanium post designed to mimic the root of a natural tooth. During a minimally invasive outpatient procedure, it’s placed into the jawbone, where it gradually fuses with the surrounding bone. This process—known as osseointegration—provides a strong, stable foundation for your new tooth.

Depending on your bone quality, a tooth may even be placed on the implant the same day. In cases where bone loss has occurred, we may wait several months for the implant to fully integrate before completing your restoration.

The titanium post is inserted into the jawbone where the tooth is missing.

Over time, your jawbone bonds with the implant, creating a secure base.
