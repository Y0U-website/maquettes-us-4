Why You Might Need a Cavity Shield Treatment?

Do you have cavity-prone teeth or sensitive teeth? Consult a dentist to find out the suitable treatment option, and we recommend dental sealants for the following reasons:

Cavity Prevention:

Dental sealants guard the chewing surfaces where food and plaque build up, preventing decay and helping enamel stay strong.

Long-Term Protection:

With proper care, a tooth sealant can last years, saving you from more complex treatments.

Fast & Painless:

Applying sealants for adults or kids is non-invasive and takes just one visit.

Reduces Sensitivity:

Dental sealants for sensitive teeth help minimize reactions to heat, cold, and sweets by covering thin enamel.

Our Dental Coating Procedure

This professional-grade teeth coating procedure stops cavities before they lead to emergency tooth extractions. At Bloomington Family Dental, our step-by-step dental coating process ensures comfort and lasting protection:

  • Teeth Cleaning: Molars and premolars are cleaned and dried for the best results
  • Etching: A mild solution prepares the surface for strong bonding
  • Application: The tooth sealant is gently brushed onto grooves and ridges
  • Curing: A special light hardens the dental coating, sealing the tooth surface
  • Evaluation: The dentist checks the placement for smoothness and durability
