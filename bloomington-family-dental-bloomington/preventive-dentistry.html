Why Preventive Dentistry Matters

Neglecting your teeth might not seem like a big deal —until it is. Small issues like plaque and minor gum inflammation turn into painful (and costly) problems if they’re not taken care of early. Preventive dentistry saves you time, money, and discomfort by keeping your mouth healthy before problems start. It’s important to visit a local dentist like Dr. Gray every six months to keep your teeth and gums in good shape.

Deep Teeth Cleaning

Regular cleanings keep plaque and tartar at bay, but sometimes, you need a little extra care. Deep teeth cleaning, also called scaling and root planing, is a more thorough process that helps prevent and treat gum disease. If you’ve noticed bleeding gums, bad breath, or gum sensitivity recently, a deep cleaning might be just what you need to get things back on track.

Periodontal Maintenance

For folks in Bloomington dealing with gum disease, regular cleanings may not be enough. Periodontal maintenance goes beyond a standard cleaning, removing plaque and tartar buildup below the gumline to prevent further damage. Gum health is just as important as keeping your teeth strong, and this service helps ensure your entire mouth stays healthy.

Oral Hygiene Education

Preventive dentistry starts at home. That’s why we take the time to show you the right way to care for your teeth, so you can keep cavities and gum disease from creeping up.
