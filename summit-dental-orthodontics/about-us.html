About Us

Built for trust. Designed for the long term.

Summit Dental & Orthodontics was built around a simple belief: dentistry isn’t transactional—it’s a long-term relationship.

Every day, we guide families and adults in Layton through decisions that affect their health, confidence, and quality of life for years—sometimes decades. Our role isn’t to rush treatment or sell procedures. It’s to provide clear guidance, honest recommendations, and the clinical leadership people need to make decisions they can feel confident about long-term.

That philosophy shapes how we plan care, how we communicate, and how we show up for our patients—year after year.

A different kind of dental practice

Summit was built for families who want a long-term dental partner, not just a place to get procedures done.

We serve generations of families across Layton and surrounding communities by thinking in decades, not visits.

From routine care to orthodontics, surgery, implants, and complex restorative planning, we stay with families through every phase.

We plan before we treat We coordinate care instead of fragmenting it We guide instead of pressure This is why patients come to Summit when dental decisions matter most.
