Meet Dr. Simpson

Jonathan Simpson, DDS Pediatric dentist Dr. Jonathan Simpson and his dental team have been serving North Platte and the surrounding area since 2008. Dr. Simpson is a native Nebraskan and graduated from North Platte High School. He received his undergraduate degree from the University of Nebraska Lincoln and then went on to complete dental school and a pediatric residency at the University of Nebraska Medical Center.

Dr. Simpson returned to serve the North Platte community and surrounding areas. He is committed to improving children’s oral health and treats his young patients just as he would treat his own children: with genuine care and a gentle approach. Dr. Simpson is also a strong advocate for oral health education within the community. He continues to encourage better access to care for some of our state’s most vulnerable citizens—children.
