What Does My First Visit Include?

At Couzens Dental, your first dental visit is designed to help us understand your health, your concerns, and your goals before recommending care.

Our team will review with you what we see, discuss what treatment options you may have, and help you feel informed about any recommended next steps.

  • A comprehensive dental exam
  • A review of your health history and medications
  • Digital imaging and advanced diagnosis
  • Evaluation of your teeth and gum tissue
  • Oral cancer screening
  • Discussion of your questions, goals, and oral health decisions
