How Long Do Implant-Supported Dentures Last?

With proper care, your implants can last decades or even a lifetime. The denture itself may need replacement every 7-10 years due to normal wear.

Is The Denture Procedure Painful?

Most patients describe the process as comfortable. We offer sedation options to help you relax, and post-procedure discomfort is typically mild and manageable.

Can I Eat Normally With Implant-Supported Dentures?

Yes, you can enjoy most foods without worry. Hard or sticky foods should be eaten with care, but chewing is much easier than with traditional dentures.

How Do I Care For Implant-Supported Dentures?

Daily cleaning, regular dental visits and good oral hygiene are essential. Our team will show you the best way to care for your new smile.

What If I Don’t Have Enough Bone For Implants?

Bone grafting or alternative implant techniques may be possible. We will evaluate your needs and discuss all options during your consultation.
