What Is The All-on-4 Implant Process in Our Carrollwood Office?

The All-on-4 process is carefully planned to maximize comfort and results. Our team guides you through every step, from your first consultation to your final, beautiful smile.

  • Consultation: We assess your oral health, review your goals and take digital images to plan your treatment.
  • Implant Placement: Four implants are placed in the jawbone at precise angles for optimal support.
  • Temporary Teeth: A fixed temporary set of teeth is attached the same day, so you never go without a smile.
  • Final Restoration: After healing, your custom, permanent teeth are placed for a natural look and feel.

Who is a Candidate for All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 implants are ideal for adults who have lost most or all of their teeth or are frustrated with loose, uncomfortable dentures. Adequate jawbone is needed, but this technique often avoids the need for bone grafting. A personalized consultation will determine if All-on-4 is right for you.

Caring for Your All-on-4 Dental Implants

Maintaining your All-on-4 implants is simple and similar to caring for natural teeth. Good oral hygiene and regular dental visits will keep your new smile healthy and beautiful.

  • Brush twice daily with a soft toothbrush
  • Floss or use interdental brushes around the implants
  • Visit Tampa Dental Wellness of Carrollwood for routine checkups and cleanings
  • Avoid chewing on hard objects
  • Follow any specific care instructions from our team
