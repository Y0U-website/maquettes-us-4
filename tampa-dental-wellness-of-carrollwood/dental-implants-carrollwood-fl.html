What Is The Dental Implant Process in Our Carrollwood Office?

The dental implant process is carefully planned and performed to maximize your comfort. Our team will guide you every step of the way, from your initial consultation to your final restoration.

  • Consultation: We assess your oral health, review your goals, and take 3D images to plan your treatment.
  • Implant Placement: The titanium post is placed into your jawbone under local anesthesia or sedation.
  • Healing: Over several months, the implant fuses with your bone in a process called osseointegration.
  • Restoration: A custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implant for a natural look and feel.

Are Dental Implants Right for You?

Dental implants are ideal for adults with healthy gums and enough jawbone to support the implant. They may not be suitable for everyone, especially those with certain medical conditions or untreated gum disease. A personalized consultation with our team will determine if implants are the best choice for your needs.

During your visit, our dentists will review your health history, discuss your goals and recommend the best options for your smile, whether that’s a single implant, implant-supported bridge or another solution.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

With proper care, dental implants can last decades or even a lifetime.

Is The Dental Implant Procedure Painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort, and we offer sedation options for your comfort.

How Soon Can I Eat After Getting An Implant?

Soft foods are recommended for a few days, but you can gradually return to your normal diet as you heal.

Are Dental Implants Covered By Insurance?

Some insurance plans may cover part of the cost. Our team will discuss your benefits and financing options.

Can Implants Replace Multiple Teeth?

Yes, dental implants can support single crowns, bridges or even full dentures for multiple missing teeth.
