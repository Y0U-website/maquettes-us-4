Who is a Candidate for a Single Tooth Implant?

Most adults with healthy gums and adequate jawbone are good candidates for a single tooth implant. However, some medical conditions or untreated dental issues may affect your eligibility. A personalized consultation will determine if this option is right for you.

During your visit, our dentists will review your health history, examine your mouth, and discuss your goals. If a single tooth implant is not the best choice, we’ll recommend other solutions that fit your needs.

How to Care for Your Single Tooth Implant

Caring for your single tooth implant is simple and similar to caring for your natural teeth. Good oral hygiene and regular dental visits are key to keeping your implant healthy and strong.

Steps for caring for your dental implant include the following:

  • Brush twice daily with a soft toothbrush
  • Floss gently around your implant and neighboring teeth
  • Visit Tampa Dental Wellness of Carrollwood for regular checkups and cleanings
  • Avoid chewing on hard objects like ice or pens
  • Follow any special care instructions from our team
