What Age Should My Child First Visit a Dentist for Orthodontics?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. Early visits allow us to identify bite or alignment issues before they progress. How Do I Care for Braces and Retainers During Treatment?

Proper care is essential to maintaining healthy teeth during orthodontic treatment. Patients should:

At Vivere Meliora Dentistry, we provide patient education and support so you feel confident caring for your braces or retainers.

  • Brush and floss regularly with special attention around brackets and wires
