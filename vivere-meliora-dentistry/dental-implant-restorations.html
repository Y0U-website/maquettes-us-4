The Dental Implant Restoration Process

Initial Consultation

During your initial visit, Dr. Sanders will conduct a thorough examination of your oral health, including digital X-rays and 3D scans, to assess the condition of your jawbone and determine the best treatment plan.

Placement of the Implant

The dental implant, a small titanium post, is surgically placed into the jawbone. This procedure is performed under local anesthesia, and sedation options are available for added comfort.

Healing and Osseointegration Phase

Over the next few months, the implant(s) need to heal and fuse with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration . This creates a solid foundation for the replacement tooth.

Attaching the Abutment and Crown

Once the implant has fully integrated, an abutment is attached to the implant post. A custom-made crown is then secured to the abutment, completing the restoration and providing a natural-looking tooth.

Post-Procedure Care

Regular check-ups are essential to monitor the implant’s integration and overall oral health. Dr. Sanders will provide detailed care instructions to ensure the longevity of your dental implant. Regular professional cleanings and exams will help keep your implant and surrounding tissues healthy.

Benefits of Dental Implant Restorations

  • Improved Oral Function: Dental implant restorations significantly enhance the ability to chew and speak without discomfort or difficulty. Unlike traditional dentures, implants function like natural teeth, allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods and speak clearly.
  • Aesthetic Advantages: Implants are designed to blend seamlessly with your existing teeth. They help maintain the natural shape of your face and prevent the sunken appearance often associated with tooth loss, giving you a confident and attractive smile.
  • Durability and Maintenance: Dental implants are designed to last many years, often a lifetime, with proper care. Routine oral hygiene practices, along with regular dental check-ups, ensure the longevity and success of your implants.
