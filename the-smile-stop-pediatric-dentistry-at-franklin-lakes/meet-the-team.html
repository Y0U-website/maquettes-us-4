Meet the Team

Dr. Kye

Dr. Jenny Kye was born and raised in Southern California and is currently residing in New York City. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree with Honors from the University of California, San Francisco, where she also served as Academic Chair. Dr. Jenny went on to pursue her specialty degree in pediatric dentistry at the University of Southern California, where she received extensive training in behavior management, sedation, and treating children with special healthcare needs.

Dr. Jenny is passionate about providing high-quality dental care for children in a caring and compassionate way. She recognizes that early childhood is a crucial time to establish healthy oral hygiene habits and positive attitudes toward dental visits. She feels honored to play a role in each child’s dental journey and to help lay the groundwork for a lifetime of strong oral health.

Dr. Jenny is a board-certified diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. In her free time, Dr. Jenny enjoys traveling and immersing herself in new cultures and cuisines. Her hobbies include cooking, painting, hiking, and cherishing quality moments with her family and friends.

Dr. Parmar (Dr. P)

Dr. Mrunal Parmar graduated from New York University (NYU) with a Bachelor of Arts and Science in Biology before earning his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from NYU College of Dentistry. Dr. Parmar was admitted to the highly sought-after Pediatric Dentistry Residency Program at Montefiore Medical Center in New York City.

Dr. Parmar is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist and a proud member of the American Dental Association and the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry . He is married, has two boys, enjoys exercising, traveling and basketball in his spare time.
