Enjoy A Same-Day Smile

You may think that dental implants aren’t for you. Perhaps they’re out of your budget, or maybe you’ve experienced too much bone loss for conventional dental implants. If you want a long-term solution to your tooth loss but fear there isn’t one, Full Arch Implant Bridges could be exactly what you’re looking for! The term “Full Arch Implant Bridges” refers to the fact that this procedure allows a full arch of teeth to be supported by just four dental implants. That’s great news for many reasons:

  • You can get a full-mouth restoration rather than several individual crowns that will take longer to heal.
  • You can enjoy a same-day smile – you’ll walk out with teeth the same day you come in for implants!
  • You can prevent any further bone loss and decrease the likelihood you’ll need a bone graft.
  • You can take advantage of our affordable pricing – this method of dental implants costs less than conventional implants.
