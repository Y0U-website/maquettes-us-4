AJ Davis, DMD, MS

Dr. Davis grew up locally in Salem. He attended Midwestern University-Arizona where he received his Doctorate of Medical Dentistry in 2015. Upon graduation, he entered service on active duty in the army and attended the Fort Campbell 12-month Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) program. Later, he completed a two-year AEGD in Hawaii where he graduated with a master’s degree in oral biology and pinned the rank of Major in the US Army .

After eight years of active duty, he completed his Army career in Fort Hood, Texas, where he served as the OIC (officer in charge) of the Fairbank Dental Clinic and worked part-time as a comprehensive dentist in a civilian office.

Dr. Davis is board certified with the American Board of General Dentistry , is fellowshipped with the Academy of General Dentistry , and is a Qualified Dentist with the American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine . His professional memberships include the American Board of General Dentistry , American Dental Association , Academy of General Dentistry , American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine , and American Academy of Sleep Medicine .

In his spare time, he enjoys adventuring with his wife and six kids, scouting, camping, gardening, farming, and anything outdoors. He has a passion for helping people get out of pain and love the smile they see in the mirror.
