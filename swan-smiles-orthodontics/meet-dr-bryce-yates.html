Bryce Yates, DDS

Dr. Bryce Yates is excited to be a part of the amazing team at Swan Smiles & Orthodontics and Nephi Smiles & Orthodontics. Like Dr. Swan, Dr. Yates wants patients to have a great experience at the dentist and receive the best care possible. He is committed to the traditional values of honesty and integrity while providing a modern experience to improve comfort and quality of care.

Dr. Yates was born and raised in Brigham City. He attended Utah State University and received his bachelor’s in nutrition science. After graduation, he spent four years in the beautiful province of Nova Scotia attending dental school at Dalhousie University .

Before coming to Swan Smiles, he practiced in Price. While there, he gained experience with the latest technology in restorative dentistry, dentures, oral sedation, implants, surgery, and more. He completed two years of working with children and treating them in hospital and office settings with the use of general anesthesia.

Dr. Yates has spent more than 100 hours of continued education learning: full-arch reconstruction, implantology, and occlusion and sleep dentistry. He is committed to improving himself to better help his patients. He is currently taking continued education in a wide variety of subjects, such as atraumatic tooth removal, immediate implant placement, the use of autologous growth factors, and more.

Bryce and his wife have four kids: two boys and two girls. He loves taking his family on adventures, whether they be local or a road trip away. He considers a day spent with his family outside hiking, biking, swimming, or just enjoying a park as a day well spent. He also enjoys playing the piano, watching a good movie, and cooking in the kitchen.

Dr. Yates hopes that when you come to the office he can help you feel comfortable in understanding your specific needs and the options available to keep your smile healthy and strong for a lifetime.
