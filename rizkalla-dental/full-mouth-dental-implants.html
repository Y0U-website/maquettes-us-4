Are Dental Implants Right for Me?

Most patients qualify for full arch dental implants with today’s minimally invasive techniques, provided they have overall stable health. Adequate bone structure is essential for optimal implant function and longevity, although patients with significant bone loss may require additional procedures to rebuild the necessary foundation.

Severe bone loss can occasionally compromise outcomes with full mouth dental implants. Certain medical conditions, such as bleeding disorders, immunosuppressive medications, and active periodontal disease, may necessitate alternative approaches to rebuilding a patient’s smile.
