What Sets Us Apart

At Bullen Beckstrand Orthodontics , we believe a beautiful smile is more than just straight teeth—it’s about confidence, health, and long-term well-being. As proud residents of Mohave County , Dr.’s Ryan Bullen and Jake Beckstrand are dedicated to providing exceptional orthodontic care to our local community. We don’t just create smiles; we create lasting relationships built on trust, honesty, and high-quality treatment.

A Health-First Approach to Orthodontics

Our philosophy goes beyond aesthetics. While we love helping our patients achieve stunning smiles, we also focus on preserving and maximizing the airway to promote better breathing, sleep quality, and overall health. Proper airway development is a crucial part of our treatment planning, ensuring that your results go beyond appearance and contribute to lifelong wellness.

Affordable, Comprehensive Treatment

At Bullen Beckstrand Orthodontics , we are proud to offer the most comprehensive and affordably priced orthodontic care in all of Mohave County . We believe that financial concerns should never stand in the way of a confident smile. That’s why we offer zero-interest payment plans, paid-in-full discounts, and price matching to make high-quality treatment accessible to every family.

Conservative, Honest Care You Can Trust

We know that orthodontic treatment is a big decision, and we will never pressure you into a plan you’re uncomfortable with. Both Dr. Ryan and Dr. Jake are conservative providers who prioritize your needs, providing honest recommendations and personalized treatment options. We take the time to educate you, so you can make the best decision for your health, lifestyle, and budget.

We Treat You Like Family

From the moment you walk through our doors, you’ll feel the difference. At Bullen Beckstrand Orthodontics , we treat every patient as if they were our own family—with kindness, respect, and a genuine commitment to your well-being. Your comfort and confidence are our top priorities, and we’re here to support you through every step of your orthodontic journey.
