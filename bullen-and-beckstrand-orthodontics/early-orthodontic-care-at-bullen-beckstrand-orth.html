When Should Your Child See an Orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age seven. While most kids won’t need treatment right away, early checkups allow us to track jaw development, tooth alignment, and bite issues—helping us address potential concerns before they become more complex. Early intervention can often simplify future treatment and lead to the best possible results.

Signs Your Child May Need Early Orthodontic Treatment

Some dental or jaw issues are easier to correct when caught early. Keep an eye out for these common warning signs that may indicate your child could benefit from orthodontic care: ✔ Crowded, crooked, or overlapping teeth ✔ Gaps between teeth or irregular spacing ✔ Protruding front teeth or a misaligned bite (overbite, underbite, crossbite) ✔ Difficulty biting, chewing, or speaking clearly ✔ Mouth breathing or thumb sucking beyond age five
