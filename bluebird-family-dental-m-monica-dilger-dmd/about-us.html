A little about Dr. Dilger

Hi, I’m Dr. Monica Dilger, owner of Bluebird Family Dental. As a Nazareth resident, I’m excited to provide care to the community I call home. I graduated from Temple University Kornberg School of Dentistry and have been practicing dentistry locally for the past eight years. Nazareth is such a tight-knit community and being able to open a practice here is so exciting for me!

Outside the office, I enjoy spending time with my husband and our two kids. We love all that Nazareth and the surrounding communities have to offer, from local events to outdoor adventures in Jacobsburg. Being part of this community means so much to me, and I look forward to building lasting relationships with every patient who walks through our doors.

Our Mission At Bluebird Family Dental , we are committed to providing high-quality dental care with a focus on integrity, comfort, and long-term oral health. Dr. Dilger and her dedicated team believe that every patient deserves honest, compassionate care tailored to their unique needs. We prioritize cost transparency, ensuring our patients fully understand their treatment options without surprises. From routine cleanings to advanced procedures, we take the time to educate and empower our patients, helping them make informed decisions about their dental health.

Dr. Dilger and her team are dedicated to providing high-quality, compassionate dental care with honesty and transparency, ensuring patients feel confident in their treatment choices.
