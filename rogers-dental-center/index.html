Welcome to Rogers Dental Center, the office of Dr. Shana Heisler , Dr. Jeff Smith , and Dr. Matt Dale . Since 1988, we have been helping our patients achieve optimal oral health and a beautiful smile. We serve the areas of Rogers , St. Michael, Albertville, Dayton, Hanover, Buffalo, Maple Grove, Elk River, Zimmerman, and Corcoran as well as many other communities.

Our doctors and staff care about you and your dental needs. We work as a team to provide personalized and thorough treatment to achieve comprehensive dental wellness. We are highly skilled and trained in several areas of dentistry, including preventative and general dentistry , periodontics (gum disease), cosmetic dentistry , and pediatric dentistry .

We offer a full range of services, from Cerec same-day crowns and teeth whitening, dental implants— all tailored to meet the individual needs of each patient. Our office is equipped with state-of-the-art technology to ensure you receive the best possible care.

Working together, we can assist you in maintaining a healthy, beautiful smile for years to come. Contact us today to schedule your appointment!

Thank you to all our patients that continue to put their trust in us at Rogers Dental Center. We continue to satisfy the most current infection control protocols exceeding the standard of care, all while continuing to meet your dental needs. Hope to see you soon!
