Common Questions

Will applying hurt my credit?

No. All three providers use soft credit checks that don’t impact your credit score.

How much can I finance?

Approvals vary by provider and your credit profile. Cherry approves up to $35,000; LendingClub and Sunbit offer similar ranges for qualified applicants.

Can I combine financing with insurance?

Yes. We’ll bill your insurance first and you can finance the remaining balance.
