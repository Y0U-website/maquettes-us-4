Dr. Erica Elannan, DDS

Dr. Erica Elannan, D.D.S.

Trained at the University of Missouri–Kansas City. A native of St. Augustine, Florida, Dr. Erica Elannan was one of 18 students selected worldwide for UMKC’s six-year combined BA/DDS dental program at the age of 17. She completed her four-year BA in just two years, then earned her doctorate of dental surgery in six years instead of the typical eight.

Specialized in cosmetic and reconstructive dentistry. Dr. Elannan developed her aesthetic skills and artistic vision through a Phoenix-based cosmetic internship, graduating with honors in cosmetic, restorative, and reconstructive dental surgery. At age 23 she became one of the youngest practicing dentists in the country and held board certifications in multiple states.

Believes great dentistry should feel comfortable — and look natural. A perfectionist by nature, Dr. Elannan creates natural smiles of distinct, unparalleled beauty — changing lives and awakening inner self-confidence one smile at a time. Her research has produced a product line and book series aimed at the widespread caries epidemic plaguing our nation.

Proud Valley of the Sun resident. Dr. Elannan has lived in the valley for over 13 years, contributes to community service through publications and educational seminars, and resides locally with her fiancé and two beautiful children.
