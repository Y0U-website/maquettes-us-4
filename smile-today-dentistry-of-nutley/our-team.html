ELIZABETH

Elizabeth grew up in Kearny and graduated with a degree in Dental Hygiene from Bergen Community College. She has since enjoyed working in the dental field and educating her patients on how to achieve optimal health. Elizabeth loves interacting with patients and getting to know them on a personal level to truly make them feel like a part of our Smile Today family. She is married to her high school sweetheart and they have three adorable children and a cat, Ernie.

HARDI

Hardi studied to become a dentist in India and came to the USA in 2008. She graduated from the NYU Dental Hygiene program in 2012 to pursue her desire to help people maintain healthy smiles. She finds working at Smile Today to be a lot of fun and is proud of the quality, pace, and professional care we deliver to the community. She has been married to her husband for 9 wonderful years and they love spending time with their two sons.

PATRYCJA

Patrycja grew up in Wallington and moved to Poland where her passion for dentistry began. She came back to the US in 2018 to pursue a degree in Dental Hygiene. She was a dental assistant for years prior to becoming a hygienist. Patrycja’s main focus is educating patients on how to improve their oral health while striving to develop a long lasting, meaningful relationship with them. She is married to her high school sweetheart and they both enjoy winter sports such as skiing and snowboarding.

DANIELLA

Daniella (Dee) has been an integral part of Smile Today for over 12 years. She has experience in dental assisting, treatment planning, insurance, and patient finances. Dee goes above and beyond to make our patients feel like a part of our dental family and is always looking for new ways to improve our office and patient experience. When she is not working, Dee enjoys spending time with her 3 beautiful kids.

CHRISTINE

When she is not in the office, she is cheering on the Mets, going to the beach, and spending time with family and friends.
