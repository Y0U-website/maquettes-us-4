The Benefits of Phase 1 Orthodontics Encourage a Healthy Bite Children with jaw discrepancies or narrow palates may struggle to develop a proper bite. Left untreated, these issues can lead to speech difficulties, uneven tooth wear, or problems when permanent teeth erupt. Early interceptive orthodontics helps guide proper jaw development and reduces the risk of future complications.

Reduce Tooth Misalignment Phase 1 treatment creates space and helps permanent teeth erupt in better positions. This can significantly reduce crowding and misalignment and, in some cases, eliminate the need for braces later on.

Address Related Health Concerns Poor alignment and bite issues can contribute to breathing problems, speech challenges, gum disease, and tooth decay. Early orthodontic care helps prevent these concerns and supports your child’s overall health.

Our Difference

No More Messy Impressions

With our intraoral scanner, we’re able to create accurate 3D models of your child’s teeth, gums, and jaw structure, all without the need for messy impressions. These images allow us to create the most efficient treatment plans, and even see what your child’s smile will look like after their treatment has finished.

The Early Interceptive Orthodontics Treatment Process Initial Consultation Your child’s journey begins with a comprehensive orthodontic evaluation. We’ll take digital X-rays, perform a thorough exam, and assess jaw and tooth development to determine whether Phase 1 treatment is recommended.

Personalized Treatment Plan

If early treatment is needed, we’ll design a plan tailored specifically to your child. This may include braces, palate expanders, habit appliances, headgear, or other solutions. We’ll walk you through every step and answer all your questions before moving forward.

Ongoing Orthodontic Visits

Regular check-ups allow us to monitor progress and make adjustments as your child grows. Our goal is to keep treatment comfortable, effective, and on track, because no two smiles develop the same way.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the benefits of early interceptive orthodontics?

Early treatment helps guide proper growth and prevents orthodontic problems from becoming more severe over time. By addressing issues early, we can create space for adult teeth and support healthy jaw development.

What causes orthodontic problems in children?

Some orthodontic issues are inherited, while others develop due to habits like thumb sucking, mouth breathing, early or late loss of baby teeth, injury, or poor oral hygiene. No matter the cause, we tailor treatment to your child’s unique needs and focus on building healthy habits.

When should my child first see an orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an initial evaluation by age 7, or sooner if a concern arises. By this age, enough permanent teeth are present to assess development and plan care effectively.

How often will I need to come in for appointments?

Most orthodontic patients are seen every 6–8 weeks, depending on the type of treatment and how your smile is progressing. These visits are typically quick and focused on making small adjustments to keep things moving right on track. We’ll always let you know what to expect and work with your schedule to make visits as convenient as possible.
