The practice

Dr. Walsh welcomes patients of all ages to his Western Avenue, Guilderland, NY office, where he and his staff have been providing quality professional dental care for over 25 years.

In addition to general dentistry, Dr. Walsh is certified to provide numerous services for his clients (see the Services menu).

The latest dental technologies are utilized to highly benefit our patients. For example, x-rays are taken using the Schick CDR X-ray sensor which is simply placed in the patient’s mouth and is directly connected to the computer. The result is excellent quality x-rays with no wait time and more importantly, far less radiation.

All this with a friendly manner that sets you at ease in the chair!
