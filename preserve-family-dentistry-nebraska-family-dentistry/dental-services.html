What Dental Services Do We Offer At Our East Lincoln NE Dental Clinic?

Our East Lincoln, NE dentist offers a variety of dental services to patients of all ages. No matter what you’re looking for, we’ve got you covered!

Family Dentistry

Family dentistry focuses on comprehensive oral care for patients of all ages. From preventive treatments to restorative procedures, these services are tailored to you to promote a healthy smile.

Preventative Dentistry

Preventative dentistry encompasses a variety of practices aimed at reducing the risk of oral diseases, minimizing the need for extensive treatment, and helps ensure a healthy smile.

At-Home Whitening

Teeth naturally shift from white to varying shades of yellow. Normal wear and tear such as drinking coffee and smoking can stain our teeth. Whitening can help revive your smile!

Composite Fillings

White, tooth-colored fillings can be used to restore teeth with cracks, chips, or decay. Composite material, shaped to match surrounding teeth, provides a natural-looking appearance.

Dental Crowns

A ceramic crown covers your entire tooth to correct misshapen, crooked, and damaged teeth and will blend flawlessly with your existing teeth. A well-cared for crown can last years.

Sedation Dentistry

Sedation dentistry provides a relaxing experience for anxious patients during dental procedures. It’s ideal for complex treatments or long-duration treatments, ensuring a comfortable visit.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone by oral surgeons. Once the surgeon has placed the implants and they’ve healed, our team will finish the rest.

Hybrid Prosthesis

If you’re missing a few or all of your teeth, you may not know what you’re really missing! The Hybrid Prosthesis offers an unmatched confidence boost and outstanding durability.

Emergency Dentistry

Emergency dentistry provides care for urgent dental issues such as severe toothaches, broken or knocked-out teeth, and abscesses. Emergency dental care is essential for minimizing damage.

Dentistry for Children

Children’s dentistry focuses on the oral health of children from infancy through adolescence. Fostering good oral hygiene early on, paves the way for a lifetime of healthy smiles!
