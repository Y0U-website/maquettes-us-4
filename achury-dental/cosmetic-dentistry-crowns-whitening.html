Looking for a fast, safe, and dramatic way to enhance your smile? We offer professional teeth whitening treatments that target deep stains caused by coffee, tobacco, aging, or certain medications.

At Achury Dental , you can choose from our custom take-home whitening trays or in-office whitening options. Our treatments are designed to protect your enamel while delivering fast, lasting results.

Crowns

Dental crowns aren’t just restorative; they’re cosmetic, too. Whether you’re covering a damaged tooth, concealing discoloration, or reshaping a worn-down molar, our same-day crowns are crafted and placed right here in our office using the latest CEREC technology.

Our all-ceramic crowns blend seamlessly with your natural teeth and restore both function and aesthetics. There’s no need for temporary crowns or multiple appointments to get your smile back in one visit.

Invisalign

If traditional braces don’t fit your lifestyle, Invisalign offers a discreet, comfortable way to straighten your teeth. Using a series of custom-made, clear plastic aligners, Invisalign gradually moves your teeth into perfect alignment with minimal disruption to your daily routine.

Invisalign is ideal for adults and teens seeking an alternative to metal brackets. The trays are removable, nearly invisible, and easy to clean, making them a popular choice for working professionals and students alike.

Lumineers

Dreaming of a flawless smile without extensive dental work? Lumineers may be the solution. These ultra-thin, durable veneers are bonded directly to the front of your teeth to correct chips, gaps, stains, or uneven shapes, all without the need to grind down your natural enamel.
