Why Choose Implants Over Dentures?

While dentures can be a good temporary fix, many patients find them uncomfortable over time. Dental implants offer long-term advantages that go beyond appearance:

  • Permanent: Implants are fused to your jawbone, so they won’t shift or slip.
  • Functional: Eat your favorite foods, including crunchy vegetables and steaks, without worry.
  • Natural-looking: Each crown or bridge is custom-made to match your existing teeth.
  • Comfortable: No need for adhesives or nighttime soaking.
  • Durable: With good care, implants can last decades or even a lifetime.

Tooth loss impacts more than just appearance. When a gap is left unfilled:

Replacing lost teeth with implants helps prevent these issues, keeping your bite strong and your smile confident.

  • Your bite shifts: Adjacent teeth may tilt or move out of place.
  • Speech can change: Missing front teeth can affect how you pronounce words.
  • Chewing gets harder: You may begin avoiding healthy foods like apples, carrots, and nuts.
  • Bone loss occurs: Without stimulation from a tooth root, the jawbone in that area may shrink over time.
  • Self-esteem drops: Many people report feeling less confident in both social and professional settings.
