The Smile Doctors are Here to Serve You

Experience the gentle and caring hands of Dr. Tinoco and Dr. Simbol at our dental facility in the Southbay area of Southern California. We are here to take care of all of you and your family’s dental health. Dental health practitioners, Dr.Diana Jane Simbol and Dr. Maritess Tinoco, would like to invite you to come and visit their clean and welcoming professional facility which exudes a friendly atmosphere and incorporates state of the art equipment. Visit Tinoco-Simbol dental care and become a part of our family.

Tinoco Simbol Dental Care knows that reputation is built one patient at a time. Doctors Simbol and Tinoco have chosen to build their professional practice through quality, meticulous, gentle, personal, and individualized service. They have earned a valuable reputation along with priceless referrals and recommendations in the process.

Dr. Tinoco and Dr. Simbol have over 47 years of Professional Dental Health experience combined. They studied Dentistry at the Centro Escolar University in the Philippines. Both becoming doctors of dental surgery and members of American Dental Association, California Dental Association, and Harbor City dental Association.
