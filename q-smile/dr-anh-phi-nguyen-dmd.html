Dr. Anh-Phi Nguyen, DMD

Dr. Nguyen, Clinical Director

Dr. Nguyen attended the University of California, Riverside, where she earned her undergraduate degree in Biochemistry. She later moved to Boston to pursue her dental education and received her Doctor of Dental Medicine from the Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine.

Following dental school, Dr. Nguyen completed her residency program at Boston Medical Center before going on to establish her own private practice. She is an active member of the American Dental Association and the Massachusetts Dental Society. Dr. Nguyen previously served as Vice President of the MDS South Shore Chapter and was a distinguished guest member of the MDS Board of Trustees, reflecting her leadership and dedication to the dental profession.

Passionate about education and mentorship, Dr. Nguyen enjoys giving back by sharing her clinical expertise with colleagues and future dentists. In addition to her work in private practice, she has devoted her time to teaching at BUGSDM as a Clinical Associate of General Dentistry.

Beyond her professional commitments, Dr. Nguyen is deeply involved in community service and philanthropy. She actively participates in and fundraises annually for organizations including The Jimmy Fund, Make-A-Wish, the Vietnamese American Community of Massachusetts, and the New England Center and Home for Veterans.

She is also a co-founder of the Cultural Empowerment Organization, a nonprofit dedicated to empowering the younger generation through leadership development and cultural education.
