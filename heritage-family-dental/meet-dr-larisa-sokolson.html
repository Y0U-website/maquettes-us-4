Professionally, nothing means more to me than providing patients with a team of knowledgeable, friendly, and caring individuals that deliver high-quality care in a comfortable environment. I take great pride in the personal relationships that are developed directly between myself and patients.

I attended Long Island University where I graduated magna cum laude . I completed post-baccalaureate pre-dental studies at the University of Hartford. Then I went to the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine (Go, Huskies!) for my doctorate. My postgraduate residency training was completed at New York Medical College.

I’m a long-time resident of Connecticut. I live with my husband, our three sons and our rescue dog, Kiki. I enjoy jogging, golf, music, skiing, food, and I absolutely love to travel. (Be prepared to book two appointments if you ask about Barcelona.)
