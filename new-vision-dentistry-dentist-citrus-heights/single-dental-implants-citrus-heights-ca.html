The Process of Getting a Single Dental Implant

The process of receiving a single dental implant is broken into several steps, typically completed over a few months:

  • Consultation : During your initial visit to New Vision Dentistry in Citrus Heights, CA, our team will help your oral health and determine if you’re a good candidate for dental implants. We’ll take X-rays to evaluate the bone structure and decide if you need any preparatory procedures, like a bone graft.
  • Implant placement : The next step is the s surgical placement of the titanium or zirconia post into the jawbone. This procedure is done under local anesthesia, and while it may sound intimidating, it is relatively simple and minimally invasive.
  • Healing and Osseointegration : After the implant is put or placed, the bone needs time to fuse with the implant in a process that can take a few months. This ensures that the implant will be stable and secure.
  • Placement of the Abutment and Dental Crown : After the implant integrates or attaches with the bone, an abutment is attached, followed by a custom-made crown that matches the shape, size, or color of your natural teeth.

Caring for Your Single Dental Implant

To care for your single dental implant, brush (2 times) every day with a soft toothbrush, floss regularly, and stay away from hard or sticky foods that could damage the implant. Schedule regular dental check-ups to monitor its health, and if you smoke, try to quit, as smoking can affect healing. If you grind your teeth, use a mouthguard to protect the implant. Proper care will ensure your implant lasts for years.
