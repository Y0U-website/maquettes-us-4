Advantages of Implant Treatment

For appropriate candidates, dental implants provide several long-term benefits.

Because implants are properly anchored in the jawbone, they provide stable support for replacement teeth during normal daily activities.

Replacing the missing tooth root helps stimulate the surrounding jawbone, which may reduce the bone loss that commonly follows tooth loss.

A single implant replaces an individual missing tooth without requiring support from adjacent healthy teeth.

Every restoration is individually fabricated to match the size, shape, and the shade of your original teeth for a seamless appearance.

With consistent home care and regular professional maintenance, implants can provide dependable tooth replacement for many years.

Your Implant Treatment Experience

Your first visit covers a comprehensive evaluation of your teeth, gums, bite, and supporting bone. Digital X-rays or three-dimensional imaging may be recommended to evaluate bone volume and gather the information needed for accurate treatment planning.

If implant treatment is recommended, the titanium post is carefully fixed into the jawbone during a planned surgical procedure. The healing period lets the implant integrate securely with the neighboring bone before the final restoration is placed.

After healing, an abutment is fixed to the implant to support the final custom restoration. Before completing treatment, your dentist carefully checks the fit, bite, appearance, and comfort of the restoration to help ensure natural function.

Maintaining Your Implant Restoration

Dental implants need the same commitment to oral hygiene as natural teeth. Brushing twice each day, flossing around the implant, and attending routine dental examinations and professional cleanings help maintain healthy surrounding tissues.

Healthy daily habits also play an important role in supporting long-term implant success. Avoiding tobacco products, managing underlying health conditions, and following your dentist’s home-care recommendations all help protect your investment. If nighttime grinding or clenching is present, your dentist may recommend a custom nightguard to help protect your implant restoration.
