Every patient’s orthodontic needs are different, so treatment is always individualized. Based on your diagnosis, your dentist may recommend one or more treatment options.

Your Orthodontic Evaluation

Your first visit begins with a conversation about your concerns, expectations, and smile goals. Your dentist carries out a thorough examination and may recommend digital X-rays, photographs, or intraoral scans to gather detailed diagnostic information.

After reviewing your records, your dentist explains the findings, reviews the available options, and outlines the recommended sequence of care. You will also receive information about the estimated treatment timeline, follow-up visits, and home care recommendations before treatment begins.

Routine appointments allow your dentist to monitor progress, make adjustments when needed, and confirm that tooth movement is progressing as expected.

Maintaining Healthy Teeth During Orthodontic Care

Healthy teeth and gums offer a strong foundation for successful orthodontic care. Brushing thoroughly, flossing daily, and attending regular dental visits support healthy teeth and gums throughout treatment.

If you wear removable aligners or retainers, keeping them clean and wearing them as instructed helps maintain your progress.
