Zirconia vs Titanium Dental Implants

Both zirconia and titanium dental implants are effective solutions for replacing missing teeth, but each has its unique benefits.

Ultimately, the choice between zirconia and titanium implants relies on your individual needs, preferences, and medical conditions. Our team at New Vision Dentistry will guide you through the process of selecting the right implant for you.

  • Aesthetic Qualities : Zirconia implants offer a more natural appearance, especially for front teeth, due to their white color, whereas titanium implants can have a metallic look that might be visible through the gums over time.
  • Biocompatibility : Zirconia implants are non-metallic, which makes them a good choice for those who have metal allergies or sensitivities. Titanium implants, on the other hand, are highly biocompatible but may not be ideal for those with metal sensitivities.
  • Strength : Titanium implants are generally considered stronger than zirconia implants, especially for back teeth. However, zirconia is still a very strong material and offers excellent long-term durability.
