How long do I need to wear Invisalign®?

Treatment typically takes six to 24 months. The duration of your treatment will depend on the severity of your case and how well you adhere to wearing your aligners as directed. Throughout your treatment, you’ll have regular check-ins with your dentist to ensure everything is progressing smoothly. It’s important to wear your Invisalign® aligners for at least 20 to 22 hours per day to see optimal results. But don’t worry, wearing them won’t disrupt the flow of your daily life. You can still eat, drink, brush, and floss normally while straightening your teeth with Invisalign®!

Invisalign® vs Braces

When it comes to straightening your teeth, both options have their own unique benefits. Braces are fixed onto your teeth and consist of brackets and wires that gradually shift the position of your teeth over time. They are highly effective in correcting even severe misalignments but can be bulky and sometimes uncomfortable.

On the other hand, Invisalign® uses a series of clear aligners that are custom made to fit snugly over your teeth. These aligners are virtually invisible, making them a popular choice for those who want a more discreet option. Additionally, Invisalign® aligners are removable, allowing you to eat whatever you like without restrictions on certain foods. However, they may not be as effective for complex dental issues as traditional braces. Overall, the choice between Invisalign® and braces ultimately depends on your specific needs and preferences.

What are the benefits of Invisalign®?

Invisalign® offers numerous benefits compared to traditional braces. One of the most significant advantages of Invisalign® is that the aligners are virtually invisible, making them a discreet option for straightening teeth. This means you can confidently smile and speak without feeling self-conscious about metal brackets or wires in your mouth.

Additionally, Invisalign® aligners are removable, allowing you to eat whatever you want without restrictions and easily maintain good oral hygiene by brushing and flossing as usual. The smooth plastic material used in Invisalign® also reduces irritation to your cheeks and gums that can occur with traditional braces.

Another benefit is that Invisalign® typically requires fewer visits to the dentist or orthodontist for adjustments, saving you time during treatment. Overall, Invisalign® provides a convenient, comfortable, and aesthetically pleasing way to achieve a straighter smile.
