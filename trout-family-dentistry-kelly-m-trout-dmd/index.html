The practice

Welcome to the official website for Trout Family Dentistry. Dr. Kelly M. Trout purchased the established practice in 2013 from Dr. George P. Elsberry. The two dentists enjoyed providing personalized care to patients together for seven years. Dr. Elsberry retired from dentistry in 2020, and he and his wife Darby, remain frequent visitors and continue to be treasured members of the practice family.

Dr. Trout has called Columbus home for over 30 years. She received her dental degree from the University of Mississippi School of Dentistry in 1997, and has certification in Advanced General Dentistry from Louisiana State University School of Dentistry.

Dr. Trout and her husband Greg, who serves as the office manager, have grown the practice by building on the foundation of high quality care and caring, friendly service. We care deeply about our patients and our community. We welcome you to the area and our practice if you are new to Columbus.
