Outside the Office

A veteran of the United States Air Force, Dr. Dunlow practiced general dentistry and orthodontics in the military for ten years. His last assignment was at Offutt Air Force Base. While assigned to Offutt, he decided to make Nebraska his permanent home. Dr. Dunlow is the proud father of two daughters.

Dr. Dunlow is a big fan of 1980s music and music trivia. He also really enjoys movies, exercising, and traveling.
