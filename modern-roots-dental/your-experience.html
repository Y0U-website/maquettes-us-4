Redefining Dentistry in Bargersville, IN

In changing our name from Austgen Family Dental to Modern Roots Dental, we wanted to emphasize the elements that make us different from traditional dental practices…

Modern

“Modern” reflects our commitment to using the best technology and techniques available, including digital scanning, annual iTero health scans, minimally invasive treatments, and more advanced hygiene approaches. We also offer the latest in comfort menu items like weighted blankets, lavender oil, and headphones.

Roots

“Roots” is about getting to the underlying cause. Why is this happening? How do we prevent it? How do we support the patient as a whole person, not just treat a tooth? It’s also about staying connected to our patients through genuine relationships as we love and serve them.
