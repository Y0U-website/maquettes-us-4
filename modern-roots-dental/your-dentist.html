Meet Holly Austgen, DDS

“We’ve created a place where we can really connect with people while also delivering beautiful, healthy smiles that look good, feel good, and function well for a lifetime.”

The Journey…

From Hygienist

Originally from Walkerton in northern Indiana, Dr. Holly began her dental journey by completing Dental Hygiene training at Indiana University South Bend. She served patients as a dental hygienist for six years, taking pre-requisites for dental school during her lunch breaks and after work.

To Dentist

After years of preparation, she was accepted to Indiana School of Dentistry where she earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. She went on to complete her residency program at the VA Roudebush Hospital in Indianapolis.

To Owner
