Dental Implants

Dental implants are a revolutionary permanent solution for replacing missing teeth we offer at Smile Center Implants consist of three parts: the implant post, the abutment, and the restoration. The implant post is made from titanium and surgically placed into the jawbone where it fuses with the bone over time. The abutment is attached to the implant and is the piece that connects the implant to the restoration. The restoration is the part of the tooth replacement that is visible above the gum line.

Dental implants can replace a single missing tooth or multiple teeth. They are often recommended over other tooth replacement options because they are permanent and look and feel natural. They also help to prevent bone loss in the jaw, which occurs when a tooth is missing.

The Procedure for Dental Implants

The implant placement process begins with a thorough oral exam. Our dentist will first examine your mouth and take X-rays to determine if you have enough bone structure to support an implant. If you do not have enough bone structure, you may need bone grafting surgery before getting the implant.

To place the implant, the dentist will make a small incision in your gum line to expose the jawbone. Then, they will place the titanium post into the jaw. The post will be secured in place using a special screw or adhesive. Once the post is in place, the dentist will close the incision with stitches.

Over the course of the next few weeks, the post will fuse with the bone, a process called osseointegration. Once fusion is complete, the dentist will reopen the incision and place the abutment on top of the post. The abutment is the piece that connects the post to the restoration. Finally, the dentist will take an impression of the mouth and send it to the lab to create your permanent tooth replacement.

The Advantages of Dental Implants

Dental implant restorations look exactly like your natural teeth. They are designed to blend in with your smile seamlessly. No one can tell that you have implants unless you tell them yourself.

Dental implants help to keep your jawbone healthy. When you lose a tooth, the bone begins to shrink over time. This can lead to other oral health problems down the road. Dental implants help keep your jawbone strong.

Losing your teeth can be embarrassing. It can make you self-conscious about your smile and even lead to depression. Dental implants can help you regain your confidence and improve your self-esteem. You’ll feel like your old self again!
