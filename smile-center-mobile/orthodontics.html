Orthodontics

A confident smile can light up a room and boost your self-esteem. Orthodontics, the branch of dentistry dedicated to aligning teeth and jaws, plays a significant role in helping patients achieve that radiant, straight smiles they’ve always dreamed of.

Orthodontics is a specialized branch of dentistry that deals with diagnosing, preventing, and treating malocclusions and other irregularities in the teeth and jaws. Our dentist is trained to identify and treat a wide range of alignment issues and can improve the aesthetics and function of your smile.

Orthodontic Treatments

Traditional braces are the most common type of orthodontic treatment. They consist of brackets and wires that pressure the teeth to move them into their proper positions gradually. The brackets are made from stainless steel. They are bonded to the front surface of the teeth and connected by a wire. This wire is tightened to move the teeth into the desired position.

Clear aligners are another popular option for orthodontic treatment. Invisalign is a famous orthodontic system that uses a series of custom-made trays to move your teeth into their ideal alignment. Each tray is made from clear plastic and removable. They are worn for about two weeks and then changed with the next set of trays in the series. You should wear your aligners for about 22 hours each day.

These braces are similar to traditional braces but feature brackets made from a clear material instead of metal. This makes them less noticeable than metal braces, making them a popular option among adult patients.

The Benefits of Orthodontics Treatment

Orthodontic treatment offers many benefits, including transforming smiles, enhancing facial aesthetics, and providing a significant boost in self-confidence. Beyond aesthetics, orthodontic treatment improves oral health by aligning the teeth, making them easier to clean, and reducing the risk of dental issues like cavities and gum disease. Correcting bite problems and jaw discrepancies enhances speech, chewing, and overall oral function, improving quality of life.

By addressing orthodontic issues early, patients can prevent future complications and enjoy the lifelong advantages of a healthy, well-aligned smile, contributing to both physical well-being and increased self-esteem.

By addressing orthodontic issues, patients can enjoy healthier, more functional smiles and reduce the risk of dental complications in the future.
