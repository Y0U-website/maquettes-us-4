Dentures

Need dentures in Enid, OK? Come to Sugar Creek Dental! We guarantee a good fit or else we do not charge you for the product. We realize some dentures need extra retention and are better fits with implant-supported dentures. Post-op care is provided to ensure a good fit for the life of your denture. How Are Dentures Made? Dentures are custom-made and designed to function as natural teeth. First, dental imprints are made using stock trays. Our team takes the impressions to the lab so the technician can place them on an articulator, where the false teeth are attached using wax. Dentures
