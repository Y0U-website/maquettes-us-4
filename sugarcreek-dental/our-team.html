Our Team

From dental assistants to our office staff, we show that the best customer experience comes from a genuine care and concern. Our goal is to ensure every appointment is comfortable, relieves any discomfort or gives you the smile you’ve always wanted! Dr. Rob Geunes offers a wide variety of dental services as well as emergency appts. and extractions.

Dr Rob Geunes DMD

Dr. Geunes grew up around the Philadelphia area earning a DMD at the University of Pennsylvania. After graduation, he served as a dentist in the U.S. Navy after graduating earning the title of lieutenant. In 2011 he moved to Oklahoma where he spent 10 years working primarily on children all over the state. He would spend as much as 6 hours a day on the road going from Lawton to Tulsa to Enid wherever he was needed in order to fill the need for Soonercare patents. He decided to settle down in Enid based on the tremendous need to help bring smiles to the area.
