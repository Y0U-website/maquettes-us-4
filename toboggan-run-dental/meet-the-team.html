Meet Our Dental Team!

Dr. Charles Crisler

Dr. Charles Crisler

In 1999, Dr. Charles Crisler opened Toboggan Run Dental along with Dr. Richard Howenstine. Dr. Crisler is a native of Rice Lake and has been practicing here since 1993, after graduating from the University of Minnesota’s School of Dentistry. He values his relationships with each and every one of his patients and is dedicated to providing high quality compassionate care from restorative to periodontal care for patients of all ages.

Dr. Rachel Engel

Dr. Rachel Engel joined our team in 2015 and is committed to giving the same dedicated, high quality and compassionate level of care our patients have come to expect from Toboggan Run Dental. She is also a Rice Lake native and graduated from the Marquette University School of Dentistry.
