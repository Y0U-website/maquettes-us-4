About Us

Providing Quality Dental Care in Southern Minnesota

Southern Heights Dental Group was founded in 1974 with a vision of creating a dental practice where everyone, regardless of age, income, or insurance, could have access to quality dental care. That vision has grown Southern Heights Dental Group to be one of the largest privately-owned dental practices in Southern Minnesota, serving Faribault and the surrounding communities.

Today, our team includes eight dentists , each having areas of interest and expertise, including oral surgery, sleep dentistry, pediatric dentistry and cosmetic dentistry. In 2013, Southern Heights Dental began using dental therapists , or mid-level providers, offering even greater access to quality oral health care to more people. Our team also includes registered dental hygienists, licensed dental assistants, along with administrative support specialists. We also have access to a network of certified interpreters to assist with your language translation needs.

Innovation that helps us better meet the needs of our patients drives us to continually improve our scope of services and our patient experience. We’ve been a member of the Faribault community for more than 40 years, and we look forward to seeing your smile in the future!
