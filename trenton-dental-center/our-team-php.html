Jenny Woods

Jenny is a 2000 graduate of Loyola College Prep in Shreveport, where she is originally from. Upon graduation she went on to attend Louisiana Tech University and graduated in 2005 with a Bachelors in Fine Arts in Graphic Design. Jenny entered the dental field in 2007. In 2012, Jenny graduated from the University of Louisiana at Monroe with a Bachelors in Science in Dental Hygiene. While in school she earned awards for patient care and community outreach. She is also licensed to give Local Anesthesia and Nitrous Oxide.

Jenny resides in Quitman, LA with her husband Mark. They have two children, Lindsey and Jonathon. Jenny and her family are members of Grace United Methodist Church in Ruston. Away from the office, Jenny enjoys traveling and spending time with family and friends.
