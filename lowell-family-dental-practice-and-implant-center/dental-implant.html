What Are Dental Implants? Dental implants are a long-term solution for replacing missing teeth. They consist of a small titanium post that is placed into the jawbone and topped with a custom dental crown.

At Lowell Family Dental Practice in Lowell, MA, we provide both implant placement and restoration under one roof — so patients from Chelmsford, Billerica, Dracut, and Tewksbury never need a referral elsewhere. We help patients regain confidence, comfort, and full chewing function.

Dental implants are designed to look natural, feel secure, and support your overall oral health.

What to Expect During Treatment

Initial consultation and evaluation

Implant placement procedure

A small titanium post is carefully placed into the jawbone to serve as a strong foundation for the replacement tooth.

Healing and integration period

The implant is given time to heal and bond naturally with the jawbone, creating a stable and secure base.

Custom crown placement

Once healing is complete, a custom-made crown is attached to the implant to restore function and appearance.

Ongoing care and maintenance

With good oral hygiene and regular dental visits, dental implants can remain healthy and long-lasting.
