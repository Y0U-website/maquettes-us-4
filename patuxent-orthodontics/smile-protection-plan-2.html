Smile Protection Plan

Whether you are an existing patient or new patient, it’s important that you continue to wear your retainers to maintain your beautiful smile! Completed orthodontic treatment does not guarantee perfectly straight teeth for the rest of your life and retainers are required to keep your teeth in their new positions.

Being that conventional orthodontic insurance does not cover retainers and/or retainer replacements, this is a great way to protect your investment. In just 1 year of having 4 new retainers, it’s already paid for itself!
