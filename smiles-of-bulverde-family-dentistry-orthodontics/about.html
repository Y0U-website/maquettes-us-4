What to Expect

Before we start any treatment, we want our patients to feel comfortable and safe. Once we get to know each other and we’ve reviewed any important medical history, we’ll take diagnostic x-rays and photographs as a part of a comprehensive oral exam. Then we’ll perform a cleaning and review any findings. Your comfort is important to us, so if you feel anxious at any time during your appointment, let us know.
