About Our Dental Center in St. Petersburg, FL

Our office is in a small business complex on 3000 66th Street North in St. Petersburg, FL. It is more than just a dental office — It’s a creative, relaxed environment where patients come for high quality dental solutions, focused dental expertise, and friendly service. Dr. Gale and our dental team are constantly searching for better ways to improve the look and function of teeth, so our patients can always expect the most modern dentistry technology and treatment options. Our office is designed for patient comfort and accommodation.

We have our own parking lot so you won’t have to pay a meter to park, park on the street, or struggle just to find a parking spot. We provide complimentary bottled water, television, and assorted magazines in our waiting room. Each of our dental stations feature a relaxing chair, open windows, and flat screen TVs. You even get control of the remote to watch whatever you want. If you like, we can provide a topical numbing cream for your gums prior to simple things like your routine cleanings. Your comfort is our primary concern.

Mission

The professionals of St. Pete Dentistry are committed to providing high-quality dental care in a comfortable, professional atmosphere through compassion, care, and education.

Dr. Gale and his staff consider patients family. As such, we’re partners in your wellness and address each patient’s physical, emotional, and financial situations uniquely. Healthy smiles and happy patients are the direct result of our holistic approach to your dental care.

The cornerstone of our practice is, of course, education. We educate our patients throughout their continued path of optimal health and wellness, explaining advantages, disadvantages, costs, and time requirements for every dental procedure, as well as discussing individual expectations and fears.

And we educate ourselves. In a field that’s constantly evolving and advancing, St. Pete Dentistry professionals actively pursue learning opportunities to ensure we provide the best modern dentistry has to offer and continue to earn your trust.
