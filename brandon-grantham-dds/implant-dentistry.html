Who is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Determining if you are a good candidate for dental implants is a critical step in your journey to a restored smile. Ideal candidates for dental implants typically include individuals who:

During your consultation at Cove Family Dental, Dr. Grantham will evaluate these factors to determine if dental implants are the right choice for you.

  • Have one or more missing teeth : Dental implants are perfect for replacing a single tooth or multiple teeth.
  • Have a fully grown jawbone : It’s essential that the jawbone has reached full growth.
  • Possess adequate bone density : Sufficient bone is needed to support the implant, or be eligible for a bone graft.
  • Have healthy oral tissues : Good gum health is vital for the success of the implant.
  • Don’t have health conditions that affect bone healing : Certain chronic disorders may impede the healing process.
  • Are non-smokers or willing to quit smoking : Smoking can adversely affect the healing of dental implants.
  • Are committed to good oral hygiene : Maintaining oral health is crucial for the longevity of dental implants.
  • Are willing to undergo a several months process : The process of getting implants requires a commitment to multiple visits over several months.

Types of Dental Implants Offered at Cove Family Dental

At Cove Family Dental, we offer various types of dental implants to cater to different needs:

Single Tooth Implants

Ideal for replacing individual missing teeth. Each implant is topped with a custom-made crown , blending seamlessly with your natural teeth.

All-on-4 Dental Implants

A revolutionary technique for those who require full upper or lower teeth replacement. All-on-4 uses four strategically placed implants to support a full arch of teeth, offering a stable and more comfortable alternative to traditional dentures.

Implant-Supported Bridges

Unlike traditional bridges, implant-supported bridges do not require the alteration of adjacent healthy teeth. They are supported by implants and are an excellent solution for replacing several missing teeth in a row.

The Consequences of Missing Teeth

The impact of missing teeth extends beyond just aesthetic concerns. It can lead to a range of problems, including:

  • Jawbone Deterioration : Without the stimulation from a natural tooth root, the jawbone can begin to deteriorate, affecting facial structure.
  • Misalignment of Remaining Teeth : Adjacent teeth may shift into the gap, leading to misalignment and bite problems.
  • Difficulty in Eating and Speaking : Missing teeth can impair your ability to chew properly and affect your speech.
  • Increased Risk of Oral Health Issues : Gaps can act as traps for food and bacteria, increasing the risk of tooth decay and gum disease.
  • Decreased Self-Esteem : Missing teeth can significantly impact your confidence and willingness to smile.
