All-On-4 Dental Implants in Orland Park

Cost-effective implant solution

Less invasive

Traditional dental implants typically require 6–8 implants for a full fixed bridge. All-On-4 dental implants use just four anchor points to achieve the same result. These implants are strategically angled, minimizing the need for bone grafting. This allows for a procedure that is more cost-effective and less invasive.

Titanium durability

The All-On-4 implants are crafted from titanium. Over time, they fuse with your jaw bone, enhancing their durability. This fusion contributes to maintaining a healthier jaw and overall jaw structure, which is especially crucial for older adults.

Natural looking smile

By replacing an entire arch at once, All-On-4 implants create a seamless and natural looking smile. If you’re considering All-On-4 Dental Implants, reach out to us for a consultation.
