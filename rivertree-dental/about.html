Dentist

David Primrose, DDS

Dr. Primrose and his wife Ali both grew up in Issaquah, WA and graduated together from Issaquah High School in 2009. He then set out for adventure in Colorado where he studied Biology, Chemistry and Physics at the University of Denver.

Dr. Primrose returned home and received his DDS from the University of Washington in 2017. During his time at UW Dr. Primrose received multiple awards for his achievements in the departments of periodontics, restorative, implant dentistry and hospital dentistry.

Dr. Primrose decided to continue his training and matched to the prestigious General Practice Residency at the Puget Sound VA Hospital. For 12 months he served our veterans and worked alongside specialists, focusing his training on dental surgery, endodontics and comprehensive restorative dentistry.

Dr. Primrose has remained committed to his education through study clubs and mentorship and has completed over 300 hours of continuing education since graduation.

Outside of dentistry you can find Dr. Primrose biking around the valley and spending time with his wife, their son and daughter and two dogs.
