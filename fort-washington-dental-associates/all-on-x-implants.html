What Process of Getting Full Mouth Dental Implants?

All-on-X is a protocol for full mouth dental implants that replaces a full arch of teeth with a permanently fixed bridge anchored to a custom number of implant posts. This is an efficient, conservative approach that provides a strong, stable set of teeth that can last a lifetime with proper care.

Here are the steps in the All-on-X dental implants process.

1. Initial Consultation and Record-Taking

During your initial consultation for full mouth dental implants in Washington Heights, our team will assess your oral health, discuss your specific needs and expectations, and use advanced digital diagnostic imaging to create an exact replica of your facial anatomy to determine whether you’re a good candidate for dental implants .

2. Planning Phase and Implant Placement

If you are a good candidate for dental implants, our restoration dentist will collaborate with our skilled implant surgeon to plan the optimal placement of your implant posts. We’ll ensure your comfort throughout the procedure with safe and effective pain control on the day of the surgery.

When you’re comfortable, we’ll make a small incision in the gums to place four to six titanium posts in strategic locations and at carefully calculated angles to the jawbone to provide a strong, stable foundation for your new teeth. We’ll provide temporary teeth the same day so you can go about your normal routine while your implants heal.

3. Integration and Permanent Implant Bridge

Over the course of a few months, the implants will fuse directly to your jawbone, providing a stable foundation. During that time, we’ll design each tooth of your renewed smile with consideration for your facial features, face shape, lip line, skin tone, and several other factors so that your results look completely natural.

Once the implants are fully integrated, you’ll return to our office for updated scans. From there, we’ll fabricate your permanent implant bridge, which we’ll then attach to the implants. Get ready to celebrate your renewed smile!

What Are the Benefits of Full Mouth Dental Implants?

Full mouth dental implants provide a permanent solution to tooth loss that is significantly more comfortable and functional than other options. Here are a few of their key benefits.

Improved Chewing and Speaking Ability

Full mouth dental implants restore your ability to chew and speak with confidence. In fact, dental implants restore 99 % of full function to your bite, meaning you’ll be able to enjoy juicy steak, fresh salad, and even bite into an apple again. Because full mouth dental implants won’t cover the roof of your mouth like a traditional denture, they also won’t interfere with the sound of your voice, meaning you’ll be able to communicate clearly without worrying about clicking, lisping, or slurring your words.

Natural-Looking Artificial Teeth

Traditional dentures can be an excellent option in some cases. However, as they’re held in place with suction, they need to be made from lightweight materials like acrylic, which don’t have the same luster or durability as heavier materials used in full mouth dental implants. Over time, these materials will become worn down, lose their shine, and accumulate stains.

The All-on-X implant procedure enables us to make your new teeth from dense, durable, high-quality materials to create natural-looking artificial teeth. The customized prosthetics are designed to mimic the look of natural enamel, so you can enjoy a beautiful and authentic smile that lasts.

Permanent Solution

Unlike removable dentures, full mouth dental implants offer a permanent solution to tooth loss. The implants fuse with the jawbone, providing a stable and long-lasting foundation for the artificial teeth. This durability eliminates concerns about slippage or discomfort associated with traditional dentures.

Their fit will never change, so you won’t need to worry about them becoming loose, rubbing and leaving painful sores, or losing their function over time. Full mouth dental implants easily last decades, and in many cases they can last a lifetime.
