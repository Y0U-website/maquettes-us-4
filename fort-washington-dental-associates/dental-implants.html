What Are Dental Implants?

Getting dental implants is like having a full set of healthy teeth again. Their unbeatable strength, durability, and longevity comes from a structure that closely mimics natural teeth.

Dental implants give you permanent replacements that look, feel, and function like natural teeth. No struggling with ill-fitting dentures that slip, shift, or rub and leave painful sores. No limits to what foods you can enjoy. And no more hiding your smile from the world.

They do this using three components: a post, an abutment, and a restoration.

Titanium Implant Post

The dental implant process begins with placing a titanium screw-like post into the jawbone. This biocompatible material allows the implant to fuse with the bone, providing a stable foundation for the replacement tooth.

Abutment

An abutment is attached to the implant post, connecting it to the crown. This component acts as a connector and provides a secure base for the prosthetic tooth.

Crown, Bridge, or Prosthesis

The restoration is the visible part of the dental implant, resembling a natural tooth. We use the highest-quality materials to create beautiful, life-like restorations that are denser and more durable than other options like acrylic dentures.

We’ll design each tooth replacement to match the shape, size, and color of your existing teeth, ensuring a seamless and natural-looking smile.

Types of Dental Implant Treatments

Dental implants are a versatile solution to tooth loss that we can tailor to your unique needs. We can use implants to replace any number of missing teeth.

Single Dental Implants

A single dental implant and crown are ideal for replacing a single missing tooth or individual tooth in separate areas. It provides a permanent replacement that blends seamlessly with your natural teeth.

An individual implant is also an ideal way to protect your remaining natural teeth. When a tooth goes missing, the other teeth can shift and become worn, cracked, or even break due to excessive strain. By permanently replacing your missing tooth, we can prevent expensive treatment needs later.

Dental Implant Bridge

Implant-supported bridges are used to replace multiple missing teeth in a row. With just two implant posts, we can attach a single restoration designed to look like several individual teeth. This approach eliminates the need for traditional dental bridges that rely on adjacent healthy teeth for support.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures offer a stable and secure alternative to traditional removable dentures. With a few strategically-placed implants to act as anchors, you’ll have a solid foundation for dentures and with better comfort and functionality.

Entire Arch Replacement with Four to Six Implants

We can replace an entire arch of teeth with a complete arch restoration permanently anchored to just four to six dental implants. This innovative approach offers a fixed and natural-looking set of teeth, improving oral health and overall quality of life.

Best of all, you’ll be able to enjoy all your favorite foods in total comfort. Imagine eating a fresh salad, enjoying juicy steak, or biting into a crisp apple again!

Using Dental Implants to Improve Oral Health

Permanent Tooth Replacements

Dental implants eliminate the need for removable dentures or temporary restorations. They are designed to last decades with proper care, making them a smart investment in your wellbeing that can cut down on dental expenses in the long run.

Enhanced Functionality and Comfort

Unlike traditional bridges or dentures, dental implants offer superior functionality and comfort. While other tooth replacements can limit your food choices, implants are securely anchored in the jawbone, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with total confidence.

Easier Maintenance

Maintaining dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. They can be brushed and flossed like regular teeth, making oral hygiene routines more straightforward and effective. This also eliminates the need for special cleansers or expensive adhesives, making them more affordable to care for than dentures.
