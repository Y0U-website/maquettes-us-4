Welcoming Patients of Dr. Elliott…

Honoring Dr. Elliott Gutman’s Legacy and Welcoming His Patients to Fort Washington Dental Associates

It is with deep sadness that we share news of Dr. Elliott Gutman’s passing. Dr. Gutman was more than a skilled dentist. He was a trusted caregiver, a professional of great integrity, and a valued member of the Washington Heights and Inwood communities. His dedication to his patients and commitment to excellence left a lasting impact that will not be forgotten.

Our hearts go out to Dr. Gutman’s family, loved ones, and the many patients who relied on him for their care over the years. At Fort Washington Dental Associates, we consider it a true honor to continue caring for Dr. Gutman’s patients. We know how important your wellbeing was to him, and we are committed to upholding the same thoughtful, patient-centered approach that defined his practice.

A Seamless and Respectful Transition of Care

We want to reassure you that your continued care is our highest priority. Our team has been working closely and respectfully with Maria and the Gutman family to ensure a smooth and stress-free transition of dental records. Every effort has been made to preserve continuity, comfort, and familiarity as you move forward with your care.

You can feel confident knowing that your dental history, treatment plans, and individual needs have been handled with care and professionalism.

A Warm Welcome to Our Washington Heights Dental Office

We are truly looking forward to welcoming you to Fort Washington Dental Associates. Our office is conveniently located on the corner of 165th Street and Fort Washington, easily accessible for patients coming from Washington Heights, Inwood, and surrounding neighborhoods.

For more than 55 years, our practice has been a trusted part of the Washington Heights community. We are proud to serve generations of local families, as well as the dedicated staff and faculty of Columbia University and NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital. Our longstanding presence reflects our commitment to quality care, strong relationships, and community trust.

We are open five days a week from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, with additional hours every other Saturday from 8:00 AM to 3:30 PM, making it easier to fit dental care into your schedule.

A Team of Caring Specialists Under One Roof

Our goal is to be your long-term home for complete dental wellness. We offer a full range of services, from preventive and restorative care such as cleanings, fillings, and custom crowns, to advanced treatments including clear aligners, laser-assisted periodontal therapy, implant dentistry, and full mouth reconstruction.

Our experienced clinical team includes three general dentists, a prosthodontist for complex restorative cases, a periodontist specializing in gum health and dental implants, and an orthodontist providing both braces and Invisalign. This collaborative approach allows us to deliver comprehensive, coordinated care tailored to your individual needs.

We also utilize modern dental technologies such as AI-assisted digital X-rays, 3D CBCT imaging, intraoral scanners, and 3D printing. These tools help us provide care that is precise, comfortable, and aligned with today’s highest standards.

We Are Here for You

Many of you were patients of Dr. Gutman for many years. We understand that you may have questions or concerns as you transition your care, and we want you to know that our team is here to support you. We invite you to call us at 212-928-9000 to speak with a friendly team member or to schedule your next appointment. You can also learn more about our practice and services throughout our website .

Above all, we want you to feel welcomed, heard, and cared for. It would be our privilege to earn your trust and continue supporting your oral health for many years to come.

With heartfelt sympathy and a warm welcome,

Dr. Victor Lee and the Fort Washington Dental Associates team
