What Are Single Tooth Implants?

So, you&#x27;re wondering, "What&#x27;s the Single Tooth implant process?" Well, dental implants are a fantastic solution for missing teeth. Single-tooth implants focus on replacing just one missing tooth. Typically made of titanium, the implant is securely placed into the jawbone. Over time, this piece of medical-grade material fuses with your bone, forming a strong foundation for a crown. This process ensures that your implant becomes a permanent part of your dazzling smile.

Why opt for Single Tooth Implants?

"Why do I even need Single Tooth Implants?" you might ask. Missing a tooth impact more than your appearance. It affects your ability to chew and may even cause the surrounding teeth to shift, leading to orthodontic issues. Single-tooth implants stand out as a superior option. Unlike dentures, they aren&#x27;t removable, and they don&#x27;t require altering the adjacent teeth. They blend seamlessly with your existing teeth, maintaining your natural smile while restoring functionality.

How Do Single Tooth Implants Work?

Curious about "How Do Single Tooth Implants Work?" The implantation process involves several steps. First, Dr. Matt Rivera creates an incision in the gums, preparing a small tunnel in the jawbone for implant placement. Once the implant is securely in place, a healing period ensues. You&#x27;ll return to our Kannapolis, NC office for the attachment of the abutment—a connector for the crown. The final step involves fitting a custom dental crown over the abutment and implant.
