What Are Dental Implants?

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

  • Initial Consultation: Dr. Uldrikson will evaluate your oral and overall health before analyzing your jawbone density. This will determine if you are cleared to undergo dental implant surgery or if you need additional treatment first.
  • Dental Implant Surgery: No referral is required for dental implant placement. Instead, Dr. Uldrikson will complete the procedure himself, allowing you to remain in one convenient location.
  • Osseointegration/Abutment: You will spend the next 3-6 months healing at home and allowing osseointegration to occur. Once your implants properly fuse with your bone, you will receive your abutments that will help hold your restoration in place.
  • Delivery of Final Restoration: We will have a comfortable and lifelike set of teeth created and attached to your implants so that your smile looks, feels, and acts natural once it is in place.
