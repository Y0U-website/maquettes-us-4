The practice

At our dental practice, we prioritize delivering exceptional care by constantly updating our staff’s knowledge and employing the latest dental technology, all while cultivating a welcoming, clean, and relaxing atmosphere to ensure your visit is a positive one.

We offer a comprehensive range of dental treatments to meet all your oral health needs.

Dr. Azita Vakili has dedicated her career to helping patients achieve healthier, more confident smiles through comprehensive restorative, cosmetic, and implant dentistry. Over the years, her experience and commitment to patient care have earned recognition from San Diego Magazine and other professional organizations.

As the dentist at Solana Smiles & Implant Center, with locations in Solana Beach and Poway, California, Dr. Vakili is proud to have her work and dedication recognized within the San Diego dental community.

For Dr. Vakili, recognition from her peers is especially meaningful. It reflects the years she has spent advancing her knowledge, refining her skills, and providing personalized care to patients throughout Solana Beach, Poway, and the greater San Diego community.

Dr. Vakili was also featured in San Diego Magazine’s 2023 Excellence in Dentistry listings. She was included in the publication’s General Dentistry listing, which identifies her alongside Solana Smiles & Implant Center in Solana Beach. She was also featured in the publication’s Excellence in Service to Community listing.

These published recognitions provide another reflection of Dr. Vakili’s longstanding presence in the San Diego dental community and her dedication to providing comprehensive care to her patients.

Her background in prosthodontics has given her extensive experience treating patients who need more than routine dental care. She takes a comprehensive approach to treatment, considering each patient’s oral health, functional needs, aesthetic goals, and long-term results.
