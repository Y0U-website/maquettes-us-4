Patient Information

Providing exceptional dental care for you and your family is our priority at Andy Hollifield, DMD PA Family Dentistry. Located in Kings Mountain, NC, we are proud to work with patients of all ages throughout the surrounding areas. A healthy smile is a beautiful smile, so we offer comprehensive services to help you achieve and maintain dental and oral health. We are here to help you and your family smile brighter!

Patient Forms

We understand that our patients are very busy. That’s why we have included our patient forms on our website, so you can fill them out before your appointment and save time in the office. Please bring the forms, along with your insurance information, to your first appointment!

Scheduling an Appointment

If you must cancel your appointment, please give us a call at 704-739-7588 with at least 24-hours notice so we can offer this time to another patient. We can reschedule your appointment if needed.

Please arrive at your appointment 10 minutes early in case we need to complete any paperwork. We do our best to see you at your scheduled time, but we apologize in advance if there is a delay due to an unexpected emergency. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late to your appointment, you may have to be rescheduled.

If you miss three appointments without 24-hours notice, we reserve the right to charge a $50 cancellation fee per missed appointment.

For more information about our office policies, or to schedule an appointment, contact us today at 704-739-7588 and a member of our team will be happy to help!
