Dental Services

Pediatric Care

Beginning your child’s dental care early is essential for ensuring they are laying the foundation for great dental hygiene habits and a healthy adult smile. The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends children go to the dentist for the first time within six months of the eruption of their first tooth. However, going to the dentist can be a scary experience for children. We recommend that you bring your child to one of your appointments in order to get them familiar with our office and what happens at a dental cleaning.

At our practice, Dr. Hollifield and his team make children’s appointments fun, comfortable, and enjoyable. After each cleaning, we will give your child a token for our toy machine, so they have something fun to look forward to! We strive to help your child enjoy going to the dentist so we can continue care for their beautiful, developing smiles.

Periodontal Care

Periodontal care refers to treatment of the surrounding tooth structures, including the gums, which can become infected or inflamed when not taken care of properly. If gum disease isn’t treated early on, it can lead to periodontitis and tooth loss. To prevent this, we perform several services, including scaling and root planing, removing infected gum tissue, and replacing lost bone and tissue. The worst cases may require surgery. We will provide you with the care you need for healthy gums.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth Whitening

We offer a variety of teeth whitening systems to purchase.

Porcelain Veneers

Disguise your smile’s imperfections with porcelain veneers! Veneers are very thin, yet very strong coverings that we custom color and shape in our laboratory to meet your smile’s unique needs. Veneers are an ideal option for covering up chips, gaps, cracks, and discoloration in the smile and can also be used to elongate or change the shape of teeth. They are permanent, resistant to staining, and give your smile a dramatically more beautiful appearance.

Invisalign

Many patients, especially adult patients, avoid orthodontic care because they don’t want to wear noticeable metal braces. Invisalign gives you the straighter smile you want, using virtually invisible aligners so no one will know you are wearing them! We provide you with sets of aligners customized to gradually move your teeth into the correct position. You wear each set for two weeks before moving on to the next. You can remove the aligners for eating and brushing your teeth. At the end of the process, you will have a more functional bite and a more beautiful smile!

Restorative Dentistry

Dental Implants

Root Canals

When a cavity or trauma causes inflammation deep within the tooth, affecting the pulp, Dr. Hollifield will perform a root canal. During a root canal, we remove the infected pulp and disinfect the inside of the tooth to eliminate remaining bacteria. Then, the tooth is restored with a crown to improve its appearance and strength. Most root canals require one to three visits.

Crowns & Bridges

A crown commonly referred to as a cap is a restorative solution that we offer for cases where a large cavity is present, a tooth has undergone root canal therapy, or to restore dental implants. Similarly, a bridge is a dental device utilized to replace one or multiple missing teeth using false teeth. Our team offers both fixed and removable bridges and takes a personalized approach, carefully evaluating your smile’s unique needs to determine which option is most suitable for you.
