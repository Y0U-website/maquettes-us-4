Invisalign

Invisalign is a clear aligner system used to straighten teeth as an alternative to traditional metal braces. It uses a series of custom-made, virtually invisible aligners that gradually shift teeth into the desired position. The aligners are removable, making it easier to eat, drink, brush, and floss compared to traditional braces.
