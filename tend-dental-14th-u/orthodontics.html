The orthodontic treatment process

When you get clear aligners with Tend, we’ll keep you informed and supported during each step. Here’s what to expect:

  • Consult and evaluation Your smile journey starts with a one-on-one consultation with your dentist. Here, we’ll take a close look at your teeth, discuss your smile goals, and explore whether Invisalign or SimplyClear aligners are right for you.
  • Digital 3D scan Forget messy impressions. Using advanced digital scanning technology, we capture a precise 3D image of your teeth to create your custom treatment plan in a way that is accurate, quick, and completely comfortable.
  • Personalized treatment plan You’ll see a digital preview of your future smile before treatment begins. Your dentist will explain how each stage works and how long treatment will take, so you know what to expect at every step.
  • Your aligners arrive Once your custom aligners are ready, we’ll make sure they fit perfectly and feel comfortable. You’ll receive your first few sets and clear instructions for wear, care, and progress tracking.
  • Progress check-ins Throughout your treatment, your Tend dentist will monitor your progress with in-person or virtual visits. These quick check-ins keep everything on track and ensure your teeth are moving as planned.
  • Enjoy your new smile When your aligner treatment is complete, you’ll receive retainers to help maintain your beautiful results. With your new smile complete, you’ll leave feeling confident, comfortable, and completely yourself.

Get an A-list smile with custom clear aligners

Straightening your teeth should be simple, comfortable, and discreet, and that’s exactly what clear aligners deliver.

We offer two modern options: SimplyClear and Invisalign aligners. Both use a series of custom, removable trays that gently guide your teeth into alignment without the metal or wires of traditional braces.

Because both SimplyClear and Invisalign clear aligners are nearly invisible and easy to remove, they fit seamlessly into your daily routine. You can eat what you love, brush and floss normally, and feel confident throughout your treatment. With Tend’s personalized guidance, cutting-edge technology, and precise digital planning, you’ll enjoy a straighter, healthier smile comfortably and on your own terms.

Why orthodontic care matters for your smile

Straight teeth are easier to care for, reducing your risk of cavities, gum disease, and uneven wear. Crowding, gaps, and misalignment can trap food and bacteria in hard-to-reach places, leading to decay or inflammation.

A straighter smile doesn’t just look better; it will help you feel better. With a straighter smile, you’ll feel more confident every time you speak, laugh, or smile.

Using clear aligners to create smiles that are as functional as they are beautiful, designed for comfort, precision, and lasting results.

Have questions about orthodontics?

Achieving a straighter, healthier smile should be easy and stress-free. Here’s what you need to know before your visit, so you can feel fully prepared and confident in your care.

  • What is orthodontic treatment? Orthodontic treatment focuses on aligning your teeth and bite to improve both function and appearance. At Tend, we use clear aligners to gently move your teeth into better positions over time, helping you achieve a healthy, balanced smile.
  • What’s the difference between SimplyClear and Invisalign? Invisalign is one of the most widely recognized clear aligner brands, but other systems, like SimplyClear, use a similar approach and can often deliver comparable results at a lower cost. At Tend, we offer both Invisalign and SimplyClear aligners at different price points, all designed for comfort, precision, and great outcomes, so you can choose the option that best fits your needs and budget.
  • How do clear aligners work? Clear aligners gently shift your teeth into alignment using a series of removable trays custom-made for your smile. Each set is worn for about one to two weeks before moving to the next, gradually guiding your teeth into position.
  • Why should I get orthodontic treatment at Tend? Our dentists are experts in the art and science of moving teeth, and thousands of patients have trusted their smiles with us. With affordable orthodontics and leading-edge care for all ages, we help create healthier, straighter smiles so patients can feel confident about their dental wellness now and in the future.
  • When should I consider orthodontic treatment? It’s never too late to improve your smile. While many people start orthodontic treatment in their teens, adults can benefit just as much. If you’ve noticed crowding, gaps, or bite issues, it’s a good time to talk to your Tend team about your options.
  • Why is having straight teeth important? Straight teeth aren’t just about looks; they’re easier to clean, which helps prevent cavities and gum disease. Proper alignment also reduces wear on your teeth and strain on your jaw, supporting long-term oral and overall health.
  • How many hours per day do I need to wear clear aligners? Once you have clear aligners, you should wear your aligners day and night, taking them out only to eat, drink, and brush. Your aligners should be in for at least 22 hours per day.
  • How often will I need to visit during treatment? Most aligner patients visit every 6 to 8 weeks for quick progress checks. Because aligners don’t require adjustments like braces do, appointments are shorter and more flexible to fit your schedule.
  • Will clear aligners hurt? With any orthodontic treatment, including Invisalign aligners, your teeth will feel sore while they’re actively moving. With clear aligners, your teeth will be in active movement mode over the first 3-5 days after switching to a new set of aligners. After that, you will finish wearing your aligners until your dentist has instructed you to switch to the next aligner set, while your teeth rest and settle into place in passive mode.
  • What can I eat or drink with aligners? Drinking water with your aligners in is completely fine, but you should remove your aligners before consuming anything else. Eating or drinking with your aligners in could cause cavities and stain your aligners, making them far more visible!
  • Can orthodontic problems come back after treatment? Teeth can shift slightly over time, which is why wearing a retainer is important. Retainers help keep your teeth in their new positions, protecting your results for years to come.
  • Does Tend provide retainers after orthodontic treatment? Absolutely! After completing your orthodontic treatment at Tend, you’ll be provided with custom retainers to help keep your smile aligned. Your treatment includes one set of clear retainers, and your doctor will guide you on how to wear them—starting full-time for the first 8 weeks and then gradually transitioning to nighttime-only wear. If you ever need a replacement, a single retainer can be replaced for $300.
  • How long do I have to wear retainers after orthodontic treatment? Once your orthodontic treatment is complete, you’ll begin by wearing your clear retainers full-time for the first 8 weeks, taking them out only to eat, drink hot or staining beverages, or to brush and floss. During your 8-week follow-up, your doctor will evaluate how your teeth are settling and provide instructions for gradually switching to nighttime-only wear. Sticking to this routine helps keep your teeth in their new positions and maintain your smile for the long term.
  • What is your late cancel and no-show policy? Keeping your smile on track takes regular appointments, and we reserve time just for you. The first and second time you cancel your appointment with less than 2 days’ notice, or miss a visit, there’s no charge. For a third missed appointment, a $100 fee may apply, and for the fourth and any additional missed appointments, a $200 fee may be charged.
