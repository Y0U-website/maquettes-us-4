About dental implants in New York City

  • Are dental implants in NYC worth it compared to bridges or dentures? Yes! Dental implants are widely regarded as the gold standard for tooth replacement. Unlike bridges or dentures, a dental implant replaces both the visible tooth and the root, which helps preserve jawbone health and maintain natural facial structure. With proper care and regular dental visits, dental implants can last 20 years or longer, making them a durable, long-term solution for missing teeth.
  • How long do dental implants in New York last? Dental implants can last decades, or even a lifetime, with proper oral hygiene and regular dental visits. The dental implant itself is designed to be permanent, while the crown may need replacement after 10–15 years due to normal wear. According to dental professionals, maintaining healthy gums and routine preventive care is essential for long-term dental implant success.
  • How long is the dental implant process in New York? The dental implant process typically takes three to six months from start to finish. This timeframe allows the implant to fuse securely with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration, which is essential for long-term stability. While some NYC dental offices may offer advanced techniques that shorten treatment time for select patients, experienced clinicians prioritize proper healing and predictable outcomes when planning dental implants in NYC.
  • Is getting a dental implant in NYC painful? Dental implant placement surgery is performed using local anesthesia and is not painful. Most patients experience minimal discomfort during the dental implant procedure and just mild soreness afterward, which is usually manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers.
  • Do I have to pay a deposit for my dental implant appointment? Booking a dental implant appointment at Tend requires a deposit of up to $500 to secure your appointment. If you need to cancel your appointment, the refund amount depends on how much notice you provide: cancellations made more than 72 hours in advance will receive a full refund, 24–72 hours’ notice will return half, and cancellations with less than 24 hours’ notice are non-refundable.
