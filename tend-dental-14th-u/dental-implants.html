Restore your smile with dental implants

The process typically begins with a consultation and imaging to plan your implant placement. Once the implants are placed, they integrate with the bone over time, and a custom restoration is attached, completing your new smile.

Our team uses advanced imaging tools to ensure precise placement and optimal results. Throughout the process, you’ll have a clear understanding of each step and what to expect because transparency isn’t just our policy; it’s how we build trust.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

Choosing dental implants means investing in a solution that supports both your smile and your overall health. Some benefits include:

  • Natural look and feel Implants are designed to match your natural teeth in color and shape. Once placed, they blend seamlessly with your smile, and most people won’t be able to tell the difference.
  • Long-lasting durability With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. They are designed to endure daily use and typically do not need replacements or adjustments over time.
  • Preserves jawbone health Missing teeth can lead to bone loss in your jaw. Implants stimulate the bone just like natural tooth roots, helping maintain your facial structure and preventing the sunken appearance that can occur with tooth loss.
  • Improved eating and speaking Because implants are securely anchored, you can enjoy all your favorite foods without worry. They won’t slip or shift when you talk or eat, giving you the freedom to live without dietary restrictions.
  • No impact on surrounding teeth In some cases, restorations may require altering adjacent teeth for support, but dental implants stand independently. This means your healthy teeth stay untouched, preserving more of your natural smile.
  • Easy maintenance Caring for implants is simple. Brush and floss just as you would with natural teeth, and keep up with regular dental hygiene visits . No special cleaning solutions or overnight soaking required.

The Tend implant process

Getting dental implants is a journey, and we’ll guide you through each phase with clarity and support.

  • Step 1 Exam & Imaging We begin with a comprehensive exam , including digital imaging, to assess your oral health and bone structure. This is also when we discuss your goals, answer any questions you may have, and create a personalized treatment plan.
  • Step 2 Treatment Planning Using cutting-edge technology, we map out the precise placement of your implant. This detailed planning ensures the best possible outcome and helps us anticipate any additional steps, like bone grafting, if needed.
  • Step 3 Dental Implant Surgery During a minor surgical procedure, we place the titanium implant post into your jawbone. This is done under local anesthesia, and we prioritize your comfort throughout. The procedure typically takes one to two hours per implant.
  • Step 4 Healing Over the next several months, the implant fuses with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration. This forms a strong foundation for your permanent restoration. We will provide guidance on caring for the site during the healing process.
  • Step 5 Abutment Placement Once healing is finished, we attach a small connector called an abutment to the implant. This procedure prepares the implant to receive your custom crown. Your gums will need a few weeks to heal around the abutment.
  • Step 6 Crown Placement The final step is placing your custom-made crown, specially crafted to match your natural teeth. Once attached, your implant is finished, and you can enjoy your fully restored smile.

Why replace missing teeth?

Losing a tooth can lead to more than just an empty space. When teeth are missing, the jawbone no longer receives stimulation from chewing and biting, leading jawbone deterioration over time.

In addition, remaining teeth may shift, causing misalignment, bite issues, and an increased risk of decay or gum disease. Missing teeth also affect eating and speaking, leading to social self-consciousness and impacting nutrition and confidence.

Replacing teeth with implants restores function, protects health, and rebuilds confidence, whether you’ve lost teeth recently or years ago. We’re here to help you find the best solution.

Have questions about dental implants?

If you’re considering dental implants, it’s normal to have questions. Here, our Tend team answer some of the things patients ask most often.

  • What is a dental implant? A dental implant is a small titanium post that’s placed into your jawbone to replace the root of a missing tooth. Once it’s securely in place and has fused with your bone, a custom-made crown is attached to the top, giving you a tooth that looks, feels, and functions just like a natural one. Think of it as a permanent foundation for your smile that’s built to last.
  • Why should I get a dental implant? Needing an implant is quite common. If you have a damaged or decayed tooth, chewing might be difficult. If you’re missing a tooth, you could have a gap in your smile. An implant helps you chew comfortably and smile confidently. Bridges address the same issue but are a bit more invasive, requiring work on neighboring teeth, and they tend not to last as long.
  • What is the dental implant process? The dental implant process replaces a missing tooth with a strong, natural-looking alternative. It typically involves placing a small titanium post in the jawbone, allowing it to heal and fuse with the bone, and then attaching a custom-made crown on top. This is a multi-visit treatment that’s customized just for you, and each visit can take up to two hours. The full process can take a few months, but the result is a durable, long-term solution that looks and functions like a natural tooth.
  • How long do dental implants last? Dental implants are as durable as they are functional and beautiful. When properly cared for, your implant can last for decades, making it a reliable long-term solution for replacing missing teeth.
  • Am I a good candidate for dental implants? Most patients with good overall health and adequate jawbone density are ideal candidates for implants. During your consultation, we’ll assess your oral health, discuss your goals, and determine if implants are suitable for you.
  • Will my dental implant look natural? Yes. Your custom restoration is designed to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth, so it blends seamlessly with your smile. Most people won’t notice the difference, and neither will you. Our goal is to give you a result that looks and feels completely natural.
  • Are dental implants covered by insurance? Coverage can vary depending on your specific plan. Some insurance policies may cover a portion of the implant procedure, while others may offer more limited coverage. Our team can help you understand how your benefits may apply and walk you through payment options that fit your budget. Coverage and reimbursement are determined by your insurance provider, but we’re here to help you navigate your options and make quality care feel more accessible.
  • Does getting a dental implant hurt? The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you won’t experience pain during placement. Afterwards, most patients feel mild discomfort similar to other dental procedures, which you can manage with over-the-counter pain relievers. We will give you detailed aftercare instructions and are always available if you have concerns during your recovery.
  • Can dental implants replace multiple teeth? Absolutely. Dental implants are highly versatile and can replace a single tooth, several teeth, or even support a full arch of teeth. We’ll tailor your treatment plan based on how many teeth you’re replacing and what will work best for your lifestyle and budget.
  • How long does the dental implant process take? The timeline varies depending on your individual needs, but most patients can expect the entire process to take between three and six months. This includes the initial placement, a healing period where the implant fuses with your jawbone, and finally, the placement of your permanent restoration. We’ll guide you through a personalized timeline during your consultation so you know exactly what to expect.
  • How long do dental implants take to heal? Dental implants typically take about 3 to 6 months to fully heal, though this can vary by patient. The gums usually heal within 1–2 weeks after placement, but it can take several months for the implant to fully fuse with the jawbone, which is what creates the stable foundation. Once healing is complete, the final crown is usually placed within a few weeks. Factors such as overall health, bone quality, and aftercare can influence the overall timeline.
  • Do I need to pay a deposit? Scheduling a dental implant appointment at Tend requires a deposit of up to $500 to hold your spot. If you cancel or reschedule, the deposit refund amount is contingent on notice: cancelling more than 72 hours before your appointment will return the full deposit, 24–72 hours’ notice will return half, and less than 24 hours’ notice means the deposit cannot be refunded.
