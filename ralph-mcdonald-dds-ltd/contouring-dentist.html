Contouring Dentist

Our dental contouring procedures require minimal intervention and can often be completed in just one appointment. The process involves carefully removing small amounts of tooth enamel to sculpt the tooth into a more pleasing shape. Since only surface enamel is removed, most patients feel little to no discomfort during the procedure.

  • Reshaping pointed or irregularly shaped teeth
  • Adjusting slightly overlapping teeth
  • Correcting minor crowding issues
  • Reducing the length of long teeth
  • Creating more uniform tooth proportions
  • Improving overall smile symmetry

Why You Might Consider Dental Contouring

Dental contouring offers an efficient solution for various cosmetic dental concerns. You might benefit from this procedure if you’re experiencing:

Unlike many other cosmetic dental procedures, contouring typically requires no anesthesia and can be completed quickly. It’s an affordable option for patients seeking subtle yet meaningful improvements to their smile. The results are permanent and can significantly enhance your overall facial appearance.

  • Lack of confidence due to minor tooth imperfections
  • Discomfort from slightly chipped or rough edges
  • Unhappiness with tooth shape or proportion
  • Interest in a more balanced, symmetrical smile
  • Need for minor cosmetic improvements
  • Concerns about slight tooth overlapping
