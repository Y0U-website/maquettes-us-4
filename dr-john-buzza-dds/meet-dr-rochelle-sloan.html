Meet Rochelle Sloan

Dr. Rochelle Sloan

Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD)

One of the greatest compliments our patients can give us is the trust they place in our team. As our practice continues to grow, it was important to me to find a doctor who shares the same philosophy of compassionate, patient-centered care that you’ve come to expect at Buzza Dental Group.

I’m excited to welcome Dr. Rochelle Sloan to our team! From the moment I met Dr. Sloan, I knew she would be a wonderful fit for our practice, and I can’t wait for you to meet her.

Originally from Reno, Nevada, Dr. Sloan earned her bachelor’s degrees in Biology and Biochemistry from the University of Nevada, Reno, followed by a master’s degree in Biomedical Sciences before receiving her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the UNLV School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Sloan’s passion for dentistry began after experiencing firsthand how restoring her own smile changed her confidence and outlook on life. That experience inspired her to pursue a career focused on helping others restore not only their smiles, but also their confidence and overall well-being.

When she’s not in the office, Dr. Sloan enjoys spending time with her husband, Devin, and their dog, Bailey. She loves fishing, skiing, kayaking, and traveling, and is currently working toward visiting all seven continents.

We’re excited for Dr. Sloan to become part of the Buzza Dental Group family, and we know you’ll enjoy getting to know her.
