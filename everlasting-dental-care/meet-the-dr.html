Meet the Doctor

Dr. Hugo Manrique

Hugo Manrique, DMD, has been in General Practice for more than 20 years, more recently a beloved family dentist in Leesburg, VA, for more than ten years. He welcomes all those looking for a new dentist and thanks you for the opportunity to earn your trust and continue to provide excellent comprehensive dental care to Falls Church and Arlington.

Dr. Manrique is an exceptional dentist who practices with precision and attention to every detail. Through his decades of experience, he has developed skillsets in every aspect of dentistry, from crowns, root canals, and implants to Invisalign and veneers. He has an easygoing but direct manner that will set you at ease, and he will present treatment plans with clarity.

Whenever you’re ready to let Dr. Manrique and his team help you with your dental needs please give us a call to learn more about how we can make your next dental visit the easiest and most pleasant you might have ever had.

Meet the Staff

Diem, Hygienist

Diem is originally from Vietnam. She has been working as a dental hygienist for 16 years and is looking forward to many more to come. She received her college education from both Virginia Commonwealth University and Northern Virginia Community College. She is also certified in CPR and often volunteers through Hope for Tomorrow. She is married with three wonderful children. When she is not busy at the office, Diem enjoys spending time with her family, gardening, traveling and cooking.

Elsi, Patient Coordinator

Elsi is from El Salvador, and she loves helping others out. Someone’s smile is the first thing that catches her eye, and for that reason, she decided to work in the dental field. She loves working in a place that will help people achieve the smile they deserve. Elsi enjoys having a good laugh and trying new things out.

Daniela, Patient Coordinator

Daniela was born and raised in Honduras. She moved to the US eight years ago. She loves helping others the same way help was given to her. In her free time, Daniela likes to cook and bake. She believes that food is the universal language of love.

Susana, Dental Assistant

Susana is from Guatemala. She is a Dental Assistant and has been working in dentistry for about 24 years. Susana loves to serve and care for people, so she also commits some of her time to dental missionary work each year with her charity, Healthy Tooth, Journey of Hope. In her free time, Susana likes to exercise, cook fresh meals, and play with her dog, Zeus.

Lily, Dental Assistant
