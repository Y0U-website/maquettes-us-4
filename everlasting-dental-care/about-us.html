New Patients

To ensure a smooth and efficient experience at our Falls Church dental clinic, we kindly encourage patients to fill out our New Patient Forms before their initial visit. If completing the forms beforehand is not possible, we request arriving 15-20 minutes before the scheduled appointment to allow ample time to complete the necessary paperwork.

Emergencies

A dental emergency includes intense pain, unusual swelling, broken appliances, or broken teeth. We understand the urgency of such situations, which is why we are proud to offer same-day and after-hours appointments specifically for emergencies. If you are experiencing a dental emergency, please call our Falls Church office immediately, and our dedicated team will provide prompt assistance and schedule your appointment accordingly.

Payment Options

Everlasting Dental Care accepts all major insurances. We also offer convenient payment plans for individuals and families, 0 % financing, in-office dental plans, senior discounts, and Cherry Payment Plans . Please give our office a call with any questions, and we’ll be happy to assist you.
