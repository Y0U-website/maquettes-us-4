About Our Minimally Invasive Approach Biomimetic Dentistry: Preserving Your Natural Smile Biomimetic dentistry is a revolutionary approach that focuses on preserving and strengthening your natural teeth. At Harbor Modern Dentistry, we embrace this innovative technique to provide conservative, minimally invasive dental procedures that treat weak, cracked, and decayed teeth effectively. The core principle of biomimetic dentistry is to maintain as much of your natural tooth structure as possible while restoring its strength and function.

By utilizing advanced materials and techniques, we can often eliminate the need for altering teeth for crowns or performing destructive root canal therapy. This approach allows us to seal teeth from bacterial invasion, significantly reducing the need for more invasive procedures traditionally used in dentistry. Studies have shown that biomimetic dentistry can reduce the need for root canals by well over 80 %. The result is more comfortable treatments, longer-lasting restorations, and better long-term outcomes for your oral health.
