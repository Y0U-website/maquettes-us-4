Meet Our Dentists

Thomas Hada

Dr. Thomas Hada graduated from Toms River High School North in 1978. He attended Muhlenberg College and then graduated from Georgetown University with Honors in 1986. He attended a post-graduate dental residency in Hartford, CT. He was awarded the Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry in 1995. The Academy of General Dentistry bestowed the Masters in Academy of General Dentistry to Dr. Hada in 2003.

Dr. Hada has two adult children. He is active in the Academy of General Dentistry Board of Directors at the state level. Photography, video production, and guitar are his artistic interests. Scuba and a variety of sports are his current physical pursuits.

Robert McTaggart

Dr. Robert McTaggart graduated from Rutgers University in 1992 and the Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in 1998. He completed a residency at Hackensack University Medical Center. He is a member of the American Dental Association®, holds a Fellowship from the American Dental Society of Anesthesiology, is a Diplomat in the National Dental Board of Anesthesia, and is an attending physician at Monmouth Medical Center with Operating Room privileges.

Dr. McTaggart is married, has a daughter, and lives in Red Bank. He is also an avid musician, golfer, and skier.

Leonard Giles

Dr. Leonard Giles graduated with a B.S. degree from Waynesburg University and obtained his dental degree in 1977 from the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey (now Rutgers School of Dental Medicine). He is a member of the American Dental Association® and American Dental Society of Anesthesiology. He has achieved master’s status in the College of Sedation.

Dr. Giles is a long-time resident of Spring Lake Heights, New Jersey, NJ. He has been the Chairperson of his town’s Local Board of Health for 10 years and serves as commissioner and Vice President of the Monmouth Regional Health Commission. Dr Giles is also the President of the Jersey Shore Regional Health Commission. He is an avid skier, golfer, runner, and outdoor enthusiast.
