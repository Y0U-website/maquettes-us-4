How Do Dental Veneers Work?

First, the dentist at Sun Dental will take an impression of your natural teeth on which to model the final veneers. The shade of the veneers will be chosen based on your skin tone and the natural shades of your teeth; you don’t want veneers that look unnaturally white and fake.

The next step of the process is roughening the teeth so that the veneers will stick. The cosmetic dentist will roughen the front surfaces of your teeth one by one. Teeth that aren’t receiving veneers won’t be changed, so they’ll stay healthy and active.

While you’re waiting for the finished product, your cosmetic dentist will fit you with temporary veneers. These are usually made of acrylic, rather than porcelain, so they are more fragile and might stain more easily.
