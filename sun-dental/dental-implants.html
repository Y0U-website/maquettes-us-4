What Should You Expect

To qualify for implants, you need to have healthy gums and a sufficient amount of bone to support the implant. Careful and frequent oral hygiene, as well as regular trips to the dentist, are required to have your implants be successful long-term.

During the procedure, the dentist will match the color of your implant to match your existing teeth. The treatment time can vary depending on your needs but plan on multiple appointments to ensure that your implants are placed correctly and will work for years. If you have questions about dental implants, call us!

Why Should You Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a common procedure to replace missing teeth. This procedure is popular because implants are more reliable, look more natural, and feel more comfortable than dentures. If you choose dental implants, you’ll be able to chew more quickly because the implants are anchored in your jaw, just like your natural teeth are. Our dentists are the best in the business when it comes to dental implants.

Dental implants provide a strong foundation for artificial teeth because the implants are fused to your jaw bone. This ensures that the fake teeth won’t shift in your mouth, so the implants look, feel, and function just as natural teeth would.
