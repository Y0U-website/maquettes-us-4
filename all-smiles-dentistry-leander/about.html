Dr. Zhiyong (John) fan d.d.s, p.a.

Dr. Fan has actively participated in community service activities. He served as the president of Asian American Student Dental Association in dental school. He is an active advocator for diversity and equality. He is an active member of the Diversity and Inclusion Committee of Greater Houston Dental Society since 2018. He is also an active member of the American Dental Association, Texas Dental Association, and Capital Area Dental Society.

When not at the office Dr. Fan spends time with his family enjoying traveling, hiking, and playing tennis.
