Invisalign

Invisalign’s® invisible, removable, and comfortable aligners will give you the beautiful straight teeth you’ve always wanted. And, best of all, no one can tell that you’re wearing them. Invisalign® is great for adults and teenagers. Invisalign® is the invisible way to straighten your teeth without traditional braces. Invisalign® uses a series of clear, removable aligners to straighten your teeth without metal wires or brackets. Invisalign® has been proven effective in clinical research and in orthodontic practices nationwide.

You wear each set of aligners for about 2 weeks, removing them only to eat, drink, brush, and floss. As you replace each aligner with the next in the series, your teeth will move little by little, week by week - until they have straightened to the their final position. You’ll visit us about once every 6 weeks to ensure that your treatment is progressing as planned. Total treatment time averages 9 - 15 months and the average number of aligners during treatment is between 18 - 30, but both will vary from case to case.

You’d Be Amazed... The aligners are made through a combination of our expertise and 3-D computer imaging technology.

Veneers

A veneer is natural looking, custom-made layer that fits over your teeth to help create your beautiful smile and protect your teeth from damage. Veneers have a longer life expectancy and color stability than composite, and they highly resistant to permanent staining from coffee, tea, or even cigarette smoking. Typically, veneer treatment takes at least three appointments: diagnosis and treatment planning, preparation, and bonding.

A healthy smile can not only make you more attractive, but it will also boost your self confidence. There are many reasons why people get veneers, and some of the most common are:

Cover up teeth stains and discoloration Cover gaps between the front teeth Change the length, color, and size of teeth Fix chipped, cracked, or broken teeth from injuries Fix worn down teeth from teeth grinding
