Our Services

Our Comprehensive Range of Dental Services

At Rose Rock Family Dentistry, we offer a wide range of services to meet the diverse needs of our patients. From preventative care to advanced restorative treatments, our goal is to keep your smile healthy and radiant.

Maintaining Healthy Smiles

Routine cleanings and checkups are essential for maintaining a healthy smile. During these visits, we conduct comprehensive exams, including checks for tooth decay, oral cancer, and other health issues. Our periodontal therapy includes regular gum evaluations and in-office nonsurgical treatments to manage and prevent gum disease effectively.

Restoring Functionality

We provide durable, natural-looking solutions to restore function and aesthetics to your smile. Our tooth-colored restorations blend seamlessly with your teeth, while crowns and bridges offer added support and protection for compromised teeth. For missing teeth, we offer complete and partial dentures that restore your smile’s functionality and appearance.

Extractions and Root Canals

Extractions may be recommended for impacted, decayed, or crowded teeth, while root canal therapy can save a damaged or infected tooth. Both procedures are conducted in-office, often with options like laughing gas to increase comfort. We handle most cases, but we also have trusted specialists for more complex procedures.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Our cosmetic services are designed to enhance your smile’s appearance. We offer professional teeth whitening through convenient take-home kits and custom whitening trays. For straighter teeth, ClearCorrect provides an effective alternative to traditional braces, using custom-made, clear aligners for a discreet look.

Dental Implants

Implants provide a permanent solution for missing teeth, offering unmatched durability and appearance. The implant process typically involves a healing period after placement, followed by the addition of a custom crown. This procedure is performed in-office, allowing you to restore your smile without a specialist referral.

Professional Support

Our team is here to help you understand your options and choose the best treatment for your needs. Contact us today to schedule a consultation and start your journey to a healthier smile.
