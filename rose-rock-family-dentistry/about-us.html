About Us

Dedicated to Creating Healthy and Confident Smiles

At Rose Rock Family Dentistry, we are driven by a mission to provide exceptional dental care that combines skill, compassion, and the latest technology. Dr. Sara Bassett and our dedicated team believe that a healthy smile can transform lives, and we are honored to support our patients in achieving their smile goals. With extensive professional experience and a commitment to ongoing education, we stay up-to-date on advancements in dental care, ensuring our patients benefit from modern techniques and technology.

An Individualized Approach to Your Health

Our individualized approach to dental care means we listen closely to each patient’s unique needs and concerns, creating a tailored plan to achieve the best possible outcomes. We believe in empowering our patients with knowledge and options, so you can make informed decisions about your oral health.

A Welcoming Environment for Everyone

We’re passionate about creating a comfortable and welcoming atmosphere for all our patients, especially those with dental anxiety. Our friendly and experienced team, which includes our skilled dental hygienist Courtney, office manager Amanda, and registered dental assistants Mady and Chloe, strives to make every visit a positive experience.

Proud to Serve Our Community

We are proud to serve Stillwater and the surrounding areas, and we look forward to welcoming you into our dental family. Contact us today to discover how Rose Rock Family Dentistry can support your smile and health.

Dr. Sara Bassett

Dr. Sara Bassett attended Oklahoma State University earning degrees in Zoology and Medical Technology. After working several years as a medical technologist, she finally pursued her lifelong dream of being a dentist. She attended OU College of Dentistry and graduated with honors. She continued her education with a General Practice Residency and went on to gain experience in family health center and corporate settings.

Dr. Bassett and her husband of twenty-five years, Daniel, have two daughters. Erin is a recent OU graduate and is now pursuing her PhD. Avery graduated from Stillwater High School and is attending Oklahoma State. When the weather is right, the Bassett family enjoys spending as much time at the lake as possible!

Office Staff

Registered Dental Hygienist

She met her husband in high school and they were married in 2019. In her spare time she enjoys baking and binge watching Netflix. She welcomed a little boy in 2020 and has one daughter, Brynlee, who is active in competitive dance at Thriller Dance Studio and attends school in Perry.
