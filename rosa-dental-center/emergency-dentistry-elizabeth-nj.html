WHAT IT IS

Emergency dentistry provides immediate attention for sudden dental issues that cannot wait for a routine appointment. This may include intense toothaches, cracked or fractured teeth, swelling, infections, damaged restorations, or dental trauma. At Rosa Dental Center, we focus on stabilizing the issue, relieving discomfort, and developing a clear treatment plan that restores function and protects surrounding teeth.

HOW IT HELPS
