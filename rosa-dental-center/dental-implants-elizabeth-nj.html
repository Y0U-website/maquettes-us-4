HOW IT HELPS

Dental implants help maintain jawbone structure by stimulating the bone similarly to a natural tooth root. This prevents bone reduction that may occur after tooth loss. Implants also provide improved chewing efficiency compared to removable options. By stabilizing adjacent teeth and supporting bite alignment, dental implants contribute to overall oral balance.
