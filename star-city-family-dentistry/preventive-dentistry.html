What to Expect During Preventive Dentistry

Preventive dentistry in Roanoke, VA, follows a step-by-step, patient-friendly process. Visits center on comfort, education, and practical steps to keep your smile stable between appointments.

Dr. Jeannene Bradley focuses on preventive dental care that fits your goals. She listens, answers questions, and designs a plan so you know exactly what to expect at every visit.

  • Comprehensive evaluation to check teeth, gums, bite, and oral tissues.
  • Gentle professional cleaning to remove plaque and tartar you cannot reach at home.
  • Cavity and gum disease screening to catch issues at the earliest stage.
  • Targeted preventive care such as fluoride or sealants when indicated.
  • Clear home-care coaching tailored to your needs and lifestyle.

Pros and Cons of Preventive Dentistry

  • Pro: Fewer invasive procedures by addressing concerns early.
  • Pro: Shorter, more comfortable visits focused on wellness.
  • Pro: Improved confidence knowing your oral health is on track.
  • Con: Requires consistent checkups and home routines to stay effective.
  • Con: Minor issues may still arise, but they are typically easier to resolve.
