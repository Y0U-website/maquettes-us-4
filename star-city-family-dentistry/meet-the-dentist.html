Meet Our Dentist in Roanoke, VA

Jeannene Bradley, DDS

Dr. Jeannene Bradley was born in Sacramento, California, and raised in Prescott, Arizona. She began her dental career during her senior year of high school in 1985, when she was employed as a dental assistant trainee. She continued to work in dental offices in San Diego for three years. The next stop was Los Angeles for two years, at which time she was employed as office manager at Roentgen Medical X-Ray Company, which is the largest radiographic company in the United States.

Dr. Bradley relocated to Chesapeake Virginia in 1992, where she resided for nine years. During this time, Dr. Bradley attended the accredited dental assisting program at Old Dominion University during 1994 and 1995, where she graduated valedictorian of the class. In June 1995, she passed the certified dental assistant board exam and started working for Dr. William B. Keeler, which soon became her mentor and encouraged her to continue with her education toward the path of becoming a dentist.

Dr. Bradley’s goal as a dentist is to provide quality dentistry for patients in a comfortable, positive and nonjudgmental atmosphere at a reasonable cost. In order to do this, she chooses to be a provider for many dental insurance providers so our patients can receive the care they need.

Dr. Bradley has been married to her husband, Thomas, for over 29 years and together they have two daughters. When not at the office, she and her husband ride motorcycles and are big Virginia Tech Hokies fans. Although not a hobby, they are very supportive of their daughter’s participation in and love of sports.
