Orthodontics

Invisalign is a convenient solution for people with mild to moderate orthodontic issues that we can treat without making you wear tradition braces. It is ideal for people who don’t want others to see metallic wires from conventional braces in their mouth.

Invisalign can fix your mild to moderate under / overbite and crooked or crowded teeth. Fixing these issues not only makes your smile beautiful, but hygiene care of such teeth is also quite difficult; therefore, straightening them also improves the health of your teeth and gums.
