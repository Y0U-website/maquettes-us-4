Got Questions About Dental Implants?

Dental Implants FAQs Cherrywood Dental Associates

  • What Are Dental Implants, and How Do They Work? Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. They provide a stable foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures, restoring both function and aesthetics.
  • What Are the Benefits of Dental Implants? Dental implants offer several advantages: Improved speech and chewing ability
  • Enhanced comfort and confidence
  • Prevention of bone loss and gum recession
  • Long-term durability and reliability
  • What Is the All-On-4 Dental Implant Procedure? The All-On-4 procedure involves placing four implants in the jaw to support a full arch of replacement teeth. This technique allows for immediate restoration, often completed in a single visit.
  • How Long Does the Dental Implant Process Take? The timeline varies based on individual needs, but Cherrywood Dental offers same-day implant and restoration placement for eligible patients, reducing the overall treatment duration.
  • Are Dental Implants Covered by Insurance? Cherrywood Dental accepts all PPO insurance plans and offers financing options through CareCredit and Lending Club to make dental implants more accessible.
