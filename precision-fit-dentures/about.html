Our Business

Precision Fit was started by Ana and Andrew McMurray in 2002 as a lab to manufacture fixed and removable prostheses for dentists. Through many years of experience we came to the conclusion that patients would get better quality results if we could examine them ourselves, and then make the work in our lab. This is when we decided to pursue denturism. Today we bring more than 20 years hands-on experience to you, ensuring you have a positive outcome. We always have the time to answer your questions and concerns.

Our Family

Like most people, our life revolves around our daughter and two sons. We love spending time together as a family. We relish in cooking cuisine from all over the world, and hosting people in our home to share it with them. We enjoy many hobbies, such as hiking, photography, and team sports, and attend church services on Sundays.
