Accessibility Statement Dental Group of Chicago is committed to ensuring our website is accessible for all users, including those with disabilities. If you experience any difficulty using the website, please contact us—help is only a call or email away. 2. Enhancing Your Experience Consider these adjustments for better accessibility: Text & Visibility : Zoom in or increase text size using your browser (e.g., Ctrl + “+” / Cmd + “+”).
  • Navigation : Use keyboard navigation (Tab, Shift+Tab).
  • Contrast & Visibility : Adjust color contrast or use browser accessibility tools.
  • Assistive Tools : Use screen readers or “read aloud” browser features.
  • Speech Tools : Voice commands via speech recognition software (e.g., Windows Narrator).

3. Our Accessibility Goals

We strive to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards and regularly review our site for improvements.

4. Disclaimer

All content on this site (e.g., service descriptions, staff information) is for informational purposes only and does not constitute dental advice. For diagnosis, treatment, or professional recommendations, please consult with our dental team directly.
