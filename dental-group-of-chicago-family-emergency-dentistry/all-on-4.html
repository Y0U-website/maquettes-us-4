What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

The All-on-4 technique utilizes a minimum of four strategically placed implants in the jaw, and these implants are a stable base for supporting a full arch of prosthetic teeth.

What makes All-on-4 unique is the angle at which the posterior implants are placed. The implants are typically angled about 30 to 45 degrees from the bite plane, which allows for increased contact by using the natural support of the bone, even in patients with diminished bone density, without requiring bone grafting.

This is particularly advantageous because it significantly reduces the overall treatment and recovery time when compared to traditional methods that often require more implants and extensive bone grafting.

Are You a Candidate for All-on-4 Dental Implants?

Deciding whether All-on-4 dental implants are right for you involves evaluating several key factors related to your oral health and overall wellness. The following is a guide to help determine if you might be an ideal candidate for this dental procedure:

Adequate Jawbone Structure

While All-on-4 implants are designed to minimize the need for bone grafting by taking advantage of the existing bone, a basic level of jawbone density and volume is still required. Your dentist will perform detailed imaging studies to assess your jawbone’s condition. If you have moderate bone loss, the strategic placement and angling of the implants may still make this procedure a viable option.

Good Overall Health

Successful implant surgery depends largely on your body’s ability to heal properly. Ideal candidates for All-on-4 implants are generally in good health. Chronic conditions, such as diabetes or heart disease, should be well-managed.

Non-Smokers or Willing to Quit

Smoking significantly impairs healing in the mouth, increasing the risk of implant failure. If you smoke, you should quit or at least be willing to stop prior to and throughout the healing process to improve your surgical outcomes.

Looking for a Long-Term Solution

Candidates should be seeking a permanent solution to tooth loss. These implants are designed to be a long-lasting treatment, providing an alternative to dentures that is more fixed and feels more like natural teeth.

Current Denture Users

Individuals who are already using dentures and are unsatisfied with the comfort, function, or fit of their current dentures are excellent candidates. These implants are a more stable and comfortable solution, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with more confidence.

What Is the Process of Getting All-on-4 Dental Implants?

The process of getting All-on-4 dental implants involves several steps, including:

Implant Consultation

During your implant consultation, your dentist at the Dental Group of Chicago will review your medical and dental histories, conduct a full oral examination, and use imaging techniques such as X-rays or CT scans to assess the density and volume of your jawbone. This is also the time for you to discuss your expectations and any concerns you may have.

Treatment Plan

We will create a personalized All-on-4 treatment plan and determine the best placement for the four implants to maximize the use of available bone and plan for the prosthetic teeth that will be attached to these implants. This phase may involve collaboration with other dental professionals to ensure all aspects of the procedure are carefully.

Surgical Procedure

The actual implant surgery is usually completed in a single day. Under local anesthesia or sedation, we will place the four implants in your jawbone – two near the front of your mouth and two at the back, angled to take full advantage of existing bone structure. After the implants are in place, temporary teeth are often attached, providing function and aesthetics until the final prosthesis is ready.

Healing and Integration

During this phase, which can last several months, the implants fuse with the jawbone to make a foundation for your new teeth. You need to follow a modified diet and adhere to specific oral hygiene practices to ensure successful healing.

Permanent Prosthesis

Once your implants have fully integrated with your jawbone, you will return to the dentist to have your permanent prosthetic teeth fitted. These teeth are custom-made to match the natural color, shape, and alignment of your smile. This step might require several visits to fine-tune the fit and function of the prosthesis.

Follow-Up Care

Follow-up visits are necessary to monitor the implants and the health of your surrounding tissues. We will check the stability of the implants, the condition of the prosthesis, and ensure your bite is functioning properly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does implant placement work in the All-on-4 technique?

Implant placement in the All-on-4 technique involves strategically positioning four dental implants in the jawbone to support a full arch of replacement teeth. Two implants are placed vertically near the front of the jaw where bone density is typically stronger, while two posterior implants are angled at 30 to 45 degrees. This careful positioning of axial and tilted implants maximizes contact with existing bone and avoids critical anatomical structures, allowing for successful tooth replacement even in patients with reduced bone density without requiring extensive bone grafting procedures.

What is the success rate of All-on-4 implants, and how long do they last?

All-on-4 dental implants demonstrate exceptional success rates, with research published in the National Center for Biotechnology Information showing survival rates of 99.8 % for treatments lasting 24 months or longer. Long-term studies reveal patient success rates of 93.8 % and prosthesis survival rates of 99.2 % at 10 years. The strategic placement of tilted posterior implants at 30-45 degree angles maximizes bone contact and distributes forces effectively. With proper oral hygiene and regular dental visits, All-on-4 implants can last 15-25 years or even a lifetime.

Can I get All-on-4 if I have bone loss?

Yes, All-on-4 implants are specifically designed for patients who need tooth extractions or have experienced bone loss. We can often extract failing teeth and place All-on-4 implants during the same appointment. The angled posterior implants are positioned to maximize contact with existing bone, often avoiding areas of significant bone loss and eliminating the need for costly bone grafting procedures. Even patients who have worn dentures for years are often excellent candidates.

What is the recovery process like after All-on-4 surgery?

Recovery from All-on-4 surgery is typically more comfortable than traditional implant procedures because you receive immediate replacement teeth. Most patients experience mild to moderate discomfort for 2-4 days, manageable with pain medications. You’ll receive temporary teeth on surgery day and can eat soft foods immediately. Most patients return to work within 2-3 days. Complete healing takes 3-6 months, during which your temporary teeth function normally while implants fuse with your jawbone.

How do I care for All-on-4 implants, and do they require special maintenance?

Caring for All-on-4 implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Daily brushing with a soft-bristled toothbrush, flossing with special floss threaders around the prosthetic teeth, and using antimicrobial mouthwash prevents gum disease. The fixed bridge requires cleaning underneath to remove food particles. Professional cleanings every six months allow specialized maintenance. Unlike dentures, All-on-4 implants stay in your mouth permanently. Avoid chewing ice or using teeth as tools, and wear a night guard if you grind your teeth.
