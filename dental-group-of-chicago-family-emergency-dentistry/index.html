The All-on-4 technique utilizes a minimum of four strategically placed implants in the jaw, and these implants are a stable base for supporting a full arch of prosthetic teeth.

What makes All-on-4 unique is the angle at which the posterior implants are placed. The implants are typically angled about 30 to 45 degrees from the bite plane, which allows for increased contact by using the natural support of the bone, even in patients with diminished bone density, without requiring bone grafting.

This is particularly advantageous because it significantly reduces the overall treatment and recovery time when compared to traditional methods that often require more implants and extensive bone grafting.
