What Makes Single Tooth Implants the Gold Standard

A single tooth implant replaces both the root and crown of your missing tooth with a titanium post and custom porcelain crown. Unlike bridges or partial dentures, implants preserve your healthy adjacent teeth and maintain jawbone structure through osseointegration.

The implant process begins with a comprehensive evaluation to assess your bone density and gum health. We place the titanium implant directly into your jawbone, where it fuses with your natural bone over several months. Once healed, we attach a custom-crafted crown that matches your surrounding teeth perfectly in color, shape, and function.

Single implants offer several advantages over traditional tooth replacement options. They provide the same biting force as natural teeth, allowing you to eat all your favorite foods without restriction. The implant stimulates your jawbone, preventing the bone loss that occurs with missing teeth. Most importantly, properly maintained implants can last decades or even a lifetime.

The Implant Process in Chicago’s South Loop

Your implant journey at our South Loop practice starts with detailed imaging and treatment planning. We use advanced technology to map your jawbone structure and determine the optimal implant placement for long-term success.

The surgical placement appointment typically takes one to two hours under local anesthesia. Most patients experience minimal discomfort and return to work within a day or two. During the healing period, we may provide a temporary restoration to maintain your appearance and function.

After osseointegration is complete, usually three to six months later, we take a scan for your final crown. Our dental laboratory creates a restoration that seamlessly blends with your natural teeth. The final crown attachment appointment is quick and comfortable, completing your transformation.

Factors That Influence Treatment Success

Several elements contribute to implant success rates, which exceed 95 % when properly planned and maintained. Adequate bone volume and density provide the foundation for stable implant integration. Good oral hygiene and regular dental cleanings help maintain healthy gums around your implant.

Certain medical conditions or medications may affect healing, which we address during your consultation. Smoking significantly impacts implant success and healing time, so we recommend cessation before treatment. Age is rarely a limiting factor, as healthy patients in their 80s and 90s successfully receive implants.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do single tooth implants protect my healthy teeth compared to other options?

Single tooth implants preserve your healthy teeth by eliminating the need to modify adjacent teeth for support. Traditional bridges require grinding down neighboring healthy teeth to serve as anchors, permanently weakening them. Implants stand independently, secured directly into your jaw bone through the titanium implant post. This approach maintains the integrity of your natural tooth structure while preventing the bone loss that occurs with a lost tooth. The implant also distributes chewing forces naturally, protecting surrounding teeth from excessive stress and potential damage over time.
