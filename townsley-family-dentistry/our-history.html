Our History

Townsley Family Dentistry has a long history of serving patients and families in Foley, AL. Our practice is built on a commitment to personal care, strong relationships, and dependable dentistry for patients of all ages.

Over the years, Townsley Family Dentistry has continued to grow while maintaining the same focus on patient comfort, communication, and high-quality dental care.

Our History

Our dental office has been part of the Foley community for many years, providing families with preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dental care in a welcoming environment.

As the practice has evolved, our team has continued to invest in modern dental technology, continuing education, and comprehensive services so patients can receive trusted care in one familiar location.

Today, Townsley Family Dentistry continues to serve the Foley area with the same patient-focused approach that has shaped the practice from the beginning.

Our Commitment

We are proud to care for generations of patients and families throughout Foley and the surrounding communities. Our goal is to create a dental home where patients feel heard, respected, and confident in their care.
