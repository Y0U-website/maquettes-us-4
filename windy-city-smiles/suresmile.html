Forget everything you’ve heard about challenges of treatments to straighten your teeth! With the SureSmile breakthrough technology – using custom built clear aligners – you get the smile you always wanted without unsightly bands, brackets or wires.

Clearly A Better Solution.

The clear, removable aligners are designed with your comfort in mind. No food getting stuck in the metal braces. No worrying about putting on rubber bands.

Invisible and Comfortable.

Invisalign is a nearly invisible solution. You won’t draw undue attention to your mouth while in treatment. In fact, virtually no one will know you’re wearing them unless you tell. Comfortable to wear, these clear aligners are removable so you can enjoy that glass of red wine at an event, and still go back to your treatment at the end of the evening!

Reduced Treatment Time

Using a state of the art iTero 5D Scanner – after scanning and planning your treatment, the actual treatment takes about 6-9 months from start to finish. You’ll see Dr. Cavero every month or two to confirm progress. Every few visits you’ll be given additional aligners to continue your treatment. Commonly, the number of aligners worn during treatment is between 18 and 30, but treatment time and number of aligners will vary from case to case.
