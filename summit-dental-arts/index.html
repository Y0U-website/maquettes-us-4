Restorative dentistry focuses on repairing damaged or decayed teeth to restore their functionality and appearance. This includes procedures like fillings, crowns, and bridges to ensure you can maintain a healthy bite and smile.

Another of Dr. Tran’s favorite dental services is veneers! Dental veneers are thin, custom-made shells that cover the front surface of teeth to improve appearance. They’re commonly used to address discoloration, chips, and gaps in teeth for a flawless smile.

Dental crowns are caps placed over damaged or weakened teeth to restore their shape, size, and strength. Crowns are often used after root canals or when a tooth is severely decayed or broken.
