Meet The Doctors

Dr. Fuscaldo

Dr. Frank Fuscaldo graduated from the West Virginia University School of Dentistry. He attended Duquesne University for his undergraduate studies. After serving two years in the US Public Health Service, Dr. Fuscaldo joined Holly Beach Dental in 1993.

A native of Fairmont, WV, Dr. Fuscaldo and his wife Josette reside in Wildwood Crest with their three daughters.

Dr. Backenstose

Dr. Thomas Backenstose is the most recent addition to the practice, our dental Shoobie! After earning his undergraduate degree from Harvard, he earned his DMD from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine and his prosthodontic certificate from Temple’s Kornberg School of Dentistry. He offers a range of experience in both restorative and implant dentistry.

Originally from Hershey, he currently splits his time between Philadelphia and Avalon. He enjoys cooking, opera, and golf, the great pastime of South Jersey.

Dr. Sachanandani

Dr. Kunal Sachanandani is a South Jersey native who grew up in Egg Harbor Township, New Jersey. He attended Richard Stockton University for his undergraduate studies. He earned a Bachelor of Science in biochemistry and molecular biology and graduated summa cum laude. He then continued his education at the Rutgers School of Dental Medicine to receive a Doctorate in Dental Medicine.

Dr. Sachanandani is passionate about dentistry and places great emphasis on the quality of care for his patients. He is also interested in meeting patients’ needs with the highest standards and newest technologies. He has a special interest in comprehensive treatment including root canals, extractions, dentures, fillings, veneers, crowns and bridges, and implant restorations. Dr. Sachanandani is an active member of the American Dental Association.

Outside of the office Dr. Sachanandani enjoys spending his time with his wife and grandparents, traveling, playing tennis, and going to the gym.

Meet The Hygienists

RoseAnn

RoseAnn is also a licensed real estate agent in both Pennsylvania and New Jersey. In her free time, she loves to travel and spend time with her family and her English Bulldog, Caesar.

Debi

Debi McGinnis grew up outside of Chicago and moved to the Philadelphia area in 1980. She graduated first in her class from Philadelphia Community College with an associates of Applied Science in Dental Hygiene in 1991. She received her certificate in local anesthesia in 2010. The first half of her career was spent in a periodontal office and later in general practices. Debi has been with Holly Beach Dental since 2010 where she has established a loyal following.

In her spare time, she has taken up golf and is determined to hit the ball every time.

Meaghan

Meaghan Fisher is a lifetime resident of Cape May County, where she has spent most of her career. She went to Harcum College and graduated in 2004 with an Associates of Applied Science in Dental Hygiene. She received her certificate of Local Anesthesia in 2019. Meaghan joined our team after spending 14 years with Dr. James Rochford, DMD, until his retirement, and looks forward to providing the same great care!

Meaghan spends her time outside of the office being a mom to her amazing children, William and Zoey, where she is involved in all things school, sports and dance. In the summer you will find her enjoying her time off at the beach.
