Meet Our Pediatric & Family Dentists

Dr. Rachael Graue

After completing her residency, Dr. Rachael accepted a position as an attending Pediatric Dentist at Children’s Mercy Hospital. She spent the next nine years treating patients at the Children’s Mercy Hospital Dental Clinic as well as teaching in the Pediatric Dentistry Residency Program. She continues to hold a position on the Hospital Medical Staff.

Dr. Rachael is a certified Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. She is also a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry and the American Dental Association, as well as multiple other local and state dental organizations. She is currently the regional representative of the Missouri Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.

Dr. Rachael and her husband Dr. Reese reside north of Liberty with their three boys Jack, Joseph, and James. When not caring for patients, Dr. Rachael enjoys reading, baking, traveling, spending time with family and friends, and being outdoors with her boys.

Dr. J. Reese Graue

Dr. J. Reese Graue is originally from Carthage Missouri, but has made Kansas City his home since moving to the city for college. He was accepted into the combined college and dental school accelerated program at the University of Missouri-Kansas City School of Dentistry right after graduating high school. He graduated with a Bachelor of Arts in Biology and a Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree in 2008.

Dr. Reese is also a member of the American Dental Association, the Missouri Dental Association, the Greater Kansas City Dental Society, and the Academy of General Dentistry.

He lives in the Northland with his wife Dr. Rachael and three sons Jack, Joseph, and James and is an active member of his community. Dr. Reese is a past president and active member of the Rotary Club of Gladstone, a service club that strives to better the Gladstone community. In his free time, Dr. Reese enjoys spending time with his family and is an avid outdoorsman.

Dr. Reese will be treating patients in Parkville as well as continuing to serve his patients in Gladstone.
