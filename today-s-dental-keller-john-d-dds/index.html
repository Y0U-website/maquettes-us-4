Tooth loss can be detrimental to your oral health. Missing teeth, when left untreated, can reduce your ability to perform normal oral functions and can have a negative impact on your jawbone’s development and your smile’s appearance.

Implants are a relatively new advance in dental technology and are a great alternative to traditional tooth replacement treatments. Implants add the element of permanence to prosthetic teeth and help to eliminate the possibility of missing or lost tooth replacements. When properly maintained a dental implant can last you your entire life.

Implants are a great solution to tooth loss. We provide dental implants in a comforting and relaxing environment. If you’re considering an implant for your missing tooth, or want more info on the implant procedure, contact our office.
