What factors make me a good candidate for dental implants?

Dental implants are great, permanent option for many with missing teeth, yet several factors must be considered before you can become a viable implant candidate.

Patients are required to have healthy gums and an adequate amount of jawbone before moving ahead with an implant procedure. Both are necessary for proper implant installation, as well as a commitment to good dental hygiene and regular visits to the dentist. Certain health issues, such as heart disease and diabetes, are also considered before proceeding, and smoking is strongly discouraged.
