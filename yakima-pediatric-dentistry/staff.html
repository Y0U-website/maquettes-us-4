Ashley Tercero, DDS Pediatric Dentist & Owner

Dr. Ashley grew up in the greater Seattle area, but attended college at UCLA, where she earned her B.S. degree in Physiological Science in 2002. Happy to return home, she earned her D.D.S. degree in 2006, followed by her Certificate in Pediatric Dentistry in 2008, from the University of Washington.

After spending her two-year Pediatric Dental Residency in Yakima, Dr. Ashley and her husband, Ryan, grew to love living in Eastern Washington. They wanted to help address the immense need for pediatric dentistry in the area and decided to make this their permanent home. In July of 2008, Dr. Ashley and her husband fulfilled their lifelong dream of working with children, and opened the first privately owned Pediatric Dental Practice in Yakima.

When she is not working, Dr. Ashley enjoys spending time with her wonderful family.

Dr. Ashley is Board Certified with the ABPD, is a member of the AAPD, WSAPD, ADA, WSDA, and has served as the President of the Yakima Valley Dental Society.

Jessica Kennedy, DDS

Dr. Jessica grew up in the Yakima valley. She attended the University of Portland, in Oregon, where she earned her B.S degree in Biology. In 2015, she earned her D.D.S degree from the University of Washington. Followed by her Certificate in Pediatric Dentistry in 2017 from the NYU Lutheran Dental Medicine. In her second year of residency, Dr. Jessica also completed a fellowship in Leadership Education in Neurodevelopmental and Related Disabilities (LEND) through the University of Washington and served as the Chief Resident.

While in dental school, Dr. Jessica developed an interest in pediatric dentistry after shadowing Dr. Ashley at Yakima Pediatric Dentistry. Dr. Jessica is excited to continue to be back home, serving the Yakima community as a member of the experienced and skilled team at Yakima Pediatric Dentistry - where her interest in pediatric dentistry was initially sparked!

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Jessica spends the majority of her time being a mom to her four young children. In any free time she comes by, Dr. Jessica enjoys spending it with her family and friends, exercising, and visiting San Juan Island.

Dr. Jessica has been a diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry since 2018. She is also an active member of the AAPD, WSAPD, ADA, WSDA, and Yakima Valley Dental Society.

Steven Karmy, DDS, DNDBA

Dr. Karmy is a dental anesthesiologist working for Eastern Washington Ambulatory Anesthesia Services. He grew up here, in the Pacific Northwest. Dr. Karmy graduated from Walla Walla University and completed dental school and his anesthesia residency at Loma Linda University in California. His practice is limited to providing general anesthesia and sedation services for dental patients in outpatient settings.

He lives in Walla Walla with his wife and son, Melissa and Vinny. He enjoys motorcycle riding and fly fishing in the summers. During the winter he can be found fishing for steelhead and sturgeon. His black lab Duke, also an avid fisherman, loves to accompany him to the water.
