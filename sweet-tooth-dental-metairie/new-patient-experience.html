Patient Experience

At a time when one-on-one healthcare is increasingly hard to come by, you will have a very different experience here at Sweet Tooth Dental.

Your first visit will be all about you. We will sit down with you, listen to your story, explain dental health/disease to you, and gather important information about your current oral health. During this time, we will take digital x-rays, a wellness oral scan, intraoral photos and traditional photos of your teeth and smile, and complete a comprehensive tour around your mouth.

When you return to the office for your second visit, we will sit down with you again to explain the current condition of your teeth and mouth. We will review your x-rays, scan, and photos together, you will be able to see exactly what we see. This will allow us the opportunity to offer you options and discuss how you would like to proceed with your care.

Together we will co-create a plan that works for you! Whether it’s a complete smile makeover or a plan to just get your mouth healthy again, we will walk along side you every step of the way. We know you will love our new patient experience, come see for yourselves!
