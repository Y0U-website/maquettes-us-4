Meet the Team

At Sweet Tooth Dental, we love our patients, and we also love teeth! We are passionate about helping you keep your natural teeth for life. We also love beautiful smiles and healthy teeth and gums.

We promise to always offer the highest quality of care to you and your family. Utilizing the latest in dental technology, as well as, the best techniques in cosmetic and restorative dentistry, we are committed to offering you the best quality dental care you have ever had!

Here at Sweet Tooth, you will become part of our family! From the moment you walk through our doors, you will feel at home. Our office is warm and inviting and full of smiling faces! Come see for yourself…we promise, we know you will love it here!

At Sweet Tooth Dental, you will have individualized attention and care. We see only one patient at a time, focusing only on you. We will listen to you, develop a real relationship with you, and ensure your comfort and oral healthcare needs are met.

We work with you to achieve a healthy, beautiful smile. A smile you will be proud of and a smile you will love!
