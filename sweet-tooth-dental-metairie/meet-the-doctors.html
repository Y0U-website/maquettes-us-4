Meet Dr. Gretchen

Christian.​ ​Wife.​ Mother. ​Daughter.​ ​Sister.​ ​Niece.​ ​Aunt.​ ​Loyal​ ​Friend.​ ​Dentist.​ ​Artist.​ ​Smile​ ​Designer. Dream​ ​chaser.​ ​People​ ​lover.

I’m​ ​a​ ​true​ ​Southern​ ​girl​ ​and​ ​I​ ​love​ ​everything​ ​about​ ​the​ ​South.​ ​I​ ​love​ ​the​ ​weather,​ ​I​ ​love​ ​SEC football,​ ​I​ ​love​ ​camp life,​ ​I​ ​love​ ​the​ ​food,​ ​and​ ​most importantly,​ ​I​ ​love​ ​the​ ​people.​ ​This​ ​is​ ​what life​ ​is​ ​about​ ​for​ ​me.​ ​This​ ​is​ ​what​ ​makes​ ​me​ ​smile! I​ ​grew​ ​up​ ​in​ ​the​ ​New​ ​Orleans​ ​area​ ​and​ ​have never​ ​ventured​ ​too​ ​far​ ​from home…typical​ ​New​ ​Orleans​ ​girl!

After​ ​graduating​ ​from​ ​Mount​ ​Carmel​ ​Academy​ ​in​ ​New​ ​Orleans,​ ​I​ ​attended​ ​LSU​ ​in​ ​Baton Rouge​ ​then​ ​returned​ ​home​ ​for​ ​dental​ ​school,​ ​graduating​ ​from​ ​Louisiana​ ​State​ ​University​ ​School of​ ​Dentistry.​ ​I’m​ ​an​ ​LSU​ ​Tiger​ ​through​ ​and​ ​through!​

​I’m​ ​married​ ​to​ ​my​ ​best​ ​friend,​ ​Nolan.​​ He​ ​has​ ​been​ ​a​ ​wellness​ ​and​ ​life​ ​coach​ ​for​ ​almost​ ​20 years.​ ​Through​ ​his​ ​business,​ ​ Salire​ ​Fitness​ ​and​ ​Wellness ,​ ​he​ ​has​ ​helped​ ​numerous​ ​clients achieve​ ​their​ ​fitness​ ​and​ ​life​ ​goals.​ ​We​ ​both​ ​love​ ​helping​ ​people. We have two small children, Eva and Levi and a labradoodle, Motown – Mo for short!

Many​ ​people​ ​often​ ​ask​ ​how​ ​I​ ​knew​ ​I​ ​wanted​ ​to​ ​become​ ​a​ ​dentist.​ ​I​ ​have​ ​always​ ​loved​ ​people and​ ​working​ ​with​ ​my​ ​hands​ ​to​ ​make​ ​beautiful​ ​things…both​ ​are​ ​two​ ​things​ ​I​ ​get​ ​to​ ​do​ ​everyday! I​ ​became​ ​a​ ​dentist​ ​to​ ​help​ ​you​ ​achieve​ ​the​ ​SMILE​ ​of​ ​your​ ​DREAMS!​ ​

I’m​ ​here​ ​to​ ​be​ ​your​ ​friend, to​ ​listen​ ​to​ ​you​ ​and​ ​your​ ​concerns,​ ​to​ ​be​ ​honest​ ​with​ ​you,​ ​and​ ​to​ ​guide​ ​you​ ​on​ ​a​ ​path​ ​to​ ​oral health​ ​that​ ​aligns​ ​with​ ​your​ ​personal​ ​goals. I​ ​can’t​ ​wait​ ​to​ ​meet​ ​you​ ​and​ ​get​ ​to​ ​know​ ​you​ ​and​ ​your​ ​family!

Dr. Gretchen
