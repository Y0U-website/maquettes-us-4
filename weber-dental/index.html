OUR PROMISE TO YOU

Our primary goal is to empower you with the knowledge and tools to maintain a lifetime of optimal oral health through preventative care.

The practice

Whether you want to restore the function of your teeth or desire a smile makeover, we will create an individualized plan for you.

We provide an environment in which your child can develop a positive attitude toward dentistry and keep a healthy smile.

Dr. Weber, Dr. Truhan and our team of hygienists, dental assistants, and front office staff are dedicated to making each visit warm, caring, and stress-free.

Whether it’s a routine cleaning or treatment, we’re here to support you and your loved ones every step of the way. Our goal is to provide personalized care that makes you feel right at home, with your family’s smile as our top priority!
