The Procedure for Single Dental Implants

A single dental implant is the best solution for replacing a missing tooth, offering lasting benefits in both function and aesthetics. Whether you’re considering a traditional titanium implant or a zirconia dental implant, Sacramento Natural Dentistry in Citrus Heights, CA, is here to provide you with the best options for your oral health.   • Consultation and Planning : The first step is to schedule a consultation with your dentist. In this appointment, your dentist will take X-rays and assess your oral health to decide if you are a good candidate for a dental implant. If necessary, bone grafting may be done to ensure there is enough bone density for the implant.
  • Implant Placement : Once you’re ready, the dental implant post will be surgically settled into the jawbone. This is usually done under local anesthesia, and the procedure is relatively painless. After the implant is placed, it will take several months for the bone to heal and fuse with the titanium post.
  • Attaching the Abutment and Crown : Once the implant has completely integrated with the bone, an abutment is placed, followed by the customized crown. Your new tooth will match the surrounding teeth in shape, size, and color.
  • Aftercare : After the procedure, it’s important to follow your dentist’s instructions for caring for the implant site to secure proper healing and avoid complications.
