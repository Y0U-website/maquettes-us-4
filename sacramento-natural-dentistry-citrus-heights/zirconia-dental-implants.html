Ceramic Dental Implants Cost

The cost of ceramic dental implants can differ depending on factors such as the complexity of the case, additional treatments required (like bone grafting), and the number of implants needed. On average, a single zirconia implant can range from $4,000 to $6,000, while full-mouth zirconia dental implants may cost between $25,000 and $50,000.

Zirconia vs Titanium Dental Implants

When comparing zirconia vs titanium dental implants, both have unique advantages:

Ultimately, your selection will depend on your particular needs and preferences. Our team will help you make a smart and informed decision.

  • Aesthetics: Zirconia is naturally white, making it superior for visible teeth.
  • Biocompatibility: Zirconia is metal-free, making it ideal for patients with metal sensitivities.
  • Durability: Both materials are highly durable, but zirconia resists corrosion and is less likely to cause inflammation.
  • Flexibility: Titanium implants are available in two-piece designs, while zirconia is traditionally limited to one-piece designs. However, advancements in zirconia technology have introduced two-piece options as well.

Zirconia Dental Implants Research

Extensive research supports the effectiveness of zirconia dental implants. Studies have demonstrated their excellent osseointegration, low risk of peri-implantitis, and high success rates comparable to titanium implants. Patients with zirconia implants report improved oral health and satisfaction with their natural-looking results.
