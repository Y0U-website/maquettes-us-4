Dental Bonding

If you’re looking for a non-invasive solution for minor cosmetic dental problems, dental bonding may be the treatment for you. This treatment involves the application of a tooth-colored composite resin material to repair cracked or chipped teeth or enhance the appearance of a tooth.

During the bonding process, our Northridge cosmetic dentist applies the resin material to the tooth, shapes it, and then bonds it securely using a special light. This procedure can effectively improve the color, shape, size, and overall aesthetics of a tooth.

Inlays and Onlays

Many patients looking to restore a single tooth can benefit from our durable inlays and onlays at Northridge Dentistry & Implants. These traditional restorations help your teeth stay sturdy following injury and decay while protecting them from further damage.

  • Inlays : Inlays help when the damage or decay is confined to the tooth center. These restorations fit precisely within the cusps (the raised points on the biting surface). Inlays use durable materials like porcelain, composite resin, or gold and are known for their excellent durability and aesthetic appeal.
  • Onlays : Onlays, also known as partial crowns, are used when the damage or decay extends beyond the tooth cusps. They’re designed to cover one or more cusps and damaged or decayed areas. Onlays provide a more extensive restoration compared to inlays and are a conservative alternative to full dental crowns.

Dental Bridges and Crowns

Restoring your smile is finally easy, thanks to dental bridges and dental crowns from our dentist near you. These custom porcelain restorations can replace missing or severely damaged teeth for a natural-looking smile.

Dental bridges use crowns attached to the surrounding natural teeth to support false teeth called pontics, which fill gaps in your smile. Dental crowns, on the other hand, are porcelain teeth ideal for completing several treatments, including dental implants and root canal therapy .

Caring For Your Cosmetic Dentistry Treatment Results

Caring for your cosmetic dentistry results is important to ensure the longevity and appearance of your new smile. Here are some tips for proper care and maintenance:

  • Oral Hygiene : Maintain excellent oral hygiene by brushing twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush and flossing once daily. This will help remove plaque and food debris, preventing staining and potential tooth decay or gum disease.
  • Avoid Staining Foods and Beverages : Certain foods and drinks like coffee, tea, red wine, and berries can stain or discolor your cosmetic dental work, such as veneers or tooth-colored fillings. Limit consumption of these items or rinse your mouth with water afterward.
  • Don’t Smoke : Smoking can cause discoloration and staining of cosmetic dental work, as well as increase your risk of gum disease and oral cancer.
  • Use a Straw : When drinking beverages that may stain, use a straw to minimize direct contact with your teeth.
  • Wear a Nightguard : If you grind or clench your teeth at night, wear a nightguard to protect your cosmetic dental work from excessive wear or damage.
  • Avoid Hard or Sticky Foods : Hard candies, ice cubes, or extremely sticky foods can chip or crack veneers, crowns, or bonding.
  • Regular Dental Visits : Visit Dr. Gropp regularly for professional dental cleanings and checkups. We can monitor the condition of your cosmetic dental work and recommend any necessary touch-ups or repairs.
  • Follow Specific Care Instructions : Our Northridge dentist may provide additional care instructions specific to the type of cosmetic dental work. Be sure to follow our recommendations for optimal results.
