Dental Services in Northridge

Keeping up with preventive care is genuinely one of the best things you can do for your long-term health — and your wallet. Catching a small cavity early costs a fraction of what a root canal or crown will run you later.

  • Oral Exams — A thorough check of your teeth, gums, and jaw to look for any early signs of decay, gum disease, or other concerns.
  • Digital X-Rays — Fast, low-radiation imaging that gives Dr. Gropp a complete picture of what’s happening beneath the surface — including cavities between teeth and changes in bone structure that aren’t visible to the naked eye.
  • Fluoride Treatments — A quick, painless application of fluoride that strengthens tooth enamel and helps protect against cavities — especially beneficial for kids and adults prone to decay.
  • Dental Sealants — A thin protective coating applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth, sealing out bacteria and food particles in the grooves where cavities most often form. A smart choice for children and adults alike.

General Dentistry

Dr. Gropp is a trusted family dentist who cares for patients from their first tooth through every stage of life. Whether you’re bringing in your kids, coming in yourself, or looking for a dentist your whole family can rely on, we make it easy.

  • Dental Fillings — When decay has created a cavity, a tooth-colored filling restores the tooth’s shape and function while blending naturally with your smile. We use composite resin that matches your tooth color — no metal, no silver.
  • Root Canal Therapy — When infection reaches the inner nerve of a tooth, a root canal removes the infection, relieves the pain, and saves the tooth. Despite its reputation, modern root canal treatment is typically no more uncomfortable than getting a filling.
  • Gum Disease Treatment — Gum disease affects nearly half of all adults and is one of the leading causes of tooth loss. We diagnose and treat all stages of gum disease — from early gingivitis to more advanced periodontitis — to help you keep your natural teeth as long as possible.
  • Emergency Dental Care — Dental emergencies don’t wait for business hours. If you’re dealing with a knocked-out tooth, severe toothache, broken crown, or dental abscess, call us right away. We do everything we can to see urgent cases quickly and get you out of pain.

Cosmetic Dentistry

If you’ve ever felt self-conscious about your teeth — whether it’s discoloration, chips, gaps, or shape — cosmetic dentistry can make a real difference. Dr. Gropp offers a range of personalized cosmetic treatments to help you feel confident every time you smile.

A cosmetic consultation is a great way to explore your options. Dr. Gropp will listen to your goals, evaluate your smile, and help you understand exactly what’s possible and what each option involves.

  • Teeth Whitening — Professional whitening delivers dramatically better results than anything you’ll find at the drugstore. In-office and take-home options are available, and results can lighten teeth by several shades in a single session.
  • Porcelain Veneers — Ultra-thin ceramic shells custom-made to fit over the front of your teeth. Veneers are one of the most transformative cosmetic treatments available, correcting color, shape, size, and minor alignment issues all at once. With proper care, they can last 10–15 years or more.
  • Tooth Bonding — A quick, affordable, and non-invasive option for repairing chips, cracks, gaps, and staining using a tooth-colored composite resin. Bonding usually takes just one appointment and requires no removal of natural tooth structure.
  • Dental Inlays & Onlays — Sometimes called “indirect fillings,” inlays and onlays are custom-crafted restorations used when a tooth has too much damage for a regular filling but doesn’t yet need a full crown. They offer excellent durability and a natural appearance.

Restorative Dentistry

Missing or severely damaged teeth affect more than just your appearance. They can make it hard to chew, cause remaining teeth to shift, and contribute to jawbone loss over time. Restorative dentistry brings your smile and your oral health back to where they should be.

  • Dental Crowns — A crown is a custom-fitted cap that fully covers a damaged, cracked, or weakened tooth — restoring its strength, shape, and appearance. Crowns are one of the most versatile and durable restorations in dentistry and can last 10–15 years with proper care.
  • Dental Bridges — A bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring a false tooth (or teeth) to the natural teeth on either side. It’s a fixed, non-removable solution that restores function and fills the gap in your smile.
  • Dentures — Full or partial dentures replace missing teeth and the surrounding tissue when multiple teeth are gone. Modern dentures are more comfortable and natural-looking than ever, and Dr. Gropp will make sure yours fits properly and feels good in your mouth.

Oral Surgery

Some dental conditions require surgical intervention to fully resolve. Dr. Gropp performs a comprehensive range of oral surgery procedures right here in his Northridge office — so you don’t have to be referred elsewhere for the care you need.

  • Dental Implants — The gold standard for replacing missing teeth. An implant is a titanium post placed directly in the jawbone, topped with a custom crown that looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth. Unlike bridges or dentures, implants don’t require modification of adjacent teeth and help preserve jawbone density over time. With proper care, they can last a lifetime.
  • Tooth Extractions — When a tooth is too damaged or decayed to save, removal is sometimes the best option. Dr. Gropp performs extractions with care and precision to minimize discomfort and set you up for a smooth recovery.
  • Wisdom Teeth Removal — Wisdom teeth that are impacted, overcrowded, or coming in at an angle can cause pain, infection, and damage to neighboring teeth. Removing them early — before problems develop — is often the smartest course of action.
  • Dental Bone Grafting — When tooth loss leads to bone loss in the jaw, a bone graft restores the volume and density needed to successfully place a dental implant. It’s a critical step for many implant patients, and Dr. Gropp performs this procedure with precision and care.
  • Soft Tissue Grafting — Used to treat receding gums, a soft tissue (gum) graft covers exposed tooth roots, reduces sensitivity, and helps protect against further recession and bone loss.
  • Sinus Augmentation — When the upper jaw lacks sufficient bone height for implant placement, a sinus augmentation (or sinus lift) creates the space needed by gently lifting the sinus membrane and adding bone graft material beneath it.
  • Dental Crown Lengthening — A surgical procedure that reshapes the gum and bone to expose more of the tooth’s surface — either to prepare a tooth for a crown that needs more structure to grip, or to improve the look of a “gummy” smile.

Advanced Dentistry

Some dental issues don’t get better on their own — they get worse every night while you sleep. Dr. Gropp offers advanced solutions to protect your teeth from the damage that happens when you’re not even aware of it.

  • Night Guards — If you grind or clench your teeth while you sleep (a condition called bruxism), you may be causing significant wear and damage without knowing it. A custom-fitted night guard creates a protective barrier between your upper and lower teeth, preventing wear, reducing jaw pain, and protecting your investment in your smile. Unlike over-the-counter options, a custom night guard fits precisely and is far more comfortable and effective.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I come in for a cleaning and checkup?

For most patients, twice a year is the standard recommendation — and for good reason. Every six months gives Dr. Gropp the chance to catch issues early and keep your teeth and gums in the best possible shape. Some patients with a history of gum disease or a higher risk of cavities may benefit from more frequent visits, which Dr. Gropp will discuss with you.

What should I do if I have a dental emergency?

Call us right away at (818) 709-8645. We make every effort to see emergency patients as quickly as possible. If you’ve knocked out a tooth, keep it moist (in milk or in your cheek), handle it by the crown (not the root), and get to an emergency dentist as fast as you can — timing matters for reimplantation.

Are dental implants right for me?

Dental implants are an excellent option for most adults in good general health, with adequate jawbone density, and who are missing one or more teeth. During your consultation, Dr. Gropp will evaluate your bone structure, gum health, and overall oral health to determine whether implants are the right fit — and walk you through every step of the process.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes. We work with a variety of insurance plans and will help you understand and maximize your benefits. For patients without insurance, we offer financing options to help make care affordable. Visit our Insurance and Finance page or call our office for details.

What’s the difference between a crown and a veneer?

A crown covers the entire tooth and is used to protect or restore a tooth that is damaged, cracked, or has had a root canal. A veneer covers only the front surface of a tooth and is primarily a cosmetic solution for improving the appearance of healthy teeth. Dr. Gropp will help you understand which option makes sense based on your specific situation.
