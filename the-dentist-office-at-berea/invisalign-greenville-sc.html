Benefits of Invisalign

Aligners are clear, making them much less noticeable than metal braces

The aligners are made from smooth plastic and are custom designed to fit your teeth.

You can remove your aligners when eating, brushing, or flossing, making it easier to maintain good oral hygiene.

Clear aligners typically require fewer adjustments and faster appointments than traditional braces.

Using advanced digital planning, we map out your treatment and show how your smile will improve over time

We evaluate your teeth, bite, and smile goals to determine whether clear aligners are the best treatment option

Advanced imaging technology allows us to create your custom treatment plan that gradually aligns your teeth.

Your invisalign aligners are custom fabricated to fit your teeth

You will wear each aligner for about 1 week before switching to the next set.

As your treatment progresses, your smile will gradually improve
