THE KEY TO DENTAL HEALTH

Fluoride is a natural mineral that helps prevent cavities and is especially helpful for patients who are prone to tooth decay. When cavities are caught early enough, fluoride treatments can even reverse decay and strengthen enamel. At Smilefolk, Dr. Lee uses a topical treatment that can be applied during regular 6-month checkups, or as necessary depending on the needs of the patient. Feel free to discuss your options with our team and get the care that’s right for you.
