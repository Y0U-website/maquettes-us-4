Some implants can be placed immediately; others should not be.

Whether an implant is placed at the time of extraction depends on infection, bone, soft tissue, anatomy and the ability to achieve appropriate stability. Treatment timing is individualized after clinical and radiographic evaluation.

Implants require ongoing professional and home care.

Dental implants cannot decay, but the tissues and bone around them can develop inflammatory disease. Long-term maintenance and hygiene are essential.

What patients ask about dental implants

Candidacy depends on general health, oral hygiene, bone and gum tissue, anatomy, smoking or other risk factors, and the restorative plan. A clinical examination and imaging are needed.

The timeline varies by extraction needs, grafting, implant stability, healing and restoration type. Some cases are completed in fewer stages while others require months of healing.

Implants are designed as long-term treatment, but no dental treatment is permanent. Implant components and surrounding tissues require lifelong maintenance.
