The practice

As a dental assistant, you will play the crucial role of providing excellent patient care and ensuring the efficient operation of dental practices.

As an Expanded-Functions Dental Assistant (EFDA) you are receiving additional training and are legally permitted to provide certain intraoral procedures beyond the task of the general chairside dental assistant.
