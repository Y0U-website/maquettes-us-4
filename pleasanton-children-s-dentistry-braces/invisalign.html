Invisalign Clear Aligners

How Does Invisalign Work?

The Invisalign journey begins with digital impressions of your child’s teeth, allowing us to create a precise treatment plan and design their custom clear aligners. Our experienced orthodontists will assess their bite and teeth to provide an accurate estimate of the treatment duration. Once the first set of aligners is ready, we’ll ensure they fit perfectly and make any necessary adjustments. Aligners should be switched out every two weeks, with periodic orthodontic visits to receive the next set of aligners and monitor the orthodontic progress.

What Are the Benefits of Invisalign?

One of the standout benefits of Invisalign is its discretion—the clear aligners are barely noticeable, allowing patients to straighten their smiles subtly. They are also comfortable enough to wear for extended periods and designed for an efficient treatment timeline, often allowing for straighter teeth in just 12 to 18 months. Since the aligners are completely removable, they are easy to maintain, and patients face no dietary or activity restrictions during treatment.

Who should get Invisalign?

Invisalign can be used to treat malocclusion in adolescents, teens, and adults. When it comes to orthodontic treatment for children, however, there are a few things to keep in mind. Patients who choose to use Invisalign must be responsible enough to keep track of their clear aligners and wear them often enough. Clear aligners also require patients to brush both their teeth and the aligners, which is an important extra step. Invisalign can treat many orthodontic issues, but some complex problems may need to be treated using traditional braces.
