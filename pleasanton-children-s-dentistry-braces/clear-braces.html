How Do Clear Braces Work?

Similar to traditional braces, clear braces involve:

  • Brackets that are transparent or tooth-colored.
  • Wires that are less visible than traditional braces.
  • Gradual adjustment to align teeth over time.

What Can You Expect During Treatment with Clear Braces?

During the treatment, patients can expect:

  • Regular check-ups for adjustments.
  • Guidance on caring for clear braces.
  • A comfortable experience with minimal impact on appearance.

Are Clear Braces Right for Your Child?

Clear braces are suitable for many, but not all, orthodontic cases.
