How Do Clear Aligners Work?

  • Using a series of custom-made, removable aligners.
  • Gradually moving teeth into the desired position.
  • Requiring regular updates to new aligner sets every few weeks.

What to Expect During Treatment?

  • Wear aligners for 20-22 hours a day for optimal results.
  • Visit our clinic periodically for progress checks.
  • Follow specific care and maintenance guidelines provided by our orthodontists.

Are Clear Aligners Suitable for Your Child?

Clear aligners are suitable for treating various orthodontic issues but may not be ideal for all cases. We recommend a consultation to determine if Invisalign ™ or SPARK™ is the right choice for your child’s orthodontic needs.
