Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What are the signs my child might need braces?

  • Crooked or crowded teeth
  • Early or late loss of baby teeth
  • Overbite or underbite
  • Thumb-sucking past age 5
  • Difficulty chewing or speaking

What’s the difference between braces and aligners for kids?

Braces are fixed appliances that use brackets and wires. Aligners are removable trays made of clear plastic. Both are effective, and we’ll help you choose the best one based on your child’s needs.

Do you offer orthodontics for children with special needs?

Yes. Our team is trained in special needs dentistry , and we provide a gentle, customized approach for all children — including those with sensory sensitivities or medical complexities.

Is orthodontic treatment painful?

Most kids experience mild soreness during initial adjustment phases. We use child-friendly techniques and provide tips to manage discomfort effectively.

How much do braces or aligners cost?

Costs vary based on treatment type and duration. We provide transparent pricing, accept most insurance, and offer payment plans to make orthodontics accessible for every family.
