Tiny Tooth blends modern pediatric dentistry with a warm, whole-child approach—supporting healthy smiles, confident kids, breathing, oral function and growth.

The practice

We keep visits human, playful and reassuring because a child who feels comfortable today is more likely to feel confident about dental care for years to come.

Tiny Tooth Pediatric Dentistry serves infants, children and teens in Milton and Cumming, Georgia. We provide comprehensive pediatric dental care in a modern, warm environment designed to help kids feel comfortable, confident and successful.

Along with routine pediatric dentistry, Dr. Monica has a special interest in airway health, tongue and lip ties, oral function, early growth guidance and the ways breathing, sleep, oral posture and facial development can connect with a child’s dental health.

We adapt the visit to the child in front of us—whether they’re ready to hop in the chair or want to meet the tools first.

Kid-friendly language and demonstrations make unfamiliar things feel manageable.

We notice courage and make room for personality, humor and connection.

Beyond teeth, we consider breathing, oral posture, tongue function, sleep-related signs and growth.

We explain before we do, welcome parent participation, and give children time to acclimate at their own pace.
