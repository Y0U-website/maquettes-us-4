Implant Restorations The best option to replace a missing tooth is a dental implant.

Implants are generally placed under the expertise of a specialist and can take several months to heal prior to the final crown or prosthesis being placed. Careful communication between Dr. Sharma and your specialist will be coordinated before the final restoration is placed to restore your missing teeth to their natural and functional state.
