The practice

Dr. Brett is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist who received his dental degree from the University of Nebraska College of Dentistry. Dr. Brett then completed a residency at the Oklahoma University (OU) Children’s Hospital in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. Dr. Brett is a member of the Nebraska Dental Association, the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the Nebraska Society of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Dental Association, and the Hispanic Dental Association. Dr. Brett is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry.

Dr. Brett currently serves as a University of Nebraska College of Dentistry adjunct faculty member. Dr. Brett serves on the Nebraska Dental Association board of trustees, is a board member for the Kearney Area Children’s Museum and is a member of the Health Service Advisory Committee.

Dr. Brett was honored to have served as an officer in the Army National Guard for eight years following dental school graduation. He is grateful to all the men and women who have served and those who currently serve in the military.
