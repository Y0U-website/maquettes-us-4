The practice

Scarsdale Dental Group is proud to offer the following dental services to patients in the Scarsdale, New York area:

Comprehensive Dentistry - Consider us your one-stop dental practice, capable of taking care of any dental need you may have from sinus lifts to diagnostic dentistry.

Cosmetic Dentistry - We offer a number of cosmetic dentistry services that can address any concerns that you have about the appearance of your teeth and smile.

Instant Teeth - Instant Teeth are an excellent full mouth tooth replacement option that won’t slide around like dentures can since they are placed into your mouth with implants.

TMD/TMJ Treatments - Our selection of TMJ and TMD treatments help patients overcome pain and reduce inflammation caused by these conditions.

Smile Makeovers - We utilize custom models of your ideal smile to plan specific treatments to give you a smile that is just as you want it.

Dental Veneers - Dental veneers are applied to the front of your teeth to help you create an evenly spaced and perfectly white smile.

Teeth Whitening - Our professional teeth whitening services can help you to have a vibrant, white smile in only a single visit.
