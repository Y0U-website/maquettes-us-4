The practice

Our practice is built on the promise to quality service, professionalism, and a high standard of safety. From your first steps into our office through your exam or procedure, we care deeply for our patients and what we do to help them maintain dental health for a lifetime.

She graduated from LSU in 1995 and then returned to her home state and attended West Virginia University School of Dentistry. She graduated in 1999 and moved to Lebanon Oregon.
