Fox Valley also known as the Fox River Valley spans across cities, counties and even state lines. Back in the day, the Tri-Cities area was as far west people could build out from Chicago before hitting the Fox River and it’s marshlands.

For that reason, the Tri-Cities became the stopping point for metropolitan expansion, but at the same time became the starting point for a “western” suburban culture in Chicagoland .

That culture flourished and is still flourishing to this day. The Tri-City area comprises the following three cities: Geneva , St. Charles , Batavia .

They are more or less the same city, just a large one, at that.
