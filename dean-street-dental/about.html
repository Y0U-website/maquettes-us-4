Who We Are

At Dean Street Dental, your comfort and confidence are just as important as your teeth. We’ve worked hard to build an exceptional team of dedicated, caring and experienced professionals committed to the dental health of you and your family. That’s why we take great care of both the smile and the person behind it.

Our mission is to provide the highest quality dentistry and patient care available. We offer a full range of treatment options that can be personalized to meet your unique needs and expectations.

Regular dental visits at Dean Street Dental are essential to make sure oral health problems — from tooth decay to oral cancer — are detected and treated in a timely manner. At our office, your oral health is our primary concern.

We want to make sure your teeth stay healthy, function well and look great! From regular cleanings and exams to advanced restorative treatment, all of your routine dental needs can be met right here.

We are a general family dentistry office , where the dentist is the primary care provider for all ages of patients and is the key person responsible for the prevention, diagnosis and treatment of a wide variety of conditions, disorders and diseases affecting the teeth, gums and maxillofacial (jaw and face) body parts.

Dean Street Dental primarily provides preventative care and restorative therapy, but we are capable of performing a wide variety of other dental procedures, including cosmetic treatments , like veneers, teeth whitening, Botox, sleep apnea and more ( view all dental treatments offered ).

Dean Street Dental serves happy patients from the following communities: St. Charles , Geneva , Batavia , Campton Hills , Elburn , South Elgin , Elgin , Wayne and more . We want your first visit to be more about getting to know you & your needs and less about in-and-out treatment plans. We look forward to serving you for the years to come!

Regular maintenance & oral health checkups help to prevent the development of serious health issues that may require more expensive & extensive treatments.

Family dentists are highly educated and trained in multiple facets of dentistry, providing a variety of different services for you and your family including, but not limited to:

Crowns and bridges, dentures, dental implants, gum disease (periodontal) treatment, root canal therapy, teeth cleanings, bonding, dental fillings, oral cancer screenings, orthodontic treatment and more ( Dr. Isbrandt has taken two more years of schooling atop of dental school so he could perform both dental procedures and orthodontic procedures ).

We also offer veneers, professional teeth cleanings, removable dentures, children’s dental sealants, teeth whitening, TMJ/TMD treatment, tooth extractions, CEREC ® single visit crowns and veneers, Invisalign ® clear aligners, emergency dental treatment , and more.
