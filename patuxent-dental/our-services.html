Restorative Dentistry for a Stronger Smile

We offer a variety of restorative services to repair and enhance your smile. Our state-of-the-art CEREC machines allow us to fabricate most crowns and bridges in just one visit, reducing your time in the chair. Our restorative treatments include:

  • Composite Fillings: Tooth-colored fillings for natural-looking repairs.
  • Crowns: Durable restorations to strengthen damaged teeth.
  • Bridges: Solutions for missing teeth and restoring function and appearance.
  • Dental Implants: Permanent replacements for missing teeth.
  • Dentures: Comfortable options for full or partial tooth loss.

Cosmetic Dentistry for a Confident Smile

Whether it’s a minor touch-up or a complete transformation, our cosmetic solutions will give you the smile of your dreams. Our cosmetic services are designed to help you achieve the look you’ve always wanted. From whitening to veneers, we offer:

  • Porcelain Veneers: Custom-designed to correct imperfections in your smile.
  • Teeth Whitening: Professional take-home kits for a brighter, whiter smile.

Specialized Care for Unique Needs

In addition to general and cosmetic care, we also provide specialized services to address more complex dental issues, including:

  • Sleep Apnea Treatments: Home sleep studies and oral appliances to improve breathing.
  • Oral Surgery: Performed in-office for select cases.
  • Root Canals: Expert care for saving severely damaged teeth.
  • Nightguards: Custom appliances to protect your teeth from grinding.
  • Nitrous Oxide: Safe, effective sedation for a more relaxed dental experience.

Important Information

  • One-Visit Services: While we strive to complete certain procedures in one visit, we cannot guarantee this. Some patients may need to return for follow-up visits, and certain crowns and bridges may need to be sent to a lab for fabrication.
  • Dental Implants: Implants are performed in our office only for candidates approved by the doctor. Other candidates will be referred to a specialist for treatment.
  • Oral Surgery and Root Canals: These procedures are conducted on a case-by-case basis in our office. Our doctors will assess each situation during the examination to determine if a referral to a specialist is necessary.
