Our Practice

Dr. Nathan Sprenger grew up in small-town Kansas, surrounded by wheat fields and wonderful people. He has been married for 22 years to his college sweetheart and they have been blessed with two beautiful children.

When not in the dental office, Dr. Sprenger is often found exploring the beauty of the Sonoron Desert via another of his life’s passions: hiking! The Sprengers have made Tucson their permanent home since 2011 and at this point they can’t imagine living anywhere else, truly appreciating all that this magnificent area offers.

Since completing dental school at the University of Colorado in 2010, Dr. Sprenger has spent over a decade treating kids exclusively. With a focus on compassionate care and conservative dentistry, Dr. Sprenger looks forward to keeping kids (and their parents) smiling for the rest of his career! Dr. Sprenger is a licensed general dentist in the state of Arizona.

Dr. Jinal Patel is a board-certified pediatric dentist who brings a gentle, personalized touch to every child’s dental visit. Originally from Alabama, Dr. Patel fell in love with the Southwest and has proudly called Arizona home for the past two years.

Dr. Patel’s favorite part of her job is helping children overcome their dental anxieties, transforming what could be a scary experience into a comforting, positive milestone. Outside the office, she loves exploring Tucson with her husband, diving into a good book, traveling, and making memories with her family and friends.

Dr. Andrew Stucki received his bachelor’s degree from the University of Louisville and completed dental school at the University of Kentucky in 2017. Following dental school, he completed two residencies: Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) at Idaho State University and pediatric training from NYU Langone Dental Medicine-Advanced Education in Pediatric Dentistry. He is a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry and also has experience working with children in private practice.

He learned to speak Spanish fluently while on a church mission in Mexico. He enjoys working with children and is excited to help them obtain optimal oral health.

Dr. Stucki was born and raised in southeast Idaho. He is married to his beautiful wife, Sarah, and they have four children. When not at the office, he enjoys spending time with family, watching sports and being outdoors.
