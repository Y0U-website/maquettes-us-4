The practice

Dr. Haroutioun Kotchinian was born in Yerevan, Armenia. He was raised in Massachusetts. He completed his undergraduate education at the prestigious Washington and Lee University where he received his B.S. in Biology. He returned to Boston and attended Tufts University School of Dental Medicine where he graduated at the top of his class and received his D.M.D.

Following his graduation from Dental School he further advanced his knowledge in General and Advanced Dentistry at the Newark Beth Israel Hospital, improving old skills and learning new skills while under the mentorship of some of the elite practicing dentists in New Jersey.

Dr. Haroutioun Kotchinian also completed the Boston maxi course in implantology and is now a Fellow in the AAID.

Dr. Kotchinian along with his wife Dr. Kokareva brings years of experience along with the newest in 3D digital technology to help treat any dental ailment.

Dr. Kotchinian’s commitment is to empower patients to take control of their oral health while removing boundaries such as fear and anxiety. He places you at the forefront and hopes to exceed your expectations.
