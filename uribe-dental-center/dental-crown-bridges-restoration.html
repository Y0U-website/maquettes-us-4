The Purpose of Dental Restoration Treatments

Teeth can get worn down and damaged due to dental decay, accidents, and regular daily activity. Restorative dentistry repairs the foundation of your smile and restores the health of your teeth and gums. If you need to get your oral health on the right path, Uribe Dental Center offers various restorative dental procedures tailored to fulfill your unique dental needs.

Crowns & Bridges

To craft a dental crown, Dr. Uribe uses porcelain material to cap or cover a structurally impaired tooth. The crown restores the damaged tooth’s original strength and appearance while preserving the flawed tooth’s structure.

Am I Missing a Tooth?

A crown is used to take its place and support a dental bridge that will close the gap. A permanent or removable dental bridge can be implanted to help correct the bite and prevent the remaining teeth from shifting. Dr. Uribe will ensure the bridge arch is customized seamlessly with your smile.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Have a small cavity or a slightly damaged tooth? In just one visit, Dr. Uribe will use a composite (a tooth-colored filling) to repair the tooth’s decay, chipping, or cracking. The composite is specially shaded to match the color of your teeth and flawlessly blends in with the healthy portion of your enamel. Due to its entirely metal-free structure, composite fillings have less sensitivity to temperature changes and expansion/ contraction than amalgam fillings. It is resulting in a healthy, comfortable, & beautiful smile.

Full & Partial Dentures

Do you have one or several missing teeth? Dr. Uribe can design a traditional or implant-supported denture that will restore your ability to speak, eat, and smile confidently again. Partial dentures can replace several missing teeth by fitting seamlessly in with the remaining teeth, while a complete denture replaces the entire row of missing teeth by creating a full or dual arch. The implant-retained denture is another option to help you achieve the most secure fit possible.
