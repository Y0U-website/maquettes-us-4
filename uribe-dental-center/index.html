The practice

Dr. Carlos E. Uribe DDS, MS has been serving Midland, MI and the Great Lakes Bay Region since 2004. Providing high quality service and exceptional comprehensive dental care for all ages.

At Uribe Dental Center we are equipped with state-of-the-art dental equipment and our team maintains up to date with the latest techniques in multiple areas of dentistry. We invite you to contact our office to schedule your initial consultation. We look forward to meeting you and your family.

We provide a variety of treatments to support your oral health and bring out the best in your smile:

Keep your teeth healthy and avoid issues with cleanings, exams, and personalized care programs.

Repair damaged or missing teeth with fillings, crowns, and bridges.

Transform your smile with whitening, veneers, and other aesthetic treatments.

Replace missing teeth permanently with advanced dental implants.

Relieve jaw pain and improve function with customized treatments.
