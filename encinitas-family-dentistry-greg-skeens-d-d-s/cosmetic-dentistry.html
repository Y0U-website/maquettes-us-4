Frequently Asked Questions – Cosmetic Dentistry in Encinitas

How long do cosmetic dental treatments take?

It depends on the treatment. Some, like teeth whitening or bonding, can be completed in a single visit, while others like ClearCorrect may take several months. We’ll provide a timeline during your consultation.

Are cosmetic procedures painful?

Most cosmetic treatments are minimally invasive and involve little to no discomfort. We always prioritize your comfort and can discuss sedation or numbing options if needed.

Does insurance cover cosmetic dentistry?

Cosmetic procedures are typically not covered by insurance, but we’ll help you understand your benefits and discuss flexible payment options to make treatment affordable.

How do I choose the right cosmetic dental treatment for me?

That’s what we’re here for! Dr. Skeens will evaluate your smile, discuss your goals, and recommend the best treatments based on your needs and preferences.

Is cosmetic dentistry permanent?

Some procedures like veneers and bonding may need maintenance over time, while aligners and whitening results can last with proper care. We’ll provide guidance on how to maintain your smile after treatment.
