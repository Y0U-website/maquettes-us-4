Bone Grafting in Encinitas – Preserving Your Jaw After Extraction

After a tooth is removed—especially a molar— bone loss in the jaw often follows. If you’re planning a future dental implant or want to preserve the natural contour of your smile, we may recommend bone grafting .

What is bone grafting?

Bone grafting is a procedure in which a special material is placed into the socket after extraction to encourage new bone growth and preserve your jawbone’s structure. Over time, your body replaces the graft material with its own natural bone.

We routinely perform bone grafting in Encinitas for patients who want to keep all their future dental options open.

  • Maintains bone volume for future dental implants
  • Preserves facial structure and appearance
  • Reduces the risk of bone shrinkage after extraction
  • Supports better healing outcomes
