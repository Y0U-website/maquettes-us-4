Preventative Dentistry Our arsenal against dental problems involve professional cleanings , sealants , sport guards, night guards, and patient education. These preventative services can prevent costly tooth/gum diseases or emergencies, ultimately increasing tooth longevity.

Sedation Dentistry

For patients who do not need a deep level of sedation, we are also a licensed provider of Nitrous Oxide. Whether you are getting your teeth cleaned or a root canal done, Nitrous Oxide can be a simple means to help you relax while your treatment is provided. Another method to help you relax involves taking certain medications in conjunction with your treatment. When taking these medications, it is necessary to have someone bring you safely to the office and take you home and monitor your recovery when the treatment is complete.

Talk with your dentist today about these forms of relaxation available for your comfort. Your dentist will be able to help you decide which method will be of greatest benefit to you. Some of our patients even receive deep sedation for the examination and x-rays in order to identify the problems in their mouth! If you know someone who would benefit from this form of treatment, someone who hasn’t had dental care for many years because of their fears or can’t have dentistry in the conventional manner, please tell them today of this service available to them.

Dental Emergencies Dental emergencies happen. Having a dentist available for prompt, comprehensive emergency dental care is vital to alleviate discomfort. Sudden toothaches, broken crowns/fillings, and traumatic injuries? Trust us to address any of your unexpected dental needs with compassion and expertise.

Dental Implants Dental implants are a revolutionary solution for missing teeth, providing stability and confidence to your smile. Made of titanium or zirconia, implants serve as bionic roots, supporting replacement teeth or dentures. They preserve jaw bone and restore natural functions like chewing and smiling. After healing, implants closely mimic natural teeth in appearance and function. Ask us today about the amazing benefits of dental implants for you or a loved one.

Teeth Whitening Get a whiter, brighter smile in just an hour with our in-office whitening . Our safe and effective power whitening technology removes yellowing and stains from aging, coffee, and tobacco use. Wilsonville Dental Group also offers state-of-the-art at-home bleaching kits for convenient whitening. Ask us today about achieving the smile you’ve always wanted!

Wisdom Teeth and Other Extractions Wisdom teeth , or third molars, often lack space to emerge fully, posing risks for infection. They can also harm neighboring healthy molars, causing decay or gum loss. Our comprehensive exams assess wisdom teeth and their impact on long-term jaw health. Most extractions are performed in-office with local anesthetics and optional sedation methods. Trust our experienced team for safe and efficient oral surgery needs.

Nitrous Oxide & Sedation Dentistry Wilsonville Dental Group offers sedation dentistry to ensure patient comfort during dental procedures. Nitrous oxide is available for relaxation during treatment, with no lingering sedation effects, allowing patients to drive home afterward. As an alternative, oral sedatives, such as halcion/triazolam are offered to help patients with anxiety or sensitive gag reflexes. Trust us to provide you with a stress-free dental experience.

General Anesthesia / Sleep Dentistry Wilsonville Dental Group partners with Serenity Sleep Dentistry to offer comprehensive dentistry under general anesthesia/sleep dentistry under the direction of an anesthesiologist. This allows for a safe, controlled environment for comfortable treatment. Sleep Dentistry is a great solution for patients with dental anxiety, special healthcare needs, undergoing lengthy complex procedures, and more. Trust us for a calm and comfortable dental experience.

Gum Surgery In cases with severe gum disease, surgery may be necessary to halt destructive processes. Cosmetic gum surgeries can also protect teeth from loss or sensitivities, and can help recover gums from gum recession. We assess gum health during exams and recommend surgeries as needed for long-term oral health. Trust Wilsonville Dental Group for comprehensive gum health care.

Veneers Veneers offer a durable and conservative solution to perfect your smile’s color, shape, and design. In just two visits, crowns/bridges can transform your smile, providing natural-looking results without dark lines. Trust Wilsonville Dental Group for comprehensive dental care.
