Wilsonville Dental Group is a compassionate general dental practice filled with people who listen and care. Whether it’s the friendly person on the phone taking your call, the hygienist cleaning your teeth, or the dentist providing your treatment, we are committed to ensuring you have the best possible experience, every time.

We are a one-stop shop for all your dental needs, providing you with access to a complete range of dental care. Whether it’s a simple filling or complete rejuvenation of your smile, it all can be completed in our convenient Wilsonville location. We have been providing the finest in family, cosmetic, and implant dentistry since 1986 and utilize the latest and most advanced equipment and techniques.

We are also one of the few practices south of Portland offering sleep dentistry (or IV sedation dentistry) with a board-certified Anesthesiologist in our adjacent Serenity suite. Sleeping through dental care and being completed in generally one visit is a lifesaver for so many of our wonderful patients! See our Serenity Sleep Dentistry website for more information.

Your convenience and comfort are foremost on our mind. We are conveniently located in the heart of Wilsonville, two minutes from I-5 and 20 minutes from downtown Portland. Multiple dentists and hygienists working in one office also allows for ease of scheduling, consistent emergency care, and the availability for same day appointments when you need our help.

We accept, bill, and work with nearly all dental insurance carriers. We are experts in helping you utilize the benefits available to you. If you have any questions about your dental plan, please call us!
