Benefits You’ll Experience After Deep Cleaning

Scaling and root planing result in dramatic improvements in gum health, hygiene, and comfort for your mouth.

Healthier gums and less bleeding or swelling Removing plaque and calculus reduces infection and promotes healing to the gums, so they return to a healthy level of inflammation.

Fresh breath Eliminating bacteria and buildup below the gumline naturally improves breath and keeps you feeling confident about your mouth.

Reduced risk of tooth loss and bone loss Treating gum disease early keeps teeth healthy and protects you from bone loss.

Better overall oral health Healthy gums reduce systemic inflammation and improve health in the rest of the body.

Long-term financial savings Stopping the progression of disease keeps you from needing more invasive treatment such as surgery or implants, saving you time, discomfort, and money.
