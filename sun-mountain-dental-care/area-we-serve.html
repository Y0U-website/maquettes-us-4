What Mat-Su Valley Patients Are Saying

Do I have to live in Wasilla to be your patient?

Not at all. The majority of our patients drive in from neighboring valley communities. We’ve designed our hours, scheduling, and emergency policies around regional patients who value quality care close to home.

How do I find driving directions for my community?

Each of our area pages includes plain-text driving directions specific to that community. Visit your nearest area page above, or call (907) 357-5757 for help.

Do you accept Alaska Medicaid and VA insurance?

Yes — we proudly accept both Alaska Medicaid and VA Insurance, plus all major dental PPOs. Insurance verification is handled before your visit.

Can patients from outside Wasilla get same-day emergency appointments?

Absolutely. Whether you’re calling from Big Lake, Houston, Palmer, or Willow, we hold daily emergency slots and prioritize patients in pain regardless of which valley community they’re driving in from.

Do you offer pediatric dentistry for valley families?

Yes. We see patients of every age, from a child’s first dental visit through adult and senior care. Many valley families schedule kids’ appointments back-to-back with their own.

What if my community isn’t listed above?

If you’re anywhere in the Mat-Su Borough — or even further out — give us a call. If we’re a fit, we’d love to welcome you. If we’re not the closest option, we’ll happily point you toward a trusted colleague.
