Typically, it consists of a titanium post that is placed into the jawbone surgically, and it serves as a stable base for a crown, bridge, or denture. Is a Dental Implant Right for You?

Dental implants are a great option for almost all healthy adults who are missing one or more teeth.

The best candidates for dental implants are those who practice excellent oral hygiene, have enough bone density in their jaw, and do not have any untreated gum disease. Usually, patients with some bone loss are also still candidates for dental implants with bone grafting or other preparatory procedures.
