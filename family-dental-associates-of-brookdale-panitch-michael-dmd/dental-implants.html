The Dental Implant Process

Every patient is unique, but the dental implant process typically includes the following steps:

Consultation and Treatment Planning

We begin with a thorough examination and digital imaging to assess your oral health and create a personalized treatment plan.

Implant Placement

The implant post is carefully placed into the jawbone. Over time, the bone naturally fuses with the implant, creating a secure foundation.

Healing and Integration

During the healing period, the implant integrates with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration. This creates the strength and stability needed for long-term success.

Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, a custom-crafted dental crown is attached to the implant, restoring your smile and bite.

Why Patients Choose Dental Implants

Unlike removable appliances, dental implants become a permanent part of your smile. They do not shift, slip, or require adhesives. Because they stimulate the jawbone, they also help preserve facial structure and prevent the bone loss that often occurs after tooth loss.

For many patients, dental implants provide the closest experience possible to having a natural tooth again.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and regular dental visits, dental implants can last for many years and often decades.

Is dental implant treatment painful?

Most patients report less discomfort than expected. Local anesthesia is used during treatment, and any post-procedure discomfort is typically manageable with recommended medications and home care instructions.

Can dental implants replace multiple missing teeth?

Yes. Dental implants can be used to replace a single tooth, multiple teeth, or even support full-arch restorations.

How do I care for dental implants?

Dental implants are cared for much like natural teeth through brushing, flossing, and routine professional cleanings.
