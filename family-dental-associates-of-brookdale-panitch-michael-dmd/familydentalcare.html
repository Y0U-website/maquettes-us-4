At Family Dental Associates of Brookdale, we believe great dentistry begins with trust, comfort, and connection. Led by Dr. Michael Panitch, DMD, a family dentist in Bloomfield, NJ, our team provides compassionate, patient-centered care for children, teens, adults, and seniors—all under one roof

Our practice is designed to feel like a true dental home: a place where your family feels welcomed, understood, and genuinely cared for at every stage of life. Whether it’s your toddler’s first visit or ongoing care for a long-time patient, we take the time to make every experience positive and stress-free.

What Family Dentistry Means to Us Family dentistry isn’t just about convenience—it’s about continuity. When your whole family receives care in the same practice, your provider truly gets to know you: your dental history, your concerns, and your long-term goals. That relationship allows us to give more personalized guidance and catch issues early, helping you build a foundation for strong lifelong oral health.

We take a gentle, communicative approach with children, a collaborative approach with adults, and a supportive, thoughtful approach with seniors. Every age has unique needs, and we tailor our care to meet them.

Your Family’s Comfort Matters From modern technology to a calm, welcoming environment, our goal is to make every appointment easy and comfortable. We explain treatment options clearly, accommodate nervous or first-time patients, and always encourage questions. You’ll never feel rushed or pressured—just supported.

Care That Grows with You As your family’s needs change, we’re here to grow with you. Whether you’re coming in for a routine checkup or navigating more complex treatment, you’ll always receive care grounded in trust, respect, and a genuine commitment to your well-being.
