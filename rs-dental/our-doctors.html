Dr. Yen Kim Vo

Associate Dentist | Family & General Dentistry Specialist

Dr. Yen Kim Vo brings a wealth of knowledge in family and general dentistry, with a focus on preventive care and patient education. She earned her dental degree with honors and has since dedicated her career to providing comprehensive dental care to patients of all ages.

Dr. Vo’s Philosophy:

Dr. Vo is known for her caring and approachable demeanor. She ensures every patient feels at ease during their visit, particularly those with dental anxiety. She is a firm believer in preventive care and takes the time to educate patients on maintaining excellent oral health between visits.
