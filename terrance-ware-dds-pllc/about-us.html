About Us

DR. TERRANCE WARE

Dr. Terrance Ware has extensive experience and expertise in the practice of dentistry for over a decade. A native of Indianola, Mississippi, Dr. Ware graduated from Tougaloo College with a B.S. in Chemistry. He later matriculated at The University of Tennessee College of Dentistry where he received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery. After dental school, Dr. Ware practiced in Memphis, TN, and later Grenada, MS. In 2011, he established Crystal Springs Dental Center in Crystal Springs, MS, that he maintained and grew until moving into his Jackson location.

Dr. Ware lives in Jackson with his wife and two children. When he isn’t practicing dentistry, Dr. Ware enjoys reading, vacationing, and spending time with his family.

Dr. Ware is passionate about providing the best quality care for his patients and he would love to meet you soon.
