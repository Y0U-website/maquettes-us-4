Our Practice

Compassionate Care. Exceptional Dentistry.

Our highly trained and compassionate team is committed to helping every patient feel informed, comfortable, and confident in their care.

Many members of our team have been part of the practice in one capacity or another since the 1990s. Together, we share the belief that clear communication paves the way to Exceptional Dentistry.

We believe informed patients are better equipped to make decisions in their own best interest. By clearly explaining treatment options and answering questions, we help patients feel supported throughout every stage of their dental care.

Care Built Around Comfort, Trust, and Communication

Clear Communication We explain treatment recommendations and options clearly so patients can make informed decisions with confidence.

Compassionate Care Every visit is approached with patience, understanding, and respect for each patient’s individual needs.

Trusted Experience Our longstanding team brings decades of shared experience and a consistent commitment to excellent patient care.

Technology Designed for Greater Comfort and Confidence

We take pride in providing the best care we can deliver in a comfortable and caring environment. To support that commitment, our practice incorporates modern dental technology that improves precision, comfort, education, and safety.

3D Digital X-Rays Advanced digital imaging helps reduce radiation exposure while providing detailed diagnostic information.

Electric Handpieces Quieter, smoother electric handpieces are designed to make treatment more comfortable for patients.

Educational Monitors Patient-facing monitors help us explain diagnoses, treatment recommendations, and dental health information more clearly.

Visible Sterilization Corridor Our state-of-the-art sterilization corridor is visibly accessible, helping demonstrate our commitment to the highest standards of infection control.

Bilingual Dental Care

Our team speaks both English and Spanish, allowing us to communicate comfortably with more patients and families throughout our community.

Meet Thalia

Helping patients feel calmer, more comfortable, and right at home.

For an added level of anxiety reduction and comfort, our team includes Thalia, a purebred South African Mastiff known as a Boerboel.

Thalia has helped ease the stress of adults and children alike. Her calm presence offers comfort to patients who may feel nervous or anxious about visiting the dentist.
