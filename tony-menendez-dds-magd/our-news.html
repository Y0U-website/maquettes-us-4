Dr. Menendez was recognized as a leader in the Florida Academy of General Dentistry, a lifelong learner, and a devoted dog enthusiast in the September edition of Florida Focus .

Completing the requirements for Mastership in the Academy of General Dentistry is an incredible accomplishment that requires two years of advanced study, participation, and dedication.

Dr. Menendez was proud to help fellow dentists strengthen their knowledge and clinical skills and thanked them for everything they taught him throughout the experience.

Congratulations, and thank you, my friends!

National Recognition for Service and Education

Dr. Menendez is one of only approximately 300 dentists in the United States and Canada to receive the Lifelong Learning and Service Recognition from the Academy of General Dentistry.

He is also one of only a handful of dentists to earn the LLSR award multiple times and was working toward his fifth recognition.

Working With Leaders in Implant Dentistry

Dr. Tony Menendez was honored to work with Dr. Will Martin, President of the United States section of the International Team for Implantology, and Dr. Luiz Gonzaga, co-director of the Implant Center at the University of Florida.

Dr. Menendez has had the privilege of working with these respected researchers and practitioners for more than 18 years through the Comprehensive Dental Program at the University of Florida.

Advanced Techniques and Dental Materials

Dr. Menendez worked with dental materials specialists Dr. Jim Haddix, Dr. Saulo Geraldo, and Dr. Jean-François Roulet at the University of Florida College of Dentistry.

Together, they demonstrated new techniques and procedures in restorative dentistry.

Current Concepts in Pharmacology

Dr. Menendez participated as a visiting faculty member during the pharmacology weekend of the University of Florida Comprehensive Dental Program with Dr. Richard Wynn.

The program covered current pharmacology concepts, including evolving guidance regarding antibiotic selection for patients with penicillin allergies and concerns related to the increased incidence of C. difficile .

Drs. Menendez, Wynn, and Haddix continued advanced dental education while observing social-distancing practices during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Supporting the Next Generation of Dentists

Dr. Menendez has long mentored dental students who demonstrate both promise and a strong desire to learn. With COVID-19 affecting many educational institutions, this commitment became more important than ever.

Kindra Dietrich, a second-year dental student from LECOM School of Dental Medicine in Sarasota, assisted Dr. Menendez with the placement of dental implants for a dental colleague following maxillary sinus augmentation.

Ms. Dietrich also learned about the importance of CBCT three-dimensional imaging when planning implant placement in the upper arch and evaluating sinus anatomy. Dr. Menendez thanked her for her enthusiasm and assistance with the case.

Named One of Port Charlotte’s Best Dentists

Advanced CBCT Training for Root Canal Care

Dr. Menendez participated as a visiting faculty member during the Florida AGD MasterTrack endodontics weekend.

The program focused on the growing importance of CBCT three-dimensional radiographs in diagnosing and treatment-planning endodontic procedures. In some cases, the additional imaging information resulted in changes to the recommended treatment approach.

Fourth Lifelong Learning and Service Recognition Award

Although the Academy of General Dentistry’s national meeting was canceled because of the COVID-19 pandemic, Dr. Menendez was awarded his fourth Lifelong Learning and Service Recognition Award.

The award represents a career of excellence in dentistry and recognizes AGD Masters who remain current in their profession while giving back to their communities and the dental profession.

At the time, fewer than 375 Academy members had received this recognition.
