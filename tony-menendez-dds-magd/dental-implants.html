Restore more than the appearance of your smile.

Dental implants are designed to replace missing tooth roots and support restorations that look, feel and function more like natural teeth. Depending on your needs, implants may replace one tooth, several teeth or help stabilize a removable denture.

Dr. Tony Menendez carefully evaluates your oral health, bone structure, bite and goals before recommending treatment. Every plan is personalized to create a result that supports comfort, function and long-term stability.

Stable support for eating, speaking and smiling.

When properly planned and maintained, implants can offer a strong, natural-feeling foundation for replacement teeth.

Improved Stability Implant-supported restorations are anchored in the jaw, helping reduce movement while eating and speaking.

Natural Appearance The visible restoration is customized to coordinate with your smile, facial features and surrounding natural teeth.

Preservation of Healthy Teeth A single-tooth implant can replace a missing tooth without relying on neighboring teeth for support.

Dental implant care in Port Charlotte.

Your treatment plan will depend on the number of missing teeth, available bone, oral health and the type of restoration you need.

Single-Tooth Dental Implants

A single implant can support an individual crown to replace one missing tooth. The restoration is designed to blend with the surrounding teeth while restoring function in the area.

Implant-Supported Bridges

When several adjacent teeth are missing, strategically placed implants may support a bridge. This can restore a larger section of the smile without requiring an implant for every missing tooth.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Dental implants may be used to help stabilize a removable denture, reducing movement and improving confidence during meals, conversation and daily activities.

Implant Restorations

The restoration is the visible portion attached to the implant. Dr. Menendez plans and restores implant crowns, bridges and other prosthetic options with attention to fit, bite and aesthetics.

Bone Grafting and Implant Preparation

Some patients need additional preparation before implant placement. Bone grafting or related procedures may be recommended when the available bone does not provide adequate support. Dr. Menendez will explain whether preparatory care is appropriate for your treatment plan.

What patients often ask about dental implants.

Candidacy depends on your oral health, available bone, medical history, medications, habits and treatment goals. A consultation and appropriate imaging are needed to determine whether implants are suitable for you.

The timeline varies. Some patients need preparatory treatment, and the implant usually requires a healing period before the final restoration is attached. Dr. Menendez will explain the anticipated stages and timing for your individual case.

Local anesthesia is used during implant placement, and comfort options may be discussed when appropriate. Some tenderness or swelling can occur afterward, but your care team will provide instructions for managing the healing period.

Implant longevity varies based on oral hygiene, gum health, smoking, medical factors, bite forces and regular professional care. The implant and attached restoration may also have different maintenance or replacement needs over time.

Implants require consistent brushing, cleaning around the restoration and regular dental visits. Although the implant cannot develop a cavity, the surrounding gum and bone tissues must remain healthy.

Coverage varies significantly between plans. Some policies may provide benefits toward portions of the procedure or restoration, while others may exclude implant treatment. Our team can help you review available benefit information.
