General & Preventive Dentistry Personalized care focused on prevention, comfort, and long-term oral health.

When several adjacent teeth are missing, strategically placed implants may support a bridge. This can restore a larger section of the smile without requiring an implant for every missing tooth.

Your treatment plan will depend on the number of missing teeth, available bone, oral health and the type of restoration you need.
