WHAT IT IS

Braces and aligners are tried-and-true ways to correct crowding, gaps, and bite problems by applying steady, gentle pressure to shift teeth into place over time. Braces are often the go-to option for kids who need more significant movement, while aligners can offer a more discreet path for cases that qualify. Many families searching for a pediatric dentist near me in Hamilton trust us to guide the right choice. For many families, both remain dependable routes to a lasting, healthy bite.

HOW IT WORKS

Treatment starts with getting a clear picture of your child&#x27;s teeth and jaw, so we can map out how their bite should ideally come together and determine whether braces or aligners are the better fit. Brackets are bonded onto the teeth and connected with a wire that&#x27;s adjusted at each visit, or aligner trays are swapped out in sequence, nudging things closer to alignment step by step. We keep families in Hamilton Square , Mercerville , Robbinsville , Lawrenceville , West Windsor , and East Windsor updated at every stage so there are no surprises.

HOW IT HELPS

Beyond a straighter smile, properly aligned teeth are simply easier to keep clean, which cuts down on the odds of cavities and gum trouble later. Bite correction can also ease jaw strain and uneven tooth wear that might otherwise build up over the years. And for a lot of kids, finishing treatment with a smile they&#x27;re proud to show off, whether with braces or aligners, makes the whole process worth it.
