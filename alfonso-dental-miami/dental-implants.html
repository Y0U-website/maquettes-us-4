Dental Implants

Dental implants

they are like artificial roots that we place in your mouth to anchor a new tooth in place. They are strong because they fuse with the bone, so it’s like having your natural teeth. At Alfonso Dental, we craft these implants so they feel and look like real teeth.

Unique Implants

They’re the perfect option for those who have lost a single tooth and are seeking a durable and aesthetically pleasing solution. In our procedure, we use a custom-made crown designed to seamlessly integrate with your natural teeth both in function and appearance. This process not only restores the fullness of your smile but also preserves the health of your adjacent teeth and the underlying bone, preventing bone loss and maintaining the structure of your face.

All in x:

It’s a groundbreaking solution for those who require extensive dental reconstruction. Depending on your specific needs, this procedure involves the placement of 4, 6, or 8 implants to support a fixed bridge. This technique offers a more stable and durable alternative compared to traditional dentures. Patients enjoy improved functionality and appearance, being able to chew and smile with complete confidence.

Over Dentures

They’re an excellent option for those who have lost most of their teeth and are seeking a more comfortable and secure alternative to conventional dentures. These removable implants are firmly attached to your jaw, providing a stable base that significantly improves chewing ability and speech. Designed to be easily removed for cleaning, Over Dentures combine the comfort of traditional dentures with the stability and functionality of implants.
