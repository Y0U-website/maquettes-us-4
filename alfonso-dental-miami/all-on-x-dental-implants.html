What Are All-on-X Dental Implants?

All-on-X dental implants are an innovative solution for patients who need a full arch of teeth replaced. At Alfonso Dental Miami, we specialize in this advanced procedure, which involves placing a series of implants that support an entire arch of prosthetic teeth. Located in the Flagler Street area of Miami, FL, our practice offers All-on-X dental implants in Flagami, FL as a more stable and permanent alternative to traditional dentures. This procedure is ideal for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth and are looking for a fixed solution that restores both function and aesthetics.
