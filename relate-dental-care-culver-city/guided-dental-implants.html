Guided Dental Implants in Culver City, CA

Guided Dental Implants combine advanced technology and minimally invasive techniques to create a predictable, natural-looking result.

Why Patients Choose Guided Dental Implants:

✅ Minimally Invasive: No scalpels, incisions, or sutures—just flapless implant placement that protects your gums and reduces discomfort.

✅ Faster Recovery: The gentle placement technique used in guided surgery means less trauma, minimal bleeding, and a quicker recovery.

✅ Greater Accuracy: Precise 3D digital imaging of the mouth ensures flawless implant placement for a perfect fit and long-term success.

✅ Fully Guided Surgery: A custom surgical guide and prosthetic design provide a secure, confident fit.

Whether you’re missing one tooth or several, guided implants offer a reliable, comfortable way to restore your smile.

The Benefits Of Guided Dental Implants

Personalized Smile Design Every case begins with your final smile in mind. We digitally map your bite and gum line to ensure your new teeth feel natural, look balanced, and fit your lifestyle.

Surgical Precision Your implants are placed using a fully customized, 3D-printed guide based on advanced digital scans. This reduces the chance of complications and improves long-term success.

Faster Healing, Less Discomfort Thanks to a minimally invasive, no-incision technique, most patients experience little to no post-op discomfort. You’ll be back to your daily routine quickly—often with minimal swelling and virtually no bleeding.

The Guided Dental Implants Treatment Process Digital Planning and Assessment Your treatment begins with a detailed digital assessment. Using advanced 3D imaging, we create a precise map of your teeth and jaw. This plan guides every step of your procedure, reducing the chance of complications and helping your implants fit naturally with your existing teeth.

Guided Implant Placement

Once your plan is complete, we place your implants using a custom surgical guide that ensures they go exactly where they should. This approach is quick, accurate, and gentle, so you spend less time in the chair and feel more comfortable during recovery.

Post-Procedure Care and Follow-Up

After placement, you’ll get clear instructions on caring for your implants, including oral hygiene and dietary tips. Follow-up visits help us monitor your healing and make any adjustments needed to keep your smile healthy. This step is vital to ensure the long-term success of your guided dental implants and your overall oral health.

Final Restoration and Crown Placement

After your implant has fully healed and bonded with the bone, we complete your smile with a custom-made zirconia crown. This strong, natural-looking restoration is designed to match the color and shape of your existing teeth for a seamless result. With proper care, your new tooth will feel secure and function just like a natural one.
