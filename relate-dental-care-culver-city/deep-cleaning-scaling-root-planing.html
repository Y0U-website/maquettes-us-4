The Benefits of a Deep Cleaning

Treats the Source, Not Just the Symptoms A routine cleaning maintains the surfaces of healthy teeth, but a deep cleaning treats an active bacterial infection beneath the gumline. By removing the deposits that sustain the infection and smoothing root surfaces, this therapy gives your gum tissue the clean environment it needs to heal and reattach.

Stops Progressive Bone and Tissue Damage Gum disease is progressive. The longer a bacterial infection persists beneath the gumline, the more it erodes the bone anchoring your teeth. Deep cleaning interrupts this process, which is far less invasive than addressing advanced bone or tooth loss later.

Reduces Bleeding, Sensitivity, and Inflammation Bleeding gums, persistent bad breath, and temperature sensitivity are signs of an active infection. By targeting the bacteria causing this inflammation, deep cleaning resolves these symptoms at their root cause rather than masking them.

Protects Your Investment in Your Smile Whether you have had cosmetic work, dental restorations, implants, or simply want to keep your natural teeth for life, healthy gums are the foundation everything else depends on. Gum therapy protects that foundation.

What to Expect From a Deep Cleaning Step 1 — A Thorough Evaluation Your visit begins with a careful examination of your gums and supporting bone. We measure the depth of the spaces (pockets) around each tooth, review X-rays for bone changes, and discuss your medical history. Our goal is to make sure you fully understand your diagnosis before any treatment begins.

Step 2 — Cleaning Below the Gumline

During treatment, we carefully remove plaque, tartar, and bacterial deposits from the tooth surfaces and root areas beneath the gums (scaling and root planing). Root planing smooths the root surfaces to discourage bacteria from reattaching. Local anesthesia is used as needed so you remain entirely comfortable throughout.

Step 3 — Adjunct Therapies (When Recommended)

In cases of deeper infection, Dr. Ramina may combine deep cleaning with advanced therapies to enhance your healing:

  • Laser Therapy: Uses focused light energy to target bacteria within deep pockets that mechanical instruments alone cannot reach, promoting faster tissue healing.
  • Antibacterial Therapy: Delivers targeted antimicrobial agents directly into the treated pockets to further reduce bacteria and support the recovery process.

Step 4 — Healing and Ongoing Maintenance

Some mild tenderness or sensitivity for a few days is normal as your gums heal. We will provide clear home care instructions and schedule follow-up visits to evaluate your recovery. Most patients are then placed on a periodontal maintenance schedule (typically every 3 to 4 months) to keep the infection from returning.
