Understanding the Augmentation Procedure

Getting a sinus lift in Lynnwood is a precise, highly routine oral surgical procedure completed right here in our modern, comfortable practice.

After ensuring the entire area is completely numb and relaxed under local anesthesia, Dr. Daining makes a tiny, precise incision in the gum tissue near your upper premolar or molar region to gently expose the underlying jawbone.

A small, specialized opening is carefully cut into the bone. Through this microscopic window, Dr. Daining utilizes delicate instruments to gently push the thin membrane lining your maxillary sinus upward, safely creating a new, elevated sinus floor.

The newly created space beneath the elevated membrane is carefully filled with premium bone grafting material. Once the material is packed flush to stimulate new bone growth, the tiny incision is closed with secure, comfortable sutures.

Frequently Asked Questions About Sinus Lifts

While the idea of a sinus lift sounds intense, the procedure itself is highly routine and very comfortable for the patient. We utilize advanced local anesthetics to completely numb the entire side of your face before we begin, ensuring you feel zero pain during the surgery. Afterward, most patients manage their recovery beautifully using standard over-the-counter anti-inflammatories or mild prescribed pain relievers for a few days, describing the sensation as a dull pressure similar to a mild sinus cold.

Healing timelines vary extensively based on your body’s natural regenerative speed and the type of grafting material selected. On average, it takes between 4 to 9 months for the bone graft to fully fuse, mature, and transform into a solid, live jawbone. Dr. Daining will track your healing progress using low-dose digital X-rays to ensure the site is structurally sound before surgically placing your permanent titanium implants.
