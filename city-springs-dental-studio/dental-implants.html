Am I a candidate for dental implants?

  • Loss of one or several teeth due to decay, injury, or periodontal disease.
  • Looking for a permanent and stable solution for missing teeth.
  • Sufficient jawbone density to support the implants, or willingness to undergo bone grafting if necessary.
  • Seeking a solution that prevents further bone loss and maintains facial structure.

What to Expect

1. An in-depth oral health assessment and discussion of the best dental implant options for you.

2. Surgical placement of the implant post, followed by a healing period for osseointegration.

3. Attachment of the custom-made crown or prosthetic teeth to the implant, ensuring a natural appearance and comfortable fit.

4. Ongoing support and regular check-ups to ensure your dental implants remain in perfect condition.
