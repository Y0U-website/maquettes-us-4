Meet Dr. Nadia Esfandiari Abbassi

Born in Tallahassee and raised in Sandy Springs, Dr. Nadia combines over 14 years of dental experience with a passion for art, design, and patient care. Her approach blends gentle dentistry with a deep understanding of esthetics, helping patients feel confident and supported on their journey to a beautiful, healthy smile. With advanced training in cosmetic dentistry — including a prestigious fellowship under Dr. Ronald Goldstein — Dr. Nadia brings a rare level of artistry, precision, and commitment to each treatment.

Good to know

  • Bachelor’s degree from Georgia State University
  • Doctor of Dental Medicine from the Dental College of Georgia
  • Completed an elite postdoctoral esthetic fellowship with Dr. Ronald Goldstein
  • Co-author in Esthetics in Dentistry (3rd Edition)
  • Featured on the cover of Dentistry Today and published in November 2017
  • Member of the American Dental Association, Georgia Dental Association, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, Academy of General Dentistry, American Academy of Facial Esthetics, Hinman Dental Society, and Dental XP
  • Named Top Beauty Dentist by New Beauty Magazine (2011–2019)
