Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

There are three factors we will consider when discussing your eligibility for implant dentures. These include:

You might feel as if there’s no way you’ll ever be eligible to receive this type of treatment; however, it is best to schedule a consultation and let our team evaluate you.

  • The amount of jawbone that is dense enough to support a fixed denture. If it’s less than what is desired, you may need a bone graft or to have a removable denture put into place.
  • Your oral health and whether you need preliminary treatments like periodontal therapy or even a tooth extraction to justify the reasons for implant dentures.
  • Your overall health and if you’re healthy enough to undergo minor oral surgery.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

It was unfathomable to think that dentures could be incorporated with any other unique tooth replacement solution, but it’s working quite well. When receiving your implant dentures, you can expect the following benefits:

  • Greater self-confidence in your appearance and ability to perform normal daily tasks
  • Improved functionality when eating and speaking by yourself or among others
  • A stronger and denser jawbone because of the continuous stimulation provided by your dental implants
  • A lowered risk for the development of systemic issues often caused by tooth loss (i.e., diabetes, heart disease, osteoporosis)
