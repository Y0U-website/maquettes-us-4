A Little About Dr. Dalia

Dr. Dalia grew up in California and relocated to Palm Beach Gardens following her husband’s career in medicine. She has spent the last seven years putting down roots here, building a practice and a life in the same community she serves. Outside the office, she is a mother of three. That combination shapes how she treats every patient who walks through the door.

Her office was built to feel different from the moment you arrive. The environment is calm and unhurried, the technology is current, and the care is genuinely concierge-level. For patients who have avoided the dentist out of anxiety or past experiences, that distinction matters.
