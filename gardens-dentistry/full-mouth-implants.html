Who Is a Candidate for Full Mouth Dental Implants?

Full mouth dental implants are an excellent option for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth, or whose remaining teeth are failing due to decay, fracture, or long-term neglect. Patients who have worn traditional dentures for years and are struggling with discomfort, poor fit, or bone loss are often ideal candidates.

A critical factor in candidacy is adequate bone volume in the jaw. Because implants rely on the bone for support, patients with significant bone loss may first require a preparatory procedure. At Gardens Dentistry, we offer bone grafting in Palm Beach Gardens to help rebuild bone structure and create the ideal foundation for implant placement. This step, while it adds time to the overall process, dramatically improves implant success rates and long-term stability.

Systemic health also plays a role in candidacy. Patients with well-managed conditions such as diabetes can often undergo implant treatment successfully, though a thorough evaluation is always performed. Non-smokers and patients committed to good oral hygiene maintenance tend to see the best long-term outcomes. Research published in the International Journal of

Oral and Maxillofacial Implants have documented high long-term survival rates for full-arch implant restorations, with patient-reported quality of life improvements being among the most consistent findings in the literature.

What to Expect From Your New Smile

Full mouth implants change more than your teeth; they change the way you live. Because implants preserve jawbone stimulation the way natural tooth roots do, they help prevent the facial bone loss that often gives long-term denture wearers a sunken appearance. You can eat a full, varied diet without restrictions. You can speak clearly without worrying about slippage. And you can smile freely.
