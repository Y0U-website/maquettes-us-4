The Process

1. Implant Placement A titanium post is placed using a guide to make sure it is in the ideal position and angle in the jawbone under local anesthesia. Most patients describe the procedure as far more comfortable than they expected.

2. Osseointegration (healing period) Over 2 5 months, the implant fuses with your bone in a process called osseointegration. This is what gives implants their permanence and strength. It is set like solid concrete during this process.

3. Crown Placement Once healing is complete, a custom crown using a 3D printer can create precise temporary restorations designed to fit comfortably on your tooth during the treatment process. Once your final crown is placed, the result looks, feels, and functions just like a natural tooth.

FULL ARCH SOLUTIONS

For a Complete Look

Missing most or all of your teeth? There’s a better way than conventional dentures.

Traditional dentures rest on the gums and rely on suction and adhesive to stay in place. They shift when you eat. They limit what you can chew. They can make speaking feel uncertain. And over time, they accelerate the bone loss they were meant to compensate for.

A complete arch of beautiful, fixed teeth supported by just four to six strategically placed implants per arch. These restorations are permanently secured; they’re not removable.

You eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence. There’s no adhesive, no palate coverage, no slipping.

For patients who prefer a removable option, implant-supported dentures snap securely onto implants for a level of stability that conventional dentures simply cannot provide. They can be removed for cleaning but stay firmly in place throughout your day.

Benefits of a full arch implant

  • Eat what you love steak, apples, corn on the cob
  • Speak clearly without worrying about movement
  • Smile with genuine confidence
  • Preserve your facial structure and prevent bone loss
  • Feel and look dramatically more natural than conventional dentures
