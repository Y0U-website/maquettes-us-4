Dental implants are the closest thing modern dentistry has to a natural tooth. They’re anchored in your jawbone, capped with a custom crown, and designed to look, feel, and function exactly like the teeth they replace. Including brushing and flossing them similarly to a natural tooth. They don’t shift. They don’t need adhesive. They don’t come out at night.
