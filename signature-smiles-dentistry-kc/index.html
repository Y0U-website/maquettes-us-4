Les soins

A single implant is a self-contained solution. Unlike a bridge, it doesn’t require crowning the adjacent healthy teeth to serve as anchors. The implant stands completely on its own a titanium post in your jawbone, topped with a custom ceramic crown.

Comprehensive Care, One Roof From extraction (if needed) to implant placement to final crown delivery, we handle every step of your implant journey in our practice. No referrals to unfamiliar providers. Continuity of care, start to finish.

Dr. Deacy uses advanced 3D imaging technology and precision digital planning to ensure that every implant is placed exactly where it needs to be for optimal support, ideal aesthetics, and long-term stability.
