About Us

A Modern Practice Built on Personal Connection

Comfort focused care for the whole family.

Signature Smiles was created with a simple belief: dental care should be different. We’ve built a practice where state-of-the-art technology meets old-fashioned hospitality.

From our intentionally designed space to our carefully selected team, every detail makes your visit comfortable, efficient, and enjoyable. Here, you’re not a number you are part of our dental family.

We are one team, united by integrity, kindness, and excellence-where everyone belongs, communication is clear, gossip doesn’t exist, and we support each other without hesitation.

We share ownership of our patients’ experience and advocate relentlessly for their health, delivering care with compassion, accountability, and pride.

Our Vision To be the dental practice people trust for outstanding care, comfort, and connection. We deliver the highest standard of dentistry with integrity, compassion, and excellence-creating an experience that feels personal, welcoming, and reassuring. Our goal is for every patient to feel seen, heard, and genuinely cared for, and to become the practice others confidently refer for the best all-around dental experience.

Our Culture We embrace transparency, persistent learning, and treating every patient the way we’d want our own families treated.

No rushed appointments. No surprise bills. Just honest, thorough care.

Investing in the Community We Serve

At Signature Smiles Dentistry KC, caring for our community goes beyond the walls of our office. We’re proud to support the Kansas City community through local partnerships, oral health education, and service initiatives that help make a lasting impact.

We partner with the Youth Volunteer Corps and donate $5 from every new patient visit to support their mission of empowering young people through volunteer service.

As active members of the Greater Kansas City Dental Society and the American Dental Association , we are committed to advancing high-quality dental care and improving access to healthier smiles throughout our community.
