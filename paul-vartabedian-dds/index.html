The practice

At the office of Dr. Paul Vartabedian, we’re committed to delivering high-quality, personalized dental care designed to keep your smile healthy, functional, and beautiful. As a prosthodontist with advanced training, Dr. Vartabedian offers a full range of services—from restorative and cosmetic dentistry to tooth replacement and TMJ therapy.

We believe every patient deserves to feel heard, respected, and comfortable. That’s why we take time to explain your treatment options clearly and answer any questions, so you can make informed decisions with confidence. You’re not just a patient here — you’re a partner in your dental health.

Every smile is unique, and so is our approach to care. Dr. Vartabedian and his team provide one-on-one attention, tailoring every treatment to fit your individual needs, goals, and lifestyle.

We’d love to welcome you to our practice. Contact us today to schedule a visit — we’re excited to help you care for your smile.

Mission Statement To truly listen to each patient’s needs and deliver care with technical excellence, genuine compassion, and a commitment to comfort at every step.

Treatment Philosophy The preservation of that which remains, and the meticulous replacement of that which has been lost.
