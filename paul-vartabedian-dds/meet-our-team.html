Meet the Doc

Serving Lake County since 1994, Dr. Paul Vartabedian is a skilled and compassionate dentist specializing in prosthodontics. With decades of experience, he’s committed to helping patients achieve strong, healthy, and confident smiles.

B.S. in Chemical Engineering, University of Michigan, 1986 DDS, University of Michigan, 1992 AEGD, University of California San Francisco, 1992 Prosthodontic Residency, Department of Veterans Affairs, 1994

Dr. Vartabedian stays at the forefront of modern dentistry through ongoing bi-monthly continuing education courses, ensuring patients receive the highest standard of care.

American Dental Association California Dental Association Redwood Empire Dental Association American College of Prosthodontists

Outside the office, Dr. Vartabedian enjoys spending time with his wife and three children. His hobbies include flying RC planes, restoring classic cars, cycling, and skiing.
