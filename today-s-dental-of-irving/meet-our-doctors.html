Meet Our Doctors Today

Dr. Bui was born and raised right here in Fort Worth, TX. He went to high school down the road at Fossil Ridge High School and then attended The University of Texas at Austin. After, he received his dental education at the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry and decided to moved back to his hometown in Fort Worth.

With the utmost care and attention to detail, Dr. Bui believes in treating every patient as he would a family member. Dr. Bui stays up to date in today’s ever changing world in dentistry taking continuing education in Invisalign, restoring implants, and occlusion.

He is the youngest of nine siblings. He enjoys cooking(and eating!), playing the guitar, attending sports and music shows, reading, and watching TV and movies. He is also a Game of Thrones fanatic, so he won’t stop talking about it if you bring it up, you have been warned!

Dr. Doan Bui

If you’re looking for a dental experience that feels more like a visit with a friend, look no further than Dr. Danny Nguyen . Known for his gentle touch and friendly demeanor, Dr. Nguyen is a "homegrown" professional dedicated to serving his community.

Dr. Nguyen’s ties to the area run deep. Born and raised right here in Grand Prairie, Texas , he is a proud alumnus of South Grand Prairie High School and UT Arlington. His journey to dentistry took an interesting turn when he decided to pivot from his MBA program at UTA to follow a calling in healthcare, eventually earning his degree from the LSU School of Dentistry .

Dr. Nguyen combines his local pride with a genuine passion for patient comfort, making him a true staple of the neighborhood
