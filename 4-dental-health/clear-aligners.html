How the Clear Aligners Process Works

  • Personalized consultation: Your goals, bite, and dental health are reviewed.
  • Digital planning and fabrication: Aligners are designed to follow a precise sequence of movements.
  • Sequential wear: Wear each set as directed and switch on schedule.
  • Periodic check-ins: Progress is monitored and refinements are planned as needed.

Benefits of Clear Aligners

  • Discreet appearance that blends into your smile.
  • Removable design for easy eating and oral hygiene.
  • Smooth comfort without metal brackets or wires.
  • Customized planning that supports efficient, reliable progress.
  • Care in one location with general dentistry support for overall health.
