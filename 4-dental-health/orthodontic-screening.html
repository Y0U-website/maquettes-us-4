Orthodontic Screening

  • Guided jaw growth can improve bite balance and long-term joint comfort.
  • Better alignment supports oral hygiene by making brushing and flossing easier.
  • Timely intervention can correct habits or airway-related concerns that affect growth.
  • Clear timelines help families plan for braces, aligners, or interceptive care.

How Orthodontic Screening Works

  • Health history and goals to understand symptoms, habits, and priorities.
  • Clinical exam to check bite, crowding, spacing, and jaw function.
  • Photos or digital scans to document tooth position and smile symmetry.
  • X-rays when indicated to assess roots, unerupted teeth, and jaw growth.
  • Discussion of findings, likely timelines, and next steps tailored to age and needs.

What to Expect at Your Visit

Most orthodontic screenings take about 30-60 minutes. You can expect a comfortable exam, simple photos or scans, and a clear explanation of what was found. If treatment is not recommended yet, a recall schedule will be set. If treatment is appropriate now, options and estimated length of care will be discussed.

Signs It May Be Time to Schedule

  • Early or late loss of baby teeth or adult teeth erupting out of place.
  • Crowding, spacing, or teeth that do not meet evenly when biting.
  • Mouth breathing, thumb or finger habits, or jaw clicking and discomfort.
  • Difficulty chewing, speech changes, or a smile that feels misaligned.
  • Interest in adult orthodontic consultation to improve function or comfort.

Did You Know?

Early orthodontic screening by age seven can identify developing issues while the jaw is still growing, often simplifying future treatment.
