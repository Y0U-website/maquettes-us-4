The practice

Our highly-experienced staff and the latest dental technologies provide our patients a broad range of services which promote dental health.

ProDental’s smile makeover combines advanced cosmetic treatments to enhance the appearance, balance, and health of your smile. Using services like veneers, whitening, and crowns, our team designs personalized treatment plans that improve confidence and function, helping patients achieve a brighter, more natural-looking smile they’re proud to share

Our reconstructive dentistry restores the health, function, and beauty of damaged or missing teeth. Our skilled team combines advanced techniques with personalized care to rebuild smiles using crowns, bridges, implants, and more. We focus on long-lasting results that improve comfort, confidence, and overall oral health for every patient.

Relaxation and sedation dentistry help patients feel calm and comfortable during every visit. Our caring team offers safe sedation options to reduce anxiety, ease discomfort, and create a stress-free dental experience. We focus on gentle care so patients can receive the treatment they need with confidence and peace of mind.

Dr. Kowalesik is originally from Oak Lawn, IL. She received a Bachelor of Science degree from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign in Molecular and Cellular Biology, with a minor in Chemistry. While in college, she worked as a dental assistant for a non-profit dental outreach program, SmileHealthy, and she was the first student representative chosen to serve on their Board of Directors.

She is currently working toward Fellowship in both the Academy of General Dentistry and the Academy of Implant Dentistry. Dr. Kowalesik takes pride in giving her patients the most comprehensive and caring treatment possible. She loves being a dentist and making her patients smile!
