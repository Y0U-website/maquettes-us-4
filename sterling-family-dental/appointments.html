What’s Next?

Our staff will call you to confirm your appointment. In the meantime, please review our Patient Safety Guidelines below that outline simple steps for you to take prior to and during your appointment.

1. Please fill out, sign, and return the required forms. Please send the signed form to info@sterlingfamilysmiles.com

2. If you have a fever or temperature of 100 degrees or higher, please do not come to the office and contact your medical doctor. We will reschedule you at a later date.

3. Brushing stations are closed. Please be sure to brush your teeth prior to coming to the office.

4. Please arrive at the office 10 minutes prior to your appointment and remain in your car. Call us at 703-430-2400 to let us know you have arrived and staff will let you know when we’re ready to see you.

5. To reduce person-to-person contact, we ask that only the person who has the appointment come into the office.

6. Please wear a mask or face covering to your appointment.

7. Once at the office, we will perform a wellness check-in; your temperature will be taken. If you have a fever of 100.0 or above we will have to reschedule your appointment.

8. The reception area is currently closed.
