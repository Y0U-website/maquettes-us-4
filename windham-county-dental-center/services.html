Emergency Care

In some cases, your first visit with us may be due to an acute problem, such as injury, bleeding, or severe pain. The good news is that we will do our best to get you in quickly for a shorter exam to address your immediate problem. After your dental emergency is addressed, we will find a convenient time for a full evaluation and comprehensive dental exam.
