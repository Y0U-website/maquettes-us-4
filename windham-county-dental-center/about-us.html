Leadership

Karen is a Certified Dental Assistant with over 20 years of experience in the dental field, now serving as a dedicated and detail-oriented Practice Manager. Her career began chairside, where she developed a deep understanding of clinical procedures, patient care, and the inner workings of a successful dental practice.

Throughout her two decades in dentistry, Karen has built a reputation for being reliable, compassionate, and highly organized. Today, as a Practice Manager, she oversees daily operations, staff coordination, scheduling, insurance processing, and patient relations — all while ensuring the practice runs smoothly and efficiently. Karen is known for her calm presence, strong work ethic, and genuine commitment to both patients and team members. She brings a unique blend of clinical experience and administrative expertise to her role, making her a valued leader in any dental office.

Outside of work, Karen enjoys traveling, gardening, and spending quality time with her family. Whether she’s tending to her plants, exploring new destinations, or relaxing with loved ones, she finds joy in life’s simple, meaningful moments.

Dr. Henriquez was born and raised in New York City and comes from a family deeply rooted in healthcare. Inspired by his family’s dedication to serving the community, Dr. Henriquez chose dentistry as his way to make a difference in people’s lives.

He earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 2013 and continued his training with an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine. He also completed a Dental Public Health Residency at Temple University Dental School in Philadelphia. During his time in Philadelphia, he helped create school-based dental clinics for the city’s public schools, expanding access to dental care for children and families.

As Dental Director, Dr. Henriquez practices all aspects of general dentistry with a special focus on patient comfort, education, and preventative care. He believes dentistry is a unique blend of science, artistry, and trust — and he strives to bring these together to help every patient achieve a healthy, confident smile.

Outside the Center, you’ll likely find Dr. Henriquez on the basketball court or unwinding with jazz music. He enjoys playing the alto saxophone, piano, and guitar.

Dental Staff

Heather brings over two decades of hands-on experience and heartfelt dedication to the world of dentistry. A 2005 graduate of the prestigious Forsyth School of Dental Hygiene, Heather’s passion for oral health started early—she’s been working in dental offices since the age of 14!

Her career has taken her from the bustling dental practices of the Boston area to the serene settings of Vermont and New Hampshire, and even abroad as a dental consultant in Liberia, Costa Rica. One of her most rewarding experiences was volunteering on a dental mission in the remote mountains near Cusco, Peru—bringing smiles and care to those who need it most.

Heather is especially skilled at working with patients who have dental anxiety or fear, and she finds great joy in building trust and comfort with children and nervous adults alike. Her gentle approach and calming presence make her a favorite among patients of all ages.

Outside the office, Heather wears many hats. She’s a devoted mom to her pride and joy, Kai, a certified yoga instructor hosting retreats and classes, and an adventurer at heart. She loves traveling to tropical beaches, hiking, and winding down with her family around a cozy bonfire. And if you’re wondering—yes, her favorite color really is Bubbles !
