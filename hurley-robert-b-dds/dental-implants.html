  • How are dental implants different than dentures? Dentures are removable sets or partial sets of teeth that often feel bulky, can be difficult to get used to, and require special care. Dental implants are a permanent replacement for missing teeth that can be cared for in the same manner and look just like natural teeth.
  • What are the benefits of dental implants? Dental implants are identical to and feel just like natural teeth. They are also permanent and allow you to continue eating the foods you love without any fear of embarrassment.
  • How much do implants cost? The price of dental implants varies based on your current oral health, how many implants you need, your insurance coverage, and more. Schedule a consultation with us to determine a price.
  • Do dental implants hurt? No. You will receive a local anesthetic before the procedure to ensure you do not feel any pain. Address any fear or anxiety you have with your dentist and discuss possible sedation options.
  • How long do implants last? Your implants could last a lifetime, if cared for properly. Regular dental visits and a proper oral hygiene routine are vital to the success of your implant.
