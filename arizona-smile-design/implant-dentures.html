How Implant Dentures Work

Implant dentures combine the stability of dental implants with the full-arch coverage of traditional dentures. Several implant posts are placed into the jawbone, and once they fuse with the bone through osseointegration, a custom-crafted denture is securely attached. This eliminates the slipping and shifting commonly associated with conventional dentures.
