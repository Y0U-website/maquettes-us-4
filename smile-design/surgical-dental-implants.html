What Does Getting Dental Implants Involve?

The process of getting implants requires a number of visits over several months.

X-rays and impressions (molds) are taken of the jaw and teeth to determine bone, gum tissue, and spacing available for an implant. While the area is numb, the implant will be surgically placed into the bone and allowed to heal and integrate itself into the bone for up to six months. Depending on the type of implant, a second surgery may be required to place the post that will hold the artificial tooth in place. With other implants, the post and anchor are already attached and placed simultaneously.

Dental Implant Aftercare

Following a healing period of several weeks, these custom-made artificial teeth are carefully crafted and securely fastened to the implant’s post.

You will receive care instructions when your treatment is completed. Good oral hygiene, eating habits, and regular dental visits will aid in the life of your new implant.
