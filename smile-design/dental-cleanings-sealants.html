Scaling & Root Planing

After your oral evaluation or check-up, your dentist may recommend a procedure called scaling and root planing in one or more quadrants or quarters of your mouth to address an issue with gum disease they have observed. Periodontal disease, or gum disease, is relatively common in adults and is caused by bacteria collected under the gum line, causing irritation and inflammation. Gingivitis is the first stage of gum disease. If left untreated, gingivitis can progress to periodontitis, which can cause bone loss and even tooth loss.

Dental Sealants Protect Your Teeth from Tooth Decay

For good oral health, brushing and flossing are excellent habits to keep. However, reaching every nook and cranny of your teeth is difficult. The back teeth (the molars) can be tough to reach. Because they have a rough, uneven surface, leftover food, and plaque can easily collect on them and cause cavities. Dental sealants are a great solution to protect these vulnerable teeth and keep them clean. These create a clear, plastic varnish over the teeth, literally sealing them off.
