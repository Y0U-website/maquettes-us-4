What is the Best Way to Find a Dentist Who Does Dental Implants?

There are a few different factors that you’ll need to consider before you choose a dentist for dental implants in Lawrenceville. First, you should decide whether you want a dentist who works with local specialists or one who can handle the entire process (including the surgical placement of the implant post) in-office. On top of that, to ensure the best results for your smile, it’s worth searching for a dental professional who has extensive training and experience in dental implants specifically.

Here at Transforming Smiles, we’re proud to offer our patients the convenience of start-to-finish dental implant treatments. As such, you can count on us to help you enjoy a complete smile again with expertly placed implant posts.

How Can I Make a Same-Day Appointment with a Dentist?

You should call our office right away if you have any sort of dental issue that you believe requires immediate attention. After learning more about your situation, we can determine whether a same-day appointment is necessary and schedule your visit. The main focus of a same-day appointment is usually to figure out what the source of the problem is so that we can create a treatment plan. Also, if you’re experiencing any immediate pain, we can take steps to relieve it.

How Much Does It Cost to Get a Tooth Pulled Without Insurance?

Our team will only recommend a tooth extraction if we determine that there’s no other viable option for getting your oral health back on track. Naturally, we’ll make sure that you have a reliable estimate of how much you can expect the procedure to cost; to create said estimate, we’ll need to consider the complexity of the procedure, the number of teeth involved, and other factors. It’s worth noting that if you need to have a wisdom tooth removed, it will likely cost more than a regular tooth extraction.

What Do You Do If You Can’t Afford a Dentist?

If you’re worried about the cost of your dental care, you can talk to our team to discuss your payment options. Rest assured that we care about making our services as affordable as possible; we’re proud to work with a variety of major PPO plans, and we accept third-party financing through CareCredit. Remember, paying for dental care now can lower your risk of developing advanced oral health issues that could require more expensive treatments.
