Orthodontics

At Perfect Smile Doc, we offer a range of orthodontic treatments to help you achieve the straight, beautiful smile you deserve. Our skilled orthodontists tailor care to your unique needs, from clear aligners to traditional braces.

Our Orthodontic Servies

Braces and Clear Aligners

We provide both traditional metal braces and clear aligner options. Braces use a system of brackets and wires to gradually shift your teeth into proper alignment. Clear custom-fit aligners offer a more discreet approach without wires or brackets. We’ll help you determine the best option!

Invisalign ®

Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable aligners to incrementally move your teeth. Aligners are swapped out every 1-2 weeks until treatment is complete. Invisalign is more comfortable, convenient, and discreet than traditional braces – an excellent option for busy teens and adults.

Orthodontics for Kids

It’s common for children to get braces between ages 8 and 14 while their jaws are still growing. Early orthodontic treatment can prevent more extensive work later and improve self-esteem. We create fun, comfortable braces experiences for kids.

Retainers

Retainers are a key part of orthodontic treatment. They hold your teeth in their newly aligned positions while the bone and gums stabilize. Custom-fit retainers prevent your smile from shifting back after braces or clear aligners. Consistent wear is important for retention.
