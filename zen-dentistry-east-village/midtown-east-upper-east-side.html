Dr. Samuel Kim, DDS Dr. Kim grew up on the north shore of Long Island. He received top honors from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine in Philadelphia, PA and served as chief resident during his final year of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery residency at Montefiore Medical Center/Albert Einstein College of Medicine.

Dr. Kim decided on dentistry and oral surgery as a career because it allowed him to work with his hands while treating the medical needs of others. He loves the opportunity to make tangible improvements in his patients’ lives, whether it entails getting his patients out of pain, restoring function or helping reconstruct their smiles. He wishes to ease the anxiety that may accompany dentistry and make every experience as enjoyable and seamless as possible.

Dr. Kim prioritizes the mission of providing the best care for patients of various communities. He enjoys helping patients achieve their dental goals whether it involves extractions, bone grafting or implants.

Dr. Kim loves spending time outdoors fishing, hiking and playing basketball. He also enjoys cooking and reading in his free time.

Dr. Kim’s commitment to excellence and detail allows him to provide the highest quality of care and compassion to his patients.

Dr. Daniel Kim, DDS Dr. Daniel Kim grew up in Long Island, NY and has been a New Yorker all his life. Attaining a love for dentistry through his father who is a general dentist, Dr. Kim continued his education at Columbia University College of Dental Medicine and was awarded the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO) Award for Exceptional Interest in the Development of the Oro-Facial Complex.

During his specialty training in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics, he also served as part of the team at the Center for Skeletal Dysplasias at the Hospital for Special Surgery (HSS) and received the Dr. Thomas Cangialosi Award in recognition of clinical excellence. Dr. Kim currently teaches as a part-time clinical faculty at Columbia University Department of Orthodontics.

Dr. Kim offers the latest advancements in orthodontic treatment. He loves what he does for a living and strives to not only deliver the highest level of care but also to build unique and impressionable relations with all of his patients.

Dr. Michelle (Chu qiao) Liang, DMD Dr. Liang is a dedicated and detail-oriented dentist who emphasizes prevention and conservative treatment to help patients maintain lasting oral health. She is dual board-certified to practice dentistry in both Canada and the United States.

Originally from Montreal, Canada, she developed an early interest in healthcare and went on to study Kinesiology at McGill University. She later earned her Doctorate in Dental Medicine from the University of Montreal.

Throughout her studies, Dr. Liang was consistently recognized for academic excellence. She received the Faculty of Dentistry’s Academic Excellence Award, the Pierre Fauchard Academy Award of Excellence in Memory of Dr. Barry Dolman Fund, and was named to the Dean’s Honor List. As part of her training, she also completed a semester abroad at the Université catholique de Louvain in Brussels, where she gained hands-on experience in managing complex dental emergencies alongside experienced European practitioners.

After graduation, she pursued an intensive general practice residency at Interfaith Medical Center in Brooklyn, where she enhanced her skills in treating medically complex patients and managing oral and maxillofacial trauma.

She is currently a member of the American Dental Association (ADA), the New York State Dental Association (NYSDA), and the Second District Dental Society (SDDS).

Dr. Liang approaches dentistry with a strong sense of responsibility and compassion. She is committed to providing individualized, thoughtful care and values clear, open communication with her patients. She is also fluent in English, French, and Mandarin.
