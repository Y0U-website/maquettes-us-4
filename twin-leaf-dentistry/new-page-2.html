Our Team

Fay - office manager

Fay is originally from sunny Florida but is adamant that she will not be moving back! She started her dental administrative career in 2019 and quickly found her niche with small, tight-knit practices. Getting to know patients and building strong relationships with her team is the best part of her job. When Fay’s not at work, you’ll probably find her reading a good book, playing board games on a rainy day, or cozying up with her fur babies for a streaming marathon.

cameron - patient coordinator

Cameron is a proud new mom to her daughter, Emersyn, who already keeps her on her toes! One of their favorite things to do is sit outside with their dogs, Chino and Peppa. She also loves to venture out and visit her husband at the barbershop where he works and stroll through downtown Apex. When she is not venturing out, Cameron is probably sneaking in some reading on her Kindle (which is a diaper bag essential)! She loves making connections with people and enjoys the sense of community found in the Apex/Holly Springs area!

Taylor - Hygienist

Taylor was born and raised in North Carolina. She graduated from the Dental Hygiene program at Wayne Community College in 2024. She takes pride in providing exceptional care to patients of all ages in the fun and friendly Twin Leaf Dentistry environment. Meeting new patients and building relationships with everyone who comes through our door is one of the things Taylor loves about her job. When she’s not at work, Taylor enjoys going to the beach with her husband, gardening and playing outside with her chocolate lab.

malerie - dental assistant

Sydney - dental assistant

Sydney is a hard-working dental assistant with a passion for patient care. She is dedicated to creating a comfortable and welcoming environment for our patients and helping them feel at ease during every visit. She takes pride in supporting her team in delivering the high-quality, efficient dental care Twin Leaf Dentistry is known for. Outside of work, Sydney enjoys traveling and spending time with her family and friends. She and her boyfriend have 2 dogs, Duncan and Dolly, and a cat named Delilah.

penny - office dog

Penny is the best girl! She comes to the office with Dr. Brace and hangs out behind the check-in/out counter. She enjoys booty scratches and greeting every patient with a friendly tail wag! Don’t be alarmed if you hear her barking during your appointment, that’s just Penny’s way of letting us know UPS or FedEx is outside.
