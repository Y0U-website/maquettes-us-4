Orthodontics For Teens

For many families, the teen years are an ideal time for orthodontic treatment. Most permanent teeth are in place, growth is still active, and results can be highly efficient. At Premier Orthodontics, Dr. Ryan Lubinsky, Dr. Troy Christensen, and Dr. Andrew Triggs create teen treatment plans that fit real life school, sports, activities, and everything in between.

Beyond appearance, teen orthodontics supports healthier bites, easier brushing, and stronger long-term oral health.

Why Treatment Matters

Straight teeth are about more than aesthetics. Proper alignment reduces uneven enamel wear, supports healthy jaw function, and lowers the risk of future dental complications.

Orthodontic treatment can also boost confidence during a stage of life where self-image plays a major role. When teens feel good about their smile, it shows in everyday interactions from photos to presentations to social settings.

Treatment Options Designed For Teens

Every teen’s needs are different, so flexibility matters. At our five Valley locations, teens may be candidates for:

Dr. Lubinsky, Dr. Christensen, and Dr. Triggs help determine the best fit based on bite concerns, goals, and responsibility level.

  • Metal braces for comprehensive correction and precise bite control
  • Clear braces for a lower-profile option with similar mechanics
  • Invisalign for teens who want removable, discreet treatment

Keeping Treatment On Track

Teen treatment works best with consistency. That means keeping appointments, protecting braces, and wearing aligners as directed.

We partner with families to set clear expectations and provide practical guidance so treatment stays smooth, efficient, and predictable. If your teen plays sports or instruments, we’ll also review ways to stay protected and comfortable throughout treatment.

Orthodontics For Adults

At Premier Orthodontics, Dr. Ryan Lubinsky, Dr. Troy Christensen, and Dr. Andrew Triggs provide adult treatment options designed to be effective, flexible, and minimally disruptive to your daily routine.

Whether your teeth have shifted over time or you never had braces as a teen, modern orthodontics makes lasting change more accessible than ever.

Why Adult Treatment Makes Sense

For adults, orthodontics often begins with function. Teeth that drift can create crowding, spacing, bite strain, and difficulty keeping teeth clean especially near the gumline.

Correcting alignment improves brushing access, reduces uneven enamel wear, and helps relieve pressure on the jaw joints. Straightening your smile isn’t just cosmetic it supports long-term dental health.

Discreet Options For Busy Lives

Many adults prefer treatment that stays subtle. Depending on your goals and bite needs, we may recommend:

The best choice depends on your bite, the movements required, and your long-term goals.

  • Invisalign for removable, low-profile treatment
  • Clear braces for a discreet fixed option
  • Metal braces when maximum control and efficiency are required

Treatment Built For Long-Term Stability

Adult orthodontic planning takes existing dental work, gum health, and bite function into account. That’s why comprehensive evaluation matters.

Our goal is a stable, functional result that holds up long after appliances come off. Retention planning is built in from the beginning, so your results stay protected.

Why Choose an Orthodontist?

Orthodontic treatment changes how your teeth fit together and how your bite functions. That requires detailed planning and expertise not just moving teeth, but protecting the health of your gums, jaw joints, and long-term stability.

As trained orthodontic specialists, Dr. Lubinsky, Dr. Christensen, and Dr. Triggs diagnose the underlying causes of misalignment and design treatment plans that balance aesthetics with function.
