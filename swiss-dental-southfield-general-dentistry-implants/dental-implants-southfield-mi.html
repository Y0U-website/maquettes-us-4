Dental Implant FAQs

Most patients report less discomfort than expected — the procedure is done under local anesthesia (sedation is available), and any post-procedure soreness is typically managed with over-the-counter pain relief for a few days.

A single implant typically takes 3–6 months from placement to final crown, allowing time for osseointegration (the implant fusing with your jawbone). Some patients qualify for same-day options — we’ll confirm what’s right for you at your consultation.

Cost depends on how many teeth you’re replacing and your treatment plan. We offer financing through CareCredit and in-house payment plans, and we’ll help you check your insurance benefits.

With proper oral hygiene and regular checkups, implants can last 20+ years — often a lifetime. The crown on top may need replacement after 10–15 years of normal wear.
