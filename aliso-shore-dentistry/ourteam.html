Dr. Daniel Giap

Medical care runs in the family of Dr. Daniel Giap. Growing up in a household with a prominent doctor, he was inspired to pursue a lifetime of providing healthcare for his community. He is an Orange County native who grew up very close to here in Anaheim Hills.

Dr. Giap is a dental graduate with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Southern California and he earned his Bachelor of Science degree from the University of California, Irvine.

Now that Dr. Giap has joined ASD he is excited to further his education under Dr. Yang and explore everything that Orthotropics has to offer for both children and adults alike.

In his spare time Dr. Giap likes to wind down and relax on a nice long hike or just spend quality time with his family.
