Signs You May Need General Dentistry Services

Recognizing when it’s time to visit the dentist is crucial for maintaining optimal oral health. At Community Dental of Pemberton, we’re here to address your concerns and provide timely care:

  • Persistent tooth pain or sensitivity
  • Bleeding or swollen gums
  • Visible cavities or damaged teeth
  • Difficulty chewing or speaking
  • Bad breath that won’t go away
  • Been over six months since your last check-up
