Meet The Team

Choosing a dentist in Westwood, MA , means selecting highly skilled professionals who provide advanced cosmetic, restorative, and dental implant services all in one place. Offering a full suite of services from comprehensive family dental care to specialized dental rehabilitation.

Dr. Epstein was born and raised in Newton and is a graduate of Newton North High School. He received his Bachelor of Arts in economics with a concentration in Pre-medical Sciences from Tufts University.

Dr. Epstein is an active member of the American Dental Association, the Massachusetts Dental Society, the Metropolitan District Dental Society, and the American Academy of General Dentistry. Outside of dentistry, Dr. Epstein enjoys playing basketball, skiing, sailing, cooking, and traveling with his wife and two young boys.
