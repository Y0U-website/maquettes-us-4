Snap-In Implant Dentures

Snap-In Dentures (also called Click-In Dentures or Overdentures) are a cost-effective alternative to traditional dentures that uses strategically placed implants for added retention. They are removable for easy cleaning, but offer superior stability compared to conventional dentures.

Implants can eliminate the palate portion of upper dentures — reducing gagging and restoring your ability to taste food fully. Snap-In Dentures can also stabilize a loose lower denture, increase your comfort and confidence, and greatly improve your quality of life.

Single & Multi-Tooth Implants

Single Tooth Implants

For patients missing a single tooth, a dental implant is the most natural-feeling replacement available. A titanium post is surgically placed in the jawbone and topped with a custom crown crafted to match your existing teeth in size, shape, and color.

Unlike a traditional bridge, a single tooth implant does not require altering the adjacent healthy teeth. It functions, looks, and feels exactly like a natural tooth — and with proper care, it can last a lifetime.

Multi-Tooth Implant Bridges

For patients missing multiple consecutive teeth, an implant-supported bridge is a permanent, fixed solution that replaces an entire section of missing teeth using just two implant posts — no need to involve adjacent healthy teeth.

Implant bridges can be brushed and flossed just like natural teeth and are built to last 30+ years with proper care. They preserve the jawbone, prevent shifting of adjacent teeth, and deliver a result that feels completely natural.
