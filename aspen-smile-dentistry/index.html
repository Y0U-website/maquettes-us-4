At Aspen Smile Dentistry, we are dedicated to helping you achieve the best smile possible. Many patients that visit the dental office are interested in our orthodontic dentistry options. We offer two orthodontic options: Invisalign and traditional braces. On our website, we have pages that explain each treatment option.

Your smile is one of the first things people notice when meeting you for the first time, so you should feel confident flashing it in public. If you find yourself shying away from photos or covering your mouth when you laugh, don’t lose hope.

If you want to feel better about the appearance of your smile, many options can help you. You can perform remedies at home, such as teeth whitening kits or improving your oral health regimen. But for those teeth that need a little more work, orthodontics may be the answer.

At Aspen Smile Dentistry, we’re proud to offer both Invisalign and traditional braces. Both are effective options with some differences that might suit your needs depending on your dental goals.

Many patients love Invisalign. Invisalign is great for patients who want to align their smiles discreetly. Traditional braces are great for many patients, especially those with a more complex treatment who enjoy the classic look of braces. Call our office today, and we can schedule you a consultation to see which orthodontic treatment is best for you.
