Dr. Alex Lee was born and raised in Buenos Aires, Argentina before moving to San Francisco, California to pursue his undergraduate education. He earned a Bachelor of Science in Molecular Biology from the University of San Francisco where he developed a strong foundation in the biological sciences and a passion for healthcare.

He went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Medicine (D.M.D.) degree from Roseman University of Health Sciences in Utah. Following dental school, Dr. Lee practiced general dentistry in Los Angeles, California, where he developed a strong interest in oral and maxillofacial surgery and the management of complex surgical patients.
