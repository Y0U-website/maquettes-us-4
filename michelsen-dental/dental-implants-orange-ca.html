What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium rod that’s used to replace the roots of a missing tooth. One or more dental implants can be inserted into the jawbone where missing teeth once were, where they will fuse with the natural structure. Once fully healed in place, dental implants can be topped with a dental crown, bridge, or denture to replace any number of missing teeth. Rebuilding lost teeth from the roots up like this provides a solution that looks, feels, and functions just like the real thing!

What’s more, immediate dental implants represent a revolution in dental implant technology. In the past, after an extraction, you would have waited at least six weeks before we could place your implant. Now, in some cases, we can perform the extraction and fit you with a dental implant at the same appointment.

Receiving an immediate implant provides you with a host of benefits. The first benefit is convenience. You won’t have to have multiple appointments, and you won’t have to wait for your implant. You’ll also have a shorter recovery time after your surgery. Finally, the process allows us to more easily preserve your natural gum tissue.

The Dental Implant Process

Dental implants replace the entirety of the missing teeth, including the roots. To achieve this, they require a multi-step process that takes several months. Then you will be able to experience all of the amazing benefits that dental implants have to offer. While each patient’s dental implant experience is slightly different, the entire process can be broken down into four steps. Here’s what you can expect.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

The first step in getting dental implants is the initial consultation. This is when we examine your smile and determine whether or not you are a good candidate for this tooth replacement option. Some patients require preparatory work before they are able to get dental implants, like gum disease treatment or bone grafting. If these apply to you, we can help you get them scheduled. Once your jaw and gums are strong and healthy, we can move forward to planning the rest of your treatment. This includes discussing the cost and timeframe.

Dental Implant Surgery

The next step is your dental implant placement surgery. This portion of the procedure is completed by our in-house dental surgeon. First, your mouth will be numbed with a local anesthetic, so you don’t feel a thing. We also have dental sedation options to help patients feel extra comfortable during their procedure. Next, an incision is made in the gum tissue and the dental implants are inserted into the jawbone. Lastly, the gums are closed, and protective caps are placed over the end of the dental implants to keep them safe as the area heals.

Dental Implant Osseointegration Abutment

After your dental implants are placed, they can take up to six months to fuse with the surrounding bone tissue. This process is known as “osseointegration,” which allows dental implants to be so stable and long-lasting. Once healed, metal abutments are attached to the dental implants, and impressions are taken of the area.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restorations

Your restorations are customized for you at the dental lab. Once they are ready, you can return to the dental office where we will affix them to your dental implants, completing your smile. If everything looks and feels as it should, you will be free to leave the practice and show off your pearly whites to the world!

Benefits of Dental Implants

Day-to-Day Benefits

  • Simplified Maintenance : Instead of needing to remove prosthetics or use special cleaning tools or products, you’ll just have to take care of your dental implants like your natural teeth. Simply brush, floss, and rinse with antibacterial mouthwash like usual, and you can maintain them for many years to come.
  • Improved Chewing Ability : Other traditional options like dentures might require you to watch out for certain foods that could accidentally make them shift out of place. With dental implants, however, you can expect to regain 80 % or more of your natural chewing ability, meaning you can enjoy almost all of your favorite meals without much worry.
  • Increased Confidence : Dental implants are designed to work and look the same as your natural teeth. Not only does this mean you’ll be able to appreciate a restored bite, but you can also feel much more confident when showing off your new pearly whites.

Health Benefits

  • Jawbone Preservation : One of the most important things dental implants do for your oral health is to help retain your youthful face structure and jawbone. After tooth loss, the bone tissue in the jaw can begin to deteriorate, causing your jawbone to shrink over time. By embedding titanium posts that will serve as your new tooth roots, you can end up preventing this from happening while promoting stronger bone tissue in the long run.
  • Prevent Dental Shifting : Once the jawbone begins to deteriorate after tooth loss, any remaining pearly whites could be in danger of moving out of place to fill the gap in your smile, which can lead to further tooth loss. Dental implants can serve as placeholders and keep dental shifting from happening.
  • Better Overall Health : Since dental implants serve as your new natural teeth and they’re easier to maintain than other treatment options, you can expect to enjoy much better oral health with a reduced risk of developing issues like gum disease, decay, and infection.

Long-Term Benefits

  • Long-Lasting Treatment : Not only will dental implants come with a high success rate of 95 % or higher, but your posts can last several decades to even the rest of your life so long as you take care of them. This is much longer than other treatment options like dental bridges and dentures, which typically need to be replaced after 10 years or so.
  • Cost-Effective Method : Dental implants tend to come with a higher upfront value compared to other options. However, they won’t require you to buy special tools or products to maintain them. Additionally, since you won’t have to replace them as often, you can end up saving more money in the long run.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

The best way to know if dental implants are right for you is to visit our office for a FREE dental implant consultation. Our dentists will carefully examine your smile, the number of teeth you’re missing, and the condition of your jawbone. This appointment also catches any preexisting oral health issues like gum disease, which must be treated before getting dental implants.

Good candidates for implants are usually those missing at least one tooth who have a strong enough jawbone for surgery. That being said, we also have several options for patients who are lacking the necessary jawbone density. Procedures like bone grafts and All-On-4 implants can allow even those with weaker jawbones to enjoy implants, which is why the initial consultation is so important.

Missing 1 Tooth

If you are missing a single tooth, we can likely replace it with a single dental implant and a custom-made dental crown. The dental implant will be inserted into the jawbone where the missing tooth once was. Once it has healed, we’ll place a dental crown atop it to complete your smile. This restoration is precisely crafted and color-matched in order to blend in seamlessly with the rest of your natural teeth.

Missing Multiple Teeth

Dental implants can also replace several missing teeth, whether they’re right next to each other or spread around the arch. Instead of using a traditional dental bridge or partial denture, which rely on healthy tooth and gum structures and can cause discomfort, we can support these classic restorations with dental implants.

Missing All Teeth

We also provide dental implants for patients needing dentures. Implants get rid of any shifting or sliding you might experience with traditional dentures. Several rods may be used to hold the dentures in place, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile without worrying about a denture that won’t stay in place.

One style of implant-supported denture is the All-on-4 structure. This is simply a method of placing four implant rods around the mouth in strategic locations. These will anchor the dentures effectively while keeping the number of implants to a minimum.

What to Do Directly After Dental Implant Surgery

While recovering, you must ensure the blood clot on your surgery site remains completely untouched. That way, the gums can heal properly around the implant. Make sure you avoid:

  • Spitting (use tissues or swallow your saliva)
  • Drinking with straws
  • Smoking
  • Touching the surgical site with your fingers or tongue

Common Side-Effects When Recovering from Dental Implant Placement

If you’ve never had oral surgery before, you may not know which symptoms are normal and which ones you should be concerned about. Generally, any initial discomfort you experience should gradually improve over time. If it ever seems to be getting worse, don’t hesitate to give our office a call.

Here are some side effects you can expect in the first few days following your procedure:

  • Intermittent Bleeding Although this can last for multiple days, you can alleviate it by applying a slight amount of pressure on the gauze that’s on the surgical site.
  • Swelling It can last over a week, but you can make it go down quicker by using a cold compress on your cheek or jaw.
  • General Discomfort You can easily manage this by taking over the counter or prescribed pain medication.

Your Diet After Dental Implant Surgery

It’s strongly recommended that you stick to a soft-food diet for the first few days following your oral surgery. Before your procedure, you should stock up on foods like:

Although you can return to your usual diet as soon as you feel up for it, you should avoid chewing directly on the implant site.

  • Mashed potatoes
  • Yogurt

Post-Op Health Oral Hygiene

If you want your implants to successfully fuse with the surrounding bone tissue, you must keep your mouth as clean as possible after your surgery. It’s okay to brush your teeth as you normally would. However, make sure you’re gentle and careful near the surgical site. Don’t forget to rinse your mouth with salt water two to three times a day! Avoid using any mouthwash that has high levels of alcohol in it, such as Scope or Listerine.

Maintaining Caring for Your Dental Implants

Your new dental implants may be able to keep your smile beautiful, strong, and healthy for a lifetime, but you’ll have to take excellent care of them if you want them to last that long. Fortunately, keeping your dental implants clean and functional is quite simple since they only require the same oral health measures as real teeth. Here’s a brief guide to how you can keep your implants in great shape, but don’t be afraid to contact our office in Orange if you’d like to know more.

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

While dental implants are completely immune to cavities, it’s still crucial to practice excellent oral hygiene after receiving them. Gum disease is one of the leading causes of implant failure, so be sure to brush and floss daily and use antibacterial mouthwash regularly.

Eat a Healthy Diet

While dental implants give you the chewing power to eat whatever you like, it’s important to make smart eating decisions to ensure that they stay healthy. Be sure to enjoy plenty of foods with important nutrients like calcium, vitamin C, and protein to keep your gums and jawbones strong. Items that are hard, sugary, or sticky can be harsh on your dental implants, so enjoy these sparingly.

Break Bad Habits

The habits you maintain will make all the difference for the success of your dental implants. Tobacco use is completely terrible for your oral health and leads to gum disease that can cause early implant failure, so quitting a smoking or chewing habit is an excellent idea. It’s also crucial to refrain from using your teeth to open packages as well as chewing on nonfood items like ice, toothpicks, and writing utensils.

Protect Your Dental Implants

While dental implants are incredibly strong and durable, they are not invincible. Always wear an athletic mouthguard when playing sports or riding a bike. If you grind your teeth at night, wear a nightguard to protect your natural and artificial teeth from unnecessary wear and tear.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Regular dental appointments at our office are a crucial part of protecting your oral health and keeping your dental implants in working order. Schedule a checkup with us every six months so we can monitor your mouth and address any problems in their early stages.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

The lifespan of dental implants typically depends on the health and lifestyle choices of the patient. To ensure that implants last, it’s important to brush, floss, and rinse with mouthwash on a daily basis. Attend regular cleanings and checkups and avoid chewing on things that are particularly hard or sticky. With proper maintenance, dental implants can last upward of 30 years. That’s several times longer than traditional bridges and dentures.

Does Getting Dental Implants Hurt?

The jawbone doesn’t have very many nerve endings, and your mouth will be numbed with a local anesthetic before the procedure begins. You will also most likely be sedated, lowering your body’s ability to register pain. While the surgery shouldn’t be painful, you may experience some soreness for a few days afterward. By taking recommended over-the-counter and prescribed pain relievers as directed, the discomfort should lessen. Cold compresses can also help. If discomfort persists after three days, give us a call.

Am I Too Young To Get Dental Implants?

If dental implants are placed before the jaw has finished growing, they can interfere with further bone development and potentially result in complications. Most dental professionals will not place implants in patients younger than 18, as most people’s jaws aren’t fully developed until adulthood. Some people’s jaws take until their mid-20s to develop. During your consultation with us, we can discuss whether or not dental implants are the best option for you at this time.
