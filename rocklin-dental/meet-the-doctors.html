James M. Robison

Dr. James M. Robison was born and raised in Central California and is happy to finally return “home” after serving in the U.S. Army for four years as a dentist and paratrooper. He continues to serve in the military in the CA National Guard. He, his wife, and their three children are now happily settled in the Roseville/Rocklin area.

Dr. Robison loves spending time with his family, especially if it involves barbecuing and water sports – he can even backflip while wakeboarding! Dr. Robison is a baseball and football trivia buff and is fluent in Spanish. He could eat chips and salsa for every meal and isn’t fussy about ice cream; he loves every flavor.

Dr. Robison is excited to see you in the community and hopes to serve you and your family as your dentist.
