Our Story

Casa Dental Group was founded in 2013 by Dr. Katie Tran with a mission to provide exceptional dental care to the Concord community. We believe everyone deserves access to quality dental care, which is why we offer our Casa Dental Plan for uninsured patients and a fully Spanish-speaking team. At Casa Dental, everyone is family.

Meet the Team

Our dedicated team of dental professionals brings together years of expertise, compassion, and a shared commitment to providing exceptional care. Get to know the faces behind your smile.

Dr. Katie Tran

Born in Concord, CA, Dr. Tran is no stranger to the neighborhood. She received her undergraduate degree from UC Berkeley and began her dental education at age 19 from NYU College of Dentistry, making her the youngest dentist to graduate from her class. She has since been practicing dentistry for over ten years in New York & California as well as Honduras outreaches. Patients love her for a soft touch and fun nature that make you forget you were at the dentist! In her free time you can find her practicing pilates and playing with her two young children and their French bulldog, Montana!

Dr. Cesar Macias

Dr. Macias, also known as Dr. Cesar, was born in Guadalajara, Mexico where he obtained his dental degree in 2001. He joined our practice in January 2013 and values whole-body approach to dental health. He loves helping others by improving their health through dentistry. Although you might not guess it, growing up Dr. Macias was afraid of going to the dentist! As a career goal he vowed to provide the most gentle and kind care to all his patients. When not at work, Dr. Macias enjoys good dinners, going to the gym, playing tennis, listening to music, and traveling.
