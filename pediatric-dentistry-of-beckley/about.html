Office Comforts & Technology

Introduce the tech and amenities and how this enhances the patient experience.

TVs

With TVs in every room, your kids can enjoy their favorite shows during their visit. Just let us know what they’d like to watch!

Digital X-Rays

With digital X-rays, we can get incredible insights into your child’s oral health. Digital X-rays are faster, use minimal radiation, and offer better image quality than old-fashioned film-based X-ray machines.

Quiet & Private Rooms

Our operatories are comfortable, quiet, and private. And in most cases, parents are welcome to sit with their kids during their treatment.

Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas)

Laughing gas sedation helps nervous kids relax, feel more comfortable, and get dental care with less stress. It wears off quickly, too, so you and your little one can get right back to your daily routines.

Hospital Privileges

Dr. Southall can offer advanced dentistry services for pediatric patients in a hospital setting. This includes special needs care, general anesthesia, and more.

Fun Kids’ Play Area

Dentistry doesn’t have to be scary! We’ve got a children’s play area with tons of activities, games, and books for kids of all ages. You and your kids can sit back, relax, and even have a bit of fun before their visit.

Giving Back To Our Community

We’re Looking For Partners

We’re proud members of the Beckley community. It’s an honor and a privilege to be able to care for the children of our friends and neighbors.

But we’re looking for other ways to become involved in the community, too! If you have any ideas for charitable partnerships or local sponsorships, please get in touch. The Pediatric Dentistry of Beckley team would love to hear from you.
