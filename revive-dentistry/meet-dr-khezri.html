Philosophy of Care

My philosophy is built on a simple belief: the best dental outcomes happen when patients truly understand their own care. I don’t believe in handing someone a treatment plan and sending them on their way. I take time to teach — to walk my patients through their X-rays, explain every option clearly, and make sure they feel genuinely involved in every decision. An informed patient is an empowered patient, and that’s the kind of relationship I’m committed to building here at Revive Dentistry.

Gentle Dentistry

Dental anxiety is real, and Dr. Khezri takes it seriously. He is known for his exceptionally slow, careful injection technique — a hallmark of his approach that patients often comment on. His team has joked that he becomes a "statue" during injections because of how still and deliberate he is, taking every measure to minimize discomfort. Combined with a calm, judgment-free chairside manner, Dr. Khezri creates an environment where even the most anxious patients feel at ease.

Modern Dentistry

Dr. Khezri believes that the right technology leads to better decisions and better results. At Revive Dentistry, he utilizes advanced 3D imaging and CT scanning technology to achieve precise diagnoses, develop accurate treatment plans, and deliver minimally invasive care. By combining this technology with a thorough, unhurried clinical process, Dr. Khezri ensures that every patient receives treatment that is not just effective — but built to last.
