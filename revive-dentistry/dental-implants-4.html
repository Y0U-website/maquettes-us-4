The Part Nobody Mentions: What Happens If You Wait

A missing tooth’s root is gone, so the jawbone underneath begins to shrink — not immediately, but steadily. A year of ‘I’ll deal with it eventually’ can turn a simple single implant into a case that needs bone grafting first. For DeBary’s mix of longtime riverfront residents and newer families, that delay is common, and it’s avoidable with a consultation that catches it early.

Beyond One Tooth

Every option is planned from the same CT scan and executed by the same provider.
