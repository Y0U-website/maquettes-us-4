What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants consist of a small titanium post that is implanted in the place where your tooth used to be. This biocompatible post then functions as a root to which a dental crown is affixed. The dental crown is created to blend in perfectly with the rest of your teeth to give you back a beautiful, complete smile with full function.

Because dental implants are designed to become a permanent part of your mouth, you can care for your implants the same way you would for own natural teeth. Your Smiles of Carolina dentist will discuss good oral hygiene techniques with you. If needed, your dentist or your dental hygienist is happy to provide a hands-on demonstration of good brushing and flossing technique. You might be surprised to find out how often people are doing it wrong!

Dental Implants Vs. Dentures

While denture technology has advanced considerably in the last few years, dental implants still have several advantages over dentures. You don’t have to remove implants to clean them, and you never have to worry about unfortunate or untimely slips. Many people find that their food choices are more limited with dentures because they are worried about the food sticking to the dentures or the dentures being damaged. While dental implants cost more than dentures, most of our patients find that the advantages of dental implants outweigh the extra cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I get dental implants or dentures?

Both dental implants and dentures are effective methods of replacing missing teeth. Your Smiles of Carolina dentist will discuss the advantages and disadvantages of each option to help you find the treatment that best fits your needs and your lifestyle. For example, while dentures cost less than dental implants, they are not a permanent option and need regular maintenance to stay in great shape. For some patients, the best solution might even be a combination of dentures and implants.
