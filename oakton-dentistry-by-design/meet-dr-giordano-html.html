Meet Our Oakton Dentist

Dr. Pasquale J. Giordano

Dr. Giordano is a member of the American Dental Association, the Northern Virginia Dental Society and the Academy of General Dentistry.

He prides himself on being at the forefront of dental technology and regularly attends continuing education courses. Dr. Giordano is certified in Invisalign, Oral Conscious Sedation and ACLS. Dr. Giordano also attends conferences and seminars on aesthetic dentistry , implant placement, and implant restoration .

Dr. Giordano and his wife, Joanna Giordano, reside in Arlington, Virginia and were wed in October 2010. Dr. Giordano played soccer throughout high school and college and currently plays in a men s indoor league. In his free time, Dr. Giordano enjoys spending time with his family, traveling, skiing, golfing, and fishing.
