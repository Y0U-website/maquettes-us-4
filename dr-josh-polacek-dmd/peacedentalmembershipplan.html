Additional Details:

  • The membership plans are discount plans and are not dental insurance.
  • Membership benefits are valid only at Peace Dental Associates.
  • Benefits are non-transferable and may not be applied to services provided by any other healthcare professional or facility.
  • Benefits expire at the end of the 12-month membership year and will be renewed upon receipt of the next year’s annual membership fee.
  • Payment for dental services are due on the day of service.
  • Benefits may not be used in conjunction with a dental insurance plan, discounts or coupons.
  • Membership fees are non-refundable and non-negotiable.
  • Any unused annual benefits will be forfeited.
  • This membership cannot be used in conjunction with any third-party liability insurance (home, auto, workers compensation).
  • Excludes all orthodontic and whitening services.

We believe everyone should have access to great dental care.

Peace Dental Membership Plans (PDMP) helps families without insurance get the proper dental care they need. For an affordable annual fee, each member of your family will receive the preventive dental care they need, and discounts on additional procedures.
