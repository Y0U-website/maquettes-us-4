Your first visit to the office establishes a vital foundation for our relationship with you. We’ll ask you for current contact and insurance information and go over a few questions about your medical history. Please arrive 15 minutes early for your appointment.

What To Expect Being well-prepared for your appointment will ensure that the doctor has all of the needed information to provide the best possible care for you. It will also help relieve any unnecessary anxiety you may be feeling. We look forward to your first visit!

Make a Payment Use the button below to pay your bill online. Payments and patient information are processed securely through PayTrace.net.

We accept all major credit cards including Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover cards.

Insurance

We accept a variety of insurance providers. Prior to your first appointment, please contact your insurance provider to confirm we are an in-network provider.

At your first appointment, please bring your insurance information or policy holder’s card, and/or group number to expedite insurance processing.

Financial Policy

For all treatments, we ask for payment of the portion of fees not covered by insurance at the time of your procedure. If you do not have dental insurance, please ask about our Healthy Smiles Discount Program.

If you have any questions regarding financial responsibilities, please call the office manager (513.772.9100) prior to your first treatment.

Contact Us for questions or information regarding billing.

Cancellations

We know there are times and situations when you cannot keep your appointment. Please Contact Us as soon as possible if you need to change your appointment.

We appreciate as much notice as possible for any cancellations.
