Implant Dentures

Implant-supported dentures to support greater stability and comfort than traditional removable solutions.

Your Trusted Team for Implant Dentures

Implant Dentures are a common dental solution for missing teeth. They’re a combination of traditional dentures and individual dental implants which are securely attached to the jawbone.

336-766-1366 Partial Dentures Partial dentures, or snap-in dentures, attach to implants with special connectors and can be removed for cleaning purposes.

Although they are removable, they are more stable than traditional dentures and are a common solution for missing teeth.

Permanent Dentures

Fixed-implant dentures, like All-On-4 Dentures , are for those looking for a long-term, permanent solution. These are suitable for those wanting a natural-like solution without having to deal with removing and inserting dentures.
