Aimee, Registered Dental Hygienist

Dentistry is a balance between comfort, empathy, and technique, and Aimee prides herself in delivering all three. As a Registered Dental Hygienist, Aimee evaluates the oral health of our patients and educates them on ways that they can improve their oral hygiene. She believes that a healthy smile and a healthy heart are the two components of a healthy life. Quality patient care is very important to her and being sure that patients receive the absolute best care while with her is one of her top priorities.

Aimee graduated from University of the Pacific, Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry with a Bachelor of Science Degree in Dental Hygiene with Honors. She received the Academic Achievement Award and the Hu-Friedy Golden Scaler award. She is also a member of the American Dental Hygienists’ Association, American Dental Education Association, and California Dental Hygienists’ Association.

Outside of work, Aimee enjoys rock climbing, reading, baking, wine tasting, and eating at Italian restaurants.
