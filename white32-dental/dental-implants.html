What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant consists of three primary components that work together to replace a missing tooth.

The titanium implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone where it functions as an artificial tooth root. Once the implant has healed and fused with the surrounding bone through a process known as osseointegration, an abutment is attached to connect the implant to the final restoration. Depending on your needs, the restoration may be a custom dental crown, bridge, or implant-supported denture.

Because each restoration is custom-made, the finished result is designed to blend naturally with your surrounding teeth while restoring full function.

During your consultation, we’ll determine which treatment approach best supports your oral health and smile goals.

  • A single missing tooth
  • Several missing teeth
  • An entire upper or lower arch
  • Teeth currently replaced with removable dentures

The Dental Implant Process

Every implant treatment begins with careful planning to ensure predictable, long-lasting results.

Consultation and Evaluation

Your first appointment includes a comprehensive examination, digital imaging, and a discussion about your goals. We’ll evaluate your teeth, gums, jawbone, and overall oral health to determine whether dental implants are the right solution.

A comprehensive dental exam allows us to identify any underlying concerns that should be addressed before implant treatment begins.

Preparing Your Smile

Some patients require additional treatment before implants can be placed.

If a severely damaged tooth cannot be saved, tooth extraction may be recommended. Patients with gum disease may also benefit from treatment before implant placement to improve long-term success.

Implant Placement

The titanium implant is precisely placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia. Most patients report the procedure is more comfortable than expected and experience only mild discomfort during recovery.

Healing and Final Restoration

During healing, the implant bonds naturally with your jawbone. Once this process is complete, your custom restoration is securely attached, restoring both function and appearance.

Whether you’re replacing one tooth or multiple teeth, the goal is a smile that feels stable, comfortable, and natural.
