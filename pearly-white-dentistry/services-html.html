All-on-X when starting over is the right answer.

For patients facing the loss of most or all of their teeth, All-on-X is one of the most meaningful restorations in dentistry. A small number of strategically placed implants typically four to six anchor a complete arch of fixed prosthetic teeth. Teeth that don’t come out, don’t shift, and don’t require the accommodations of a traditional denture.

This isn’t a procedure to rush into. We approach it the same way we approach every significant case: by understanding the full picture first bone structure, bite mechanics, overall health, and what function means to you specifically. Dr. Brittany handles the restoration work in every All-on-X case designing and fitting the final prosthetic arch working in close partnership with a specialized local lab whose craftsmanship she trusts. Every detail is calibrated to your anatomy, and she stays involved from the first conversation through final placement.

  • Full-arch implant-supported restorations (upper, lower, or both)
  • Fixed prosthetics no removal, no adhesives
  • Restorations designed for long-term fit and function
  • Coordinated care from consultation through final placement

Botox & Beyond cosmetic calm and therapeutic relief.

Botox and Daxxify work beautifully for smoothing forehead lines and softening the brow but at a dental practice, we think about them a little differently than a med spa does. The muscles that create expression are often the same muscles involved in how your jaw moves, how you hold tension, and how your teeth meet when you clench at night.

For patients who grind or clench, therapeutic injections to the masseter and temporalis muscles can meaningfully reduce the muscular load driving that discomfort giving the jaw a chance to settle and often easing the headaches and soreness that come with it. It’s the same treatment, in a whole-health context.

  • Botox & Daxxify
  • Dermal fillers
  • Cosmetic wrinkle smoothing
  • Masseter & temporalis therapy
