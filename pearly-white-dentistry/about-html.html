About

A total-health dental practice in Vestavia Hills, Alabama.

Most dental practices lead with the finished product the gleaming smile, the dramatic before-and-after. Pearly White Dentistry leads with the foundation that makes the result last. Health first. Beautiful always.

A dentist shaped by science, guided by one clear principle.

Dr. Henry completed her undergraduate degree at Nebraska Wesleyan University a B.S. in Biology with minors in Chemistry and Psychology and earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery from Creighton University School of Dentistry. Her continuing education spans restorative, cosmetic, and preventive dentistry, Invisalign and clear aligner therapy, Botox and neurotoxins, and dermal fillers. She is a member of the American Dental Association, Alabama Dental Association, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, and Birmingham District Dental Society.

Outside the office, Dr. Henry is married to Dr. R. Gordon Henry, D.D.S. also a dentist and they have two sons. They are members of St. Francis Xavier Catholic Church.

Transparency as Care

Transparency isn’t a value on a wall it’s a clinical method. Technology is used not to impress, but to include you in your own diagnosis so that trust is built through shared understanding rather than blind authority.

Health Before Aesthetics

Beauty is never the starting point it’s the outcome of health done right. Every cosmetic decision begins with function, airway, bite mechanics, and whole-body wellness. We reject the industry pattern of leading with smile transformations and instead lead with the foundation that makes a beautiful result last.

Honest Partnership

You are an intelligent adult and a partner in your own care never talked down to, never pressured, and never given a recommendation the doctor doesn’t fully believe in. Honesty includes the willingness to say I don’t know yet and to frame treatment in terms of predictability rather than fear.

Quiet Excellence

Our standard is demonstrated, not announced. Advanced technology, extensive continuing education, and professional affiliations serve as proof of philosophy not marketing features. Pearly White Dentistry is one of just two certified GBT Airflow providers in the entire state of Alabama, and we’d rather you notice the experience than hear about it.

Dr. Brittany Henry is a member of the American Dental Association, Alabama Dental Association, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, and the Birmingham District Dental Society.

Healthy first. Beautiful always.
