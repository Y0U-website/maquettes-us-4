How Do I Care for Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a great way to replace missing teeth and restore your smile. However, it is important to remember that they require the same care as real teeth. This means brushing, flossing, and rinsing with an antibacterial mouthwash on a regular basis. In addition, it is important to visit the dentist for regular check-ups. These appointments will allow your dentist to assess the condition of your implants and make sure they are still healthy. By taking good care of your implants, you can ensure that they will last for many years to come.
