The practice

​Dr. Maria C. Galdiano has been in private practice in Oceanside for over 27 years. She graduated from the University of Philippines-Manila College of Dentistry, and furthered her education at University of California Los Angeles (UCLA) School of Dentistry, with Post Graduate training in Advanced Dental Esthetics.

We love welcoming new patients and making every visit feel comfortable, positive, and stress-free. From your first appointment, Dr. Maria Galdiano and our compassionate team take the time to understand your needs, explain your options, and provide personalized dental care in a relaxing environment.
