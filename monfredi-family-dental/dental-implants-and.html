When a missing tooth is not replaced, the jawbone in that area begins to shrink over time. This bone loss can affect facial structure and cause neighboring teeth to shift. Implant placement helps stimulate the jawbone, much like a natural tooth root, supporting healthier bone levels for years to come.

  • Enhanced appearance and renewed confidence
  • Prevention of further jawbone loss
  • Preservation of surrounding natural teeth
  • Greater stability compared to removable appliances
  • Long-term durability with excellent success rates

Types of Dental Implants and Restorations

Single Tooth Implants &#x2014; A single tooth implant replaces one missing tooth without affecting adjacent teeth. A custom crown is attached to the implant to create a highly natural appearance and full chewing function.

Implant-Supported Bridges &#x2014; Patients missing several consecutive teeth may benefit from a fixed bridge supported by implants, restoring multiple teeth without requiring an implant for each individual tooth.

Implant-Supported Dentures &#x2014; Implants may also support a removable or fixed denture for patients missing a full arch of teeth. Implant-supported restorations provide greater stability and comfort than traditional dentures.

Dr. Hamza evaluates the number of missing teeth, available bone structure, and oral health goals before recommending the most appropriate restoration.

The Dental Implant Process at Monfredi Family Dental

Initial Consultation and Treatment Planning The process begins with Dr. Hamza completing a comprehensive evaluation of the teeth, gums, jawbone, and overall oral health. Digital imaging helps evaluate available bone volume and identify any concerns involving the jaw, sinus, or surrounding soft tissue. Patients with gum disease or other health concerns may require additional treatment before implant surgery can safely proceed.

Tooth Extraction and Bone Grafting If a damaged tooth remains, extraction may be necessary before implant placement. Some patients also require bone grafting if significant bone loss has occurred. Grafting helps rebuild support in the jawbone so implants can be placed securely. Grafting material may come from synthetic sources, the patient s own bone, or a tissue bank.

Implant Placement Surgery During implant surgery at our Galloway, Ohio office, the implant post is carefully positioned in the jawbone under local anesthesia. Most patients tolerate the procedure comfortably with appropriate numbing. A healing cap is placed temporarily to protect the area while osseointegration begins.

Healing and Final Restoration Over several months, bone cells gradually fuse to the implant surface, creating the stable foundation needed for the final restoration. Once healing is confirmed, the custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached. In some cases, a temporary restoration is worn during healing to maintain appearance and function.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Many adults in Galloway, Ohio with missing teeth qualify for dental implants. Healthy gums, adequate jawbone support, and good oral hygiene are important for long-term implant success.

Ideal candidates typically include patients who are missing one or more teeth, have healthy gum tissue, maintain good oral hygiene, have sufficient jawbone support, want a long-term tooth replacement solution, and are healthy enough for an outpatient surgical procedure.

Certain factors such as smoking, uncontrolled gum disease, or limited bone support may affect candidacy but can often be addressed through preparatory treatment. Patients with inadequate bone may still qualify through bone grafting or other techniques. Dr. Hamza will discuss your specific situation during your consultation at our Galloway, Ohio office.

Longevity and Success Rates

Dental implants are known for excellent long-term success rates. With proper care, many implants last decades and in some cases a lifetime. Factors that influence longevity include oral hygiene habits, gum health, bite forces, smoking, and consistent attendance at routine dental visits. Unlike other tooth replacement options, implants preserve surrounding bone and do not rely on neighboring natural teeth for support.

Understanding Possible Risks and Complications

Every surgical procedure carries some level of risk. Possible complications with implants may include infection, delayed healing, nerve irritation, implant failure, or sinus involvement near the upper jaw. Careful treatment planning, thorough evaluation, and following all postoperative instructions significantly reduce these risks. Our team at Monfredi Family Dental monitors healing closely throughout the process to help patients achieve the best possible outcomes.
