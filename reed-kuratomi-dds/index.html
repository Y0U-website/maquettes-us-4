The practice

At our practice, we are true believers that preventative care and education are the keys to maximizing dental health. To achieve that we focus on thorough exams to ensure the overall health of your teeth and gums. We also perform oral cancer exams, and take x-rays when necessary. We stress routine cleanings, flossing, sealants and fluoride to prevent dental disease.

When you visit our practice, your smile is our top priority. Our entire team is dedicated to providing you with the personalized, gentle care you deserve. Your trust is important to us. As your dental health professionals, we want you to be confident knowing that we are a team of highly trained and skilled clinicians.
