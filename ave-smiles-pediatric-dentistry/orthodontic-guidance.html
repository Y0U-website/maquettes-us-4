Two Common Tools

Space Maintainers and Habit Appliances

Two simple, effective tools we use to guide healthy permanent tooth development.

Space Maintainers

Holding the Spot for the Next Tooth

When a baby tooth in the back of the mouth is lost prematurely, often from extensive decay, the surrounding teeth can drift into the empty space. That can leave the permanent tooth without room to come in properly.

A space maintainer is a small, custom-made appliance that holds the open space until the permanent tooth is ready to erupt. It’s a simple, low-impact step that can prevent more involved orthodontic treatment later.

If your child has lost a back baby tooth early, we’ll talk through whether a space maintainer makes sense based on age, tooth position, and timing of the permanent tooth’s expected arrival.

Why It Matters

  • Prevents neighboring teeth from drifting
  • Preserves space for the permanent tooth
  • Custom-fit and comfortable for daily wear
  • Often avoids more complex orthodontics later
  • Removed once the permanent tooth is ready to erupt

Habit Appliances

A Gentle Stop to Habits That Affect Smiles

When all efforts at home haven’t been enough to break a habit like thumb sucking, and the permanent front teeth are starting to come in, a fixed habit appliance can help your child finally let go.

The appliance gently interrupts the habit so the child no longer gets the comforting feedback that reinforces it. It’s not punitive — it’s simply a small physical reminder that gives the natural tooth and jaw development a chance to get back on track.

We’ll talk through whether a habit appliance is the right next step based on your child’s age, the habit, and how the front teeth are erupting.

Common Habits We Help With

  • Persistent thumb sucking
  • Finger sucking
  • Tongue thrusting
  • Other habits affecting tooth or jaw development
