Frequently Asked Questions

How long do All-on-X implants last?

With proper care and maintenance, All-on-X dental implants can last a lifetime. The implants themselves are designed to be permanent, while the prosthesis may need replacement after 10-15 years depending on wear and care.

Is the procedure painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during the procedure thanks to local anesthesia. Post-operative discomfort is typically mild and can be managed with over-the-counter pain medication. Many patients are surprised by how comfortable the process is.

How long is the recovery period?

Initial healing takes about 1-2 weeks, during which you’ll eat soft foods. The implants will fully integrate with your jawbone over 3-6 months, at which point your permanent prosthesis will be placed.

Can I eat normally with All-on-X implants?

Yes! Once fully healed, All-on-X implants allow you to eat virtually any food you want, including steak, apples, corn on the cob, and other foods that are difficult with dentures. The implants provide the same biting force as natural teeth.
