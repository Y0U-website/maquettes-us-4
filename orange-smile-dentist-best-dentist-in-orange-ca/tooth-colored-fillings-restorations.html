Tooth-Colored Fillings & Restorations

We are proud to be a mercury-free dental practice, offering safe, durable, and natural-looking alternatives to traditional silver fillings. While silver/mercury (“amalgam”) fillings were common in the past, they can weaken teeth over time and affect the appearance of your smile.

Today’s porcelain inlays and tooth-colored onlays blend seamlessly with your natural teeth while adding strength and protection. With modern bonding technology, they look and feel just like your own tooth—only healthier.

Why Upgrade From Silver Fillings?

  • Silver fillings can expand and contract with temperature changes, risking cracks or fractures.
  • Over time, they may wear down or leak, allowing decay to return.
  • They can discolor your teeth and gums.

The Benefits of Tooth-Colored Restorations

  • Natural appearance – matches your tooth color perfectly.
  • Added strength – bonded securely to protect your tooth.
  • Healthier for gums – no placement at the gum line.
  • Decay protection – some materials contain fluoride.
  • Long-lasting – designed to stand up to years of chewing and smiling.

The Process – Just Two Easy Visits

First Visit

Second Visit

  • We remove your old filling and any decay.
  • We take an impression to create a custom-fit restoration.
  • A temporary onlay is placed for comfort and protection.
  • We replace the temporary with your custom onlay.
  • It’s bonded securely in place and polished to perfection.
  • You leave with a tooth that looks natural, feels strong, and functions beautifully.
