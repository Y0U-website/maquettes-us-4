First Visit

Your First Visit and Care Plan

Every new patient begins with a comprehensive evaluation so our doctors can understand your unique needs and determine the best path forward.

Advanced Imaging

We use digital X-rays and 3D CBCT scans to capture detailed views of your tooth for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.

Clinical Examination

Your endodontist will examine the tooth, evaluate symptoms, and look for any underlying concerns.

Diagnosis & Treatment Plan

Your doctor will review your results, explain your options, and may complete treatment the same day if appropriate.

What to Bring to Your First Visit

To help us provide the best and most efficient care, please have the following items ready:

A valid photo ID

Insurance card (optional)

Medical history & medication list

Referral slip (if provided by your dentist)
