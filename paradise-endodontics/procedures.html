Procedures and services

We provide advanced, precise treatments to relieve pain, save your teeth, and restore your oral health—delivered comfortably with the latest technology.

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal therapy treats infection within the tooth by removing the inflamed or infected pulp, thoroughly disinfecting the canal system, and sealing it to prevent reinfection. This procedure relieves pain and preserves your natural tooth structure.

Root Canal Retreatment

If a previously treated tooth does not heal properly or develops new issues, root canal retreatment may be needed. The tooth is reopened, old materials are removed, and the canals are cleaned, reshaped, and sealed again to address persistent or recurring infection.

Endodontic Microsurgery

Endodontic microsurgery, often an apicoectomy, is performed when conventional root canal therapy cannot fully resolve an infection. Using high magnification and microsurgical techniques, the root tip and surrounding infected tissue are removed and sealed to promote proper healing.
