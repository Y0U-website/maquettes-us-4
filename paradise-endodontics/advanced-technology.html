Advanced Technology

We use the latest dental technology to deliver safer, more accurate, and more comfortable care. Advanced tools like 3D imaging, microscopes, and laser-assisted techniques allow us to diagnose issues earlier, treat with greater precision, and provide the highest standard of endodontic care.

CBCT 3D Imaging

Provides highly detailed, three-dimensional images of your teeth, bone, and surrounding structures. This advanced view allows for more accurate diagnosis, clearer visualization of complex anatomy, and precise treatment planning.

Microscopes

Surgical microscopes offer magnified, well-lit views of delicate internal tooth structures. This enhanced visibility allows your doctor to perform procedures with exceptional precision, improving accuracy and long-term outcomes.

Digital Imaging (X-Rays)

Produces crisp, high-resolution images with significantly reduced radiation compared to traditional X-rays. Digital imaging helps identify issues early, guides treatment decisions, and makes the diagnostic process faster and more comfortable.

Laser

Some teeth have intricate anatomy that traditional instruments can’t always reach. WaterLase iPlus™ technology uses laser energy to access and disinfect these areas, especially in complex cases, improving root canal cleaning and outcomes. Your doctor will determine if this advanced laser treatment is right for you.

Advanced Air Filtration

Incorporates medical-grade filtration and purification systems that continuously refresh and clean the air throughout our office. This technology enhances safety, reduces airborne particles, and supports a healthier environment for both patients and staff.
