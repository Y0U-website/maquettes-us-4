If snoring or interrupted sleep is impacting your quality of rest, a custom oral appliance may help. This appliance is designed to gently open the airway, reduce obstructions, and improve oxygen flow during sleep. You’ll be fitted and guided on proper use in our office, with follow-up visits to track sleep improvements and adjust the fit for optimal comfort and effectiveness.

Jaw stiffness, popping, or headaches can disrupt your daily life. We use precise splint therapy to realign your jaw and ease muscle strain. We create a custom device and fit it in minutes, then guide you through easy exercises to support healing. Alongside heat therapy and relaxation tips, this treatment relieves pain and improves jaw movement.
