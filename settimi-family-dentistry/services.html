Regular Exams Cleaning

Cleanings and exams are generally scheduled every six to eight months. During your professional cleaning, we remove plaque and tartar from your teeth to keep it from building up and causing cavities or gum disease. We polish your teeth and then Dr. Settimi performs an oral exam, looking for cavities, oral cancer, or other areas of concern. X-rays may be taken to view any problems that cannot be seen visually.
