Important Information

This plan is NOT insurance. This is a dental membership discount plan, not a qualified health plan under the Affordable Care Act.

This plan provides discounts for dental services at certain participating health care providers. This plan does not make payments directly to providers of dental services. Members are obligated to pay for all dental services at the time of service but will receive a discount from participating providers.

This plan is valid only at participating Well Group Dentistry practices, including AP Dental and South Boston Family Dental locations in Massachusetts and Florida. Plan benefits are not transferable to other providers, offices, or specialists.

Discounts apply only to eligible services and may not be combined with other offers, promotions, or insurance benefits. Additional exclusions and limitations apply. Savings vary by treatment. Full terms and conditions are provided in the membership agreement at the time of enrollment.
