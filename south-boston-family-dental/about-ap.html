Dr. Anya Pukhovitskaya

DMD · Diamond Invisalign Provider · Boston University

Dr. Anya’s journey began in St. Petersburg, Russia before relocating to Boston. She graduated cum laude in Biology from UMass, earned her DMD from Boston University, and completed an AEGD residency.

In 2015, she co-founded her first dental office and launched her own practice in East Boston. Today, AP Dental spans nine locations across Massachusetts and Florida. She focuses on restorative dentistry, clear aligner therapy, and facial aesthetics.
