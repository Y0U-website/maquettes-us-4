It’s not just about spotting cavities — it’s about catching small issues before they turn into big ones. We’ll take a close look at your teeth, gums, and jaw, do a professional cleaning, and use digital X-rays if needed to see what’s happening below the surface.

You’ll also get a personalized care plan so you know exactly what’s next. These checkups are key for keeping your mouth healthy, your smile strong, and avoiding more intensive treatments down the line.

What’s Included Everything that comes with your visit A complete dental exam covers much more than you’d think — here’s what you can expect every time. Complete oral check We take a thorough look at everything — teeth, gums, tongue, and jaw — to catch any signs of cavities, inflammation, or anything that needs attention.

Digital X-rays Only taken when needed — no overdoing it. X-rays help us spot what we can’t see, like cavities between teeth, bone loss, or anything hiding below the surface.

Professional cleaning A deep clean that feels like a fresh start. We remove plaque and tartar buildup, polish your teeth, and floss like a pro so your smile leaves feeling brand new.

Gum health check Healthy gums mean a strong foundation. We check for early signs of gum disease like gingivitis before it becomes a bigger issue.

Custom care plan Based on what we see, you’ll get personalized next steps — whether it’s a fluoride boost, a small fix, or just a “see you in 6 months.”

Fluoride treatments A quick, powerful way to strengthen enamel and help prevent cavities — especially if your teeth need a little extra support.

Ask your doctor Nightguards Grinding while you sleep? A custom nightguard protects your teeth and jaw from damage you don’t even know you’re doing.

Ask your doctor Sealants Tiny shields for your molars — sealing out food and bacteria to keep cavities away, especially in those hard-to-reach spots.
