Compatibility with Browsers and Assistive Technology

Our websites are designed to be compatible with the following browsers and assistive technologies:

  • Browsers : the latest two versions of Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, and Microsoft Edge
  • Screen readers : Apple VoiceOver (macOS and iOS), NVDA (Windows), JAWS (Windows), and TalkBack (Android)
  • Keyboard navigation : all functionality is available using the Tab , Shift+Tab , Enter , Space , arrow keys, and Escape

Tips for the Best Experience

  • Use the latest version of your browser.
  • Enable your preferred assistive technology in your operating system settings.
  • Adjust browser zoom ( Cmd + + on Mac, Ctrl + + on Windows) to enlarge text and images.
  • Enable "Reduce Motion" in your operating system settings if animations cause discomfort.

Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some content on our website may not yet be fully accessible. Known limitations include:

  • Embedded third-party content such as Google Maps may not fully meet accessibility standards. These services are operated by Google and we have limited control over their accessibility characteristics.
  • PDF documents linked from the site may not be fully tagged for screen readers. If you need a document in a different format, please contact us using the information below.
