Your smile transformation through braces creates permanent structural changes to your teeth positioning. We see patients who completed treatment years ago still enjoying perfectly aligned teeth. The key to lasting success lies in following your post-treatment care plan and wearing retainers as directed by PhD Orthodontics.

Different brace types offer unique advantages depending on your specific needs and lifestyle preferences. Traditional metal braces remain the most reliable choice for complex orthodontic issues, while ceramic options provide discretion without sacrificing effectiveness.
