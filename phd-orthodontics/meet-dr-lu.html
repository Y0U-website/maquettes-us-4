As a Craniofacial Orthodontist, I’m among the few specialists with additional Fellowship Training to care for patients with Cleft Lip/Palate and other Congenital conditions requiring Presurgical Orthodontics.

I served as the Attending Orthodontist on the renowned Craniofacial Team at Children’s Hospital Los Angeles (CHLA), where I worked closely with multidisciplinary teams to guide families through complex care with empathy and precision.

Outside the clinic, I’m a father to Dustin, a bright and adventurous 9-year-old in the fourth grade at Hugo Raider Elementary. He loves LEGO, Roblox, Minecraft, and (possibly) piano and math homework. We love traveling, camping, and discovering the unknown together—whether it’s in the great outdoors or in daily adventures.

Running is another important part of my life. I ran my first marathon in Chicago in 2013 to overcome a personal battle with depression, and that race marked the beginning of a healing journey. Since then, I’ve completed 30 full marathons—each 26.2 miles a symbol of perseverance, growth, and strength.

I was made in China, but my last name is USA now. As a new immigrant, I’m deeply grateful for the opportunities this country has given me to pursue my American dream. With the skills and experience I’ve gained, my greatest hope is to give back—to serve our community, and care for your loved ones as I do my own.
