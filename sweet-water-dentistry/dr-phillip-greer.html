Meet Dr. Phillip Greer

Dr. Phillip Greer

Dr. Phillip Greer is a native of Mobile, Alabama. He earned his Bachelor of Science degree in Marine Biology from Auburn University before receiving his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Meharry Medical College in Nashville, Tennessee.

Dr. Greer works closely with Glidewell Dental Laboratories and other respected labs across the country to provide high-quality, customized restorations. Throughout his career, he has helped create more than 1,000 smiles through treatments including dental implants, implant crowns and dentures, crowns, veneers, Invisalign, full and partial dentures, and restorative dentistry.

Dr. Greer has been involved with the American Dental Association, Alabama Dental Association, and Christian Medical and Dental Association. His career was deeply influenced by Dr. John Morgan, a Mobile Bay-area orthodontist who passed away from brain cancer in 2001. Inspired by Dr. Morgan’s legacy, Dr. Greer pursued dentistry and established Sweet Water Dentistry in his honor.

He has also served on mission trips, providing oral healthcare in Latin American countries. Outside the office, Dr. Greer enjoys being active in the community through organizations including Fairhope Sunset Rotary, Kiwanis Club, Kairos Prison Ministry, the Eastern Shore Chamber of Commerce, and local churches. He and his wife, Ashley, share three children.
