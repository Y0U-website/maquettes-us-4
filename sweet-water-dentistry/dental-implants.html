  • Crown, Bridge, or Denture Placement : Once your implant has fully integrated, Dr. Greer attaches your custom restoration, designed to match your natural teeth in size, shape, and shade. The appointment takes just a couple of hours, and you’ll leave with your complete, confident smile.

The Benefits That Matter the Most

  • Built to Last a Lifetime : Dentures and bridges typically need replacing every 5 to 7 years. Dental implants, cared for properly, last decades, and often a lifetime. It’s the last tooth replacement you’ll ever need.
  • Looks and Feels Like the Real Thing : Because the implant anchors into your jawbone the way a natural root does, it feels stable and natural in a way that no denture can replicate. Your restoration is custom-crafted to match your existing teeth. Most patients say they forget it’s there.
  • Protects the Bone You Have Left : When a tooth is missing, the jawbone beneath it begins to resorb, and it literally shrinks away over time. An implant acts as a root, stimulating the bone and keeping your jaw structure intact. This is something dentures and bridges simply cannot do.
  • Preserves Your Surrounding Teeth : Traditional bridges require grinding down the healthy teeth on either side to anchor the bridge. Implants don’t touch those teeth at all. Your healthy smile stays healthy.
  • Zero Special Maintenance : Brush, floss, and come in for your regular cleanings at Sweet Water. That’s it. No adhesives, no soaking trays, no special kits. Implants fit seamlessly into your daily routine.
  • Gives You Your Life Back : Eating what you want. Laughing without self-consciousness. Smiling in photos without hesitation. These are the things patients tell us they’ve gotten back after implants, and that’s why we love doing this work.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do implants compare to dentures or bridges?

Implants are the only solution that replaces the root as well as the tooth. That means they protect your jawbone, don’t affect neighboring teeth, require no adhesives, and last far longer. Dentures and bridges are valid options in some cases, but for patients who are good candidates, implants are consistently the higher-value long-term choice.

What if I don’t have enough bone for an implant?

This is more common than you’d think, and it doesn’t automatically rule you out. With our 3D Cone Beam CT scan, Dr. Greer can assess your exact bone volume before we begin. If grafting is needed, it’s a straightforward preparatory step we can often handle in-house before placing the implant.
