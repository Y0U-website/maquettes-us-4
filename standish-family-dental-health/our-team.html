Dr. Christiana Dagher, DDS

Dr. Christiana Dagher was born and raised in Bangor, Maine. She comes from a multicultural household, with three younger siblings, her father being from Bikfaya, Lebanon, and her mother from the state of Wisconsin. During her childhood, she enjoyed yearly trips to visit her Lebanese family and embracing their culture.

She earned her Bachelor of Science degree in Pre-Medical studies and Human Nutrition at the University of Maine, in Orono. She went on to spend a year working in a dental office, where she participated in every role from administrative work to chairside assisting and sterilizing equipment. She received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Dalhousie University in Halifax, Nova Scotia.

After graduating she was eager to return to her home state to serve Maine people. Following her passions for oral surgery and dental implantology, she went on to earn her Certificate in Implantology from the International Postgraduate Medical College at the Dental Institute of the Caribbean. She is currently working on her fellowship with the International Congress of Implantologists (ICOI).

Dr. Dagher is passionate about delivering gentle, compassionate, and comprehensive care to all patients in a positive environment. She is dedicated to staying current on the latest dental technologies and treatment options through continuing education courses. In her spare time, she enjoys weight lifting, running, cooking, traveling, and spending time with her family.

Dr. Joshua Stephens, DDS

A midwest man, Dr. Stephens grew up in Missouri, Michigan, a few years overseas in Wales, and ended up in Indiana where he started his dental career in Indianapolis. When he and his wife from Maine got pregnant they decided it was time for a change. Right in the middle of the pandemic, the family packed up and moved to Cumberland, Maine. Excited about all the mountains, ocean, and breweries, Dr. Stephens has been exploring Maine by hiking, skiing, boating, and tasting everything possible. In his free time, Dr. Stephens enjoys woodworking, painting, and spending time with his wife and two sons.

Graduating from Indiana University School of Dentistry and earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree Dr. Stephens started at Aspen Dental focusing on surgical extractions, dentures, restorative procedures, and Invisalign. Dr. Stephens prides himself on relating to patients and trying to make them feel comfortable in an uncomfortable environment. Dr. Stephens is a member of the American Dental Association as well as the Maine Dental Association.

If you’re looking for a dentist that will look out for your oral health without making you feel ashamed then you are in the right place. Dr. Stephens knows how important integrity is in dentistry and always strives to treat a patient like they are family.
