The practice

Lake Ridge is a census-designated place (CDP) in Prince William County, Virginia, United States. It is an annex of Woodbridge, Virginia. The population was 41,058 at the 2010 census, an increase of 35 % from 2000. Lake Ridge is at 38°41′17″N 77°18′32″W (38.688190, −77.308953), along Old Bridge Road between Harbor Drive and Westridge Drive.

Lake Ridge Pediatric & Adult Dentistry is proud to provide comprehensive dental care to residents of Lake Ridge, VA and the surrounding communities.
