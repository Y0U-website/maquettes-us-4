How Guided Biofilm Therapy Works

GBT follows a systematic approach that allows our hygiene team to provide care based on your individual oral health needs.

Making the biofilm visible helps guide the cleaning process and allows you to better understand where plaque tends to collect in your mouth.

  • Evaluate your teeth, gums, restorations, and dental appliances
  • Apply a harmless disclosing solution that makes biofilm visible
  • Show you areas that may need additional attention at home
  • Use Airflow® technology to remove biofilm and many surface stains
  • Use Perioflow® when appropriate to clean deeper periodontal areas
  • Remove remaining hardened tartar with gentle instrumentation
  • Provide personalized brushing, flossing, and home-care recommendations

Comfortable Care for Sensitive Teeth and Gums

Patients with sensitivity sometimes feel nervous about professional cleanings. The warm water and fine powder used during Guided Biofilm Therapy can make the process more comfortable than traditional cleaning methods for many patients.

Our team will adjust your treatment based on your sensitivity, gum health, restorations, and comfort level. We’ll also explain each step so you know what to expect throughout your appointment.

GBT for Dental Implants, Braces, and Restorations

Biofilm can collect around orthodontic brackets, dental implants, crowns, bridges, and veneers. These areas often require extra attention because they can be more difficult to clean with a toothbrush and floss alone.

Guided Biofilm Therapy allows our team to clean carefully around these areas while helping protect your dental work. We’ll also recommend home-care tools that can make cleaning around restorations and appliances easier between visits.

Frequently Asked Questions About Guided Biofilm Therapy

Is Guided Biofilm Therapy painful?

GBT is designed to be gentle and comfortable. Many patients prefer the warm water and fine powder used during the Airflow® portion of the cleaning, especially if they have sensitive teeth or gums.

Does GBT replace every traditional cleaning tool?

Not always. Airflow® removes biofilm and many surface stains, but additional instruments may still be needed to remove hardened tartar. Your cleaning will be customized to your individual needs.

Can GBT remove tooth stains?

GBT can remove many external stains caused by coffee, tea, wine, and tobacco. It does not change the natural internal color of your teeth. For deeper discoloration, you may benefit from our professional teeth whitening options .

How often should I schedule a cleaning?

Most patients benefit from routine cleanings every six months. Patients with active gum disease or a history of periodontal problems may need more frequent maintenance appointments.
