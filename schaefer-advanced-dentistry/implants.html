Why Choose Dental Implants?

Help Preserve the Jawbone

When a tooth is lost, the jawbone in that area no longer receives the stimulation previously provided by the natural tooth root. Dental implants interact with the jawbone and can help maintain the bone and surrounding facial structure.

Long-Lasting and Convenient

With excellent oral hygiene, routine dental visits, and proper maintenance, dental implants can provide many years of reliable service. Unlike removable dentures, implant restorations do not require dental adhesives and are designed to remain stable while you eat and speak.

Look and Function Like Natural Teeth

The restoration placed over an implant is carefully designed to complement your surrounding teeth. Dental implants can make it easier to enjoy a varied diet, speak clearly, and smile without worrying about a removable appliance shifting.

Restore Confidence and Quality of Life

Replacing missing teeth can improve more than the appearance of your smile. A complete, stable smile may help you feel more comfortable eating in public, speaking with others, and participating confidently in everyday activities.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

If you are missing one or more teeth because of decay, injury, gum disease, or another dental concern, you may be a candidate for dental implants. Good candidates generally have healthy gums, sufficient jawbone support, and overall health that allows for normal healing.

What to Expect During the Dental Implant Process

Dental implant treatment is customized for every patient and is typically completed in several stages:

The completed result is designed to provide a secure, functional tooth replacement that blends naturally with your smile.

  • Consultation and planning: We evaluate your oral health, take detailed images, and create a personalized treatment plan.
  • Implant placement: The titanium implant is carefully positioned within the jawbone using the treatment plan prepared for your anatomy.
  • Healing and integration: The implant is given time to bond with the surrounding bone. The length of this stage varies according to the patient and treatment area.
  • Final restoration: Once the implant is stable, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to restore the appearance and function of your smile.

How Do You Care for Dental Implants?

Dental implants do not develop cavities, but the gums and natural teeth surrounding them still require consistent care. Maintaining healthy gum tissue is important for protecting the implant and supporting its long-term success.

Our hygienists will show you how to properly clean around your implant restoration and recommend any tools that may make home care easier.

  • Brushing thoroughly at least twice each day
  • Cleaning between the implant and surrounding teeth
  • Attending regular professional cleanings and examinations
  • Avoiding tobacco products
  • Following any personalized care recommendations from our team

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long does dental implant treatment take?

The timeline varies based on the number of implants being placed, the condition of your jawbone, your healing response, and whether preparatory treatment is required. After examining your smile, we can provide a more personalized estimate.

Are dental implants comfortable?

The implant placement procedure is performed with local anesthesia to help keep you comfortable. Some temporary tenderness or swelling may occur afterward, and our team will provide detailed instructions for managing your recovery.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are designed to be a long-term tooth-replacement option. Their longevity depends on oral hygiene, gum health, lifestyle factors, routine professional care, and the forces placed on the implant.

Can an implant replace a front tooth?

Yes. A dental implant can be used to replace a missing front tooth with a customized restoration designed to complement the shape and color of the surrounding teeth. View examples in our esthetic front teeth implant gallery .

Can dental implants support dentures?

Dental implants can be used to stabilize certain full or partial dentures. Implant-supported restorations may provide greater stability than traditional removable dentures, although the appropriate option depends on your individual needs.

What happens if I do not have enough jawbone?

Patients who have experienced jawbone loss may require a preparatory procedure before implant placement. Advanced imaging allows our doctors to assess your bone structure and determine what options are available.
