661-861-8000 8701 Camino Media, Bakersfield, CA 93311 Book Appointment Book Appointment Dr. Brian Kim is an established and highly skilled dentist with over 30 years of experience in general dentistry and orthodontics. He has been delivering compassionate dental care to residents of Kern County and has earned a great reputation for his quality and patient-centered care.

He strongly believes in building relationships based on trust, helping you make informed decisions about your dental care.

Dr. Kim first had the inspiration to enter the field of dentistry as a teen, when he realized how much his classmates and even some of the adults he knew feared going to the dentist. He wanted to make the experience more patient-friendly and comfortable, and he decided that the best way to do it was to become a dentist himself. He attended the University of California, Irvine, for undergraduate studies and received his Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachusetts.

In his spare time, Dr. Kim enjoys traveling, skiing, golfing and spending time outdoors.
