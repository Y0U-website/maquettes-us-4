Clear Aligners

  • Permanent attachment: Since they are not removable, there’s no need to worry about discipline in wearing time, ensuring continuous treatment.
  • Oral hygiene challenges: Cleaning teeth can be more challenging with braces, increasing the risk of plaque buildup and potential for gum disease if not managed properly.
  • Discomfort: Brackets and wires can cause irritation to the mouth’s interior, especially after adjustments.
  • Dietary restrictions: Hard, sticky, or chewy foods can damage braces, limiting dietary choices during treatment.

Clear Aligners

  • Aesthetic and discreet: Clear aligners are virtually invisible, making them a more aesthetically pleasing option compared to traditional braces.
  • Improved oral hygiene: Removable, allowing for better access to clean teeth and gums thoroughly.
  • Comfort: No wires or brackets that could irritate the mouth, plus each set of aligners is custom-made to fit snugly over the teeth.
  • Requires self-discipline: Patients must be diligent about wearing the aligners for the required 20-22 hours per day, which can be challenging, especially for younger patients.
  • Possible for misplacement or loss: Being removable means aligners can easily be lost or forgotten, potentially delaying treatment progress.
  • Initial speech adjustment: Some people might experience a temporary lisp or slight alteration in speech until they get used to the aligners.
