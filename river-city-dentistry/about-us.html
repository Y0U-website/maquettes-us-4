Our Team

At River City Dentistry, we provide a full range of dental services designed to help patients of all ages achieve and maintain healthy, confident smiles. From preventive care and routine checkups to cosmetic enhancements and restorative treatments, our experienced team is committed to delivering personalized care in a welcoming environment. Proudly serving Owensboro and the surrounding communities, we continue a tradition of trusted dental care that families have relied on for more than 41 years.

Wm. Casey Callis, DMD PLLC

Dr. Wm. Casey Callis founded this dental practice in 1988 and has been a trusted provider in the Owensboro community for more than 40 years. A graduate of the University of Louisville School of Dentistry (Class of 1985), Dr. Callis is passionate about building lasting relationships with his patients and caring for generations of local families. Known for his gentle approach, dedication, and genuine concern for those he serves, he continues to enjoy practicing dentistry as much as ever.

MaryBeth Reynolds, DMD PLLC

Dr. MaryBeth Reynolds joined the practice in 2021 and is thrilled to be practicing dentistry in her hometown. After graduating from Owensboro High School and earning her dental degree from the University of Louisville School of Dentistry in 2016, she spent the first several years of her career in Florida before returning home to be closer to family and raise her own. Today, she enjoys serving the community that helped shape her and caring for patients of all ages.
