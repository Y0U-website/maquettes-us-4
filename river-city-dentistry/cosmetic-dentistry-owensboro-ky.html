Invisalign®

If you want a straighter smile without traditional braces, Invisalign may be the perfect solution. Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable aligners that gradually move your teeth into their desired position.

  • Nearly invisible appearance
  • Comfortable fit
  • Removable for eating and brushing
  • Fewer office visits
  • Improved smile aesthetics

Smile Makeovers

A smile makeover combines multiple cosmetic treatments into a customized plan designed around your goals.

Depending on your needs, a smile makeover may include:

  • Teeth whitening
  • Veneers
  • Invisalign
  • Dental crowns
  • Tooth-colored restorations

Professional Teeth Whitening

Years of coffee, tea, wine, tobacco, and everyday life can leave teeth looking stained or dull. Professional teeth whitening can dramatically brighten your smile in a safe and effective way.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are thin, custom-crafted shells that are bonded to the front surface of your teeth.
