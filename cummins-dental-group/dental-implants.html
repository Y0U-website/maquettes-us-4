How Does an Implant Work?

We surgically place a dental implant where the root of your missing tooth would be, and later top it with an artificial crown to create a new, artificial tooth.

However, to begin the procedure, we administer local anesthesia to prevent any pain. Dr. Cummins then places the post (a metal screw) into the jawbone. The jawbone then will need time (usually a couple of months) to grow around the implant so that the implant is cemented firmly into the mouth.

Abutment

Next, once the jaw has fully grown around the post, we place the abutment on top. The abutment is the piece that connects the artificial tooth to the post. Plus, we can place it during the first surgery or on a follow-up visit. However, your gums will need time (several weeks) to heal around the abutment before we can place the crown.

Then, once we place the post and abutment and your mouth heals, Dr. Cummins will take a mold impression of your teeth and a fake crown will be made. The crown will be the same color as your teeth, and we will mount it onto the abutment. Moreover, the tooth will look and act like a real tooth.

Receiving a dental implant can take some time. Still, avoiding future pain, disease, decay, jawbone deterioration, and many other problems with teeth not being replaced is well worth it.

The Benefits of Dental Implants in Silver Spring

Dental implants have become increasingly popular as a solution for missing teeth in recent years. Additionally, they are a more permanent and long-lasting option than tooth replacement methods, such as dentures or bridges. Plus, if you are considering getting dental implants, here are some reasons why it may be the right choice for you.

Improved Appearance and Confidence

One of the most significant benefits of dental implants is that they look and feel just like natural teeth. We design them to blend seamlessly with your existing teeth, giving you a more natural smile. This can significantly impact your self-confidence, especially if you have been self-conscious about missing teeth.

Dental implants also help maintain the structure of your face by preventing bone loss in the jaw. This can prevent a sunken or aged appearance often associated with missing teeth.

Better Functionality

Dental implants are anchored directly into the jawbone, just like natural teeth. This makes them much more stable and functional than other tooth replacement options. With dental implants, you can eat, speak, and chew without worrying about them shifting or falling out. This can significantly improve your quality of life and allow you to enjoy all your favorite foods without limitations.

Long-Lasting Solution

Unlike dentures or bridges, which may need to be replaced every few years, dental implants are a long-term solution for missing teeth. With proper care and maintenance, they can last a lifetime. This can save you time and money in the long run and eliminate the hassle of constantly having to replace your tooth replacements.

Improved Oral Health

Unlike bridges, dental implants do not require alterations to your other teeth, which may require us to file down adjacent teeth for support. This preserves the integrity of your natural teeth and helps maintain good oral health. In addition, dental implants do not increase the risk of tooth decay or gum disease, like dentures can when not properly cleaned.

Convenience and Comfort

Dental implants are a much more convenient option than dentures, which require daily removal for cleaning. With dental implants, you can brush and floss just like natural teeth, making them easy to maintain. They also eliminate the need for messy adhesives or uncomfortable adjustments often necessary with dentures.

Improved Speech

Missing teeth can significantly affect your speech, causing you to mumble or slur certain words. Dental implants fill in the gaps in your smile and allow you to speak clearly and confidently without any hindrance.

Natural-Looking Results

Thanks to advancements in dental technology, dental implants are now designed to look just like natural teeth. They are custom-made to match the shape, size, and color of your existing teeth, making them virtually indistinguishable. This can give you a more natural and aesthetically pleasing smile.

Increased Comfort

Dental implants in Silver Spring anchor directly into the jawbone, which helps distribute biting and chewing forces evenly. This makes them much more comfortable compared to other tooth replacement options that may put pressure on the gums or surrounding teeth.

Suitable for Anyone

Dental implants are suitable for almost anyone with missing teeth, regardless of age. You can benefit from dental implants as long as you have a healthy jawbone and gum tissue. Dr. Cummins will evaluate your case and determine if you are a good candidate for the procedure.

So, it is clear that dental implants offer a variety of benefits for those looking to replace missing teeth. Dental implants are a long-lasting and convenient solution for restoring your smile, improving appearance and confidence, functionality, or oral health.
