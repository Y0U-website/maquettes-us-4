The Process

How It Works

Getting implant-supported dentures is a comfortable, well-planned process. We take the time to position your implants correctly and fit your denture precisely before it ever snaps into place, so the final result feels secure and natural.

Consultation

We evaluate your jaw and gums and plan implant placement with a 3D scan.

Placement

A few implants are placed in the jaw to serve as secure anchors for your denture.

Healing

The implants integrate with the bone over a few months, forming a strong foundation.

Attachment

Your custom denture is fitted to snap firmly and comfortably onto the implants.

If you already wear a denture, we can often discuss whether it can be adapted, and in the meantime you’ll have a functional smile throughout the process.

Your Options

Snap-In or Fixed

There’s more than one way to secure an implant denture, and the best choice depends on your comfort, budget, and how you’d like to care for it. We’ll explain both clearly and help you decide, and either option is a dramatic upgrade over a traditional removable denture.

Many patients start with a snap-in design for its easy cleaning and value, while others prefer a fixed bridge for the most tooth-like experience. There’s no wrong answer, only the one that fits your life best.

  • Snap-In Overdenture Clips firmly onto implants and lifts out easily for cleaning.
  • Fixed Option Stays in permanently for the most natural, tooth-like feel.
  • Protects the Jaw Implants slow the bone loss that causes dentures to loosen. Long-term health
  • Stronger Bite You can enjoy foods that are difficult or impossible with a standard denture.

Is It Right for You?

Who It Helps

If a standard denture has held you back from eating the foods you enjoy or smiling freely, implant support is often the change that gives you your confidence back.

  • Frustrated by a loose, slipping, or rocking denture
  • Missing most or all of the teeth in an arch
  • Tired of relying on denture adhesives
  • Looking for a more secure, comfortable, lower-maintenance fit
