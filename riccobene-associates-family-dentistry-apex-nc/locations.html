Frequently Asked Questions

Do all locations offer the same services?

Riccobene Associates offers skilled general dentists at every location, ensuring comprehensive care wherever you visit. In most major cities, we also provide specialty services—so there’s always a Riccobene office nearby to meet your dental needs. While specialty care is not available at every location, our network ensures a convenient office is always close by to provide the specialized treatment you need.

What insurance plans do you accept?

Yes, all Brush & Floss locations provide comprehensive dental care including general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, and oral surgery. Some locations may have specialized equipment or additional services - please contact your preferred location for specific details.

How do I choose the right location for me?

We have over 70 locations throughout North Carolina, South Carolina, and Virginia. You can choose a location near you that fits your needs. You can view the full list of locations on their website and pick the one that’s most convenient for you.

Can I transfer my records between locations?

Yes, you can easily transfer dental records between Riccobene locations, and we also facilitate transfers from your previous dentist to our office for continuity of care.

What are your typical office hours?

Hours vary slightly by location, but a typical Riccobene Dentistry office schedule is: Monday – Thursday: 8:00 AM – 4:00 or 5:00 PM, Friday: 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM. Allo locations are closed on Saturday & Sunday.
