Meet the Teams at Our Locations

All staff at The Little Tooth Company has multiple years of experience treating children in a dental setting, as well as formal training in the field.

Jay Leung, DMD

Outside of work, Dr. Jay volunteers for Mission of Mercy, which provides free dental care, healthcare and prescription medication to uninsured people in Connecticut and other states. He also shows young children how to take care of their teeth through Head Start, a state-run early child development program. Dr. Jay is an instructor for the dental assistant program at Lincoln College of New England and a member of the Southington Dental Society. He also serves on the board of the Connecticut Society of Pediatric Dentists.

Dr. Jay lives in West Hartford with his wife Lindsey. Once a competitive runner, he now does most of his running chasing after their two young children, Lena and Everett.

Sarah Mir, DDS

While in dental school, Dr. Sarah travelled to Madagascar where she treated children with no access to dental care. This experience solidified her decision to become a pediatric dentist. She is committed to making every dental visit fun for both the patient and parent.

Dr. Sarah loves baking, traveling, and reading. She can t wait to meet you at your next visit!
