Meet with Dr. Alejandra Raftacco, DDS, to discuss whether a dental implant may be an appropriate option for replacing a missing tooth or supporting a restoration.

Dental implants are placed in the jaw to support replacement teeth. Because every patient’s health, bone structure and treatment goals are different, an examination is needed before a dentist can recommend an implant plan.

Dr. Raftacco has advanced training in implantology and oral rehabilitation. She will review your concerns, explain the steps that may apply to your situation and discuss alternatives when appropriate. Consultations are available in English and Spanish.

Depending on the examination, implants may be considered as part of a bridge or other restorative plan.
