Meet Dr. Kristina Zoulas, DDS

Conservative dentistry, personally delivered

After years of rigorous education and diverse clinical practice, Dr. Kristina Zoulas built a practice around a simple idea — treat fewer patients per day, and spend more time with each one.

A green-friendly, mercury-free office Dr. Kristina Zoulas stays on the forefront of preventative dentistry through advanced continuing dental education. The office is committed to keeping dentistry "green-friendly" with computerized X-rays (minimizing radiation exposure and eliminating film-processing chemicals), a mercury-free policy, and digital impressions that replace the traditional "goopy" trays.

Dr. Zoulas graduated from the University of the Pacific Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco and participated in the prestigious Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency program in Union City. She also completed a one-year implant externship accredited by the Stony Brook University School of Dental Medicine. Dr. Zoulas also served as a clinical instructor at her alma mater in San Francisco.
