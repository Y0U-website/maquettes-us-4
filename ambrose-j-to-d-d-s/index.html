The practice

Serving the local community and surrounding areas since 1968.

Since 1968 Damascus Dental Group has offered professional and reliable service. We cherish the long-term relationships we create with our clients. Our staff is determined to make your experience as comfortable as possible.

We are a full-service dental office offering the most comprehensive care. In fact, we have multiple hygienists on hand. So our crew can serve your whole family conveniently at the same time!

Our family-focused team is proud of the time and effort we take to get to know every one of our patients on a personal level. The Damascus Dental Group staff is dedicated to improving the oral health of you and your loved ones. We believe that good oral health leads to a better quality of life!

From toddlers to the elderly, our skilled team is familiar with the unique dental needs of every patient. Our goal is to help everyone develop great oral habits that will last a lifetime!

Since becoming owners in 2005, partners Dr. Ambrose To and Dr. Sanjay Rajani and their staff have been dedicated to serving the dental hygiene needs of individuals and families throughout Damascus and surrounding areas. Dr. Kyong Choe is a Perio/Prosthodontic specialist that visits our office monthly to place implants and help restore your smile.

We use a team approach of general dentists and specialists for comprehensive dental care.
