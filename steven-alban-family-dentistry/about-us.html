About Our Dental Practice

Dedicated to Healthy, Confident Smiles

At our dental practice, we believe that every patient deserves high-quality dental care in a comfortable and welcoming environment. Our mission is to provide personalized treatments that help individuals and families achieve long-lasting oral health and confident smiles.

With a focus on patient comfort and modern dental techniques, our team works hard to ensure that every visit is positive, informative, and stress-free. We proudly serve patients from Sussex County and Kent County , providing compassionate dental care for families and individuals throughout the community.

Meet Our Team

Dr. Steven Alban

After three years of studying for a Bachelor of Science from the University of Maryland, College Park, Dr. Alban was accepted to dental school. He graduated from the University of Maryland Dental School in 1999.

After earning his dental degree, Dr. Alban participated in a hospital residency program at Christiana Care hospital in Wilmington, DE. He has practiced privately in Delaware since 2000 and in Milford since 2005. His practice serves patients from Kent and Sussex Counties, including Felton, Lincoln, Milton, Harrington, Houston, DE.

Dr. Alban is an attending dentist with the Christiana Care General Dental residency program, and a Fellow in the Academy of General Dentistry. He is a member of the American Dental Association, the Delaware State Dental Society, and the Kent-Sussex Dental Society, too.

Besides practicing dentistry, he enjoys playing board games, reading, listening to music, playing the oboe, and English horn.

Dr. Shannon Noroozi

Originally from Iran, Dr. Shannon Noroozi immigrated to the United States at the age of 10 and was raised in Smyrna, Delaware. Her journey into dentistry began early—She earned her dental assisting certification from Delaware Technical Community College and worked as a dental assistant for over five years while completing her undergraduate studies.

Dr. Noroozi went on to graduate from the University of Delaware with a Bachelor of Arts before earning her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Temple University’s Maurice H. Kornberg School of Dentistry, where she ranked 5th in her class. She then completed a General Practice Residency at Christiana Care Hospital in Wilmington, Delaware, further advancing her clinical skills and patient care experience.

She is an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA), Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), Delaware State Dental Society (DSDS), and Omicron Kappa Upsilon, the National Dental Honor Society.

Dr. Noroozi is a warm and compassionate general dentist who values getting to know her patients. She is committed to making each visit comfortable while delivering high-quality, individualized care.

When she’s not in the office, Dr. Noroozi enjoys weightlifting, traveling, hiking in national parks, cooking, and spending time at the beach with her boyfriend, Alvin.
