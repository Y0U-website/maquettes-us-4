The Porcelain Crowns Procedure at Quality Dental Group in San Gabriel, CA

The procedure for getting porcelain crowns usually involves several steps: 1. Consultation : You’ll have an initial consultation with your dentist to discuss your dental concerns and determine if porcelain crowns are the right option for you. The dentist will examine your teeth, take X-rays if necessary, and develop a treatment plan based on your needs.

2. Preparation : During the preparation appointment, the dentist will numb the tooth and surrounding area with local anesthesia to ensure your comfort. Then, they will remove any decay or damaged portions of the tooth and reshape it to create space for the crown. This may involve reducing the tooth’s size and contouring it to ensure proper fitment of the crown.

3. Impressions : After preparing the tooth, the dentist will take impressions of your teeth. These impressions will be used by a dental laboratory to fabricate a custom porcelain crown that matches the size, shape, and color of your natural teeth.

4. Temporary Crown (if needed) : While your permanent crown is being fabricated, the dentist may place a temporary crown over the prepared tooth to protect it and maintain your smile’s appearance.

5. Placement : Once the permanent crown is ready, usually within a couple of weeks, you’ll return to the dental office for its placement. The dentist will ensure that the crown fits properly and matches your surrounding teeth in color and alignment. Once approved, the crown will be permanently bonded to the tooth using dental cement.

6. Final Adjustments : After the crown is bonded in place, the dentist may make final adjustments to ensure proper bite alignment and comfort.

7. Follow-Up : You may have a follow-up appointment to check on the fit and function of your crown. Your dentist will also provide instructions on how to care for your new restoration, including proper oral hygiene and maintenance.

What are Composite Fillings?

Composite fillings are custom-made dental restorations designed to fill in damaged or decayed areas of your teeth. They are made from a combination of resin and glass particles moulded to fit your teeth and then cured with a special light. Composite fillings are used to: - Repair cavities - Fill in small chips or cracks in teeth. - Improve the appearance of stained or misshapen teeth.
