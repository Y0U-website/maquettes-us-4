Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene Online Degree Completion

The high degree of faculty-student engagement is what sets our online B.S. in Dental Hygiene completion program apart from those of other universities. We’re always accessible to our students, getting to know each of them as we guide them toward success in the next phase of their careers.

Advance Your Career By Completing Your B.S. in Dental Hygiene

There are many reasons to earn your B.S. in Dental Hygiene when you already hold an A.S. degree in dental hygiene. The B.S. will enable you to seek careers in dental care with higher salaries, pursue careers beyond clinical practice, and gain a competitive edge over job applicants who don’t hold a bachelor’s degree. You must be a licensed dental hygienist with an A.S. degree in dental hygiene to pursue this online degree completion program.

The University of New Haven’s dental hygiene program is one of the most prestigious in the country, thanks to both its rigorous standards and student-centered approach. That’s the opinion of both The Best Colleges.org and GradReports.com, who have ranked the program as one of the best in the United States.

Those standards and student focus are fully present in our online degree completion program, which is specifically designed for practicing dental hygienists. It offers you:

Upon completion of the program, you also will be fully qualified to move out of the dental office and pursue professional opportunities in the areas of education, research, administration, public health, and corporate business, if you desire.

In addition, you will have carved out a perfect pathway to graduate study in the Master of Public Health and the Master of Health Administration degrees within the University of New Haven’s School of Health Sciences.

  • Fully online, asynchronous, self-paced learning that you can work into your busy professional and personal life
  • Instructors who are licensed dental hygienists, leaders in their field, and published researchers in the subjects you will study
  • A deeply dedicated faculty who mentor students through every step of the program
  • One-on-one help with adjusting to online technology and learning

Hear it From a Charger

Listen to what these former students have to say about our online degree completion program.

Unfailing faculty support helps her launch a career

“I couldn’t have done it without the support of the amazing faulty who helped me every step of the way.” That’s the bottom line for Alexa M. Houle, ’21, ’22 who received her B.S. in Dental Hygiene through the School of Health Sciences’ online completion program.

“I am so thankful I decided to pursue my bachelor’s degree in dental hygiene at the University of New Haven,” she said. “By having a completely remote schedule, I was able to kick off my career as a dental hygienist at the same time I was expanding my knowledge both clinically and online.”

A flexible program takes the pressure off for a working student

For Savannah Papuga ’21, the online completion program for the B.S. in Dental Hygiene fit right into her busy working schedule. “The program has given me the opportunity to continue my education while still being able to work full-time,” she explained. “Since the coursework is completely remote, you have the option to have a flexible schedule tailored around when you want to work on your assignments.”

Papuga added that not being required to meet at a specific time each week was a huge advantage. “Typically, your assignments open on a Sunday and are not due until the following week, which gives you ample time to complete your work.”

Since the human element is so central to the study of dental hygiene, some prospective students may wonder if the online program can meet the need for interaction between student and instructor. Papuga puts that worry to rest.

“Some may question whether there is enough one-on-one guidance with an online course, but you certainly get more than enough with this program,” she said. “I found that all of my instructors were easily accessible via email, Zoom, or cellphone whenever I had any questions or needed assistance. Our faculty truly want you to succeed and will go above and beyond to make sure you do!”

Gaining clinical experience while doing coursework was a definite asset

When Gabrielle Russell was looking at dental hygiene programs, the online B.S. in Dental Hygiene was what drew her to the University of New Haven. “I loved the idea of being able to work as a registered dental hygienist while I finished up my degree in my senior year,” she explained.

She enrolled in the program and immediately found that the flexibility of the online degree made it easy to do homework while she was beginning her clinical experience. To her, that had a significant career advantage. “Because the online dental hygiene program allows you to work while you complete your degree, it’s almost like having a head start,” she said.

Another great aspect of the program, according to Russell, is the close relationships that students are able to create with faculty. “During my time at the University, I always felt that I had a professor to communicate with, whether it was academic-related or personal,” she said. “The faculty in the Dental Hygiene program always had the right answers to the crazy questions we would ask!”
