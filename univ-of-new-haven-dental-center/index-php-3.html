School of Health Sciences

The University of New Haven School of Health Sciences delivers innovative, interdisciplinary allied health and health sciences education that prepares students to be caring, compassionate health professionals who improve the health of their communities.

Healthcare Innovation Is Here

The School of Health Sciences is known for its progressive student-centered curriculum. Our community-focused, interprofessional education provides students the opportunity to apply learned competencies in real world clinical settings. Students develop the skills necessary to be a successful healthcare professional in the 21 st century through hands-on experiences and a curriculum focused on healthcare innovation, systems thinking, and using evidence-based approaches to solve today’s biggest challenges in healthcare.

We are educating future health care leaders!

Message From the Dean

Welcome to the School of Health Sciences at University of New Haven!

No matter where our students begin their journey, they arrive at our school with a desire to make a difference. They are motivated by a belief that their work can improve lives, strengthen communities, and contribute to a healthier future. Some are drawn to research and discovery. Others see themselves caring for patients, improving systems, shaping policy, promoting wellness, or addressing challenges that affect the health of entire communities. Their paths may be different, but they are united by a shared commitment to improving the lives and well-being of others.

The School of Health Sciences is home to programs that span the full spectrum of health, from bench-top research to bedside care to population health and policy. Our students learn alongside peers from a wide range of disciplines, where they gain a broader understanding of health that prepares them to collaborate, adapt, and lead in a world where the most meaningful solutions rarely come from a single perspective.

Across every program, you will find faculty and staff who are deeply invested in student success. We challenge our students to think critically, act compassionately, and develop the knowledge and skills needed to lead in a rapidly evolving healthcare landscape. Just as importantly, we strive to know our students as people, supporting their goals, challenging them to grow, and helping them discover what they can achieve.

We believe that investing in our students is ultimately an investment in healthier communities. At its core, the School of Health Sciences is about preparing the next generation of professionals to meet complex challenges with knowledge, curiosity, and care. The next generation of clinicians, researchers, advocates, educators, and healthcare leaders is learning here today.

Whether your interests lie in science, clinical care, public health, healthcare leadership, or somewhere in between, you will find a community that is invested in your success and excited about the difference you can make.

I invite you to explore our programs, connect with our community, and discover what makes the School of Health Sciences such a special place to learn, grow, and lead.

Sarah P. Shultz, Ph.D. Dean, School of Health Science

The School of Health Sciences Difference

Our Mission

The School of Health Sciences’ mission is to educate competent, caring, and resilient health professionals by delivering innovative, interdisciplinary healthcare education and services.

The University of New Haven’s School of Health Sciences is one of first schools in the United States to pursue higher education certification from Planetree International.

The future of healthcare is filled with challenges that healthcare professionals will be asked to solve. Our core curriculum provides students with the courses and experiences necessary to build the tools you need to develop solutions for our complex healthcare system.

Every program in the School of Health Sciences requires you to get at least one hands-on experience outside of the classroom prior to graduation. Our on-campus facilities provide you with the training and confidence to prepare you for success in the real world.

We have our finger on the pulse of healthcare industry trends. Our advisory board comprises leaders from some of the largest healthcare systems and corporations in the U.S.
