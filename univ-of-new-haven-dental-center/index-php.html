Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene

The health of the mouth can affect the health of the entire body. Infection and inflammation of the teeth and gums can have long-term systemic consequences. In our B.S. in Dental Hygiene program — one of the finest dental hygiene programs in the U.S. — you’ll learn just how important the dental hygienist is to patients’ overall health and acquire the in-classroom knowledge and hands-on expertise you need for success in this highly rewarding field.

"At the University of New Haven, I not only learned about oral health care but also about myself and what I could truly accomplish," said Rachel Seggerman ‘15, ‘17 M.S. "The University gives students all the opportunities they could imagine to achieve their full potential."

Help protect the gateway to the body

The University of New Haven’s Dental Hygiene program emphasizes a hands-on approach to learning about this fast-growing and essential healthcare field. Our students learn cutting-edge prevention and detection skills, becoming both knowledgeable practitioners and careful guardians of their patients’ health. They learn from our skilled faculty, with their vast clinical experience, and apply those lessons in real-world settings, including the University’s Dental Center.

You will learn important prevention and detection skills as you become both a knowledgeable practitioner and a guardian of your patients’ health. You’ll also:

  • Learn about the latest advances in dental hygiene standards and infection control
  • Gain experience with modern treatment stations and techniques
  • Benefit from cutting-edge virtual instruction programs
  • Experience modern digital radiology and dental materials laboratories
  • Learn from experienced faculty with vast clinical experience
  • Apply what you learn while working with patients at the University of New Haven Dental Center – which has been called "a tremendous hidden gem" – and other internship sites

Accreditation

The University of New Haven’s Dental Hygiene program is accredited by the American Dental Association’s Commission on Dental Accreditation (CODA), and has been granted the accreditation status of "approval without reporting requirements." The Commission is a specialized accrediting body recognized by the United States Department of Education, and is the only agency to accredit post-secondary dental and dental-related education programs nationwide.

Dental Hygiene Courses and Learning Outcomes

As a graduate of the associate degree program, you will be ready for the necessary written National Board Dental Hygiene Examination and clinical board examination — and then employment in a dental office. Graduates of the Bachelor of Science program will be prepared to work in private dental offices as well as a variety of other healthcare settings. Bachelor of Science degree students also possess the knowledge and skills necessary to pursue a master’s degree.

B.S. in Dental Hygiene/Master of Public Health (Accelerated 3+1 or Direct Entry 4+1)

Being on the front lines of encouraging the practice of good oral hygiene is essential in helping individuals protect their overall health and well-being. Combining your B.S. in Dental Hygiene with a Master of Public Health will allow you to go beyond rendering individual dental care, enabling you to make a difference across an entire community.

B.S. in Dental Hygiene/Master of Healthcare Administration (Accelerated 3+1 or Direct Entry 4+1)

Unite your background in dental hygiene with an understanding of healthcare administration to prepare for a leadership role in the dental field. You will be equipped to oversee the strategic direction of a dental clinic, oral health product company, or related practice and to advocate for the importance of dental care in an overall treatment plan. You can also pursue opportunities in teaching and education.

Health Sciences Enhanced Learning Community

What it is: A community for students preparing for careers in healthcare and wellness.

Community focus: Leadership, teamwork, and holistic well-being.

Experiential Learning: Health Sciences ELC students explore how science, technology, and human care intersect through clinical simulations, professional site visits, and hands-on learning experiences across a wide range of health-related fields.

Adventures: Relax and recharge through nature hikes, apple picking, and friendly cooking challenges that promote wellness, connection, and balance. Students also go behind the scenes in local hospitals and healthcare facilities, gaining an inside look at real-world patient care and the many career paths within the health sciences. Along the way, students hear directly from healthcare professionals who share their career journeys, advice, and insight into the future of the field.

University Dental Center

Across the street from our main campus, the Dental Center features a preventive dental clinic with 18 chairs, three private radiology rooms, and a dental materials laboratory. The Center provides patients with a variety of oral healthcare services, from cavity and periodontal disease assessments to X-rays and oral cancer screenings. We serve approximately 1,000 patients per year.

Exciting news! As of September, 2026, the University has sent out an RFP (Request for Proposals) for architectural planning and design services for a for a renovated and expanded dental clinic of approximately 14,000 square feet. Although the project is still in its early stages, it’s a testament to the longevity and success of our program - with the best yet to come!
