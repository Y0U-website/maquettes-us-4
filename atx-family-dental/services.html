We offer comprehensive dental care in Austin, Texas

We’re a welcoming, full-service dental practice in Austin, providing everything from routine family care to cosmetic treatments that boost confidence.

Patients come to us from all over Austin and beyond, and we’re truly humbled by their trust. Putting patients first is what we do we are leaders in modern dental care that feels easy and comfortable. We’re proud to be a trusted spot for healthy smiles

Routine Care

Routine dental cleanings + exams ensure a healthy smile. They also contribute to your overall health well-being

Emergency Dentistry

Dental emergency? We’ve got you – same day care.

Tooth pain and emergencies are never fun, but you don’t have to deal with them alone. We make urgent dental care a priority, so if you’re hurting, we’ll do everything we can to get you in and feeling better—fast.

Invisalign

Invisalign can transform your teeth in as little as six months comfortably and discreetly. With advanced technology and a custom treatment plan, we’ll help you get a smile that’s not just straight, but healthy and confident. A straighter smile, made simple.

Dental Implants

Dental Veneers

Porcelain veneers can bring a complete smile transformation. These custom-made, ultra-thin shells are designed to cover the front surface of your teeth, effectively changing their color, shape, and length

Cosmetic Dentistry

If you’re unhappy with dark fillings, old crowns, food stains, pronounced white marks or other cosmetic issues, we can help. Tooth whitening with Zoom or KoR, bonding or natural-looking fillings are some of the tools we use to deliver the smile you want.

Root Canals

dental crowns

A dental crown is like a helmet that fits over a damaged tooth, restoring its shape, size, and strength. It’s often used when a tooth is cracked, worn down, or after a root canal. Think of it as a little makeover for your tooth—giving it a fresh, strong start!

Digital Impressions

3-D Scanning for Crowns, Night Guards, Retainers

We’re all about next-level dental tech. That’s why we’ve invested in cutting-edge 3D scanning—no more goopy, messy impressions. This digital tech captures your smile with incredible accuracy, making treatments like clear aligners, crowns, and retainers more precise and comfortable than ever. Better tech, better results, better experience.
