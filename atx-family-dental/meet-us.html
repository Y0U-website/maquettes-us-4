Welcome to ATX Family Dental, a staple in the Austin dental community since 2016

We’ve put a lot of thought into your visit so you feel informed, comfortable, and cared for.

We believe educated patients make the best decisions, so we’re all about giving you the full picture—how your oral health connects to your overall well-being and why tackling small issues now prevents bigger problems later.

With advanced technology and personalized care, we focus on results that look great and last. But more than that, we see you as part of our community, not just a patient—we’re invested in your health, your confidence, and your experience.

Since 2016, we’ve been living, working, and growing right here in 78704. This is our home, and we’re proud to be a part of the neighborhood. We’re here to be your partner in dental wellness, making every step clear, doable, and stress-free.

Because at the end of the day, everything is figureoutable. Come as you are—we’re here to help
