James Landholm, D.D.S

Hi, I’m Dr. Landholm! I grew up in Columbus, Nebraska, and after years away for school, I’m proud to be back serving patients in our community. I graduated from Nebraska Wesleyan University, where I played baseball, then went on to earn my dental degree from Creighton University. I’ve been practicing since 2018, and every day, I’m reminded why I love what I do—it’s all about the people.

Dentistry isn’t just about teeth; it’s about connection. I get to meet incredible patients every day, helping them feel comfortable, confident, and cared for. If I can help even one patient hate the dentist a little less, I consider that a win! My goal is to make every visit as easy and stress-free as possible, whether it’s a routine cleaning or a more complex treatment.

Outside the office, my wife, Ali, and I stay busy with our almost 3-year-old daughter, Lily, and we’re excited to welcome our second daughter this July (2025)! We also have a one-year-old golden retriever who keeps us on our toes. In my free time, I love golfing, grilling on my Big Green Egg, and spending time by the pool. Ali and I are big fans of traveling, concerts, musicals, and shows—anything that makes for a fun night out!

I’m grateful to work with an amazing team, and I want both our patients and our staff to love coming into the office every day. If you’re looking for a dental home where you’ll be treated like family, we’d love to welcome you!
