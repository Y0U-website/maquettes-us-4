Signs You May Need All-on-4 Dental Implants

All-on-4 suits patients with significant tooth loss who want to avoid grafting.

Full Arch You have several missing teeth, or an entire arch that needs replacing rather than a single isolated gap somewhere along your gumline.

Long-Term Solution You want a long-term, natural-looking replacement instead of an appliance you take out at the end of each day and soak in a glass overnight.

Grafting Avoided You prefer a solution that minimizes additional procedures such as the bone grafts other implant plans require before implants can be placed.

Denture Frustration Your current dentures rub, rock, or slip when you talk or eat, and you want something anchored permanently into your jaw instead.
