Signs You May Need a Single Tooth Implant

Single tooth implants suit patients seeking a permanent, self-supporting solution.

One Gap You have a missing tooth needing replacement, and the teeth on either side are healthy enough to leave alone and need no restoration of their own.

Healthy Foundation You have healthy gums and adequate bone structure, which is what allows an implant to integrate successfully with the bone around it over time.

Stability Wanted You want a stable and aesthetic solution rather than something removable that comes out at the end of the day and goes back in the next morning.

Bridge Reluctance You would rather not grind down two healthy neighboring teeth simply to anchor a traditional dental bridge across the space in your smile.
