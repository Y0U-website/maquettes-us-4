Signs You May Need Implant-Supported Dentures

These dentures suit patients wanting a stable alternative to removable plates.

Unstable Dentures You experience discomfort or instability with your current dentures, which slip or rub as you eat and talk throughout the course of a normal day.

Sufficient Density You have enough jawbone density to support implants, which our imaging confirms before treatment is planned or any surgery is scheduled.

Long-Lasting Wanted You want a long-lasting, effective solution for missing teeth rather than replacing an appliance every few years as your jaw shape changes.

Adhesive Fatigue You are tired of messy adhesives and pastes, and you want an appliance that snaps securely into position and stays there all day.
