Inlays & Onlay

When performing an inlay or onlay procedure, our cosmetic dentist Dr. Youngquist will begin by cleaning and removing any decay or fillings present. Then he will move to take a digital impression and use a CEREC machine to create a custom inlay or onlay. After Dr. Youngquist has created your custom restoration, he will cement the final inlay or onlay into the proper position and ensure that you have the proper bite alignment. The final step of this cosmetic dentistry procedure is to match your inlay or onlay with the surrounding teeth by polishing the teeth to help create the perfect match.
