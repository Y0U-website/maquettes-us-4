How Do All-ON-4 Dental Implants Work?

All-On-4 dental implants are similar to traditional prosthetics; however, they require only four implant posts as opposed to between four and eight. Positioned within the densest areas of the jawbone, two are placed toward the back of the mouth, while two are positioned toward the front. This creates a balance so that the custom denture sits comfortably on top and allows for maximum strength and durability.

Am I a Candidate for All-On-4 Dental Implants?

Typically, those who do not qualify for traditional dental implants can find help with All-On-4. When a lack of jawbone density keeps a person from enjoying life with regular implant dentures, these prosthetics can be the answer to achieving the same great results.

Our team will first need to perform a thorough examination of the mouth, though, to ensure that no additional problems exist, such as gum disease or existing tooth decay. If so, we will need to perform preliminary procedures before beginning the process of placing the All-On-4 dental implants.

Benefits of All-On-4 Dental Implants

All-On-4 dental implants possess many unique benefits that patients may not find with other forms of tooth replacement, such as:

  • Greater jawbone stimulation
  • Improved oral health
  • Younger facial aesthetics
  • Reduced risk of systemic diseases and conditions
  • Stronger bite force
  • Greater confidence
  • Can last decades and even a lifetime with the right kind of care

All-On-4 VS Traditional Dental Implants

Although it might seem as if All-On-4 is the same as traditional dental implants, there are a few key differences, such as:

  • Traditional dental implants require up to 8 or 10 implant posts, whereas All-On-4 only requires four titanium posts.
  • The cost is likely to be less with All-On-4 because of the fewer implant posts and less invasive procedure.
  • Recovery from All-On-4 may be quicker than when receiving traditional dental implants.
  • Depending on your mouth, our team may be able to place your new teeth on the same day as your All-On-4 placement.
