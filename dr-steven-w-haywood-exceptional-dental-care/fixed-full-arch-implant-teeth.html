Full-arch implant teeth questions, answered

What are fixed full-arch implant teeth?

A complete set of teeth that is screwed in or cemented onto implants, so it stays put. You may also hear it called All-on-Four or All-on-Five implants, a full fixed implant denture, a fixed hybrid implant denture, full fixed implant bridges, or “permanent dentures.” See all dental implant options .

How many implants do I need per arch?

At least 4 implants are basic to any good full-arch implant plan. More than the basic 4 are often suggested, and a lack of good bone support may lead Dr. Haywood to recommend more implants as your foundation.

What are the advantages of fixed implant teeth?

They are the most like natural teeth: they don’t move or wobble while eating, and chewing is the most natural of all the options. They offer strength and long-term stability, they’re the smallest full replacement style available, they can last a lifetime with professional care, and the teeth themselves can be repaired if they chip or break.

What are the downsides?

Fixed teeth can’t be removed to clean underneath, and they are the costliest option. They only last with professional care, so plan for that expense too. Chewing strength is so high that the teeth can be expected to need service at some point, which is why a spare set is always made, so you don’t have to go without teeth.

How are fixed implant teeth cleaned and maintained?

Only the practice can remove the teeth, and food collects underneath however carefully you clean. Removing the set every 3–4 months is recommended in most cases, to clean and sterilize the appliance and screws, re-torque the screws, clean the implants and give you specialized cleaning tools. See implant care .

Should I choose fixed or snap-on implant teeth?

Fixed teeth stay in and feel the most like natural teeth, but they cost the most. Snap-on implant dentures are removable and cost less; a full upper snap-on set is roughly 30 % less than a comparable fixed set. Dr. Haywood can go through both with you at a consultation. See snap-on implant teeth .

What are my options for the teeth themselves?

A hybrid implant restoration is mostly a plastic outer build with high-strength denture teeth as a starting point. For the ultimate durability and beauty, custom-made ceramic teeth are recommended, at additional cost. All-on-Four restorations are supported by custom CNC-milled titanium bars.

Can you give a second opinion on another office’s plan?

Yes. Plans from other offices, including large corporate implant centers, are often reviewed and a proposal prepared for you. Second opinions are always complimentary, and patients often find a personalized plan with a full range of restorative options more appealing.

Can I get teeth in a day?

Implants can support a full set of teeth in one day, and some implant replacements take only one day to complete. While implants integrate with the bone, temporary teeth are in place so you can chew comfortably. Whether that approach suits you depends on your scan and plan.
