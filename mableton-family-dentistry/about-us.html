Welcome To Your Cobb County Dentist

You can expect caring and comprehensive professional dental care for adults and children in the Atlanta metro and surrounding areas. Located on Floyd Rd SW, our practice has been in the heart of Mableton, GA, for over 25 years. Our dedicated and trained staff provide the full spectrum of implant dentistry, cosmetic and restorative dentistry, and general dentistry procedures.

When it comes to your dental health we provide the information you need to make an informed decision on the treatment and services you would like to receive. Prior to your dental treatment, we will discuss the materials that will be used, how the procedure will be performed, what your teeth will look like afterward, and the results you can expect in the future.

Meet Dr. Mit Brahmbhatt

Dr. Mit is a Georgia native and received his Bachelors degree in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from the University of Georgia. He then went to receive his Doctorate in Dental Medicine (DMD) from Midwestern University in Downers Grove, Illinois.

Since graduating with his dental degree, Dr. Mit has acquired an extensive amount of Continuing Education (CE) training that has enabled him to provide the latest technology and treatment options such as cosmetic dentistry, implant placement, and restoration, and Invisalign invisible braces.

Dr. Mit’s favorite part about dentistry is the relationships that he gets to build with his patients as he helps them achieve and maintain their dental health goals. He strives to make Mableton Family Dentistry a testament to the long-lasting bond between a patient and their dentist.

Meet Dr. Yokeca N. Watts

When investing in your smile, you deserve a dentist who combines clinical excellence with genuine compassion—that’s exactly what you’ll find in Dr. Yokeca Watts. At Mableton Family Dentistry , Dr. Watts brings advanced surgical training and a heart for people, offering procedures like wisdom tooth removal, implant placement, and sedation dentistry with skill and care.

What truly sets Dr. Watts apart is her ability to connect. She takes time to understand each patient’s goals and concerns, creating personalized, thoughtful treatment plans. Her warmth extends beyond the office, where she enjoys life with her husband and daughter, bringing the same empathy and care to her patients that she does to her family.

Meet Dr. Darling Rojas

When you meet Dr. Darling Rojas, you’ll quickly see her passion for dentistry goes far beyond teeth—it’s about people, connection, and helping patients feel genuinely cared for. Known for her gentle approach and attention to detail, Dr. Rojas believes every smile tells a story, and she’s honored to be part of her patients’ journeys toward healthier, more confident lives.
