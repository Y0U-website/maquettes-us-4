What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium or zirconia post that replaces the natural tooth root within your jawbone. Once healed, a custom crown or bridge is attached to restore full function and aesthetics. Unlike dentures, implants are fixed, stable, and maintain bone density — keeping your smile youthful and healthy for years to come.

  • A long-term solution to replace one or more missing teeth
  • Includes three parts: implant post, abutment, and crown
  • Feels and functions just like a natural tooth root Prevents bone loss and supports your facial structure
