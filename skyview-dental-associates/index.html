The practice

Welcome to Skyview Dental Associates, where our team of skilled dentists, led by Dr. Kim, is committed to delivering exceptional dental care with a focus on oral health and aesthetics. With state-of-the-art technology and a gentle touch, we strive to create beautiful smiles and lasting relationships with our patients.

Dr. Paul Kim, DDS. is a skilled general dentist with 17 years experience of clinical experience. Dr. Kim is leading the team at Skyview Dental Associates on River Road in Edgewater, New Jersey.

Dr. Kim graduated from Columbia University in 2002 with a Bachelor of Science in biomedical engineering. He continued to study at Columbia University Dental School and graduated as a Doctor of Dental Surgery in 2007. He then served as a captain in the US Army as a general dentist stationed in South Korea for three years.

Dr. Kim is excited to provide his services and care in the Edgewater area, at Skyview Dental Associates which opened in November 2020. At the practice, Dr. Kim offers many dental services, such as exams, cleanings, emergencies, fillings, and implants. He also offers a range of aesthetic services, including crowns , bridges, teeth whitening, and orthodontic treatment with Invisalign .

Dr. Kim lives in Englewood Cliffs with his wife, son, and his dog Bigmac. In his free time Dr. Kim loves to go hiking, play tennis, and explore the city.

To schedule an appointment with Dr. Kim and the team at Skyview Dental Associates, call the office or book online today.
