As a mom of two little ones, I know how difficult it can be to find a dentist you trust. Boring waiting rooms can be a nightmare that only leads to negative experiences while receiving the care your child needs. At Brush Kids Dentistry, our mission is to provide Lawrenceburg with comprehensive pediatric dental services for infants, children, adolescents, and patients with special needs in a loving, friendly, and caring environment while focusing on primary preventive techniques and quality educational experience for the family.
