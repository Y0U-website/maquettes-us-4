Tongue & Lip Ties Tongue -ties are also called ankyloglossia and known as a condition that restricts the tongues range of motion. The condition is present at birth. A short, tight band of tissue tethers the tongue’s tip to the floor of the mouth. It can affect how a child eats, sleeps, and can interfere with breastfeeding. At Brush Kids Dentistry, we treat the symptoms so your child can have full tongue function that’s essential for the following:

  • Airways & breathing
  • Feeding & swallowing
  • Speech development
  • Sensory processing
  • Cranial growth & shape
  • Nervous system

DO’S and dont’s

Helpful tips to make your first dental visit fun

  • Tell your child that they are going to the pediatric dentist to get their teeth counted, probably take some photos (x-rays), and clean their teeth with a very special toothbrush!
  • If you don’t know the answer to your child’s questions, respond with "I don’t know, let’s ask Dr. Duff when we get there!"
  • Please refrain from discussing any negative dental experiences or dental anxiety you may have in order to ensure a positive experience for your child.
  • Sometimes, in trying to prepare your child for your appointment with us, you may use a scary word when describing what to expect. Let’s avoid using words such as needle, shot, pinch, injection, pull, and pain.
