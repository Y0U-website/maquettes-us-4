Meet The Doctors

Dr. Mena Botros, DDS

Dr. Mena Botros, holds a Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) Degree from New York University and Bachelor’s Degree of Dental Surgery (BDS) that was achieved in 2004 from Egypt. Wanting to help others in his profession for years he taught dental assistants the skills they would need to succeed, while at the same time continuing his education and graduating from his Alma Mater, New York University College of Dentistry.

Dr. Botros is licensed by the Commonwealth of Virginia and is an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA), Virginia Dental Association (VDA), Northern Virginia Dental Association (NVDA), Valley Dental Club member, NYU Alumni Association.

When he is not practicing dentistry, Dr. Botros can be found volunteering at Mission of Mercy, Holiday food banks, and at Saint Marina Coptic Orthodox Church. He has also preformed much charitable dentistry with a focus on education, believing good oral health is learned and then practiced.

After practicing and serving the City of Winchester for few years he chose to open his own private practice in Town of Strasburg, Virginia, where he has embraced the community and is happily helping and serving the citizens of Strasburg and neighboring towns and cities.

During his free time, Dr. Botros enjoys watching and playing sports (soccer and Basketball), enjoys playing volleyball and table tennis, and spending time with his wife, daughter, and Goldendoodle.
