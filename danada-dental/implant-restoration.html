Implant Restoration

Dental implants are artificial teeth root replacements and are used in prosthetic dentistry to support restorations. With implant dentistry, your mouth can be returned to its original chewing capability. Implants are a stronger and more permanent solution to missing teeth than older, traditional methods (dentures, removable bridges). Implants feel like natural, healthy teeth and because they bond with the bone, can help prevent bone loss.

Danada Square Dental Center dentists provide expert implant placement and restoration services. Come in for an evaluation to see if this solution is right for you.
