Our Implant Treatment Process

1. Advanced Digital Planning

Using CBCT technology for detailed 3D imaging and the newest iTero Lumina digital scanner for highly accurate digital impressions, Dr. Bowles and Dr. Karow carefully plan every aspect of treatment before surgery begins.

2. Same-Day Implant Placement On the day of surgery, any damaged or failing teeth are removed before the implants are placed. When appropriate for healing and long-term success, temporary teeth may also be attached to the implants the very same day.

3. Advanced Healing Therapies

To support healing and long-term implant success after surgery, advanced healing therapies like platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) may be used to help your body recover more effectively.

4. Implant Integration Over the next several months, the implant gradually integrates with the surrounding bone to create a strong, stable foundation designed for long-term function and durability.

5. Final Smile Restoration

Once healing is complete, your final implant crown, bridge, or full-arch restoration is placed to complete your smile.

Implant FAQs

How Long Will Dental Implants Last?

With proper care, there’s no reason a dental implant can’t last a lifetime. Regular cleanings, healthy habits, and good home hygiene all help support long-term implant success.

How Do I Clean My Dental Implants?

Dental implants should be brushed and flossed similarly to natural teeth while also maintaining regular professional cleanings and checkups. SRQ Smiles includes implant cleanings at no additional charge during recommended maintenance visits to help support long-term implant health.

Can My Dental Implant Become Infected?

Yes. Like natural teeth, dental implants can develop infections if proper oral hygiene and maintenance are neglected. Regular cleanings, healthy gums, and routine dental visits play an important role in helping protect the long-term health and stability of dental implants.
