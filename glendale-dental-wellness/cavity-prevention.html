Prevention Services We Offer

Dr. Chan combines professional treatments with thorough examinations to catch problems early and keep your teeth cavity-free:

Fluoride Treatments

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants create a thin protective barrier on the chewing surfaces of molars, where cavities most commonly form. Sealants are especially effective for children but benefit adults as well.

Professional Cleanings

Regular professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup that brushing and flossing miss. Cleanings reach areas beneath the gumline and between teeth where decay often starts.

Comprehensive Exams

During your dental checkup , Dr. Chan examines every tooth for early signs of decay, weak enamel, and risk factors. Catching problems early means simpler, less invasive solutions.

Digital X-Rays

Advanced digital imaging reveals decay between teeth and beneath existing restorations that visual exams cannot detect. Early detection through X-rays prevents small issues from becoming large problems.

Patient Education

Dr. Chan and our hygiene team provide personalized guidance on brushing technique, flossing, diet, and product recommendations tailored to your specific risk factors and prevention goals .

At-Home Cavity Prevention Tips

Use a soft-bristled toothbrush at a 45-degree angle to the gumline. Brush all surfaces with gentle circular motions, including the backs of your teeth and your tongue.

Flossing removes plaque and food particles from between teeth where your toothbrush cannot reach. Curve the floss around each tooth and slide it gently beneath the gumline.

Sugar feeds cavity-causing bacteria, and acidic drinks erode enamel. Choose calcium-rich foods like cheese and yogurt, and crunchy vegetables that naturally clean your teeth.

An antimicrobial or fluoride rinse adds an extra layer of protection after brushing and flossing, reaching areas that mechanical cleaning may miss.

Water washes away food particles and bacteria throughout the day. Drinking fluoridated water provides additional enamel protection between brushings.

Professional cleanings and checkups every six months catch early decay and remove buildup that home care alone cannot address.

Cavities form when bacteria in your mouth produce acids after consuming sugars and starches. These acids attack tooth enamel, creating small holes over time. Regular brushing, flossing, and limiting sugar intake help prevent this process.

Cavity prevention involves a combination of good home care (brushing twice daily and flossing), limiting sugary foods and drinks, professional cleanings every six months, and optional preventive treatments like sealants and fluoride.
