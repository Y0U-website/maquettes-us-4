Meet Dr. Moji Ghalambor

Dr. Moji Ghalambor, DDS

For Dr. Moji Ghalambor, dentistry has always been about more than fixing teeth -- it’s about preserving health, relieving pain, and helping people feel whole again. Born and raised in Southern California, Dr. Ghalambor grew up in Glendora, where sports, family, and community shaped his early years.

Discovering a Passion for Endodontics

His love for teamwork carried through college, where he played soccer at Hope International University in Fullerton while earning his degree in Biology.

Dr. Ghalambor then pursued dentistry at the University of Southern California (USC) School of Dentistry , earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS). He first worked as a general dentist, but soon realized his true passion lay in Endodontics -- the specialty dedicated to saving natural teeth through root canal therapy and advanced treatments. Returning to USC, he completed his Endodontics specialty training .

Service Beyond Borders

Class President Served as class president for four years at USC, deepening his leadership skills and dedication to teamwork.

Global Mission Trips Traveled to Belize, Cambodia, Nicaragua, and Panama to provide dental care in underserved communities.

USC Mobile Clinic Delivered essential dental care to families across Los Angeles through the USC Mobile Clinic program.

Life Outside the Chair

Away from the dental chair, Dr. Ghalambor enjoys barbecuing with family and friends and stays active playing pick-up soccer and basketball during the week. For relaxation, he loves to travel -- his favorite destinations include Hawaii and Mexico, where he recharges and finds new inspiration.

Endodontics in Glendale

As an Endodontist at Glendale Dental Wellness , Dr. Ghalambor combines advanced training, modern technology, and a patient-first approach to deliver gentle, effective care for patients in Glendale, CA and surrounding communities including Burbank, Pasadena, La Crescenta, and Eagle Rock.

Whether performing root canal therapy or treating complex cases, his goal is always the same: to relieve pain, protect natural teeth, and give patients lasting confidence in their smiles.
