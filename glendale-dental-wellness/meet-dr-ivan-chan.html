Meet Dr. Ivan Chan

Dr. Ivan Chan, DDS

For Dr. Ivan Chan, dentistry has always been more than a profession -- it’s a calling shaped by family, community, and a deep desire to make a difference. Born in Hong Kong and raised in Sacramento, Dr. Chan grew up surrounded by dentistry.

Coming from a family of dentists, he witnessed early on how oral health could transform not just smiles, but lives. Those experiences inspired him to pursue a path where science, skill, and compassion come together.

From Student to Dentist

His journey began at the University of California, San Diego , where he majored in Biology. While there, Dr. Chan found purpose beyond the classroom, volunteering with Student-Run Free Dental Clinics that provided care for underserved communities in San Diego. It was during this time that he realized dentistry was not only about teeth -- it was about restoring dignity, confidence, and hope.

Driven to expand his knowledge, Dr. Chan continued his education at the University of California, San Francisco School of Dentistry . At UCSF, he received advanced training in preventive, restorative, cosmetic, and implant dentistry, equipping him with the skills to serve patients with a wide range of needs.

Service Beyond Borders

Free Dental Clinics Volunteered with Student-Run Free Dental Clinics in San Diego, providing care for underserved communities.

USNS Mercy Served aboard the USNS Mercy Hospital Ship with the U.S. Navy, delivering dental care to communities in need.

Mission Trips Provided dental care in rural communities in Tijuana and Ensenada, Mexico, reinforcing his commitment to accessible care.

Life Outside the Chair

When he’s not helping patients at Glendale Dental Wellness, Dr. Chan enjoys life’s simple joys -- watching basketball, exploring local breweries, and traveling to new cities and countries. He believes balance outside of work makes him a better dentist inside the office, allowing him to approach each day with energy, empathy, and focus.

Today, Dr. Chan is proud to call Glendale his professional home. Every patient who sits in his chair is met with the same values that shaped his journey: compassion, precision, and a genuine commitment to lifelong oral health. For Dr. Chan, every smile is a story -- and he looks forward to helping you write yours.

Care Dr. Chan Provides

Dr. Chan handles the full range of general, preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry under one roof at Glendale Dental Wellness, coordinating with our in-house orthodontist Dr. Jonathan Gao and endodontist Dr. Moji Ghalambor when specialty expertise is needed.

Dr. Chan welcomes patients from across Glendale , Burbank , Pasadena , Eagle Rock , La Cañada Flintridge , Montrose , Atwater Village , Los Feliz , and Highland Park .
